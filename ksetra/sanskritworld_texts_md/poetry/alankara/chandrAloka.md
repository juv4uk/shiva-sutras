śrīḥ

pīyuṣavarṣaśrījayadevakavikṛtaḥ
candrālokaḥ





uccairasyati mandatāmarasatāṃ jāgratkalaṅkairavadhvaṃsaṃ hastayate ca yā sumanasāmullāsinī mānase  |
dhṛṣṭodyanmadanāśanārciramalā lokatrayīdarśikā sā netratritayīva khaṇḍaparaśorvāgdevatā dīvyatu  || 1.1 ||


haṃ ho cinmayacittacandramaṇayaḥ saṃvardhayadhvaṃ rasān re re svairiṇi nirvicārakavite māsmatprakāśībhava  |
ullāsāya vicāravīcinicayālaṅkāravārāṃ nidheścandrālokamayaṃ svayaṃ vitanute pīyūṣavarṣaḥ kṛtī  || 1.2 ||


yuktyāsvādyalasadrasaikavasatiḥ sāhityasārasvatakṣīrāmbhodhiragādhatāmupadadhatsevyaḥ samāśrīyatām  |
śrīrasmādupadeśakauśalamayaṃ pīyūṣamasmājjagajjāgradbhāsurapadmakeśarayaśaḥśītāṃśurasmādbudhāḥ  || 1.3 ||

taṃ pūrvācāryasūryoktijyotiḥ stomodgamaṃ stumaḥ  |
yaṃ prastūya prakāśante madguṇāstrasareṇavaḥ  || 1.4 ||


nāśaṅkanīyameteṣāṃ matametena dūṣyate  |
kiṃ tu cakṣurmṛgākṣīṇāṃ kajjaleneva bhūṣyate  || 1.5 ||

pratibhaiva śrutābhyāsasahitā kavitāṃ prati  |
heturmṛdambusambaddhā bījamālā latāmiva  || 1.6 ||

nirdoṣā lakṣaṇavatī sarītirguṇabhūṣaṇā  |
sālaṃkārarasānekavṛttirvākkāvyanāmabhāk || 1.7 ||

aṅgīkaroti yaḥ kāvyaṃ śabdārthāvanalaṃkṛtī  |
asau na manyate kasmādanuṣṇamanalaṃ kṛtī  || 1.8 ||

vibhaktyutpattaye yogyaḥ śāstrīyaḥ śabda iṣyate  |
rūḍhayaugikatanmiśraiḥ prabhedaiḥ sa punastridhā  || 1.9 ||

avyaktayoganiryogayogābhāsaistridhādimaḥ  |
te ca vṛkṣādibhūvādimaṇḍapādyā yathākramam  || 1.10 ||

śuddhatanmūlasaṃbhinnaprabhedairyaugikastridhā  |
te ca bhrāntisphuratkāntikaunteyādisvarūpiṇaḥ  || 1.11 ||

tanmiśro'nyonyasāmānyaviśeṣaparivartanāt |
nīradhiḥ paṅkajaṃ saudhaḥ sāgaro bhūruhaḥ śaśī  || 1.12 ||

kṣīranīradhirākāśapaṅkajaṃ tena siddhayati  |
vibhaktyantaṃ padaṃ vākyaṃ tadvyūho'rthasamāptitaḥ  || 1.13 ||

yuktārthatāṃ tāṃ ca vinā khaṇḍavākyaṃ sa iṣyate  |
vākyaṃ ca khaṇḍavākyaṃ ca padamekamapi kvacit || 1.14 ||

dhūmavattvāditi yathā devetyāmantraṇaṃ yathā  |
vākyānyekārthaviśrāntānyāhurvākyakadambakam  || 1.15 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
anenāsāvādyaḥ sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke sukhayatu mayūkhaḥ sumanasaḥ  || 1.16 ||


syācceto viśatā yena sakṣatā ramaṇīyatā  |
śabde'rthe ca kṛtonmeṣaṃ doṣamudghoṣayanti tam  || 2.1 ||

bhavecchrutikaṭurvarṇaḥ śravaṇodvejane paṭuḥ  |
saṃvidrate vyākaraṇaviruddhaṃ cyutasaṃskṛti  || 2.2 ||

aprayuktaṃ daivatādiśabde puṃlliṅgatādikam  |
asamarthaṃ tu hantyādeḥ prayogo gamanādiṣu  || 2.3 ||

sa hanti hanta kāntāre kāntaḥ kuṭilakuntalaḥ  |
nihatārthaṃ lohitādau śoṇitādiprayogataḥ  || 2.4 ||

vyanaktyanucitārthaṃ yatpadamāhustadeva tat |
iyamadbhutaśākhyagrakelikautukavānarī  || 2.5 ||

nirarthakaṃ tuhītyādi pūraṇaikaprayojanam  |
arthe vidadhadityādau dadhadādyamavācakam  || 2.6 ||

dhatte nabhastalaṃ bhāsvānaruṇaṃ taruṇaiḥ karaiḥ  |
ekākṣaraṃ vinā bhūbhrūkṣmādikaṃ khatalādivat || 2.7 ||

aślīlaṃ trividhaṃ vrīḍājugupsāmaṅgalātmanā  |
āhlādasādhanaṃ vāyuḥ kāntānāśe bhavetkatham  || 2.8 ||

syāddvyarthamiha sandigdhaṃ nadyāṃ yānti patatriṇaḥ  |
syādapratītaṃ śāstraikagamyaṃ vītānumādivat || 2.9 ||

śithilaṃ śayane lilye maccitaṃ te śaśiśriyi  |
mastapiṣṭakaṭīloṣṭagallādi grāmyamucyate  || 2.10 ||

neyārthaṃ lakṣaṇātyantaprasarādamanoharam  |
himāṃśorhāradhikkārajāgare yāmikāḥ karāḥ  || 2.11 ||

kliṣṭamartho yadīyo'rthaśreṇiniḥśreṇimṛcchati  |
haripriyāpitṛvadhūpravāhapratimaṃ vacaḥ  || 2.12 ||
avimṛṣṭavidheyāṃśaḥ samāsapihite vidhau  |
viśanti viśikhaprāyāḥ kaṭākṣāḥ kāmināṃ hṛdi  || 2.13 ||

aparādhīna ityādi viruddhamatikṛnmatam  |
anyasaṅgatamuttuṅgahāraśobhipayodharau  || 2.14 ||

rasādyanucite varṇe pratikūlākṣaraṃ viduḥ  |
na māmaṅgada jānāsi rāvaṇaṃ raṇadāruṇam  || 2.15 ||

yasminnupahato lupto visarga iha tattathā  |
kusandhiḥ paṭavāgaccha visandhirnṛpatī imau  || 2.16 ||

hatavṛttamanukto'pi chandodoṣaścakāsti cet |
viśālalocane! paśyāmbaraṃ tārātaraṅgitam  || 2.17 ||

nyūnaṃ tvatkhaḍgasaṃbhūtayaśaḥpuṣpaṃ nabhastaṭam  |
adhikaṃ bhavataḥ śatrūn daśatyasilatāphaṇī  || 2.18 ||

kathitaṃ punaruktā vākśyāmābjaśyāmalocanā  |
vikṛtaṃ dūravivṛtairaiyaruḥ kuñjarāḥ puram  || 2.19 ||

patatprakarṣaṃ hīnānuprāsāditve yathottaram  |
gambhīrārambhadambholipāṇireṣaḥ samāgataḥ  || 2.20 ||

samāptapunarāttaṃ syādeṣa pīyūṣabhājanam  |
netrānandī tuṣārāṃśurudetyambudhibāndhavaḥ  || 2.21 ||

ardhāntarapadāpekṣi krīḍānṛtyeṣu sasmitam  |
moghārambhaṃ stumaḥ śambhumardharambhoruvigraham  || 2.22 ||

abhavanmatayogaḥ syānna cedabhimato'nvayaḥ  |
yena baddho'mbudhiryasya rāmasyānucarā vayam  || 2.23 ||

dviṣāṃ sampadamācchidya yaḥ śatrūn samapūrayat |
asthānasthasamāsaṃ na vidvajjanamanoramam  || 2.24 ||

mithaḥ pṛthagvākyapadaiḥ saṃkīrṇaṃ yattadeva tat |
vaktreṇa bhrājate rātriḥ kāntā candreṇa rājate  || 2.25 ||
brahmāṇḍaṃ tvadyaśaḥpūragarbhitaṃ bhūmibhūṣaṇa  |
ākarṇaya payaḥpūrṇasuvarṇakalaśāyate  || 2.26 ||

bhagnaprakramamārabdhaśabdanirvāhahīnatā  |
akramaḥ kṛṣṇa pūjyante tvāmanabhyarcya devatāḥ  || 2.27 ||

amatārthāntaraṃ mukhye'mukhyenārthe virodhakṛt |
tyaktahāramuraḥkṛtvā śokenāliṅgitāṅganā  || 2.28 ||

apuṣṭārtho viśeṣye cenna viśeṣo viśeṣaṇāt |
viśanti hṛdayaṃ kāntākaṭākṣāḥ khañjanatviṣaḥ  || 2.29 ||

kaṣṭaḥ spaṣṭāvabodhārthamakṣamo vācyasannibhaḥ  |
vyāhataścedvirodhaḥ syānmithaḥ pūrvāparārthayoḥ  || 2.30 ||

sahasrapatramitraṃ te vaktraṃ kenopamīyate  |
kutastatropamā yatra punaruktaḥ sudhākaraḥ  || 2.31 ||

duṣkramagrāmyasandigdhāstrayo doṣāḥ kramādamī  |
tvadbhaktaḥ kṛṣṇa! gaccheyaṃ narakaṃ svargameva vā  || 2.32 ||

ekaṃ me cumbanaṃ dehi tava dāsyāmi kañcukam  |
brūta kiṃ sevyatāṃ candramukhīcandrakirīṭayoḥ  || 2.33 ||

anaucityaṃ kīrtilatāṃ taraṅgayati yaḥ sadā  |
prasiddhyā vidyayā vāpi viruddhaṃ dvividhaṃ matam  || 2.34 ||

nyasteyaṃ paśya kandarpapratāpadhavaladyutiḥ  |
ketakī śekhare śambhordhatte candrakalātulām  || 2.35 ||

sāmānyaparivṛttiḥ syātkuṇḍalacchavivigrahā  |
viśeṣaparivṛttiḥ syādvanitā mama cetasi  || 2.36 ||

tathā sahacarācāruviruddhānyonyasaṃgatī  |
dhvāṅkṣāḥ santaśca tanayaṃ svaṃ paraṃ ca na jānate  || 2.37 ||

sarojanetra putrasya mukhendumavalokaya  |
pālayiṣyati te gotramasau narapurandaraḥ  || 2.38 ||
pade tadaṃśe vākyāṃśe vākye vākyakadambake  |
yathānusāramabhyūheddoṣān śabdārthasaṃbhavān  || 2.39 ||

doṣamāpatitaṃ svānte prasarantaṃ viśṛṅkhalam  |
nivārayati yastredhā doṣāṅkuśamuśanti tam  || 2.40 ||

doṣe guṇatvaṃ tanute doṣatvaṃ vā nirasyati  |
bhavantamathavā doṣaṃ nayatyatyājyatāmasau  || 2.41 ||

mukhaṃ candraśriyaṃ dhatte śvetaśmaśrukarāṅkuraiḥ  |
atra hāsyarasoddeśe grāmyatvaṃ guṇatāṃ gatam  || 2.42 ||

tava dugdhābdhisaṃbhūteḥ kathaṃ jātā kalāṅkitā  |
kavīnāṃ samayādvidyāviruddho'doṣatāṃ gataḥ  || 2.43 ||

dadhāra gaurī hṛdaye devaṃ himakarāṅkitam  |
atra śleṣodayānnaiva tyājyaṃ hīti nirarthakam  || 2.44 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
dvitīyastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke sukhayatu mayūkhaḥ sumanasaḥ  || 2.45 ||




alpākṣarā vicitrārthakhyātirakṣarasaṃhatiḥ  |
uṣākāntenānugataḥ śūraḥ śaurirayaṃ punaḥ  || 3.1 ||

śobhā khyātāpi yaddoṣo guṇakīrtyā niṣidhyate  |
mudhā nindanti saṃsāraṃ kaṃsāriryatra pūjyate  || 3.2 ||

abhimāno vicāraścedūhitārthaniṣedhakṛt |
induryadi kathaṃ tīvraḥ sūryo yadi kathaṃ niśi  || 3.3 ||

hetustyaktvā bahūn pakṣān yuktyaikasyāvadhāraṇam  |
nendurnārko'yamaurvāgniḥ sāgarādutthito dahan  || 3.4 ||

pratiṣedhaḥ prasiddhānāṃ kāraṇānāmanādaraḥ  |
na yuddhena bhruvoḥ spandenaiva vīrā nipātitāḥ  || 3.5 ||
niruktaṃ syānnirvacanaṃ nāmnaḥ satyaṃ tathānṛtam  |
īdṛśaiścaritai rājan satyaṃ doṣākaro bhavān  || 3.6 ||

syānmithyādhyavasāyaścedasatī sādhyasādhane  |
candrāṃśusūtragrathitāṃ nabhaḥpuṣpasrajaṃ vaha  || 3.7 ||

siddhiḥ khyāteṣu cennāma kīrtyate tulyatoktaye  |
yuvāmeveha vikhyātau tvaṃ balairjaladhirjalaiḥ  || 3.8 ||

yuktirviśeṣasiddhiścedvicitrārthāntarānvayāt |
navastvaṃ nīradaḥ ko'pi svarṇairvarṣasi yanmuhuḥ  || 3.9 ||

kāryaṃ phalopalambhaścedvyāpārādvastuto'tha vā  |
asāvudeti śītāṃśurmānacchedāya subhruvām  || 3.10 ||

ityādi lakṣaṇaṃ bhūri kāvyasyāhurmaharṣayaḥ  |
svarṇabhrājiṣṇubhālatvaprabhṛtīva mahībhujaḥ  || 3.11 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
tṛtīyastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke sukhayatu mayūkhaḥ sumanasaḥ  || 3.12 ||



śleṣo vighaṭamānārthaghaṭamānatvavarṇanam  |
sa tu śābdaḥ sajātīyaiḥ śabdairbandhaḥ sukhāvahaḥ  || 4.1 ||

ullasattanutāṃ nīte'nante pulakakaṇṭakaiḥ  |
bhītayā mānavatyaiva śriyāśliṣṭaṃ hariṃ stumaḥ  || 4.2 ||

yasmādantaḥsthitaḥ sarvaḥ svayamartho'vabhāsate  |
salilasyeva sūktasya sa prasāda iti smṛtaḥ  || 4.3 ||

samatālpasamāsatvaṃ varṇādyaistulyatātha vā  |
śyāmalā komalā bālā ramaṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ gatā  || 4.4 ||

samādhirarthamahimā lasadghanarasātmanā  |
syādantarviśatā yena gātramaṅkuritaṃ satām  || 4.5 ||

mādhuryaṃ punaruktasya vaicitryaṃ cārutāvaham  |
vayasya paśya paśyāsyāścañcalaṃ locanāñcalam  || 4.6 ||

ojaḥ syātprauḍhirarthasya saṃkṣepo vātibhūyasaḥ  |
ripuṃ hatvā yaśaḥ kṛtvā tvadasiḥ kośamāviśat || 4.7 ||

saukumāryamapāruṣyaṃ paryāyaparivartanāt |
sa kathāśeṣatāṃ yātaḥ samāliṅgya marutsakham  || 4.8 ||

udāratā tu vaidagdhyamagrāmyatvātpṛthaṅmatā  |
mānaṃ muñca priye kiṃcillocanāntamudañcaya  || 4.9 ||

śṛṅgāre ca prasāde ca kāntyarthavyaktisaṃgrahaḥ  |
amī daśa guṇāḥ kāvye puṃsi śauryādayo yathā  || 4.10 ||

tilakādyamiva strīṇāṃ vidagdhahṛdayaṅgamam  |
vyatiriktamalaṅkāraṃ prakṛterbhūṣaṇaṃ girām  || 4.11 ||

vicitralakṣaṇo nyāso nirvāhaḥ prauḍhiraucitī  |
śāstrāntararahasyoktiḥ saṃgraho dikpradarśitā  || 4.12 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
caturthastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke sukhayatu mayūkhaḥ sumanasaḥ  || 4.13 ||



śabdārthayoḥ prasiddhyā vā kaveḥ prauḍhivaśena vā  |
hārādivadalaṅkāraḥ sanniveśo manoharaḥ  || 5.1 ||

svaravyañjanasaṃdohavyūhāmandohadohadā  |
gaurjagajjāgradutsekā chekānuprāsabhāsurā  || 5.2 ||

āvṛttavarṇasampūrṇaṃ vṛttyanuprāsavadvacaḥ  |
amandānandasandohasvacchandāspadamandiram  || 5.3 ||

lāṭānuprāsabhūrbhinnābhiprāyā punaruktatā  |
yatra syānna punaḥ śatrorgarjitaṃ tajjitaṃ jitam  || 5.4 ||

ślokasyārdhe tadardhe vā varṇāvṛttiryadi dhruvā  |
tadā matā matimatāṃ sphuṭānuprāsatā satām  || 5.5 ||

upameyopamānādāvarthānuprāsa iṣyate  |
candanaṃ khalu govindacaraṇadvandvavandanam  || 5.6 ||

punaruktapratīkāśaṃ punaruktārthasannibham  |
aṃśukāntaṃ śaśī kurvannambarāntamupaityasau  || 5.7 ||

āvṛttavarṇastabakaṃ stavakandāṅkuraṃ kaveḥ  |
yamakaṃ prathamā dhuryamādhuryavacaso viduḥ  || 5.8 ||

kāvyavitpravaraiścitraṃ khaḍgabandhādi lakṣyate  |
teṣvādyamucyate ślokadvayīsajjanarañjikā  || 5.9 ||

kāminīva bhavetkhaḍgalekhā cārukarālikā  |
kāśmīrasekā raktāṅgī śatrukaṇṭhāntikāśritā  || 5.10 ||

upamā yatra sādṛśyalakṣmīrullasati dvayoḥ  |
hṛdaye khelatoruccaistanvaṅgīstanayoriva  || 5.11 ||

upamānopameyatve yatraikasyaiva jāgṛtaḥ  |
indurindurivetyādau bhavedevamananvayaḥ  || 5.12 ||

paryāyeṇa dvayostaccedupameyopamā matā  |
dharmo'rtha iva pūrṇaśrīrartho dharma iva tvayi  || 5.13 ||

vikhyātasyopamānasya yatra syādupameyatā  |
indurmukhamivetyādau syātpratīpopamā tadā  || 5.14 ||

upamāne tu līlādipadāḍhye lalitopamā  |
tvannetrayugalaṃ dhatte līlāṃ nīlāmbujanmanoḥ  || 5.15 ||

anekasyārthayugmasya sādṛśyaṃ stabakopamā  |
śrito'smi caraṇau viṣṇorbhṛṅgastāmarasaṃ yathā  || 5.16 ||

syātsaṃpūrṇopamā yatra dvayorapi vidheyatā  |
padmānīva vinidrāṇi netrāṇyāsannaharmukhe  || 5.17 ||
yatropamānacitreṇa sarvathāpyuparajyate  |
upameyamayī bhittistatra rūpakamiṣyate  || 5.18 ||

samānadharmayuksādhyāropātsopādhirūpakam  |
utsiktakṣitibhṛllakṣyapakṣacchedapurandaraḥ  || 5.19 ||

pṛthakkathitasādṛśyaṃ dṛśyaṃ sādṛśyarūpakam  |
ullasatpañcaśākhaste rājate bhujabhūruhaḥ  || 5.20 ||

syādaṅgayaṣṭirityevaṃvidhamābhāsarūpakam  |
aṅgayaṣṭidhanurvallītyādi rūpitarūpakam  || 5.21 ||

pariṇāmo'nayoryasminnabhedaḥ paryavasyati  |
kāntena pṛṣṭā rahasi maunamevottaraṃ dadau  || 5.22 ||

bahubhirbahudhollekhādekasyollekhitā matā  |
strībhiḥ kāmaḥ priyaiścandraḥ kālaḥ śatrubhiraikṣi saḥ  || 5.23 ||

atathyamāropayituṃ tathyāpāstirapahnutiḥ  |
nāyaṃ sudhāṃśuḥ kiṃ tarhi vyomagaṅgāsaroruham  || 5.24 ||

paryastāpahnutiryatra dharmamātraṃ niṣidhyate  |
nāyaṃ sudhāṃśuḥ kiṃ tarhi sudhāṃśuḥ preyasīmukham  || 5.25 ||

bhrāntāpahnutiranyasya śaṅkayā tathyanirṇaye  |
tāpaṃ tanoti sotkampaṃ jvaraḥ kiṃ na sakhi smaraḥ  || 5.26 ||

chekāpahnutiranyasya śaṅkayā tathyanihnave  |
prajalpanmatpade lagnaḥ kāntaḥ kiṃ na hi nūpuraḥ  || 5.27 ||

kaitavāpahnutirvyakte vyājādyairnihnave padaiḥ  |
niryānti smaranārācāḥ kāntādṛkpātakaitavāt || 5.28 ||

utprekṣonnīyate yatra hetvādirnihnutiṃ vinā  |
tvanmukhaśrīkṛte nūnaṃ padmairvairāyate śaśī  || 5.29 ||

ivādikapadābhāve gūḍhotprekṣāṃ pracakṣate  |
yatkīrtirvibhramaśrāntā viveśa svarganimnagām  || 5.30 ||
syātsmṛtibhrāntisaṃdehaistadevālaṃkṛtitrayam  |
paṅkajaṃ paśyatastasyā mukhaṃ me gāhate manaḥ  || 5.31 ||

ayaṃ pramattamadhupastvanmukhaṃ veda paṅkajam  |
paṅkajaṃ vā sudhāṃśurvetyasmākaṃ tu na nirṇayaḥ  || 5.32 ||

mīlitaṃ bahusādṛśyādbhedavaccenna lakṣyate  |
raso nālakṣi lākṣāyāścaraṇe sahajāruṇe  || 5.33 ||

dvau vivakṣitavācyasya lakṣyālakṣyakramātmakau  |
catvāriṃśadyutaikena bhedāḥ ṣaṭcānayoḥ kramāt || 7.4 ||

tridhā śabdārthatadyugmaśaktijanmā sphuṭakramāt |
rasabhāvatadābhāsapramukhastvasphuṭakramāt || 7.5 ||

vastvalaṅkārayorvyakterbhedau dvau śabdaśaktijau  |
arthaśaktisamutthasya bhedā dvādaśa tadyathā  || 7.6 ||

catvāro vastvalaṃkāramalaṃkārastu vastu yat |
alaṃkāramalaṃkāro vastu vastu vyanakti tat || 7.7 ||

vaktuḥ kavinibaddhasya kavervā prauḍhinirmitaḥ  |
svasiddho vā vyañjako'rthaścatvārastriguṇāstataḥ  || 7.8 ||

śabdārthobhayabhūrekaḥ sa ca vākyaikasaṃśrayaḥ  |
padaikadeśe racanāvarṇavākyapadeṣvapi  || 7.9 ||

prabandhe ceti ṣoḍhāsau rasādyākhyo'sphuṭakramaḥ  |
eṣu saptadaśasvekaṃ parityajyāsphuṭakramam  || 7.10 ||

ye ṣoḍaśādyā dviguṇāste syurvākyapadāśrayāt |
prabandhe'pi dvādaśa syurarthaśaktibhuvo bhidaḥ  || 7.11 ||

dvātriṃśaddvādaśaikaḥ ṣaṭsarvasaṃkalitadhvaneḥ  |
bhedāḥ syurekapañcāśatsaṃbhinnāstu sahasraśaḥ  || 7.12 ||

vaktṛsyūtaṃ bodhayituṃ vyaṅgyaṃ vakturabhīpsitam  |
svāṅkuritamatadrūpaṃ svayamullasitaṃ giraḥ  || 7.13 ||

kaścitsādhāraṇaḥ kaścidāmantrya pratibodhitaḥ  |
kaścittaṭasthaḥ kaścicca bodhitapratibodhitaḥ  || 7.14 ||

ityevaṃ boddhṛvaicitryādvaktṛsyūtaṃ caturvidham  |
upekṣānihnavābhyāṃ ca dvidhā svāṅkuritaṃ matam  || 7.15 ||

bhūtādikālabhedena nihnavaḥ syādanekadhā  |
anenāpi prabhedena vyaktivallī vijṛmbhate  || 7.16 ||

nānāprabhedā niyatā kvacitprakaraṇādinā  |
arthe'rthamanyaṃ yaṃ vakti tadvācyavyaṅgyamiṣyate  || 7.17 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
mayūkhastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke sukhayatu mahati munisaṃkhyaḥ  || 7.18 ||


yadvyajyamānaṃ manasaḥ staimityāya sa no dhvaniḥ  |
anyathā tu guṇībhūtavyaṅgyamāpatitaṃ tridhā  || 8.1 ||

vyaktireva kvacidvyaṅgyaḥ kvacidarthasvabhāvataḥ  |
kvaciccārutarasyāgre sa vimuñcati cārutām  || 8.2 ||

agūḍhaṃ kalayedarthāntarasaṃkramitādikam  |
vismṛtaḥ kimapāṃnātha sa tvayā kumbhasaṃbhavaḥ  || 8.3 ||

aparasya rasādeścedaṅgamanyadrasādikam  |
hā hā! matkucakāśmīraliptaṃ bhinnamuraḥ śaraiḥ  || 8.4 ||

tathā vācyasya siddhyaṅgaṃ naurartho vāridheryathā  |
saṃśritya taraṇiṃ dhīrāstaranti vyādhivāridhīn  || 8.5 ||

asphuṭaṃ stanayoratra kokasādṛśyavanmatam  |
kuṅkumāktaṃ stanadvandvaṃ mānasaṃ mama gāhate  || 8.6 ||

saṃdigdhaṃ yadi saṃdeho dairghyādyutpalayoriva  |
saṃprāpte nayane tasyāḥ śravaṇottaṃsabhūmikām  || 8.7 ||

tulyaprādhānyamindutvamiva vācyena sāmyabhṛt |
kānte tvadānanarucā glānimeti saroruham  || 8.8 ||

asundaraṃ yadi vyaṅgyaṃ syādvācyādamanoharam  |
sarasyāmīladambhoje cakraḥ kāntāṃ vilokate  || 8.9 ||

kākusthaṃ praṇato'mbhodhiradya mādyatu rāvaṇaḥ  |
ityaṣṭadhā guṇībhūtavyaṅgyamaṅgīkṛtaṃ budhaiḥ  || 8.10 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
mayūkhastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke mahati vasusaṃkhyaḥ sukhayatu  || 8.11 ||


mukhyārthasyāvivakṣāyāṃ pūrvārvācī ca rūḍhitaḥ  |
prayojanācca saṃbaddhaṃ vadantī lakṣaṇā matā  || 9.1 ||

lakṣaṇīyasvaśabdasya mīlanāmīlanāddvidhā  |
lakṣaṇā sā tridhā siddhasādhyasādhyāṅgabhedataḥ  || 9.2 ||

sphuṭāsphuṭaprabhedena prayojanamapi dvidhā  |
viduḥ sphuṭaṃ taṭasthatvādarthagatvāddvidhā budhāḥ  || 9.3 ||

asphuṭaṃ cārthaniṣṭhatvāttaṭasthatvādapi dvidhā  |
lakṣyalakṣakaniṣṭhatvādarthasaṃsthamapi dvidhā  || 9.4 ||

lakṣakasthaṃ sphuṭaṃ yatra sā vicakṣaṇalakṣaṇā  |
asphuṭatvaṃ taṭasthatvaṃ lakṣyasthatvamamuṣya ca  || 9.5 ||

anyāstisra iti vyaktā śaktitaḥ sā caturvidhā  |
indurevaiṣa tadvaktramutkarṣo lakṣyate mukhe  || 9.6 ||

pradīpaṃ vardhayetyatra taṭasthaṃ maṅgalodayaḥ  |
paṭo'yaṃ dagdha ityādau sphuṭaṃ nāsti prayojanam  || 9.7 ||

amṛtaṃ sūktamityādau lakṣyasthamatihṛdyatā  |
ābhimukhyātsaṃnidhānāttathākārapratītitaḥ  || 9.8 ||

kāryakāraṇabhāvātsā vācyavācakabhāvataḥ  |
ityevamādeḥ saṃbandhātkiṃcānyasmāccatuṣṭayāt || 9.9 ||

sādṛśyātsamavāyātsā vaiparītyātkriyānvayāt |
sāropādhyavasānākhye gauṇaśuddhe pṛthakpṛthak || 9.10 ||

gauṇaṃ sāropamuddiṣṭamindurmukhamitīdṛśam  |
gauṇaṃ sādhyavasānaṃ syādindurevedamīdṛśam  || 9.11 ||

śuddhaṃ sāropamuddiṣṭamāyurghṛtamitīdṛśam  |
śuddhaṃ sādhyavasānaṃ syādāyurevedamīdṛśam  || 9.12 ||
upādānārpaṇadvāre dve cānye iti ṣaḍvidhā  |
kuntā viśanti gaṅgāyāṃ ghoṣo nivasatīti ca  || 9.13 ||

lakṣyalakṣakavaiśiṣṭyavaiśiṣṭyāddvividhā punaḥ  |
sarasaṃ kāvyamamṛtaṃ vidyā sthirataraṃ dhanam  || 9.14 ||

tathā saheturatathābhedabhinnā ca kutracit |
saundaryeṇaiṣa kandarpaḥ sā ca mūrtimatī ratiḥ  || 9.15 ||

śabde padārthe vākyārthe saṃkhyāyāṃ kārake tathā  |
liṅge ceyamalaṅkārāṅkurabījatayā sthitā  || 9.16 ||

mahādevaḥ satrapramukhamakhavidyaikacaturaḥ sumitrā tadbhaktipraṇihitamatiryasya pitarau  |
mayūkhastenāsau sukavijayadevena racite ciraṃ candrāloke mahati navasaṃkhyaḥ sukhayatu  || 9.17 ||


dharmaṃ kaṃcitpuraskṛtya prāyaḥ śabdaḥ pravartate  |
yayārthaṃ spaṣṭamācaṣṭe śabdastāmabhidhāṃ viduḥ  || 10.1 ||

jātyā guṇena kriyayā vastuyogena saṃjñayā  |
nirdeśena tathā prāhuḥ ṣaḍvidhāmabhidhāṃ budhāḥ  || 10.2 ||

gaurnīlaḥ pācako daṇḍī ḍitthaḥ kaṃsa iti kramāt |
kaṃ saṃhinasti kaṃsārirnaraṃ ca kaṃ samāśritam  || 10.3 ||

na yogāderāyatanaṃ na saṅketaniketanam  |
vṛttyā nirdeśaśabdo'yaṃ mukhyayā svābhidheyayā  || 10.4 ||

pīyūṣavarṣaprabhavaṃ candrālokamanoharam  |
sudhānidhānamāsādya śrayadhvaṃ vibudhā mudam  || 10.5 ||

jayanti yājñikaśrīmanmahādevāṅgajanmanaḥ  |
sūktipīyūṣavarṣasya jayadevakavergiraḥ  || 10.6 ||
