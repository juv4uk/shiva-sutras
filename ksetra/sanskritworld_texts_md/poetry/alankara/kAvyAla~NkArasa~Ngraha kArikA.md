punaruktavadābhāsaṃ chekānuprāsa eva ca |
anuprāsastridhā lāṭānuprāso rūpakaṃ catuḥ || 1.1 ||
upamā dīpakaṃ caiva prativastūpamā tathā |
ityeta evālaṅkārā vācāṃ kaiścidudāhṛtāḥ || 1.2 ||
punaruktābhāsamabhinnavastvivodbhāsi bhinnarūpapadam |
chekānuprāsastu dvayordvayoḥ susadṛśoktikṛtau || 1.3 ||
śaṣābhyāṃ rephasaṃyogaiṣṭavargeṇa ca yojitā |
paraṣā nāma vṛttiḥ syāthlahvahyādyaiśca saṃyutā || 1.4 ||
sarūpasaṃyogayutāṃ mūrdhnivargāntyayogibhiḥ |
sparśairyutāṃ ca manyante upanāgarikāṃ budhāḥ || 1.5 ||
śeṣairvaṇairyathāyogaṃ kathitāṃ komalākhyayā |
grāmyaṃ vṛrtti praśaṃsanti kāvyeṣvādṛtabuddhayaḥ || 1.6 ||
sarūpavyañjananyāsaṃ tisṛṣvetāsu vṛttiṣu |
pṛthakpṛthaganuprāsamuśanti kavayaḥ sadā || 1.7 ||
svarūpārthāviśeṣe'pi punaruktiḥ phalāntarāt |
śabdānāṃ vā padānāṃ vā lāṭānuprāsa iṣyate || 1.8 ||
sa padadvitayasthityā dvayorekasya pūrvavat |
tadanyasya svatantratvāddvayorvaikapadāśrayāt || 1.9 ||
svatantrapadarūpeṇa dvayorvāpi prayogataḥ |
bhidyate'nekadhā bhedaiḥ pādābhyāsakrameṇa ca || 1.10 ||
śrutyā saṃbandhavirahādyatpadena padāntaram |
guṇavṛtti pradhānena yujyate rūpakaṃ tu tat || 1.11 ||
bandhastasya yataḥ śrutyā śrutyarthābhyāṃ ca tena tat |
samastavastuviṣayamekadeśavivarti ca || 1.12 ||
samastavastuviṣayaṃ mālārūpakamucyate |
yadvaikadeśavṛtti syātpararūpeṇa rūpaṇāt || 1.13 ||
ādimadhyāntaviṣayāḥ prādhānyetarayoginaḥ |
antargatopamādharmā yatra taddīpakaṃ viduḥ || 1.14 ||
yaccetohārisādharmyamupamānopameyayoḥ |
mitho vibhinnakālādiśabdayorupamā tu tat || 1.15 ||
yathevaśabdayogena sā śrutyānvayamarhati |
sadṛśādipadāśleṣādanyathetyuditā dvidhā || 1.16 ||
saṃkṣepābhihitāpyeṣā sāmyavācakavicuyataiḥ |
sāmyopameyatadvāciviyogācca nibadhyate || 1.17 ||
upamānopameyoktau sāmyatadvācivicyavāt |
kvacitsamāse tadvāciviraheṇa kvacicca sā || 1.18 ||
tathopamānādācārakyacpratyayabaloktitaḥ |
kvacitsā karturācāre kyaṅā sā ca kvipā kvacit || 1.19 ||
upamāne karmaṇi vā kartari vā yo ṇamulkaṣādagataḥ |
tadvācyā sā vatinā ca karma sāmānyavacanena || 1.20 ||
ṣaṣṭhīsaptamyantācca yo vatirnāmatastadabhidhaye |
kalpatprabhṛtibhiranyaiśca taddhitaiḥ sā nibadhyate kavibhiḥ || 1.21 ||
upamānasaṃnidhāne sāmyavācyucyate budhairyatra |
upameyasya ca kavibhiḥ sā prativastūpamā gaditā || 1.22 ||
prākaraṇiketaratvasthityaikaścopameyatāṃ labhate |
upamānatvaṃ cāpara ityupamāvāciśūnyatvam || 1.23 ||


ākṣepor'thāntaranyāso vyatireko vibhāvanā |
samāsātiśayoktī cetyalaṅkārānpare viduḥ || 2.1 ||
pratiṣedha iveṣṭasya yo viśeṣābhidhitsayā |
ākṣepa iti taṃ santaḥ śaṃsanti kavayaḥ sadā || 2.2 ||
vakṣyamāṇoktaviṣayaḥ sa ca dvividha iṣyate |
niṣedheneva tadbandho vidheyasya ca kīrtitaḥ || 2.3 ||
samarthakasya pūrvaṃ yadvaco'nyasya ca pṛṣṭhataḥ |
viparyayeṇa vā yatsyāddhiśabdoktyānyathāpi vā || 2.4 ||
jñeyaḥ sor'thāntaranayāsaḥ prakṛtārthasamarthanāt |
aprastutaparaśaṃsāyā dṛṣṭāntācca pṛthaksthitaḥ || 2.5 ||
viśeṣopādānaṃ yatsyādupamānopameyayoḥ |
nimittādṛṣṭidṛṣṭibhyāṃ vyatireko dvidhī tu saḥ || 2.6 ||
yo vaidharmyeṇa dṛṣṭānto yathevādisamanvitaḥ |
vyatireko'tra so'pīṣṭo viśeṣāpādanānvayāt || 2.7 ||
śliṣṭoktiyogyaśabdasya pṛthakpṛthagudāhṛtau |
viśeṣāpādanaṃ yatsyādyvatarekaḥ sa ca smṛtaḥ || 2.8 ||
kriyāyāḥ pratiṣedhe yā tatphalasya vibhāvanā |
jñeyā vibhāvanaivāsau samādhau sulabhe sati || 2.9 ||
prakṛtārthena vākyena tatsamānairviśeṣaṇaiḥ |
aprastutārthakathanaṃ samāsoktirudāhṛtā || 2.10 ||
nimittato yattu vayo lokātikrāntagocaram |
manyante'taśayoktiṃ tāmalaṃkāratayā budhāḥ || 2.11 ||
bhede'nanyatvamanyatra nānātvaṃ yatra badhyate |
tathā saṃbhāvyamānārthanibandhe'tiśayoktigīḥ || 2.12 ||
kāryakāraṇayoryatra paurvāparyaviparyayāt |
āśubhāvaṃ samālambya badhyate so'pi pūrvavat || 2.13 ||


yathāsaṃkhyamathotprekṣāṃ svabhāvoktiṃ tathaiva ca |
apare trīnalaṅkārān girāmāhuralaṅkṛtau || 3.1 ||
bhūyasāmupadiṣṭānāmarthānāmasadharmaṇām |
kramaśo yo'nunirdeśo yathāsaṃkhyaṃ taducyate || 3.2 ||
sāmyarūpāvivakṣāyāṃ vācyevādyātmabhiḥ padaiḥ |
atadguṇakriyāyogādutprekṣātiśayānvitā || 3.3 ||
lokātikrāntaviṣayā bhāvābhāvābhimānataḥ |
saṃbhāvaneyamutprekṣā vācyevādibhirucyate || 3.4 ||
kriyāyāṃ saṃpravṛttasya hevākānāṃ nibandhanam |
kasyacinmṛgaḍimbhādeḥ svabhāvoktirudāhṛtā || 3.5 ||


preyorasavadūrjasava paryāyoktaṃ samāhitam |
dvidhodāttaṃ tathā śliṣṭamalaṅkārānpare viduḥ || 4.1 ||
ratyādikānāṃ bhāvānāmanubhāvādisūcanaiḥ |
yatkāvyaṃ badhyate sadbhistatpreyasvadudāhṛtam || 4.2 ||
rasavaddarśitaspaṣṭaśṛṅgārādirasādayam |
svaśabdasthāyisaṃcārivibhāvābhinayāspadam || 4.3 ||
śṛṅgārahāsyakaraṇaraudravīrabhayānakāḥ |
bībhatsādbhutaśāntāśca nava nāṭye rasāḥ smṛtāḥ || 4.4 ||
anaucityapravṛttānāṃ kāmakrodhādikāraṇāt |
bhāvānāṃ ca rasānāṃ ca bandha ūrjasvi kathyate || 4.5 ||
paryāyoktaṃ yadanyena prakāreṇābhidhīyate |
vācyavācakavṛttibhyāṃ śūnyenāvagamātmanā || 4.6 ||
rasabhāvatadābhāsavṛtteḥ praśamabandhanam |
anyānubhāvaniḥśūnyarūpaṃ yattatsamāhitam || 4.7 ||
udāttamṛddhimadvastu caritaṃ ca mahātmanām |
upalakṣaṇatāṃ prāptaṃ netivṛttatvamāgatam || 4.8 ||
ekaprayatnoccāryāṇāṃ tacchāyāṃ caiva bibhratām |
svaritādiguṇairbhinnairbandhaḥ śliṣṭamihocyate || 4.9 ||
alaṅkārāntaragatāṃ pratibhāṃ janayatpadaiḥ |
dvividhairarthaśabdoktiviśiṣṭaṃ tatpratīyatām || 4.10 ||


apahnutiṃ viśeṣoktiṃ virodhaṃ tulyayogitām |
aprastutapraśaṃsā ca vyājastutividarśane || 5.1 ||
upameyopamāṃ caiva sahoktiṃ saṃkaraṃ tathā |
parivṛttiṃ ca jagaduralaṅkārānpare girām || 5.2 ||
apahnutirabhīṣṭā ca kiñcidantargatopamā |
bhūtārthāpahnavenāsyā nibandhaḥ kriyate budhaiḥ || 5.3 ||
yatsāmagrye'pi śaktīnāṃ phalānutpattibandhanam |
viśeṣasyābhidhitsātastadvaśeṣoktirucyate || 5.4 ||
darśitena nimittena nimittādarśanena ca |
tasyā bandho dvidhā lakṣye dṛśyate lalitātmakaḥ || 5.5 ||
guṇasya vā kriyāyā vā viruddhānyakriyāvacaḥ |
yadviśeṣābhidhānāya virodhaṃ taṃ pracakṣate || 5.6 ||
upamānopameyoktiśūnyairaprastutairvacaḥ |
sāmyābhidhāyi prastāvabhāgbhirvā tulyayogitā || 5.7 ||
adhikārādapetasya vastuno'nyasya yā stutiḥ |
aprastutapraśaṃseyaṃ prastutārthanubandhinī || 5.8 ||
śabdaśaktisvabhāvena yatra nindeva gamyate |
vastutastu stutiśceṣṭā vyājastutirasau matā || 5.9 ||
abhavanvastusaṃbandho bhavanvā yatra kalpayet |
upamānopameyatvaṃ kathyate sā vidarśanā || 5.10 ||
anekālaṅkriyollekhe samaṃ tadvṛttyasaṃbhave |
ekasya ca grahe nyāyadoṣābhāve ca saṃkaraḥ || 5.11 ||
śabdārthavartyalaṅkārā vākya ekatra bhāsinaḥ |
saṃkaro vā ekavākyāṃśapraveśādvābhidhīyate || 5.12 ||
parasparopakāreṇa yatrālaṅkṛtayaḥ sthitāḥ |
svātantryeṇātmalābhaṃ no labhante so'pi saṃkaraḥ || 5.13 ||
anyonyameva yatra syādupamānopameyatā |
upameyopamāmāhustāṃ pakṣāntarahānigām || 5.14 ||
tulyakāle kriye yatra vastudvayasamāśrite |
padenaikena kathyete sā sahoktirmatā satām || 5.15 ||
samanyūnaviśeṣṭaistu kasyacitparivartanam |
arthānarthasvabhāvaṃ yatparivṛttirabhāṇi sā || 5.16 ||


ananvayaṃ sasaṃdehaṃ saṃsṛṣṭiṃ bhāvikaṃ tathā |
kāvyadṛṣṭāntahetū cetyalaṅkārānpare viduḥ || 6.1 ||
upamānena tattvaṃ ca bhedaṃ ca vadataḥ punaḥ |
sasaṃdehaṃ vacaḥ stutyai sasaṃdehaṃ vadurbudhāḥ || 6.2 ||
alaṅkārāntaracchāyāṃ yatkṛtvā dhīṣu bandhanam |
asaṃdehe'pi saṃdeharūpaṃ saṃdehanāma tat || 6.3 ||
yatra tanaiva tasya syādupamānopameyatā |
asādṛśyavivakṣātastamityāhurananvayam || 6.4 ||
alaṅkṛtīnāṃ bahvīnāṃ dvayorvāpi samāśrayaḥ |
ekatra nirapekṣāṇāṃ mithaḥ saṃsṛṣṭirucyate || 6.5 ||
pratyakṣā iva yatrārthā dṛśyante bhūtabhāvinaḥ |
atyadbhutāḥ syāttadvācāmanākulyena bhāvikam || 6.6 ||
śrutamekaṃ yadanyatra smṛteranubhavasya vā |
hetutāṃ pratipadye kāvyaliṅgaṃ taducyate || 6.7 ||
iṣṭasyārthasya vispaṣṭapratibimbanidarśanam |
yathevādipadaiḥ sūnyaṃ budhairdṛṣṭānta ucyate || 6.8 ||
