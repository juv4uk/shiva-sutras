daśarūpakam  |

[pariccheda 1]

namastasmai gaṇeśāya yatkaṇṭhaḥ puṣkarāyate  |
madābhegaghanadhvāno nīlakaṇṭhasya tāṇḍave  || 1.1 ||

daśarūpānukāreṇa mādyanti bhāvakaḥ  |
namaḥ sarvavide tasmai viṣṇave bharatāya ca  || 1.2 ||

kasyacideva kadāciddayayā viṣayaṃ sarasvatī viduṣaḥ  |
ghaṭayati kamapi tamanyo jajati jano yena vaidagdhīm  || 1.3 ||

uddhṛtyoddhṛtya sāraṃ yamakhilanigamānnāṭyavedaṃ viriñciś
cakre yasya prayogaṃ munirapi bharatastāṇḍavaṃ nīlakaṇṭhaḥ  |
śarvāṇī lāsyamasya pratipadamaparaṃ lakṣma kaḥ kartumīṣṭe
nāṭyānāṃ kintu kiñcitpraguṇaracanayā lakṣaṇaṃ saṅkṣipāmi  || 1.4 ||

vyākīrṇe mandabuddhīnāṃ jāyate mativibhramaḥ  |
tasyārthastatpadaistena saṅkṣipya kriyate'ñjasā  || 1.5 ||

ānandanisyandiṣu rūpakeṣu
vyutpattimātraṃ phalamalpabuddhiḥ  |
yo'pītihāsādivadāha sādhus
tasmai namaḥ svāduparāṅmukhāya  || 1.6 ||

avasthānukṛtirnāṭyaṃ rūpaṃ dṛśyatayocyate  |
rūpakaṃ tatsamāropāddaśadhaiva rasāśrayam  || 1.7 ||

nāṭakaṃ saprakaraṇaṃ bhāṇaḥ prahasanaṃ ḍimaḥ  |
vyāyogasamavakārau vīthyaṅkehāmṛgā iti  || 1.8 ||

anyada bhāvāśrayaṃ nṛtyaṃ nṛttaṃ tālalayāśrayam  |
ādyaṃ padārthābhinayo mārgo deśo tathā param  || 1.9 ||

madhuroddhatabhedena taddvayaṃ dvividhaṃ punaḥ  |
lāsyatāṇḍavarūpeṇa nāṭakādyupakārakam  || 1.10 ||

vastu netā rasasteṣāṃ bhedako vastu ca dvidhā  |
tatrādhikārikaṃ mukhyamaṅgaṃ prāsaṅgikaṃ viduḥ  || 1.11 ||

adhikāraḥ phalasvāmyamadhikāro ca tatprabhuḥ  |
tanniravartyamabhivyāpi vṛttaṃ syādādhikārikam  || 1.12 ||

prāsaṅgikaṃ parārthasya svārtho yasya prasaṅgataḥ  |
sānubandhaṃ patākākhyaṃ prakarī ca pradeśabhāk || 1.13 ||

prāsaṅgikaṃ parārthasya svārtho yasya prasaṅgataḥ  |
sānubandhaṃ tulyasaṃvidhānaviśeṣaṇam  || 1.14 ||

prakhyātotpādyamiśratvabhedāttredhāpi tattridhā  |
prakhyātamitihāsāderutpādyaṃ kavikalpitam  |
miśraṃ ca saṅkarāttābhyāṃ divyanartyādibhedataḥ  || 1.15 ||

kāryaṃ trivargastatśuddhamekānekānubandhi ca  |
svalpoddiṣṭastu taddheturbījaṃ vistāryanekadhā  |
avāntarārthavicchede binduracchedakāraṇam  || 1.16 ||

bījabindupatākākhyaprakarokāryalakṣaṇāḥ  |
arthaprakṛtayaḥ pañca tā etāḥ parikīrtitāḥ  || 1.17 ||

avasthāḥ pañca kāryasya prārabdhasya phalārthibhiḥ  |
ārambhayatnaprāptyāśāniyatāptiphalāgamāḥ  || 1.18 ||

autsukyamātramārambhaḥ phalalābhāya bhūyase  |
prayatnastu tadaprāptau vyāpāro'titvarānvitaḥ  || 1.19 ||

upāyāpāyaśaṅkābhyāṃ prāptyāśā prāptisambhavaḥ  |
apāyābhāvataḥ prāptirniyatāptiḥ suniścitā  |
samagraphalasampattiḥ phalayogo yathoditaḥ  || 1.20 ||

arthaprakṛtayaḥ pañca pañcāvasthāsamanvitāḥ  |
yathāsaṅkhyena jāyante mukhādyāḥ pañca sandhayaḥ  || 1.21 ||

antaraikārthasambandhaḥ sandhirekānvaye sati  |
mukhapratimukhe garbhaḥ sāvamarśopasaṃhṛtiḥ  || 1.22 ||

mukhaṃ bījasamutpattirnānārtharasasambhavā  |
aṅgāni dvādaśaitasya bījārambhasamanvayāt || 1.23 ||

upakṣepaḥ parikaraḥ parinyāso vilobhanam  |
yuktiḥ prāptiḥ samādhānaṃ vidhānaṃ paribhāvanā  |
udbhedabhedakaraṇānyanvarthānyatha lakṣaṇam  || 1.24 ||

bījanyāsasupakṣepaḥ tadbāhulyaṃ parikriyā  |
tannniṣpattiḥ parinyāso guṇākhyānādvilobhanam  || 1.25 ||

sampradhāraṇamarthānāṃ yuktiḥ prāptiḥ sukhāgamaḥ  |
bījāgamaḥ samādhānaṃ vidhānaṃ sukhaduḥkhakṛt || 1.26 ||

paribhāvodbhutāveśa udbhedo gūḍhabhedanam  |
karaṇaṃ prakṛtārambho bhedaḥ protsāhanā matā  || 1.27 ||

lakṣyālakṣya ivodbhedastasya pratimukhaṃ bhavet |
binduprayatnānugamādaṅgānyasya trayodaśa  || 1.28 ||

vilāsaḥ parisarpaśca vidhūtaṃ śamanarmaṇo  |
narmadyutiḥ pragayaṇaṃ nirodhaḥ paryupāsanam  || 1.29 ||

vajraṃ puṣpamupanyāpto varṇa saṃhāra ityapi  |
ratyarthehā vilāsaḥ syāddṛṣṭanaṣṭānusarpaṇam  |
parisarpā vidhūtaṃ syādaratistacchamaḥ śamaḥ  || 1.30 ||

parihāsavatto narma dhṛtistajjā dyutirmatā  |
uttarā vākpragayaṇaṃ hitarodho nirodhanam  || 1.31 ||

paryupāstiranunayaḥ puṣpaṃ vākyaṃ viśeṣavat |
upanyāsastu sopāyaṃ vajraṃ pratyakṣaniṣṭhuram  |
cāturvarṇopagamanaṃ varṇasaṃhāra iṣyate  || 1.32 ||

garbhastu dṛṣṭanaṣṭasya bījasyānveṣaṇaṃ muhuḥ  |
dvādaśāṅgaḥ patākā syānna vā syātprāptisambhavaḥ  || 1.33 ||

abhūtāharaṇaṃ mārgā rūpodāharaṇe kramaḥ  |
saṅgahaścānumānaṃ ca toṭakādhivale tathā  || 1.34 ||

udvegasambhramākṣepā lakṣaṇaṃ ca praṇīyate  |
abhūtāharaṇaṃ chadma mārgastattvārthakīrtanam  || 1.35 ||

rūpaṃ vitarkavadvākyaṃ sotkarṣaṃ syādudāhṛtiḥ  |
kramaḥ sañcintyamānāptirbhāvajñānamathāpare  || 1.36 ||

saṅgrahaḥ sāmadānoktirabhyūho liṅgato'numā  |
adhivalamabhisandhiḥ saṃrabdhaṃ toṭakaṃ vacaḥ  || 1.37 ||

udvego'richa(?)tā bhītiḥ śaṅkātrāsau ca sambhramaḥ  |
garbhabījasamudbhedādākṣepaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  || 1.38 ||

krodhenāvamṛśedyatra vyasanādyā vilobhanāt |
garbhanirbhinnabījārthaḥ so'vamarśo'ṅgasaṅgahaḥ  || 1.39 ||

tatrāpavādasampheṭau vidravadravaśaktayaḥ  |
dyutiḥ prasaṅgaśchalanaṃ vyavasāyo virodhanam  |
prayocanā vicalanamādānaṃ ca taryodaśa  || 1.40 ||

doṣaprakhyāpavādaḥ syātsampheṭo roṣabhāṣaṇam  |
vidravo vadhabandhādirdravo gurutiraskṛtiḥ  || 1.41 ||

virodhaśamanaṃ śaktistarjanodvejane dyutiḥ  |
gurukīrtanaṃ prasaṅgaśchalanaṃ cāvamānanam  || 1.42 ||

vyavasāyaḥ khaśaktyuktiḥ saṃrabdhānāṃ virodhanam  |
siddhāmantraṇato bhāvidarśikā syātprayocanā  |
vikatthanā vicalanamādānaṃ kāryasaṅgrahaḥ  || 1.43 ||

bījavanto mukhādyarthā viprakīrṇā yathāyatham  |
aikārthyamupanīyante yatra nirvahaṇaṃ hi tat || 1.44 ||

sandhirvibodho grathanaṃ nirṇayaḥ paribhāṣaṇam  |
prasādānandasamayāḥ kṛtibhāṣopagūhanāḥ  |
pūrvabhāvopasaṃhārau praśastiśca caturdaśa  || 1.45 ||

sandhirbījopagamanaṃ vibodhaḥ kāryamārgaṇam  |
grathanaṃ tadupakṣepo'nubhūtākhyā tu nirṇayaḥ  || 1.46 ||

paribhāṣā mitho jalpaḥ prasādaḥ paryupāsanam  |
ānando vāñchitāvāptiḥ samayo duḥkhanirgamaḥ  || 1.47 ||

kṛtirlabdhārthaśamanaṃ mānādyāptiśca bhāṣaṇam  |
kāryadṛṣṭyadbhutaprāpto pūrvabhāvopagūhane  |
varāptiḥ kāvyasaṃhāraḥ praśastiḥ śubhaśaṃsanam  || 1.48 ||

iṣṭasyārthasya racanā gopyaguptiḥ prakāśanam  |
rāgaḥ prayogasyāścaryaṃ vṛttāntasyānupakṣayaḥ  || 1.49 ||

dvedhā vibhāgaḥ kartavyaḥ sarvasyāpoha vastunaḥ  |
sūcyameva bhavetkiñciddṛśyaśravyamathāparam  || 1.50 ||

nīraso'nucitastatra saṃsūcyo vastuvistaraḥ  |
dṛśyastu madhurodāttarasabhāvanirantaraḥ  || 1.51 ||

arthopakṣepakaiḥ sūcyaṃ pañcabhiḥ pratipādayet |
viṣkambhacūlikāṅgāsyāṅkāvatārapraveśakaiḥ  || 1.52 ||

vṛttavartiṣyamāṇānāṃ kathāṃśānāṃ nidarśakaḥ  |
saṅkṣepārthastu viṣkambho madhyapātraprayojitaḥ  |
ekānekakṛtaḥ śuddhaḥ saṅkīrṇo nīcamadhyamaiḥ  || 1.53 ||

tadvadevānudāttoktyā nīcapātraprayojitaḥ  |
praveśo'ṅkadvayasyāntaḥ śeṣārthasyopasūcakaḥ  || 1.54 ||

antaryavanikāsaṃsthaiścūlikārthasya sūcanā  |
aṅkāntapātrairaṅkāsyaṃ chinnāṅkasyārthasūcanāt || 1.55 ||

aṅkāvatārastvaṅkānte pāto'ṅkasyāvibhāgataḥ  |
ebhiḥ saṃsūcayetsūcyaṃ dṛśyamaṅkaiḥ pradarśayet || 1.56 ||

nāṭyadharmamapekṣyaitatpunarvastu tridheṣyate  |
sarveṣāṃ niyatasyaiva śrāvyamaśrāvyameva ca  || 1.57 ||

sarvaśrāvyaṃ prakāśaṃ syādaśrāvyaṃ svagataṃ matam  |
dvidhānyannāṭyadharmākhyaṃ janāntamapavāritam  || 1.58 ||

tripatākākareṇānyānapavāryāntarā kathām  |
anyonyāmantraṇaṃ yatsyājjanānte tajjanāntikam  |
rahasyaṃ kathyate'nyasya parāvṛttyāpavāritam  || 1.59 ||

kiṃ bravīṣyevamityādi vinā pātraṃ bravīti yat |
śrutvevānaktamapyekastatsyādākāśamāpitam  || 1.60 ||

ityādyaśeṣamiha vastuvibhedajātaṃ
rāmāyaṇādi ca vibhāvya bṛhatkathāṃ ca  |
āsūtrayettadanu netṛrasānuguṇyāc
citrāṃ kathāmucitacāruvacaḥprapañcaiḥ  || 1.61 ||

[pariccheda 2]


netā vinīto madhurastyāgī dakṣaḥ priyagvadaḥ  |
raktalokaḥ śucirvāṅmī rūḍhavaṃśaḥ sthiro yuvā  || 2.1 ||

buddhyutsāhasmṛtiprajñākalāmānasamanvitaḥ  |
śūro dṛḍhaśca tejasvī śāstracakṣuśca dhārmikaḥ  |
bhedaiścaturdhā lalitaśāntodāttoddhatairayam  || 2.2 ||

niścinto dhīralalitaḥ kalāsaktaḥ sukhī mṛduḥ  |
sāmānyagu.ayuktastu dhīraśānto dvijādikaḥ  || 2.3 ||

mahāsattvo'tigambhīraḥ kṣamāvānavikatthanaḥ  |
sthiro nigūḍhāhaṅkāro dhīrodātto dṛḍhavrataḥ  || 2.4 ||

darpamātsaryabhūyiṣṭho māyāchadmaparāyaṇaḥ  |
dhīroddhatastvahaṅkāro calaścaṇḍo vikattthanaḥ  || 2.5 ||

sa dakṣiṇaḥ śaṭho dhṛṣṭaḥ pūrvāṃ pratyanyayā hṛtaḥ  |
dakṣiṇo'syāṃ sahṛdayaḥ gūḍhavipriyakṛtśaṭhaḥ  |
vyaktāṅgavaikṛto dhṛṣṭo'nukūlastvekanāyikaḥ  || 2.6 ||

patākānāyakastvanyaḥ pīṭhamardo vicakṣaṇaḥ  |
tasyaivānucaro bhaktaḥ kiñcidūnaśca tadguṇaiḥ  || 2.7 ||

ekavidyo viṭaścānyo hāsyakṛcca vidūṣakaḥ  |
lubdho dhīroddhatastabdhaḥ(?) pāpakṛdvyasanī ripuḥ  || 2.8 ||

śobhā vilāso mādhuryaṃ gāmbhīryaṃ dhairyatejasī  |
lalitaudāryamityaṣṭau sattvajāḥ pauruṣā guṇāḥ  || 2.9 ||

nīce dhṛṇādhike spardhā śobhāyāṃ śauryadakṣate  |
gatiḥ sadhairyā dṛṣṭiśca vilāse sammitaṃ vacaḥ  || 2.10 ||

ślakṣṇo vikāro mādhuryaṃ saṅkṣobhe sumahatyapi  |
gāmbhīryaṃ yatprabhāvena vikāro nopalakṣyate  || 2.11 ||

vyavasāyādacalanaṃ sthairyaṃ vighnakulādapi  |
adhikṣepādyasahanaṃ tejaḥ prāṇātyayeṣvapi  || 2.12 ||

śṛṅgārākāraceṣṭātvaṃ sahajaṃ lalitaṃ mṛdu  |
priyoktyājīvitāddānamaudāryaṃ sadupagrahaḥ  || 2.13 ||

svānyā sādhāraṇastrīti tadguṇā nāyikā tridhā  |
mugdhā madhyā pragalbheti svīyā śīlārjavādiyuk || 2.14 ||
mugdhā navavayaḥkāmā ratau vāmā mṛduḥ krudhi  |
madhyodyadyāvanānaṅgā mohāntasuratakṣamā  || 2.15 ||

dhīrā sotprāsavakroktyā madhyā sāśru kṛtāgamam  |
khedayeddayitaṃ kopādadhīrā paruṣākṣaram  || 2.16 ||

yauvanāndhā smaronmattā pragalbhā dayitāṅgake  |
vilīyamānevānandādratārambhe'pyacetanā  || 2.17 ||

sāvahitthādarodāste ratau dhīretarā krudhā  |
santarjya tāḍayenmadhyā madhyādhīreva taṃ vadet |
dvedhā jyeṣṭhā kaniṣṭā cetyamugdhā dvādaśoditāḥ  || 2.18 ||

anyastrī kanyakoḍhā ca nānyoḍhāṅgirase kvacit |
kanyānurāgamicchātaḥ kuryādaṅgāṅgisaṃśrayam  || 2.19 ||

sādhāraṇastrī gaṇikā kalāprāgalbhyadhaurtyayuk |
channakāmasukhārthājñasvatantrāhaṃyupaṇḍakān  || 2.20 ||

rakteva rañjayedāḍhyānniḥsvānmātrā vivāsayet |
raktaiva tvaprahasane naiṣā divyanṛpāśraye  || 2.21 ||

āsāmaṣṭāvavasthāḥ syuḥ svādhīnapatikādikāḥ  |
āsannāyattaramaṇā hṛṣṭā svādhīnabhartṛkā  || 2.22 ||

mudā vāsakasajjā svaṃ maṇḍayatyeṣyati priye  |
cirayatyavyalīke tu virahotkaṇṭhitonmanāḥ  || 2.23 ||

jñāte'nyāsaṅgavivṛte khaṇḍterṣyākaṣāyitā  |
kalahāntaritāmarṣādvidhūte'nuśayārtiyuk || 2.24 ||

vipralabdhoktasamayamaprāpte'tivimānitā  |
dūradeśābtarasthe tu kāryataḥ proṣitapriyā  |
kāmārtābhisaretkāntaṃ sārayedvābhisārikā  || 2.25 ||

cintāniḥśvāsakhedāśruvaivarṇyaglānyabhūṣaṇaiḥ  |
yuktāḥ ṣaḍantyā dve cādye kroḍaujjvalyapraharṣitaiḥ  || 2.26 ||

dūtyo dāsī sakhī kārūrdhātreyī prativeśikā  |
liṅginī śilpinī svaṃ ca netṛmitraguṇānvitāḥ  || 2.27 ||

yauvane sattvajāḥ strīṇāmalaṅkārāstu viṃśatiḥ  |
bhāvo hāvaśca helā ca trayastatra śarīrajāḥ  || 2.28 ||

śobhā kāntiśca dīptiśca mādhuryaṃ ca pragalbhatā  |
audāryaṃ dhairyamityete sapta bhāvā ayatnajāḥ  || 2.29 ||

līlā vilāso vicchittirvibhramaḥ kilakiñcitam  |
moṭṭāyitaṃ kuṭṭamitaṃ bibboko lalitaṃ tathā  |
vihṛtaṃ ceti vijñeyā daśa bhāvāḥ svabhāvajāḥ  || 2.30 ||

nirvikārātmakātsattvādbhāvastatrādyavikriyā  |
hevākasastu śṛṅgāro hāvo'kṣibhrūvikārakṛt || 2.31 ||

sa eva helā suvyaktaśṛṅgārarasasūcikā  |
rūpopabhogatāruṇyaiḥ śobhāṅgānāṃ vibhūṣaṇam  || 2.32 ||

manmathāvāpitacchāyā saiva kāntiriti smṛtā  |
anulabaṇatvaṃ mādhuryaṃ dīptiḥ kāntestu vistaraḥ  || 2.33 ||

niḥsādhvasatvaṃ prāgalbhyaṃ audāryaṃ praśrayaḥ sadā  |
cāpalāvihatā dhairyaṃ cidvṛttiravikatthanā  || 2.34 ||

priyānukaraṇaṃ līlā madhurāṅgaviceṣṭitaiḥ  |
tātkāliko viśeṣastu vilāso'ṅgakriyādiṣu  || 2.35 ||

ākalparacanālpāpi vicchittiḥ kāntipopa(?)kṛt |
vibhramastvarayā kāle bhūṣāsthānaviparyayaḥ  || 2.36 ||

krodhāśruharṣabhītyādeḥ saṅkaraḥ kilakiñcitam  |
moṭṭāyitaṃ tu tadbhāvabhāvaneṣṭakathādiṣu  || 2.37 ||

sānandāntaḥ kuṭṭamitaṃ kupyetkeśādharagrahe  |
garvābhimānādiṣṭe'pi bibboko'nādarakriyā  || 2.38 ||

sukumārāṅgavinyāso masṛṇo lalitaṃ bhavet |
prāptakālaṃ na yadbrūyādvrīḍayā vihṛtaṃ hi tat || 2.39 ||

mantrī svaṃ vobhayaṃ vāpi sakhā tasyārthacintane  |
mantriṇā lalitaḥ śeṣāḥ mantrisvāyattasiddhayaḥ  || 2.40 ||

ṛtvikpurohitau dharme tapasvibrahmavādinaḥ  |
suhṛtkumārāṭavikā daṇḍe sāmantasainikāḥ  || 2.41 ||

antaḥpure varṣavarāḥ kirātā mūkavāmanāḥ  |
mlecchābhīraśakārādyāḥ svasvakāryopayoginaḥ  || 2.42 ||

jyeṣṭhamadhyādhamatvena sarveṣāṃ ca trirūpatā  |
tāratamyādyathoktānāṃ guṇānāṃ cottamāditā  |
evaṃ nāṭye vidhātavyo nāyakaḥ saparicchadaḥ  || 2.43 ||

tadvyāpārātmikā vṛttiścaturdhā tatra kaiśikī  |
gatinṛtyavilāsādyairmṛduḥ śṛṅgāraceṣṭitaiḥ  |
narmataisphiñjatatsphoṭatadgaibhaiścaturaṅgikā  || 2.44 ||

vaidagdhyakrīḍitaṃ narma priyopacchandanātmakam  |
hāsyenaiva saśṛṅgārabhayena vihitaṃ tridhā  || 2.45 ||

ātmopakṣepasambhogamānaiḥ śṛṅgāryapi tridhā  |
śuddhamaṅgaṃ bhayaṃ dvedhā vāgveṣaceṣṭitaiḥ  |
sarvaṃ sahāsyamityevaṃ narmāsṭādaśadhoditam  || 2.46 ||

narmasphiñjaḥ sukhārambho bhayānto navasaṅgame  |
narmasphoṭastu bhāvānāṃ sūcito'lparaso lavaiḥ  || 2.47 ||

channanetrapratīcāro narmagarbho'rthahetave  |

sācīkṛtāṅgāvaraṇavaivarṇyādhomukhādibhiḥ  || 4.22 ||

āveśo grahaduḥkhādyairapasmāro yathāvidhiḥ  |
bhūpātakampaprakhedalālāphenodgamādayaḥ  || 4.23 ||

moho vicittatā bhītiduḥkhāveśānucintanaiḥ  |
tatrājñānabhramāghātaghūrṇanādarśanādayaḥ  || 4.24 ||

bhrānticchedopadeśābhyāṃ śāstrādestattvadhīrmatiḥ  |
ālasyaṃ śramagarbhādejahyajṛmbhāsitādimat || 4.25 ||

āvegaḥ samgramo'sminnabhisarajanite śastranāgābhiyogo
vātātpāṃsūpadigdhastvaritapadagatirvarṣaje piṇḍitāṅgaḥ  |
utpātātsrastatāṅgeṣvahitahitakṛte śokaharṣānubhāvā
vahnerdhūmākulāsyaḥ karijamanubhayastammakampāpasārāḥ  || 4.26 ||

tarko vicāraḥ sandehādbhrūśiroṅgulinartakaḥ  |
lajjādyairvikriyāguptāvavahitthāṅgavikriyā  |
vyādhayaḥ sannipātādyāsteṣāmanyatra vistaraḥ  || 4.27 ||

aprekṣākāritonmādaḥ sannipātagrahādibhiḥ  |
assminnavasthā ruditagītahāsāsitādayaḥ  || 4.28 ||

prārabdhakāryāsiddhyāderviṣādaḥ sattvasaṅkṣayaḥ  |
niḥśvāsocchvāsahṛttāpasahāyānveṣaṇādikṛt || 4.29 ||

kālākṣamatvamautmukyaṃ ramyecchāratisamgramaiḥ  |
tatrocchvāsatvaniḥśvāsahṛttāpasvedavibhramāḥ  || 4.30 ||

mātsaryadveṣarāgādeścāpalaṃ tvanavasthitiḥ  |
tatra bhartsanapāruṣyasvacchandācaraṇādayaḥ  || 4.31 ||

viruddhairaviruddhairvā bhāvairvicchidyate  |
ātmabhāvaṃ nayatyanyān sa sthāyī lavaṇākaraḥ  || 4.32 ||

ratyutsāhajgupsāḥ krodho hāsaḥ smayo bhayaṃ śokaḥ  |
śamamapi kecitprāhuḥ puṣṭirnāṭyeṣu naitasya  || 4.33 ||

nirvedādiratādrūpyādasthāyī svadate katham  |
vairasyāyaiva tatpoṣastenāṣṭau sthāyino matāḥ  || 4.34 ||

vācyā prakaraṇādibhyo buddhisthā vā yathā kriyā  |
vākyārthaḥ kārakairyuktā sthāyī bhāvastathetaraiḥ  || 4.35 ||

rasaḥ sa eva svādvyatvādrasikasyaiva vartanāt |
nānukāryasya vṛttatvātkāvyasyātatparatvataḥ  || 4.36 ||

draṣṭuḥ pratītirvrīḍerṣyārāgadveṣaprasaṅgataḥ  |
laukikasya svaramaṇīsaṃyuktasyeva darśanāt || 4.37 ||

dhīrodāttādyavasthānāṃ rāmādiḥ pratipādakaḥ  |
vibhāvayati ratyādīn svadante rasikasya te  || 4.38 ||

krīḍatāṃ mṛnmayairyadvadbālānāṃ dviradādibhiḥ  |
svotsāhaḥ svadate tadvacchrotṛṇāmarjunādibhiḥ  || 4.39 ||

kāvyārthabhāvanāsvādo nartakasya na vāryate  |
svādaḥ kāvyārthasambhedānātmānandasamudbhavaḥ  || 4.40 ||

vikāśavistarakṣobhavikṣepaiḥ sa caturbidhaḥ  |
śṛṅgāravīrabībhatsaraudreṣu manasaḥ kramāt || 4.41 ||

hāsyādbhutabhayotkarṣakaruṇānāṃ ta eva hi  |
atastajjanyatā teṣāmata evāvadhāraṇam  || 4.42 ||

śamaprakarṣo nirvācyo muditādestadātmatā  |
padārthairindunirvedaromāñcādisvarūpakaiḥ  |
kāvyādvibhāvasañcāryanubhāvaprakhyatāṃ gataiḥ  || 4.43 ||

bhāvitaḥ svadate sthāyī rasaḥ sa parikīrtitaḥ  |
lakṣaṇaikyaṃ vibhāvaikyādabhedādrasabhāvayoḥ  || 4.44 ||

ramyadeśakalākālaveṣabhogādisevanaiḥ  |
pramodātmā ratiḥ saiva yūnoranyonyaraktayoḥ  |
prahṛṣyamāṇā śṛṅgāro madhurāṅgaviceṣṭitaiḥ  || 4.45 ||

ye sattvajāḥ sthāyineva cāṣṭau
triṃśattrayo ye vyabhicāriṇaśca  |
ekonapañcāśadamī hi bhāvāḥ
yuktyā nibaddhāḥ paripoṣayanti  |
ālasyamaugryaṃ maraṇaṃ jugupsā
tasyāśrayādvaitaviruddhamiṣṭam  || 4.46 ||

tatrāyogo'nurāge'pi navayorekacittayoḥ  |
pāratantryeṇa daivādvā viprakarṣādasaṅgamaḥ  || 4.47 ||

daśāvasthaḥ sa tatrādāvabhilāṣo'tha cintanam  |
smṛtirguṇakathodvegapralāponmādasañjvarāḥ  |
jaḍatā maraṇaṃ ceti duravasthaṃ yathottaram  || 4.48 ||

abhilāpaḥ spṛhā tatra kānte sarvāṅgasundare  |
dṛṣṭe śrute vā tatrāpi vismayānandasādhvasāḥ  || 4.49 ||

sākṣātpratikṛtisvapnacchāyāmāyāsu darśanam  |
śrutirvyājātsakhīgītamāgadhādiguṇastuteḥ  || 4.50 ||

sānubhāvavibhāvāstu cintādyāḥ pūrvadarśitāḥ  |
daśāvasthatvamācāryaiḥ prāyo vṛtyā nidarśitam  |
mahākaviprabandheṣu dṛśyate tadanantatā  || 4.51 ||

dṛṣṭe śrute'bhilāṣācca kiṃ nautsukyaṃ prajāyate  |
aprāptau kiṃ na nirvedo glāniḥ kiṃ nāticintanāt || 4.52 ||

viprayogastu viśleṣo rūḍhavibhrambhayordvidhā  |
mānapravāsabhedena māno'pi praṇayerṣyayoḥ  || 4.53 ||

tatra praṇayamānaḥ syātkopāvasitayordvayoḥ  |
strīṇāmīrṣyākṛto mānaḥ kopo'nyāsaṅgini priye  |
śrute vānumite dṛṣṭe śrutistatra sakhīmukhāt || 4.54 ||

utsvapnāyitabhogāṅkagotraskhalanakalpitaḥ  |
tridhānumāniko dṛṣṭaḥ sākṣādindriyagocaraḥ  || 4.55 ||

yathottaraṃ guruḥ ṣaḍbhirupāyaistamupācaret |
sāmnā bhedena dānena natyupekṣārasāntaraiḥ  || 4.56 ||

tatra oriyavacaḥ sāma bhedastatsakhyupārjanam  |
dānaṃ vyājena bhūṣādeḥ pādayoḥ patanaṃ natiḥ  || 4.57 ||

sāmādau tu parikṣīṇe syādupekṣāvadhīraṇam  |
rabhasatrāsaharṣādeḥ kopabhraṃśo rasāntaram  |
kopaceṣṭāśca nārīṇāṃ prāgeva pratipāditāḥ  || 4.58 ||

kāryataḥ sambhramācchāpātpravāso bhinnadeśatā  |
dvayostatrāśruniḥśvāsakārśyalambālakāditā  || 4.59 ||

dvitīyaḥ sahasotpanno divyamānupaviplavāt |
svarūpānyatvakaraṇācchāpajaḥ sannidhāvapi  || 4.60 ||

mṛte tvekatra yatrānyaḥ pralapecchoka eva saḥ  |
vyāśrayatvānna śṛṅgāraḥ pratyāpanne tu netaraḥ  || 4.61 ||

praṇayāyogayorutkā pravāse proṣitapriyā  |
kalahāntariterṣyāyāṃ vipralavdhā ca khaṇḍitā  || 4.62 ||

anukūlau niṣevete yatrānyonyaṃ vilāsinau  |
darśanasparśanādīni sa sambhogo mudānvitaḥ  || 4.63 ||

ceṣṭāstatra pravartante līlādyā daśa yoṣitām  |
dākṣiṇyamārdavapremṇāmanurūpāḥ priyaṃ prati  || 4.64 ||

ramayeccāṭukṛtkāntaḥ kalākrīḍādibhiśca tām  |
na grāmyamācaretkiñcinnarmabhraṃśakaraṃ na ca  || 4.65 ||

vīraḥ pratāpavinayādhyavasāyasattva-
mohāviṣādanayavismayavikramādyaiḥ  |
utsāhabhūḥ sa ca dayāraṇadānayogāt
tredhā kilātra matigarvadhṛtipraharṣāḥ  || 4.66 ||

bībhatsaḥ kṛmipūtigandhivamathuprāyairjugupsaikabhūr
udvego rudhirāntrakīkasavasāmāṃsādibhiḥ kṣobhaṇaḥ  |
vairāgyājjaghanastanādiṣu ghṛṇāśuddho'nubhāvairvṛto
nāsāvaktravikūṇanādibhirihāvegārtiśaṅkādayaḥ  || 4.67 ||

krodho matsaravairivaikṛtamayaiḥ poṣo'sya raudro'nujaḥ
kṣobhaḥ khādharadaṃśakampabhrukuṭisvedāsyarāgairyutaḥ  |
śatrollāsavikatthamāṃsadharaṇīghātapratijñāgrahair
atrāmarṣamadau smṛtiścapalatāsūyāgryavegādayaḥ  || 4.68 ||

vikṛtākṛtivāgveṣairātmano'tha parasya vā  |
hāsaḥ syātparipoṣo'sya hāsyastriprakṛtiḥ smṛtaḥ  || 4.69 ||

smitamiha vikāsinayanaṃ kiñcillakṣyadvijaṃ tu hasitaṃ syāt |
madhurasvaraṃ vihasitaṃ saśiraḥkampamidamupahasitam  || 4.70 ||

apahasitaṃ sāsrākṣaṃ vikṣiptāṅgaṃ bhavatyatihasitam  |
dve dve hasite caiṣāṃ jyeṣṭhe madhye'dhame kramaśaḥ  || 4.71 ||

nidrālasyaśramaglānimūrchāśca sahacāriṇaḥ  |
atilokaiḥ padārthaiḥ syādvismayātmā raso'dbhutaḥ  || 4.72 ||

karmāsya sādhuvādāśruvepathusvedagadgadāḥ  |
harṣāvegadhṛtiprāyā bhavanti vyabhicāriṇaḥ  || 4.73 ||

vikṛtasvarasattvāderbhayabhāvo bhayānakaḥ  |
sarvāṅgavepathusvedaśoṣavaicittyalakṣaṇaḥ  |
dainyasambhramasammohatrāsādistatsahodaraḥ  || 4.74 ||

iṣṭanāśādaniṣṭāptau śokātmā karuṇo'nu tam  |
niḥśvāsocchvāsaruditastambhapralapitādayaḥ  || 4.75 ||

svāpāpasmāradainyādhimaraṇālasyasambhramāḥ  |
viṣādajaḍayonmādacintādyā vyabhicāriṇaḥ  || 4.76 ||

prītibhaktyādayo bhāvā mṛgayākṣādayo rasāḥ  |
harṣotsāhādiṣu spaṣṭamantarbhāvānna kīrtitāḥ  || 4.77 ||

ṣaṭtriṃśadbhūṣaṇādīni sāmādīnyekaviṃśatiḥ  |
lakṣyasandhyantarāṅgāni sālaṅkāreṣu teṣu ca  || 4.78 ||

ramyaṃ jugupsitamudāramathāpi nīcam
ugraṃ prasādi gahanaṃ vikṛtaṃ ca vastu  |
yadvāpyavastu kavibhāvakabhāvyamānaṃ
tannāsti yanna rasabhāvamupaiti loke  || 4.79 ||

viṣṇoḥ sutenāpi dhanañjayena
vidvanmanorāganibandhahetuḥ  |
āviṣkṛṭaṃ muñjamahīśagoṣṭhī-
vaidagdhyabhājā daśarūpametat || 4.80 ||
