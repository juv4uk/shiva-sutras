svapnavāsavadattam
{nāndyante tataḥ praviśati sūtradhāraḥ (01)}
*{sūtradhāraḥ}*

udayanavendusavarṇāvāsavadattābalau balasya tvām  |
padmāvatīrṇapūrṇau vasantakamrau bhujau pātām  || 1.1 ||

evamāryamiśrān vijñāpayāmi  (1.11)
aye kinnu khalu mayi vijñāpanavyagre śabda iva śrūyate  (1.12)
aṅga paśyāmi  (1.13)
{nepathye} ussaraha ussaraha ayyā ussaraha  (1.14)
[utsaratotsaratāryāḥ utsarata |  ]

*{sūtradhāraḥ}*   bhavatu vijñātam  (1.15)

bhṛtyairmagadharājasya snigdhaiḥ kanyānugāmibhiḥ  |
dhṛṣṭhamutsāryate sarvastapovanagato janaḥ  || 1.2 ||


{niṣkrāntaḥ  (1.21)}
sthāpanā |
{praviśya}
*{bhaṭau}*   ussaraha ussaraha ayyā ussaraha  (1.22)
[utsaratotsaratāryāḥ utsarata |  ]

{tataḥ praviśati parivrājakaveṣo yaugandharāyaṇa āvantikāveṣadhāriṇī vāsavadattā ca  (1.23)}
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*
{karṇaṃ dattvā}   kathamihāpyutsāryate  (1.24)
kutaḥ

dhīrasyāśramasaṃśritasya vasatastuṣṭasya vanyaiḥ phalairmānārhasya janasya valkalavatastrāsaḥ samutpādyate  |
utsikto vinayādapetapuruṣo bhāgyaiścalairvismitaḥ ko'yaṃ bho nibhṛtaṃ tapovanamidaṃ grāmīkarotyājñayā  || 1.3 ||


*{vāsavadattā}*   ayya ko eso ussāredi  (1.31)
[ārya ka eṣa utsārayati |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   bhavati yo dharmādātmānamutsārayati  (1.32)
*{vāsavadattā}*   ayya ṇa hi evvaṃ vattukāmā ahaṃ vi ṇāma ussāraidavvā homi tti  (1.33)
[ārya na hyevaṃ vaktukāmā ahamapi nāmotsārayitavyā bhavāmīti |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   bhavati evamanirjñātāni daivatānyavadhūyante  (1.34)
*{vāsavadattā}*   ayya taha parissamo parikhedaṃ ṇa uppādedi jaha aaṃ paribhavo  (1.35)
[ārya tathā pariśramaḥ parikhedaṃ notpādayati yathāyaṃ paribhavaḥ |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   bhuktojjhita eṣa viṣayo'trabhavatyā  (1.36)
nātra cintā kāryā  (1.37)
kutaḥ

pūrvaṃ tvayāpyabhimataṃ gatamevamāsīcchlāghyaṃ gamiṣyasi punarvijayena bhartuḥ  |
kālakrameṇa jagataḥ parivartamānā cakrārapaṅktiriva gacchati bhāgyapaṅktiḥ  || 1.4 ||


*{bhaṭau}*   ussaraha ayyā ussaraha  (1.41)
[utsaratāryāḥ utsarata |  ]

{tataḥ praviśati kāñcukīyaḥ  (1.42)}
*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   sambhaṣaka na khalu na khalūtsāraṇā kāryā  (1.43)
paśya

pariharatu bhavānnṛpāpavādaṃ na paruṣamāśramavāsiṣu prayojyam  |
nagaraparibhavān vimoktumete vanamabhigamya manasvino vasanti  || 1.5 ||


*{ubhau}*   ayya taha  (1.51)
[ārya tathā |  ]

{niṣkrāntau  (1.52)}
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   hanta savijñānamasya darśanam  (1.53)
vatse upasarpāvastāvadenam  (1.54)
*{vāsavadattā}*   ayya taha  (1.55)
[ārya tathā |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*
{upasṛtya}   bhoḥ kiṅkṛteyamutsāraṇā  (1.56)
*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   bhostapasvin  (1.57)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*
{ātmagatam}   tapasvinniti guṇavān khalvayamālāpaḥ  (1.58)
aparicayāttu na śliṣyate me manasi  (1.59)
*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   bhoḥ śrūyatām  (1.510)
eṣā khalu gurubhirabhihitanāmadheyasyāsmākaṃ mahārājadarśakasya bhaginī padmāvatī nāma  (1.511)
saiṣā no mahārājamātaraṃ mahādevīmāśramasthāmabhigamyānujñātā tatrabhavatyā rājagṛhameva yāsyati  (1.512)
tadadyāsminnāśramapade vāso'bhipreto'syāḥ  (1.513)

tadbhavantaḥ

tīrthodakāni samidhaḥ kusumāni darbhān svairaṃ vanādupanayantu tapodhanāni  |
dharmapriyā nṛpasutā na hi dharmapīḍāmicchettapasviṣu kulavratametadasyāḥ  || 1.6 ||


*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*
{svagatam}   evam  (1.61)
eṣā sā magadharājaputrī padmāvatī nāma yā puṣpakabhadrādibhirādeśikairādiṣṭā svāmino devī bhaviṣyatīti  (1.62)
tataḥ

pradveṣo bahumāno vā saṅkalpādupajāyate  |
bhartṛdārābhilāṣitvādasyāṃ me mahatī svatā  || 1.7 ||


*{vāsavadattā}*
{svagatam}   rāadāria tti suṇia bhaiṇiāsiṇeho vi me ettha sampajjai  (1.71)
[rājadāriketi śrutvā bhaginikāsneho'pi me'tra saṃpadyate |  ]

{tataḥ praviśati padmāvatī saparivārā ceṭī ca  (1.72)}
*{ceṭī}*   edu edu bhaṭṭidāriā  (1.73)
idamassamapadaṃ pavisadu  (1.74)
[etvetu bhartṛdārikā idamāśramapadaṃ praviśatu |  ]

{tataḥ praviśatyupaviṣṭā tāpasī  (1.75)}
*{tāpasī}*   sāadaṃ rāadāriāe  (1.76)
[svāgataṃ rājadārikāyāḥ |  ]

*{vāsavadattā}*
{svagatam}   iaṃ sā rāadāriā  (1.77)
abhijaṇāṇurūvaṃ khu se rūvaṃ  (1.78)
[iyaṃ sā rājadārikā |  abhijanānurūpaṃ khalvasyā rūpam |  ]

*{padmāvatī}*   ayye vandāmi  (1.79)
[ārye vande |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   ciraṃ jīva  (1.710)
pavisa jāde pavisa  (1.711)
tavovaṇāṇi ṇāma adihijaṇassa saagehaṃ  (1.712)
[ciraṃ jīva |  praviśa jāte praviśa |  tapovanāni nāmātithijanasya svageham |  ]

*{padmāvatī}*   bhodu bhodu  (1.713)
ayye vissatthahmi  (1.714)
imiṇā bahumāṇavaaṇeṇa aṇuggahida hmi  (1.715)
[bhavatu bhavatu |  ārye viśvastāsmi |  anena bahumānavacanenānugṛhītāsmi |  ]

*{vāsavadattā}*
{svagatam}   ṇa hi rūvamevva vāā vi khu se mahurā  (1.716)
[na hi rūpameva vāgapi khalvasyā madhurā |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   bhadde imaṃ dāva bhaddamuhassa bhaiṇiaṃ kocci rāā ṇa varedi  (1.717)
[bhadre imāṃ tāvadbhadramukhasya bhaginikāṃ kaścidrājā na varayati |  ]

*{ceṭī}*   atthi rāā pajjodo ṇāma ujjaiṇīe  (1.718)
so dāraassa kāraṇādo dūdasampādaṃ karedi  (1.719)
[asti rājā pradyoto nāmojjayinyāḥ |  sa dārakasya kāraṇāddūtasampātaṃ karoti |  ]

*{vāsavadattā}*
{ātmagatam}   bhodu bhodu  (1.720)
esā a attaṇīā dāṇiṃ saṃvuttā  (1.721)
[bhavatu bhavatu |  eṣā cātmīyedānīṃ saṃvṛttā |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   arhā khu iamāidī imassa bahumāṇassa  (1.722)
ubhaāṇi rāauḻāṇi mahattarāṇi tti suṇīadi  (1.723)
[arhā khalviyamākṛtirasya bahumānasya |  ubhe rājakule mahattare iti śrūyate |  ]

*{padmāvatī}*   ayya kiṃ diṭṭho muṇijaṇo attāṇamaṇuggahīduṃ  (1.724)
abhippedappadāṇeṇa tavassijaṇo uvaṇimantīadu dāva ko kimettha icchadi tti  (1.725)
[ārya kiṃ dṛṣṭo munijana ātmānamanugrahītum |  abhipretapradānena tapasvijana upanimantryatāṃ tāvatkaḥ kimatrecchatīti |  ]

*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   yadabhipretaṃ bhavatyā  (1.726)
bho bho āśramavāsinastapasvinaḥ  (1.727)
śṛṇvantu śṛṇvantu bhavantaḥ  (1.728)
ihātrabhavatī magadharājaputrī anena visrambheṇotpāditavisrambhā dharmārthamarthenopanimantrayate  (1.729)

kasyārthaḥ kalaśena ko mṛgayate vāso yathāniścitaṃ dīkṣāṃ pāritavān kimicchati punardeyaṃ guroryadbhavet |
ātmānugrahamicchatīha nṛpajā dharmābhirāmapriyā yadyasyāsti samīpsitaṃ vadatu tatkasyādya kiṃ dīyatām  || 1.8 ||


*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   hanta dṛṣṭa upāyaḥ  (1.81)
{prakāśam}   bhoḥ ahamarthī  (1.82)
*{padmāvatī}*   diṭṭhiā sahaḻaṃ me tavovaṇābhigamaṇaṃ  (1.83)
[diṣṭyā saphalaṃ me tapovanābhigamanam |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   saṃtuṭṭhatapassijaṇamidamassamapadaṃ  (1.84)
āantueṇa imiṇā hodavvaṃ  (1.85)
[santuṣṭatapasvijanamidamāśramapadam |  āgantukenānena bhavitavyam |  ]

*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   bhoḥ kiṃ kriyatām  (1.86)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   iyaṃ me svasā  (1.87)
proṣitabhartṛkāmimāmicchāmyatrabhavatyā kaṅcitkālaṃ paripālyamānām  (1.88)
kutaḥ

kāryaṃ naivārthairnāpi bhogairna vastrairnāhaṃ kāṣāyaṃ vṛttihetoḥ prapannaḥ  |
dhīrā kanyeyaṃ dṛṣṭadharmapracārā śaktā cāritraṃ rakṣituṃ me bhaginyāḥ  || 1.9 ||


*{vāsavadattā}*
{ātmagatam}   haṃ  (1.91)
iha maṃ ṇikkhividukāmo ayyayogandharāyaṇo  (1.92)
hodu aviāria kamaṃ ṇa karissadi  (1.93)
[ham |  iha māṃ nikṣeptukāma āryayaugandharāyaṇaḥ |  bhavatu |  avicārya kramaṃ na kariṣyati |  ]

*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   bhavati mahatī khalvasya vyapāśrayaṇā  (1.94)
kathaṃ pratijānīmaḥ  (1.95)
kutaḥ

sukhamartho bhaveddātuṃ sukhaṃ prāṇāḥ sukhaṃ tapaḥ  |
sukhamanyadbhavetsarvaṃ duḥkhaṃ nyāsasya rakṣaṇam  || 1.10 ||


*{padmāvatī}*   ayya paḍhamamugghosia ko kimicchadi tti ajuttaṃ dāṇiṃ viāriduṃ  (1.101)
jameso bhaṇādi tamaṇuciṭṭhadu ayyo  (1.102)
[ārya prathamamudghoṣya kaḥ kimicchatītyayuktamidānīṃ vicārayitum |  yadeṣa bhaṇati tadanutiṣṭhatvāryaḥ |  ]

*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   anurūpametadbhavatyābhihitam  (1.103)
*{ceṭī}*   ciraṃ jīvadu bhaṭṭidāriā evaṃ saccavādiṇī  (1.104)
[ciraṃ jīvatu bhartṛdārikaivaṃ satyavādinī |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   ciraṃ jīvadu bhadde  (1.105)
[ciraṃ jīvatu bhadre |  ]

*{kāñcukīyaḥ}*   bhavati tathā  (1.106)
{upagamya  (1.107)}
bho abhyupagatamatrabhavato bhaginyāḥ paripālanamatrabhavatyā  (1.108)

*{padmāvatī}*   bhodu bhodu  (1.1013)
attaṇīā dāṇiṃ saṃvuttā  (1.1014)
[bhavatu bhavatu |  ātmīyedānīṃ saṃvṛttā |  ]

*{tāpasī}*   jā īdisī se āidī iyaṃ vi rāadāria tti takkemi  (1.1015)
[yā īdṛśyasyā ākṛtiḥ iyamapi rājadāriketi tarkayāmi |  ]

*{ceṭī}*   suṭṭhu ayyā bhaṇādi  (1.1016)

[suṣṭhu āryā bhaṇati |  ahamapyanubhūtasukheti paśyāmi |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*
{ātmagatam}   hanta bhoḥ ardhamavasitaṃ bhārasya  (1.1018)

bhūyiṣṭhaṃ kapilāni gokuladhanānyakṣetravatyo diśo niḥsandigdhamidaṃ tapovanamayaṃ dhūmo hi bahvāśrayaḥ  || 1.12 ||

*{padmāvatī}*   ammo parapurusadaṃsaṇaṃ pariharadi ayyā  (1.129)

*{brahmacārī}*
{ācamya}   bhavatu bhavatu  (1.1213)

rājagṛhato'smi  (1.1219)

*{vāsavadattā}*
{ātmagatam}   hā ḻāvāṇaaṃ ṇāma  (1.1221)

[hā lāvāṇakaṃ nāma |  lāvāṇakasaṅkīrtanena punarnavīkṛta iva me santāpaḥ |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   atha parisamāptā vidyā  (1.1223)
*{brahmacārī}*   na khalu tāvat (1.1224)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   yadyanavasitā vidyā kimāgamanaprayojanam  (1.1225)
*{brahmacārī}*   tatra khalvatidāruṇaṃ vyasanaṃ saṃvṛttam  (1.1226)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   kathamiva  (1.1227)
*{brahmacārī}*   tatrodayano nāma rājā prativasati  (1.1228)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   śrūyate tatrabhavānudayanaḥ  (1.1229)
kiṃ saḥ  (1.1230)
*{brahmacārī}*   tasyāvantirājaputrī vāsavadattā nāma patnī dṛḍhamabhipretā kila  (1.1231)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   bhavitavyam  (1.1232)
tatastataḥ  (1.1233)
*{brahmacārī}*   tatastasminmṛgayāniṣkrānte rājani grāmadāhena sā dagdhā  (1.1234)
*{vāsavadattā}*
{ātmagatam}   aḻiamaḻiaṃ khu edaṃ  (1.1235)

[alīkamalīkaṃ khalu etat |  jīvāmi mandabhāgā |  ]

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   tatastataḥ  (1.1237)
*{brahmacārī}*   tatastāmabhyavapattukāmo yaugandharāyaṇo nāma sacivastasminnevāgnau patitaḥ  (1.1238)
*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   satyaṃ patita iti  (1.1239)

*{vāsavadattā}*

*{yaugandharāyaṇaḥ}*   tatastataḥ  (1.1243)

*{brahmacārī}*   tataḥ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ pratilabdhasaṃjñaḥ saṃvṛttaḥ  (1.1252)
*{padmāvatī}*   diṭṭhiā dharai  (1.1253)
mohaṃ gado tti suṇia suṇṇaṃ via me hiaam  (1.1254)
