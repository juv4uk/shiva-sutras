|| vṛttamālāstutiḥ ||
vṛttaṃ samamardhasamaṃ viṣamaṃ cetyāmananti vāgīśaḥ  |
trividhaṃ parārthavidhaye samāsato vyāsato'nantam  || 1 ||

pratiniyatākṛtirūḍhaṃ varamunibhiryatra nāma saṃgītam  |
chandaḥ padaṃ ca paramaṃ sphurati yathāvividhavinyāsaiḥ  || 2 ||

pratirūpadarśanādapi śakyāḥ sakalakrameṇa gurulaghavaḥ  |
vigaṇayituṃ dhīmadbhiḥ kimaparamatrānuyogena  || 3 ||

prabhavatu vibhramahataye vicintyamānaṃ tadādarādbhavatām  |
sukṛtimukhābhijanānāṃ vācāṃ nijamekamābharaṇam  || 4 ||

|| prastāvanā ||
dhī-gī[ḥ]- |
<śrī->ṇām  || 5 ||

nijai-kabhūḥ  |
vibhā-ti yā  || 6 ||

tadguṇā-śaṃsibhiḥ  |
sevyate cāniśam  || 7 ||

samāśraya-skhalanmalaiḥ  |
surāsurā-diṣūdgataiḥ  || 8 ||

naikabhavīyā-bhyāsavivṛddham  |
bodhaviśeṣaṃ sādhu dadhānaiḥ  || 9 ||

gītā <gāyatrī>va cchandaḥsvādyā sadbhiḥ  |
vṛttaṃ bhāvi śreyo yasyāṃ sadvā <vaiśvam>  || 10 ||

nīlotpalapāṇerlīlānilayasya  |
śāsturjayatīyaṃ mūrtis<tanumadhyā>  || 11 ||

prasīda bhagavan vilokaya manāk |
jaḍaṃ janamimaṃ tvadeka<śaraṇam>  || 12 ||

jājvalīti śāsanaṃ tvāṃ sametya saugatam  |
sūryadhāma duḥsahaṃ <sātirekam> <uṣṇi> hi  || 13 ||

dadhatyapi nikāmaṃ kumāralalitāni  |
tanustava jinānāṃ <kumāra lalitai>va  || 14 ||

jāyate janarāśeryāmanusmarato'pi  |
nirdhutādharabhāvam <ūrdhvagāmi> ca rūpam  || 15 ||

yadguṇastutivistarādastasaṅgam <anuṣṭubhi>  |
dhīramekamudīrayanty<uttamaṃ> padam <udgatāḥ>  || 16 ||

ghoramohatāmasāva-ruddhabuddhilocanasya  |
ratnayaṣṭidīpikeva yā <samāny> abhūjjanasya  || 17 ||

jagattrayātiśāyinīṃ munīndrasaṃpadaṃ prati  |
sphurantyaśeṣanāyaka <pramāṇikā>pi saiva naḥ  || 18 ||

tīvrakleśaploṣadhvaṃsa-prauḍhe vaṃśe saṃbuddhānām  |
dhatte bhūṣāṃ bhātīyaṃ te'mbhode yadvad<vidyunmālā>  || 19 ||

ketanadaṇḍaṃ dadhato'dhyāpayituṃ nītimiva  |
sadgurulīlā bhavato bhāti jagan<māṇavakam>  || 20 ||

trijagato nijagatiṃ prathayataḥ pṛthudhiyaḥ  |
karatalaṃ tava calat-<kisalayaṃ tulayati>  || 21 ||

sādhigamaṃ samakālaṃ tattvaraseṣu vidhatte  |
viśvamadhīśvara citraṃ <citrapadā> tava vāṇī  || 22 ||

<bṛhatyāṃ> vibhūtyāṃ ta eke padanyāsamāsādayanti  |
kṣaṇaṃ yatra te labdhalakṣyaḥ <kaṭakṣo>'pi viśrāntimeti  || 23 ||

tvaddṛśaiva parimalanādbhavyatāmiyamupagatā  |
bhāti bhūmiriva janatā saṃtatāhita<halamukhī>  || 24 ||

varada sā hi saṃjanyate pariṇatistayāsyāṃ yataḥ  |
vacanabījamuptaṃ tvayā phalati śānti<rūpaṃ> phalam  || 25 ||

kalayitumiha kaḥ śaktastava khalu caritānīśa  |
satamasi samaye toye <bhujagaśiśusṛtā>nīva  || 26 ||

kintūdasyati dhairyadhvasrī tvatkīrtiśrutijanmānandaḥ  |
svaprauḍhipratipattinyastāṃ lokasyānana<mudrā>mīśa  || 27 ||

śrīniketa līlācaturaṃ te prāptapāra vidyācaraṇānām  |
bhāti varṣma buddhātmaja<paṅktau> yadvadambujanmasv<aravindam>  || 28 ||

tvāmudgītakulodayaśailaṃ prajñālokamupāyarathastham  |
vande jāḍyavikhaṇḍanaśauṇḍaṃ trailokī<nalinī>savitāram  || 29 ||

buddhasutānāmabhyudayorvī bhāti bhṛśaṃ bhūtyā tava nātha  |
śailaviśeṣasyeva śikhābhū <rukmavatī> ratnāṅkurakāntyā  || 30 ||

niḥśeṣastava vijayadhvānaṃ tanvan bhrāmyati bhuvanoddyota  |
sānandaḥ kṛtinivahaḥ svāmin viṣvagghoṣaṇa<paṇavo> yadvat || 31 ||

vādivrātapravaraśiro'bja śrīdhāmnaste guṇamadhuyogāt |
dhīman dhīrānapi mādayantī trailokī vāgvilasati <mattā>  || 32 ||

viśvacakravartināṃ jinānāṃ vaṃśabhūṣaṇe tvayi sphuṭaiva  |
mlāyatīva sā kumāratākhyā yā <mayūrasāriṇī>ṣyate tu  || 33 ||

nānāratnamarīcimālinī pronmīlannijakāntisaṃpadā  |
pratyaṅgaṃ tava bhūṣaṇāvalī nītā <śuddhavirāṭ> parābhavam  || 34 ||

pātu jagattava tattvanayā-khyānavidhau karavalgitakam  |
vādipate vyatighaṭṭanato valguninādacalad<valayam>  || 35 ||

rūpaṃ te guṇagaṇamatha kīrtiṃ saṃvīkṣyākhilamidamatiśāyi  |
bibhrāṇaṃ nijaviṣayaviśrāmaṃ mandaṃ <triṣṭubhi> jagad<upajātam>  || 36 ||

tanuratanudayādravopama-sphuradanupamakāntisaṃtatiḥ  |
janayati bhavato na kasya vā mudamadhipa samanta<bhadrikā>  || 37 ||

śaradamṛtakarakaraśreṇī-samahimagatamalagaurīyam  |
guṇasamuditiriva muktālī vilasati hṛdi bhavato <vṛttā>  || 38 ||

gīyamānasumanaḥsumanasvaj-jātikīrtivijayeva taveyam  |
bhāti dūratatasaurabhalobha-<svāgatā>lipaṭalakvaṇitena  || 39 ||

sphārāmodā madhunidhiradhikaṃ cūrṇaṃ cātuḥsamaṃ iva dadhatī  |
nīlābjaśrīrdalasukhaśayanā-saṃparyāpta<bhramaravilasitā>  || 40 ||

rūparasāyanabhāvanayā te vāsava eva paraṃ bahumānyaḥ  |
yena cirāya nirastanimeṣaṃ labdhama<dodhakam> ambakajātam  || 41 ||

haktyānataratnakirīṭacakrairābaddhakarāñjalibhiḥ prahṛṣṭaiḥ  |
dhanyaiḥ suciraṃ caraṇadvayī te vītānyavidheyam <upasthite>yam  || 42 ||

kimindravibhavairbrahmaśriyā vā tato'pyatiśayinyā saṃpadā kim  |
prasidhyati dhiyo'dhīśa prakāmaṃ tvadaṅghrikamalasy<opasthitaṃ> cet || 43 ||

tvatsaṃsevāvimukhaṃ janmadurge karmakleśau vivaśaṃ nāśamasmin  |
saṃbhūyālaṃ nayato'vaśyamugrau <vātormī> potamivāmbhodhimadhye  || 44 ||

dūradarśimanasāmanāgasāṃ kāpi visphurati cittasaṃtatiḥ  |
nirvirāmatamamābhavaṃ bhavat-paryupāsanamano<rathoddhatā>  || 45 ||

<chede hṛdyā> vibhramāṇāṃ <caturṇāṃ> tūrṇaprāptāvāryasatyāmṛtānām  |
puṇyāvasthā tvatkaṭākṣekṣane'pi prauḍhaprajñā<śālinī> kāpyudeti  || 46 ||

kīrtirartikarttarastava stava-stomasadmano'tisadmanohṛtaḥ  |
sāndracandracandrikārdracandana-<śyeny> alaṅkarotyalaṃ diśo daśa  || 47 ||

abhyudgatānekaśikhābhiruccaistvatsaṃśrayāddustyajasattvadṛṣṭiḥ  |
pradhvasramanvañcati śailamūrtistīvr<endravajrā>hatidāriteva  || 48 ||

virūḍhadharmādhipabhāvabhavya-pratuṅgasiṃhāsanasaṃniṣaṇṇam  |
upāsate kāntiśivāḥ śivāpaty-<upendravajrā>yudhavedhasastvām  || 49 ||

anantarodbhāvitalakṣaṇāḍhyau pādau bhavetāmapi gocarau te  |
yāsāṃ dṛśaḥ śravyayatiprapañcau dhanyāḥ surāṇām <upajātayas> tāḥ  || 50 ||

asaṃśayaṃ tvāmapareṣu satsvapi prakāmasarvātmaguṇānuhāriṣu  |
ananyasādhāraṇayā dhiyādhipā uśanti <vaṃśastham> anaṅgajiṣṇavaḥ  || 51 ||

bālākṛtiṃ kāntavicitrabhūṣaṇaṃ hemābhamakṣobhyasanāthaśekharam  |
vāgīśvaraṃ vāgmatimūrtibhirname maun<īndravaṃśā>bharānodayaśriyam  || 52 ||

ativistarairapi girāṃ sugiro guṇapāramīyuradhinātha na te  |
nijamodamātrajananīti nutiḥ <pramitākṣarā>pi subhagaiva mama  || 53 ||

dīpyamānākhilālaṅkṛtiślāghinī puṇyabhājāṃ mukhāmbhojalabdhodayā  |
bhāratī bhāratīśa svabhāvojjvalā tvāmupaiti svayaṃ sadguṇa<sragviṇī>  || 54 ||

bhaṅgaṃ nītāste pañcabāṇasya bāṇāḥ śānto dveṣāgnirmohajāḍyaṃ nirastam  |
nātha trailokyānugrahavyagramūrte saṃpatsaṃprāptā <vaiśvadevī> tvayaiva  || 55 ||

saṃpūrṇo'ntarguṇagaṇaratnaiścitrairambhorāśeryatimatidhairyāddhatse  |
saṃkleśāgnipraśamanasaddharmāmbho-dhārāsārair<jaladharamālā>līlām  || 56 ||

tava puraḥ paramottama saṃmatāḥ pratibhayā vikalāḥ prativādinah |
sulabhamūkadaśā bahu manyante <drutavilambitam> apyuditaṃ vacaḥ  || 57 ||

tritayamapi pavitrayantī jagattuhinakarakarāvadātadyutiḥ  |
prabhavati bhavataśca kīrtiściraṃ suragiriśirasaśca <mandākinī>  || 58 ||

harati nitāntamiyaṃ tava kīrtiḥ kṛtihṛdayāni dayānijavāsa  |
prasavacayena citeva samantāddiśi diśi <kundalatā> dalatālam  || 59 ||

dalayatyupāttaśobhāsamavāyo jagatāmaghāni ko'pyeṣa kṛpābdhiḥ  |
bahubuddhakoṭinirmāṇapaṭīyān dhṛtadharmadhātu<vāgīśvaralīlaḥ>  || 60 ||

pravikiratīva viyattaraṅgiṇī-taralataraṅgaruco rucāṃ cayān  |
vilasati śaśvadasirdaśāntare tava <yamunā>jalavīcimecakaḥ  || 61 ||

*manohārihārāvalīramyarūpaṃ kva nāthedṛśaṃ te surūpādhirāja  |
kva taccātidurdāntasattvapraśāntyai sphuradbhīmabhūṣā<bhujaṅgaprayātam>*  || 62 ||

aviralamauktikaprakarabhūṣā harati na kasya cittamiyamīśa  |
suruciramallikāsamayasevya-prati<navamālinī>va tava mūrtiḥ  || 63 ||

tava nātha paraṃ yadi kāntisudhā-jaladhau śramamujjhati jhātkṛtini  |
śubharūpanirūpaṇalampaṭakaṃ pari<to> <'ṭakam> aṅga jagannayanam  || 64 ||

varada viratibhāñji vyaktamaṅgānyatibhava bhavitā no'vaśyameva  |
tava vacanasudhāyāḥ pānapātraṃ śravaṇayuga<puṭo>'yaṃ prītihetuḥ  || 65 ||

vṛṇe varamimaṃ tvadekamatula-prabhāva bhavatānna mādṛśajanaḥ  |
bhavantamapi yahśrayañchamasudhā-<raseṣu viratir> <jaḍoddhatagatiḥ>  || 66 ||

tvatprasādasamudīrṇaśubhavaśād<aṣṭabhedi> sukhameva kṛtijanaḥ  |
locanotsavavidhāsu tanubhṛtāṃ <candra> <vartma> samupaiti bhavabhidām  || 67 ||

sukhaduḥkhātiśayādihetujātairaviluptasmṛtayo bhavanti bhavyāḥ  |
tava pādāśrayalabdhabuddhisārāḥ sa<kalā>yāmapi vādirā<ja gatyām>  || 68 ||

tava caraṇaparicaraṇarucīnāṃ kimapi sukhamudayati kṛtirūpam  |
nikhilamalapaṭalavigamadīpraṃ prathitaguṇa<mahitamati jagatyām>  || 69 ||

kaladhautakāntavapuruttamalīlaḥ paṭubhāratīvaraṭayā varivasyaḥ  |
iha mañjughoṣa iti vaḥ prathito vyājjinamānasaikanalinī<kalahaṃsaḥ>  || 70 ||

<vicchedastriṣu> yadi duḥsaheṣvamīṣu kleśeṣu sphuṭamaṇuśo'pi duṣkaraḥ syāt |
prāpyeta kva nu khalu nātha mūrtirīdṛktrailokīnayanamanaḥ<praharṣiṇī>ha  || 71 ||

<payonidhau viratim> apāṃ <vidhāya> ca sphuranmahākiraṇakalāparatnadhau  |
bhavādṛśāṃ vapuṣi ca kāntisaṃtatiḥ samīkṣyate yadi <rucire>yamīdṛśī  || 72 ||

mandradhvānairyāmṛtadhārāstvayi ramyā muñcatyuccairvisphurati prīṇitabhavye  |
nāthānveti vyaktatayātyantamanojñaṃ varṣāmbhodaṃ <saiva yatir> <mattamayūram>  || 73 ||

jayatīyamutpalataroranuttara-trijagatpramodajanane madhāviva  |
tvayi netrakānta<kusumodgatā> latā madhumattabhṛṅgarava<mañjubhāṣiṇī>  || 74 ||

sarvātiśāyiguṇanirbharalabdhamodairvidvanmadhuvratakulairupagīyamānam  |
manyāmahe jinanayābharaṇaṃ tavaiva vṛttaṃ <vasantatilakaṃ> navacūtameva  || 75 ||

saṃmukhavalanmukhamṛgādhipatipṛṣṭhā-rūḍhamatigāḍhadayamabhyudayakandam  |
sundarimabandhuritahemahimapādaṃ vādikula<daivatam> amandamabhivande  || 76 ||

praśamitasakalaprapañcanirāvṛti-prasaradamaladhīsamādhisudhājuṣaḥ  |
damayati bhavato hatāhitavikramā <muniyatir> <aparājitā> bhuvanatrayam  || 77 ||

pratibhayavapuṣāmakaruṇacarita-praśamanavidhaye kva cidapi kṛpayā  |
tava bhujaparighāvalirativiṣamā vilasati vividha<praharaṇakalikā>  || 78 ||

śreyaḥsaṃbhogaṃ <viṣayaviratim> avyagrāṃ mārapradhvaṃsaṃ tribhuvanahitasaṃpattim  |
duṣprāpāhāryaṃ padam <abhilaṣatāṃ> tacca tvadbhakteranyā varada na dig<asaṃbādhā>  || 79 ||

bhavataḥ kalāṃ kimapi <saṃyatairindriyaiḥ> kva cidādarastimitadhīryadi dhyāyati  |
acirātsvayaṃ varayatīyamenaṃ janaṃ <vasudhā> dhruvaṃ jaladhiśakvarībhūṣaṇā  || 80 ||

svedapūravilasatpulakocchvasitaistruṭat-kañcukaṃ dadhati nirdhutamapyamarastriyaḥ  |
kāntikamramiva te samitāv<atiśakvarī>-bandhasāramurukampam <anaṅgavijṛmbhitam>  || 81 ||

anupamaramaṇīyā <bhaṅgiraṅgeṣu> yasyā harati ratimudārāśeṣarūpāntareṣu  |
jayati tanurakhaṇḍākhaṇḍaleṣvāsalakṣmī-vijayaparamadhāmavyāmarug<mālinī>yam  || 82 ||

avikalatālaśālikalakākalīkala-vyatikaravallakīvalitacārupañcamā  |
bhaṇati mudā pratīṣṭajinabhāra bhāratī svamahima<kāminī> tava sabhāsu <madrakam>  || 83 ||

jayati nātha jagajjayakuñjaraniścalī-karaṇaśṛṅkhalajālanibhāṅgadabhūṣitaḥ  |
bhujatarustava durgatimārgamahārgalaḥ pramadamanmathanirmathanaḥ <sugatapriyaḥ>  || 84 ||

bhavaśirasi mahitaguṇamahimabhuvaṃ vyapagatasakalamalaśucimadhipate  |
tava varavijayasamupacitayaśasaḥ śriyamanuharati hi śaradi <śaśikalā>  || 85 ||

paṭuparimalamilitamadhukarayuva-bhramasahaśitisugatasamucitasukhā  |
vilasati tava śirasi <rasalayavatī> <srag> iyamamarapatiyuvativiracitā  || 86 ||

na <yatipadamayasi> yadatidayadhiyaḥ prathayasi jagati ca parahitakṛtinaḥ  |
janayati himacayaruciriti bhavato viyadupahatanija<maṇi guṇanikaraḥ>  || 87 ||

atiratikarakatha kathamiva samiyādaviratanavanavanava tava paramaiḥ  |
guṇagaṇaparimitim <udadhiṣu yatibhiḥ> suyatibhiragaṇita<guṇa maṇinikaraḥ>  || 88 ||

jayati tava tanuriyamurutarakaruṇa nikhilajagaducitaparibhugavaniriva  |
śiśumatikalitasakalajanabahuvidha-vimatilaḍitavidhibhir<acaladhrtir>iha  || 89 ||

prati <jaḍadhiyami>ha samadhikagurudaya mṛdumatirapi tava śubhamayasamudaya  |
karatalavinihitakuvalaya kalayati jinakula<kuvalayam> aviṣamabharamiti  || 90 ||

vyomavikīrṇakirtimadaparimalakalanā-bhaṅguratuṅgavādimadakalakarinikaraṃ  |
viśvavanodare'tra <ravayati>jitajaladaṃ nātha dadhāsyudāram <ṛṣabhagajavilasitam>  || 91 ||

kastvayi baddhavarmaṇi jagaddhitadhānadhiyā nirvihatapratijña paritāpakaṇaḥ kva cana  |
māravadhūjanasya kamanīyakapolatale yannava<pattrabhaṅgaracanā>lasa eva karaḥ  || 92 ||

nirupamaramyarūpa jinajātadṛśvano <'ṣṭau> madayati te'dhikaṃ vidhutaśoka lokapālān  |
sadupadhipuṇḍarīkanakharājirājiteyaṃ viracitapañcacīrarucirā <kumāralīlā>  || 93 ||

guṇānām <atyaṣṭiḥ> kathamiha mamevāstu jagatāmiti <cchandaḥ> svairaṃ <pariṇatimavāpat> tava <tathā>  |
<yathā> tvayyevāsmin parama<rasabhedapraṇayanī> <samudbhūtā> bhūtirnikhilasukhaśākhā<śikhariṇī>  || 94 ||

jayatyamaramandiropalaviśālarakṣaḥsthalaḥ sthalīkamalakānanapratimapādakandaprabhaḥ  |
prabhākaraśatādhikaprakaṭatejasālaṅkṛtaḥ kṛtānta<madamardanas> tribhavanātha<pṛthvī>dharaḥ  || 95 ||

vimohabahalāndhakārapaṭalāvanaddhairmudhā rasātalaguhātiśāyibhavasaṃkaṭeṣu sthitam  |
tvadaṅghrikamalāśrayotsavasukhaṃ na cedādṛtaṃ cirasya jinavaṃśabhāskara kumā<ra mā>rairjitam  || 96 ||

kalpaśatopapāditamahāphalamiva vihitaṃ cañcalametadāyuradhipa tvadavanatiparaiḥ  |
bhūṣaṇabhavyabhāvagamitaṃ śritagurukaruṇā-<digyativaṃśa> <pattrapatitaṃ> kamiva jalaruhaḥ  || 97 ||

tava guṇavistarapraṇayapūtavacovibhavaṃ varada sa eka eva sumukho mukhamāvahati  |
madhumayasāmadhāma yajuṣā sajuṣānugataṃ śravaṇasudhābhimānabhava<narkkuṭakaṃ> ca vidhiḥ  || 98 ||

tribhuvanasaṃmadapradajinavyavasāyamadhau sphuṭasahakāratāmahitamīhitameti tava  |
pravacanamañjarīsrutaśamāmṛtapānamudā-bhṛta <yatiṣaṭpadaṃ> kalakaṇatkṛti<kokilakam>  || 99 ||

kva cidapi bhavadbhītyā bhūripravādimadadvipod-dalana sugatasvāntakṣmābhṛdgabhīraguhāgṛha  |
tribhavanavanādhīśa krīḍā<rasārṇavasaṃyatā> dravati <hariṇī>vāraṃ mārapracaṇḍamahācamūḥ  || 100 ||

durdāntānāṃ damanavidhaye kvāpi kāruṇyavegātdhatse mūrtiṃ caraṇaśikhayā khyātavikrānta yasyāḥ  |
