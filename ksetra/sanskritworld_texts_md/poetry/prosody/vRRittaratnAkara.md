[śrīḥ]

[vṛttaratnākaraḥ]

prathamo'dhyāyaḥ

sukhasantānasiddhyarthaṃ natvā brahmā'cyutā'rcitam |
gaurīvināyakopetaṃ śaṅkaraṃ lokaśaṅkaram || 1.1 ||

vedārthaśaivaśāstrajñaḥ pavyeko'bhūdlokaśaṅkaram |
tasya putro'sti kedāraḥ śivapādārcane rataḥ || 1.2 ||

tenedaṃ kriyate chando lakṣyalakṣaṇasaṃyutam |
vṛttaratnākaraṃ nāma bālānāṃ sukhasiddhye || 1.3 ||


piṅgalādibhrācāryairyaduktaṃ laukikaṃ dvidhā |
mātrāvarṇavibhedena cchandastadiha kathyate || 1.4 ||

ṣaḍadhyāyanibaddhasya cchandaso'sya parisphuṭam |
pramāṇamapi vijñeyaṃ ṣaḍtriṃśadadhikaṃ śatam || 1.5 ||

myarastajabhnagairlāntairebhirdaśabhirakṣaraiḥ |
samastaṃ vāṅmayaṃ vyāptaṃ trailokyamiva viṣṇunā || 1.6 ||

sarvagurmo mukhāntarlau yarāvantagalau satau |
gmadhyādyau jmau trilo no'ṣṭau bhavantyatra gaṇāstrikāḥ || 1.7 ||

jñeyāḥ sarvāntamadhyādiguravo'tra catuṣkalāḥ |
gaṇāścaturlaghūpetāḥ pañcāryādiṣu saṃsthitāḥ || 1.8 ||

sānusvāro visargānto dīrgho yuktaparaśca yaḥ |
vā pādānte tvasau gvakro jñeyo'nyo mātriko lṛjuḥ || 1.9 ||

pādādāviha varṇasya saṃyogaḥ kramasaṃjñakaḥ |
puraḥsthitena tena syāllaghutā'pi kvacidguroḥ || 1.10 ||

taruṇaṃ sarṣapaśākaṃ navaudanaṃ picchilāni ca dadhīni |
alpavyayena sundari (!) grāmyajano miṣṭamiśnāti || 1.11 ||

abdhibhūtarasādīnāṃ jñeyāḥ saṃjñāstu lokataḥ |
jñeyaḥ pādaścaturthāśo yatirvicchedasaṃjñitaḥ || 1.12 ||

yuksamaṃ viṣamaṃ cālūksthānaṃ sadbhirnigadyate |
samamardhasamaṃ vṛttaṃ viṣamaṃ ca tathāparam || 1.13 ||

aṅghrayo yasya catvārastulyalakṣaṇalakṣitāḥ |
tacchandaḥśāstratattvajñāḥ samaṃ vṛttaṃ pracakṣate || 1.14 ||

prathamāṅghrisamo yasya tṛtīyaścaraṇo bhavet |
dvitīyasturyavadvṛttaṃ tadardhasamamucyate || 1.15 ||

yasya pādacatuṣke'pi lakṣma bhinnaṃ parasparam |
tadāhurviṣamaṃ vṛttaṃ chandaḥśāstraviśāradāḥ || 1.16 ||

ārabhyaikākṣārātpādādekaikakṣaravaddhitaiḥ |
pṛthakchando bhavetpādairyāvatṣaḍviṃśatiṃ gatam || 1.17 ||

tadūrdhvaṃ caṇḍavṛṣṭyādidaṇḍakāḥ parikīrtitāḥ |
śeṣaṃ gāthāstribhiḥ ṣaḍbhiścaraṇaiścopalakṣitāḥ || 1.18 ||

uktā'tyuktā tathā madhyā pratiṣṭhā'nyā supūrvikā |
gāyatryuṣṇiganuṣṭupca bṛhatī paṅktireva ca || 1.19 ||

triṣṭupca jagatī caiva tathā'tijagatī matā |
śakvarī sā'tipūrvā syādaṣṭyatyaṣṭī tataḥ smṛte || 1.20 ||

dhṛtiścā'tidhṛtiścaiva krṭiḥ prakṛtirākṛtiḥ |
vikṛtiḥ saṅkṛtiścaiva tathā'tikṛtirutkṛtiḥ || 1.21 ||

ityuktāśchandasāṃ saṃjñāḥ kramato vacmi sāmpratam |
lakṣaṇaṃ sarvavṛttānāṃ mātrāvṛttā'nupūrvakam || 1.22 ||

iti śrīkedārabhaṭṭaviracite vṛttaratnākare saṃjñābhidhāno nāma prathamo'dhyāyaḥ |

dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ

[ āryā-prakaraṇam (1-7) ]

lakṣmaitatsapta gaṇā gopetā bhavati neha viṣame jaḥ |
ṣaṣṭho'yaṃ nalaghū vā prathame'rdhe niyatamāryāyāḥ || 2.1 ||

ṣaṣthe dvitīyalātparake nle mukhalācca sayatipadaniyamaḥ |
carame'rdhe pañcake tasmādiha bhavati ṣaṣṭho laḥ || 2.2 ||

triṣvaṅkeṣu pādo dalayorādyeṣu dṛśyate yasyāḥ |
pathyeti nāma tasyāḥ prakīrtitaṃ nāgarājena || 2.3 ||

saṃlaṅghya gaṇatrayamādimaṃ śakalayordvayorbhavati pādaḥ | [saṃ(anunāsika)-laṅghya]
yasyāstāṃ piṅgalanāgo vipulāmiti samākhyāti || 2.4 ||

ubhayārdhayorjakārau dvitīyaturyau gamadhyagau tasyāḥ |
capaleti nāma tasyā prakīrtitaṃ nāgarājena || 2.5 ||

ādyaṃ dalaṃ samastaṃ bhajeya lakṣma capalāgataṃ yasyāḥ |
śeṣe pūrvajalakṣmā mukhacapalāsoditā muninā || 2.6 ||

prākpratipāditamardhe prathame prathametare ca capalāyāḥ |
lakṣmāśrayate soktā viśuddhadhībhirjaghanacapalā || 2.7 ||

[ gīti-prakaraṇam (8-11) ]

āryāorathamadaloktaṃ yadi kathamapi lakṣaṇaṃ bhavedubhayoḥ |
dalayoḥ krṭayatiśobhāṃ tāṃ gītiṃ gītavānbhujaṅgeśaḥ || 2.8 ||

āryādvitīyake'rdhe yadgaditaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tatsyāt |
yadyubhayorapi dalayorupagītiṃ tā munirbrūte || 2.9 ||

āryāśakaladvitayaṃ vyatyayaracitaṃ bhavedyasyāḥ |
sodgītiḥ kila gaditā tadvadyatyaṃśabhedasaṃyuktā || 2.10 ||

āryāpūrvārdhaṃ yadi guruṇaikenādhikena nidhane yuktam |
itarattadvannikhilaṃ bhavati yadīyamarddhamuditāryāgītiḥ || 2.11 ||

[ vaitālīya-prakaraṇam (12-20) ]

ṣaḍviṣame'ṣṭau same kalāstāśca same syurno nirantarāḥ |
na samātra parāśritā kalā vaitālīye'nte ralā guruḥ || 2.12 ||

paryante ryau tathaiva śeṣamaupacchandasikaṃ sudhībhiruktam || 2.13 ||

āpātalikā kathiteyaṃ bhādgurukāvatha pūrvavadanyat || 2.14 ||

tṛtīyayugdakṣiṇāntikā samastapādeṣu dvitīyalaḥ || 2.15 ||

udīcyavṛttirdvitīyalaḥ sakto'greṇa bhavedayugmayoḥ || 2.16 ||

pūrveṇa yuto'tha pañcamaḥ prācyavṛttiruditeti yugmayoḥ || 2.17 ||

yadā samāvojayugmakau pūrvayorbhavati tatpravṛttakam || 2.18 ||

asya yugmaracitā'parāntikā || 2.19 ||

ayugbhavā cāruhāsinī || 2.20 ||

[ vaktra-prakaraṇam (21-30) ]

vaktraṃ nādyānnasau syātāmabdheryo'nuṣṭubhi khyātam || 2.21 ||

yujorjena saridbhartuḥ pathyāvaktraṃ prakīrtitam || 2.22 ||

ojayorjena vāridhestadeva viparītādi || 2.23 ||

capalāvaktramayujornakāraśvetpayorāśeḥ || 2.24 ||

yasyā laḥ saptamo yugme sā yugmavipulā matā || 2.25 ||

sautavasyā'khileṣvapi || 2.26 ||

bhenā'bdhito bhādvipulā || 2.27 ||

itthamanyā raścaturthāt || 2.28 ||

no'mbudheścennavipulā || 2.29 ||

to'bdhestatpūrvānyā bhavet || 2.30 ||

[ mātrāsamaka-prakaraṇam (31-38) ]

dviguṇitavasuladhuracaladhṛtiriti || 2.31 ||

mātrāsamakaṃ navamo lgāntam || 2.32 ||

jo nlāvathāmbudherviślokaḥ || 2.33 ||

tadyugalādvānavāsikā syāt || 2.34 ||

bāṇāṣṭanavasu yadi laścitā || 2.35 ||

upacitrā navame parayukte || 2.36 ||

yadatītakṛtavividhalakṣmayutairmātrāsamādipādaiḥ kalitam |
aniyatavṛttaparimāṇayuktaṃ prathitaṃ jagatsu pādākulakam || 2.37 ||

vṛttasya lā vinā varṇairgā varṇā gurubhistathā |
guruvo lairdale nityaṃ pramāṇamiti niścitam || 2.38 ||

śikhiguṇitadaśalaghuracitamapagatalaghuyugalamaparamidamakhilam |
saguru śakalayugalakamapi suparighaṭitalalitapadavitati bhavati śikhā || 2.39 ||

vinimayavinihitaśakalayugalakalitapadavitativiracitaguṇanicayā |
śrutisukhakṛdiyamapi jagati ñi jaśira upagatavati sati bhavati khajā || 2.40 ||

aṣṭāvardhe gā dvayabhyastā yasyāḥ sā'naṅgakrīḍoktā |
dalamaparamapi vasuguṇitasalilanidhilaghu kaviracitaṃ padavitati bhavati || 2.41 ||

triguṇanavalaghuraṃvasitigururitidalayugakṛtatanuratirucirā || 2.42 ||

iti śrīmadbhaṭṭarāmeśvarasūnunārāyaṇabhaṭṭaviracitāyāṃ vṛttaratnākaravyākhyāyāṃ
mātrāvṛttādhikāro nāma dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ ||

pañcamo'dhyāyaḥ

[padacaturūrdhva-prakaraṇam (1-5)]

mukhavādo'ṣṭabhirvarṇaiḥ pare syurmakarālayaiḥ kramādvṛddhaiḥ ||
satataṃ yasya vicitraiḥ pādaiḥ sampannasaundaryaṃ
taduditamamalamatibhiḥ padacaturūrdhvābhidhaṃ vṛttam || 5.1 ||

prathamamuditavṛtte viracitaviṣamacaraṇabhāji ||
gurukayugalanidhana iha sahita āṅā
laghuviratapadavitatiyatiriti bhavati pīḍaḥ || 5.2 ||

prathamamitaracaraṇasamutthaṃ śrayati sa yadi lakṣma |
itaraditaragatitamapi yadi ca turyaṃ
caraṇayugalakamiti kalikā sā || 5.3 ||

dviguruyutasamalacaraṇāntā mukhacaraṇagatamanubhavati ca tṛtīyaḥ |
aparamiha lakṣma prakṛtamakhilamapi yadidamanubhavati lavalī sā || 5.4 ||

prathamadhivasati yadi turyaṃ, caramacaraṇapadamavasitaguruyugmam |
nikhilamaparamuparigatamiti lalitapadayuktā, tadidamamṛtadhārā || 5.5 ||

[udgatā-prakaraṇam (6-8)]

sajādime salaghukau ca nasajagurukairathodgatā |
tryaṅghrigatabhanajalā gayutāḥ sajasā agau caraṇamekataḥ paṭhet || 5.6 ||

caraṇatrayaṃ vrajati lakṣma yadi sakalamudgatāgatam |
nā bhagau bhavati saurabhakaṃ caraṇe yadīha bhavatastṛtīyake || 5.7 ||

nayugaṃ sakārayugalaṃ ca bhavati caraṇe tṛtīyake |
taduditamurumatibhirlalitaṃ yadi śeṣamasya khalu pūrvatulyakam || 5.8 ||

[upasthitapracupita-prakaraṇam (9-11)]

msau jbhau gau prathamāṅghrirekataḥ pṛthaganyattritayaṃ sanajaragāstato nanau saḥ |
trinaparikalitajayau pracupitamidamuditamupasthitapūrvam || 5.9 ||

nau pāde'tha tṛtīyake sanau nasayuktau prathamāṅghrikṛtayatistu vardhamānam |
tritayamaparamapi pūrvasadṛśamiha bhavati pratatamatibhiriti gaditaṃ laghu vṛttam || 5.10 ||

prathame ca viratirārṣabhaṃ bruvanti ||
tacchuddhavirāṭpuraḥ sthitaṃ tritayamaparamapi yadi pūrvasamaṃ syāt || 5.11 ||

viṣamākṣarapādaṃ vā pādairasamaṃ daśadhrmavat |
yacchando noktamatra gātheti tatsūribhiḥ proktam || 5.12 ||

iti śrīmadbhaṭṭarāmeśvarasūnunārāyaṇabhaṭṭaviracitāyāṃ vṛttaratnākaravyākhyāyāṃ
pañcamo'dhyāyaḥ ||

---------------------------------

ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ

[prastāraḥ]

prastāro naṣṭamuddiṣṭamekadvayādilagakriyā |
saṅkhyānamadhvayogaśca ṣaḍete pratyayāḥ smṛtāḥ || 6.1 ||

pāde sarvagurāvādyāllaghuṃ nyasya guroradhaḥ |
yathopari tathā śeṣaṃ bhūyaḥ kuryādamuṃ vidhim || 6.2 ||

ūne dadyādgurūneva yāvatsarvalaghurbhavet |
prastāro'yaṃ samākhyātaśchandovicitivedibhiḥ || 6.3 ||

[naṣṭam]

naṣṭasya yo bhavedaṅkastasyārdhe'rdhe same ca laḥ ||
viṣame caikamādhāya syādardhe'rdhe gurubhavet || 6.4 ||

[uddiṣṭam]

uddiṣṭaṃ dviguṇānādyāduparyaṅkānsamālikhet |
laghusthā ye ca tatrāṅkāstaiḥ saikairmiśritairbhavet || 6.5 ||

[ekadvayādilagakriyā]

varṇānvṛttabhavānsaikānauttarādharyataḥ sthitān |
ekādikramataścaitānuparyupari nikṣipet || 6.6 ||

upāntyato nivarteta tyajannekaikamūrdhvataḥ |
uparyādyādgurorekamekadvyādilagakriyā || 6.7 ||

[saṅkhyānam]

lagakriyāṅkasandohe bhavetsaṅkhyā vimiśrite |
uddiṣṭāṅkasamāhāraḥ saikā vā janayedimām || 6.8 ||

[adhvayogaḥ]

saṅkhyaiva dviguṇaikonā sadbhiradhvā prakīrtitaḥ ||
vṛttasyāṅgulikā vyāptiradhaḥ kuryāttathāṅgulim || 6.9 ||

vaṃśe'bhūtkaśyapasya prakaṭaguṇagaṇaḥ śaivasiddhāntavettā
vipraḥ pavyekanāmā vimalataramatirvedatattvārthabodhe |
kedārastasya sūnuḥ śivacaraṇayugārādhanaikāgracittaśchandastenābhirāmaṃ
vṛttaratnākarākhyam || 6.10 ||

iti śrīmadbhaṭṭarāmeśvarasūnunārāyaṇabhaṭṭaviracitāyāṃ vṛttaratnākaravyākhyāyāṃ
prastārādhyāyaḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ samāptaḥ ||
