prathamaḥ sargaḥ

atha mālyavataḥ prasthe kākutsthasya viyoginaḥ  |
durnivārāśrusaṃvego jagāma jaladāgamaḥ  || 1.1 ||

śaśāma vṛṣṭirmeghānāmutsaṅge tasya bhūbhṛtaḥ  |
virarāma na rāmasya dhārāsantatiraśruṇaḥ  || 1.2 ||

itastataḥ pariṇatiṃ bheje barhiṇakūjitam  |
hā priye rājaputrīti na rāmaparidevitam  || 1.3 ||

kāpyabhikhyā virajasoḥ sūryācandramasorabhūt |
dāśarathyostathaivāsīdayogopahatā ruciḥ  || 1.4 ||

nirmalendu nabho reje vikacābjaṃ babhau saraḥ  |
paraṃ paryaśruvadanau mamlaturbhrātarāvubhau  || 1.5 ||

smartavyakalikājālaṃ jajñe kuṭajakānanam  |
āsīttathaiva tu mano rāmasyotkalikākulam  || 1.6 ||

prapede punarudbhedaḥ śucinā kacchaketakaiḥ  |
upakriyāyāḥ sadṛśaṃ nārebhe ravisūnunā  || 1.7 ||

ākṛṣṭamālatīgandhaḥ siṣeve rāghavaṃ marut |
ānītamaithilīvārtastamārtaṃ na tu mārutiḥ  || 1.8 ||

rāmāya pūrāpagamavyaktoddeśāścakāśire  |
āste'smāsu na sīteti śaṃsantya iva nimnagāḥ  || 1.9 ||

yadārdraviraho rāmaḥ prāpitaḥ prāṇasaṃśayam  |
nūnaṃ tenānutāpena jagmurjaladarājayaḥ  || 1.10 ||

dyaurmuktameghāvaraṇā savitarkonmukhaṃ muhuḥ  |
yayāce sārasarutairvicāramiva rāghavam  || 1.11 ||

diśo dadṛśire tena stenaṃ taṃ parimārgatā  |
mārgadānāparādhinyo gatā dūraṃ bhayādiva  || 1.12 ||

tasyākṛtiviśeṣasya viśeṣātkaṣṭamīdṛśam  |
kacchāḥ kāśacchalādūhuritīva palitāgamam  || 1.13 ||

paśyantyā iva kaṣṭāṃ tāmavasthāṃ maithilīpateḥ  |
śuṣkapaṅkacayavyājādvidadre hṛdayaṃ bhuvaḥ  || 1.14 ||

vṛṣṭivicchedajātena tānavenāsamāptinā  |
prapedire jaladharāstasya sabrahmacāritām  || 1.15 ||

yayau virahasaṃvartaḥ padminīrājahaṃsayoḥ  |
apāraduḥkhārṇavayorna sītārāmabhadrayoḥ  || 1.16 ||

śāntanirjharajhātkāradhārāḥ śikhariṇo babhuḥ  |
rāmasya viśadālāpaśuśrūṣānibhṛtā iva  || 1.17 ||

tasya cakruścamatkāraṃ vyatītasamayā api  |
smitābhamukulodbhedāḥ kadambavanarājayaḥ  || 1.18 ||

adṛśyanta purastena khelāḥ khañjanapaṅktayaḥ  |
asmaryanta ca niśvasya priyānayanavibhramāḥ  || 1.19 ||

abravīdatha kālajñastatkālapratipattaye  |
praṇamya lakṣmaṇo rāmaṃ rāmāvirahaniḥsaham  || 1.20 ||

jātaṃ vibhātamamalaṃ paśya prāvṛṇṇiśā gatā  |
utsāhakamalasyāyaṃ vikāsāvasarastava  || 1.21 ||

ucchrayaṃ tīrataravo raverūrjamabhīṣavaḥ  |
madaṃ gopatayo gṛhṇantyabhiyogaṃ jigīṣavaḥ  || 1.22 ||

prasīda dīyatāṃ karṇaḥ kūjadbhiḥ kurarairamī  |
prārthayantīva saṃcāraṃ taṭāḥ kardamadurgatāḥ  || 1.23 ||

bhuvi bhāsvadabhīṣūṇāṃ tvadiṣūṇāṃ ca śatruṣu  |
ayaṃ sampatituṃ kālaḥ sa duṣkālo'tivāhitaḥ  || 1.24 ||

sudūronnataparyantāstīryante saṃkramairiva  |
analpagādhāḥ saritastarubhiḥ pūrapātitaiḥ  || 1.25 ||

āviṣkṛtaviśuddhibhyāṃ nadyaḥ sumahilā iva  |
kulābhyāmiva kūlābhyāmāpatsvapi cakāsati  || 1.26 ||

jalajānāṃ sumanasāṃ vayasāṃ jalasevinām  |
asya pratijalādhāraṃ sāmagryamavalokyate  || 1.27 ||

yuyutsārabhasotkhātasaritkhātakarodhasaḥ  |
kukudmanto madakalāḥ kalayanti jagantyadhaḥ  || 1.28 ||

viśveśa kuru viśvāsamāvartante na te ghanāḥ  |
amī śyāmalayantyāśāḥ śukāḥ śālivanonmukhāḥ  || 1.29 ||

mṛgāḥ samprati śāleyaṃ sampannaṃ sampatantyamī  |
sāketamadhitiṣṭhantamarthinastvāmivonmukhāḥ  || 1.30 ||

kāryadiggṛhyatāṃ kāpi kimārya sthīyate vṛthā  |
evamevāyamasmākamiṣṭo'pi kimupaiṣyati  || 1.31 ||

vijayāya bhavāsīno mayyarpitavapurbharaḥ  |
mūrcchānuvalanavyastaḥ prastarastyajyatāmayam  || 1.32 ||

pratīṣṭopatyakāsāladalāgrajalabindavaḥ  |
sāryantāṃ kṣaṇamuddhūya sthagitāṃsataṭā jaṭāḥ  || 1.33 ||

muhūrtaṃ kriyatāmārya kapolavirahī karaḥ  |
vyuṣito valkalagranthiraṃsādunmocyatāmayam  || 1.34 ||

ito vitatya dīyantāmiṣavaḥ klinnayantraṇāḥ  |
nirmuktārdrajaraccailamādattāmāyataṃ dhanuḥ  || 1.35 ||

ito'vatīryatāṃ prāsthādasvāsthyamidamujjhyatām  |
jahīṣubhirdaśagrīvaṃ daśadigvedhaśodhitaiḥ  || 1.36 ||

ṛjurādīyatāṃ panthāḥ kāryakanthā garīyasī  |
utsāryatāmidaṃ dūraṃ tamaḥ kiṃkāryatāmayam  || 1.37 ||

āryāyāḥ śivamevāsti śatrurnihata eva te  |
alaṃ dattvāvasādāya trailokyaśaraṇaṃ vapuḥ  || 1.38 ||

rākṣasāpaśadaḥ ko'sau tvayi sajyaśarāsane  |
anena te'vasādena dūramāropitaḥ punaḥ  || 1.39 ||

aśruvegaiḥ pramīlābhirvilāpairakhilaḥ sa te  |
āmṛṣṭadehayātrasya māsaḥ prauṣṭhapado gataḥ  || 1.40 ||

kriyatāmadhunotsāhaścintyatāṃ nidhanaṃ dviṣaḥ  |
kaḥ svabāhusahāyānāṃ mahatāmātmanigrahaḥ  || 1.41 ||

bhadraṃ bhādrapadacchedacchinnābhrājinarajjave  |
tubhyaṃ puruṣanāgāya nirvighnatarase'dhunā  || 1.42 ||

prasīda kuru bhūyo'pi dehayātrāṃ yathā tathā  |
anullaṅghitavijñaptirlakṣmaṇo'varajeṣu te  || 1.43 ||

agādhaṃ dhairyamāryasya triṣu lokeṣu gīyate  |
janamātrocitaḥ ko'yamākalpakaparigrahaḥ  || 1.44 ||

aropaśamitodagrajāmadagnyāgnitejasaḥ  |
pratīcchati śarāsāraṃ tavārya ruṣitasya kaḥ  || 1.45 ||

svakṛtyabhārasannyāsī yāvaditthaṃ viṣīdasi  |
tāvadantakadaṃṣṭrāṇāmarātiratithirna te  || 1.46 ||

ātatāyī sa vijñāto jīvatyāryeti ca śrutam  |
prāpto yātrāsahaḥ kālaḥ kālakṣepasya ko guṇaḥ  || 1.47 ||

na jñāyate paraṃ vyaktamarermāyāvinaḥ padam  |
tanmārgaṇaṃ ca suhṛdā sugrīveṇa pratiśrutam  || 1.48 ||

diṅmukhāhūtavikhyātakapiyūthaḥ kapīśvaraḥ  |
kṣaṇānnūnamasavāryapādamūle patiṣyati  || 1.49 ||

śālipiṅgamahīpṛṣṭhaspardhayeva bhaviṣyati  |
raṃhasviharisaṃghātasampātakapilaṃ nabhaḥ  || 1.50 ||

teṣāmāroṣarucirairacirānmukhamaṇḍalaiḥ  |
drakṣyate taruṇādityaśatacchannamivāmbaram  || 1.51 ||

aho dīptiraho kāntiraho śīlamaho balam  |
aho śaktiraho bhaktiraho prajñā hanūmataḥ  || 1.52 ||

pragalbhenāpramattena tenābhyantaramantriṇā  |
nūnamasmatkṛte svaptuṃ sugrīvāya na dīyate  || 1.53 ||

sa ca pratyupakārāya tvarayatyenamādṛtaḥ  |
dhruvaṃ buddhivayovṛddhaḥ sacivo jāmbavānapi  || 1.54 ||

tadevaṃ hetunā kena vānarendro vilambate  |
nāsau kṛtaghnasya bhavedguṇavadbhṛtyasaṃgrahaḥ  || 1.55 ||

āgantavyaṃ dhruvaṃ tena mārgitāryā pravṛttinā  |
prapannaparakṛtyānāmatyuṣṇo hi samudyamaḥ  || 1.56 ||

tvatprasādāptasambhogasukhasaṃmīlitena vā  |
na tenāmūrdadṛśire diśaḥ proṣitakālikāḥ  || 1.57 ||

kiyacciraṃ punarasau tasya na śrutimeṣyati  |
krīḍātaṭākahaṃsānāṃ ninādo madamūrchitaḥ  || 1.58 ||

uktaiḥ kimatha bhūyobhiradya śvo vāgamiṣyati  |
paryutsukitakiṣkindhaḥ satyasandho harīśvaraḥ  || 1.59 ||

vyatireke'pi yatkāryaṃ tadapyārya pradhāryatām  |
gṛhyatāṃ kāryasiddhirnastadvilambatiraskṛtā  || 1.60 ||

yastathopakṛtaḥ pūrvamāryeṇāryākṛte'dhunā  |
sa śītalaḥ syādathavā paracittāni vetti kaḥ  || 1.61 ||

sa pratiśrutamarthaṃ naḥ sphīto vismṛtavāniva  |
gatvāśu smāryatāmārya kāryataptasya kā kṣamā  || 1.62 ||

tamahaṃ bodhayiṣyāmi daṇḍagarbhābhiruktibhiḥ  |
sāma saṃmīlayatyeva sukhasaktān pramādinaḥ  || 1.63 ||

prasīda jīyatāṃ mohaḥ ko'hamityavadhīyatām  |
drutamuttīrṇa evāste vyasanābdhiḥ kiyānayam  || 1.64 ||

sūnṛtairiti saumitreḥ prītaḥ pīyūṣavarṣibhiḥ  |
tamantarvedanāvegaṃ jigāyeva raghūdvahaḥ  || 1.65 ||

unmīlayāmāsa dṛśau diśaḥ kiṃcidalokata  |
unnanāma ca talpāntānmocayan pakṣmalā jaṭāḥ  || 1.66 ||

satvaraprahvasaumitricīrotsāritareṇuni  |
śucini kvacidāsanne niṣasāda śilātale  || 1.67 ||

upaviṣṭastadā rāmo na reje sītayā vinā  |
dhvajo nirvaijayantīkaḥ paurandara ivocchritaḥ  || 1.68 ||

anūpaviṣṭamanujaṃ danujārimivādrijit |
pratyabhāṣata vātsalyagadgadāni padāni saḥ  || 1.69 ||

ehyehi priyasaṃlāpa hlādayāliṅgitena mām  |
ruddhaṃ ruddhamupaityaśru tvāmapīkṣe na lakṣmaṇa  || 1.70 ||

hā vatsa bhavato'pīyamavasthāṅgeṣu vartate  |
dhriyatāṃ rāmahatakaḥ kleśayannekabāndhavam  || 1.71 ||

nāsti vatsa cikitsāsya vyādhervidhurajanmanaḥ  |
yasya yāpanayā kṣīṇamidaṃ te sāntvanāmṛtam  || 1.72 ||

naikadurvāraghorādhighaṭṭanācalitā api  |
tavāśvāsanayā vatsa na gacchanti mamāsavaḥ  || 1.73 ||

piturnaḥ priyamitrasya lūnapakṣasya maddviṣā  |
saṃsmarannotsahe vatsa marmacchedaṃ jaṭāyuṣaḥ  || 1.74 ||

tātasya tātamitrasya vatsavatsalayostayoḥ  |
ahameva gataḥ pāpo nidhanāya nimittatām  || 1.75 ||

priyāpahārajaḥ śāmyetsamyagunmūlite ripau  |
manyurdehāvadhirayaṃ tātamitravipattibhūḥ  || 1.76 ||

nabhastalātkhagapatau patati cchinnapakṣatau  |
ko'parastāṃ mama suhṛnmā bhaiṣīrityabhāṣata  || 1.77 ||

syādvatsa rāmahatakātklībaḥ kaḥ puruṣo'paraḥ  |
ekapatnīmanugatāmarāteryo na rakṣati  || 1.78 ||

tayā teṣu tapasvinyā sīdatorāvayorapi  |
daṇḍakāraṇyakaṣṭeṣu mukhaṃ na vidhurīkṛtam  || 1.79 ||

videharājasya sutā snuṣā daśarathasya sā  |
hatarāmasya jāyeti bhramati sma vane vane  || 1.80 ||

svamapyapuṣṇatā kāyamapi jāyāmarakṣatā  |
mayā samaḥ śrutaḥ ko'pi jātaḥ klībo raghoḥ kule  || 1.81 ||

tvaṃ kevalaṃ na subhrātā tathā vilapatā muhuḥ  |
tiryañco'pi mayāmuṣmin vane lakṣmaṇa roditāḥ  || 1.82 ||

dikṣu sannaddhameghāsu mama ghorādhisākṣiṇām  |
avahannirjharanibhānnagānāmaśrunimnagāḥ  || 1.83 ||

ārādākarṇitasnigdhanavābdaninadairapi  |
matkāruṇyādaraṇye'pi nānṛtyata kalāpibhiḥ  || 1.84 ||

satyameṣyati sugrīvaḥ satyaṃ jīvati jānakī  |
tvadvaco na viparyeti bhrātarbrūhi punaḥ punaḥ  || 1.85 ||

tvadvākyaśīkarairebhirniruddhabahirudgamaḥ  |
mamāntarleḍhi marmāṇi sītāvirahamurmuraḥ  || 1.86 ||

tasmādapi dahatyuccairayaṃ māmaparaḥ śikhī  |
yadvanaukasi nirvaire mukto vālini mārgaṇaḥ  || 1.87 ||

gurvī punaśca lajjeyamuttamarṇairivādhunā  |
mārgitavyo yadasmābhiḥ plavagāḥ pratyupakriyām  || 1.88 ||

nāsti pratyupakārāśā tatra naścapale kapau  |
yenātilaghunottuṅgo laṅghitaḥ satyapādapaḥ  || 1.89 ||

uttiṣṭha vatsa gacchāvaḥ sādhayāmo'nyato'dhunā  |
navaiśvaryasukhavyagraḥ sugrīvo nāgamiṣyati  || 1.90 ||

tapasvī ramatāmevaṃ cirāddāraiḥ samāgataḥ  |
sa pīḍyamānaḥ praṇayādvirasaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati  || 1.91 ||

kiṃ ca vatsa daśāsāmyājjātāḥ smaḥ suhṛdaḥ purā  |
tasyādhunā tu vistīrṇavibhavāndhasya ke vayam  || 1.92 ||

gṛhītasādhuvartmāpi sadyaḥ samprāpya sampadam  |
patati skhalito'tīva pramattaḥ prahate pathi  || 1.93 ||

sa vatsa viralo janturvipatkālapratiśrutam  |
sadyaḥ sampanmado yasya na vilumpati cetasi  || 1.94 ||

astu sādhurasādhurvā na vicāryaḥ sa me'dhunā  |
vatsa notsahate cetastatra dogdhumupakriyām  || 1.95 ||

asāvanāgataḥ śreyānāgacchan vatsa vāryatām  |
praveśayanti suhṛdaṃ na dhīrāḥ svārthasaṃkaṭe  || 1.96 ||

kartavyopakṛtiḥ sadbhiryatra tatra vipadgate  |
avimṛśya kṛto'smābhiḥ sa punaḥ kṛpaṇaḥ paṇaḥ  || 1.97 ||

kiṃ kṣiptamacalaprāntādantaḥsuptaśivāśatam  |
nirdeśapuruṣeṇeva kaṅkālaṃ dundubhermayā  || 1.98 ||

kasyārthasya kṛte viddhāḥ sapta sālāstapasvinaḥ  |
sa mā pratyetu vaidheyaḥ kiṃ na sādhitamanyataḥ  || 1.99 ||

akṣātraṃ kṣatriyayuvā ko nu rāma iva kṣipet |
viśikhaṃ vālini vyagre sugrīvasya paśoḥ kṛte  || 1.100 ||

na parityajyate mārgastāvannirvācyakardamaḥ  |
yāvatpihitakaṣṭāya kāryāyātmā na dīyate  || 1.101 ||

iṣvaikayā mayā viddho bāliśena valīmukhaḥ  |
anuddhārāḥ śarāstena ropitā mayi vāṅmayāḥ  || 1.102 ||

anavāptavipatpāraḥ smariṣyāmi kiyanti vā  |
vatsa kālocitaṃ śādhi na rāmaḥ kiṃcidīkṣate  || 1.103 ||

avalambasva māṃ bhrātaḥ sītāsmaraṇanissaham  |
dūraṃ punaḥ prabhavatā mano mohena lupyate  || 1.104 ||

sanāthobhayapārśvasya tvayā vatsa tayā ca me  |
vaneṣu ca sataḥ kaṣṭamajaniṣṭa na kiṃcana  || 1.105 ||

hā bhīru hā priyatame hā madvirahakātare  |
kvāsītyardhoktinissaṃjño jagāma sa mahītalam  || 1.106 ||

mātaḥ kaikeyi tuṣṭāsi bhuṅkṣva rājyamakaṇṭakam  |
vadannityapatattasya lakṣmaṇaścaraṇābjayoḥ  || 1.107 ||

tatastau sāśrunayanau mṛgairvidhuravīkṣitau  |
nipetaturdiva iva bhraṣṭau vidhuravī kṣitau  || 1.108 ||

iti nipatitayostayordvayordaśarathanandanayormahītale  |
daśabhirapi digaṅganāmukhairadhṛtivaśādiva tatyaje ruciḥ  || 1.109 ||

ete nikāmarasikasya jayanti pādāḥ śrīhāravarṣayuvarājamahītalendoḥ  |
yairdvādaśārkakiraṇotkaradurnivāraḥ sṛṣṭo'bhinandakumudasya mahāvikāsaḥ  || 1.110 ||

ityabhinandakṛtau rāmacarite mahākāvye prathamaḥ sargaḥ samāptaḥ  ||

dvitīyaḥ sargaḥ

tāmanīkṣitacarīmadhīratāmātmano'tha viniyamya lakṣmaṇaḥ  |
sāntvanārthamiti vācamabravīttīvramanyurayamūḍhamagrajam  || 2.1 ||

kāryamārya pariśiṣṭamucyatāṃ mucyatāmidamayogajaṃ tamaḥ  |
rājatūdyamadivākarodayasmeramāśu mukhapaṅkajaṃ tava  || 2.2 ||

āsituṃ samaya eṣa nāvayormohamitthamavalambituṃ na ca  |
utsahasva nanu hṛṣyatāṃ cirādiṣvadhīnamadhunābhikāṅkṣitam  || 2.3 ||

kevalaṃ phalabhujo na tasya nastaskarasya nilaye'sti niścayaḥ  |
abhyupaitu ravinandanaḥ kṣaṇādandhakāramidamapyapaiṣyati  || 2.4 ||

aurasānapi sutānarīniva ghnanti kāraṇavaśānmahībhujaḥ  |
nigrahādanujavidviṣaḥ kapeḥ kiṃ pṛthagjana ivānutapyase  || 2.5 ||

krīḍatāṃ mṛgavaneṣu dhanvināṃ jīvakoṭikadane'pi kā kṣatiḥ  |
khidyase kimaniśaṃ prayojanādekavānaravadhaḥ kutaḥ kṛtaḥ  || 2.6 ||

tasya ca pravayaso jaṭāyuṣaḥ svargiṇaḥ kimadhunānuśocyate  |
yena jarjarakalevaravyayātkrītamindukiraṇojjvalaṃ yaśaḥ  || 2.7 ||

etadekamativāhyatāmahaḥ śvo na sa plavagarāṭpratīkṣyate  |
tatprasīda parito vicīyatātmanaiva sanagārṇavāvaniḥ  || 2.8 ||

tatkabandhaśabarīhanūmatāmāvayorajani saṅgataṃ yathā  |
iṣṭaśaṃsi vanaśailapānthayoḥ kiṃ tathānyadapi nāpatetpunaḥ  || 2.9 ||

yatra yatra tava siddhiragrataḥ kaḥ kariṣyati na te samīhitam  |
ujjvale kṛtavidāṃ na kevalaṃ tena vartmani durātmanā sthitam  || 2.10 ||

sambhavatyabhimaraḥ pure'thavā tasya tīvramayaśaḥ kimucyate  |
duḥkhamutkhanati kaṇṭakāvalīraṅgasandhiṣu navaḥ kileśvaraḥ  || 2.11 ||

samprati prathamamārya gṛhyatāṃ tatpravṛttimupalabdhumudyamaḥ  |
marṣaṇīyaparilambadūṣaṇaḥ so'dhunāpyakapaṭo ghaṭate cet || 2.12 ||

muñca mohamavatīryatāmitaḥ sevyatāmiyamanāvilā nadī  |
eṣa gacchati javājjapāruṇo vāruṇīmaruṇasārathirdiśam  || 2.13 ||

unmimīla raghunandanaścirāttanniśamya vacanaṃ camatkṛtaḥ  |
apyayogavidhurā na śerate saṃmukhīnavidhayastathāvidhāḥ  || 2.14 ||

samrāṭpadasthasya padādapetāḥ samprītaye samprati mā sma bhūmaḥ  |
dṛṣṭāstrasārā api tasya bhīroḥ kiṃ bhītaye vatsa vayaṃ na bhūtāḥ  || 3.12 ||

sthāne nirastaḥ sudhiyāgrajena sa tena māyāmasṛṇo durātmā  |
mayā madāndhena punarvṛthaiva kroṣṭā mṛgendrasya pade'bhiṣiktaḥ  || 3.13 ||

nirveśaniryātanaśāṭhyanidrāṃ nīceṣu daṇḍaḥ paruṣo nihanti  |
tathāpi dākṣiṇyavilambitavyamidaṃ dhanustaṃ prati māmakīnam  || 3.14 ||

yāvanna viśrāmyati vegajanmā dikṣu dhvanirvālibhidaḥ śarasya  |
tāvatpunastaṅka iha trilokyāmākrāntakiṣkindhamupakrameta  || 3.15 ||

asmāsu nāvedayati sma garvātkurvīta śeṣārivadhaṃ bhujābhyām  |
vidhāya saurājyamasaūpeyādityarthitā paśyati na pramāṇam  || 3.16 ||

tadeṣa me nirmalasargasargaḥ ṣaḍvargaruddho ripuvatsa heyaḥ  |
nāsau niṣiddhastava yānapakṣo mayā bata prāgasamāhitena  || 3.17 ||

asaṃśayaṃ sa pratihārapālīsasambhramāveditasannikarṣam  |
tvāṃ pratyasūyāṃ hṛdayena dhāsyatyupasthitāntaḥpuraviprayogaḥ  || 3.18 ||

tadvaktramārkestaruṇārkatāmraṃ tvatprītivākyairakṛtapramodam  |
bhaviṣyati pratyupakārabhāranirvāhacintāgamasāndhakāram  || 3.19 ||

smariṣyati tvāṃ na sa bodhyamāno'pyālokayiṣyatyathavā na pṛṣṭaḥ  |
pratyeṣi kāntājanamadhyavartī sa cintayāsmadgatayādhunāste  || 3.20 ||

āvāṃ yadaivādritaṭe niviṣṭau sa kālikāṃ vīkṣya purīṃ praviṣṭaḥ  |
āmṛṣṭamāsīccapalena tena sauhārdamasmadviṣayaṃ tadaiva  || 3.21 ||

ahaḥsvamīṣu kṣaṇa eva nāsīdāsīnna saṃdeśaharo'nurūpaḥ  |
ityādi sa vyaktamalīkameva tvatprītyupālambhapadeṣu vaktā  || 3.22 ||

kṛtāvahelaḥ praṇayaprasannaṃ niruttaraḥ satvarakāryavādam  |
prekṣiṣyate roṣalavānuraktaṃ valīmukhaḥ śuṣkamukho mukhaṃ te  || 3.23 ||

tasyāgamādyaḥ sahasā bhaviṣyatyarthasya tasyābhavanaṃ mamāstu  |
bādhiṣyate vālivadhātiriktastadveśmayātrānuśayaḥ sadā nau  || 3.24 ||

sa prāktano nūnamanarthabhājā nītastalaṃ tena hitaiṣivargaḥ  |
utthāpitairdūramasāvidānīṃ divāniśaṃ dīvyati durvidagdhaiḥ  || 3.25 ||

akṣā mṛgākṣyo mṛgayā madhūni prekṣāśca tasyādya haranti kālam  |
na prāpnuvanti kṣaṇamāptavācaḥ sadādṛtāścāraṇacāṭuvādāḥ  || 3.26 ||

vadanti bhītāḥ sacivā na kiṃcitkiṃ cintayāsmadgatayā viṭānām  |
svayaṃ na sampratyanusaṃdadhāti pūrvāparaṃ kāmaparaḥ sa rājā  || 3.27 ||

na sūrayo na sthavirā na dhīrāḥ puraḥsthitāḥ prāśnikatāṃ prayānti  |
saṃtiṣṭhate saṃsadi saṃśayānaḥ pṛṣṭhopaviṣṭāsu vilāsinīṣu  || 3.28 ||

itthaṃgate tatra gatasya kena manyustavodīrṇarayo nivāryaḥ  |
vicāyamāṇo na guṇāya naḥ syādasau kapīnāṃ kadanānubandhaḥ  || 3.29 ||

prapannaśāṭhyasya haṭhopanāmādāneṣyamāṇasya guṇaṃ na vīkṣe  |
tathāvidhā hi dviṣatāṃ stavena kaṣanti karṇau paramantikasthāḥ  || 3.30 ||

spṛṣ.o na cedvikriyayā kayāpi nopasthitaḥ kāryavaśādidānīm  |
etu svayaṃ na kriyate vṛthaiva sa tvadbalātkāraniruddhaśobhaḥ  || 3.31 ||

yathāvayostātajaṭāyuṣoktamuktaṃ kabandhena yathā ca tena  |
tathopayāsyatyacirādavaśyamāhūtacakro haricakravartī  || 3.32 ||

yatsatyamādityasamudbhavatvādudbhūtimeṣyanti guṇāstadīyāḥ  |
tulāgrasampātavilambadoṣe tasmin visaṃvādamahaṃ na śaṅke  || 3.33 ||

viropataptasya videśavāsādājagmuṣastasya nijādhivāsam  |
iyantyahānyutsukabandhuvargasaṃvargaṇavyagratayaiva yānti  || 3.34 ||

sa nūnamāyāsyati vānarendraḥ saṃdeśakārpaṇyamasaṃmataṃ me  |
sarvasvabhūtaṃ visṛjanti vatsa kāryātipāte'pi na dhairyamāryāḥ  || 3.35 ||

vyatītavighnaḥ suhṛdarthanighno na śītalaḥ sthāsyati kiṃ gatena  |
sa tvaryamāṇastvaramāṇa eva dhairyasya naḥ pāramavāpsyatīva  || 3.36 ||

dhigastu māṃ taṃ prati vipratīpamāśaṅkitaṃ tadbahu yena yadvā  |
svakāryadolāsu jano'lpabhāgyaḥ prāṅniścinoti vyatirekameva  || 3.37 ||

itthaṃ na māṃ pratyupapattimeti tatrādyapakṣadvitaye'pi yātrā  |
varaṃ visoḍhāni kiyantyapīha saṅkhyātakālābhyadhikānyahāni  || 3.38 ||

svālocitaṃ vaktu bhavānidānīmityanuṣṭheyamasaṃśayaṃ yat |
bālyātprabhṛtyeva tavottarā dhīrna pūrvapakṣe'bhiniveśameti  || 3.39 ||

iti prasannāṃ giramagrajasya śrutvā cirādvītaviṣādapaṅkaḥ  |
mukhaśriyaivojjvalayābhinandannidaṃ sumitrātanayo jagāda  || 3.40 ||

samarthitaṃ sādhu samastameva kṛtī kapiḥ kiṃ bahunoditena  |
pravyaktamapyetadadoṣadṛṣṭyā gṛhītamāryeṇa na yattadāgaḥ  || 3.41 ||

tasyorjitenātiraveravadyaṃ naivekṣate'ntaḥkaraṇaṃ mamāpi  |
ayaṃ punastena kuto na jānāmyatikramaḥ kālakṛto na dṛṣṭaḥ  || 3.42 ||

visarjitā vā punarāgamāya samāpatantyadya na te nalādyāḥ  |
samāharatiudyatavittavarṣo varṣāntarebhyaḥ pravarān harīn vā  || 3.43 ||

aṣṭādaśadvīpakapīndramaulirmaulānasau vālivadhapralīnān  |
naikābhyupāyopahitānidānīmuta pratīcchatyatisatkriyābhiḥ  || 3.44 ||

aprāptasaṃtoṣamasaṃvibhaktasambhuktasarvasvamanuplaveṣu  |
athādiśatyarpitasārakośaḥ prakāmapātheyaparigrahāya  || 3.45 ||

atho sa bhītāturabālavṛddhān vṛddhopasevī sanibhaṃ niyuṅkte  |
yathāyathaṃ sadmani padmasaṃkhyāsametaśākhāmṛgayūthanāthaḥ  || 3.46 ||

taditthamutthānasamākulatvānna tvaryamāṇo'pi sa dūṣaṇāya  |
āryeṇa tatrāpyasamāgatasya chāyācyutiḥ kaiva nirūpitā me  || 3.47 ||

jñeyo'sti naḥ pūrvamarerudantastatastadantāya vidheyamasti  |
ityākulatvānmama tāvadārya kāryopataptiḥ kṣamate na dhairyam  || 3.48 ||

anarthibhāvādupacāravādādākāragupteśca parībhavanti  |
yathātathārthapratipādanena prayānti mitrāṇi nirantaratvam  || 3.49 ||

mithaḥ prasādaskhalanādupādherupaiti sakhyaṃ khalayoḥ khilatvam  |
gṛhṇāti satpūruṣayorna doṣamaudāryasaṃvādakṛtā tu maitrī  || 3.50 ||

niryantraṇaṃ yatra na vartitavyaṃ na moditavyaṃ praṇayātivāde  |
viśaṅkitānyonyabhayaṃ vidūrānnamaskriyāmarhati sauhṛdaṃ tat || 3.51 ||

visrabdhamājñāpaya kiṃ karomi kathaṃ ghaṭena plavagādhirājaḥ  |
yatkiṃcidādiśya nibhena kiṃ māṃ niṣedhasi kleśamivekṣamāṇaḥ  || 3.52 ||

sthito'smi tāvaddinametadeṣyatyanyedyurājñāṃ tava na pratīkṣe  |
āryo'nurodhānna yunakti yatra tatrāhamambāniyamātsvatantraḥ  || 3.53 ||

tadeṣa niṣkramya tathā karomi tatpūrvasaṅgaḥ plavago yathāśu  |
taṃ vairisaṃhāramahopakāramāryāya niryātayituṃ yatena  || 3.54 ||

dhiktaṃ dhanuṣpāṇimanuplavaṃ māṃ mudhā manaḥparyuṣitāvalepam  |
anvīkṣate yasya mamāvasādādāryaḥ sahāyāntaramantarajñaḥ  || 3.55 ||

dhikśātatāṃ sannataparvatāṃ dhigdhikpātavegaṃ ca mamāśugānām  |
alakṣitoddeśamapi dviṣantaṃ nihatya nāyānti hi yatkṣaṇena  || 3.56 ||

kharādirakṣaḥkadaneṣu dīrghamamī parijñātarasāḥ kimanyat |
asṛgbhujāṃ bhoktumasṛktvarantāṃ bhūyo'pi bhūcandra tavārdhacandrāḥ  || 3.57 ||

ātāṭakāsaṃjñapanātprapannastvayaiṣa varṇāśramarakṣaṇāya  |
samāpyatāṃ śeṣanibarhaṇena kṣapācarāṇāṃ nidhanādhikāraḥ  || 3.58 ||

uddhṛtya ghorāyudhavṛndavṛṣṭimanuṣṭhitāmoghaśaravyayena  |
sa rākṣasebhyo'mbaragocarebhyastrātastvayaikena makho maharṣeḥ  || 3.59 ||

taṃ kālakalpaṃ kiyatā śrameṇa tathāvadhīrmārgarudhaṃ virādham  |
āsanmuhūrtāḥ kati ca ghnataste sainyaṃ janasthānapateḥ svarasya  || 3.60 ||

āsītkṣuraprasya kiyān vilambaḥ krūreṣu teṣu triśiraḥśiraḥsu  |
katīṣavo dūṣaṇadehayātrānivāraṇārthaṃ ca raṇe visṛṣṭāḥ  || 3.61 ||

kasyāvalambādakarotparāsuṃ mārīcamuccāvacamāyamāryaḥ  |
jñātā ca diksaṃjñapitastvayaiva kṣapācarāṇāmadhipaśca so'pi  || 3.62 ||

ityaprapañcoditakāraṇāni mithastayostathyaguṇagrahāṇi  |
neduḥ stuvānā iva sūnṛtāni khagāḥ kulāyeṣu kṛtaikamatyāḥ  || 3.63 ||

āropya sandhyāṃ diśi vajrapāṇeḥ paryasya candraṃ kakubhi pratīcyām  |
utsaṅgitoḍuprakarā khagānāmādāya nidrāṃ rajanī jagāma  || 3.64 ||

kaviśca jīvaśca sutaśca bhūmeḥ sa dakṣiṇāśātilako muniśca  |
āsannasūryāṃśubhayadrutasya kṣaṇaṃ rarakṣurbhagaṇasya pṛṣṭham  || 3.65 ||

digdarśayāmāsa mukhaṃ sarāgaṃ na yāvadaindrī gaṇikeva mattā  |
tāvaddvijeśo'ṅkamiyāya sindhoḥ sandhyāsurāśīkarasekaśaṅkī  || 3.66 ||

visarjitoḍuḥ kṣaṇamindurāsīdāsīdupānteṣvapi nāndhakāram  |
bhṛśaṃ diśīndrasya rarāja sandhyā sindūriṇīva graharājamudrā  || 3.67 ||

uccetumuccairuḍukuḍmalāni vyākurvatī rāgamatipragāḍham  |
śākhāmivālambya diśaṃ maghonaḥ sandhyā viyadbhūruhamāruroha  || 3.68 ||

śeṣoḍupuṣpastabakoccayāya saṃjātarāgātiśayeva sandhyā  |
apyuccakaiḥ pūrvamahīdhramūrdhni sthitvā viyadbhūruhamāruroha  || 3.69 ||
(tadevedam)

visṛjya pāṇḍucchadanaughatulyaṃ tārāgaṇaṃ vyomatarustadānīm  |
babhāra sandhyārucimañjarīṇāṃ jālāni bālādharakomalāni  || 3.70 ||

utsāritevābhrakareṇukumbhājjambhāriyugyādhikṛtairmarudbhiḥ  |
tatāna sandhyeti samullasantī nabhoṅgaṇaṃ raṅgavikāradhūliḥ  || 3.71 ||

saṃvittayastattvavigāhaśubhrāḥ sāṃsārike rāga ivādhirūḍhe  |
śanaiḥ śanairmlānaruco mamajjustārāḥ prasarpatyaruṇe cireṇa  || 3.72 ||

kaiḥ śikṣitā vartayituṃ tadāsīdunmīlitaṃ tūlikayeva citram  |
taraṅgavattuṅgaśaradghanālīvisaṃsthule vyomani bālasandhyā  || 3.73 ||

pratyuptamātraṃ diśi devabharturabhyaktakhaṇḍābhrataraṅgamālam  |
babhau visarpatyayasīva tailaṃ nabhastale bālapataṅgatejaḥ  || 3.74 ||

prasattinirvyūḍhasudhāmayūkhaṃ khamuṣṇaraśmāvapi raktimūhe  |
bharaṃ pratīcchanti mahodayānāṃ pātrāṇi paryāyavaśaṃvadāni  || 3.75 ||

tāpiñchakalpaḥ kakubhi pratīcyāmācchādayāmāsa mṛgo mṛgāṅkam  |
dūrādhirūḍhaṃ janamāpadīva prakāśamāno'tiśayādavarṇaḥ  || 3.76 ||

pradīptasandhyāgni nadaddvijaughaṃ niraṃśukadhvastaparāṅmukhendu  |
viyadviluptoḍukulaṃ tadābhūccakrāntarākrāntapuropameyam  || 3.77 ||

mithaḥ kṛtālokamahotsavānāṃ kṛtāravaḥ sārasadampatīnām  |
na bālabhānoḥ kiraṇādabhaiṣīnnadīṣu nīhāramayo'ndhakāraḥ  || 3.78 ||

tatāra saṃtīrya himāndhakāraṃ tatā rasantī saritaḥ khagālī  |
raktāravindasya rajobhirārdrairaktā raviṃ vāriṇi vīkṣamāṇā  || 3.79 ||

smaravyayaklāntavalatpulindīsarvāṅgasaṃvāhakalāvidagdhaḥ  |
vilambya kośeṣu kuśeśayānāṃ sasāra kāsārataraṅgavātaḥ  || 3.80 ||

atītya śaityaṃ taṭavālukānāṃ koṣṇāsu sasnurmunayo nadīṣu  |
apāmapūryanta ca nairjharīṇāṃ kuṭā girigrāmakuṭumbinībhiḥ  || 3.81 ||

jātaprakāśāsu bahiḥsthalīṣu prakāmacārotsavajāgarūkaḥ  |
ibho bhuvi praskhaladagrahastaḥ sasmāra śayyodarakuñjaśayyām  || 3.82 ||

sametya jṛmbhānupadaṃ svayūthyairmithaḥ kṣaṇaṃ līḍhatuṣāralepāḥ  |
pratasthire digvalayaṃ vilokya yathāyathaṃ kacchabhuvaḥ kuraṅgāḥ  || 3.83 ||

adṛśyatārāttaralaiḥ sthalīṣu vanecarāṇāṃ pṛtanā kuraṅgaiḥ  |
papau raveḥ saṃjvalitograbhāsaṃ bhujaṅgamaughaḥ śritanākurandhraiḥ  || 3.84 ||

sthalaṃ kuraṅgīnayanairanidrairunmuktamudrairjalajairjalaṃ ca  |
vilolatāraistaraladvirephairuvāha bhedaṃ na mithastadānīm  || 3.85 ||

jṛmbhābharāddūravisaṃgatānāṃ mudrāvilambena dalāvalīnām  |
nirviśya niḥśeṣamaliḥ parāgamagādabaddhaḥ kumudākarebhyaḥ  || 3.86 ||

viveśa gartaṃ gatamuttamohiruccaiḥ padaṃ santatamuttamo hi  |
āsīnmahaḥ sampadi tuṅgatāyāṃ nabho na kaiścidgadituṃ gatāyām  || 3.87 ||

naiḥsaṅgyamālambyata kairaveṣu yaiḥ śāntimadbhiḥ śanakaī raveṣu  |
saujanyamambhojagṛhe sametaiścirāddvirephairjagṛhe same taiḥ  || 3.88 ||

reje'ravindairaravindabandhoḥ saṃdarśane darśitaharṣavegaiḥ  |
vidhorviṣehe virahaḥ kathaṃcinnidrāvalambātkumudākareṇa  || 3.89 ||

ūhe jalairāhitakoparāgaḥ kṣobhādalnīnāṃ nalinīparāgaḥ  |
vyasarjayaddyāṃ vivṛtāparāgaḥ śaśī cirādadhyuṣitāparāgaḥ  || 3.90 ||

yathā viluptoḍukadambakāyāṃ bālārkapādairdivi dīpyate sma  |
tathaiva suptākhilakairavāyāṃ raktāravindairabhitaḥ sarasyām  || 3.91 ||

mukhena bhāti sma sahasrapatraṃ mitrodayālokavikasvareṇa  |
mañjusvanānāmalimārgaṇānāṃ svayaṃgrahāyaiavvimudrakośam  || 3.92 ||

ślathena kecidviviśurmukhena talena keciddalasaṃhatīnām  |
madhūtsukānāṃ madhupavrajānāmabjeṣu na dvāraviniścayo'bhūt || 3.93 ||

luloṭha dhūlīṣu papau madhūni pakṣmāṇi līḍhāni punarlileha  |
āgantave stokamapi dvirephaḥ sthito dadau nāntaramabjamadhye  || 3.94 ||

bheje vinidrāṃ nalinīṃ na kūlānna śīlayāmāsa navārkabhāsam  |
priyāṃ pratīyāya tathaiva mūḍhāṃ khagaḥ svabodhāgamasābhyasūyaḥ  || 3.95 ||

bahūnasau patrarathastapasvī cakāra cāṭūnupakaṇṭhalīnaḥ  |
dṛśaṃ pramīlāpagamātkaṣāyāmunmīlayāmāsa cireṇa cakrī  || 3.96 ||

pāraṃ tuṣārakṣaṇasāndhakāramapi smarārtitvaritaḥ pavitrī  |
śrutapriyāhnvānaravaḥ pratasthe sīmantayannabjavanaṃ vinidram  || 3.97 ||

jagāma bhañjankamalāni kokaḥ kokīpariṣvaṅgamanorathotkaḥ  |
sāpyutsukā tatkṣaṇamājagāma vilokayantī vanamutpalānām  || 3.98 ||

kurvāṇayoḥ sarasi padmavanāvanaddhe bhinnādhvanorvitathameva gatāgatāni  |
saṃvādidikpatitayoḥ patatoḥ kathaṃciddvandvaṃ cirādghaṭitamardhapathe babhūva  || 3.99 ||

vyasmaryatāmṛtamarīcikarābhitāpaḥ prātaḥ patatrimithunaistapanaṃ vilokya  |
jātyaiva śītalamathoṣṇamalaṃ na kiṃciccitrā gatirjagati jantudaśāvaśena  || 3.100 ||

kartavyanirṇayavirūḍhamanaḥprasādaśaṃsismitena mukhacandramasā virājan  |
rāmaḥ prabhātapavanāhitavīcikampāṃ pampāmiyāya sarasīṃ saha lakṣmaṇena  || 3.101 ||

pārśvasthāvarajasasambhramopanītapratyagrasphuṭitavilohitāravindaḥ  |
susnātaḥ kṛtavidhirañjasopatasthe kākutsthaḥ kanakarasāvadātamarkam  || 3.102 ||

tau dīrghaṃ vidhivadupāsya pūrvasandhyāṃ pūṣṇīṣatprakaṭitaraśmibarbarīke  |
āsātāṃ rahitamahīruhopakaṇṭhaūdvellastimitajaṭāvanau vanānte  || 3.103 ||
