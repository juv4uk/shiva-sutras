ādyanātha anuttaraprakāśapañcāśikā


oṃ namo'nuttaraprakāśaśaṅkarāya

akṛtrimāhamāmarśa- prakāśaikaghanaḥ śivaḥ  |
śaktyā vimarśavapuṣā svātmano'nanyarūpayā  || 1 ||

śivādikṣitiparyantaṃ viśvaṃ vapurudañcayan  |
pañcakṛtyamahānāṭya- rasikaḥ krīḍati prabhuḥ  || 2 ||

pṛthagarthamarthavattā viśvaviśvaśarīrayoḥ  |
na viśvaviśvavapuṣorbhinnatā kāpi tāttvikī  || 3 ||

bhede sattāsphurattābhyāṃ bhinnaṃ kiṃ tu jagadbhavet |
sattāsphurattāsaṃbandhātsattābhānaṃ ca tanna cet || 4 ||

asataḥ kiṃ satastābhyāṃ saṃbandhaḥ so'yamiṣyate  |
svarūpalābhasulabhaḥ saṃbandho na hyavastunaḥ  || 5 ||

na labdharūpasaṃbandhe tābhyāṃ kiṃ cidupekṣyate  |
upekṣāyāmavasthānaṃ na syādetasya kiṃ cana  || 6 ||

svabhāvataḥ sphurattā ca sattā ca na vināśinī  |
vināśānabhupagame jaḍatāpi nivartate  || 7 ||

tato gatyantarābhāvāccideva pariśiṣyate  |
sataścidaikyaṃ prakṛtaṃ kadā cinna nivartate  || 8 ||

prakāśe'nanyato bhāvaḥ svātantryollāsakevalaḥ  |
parichinnātmikā śaktiḥ śambhorviśvātiśāyinaḥ  || 9 ||

yatprakāśātmakaṃ sarvaṃ tamaḥkevalatāṃ gatam  |
yacca kiṃ cijjagadetatprakāśādatiricyate  || 10 ||

vimṛśyasaraṇīṃ prāptamityeṣā tāttvikī matiḥ  |
avabhāsaikatānānāṃ matirekātra sākṣiṇī  || 11 ||

kiṃ pramāṇāirvarākaistaiścidānuprāṇitātmabhiḥ  |
na hi vaikartanaṃ jyotirdīpālokamapekṣate  || 12 ||

śaktipātapavitre'smin dhītattve ca parīkṣyatām  |
ādarśavimalābhoge na tu sarvaṃ prakāśate  || 13 ||

itthaṃ cidātmakaṃ sarvaṃ ṣaṭtriṃśadbhedatattvataḥ  |
ādau śuddhātmakaṃ tattvaṃ pañcadhātamasaḥ param  || 14 ||

śivaḥ śaktiśca sādākhyamīśo vidyeti bhidyate  |
akṣādiśāntavarṇātmā- niramāyi śivena tu  || 15 ||

kalāvidyārāgakāla- niyatirbandha ucyate  |
māyāpūrvo vakārādi- kṣakārāntākṣarātmakaḥ  || 16 ||

pumāñśaktirmano buddhirahaṃkārādipañcakam  |
śrotrādipañcakaṃ tādi ṭādi vāgādipañcakam  || 17 ||

tanmātrapañcakaṃ cādi kādi vyomādipañcakam  |
sisṛkṣoḥ prathamaspandaḥ śivatattvaṃ vibhoḥ smṛtam  || 18 ||

icchaiva sāparimlānā śaktitattvamudañcayan  |
svecchayāsūcitaṃ viśvamācchādyāhantayā sthitam  || 19 ||

sa eva tattvaṃ sādākhyaṃ sarvānugrahaṇodyatam  |
sa eveśvaratattvaṃ syātpaśyan viśvamidantayā  || 20 ||

idantāhantayoraikyamiti vidyā nigadyate  |
svāṅgakalpeṣu bhāveṣu māyātattvaṃ vibhedadhīḥ  || 21 ||

māyāgṛhītasaṃkocaḥ śivaḥ puṃtattvamucyate  |
ayameva hi saṃsārī jīvo bhoktaiva dṛśyate  || 22 ||

jñatvakartṛtvapūrṇatva- nityatvānyasya śaktayaḥ  |
tatsaṃkocātsaṃkucitaḥ kalādyātmatayā mataḥ  || 23 ||

māyātmakaṃ kalā nāma kiṃcitkartṛtvakāraṇam  |
kālaḥ paricchedakaro niyatiścedameva me  || 24 ||

kartavyaṃ nānyadityeṣā vyavasthā yantraṇākṛitiḥ  |
prakṛtirguṇasāmyaṃ syādahaṃkārādijanmabhūḥ  || 25 ||

ahaṃ mamedamityetadbuddhiheturahaṃkṛtiḥ  |
buddhiradhyavasāyasya kāraṇaṃ niścayātmanaḥ  || 26 ||

saṃkalpasya vikalpasya bījaṃ mana udīryate  |
vacanādeśca śabdādervāgādiśravaṇādikam  || 27 ||

kāraṇaṃ śravaṇādīnāṃ grāhyaṃ tanmātrapañcakam  |
ākāśādyavakāśādi- kāraṇaṃ bhūtapañcakam  || 28 ||

parāparāśaktimaye śuddhe vidyādipañcakam  |
tadanyadaparāśaktirityetattattvamīritam  || 29 ||

iyaṃ devī parā śaktiḥ śuddhāśuddhādhvagarbhiṇī  |
pṛthivyādīni tattvāni yadālīnāni kāraṇe  || 30 ||

tadā kāraṇamātrāṇi bahirudvamate vibhuḥ  |
anuttarecche unmeṣe ānandeśanamūnatā(?)  || 31 ||

kriyecchājñānaśaktīnāṃ sattā ṣaṭ         *  |
iccheśanāntarārūḍhā sphuṭāsphuṭajaganmayī  || 32 ||
*comm  anuttaretyāditripadyeṣvarthāsaṃgatyā pāṭhāśuddhiḥ pratibhāti

catvāraḥ parato varṇāḥ ṣaṇḍātmānaḥ pracoditāḥ  |
anuttarānandaśāktistrikoṇādvṛttiyogataḥ  || 33 ||

tathaivoneṣayogena kriyāśakteḥ sphuṭaṃ vapuḥ  |
uktaṃ triśaktisaṃghaṭṭāttriśūlaṃ dvaitaghasmaram  || 34 ||

parasparavirodhe tu kāryeṣu pravirohati  |
na kathaṃcidupādeyamāsāṃ rūpamidaṃ bhavet || 35 ||

bindurvedyasya saṃskāro vimarśaḥ sarga ityasau  |
kalāṣoḍaśakākārā śaktirvijayate parā  || 36 ||

tithayaḥ pratipatpūrvāḥ pañcadaśeti māyayā  |
sūryācandramasau svāntaś- carantau sthitihetave  || 37 ||

yathāvimarśavapuṣaḥ sargasyādyāḥ kalāḥ smṛtāḥ  |
dvidheyaṃ mātṛkā devī bījayonyātmanā sthitā  || 38 ||

nityapravṛttaśṛṅgāṭa- vapurviśvasya janmabhūḥ  |
hṛdayaṃ bījametasyāṃ sāraṃ yattatparaṃ mahaḥ  || 39 ||

vaṭabīje yathā vṛkṣastathātra nihitaṃ jagat |
vicāryamāṇe naivedaṃ kāraṇādatiricyate  || 40 ||

mṛdādeḥ kalaśādīnāṃ tattvaṃ nānyannirūpaṇe  |
ityāhustattvavādinyaḥ śrutīnāmantimā giraḥ  || 41 ||

idaṃ sarvaṃ sadevāsīdagre iti viniścayāt |
sattāvācini bīje'smin bhāti māyā tvidaṃ jagat || 42 ||

viluptapratyayākārametatsa pariśiṣyate  |
tato jñānakriyāsāra- vidyeśvarasadāśivāḥ  || 43 ||

śaktitriśūle līyante caturdaśakalātmani  |
ūrdhvādhaḥ sṛṣṭivapuṣi sarvaṃ līnamataḥ [param]  || 44 ||

itthaṃ parasyāṃ saṃvittau sarvaṃ saṃkucitaṃ kramāt |
athavā manasātīte yatra kvāpi nirañjane  || 45 ||

ṣaṭtriṃśattattvalaharī- kalahātītagocare  |
viśvātmani mahāmantre svabhāve sā vilīyate  || 46 ||

kṛtaścainmathite dhāmni dīpte kenāpi hetunā  |
sarvaṃ haviridaṃ juhvanna dāridryeṇa pīḍyate  || 47 ||

pañcapañcātmakaṃ viśvaṃ pañcaspandavijṛmbhitam  |
saṃkocayatparāmarśātsāmānyaspandakevalam  || 48 ||

ahami pralayaṃ kurvannidamaḥ pratiyoginaḥ  |
parākramaparo bhuṅkte svabhāvamaśivāpaham  || 49 ||

iti ṣaḍadhikatriṃśadbhedaprasaktajagattanuḥ prasarati mahāśaktyullekhavicitramahāpaṭī  |
jayati bahuśaḥ spandākārā parā cidanuttarā vimṛśata janāḥ kiṃ no svabhāvavijṛmbhitam  || 50 ||

iti śrīmadādyanāthaviracitā anuttaraprakāśapañcāśikā samāptā

seyaṃ kāśmīrabhūpāla- saṃśritena prakāśitā  |
satā mukundarāmeṇa sanmude'stu śive'rpitā  || *1 ||
