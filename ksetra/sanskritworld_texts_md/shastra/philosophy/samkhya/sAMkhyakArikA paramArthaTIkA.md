	<< duḥkhatrayābhighātājjijñāsā tadabhighātake hetau |
	dṛṣṭe sāpārthā cennaikāntātyantato'bhāvāt‌ || 1 || >>
	
	Ch: trividhaduḥkhābhighātājjijñāsā tadabhighātake hetau |
	dṛṣṭe'pārthā [cet‌] na,  [heto]ranaikāntikānātyantikatvāt‌ ||

	asyā āryāyā upodghāta ucyate | purā kila maharṣirākāśajātaḥ (brahmasutaḥ ?) svābhāvikadharmajñānavairāgyaiśvaryacaturguṇātmakaḥ kapilo nāma babhūva | andhatamasi majjajjagadidamavalokya [asya] mahākāruṇyamudapādi aho khalu jagad‌ [idam‌] andhe tamasi vartata iti | [evaṃ] lokaṃ vicintayan‌ ekaṃ brāhmaṇamāsurisagotraṃ varṣasahasrayājinamadrākṣīt‌ | nigūḍhātmā tamupetya vācamevamuvāca bho āsure ramase gṛhasthadharmeṇa iti | imāṃ vācamuktvā anantaḥ pratijagāma | bhūyo'pi varṣasahasre pūrṇe pratyāgamya tathaivābhyuvāca | evamukte brāhmaṇo muniṃ pratyuvāca satyaṃ bhagavan‌ rame gṛhasthadharmeṇa iti | tadā maharṣiḥ śrutvaitat‌ punarniragāt‌ | sa paścādbhūya abhyupetya [tāmeva] vācamabhyuvāca | brāhmaṇastaṃ pratyāha evameveti | maharṣistu apṛcchat‌ | utsahase śuddhaṃ brahmacaryaṃ vastuṃ na vā iti | brāhmaṇa āha utsahe tathā vastumiti | atha gṛhasthadharmamapahāya pravrajyācaryāmācaran‌ kapilaśiṣyo babhūva |
	para āha | kasmāddhetorasya brāhmaṇasya jijñāsā samutpannā | uttaramāha | trividhaduḥkhābhighātāt‌ | kiṃ punastrividhaṃ duḥkham‌ | (1) ādhyātmikam‌ (2) ādhibhautikam‌ (3) ādhidaivikam‌ | [tatra] ādhyātmikaṃ yad‌ vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vaiṣamyāt‌ pravartate vyādhiduḥkham‌ | yathoktaṃ cikitsā[tantre] | "nābheradho vātasthānamucyate | hṛdayādadhaḥ pittasthānamucyate | hṛdayādūrdhvaṃ sarvaṃ śleṣmasambandhi | vātamutkaṭaṃ sat‌ śleṣmapitte abhibhūya vātarogamutpādayati | pittaśleṣmaṇī apyevam‌ ||" idamucyate śārīraṃ duḥkham‌ | mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ- priyaviprayogaḥ apriyasaṃyogaḥ prārthitālābhaḥ etattrividhaṃ mānasaṃ duḥkham‌ | īdṛśaṃ duḥkhamādhyātmikaṃ duḥkhamabhidhīyate | ādhibhautikaṃ tu yanmartyapaśupakṣyuragataṭasetubhedādinā pravartate duḥkham‌ | [ida]mādhibhautikaṃ duḥkhamucyate | ādhidaivikaṃ yat‌ śītātapavātavarṣāśanyādirnānāvidho marmabhedī | [ida]mādhidaivikaṃ duḥkhamabhidhīyate | [etaiḥ] tribhirduḥkhairabhighātāt‌ [1245, c. 2] samutpannā jijñāsā duḥkhābhighātake hetau |
	para āha | etadduḥkhatrayābhighātako [dvividho]'yaṃ hetuḥ vispaṣṭaṃ pratyakṣaḥ | prathamaḥ, aṣṭāṅgāyurvedoktaḥ, śārīraduḥkhasyābhighātakaḥ | dvitīyaḥ, priyāḥ ṣaḍ‌ viṣayā mānasaduḥkhasyābhighātakāḥ | imau hetū pratyakṣāveva | kathaṃ punarjijñāsā | samādhīyate | nāyamartho yujyate | dvividhadoṣasattvāt‌ | jijñāsā na yuktiviruddhā | sa dvividho doṣaśca [hetoḥ] (1) anaikāntikatā (2) anātyantikatā ceti || 1 ||

	para āha | yadi aṣṭāṅgāyurvedādikaṃ doṣadvayayuktatvān na heturalaṃ duḥkhābhighātāya | caturṣu vedeṣu tu hetvantaramasti | ayaṃ heturaikāntikamātyantikañca phalaṃ prāpayati | ato bhavato jijñāsā apārthā | uktaṃ hi vede |
	apāma somamamṛtā abhūmāganma jyotiravidāma devān‌ |
	kiṃ nūnamasmān‌ kṛṇavadarātiḥ kimu dhūrtiramṛta martyasya || iti | 
	uttaramāha |

	<< dṛṣṭavadānuśravikaḥ sa hyaviśuddhikṣayātiśayayuktaḥ |
	tadviparītaḥ śreyān‌ vyaktāvyaktajñavijñānāt‌ || 2 || >>

	Ch: dṛṣṭavadānuśravikaḥ aviśuddhikṣayātiśayayuktaḥ |
	etadvividhahetuviparītaḥ śreyān‌ vyaktāvyaktajñavijñānāt‌ || 

	dṛṣṭo heturāyurvedoktaḥ anaikāntikānātyantika[rūpa]doṣayuktaḥ | ānuśraviko hetuḥ upadeśaśravaṇalabdhaḥ brahmaṇa ārabhya [kapila]maharṣiparyantam‌ | ataścatvāro vedā ānuśravikā ucyante | ime vedā api dvidhā duṣṭāḥ yathā āyurvedo dṛṣṭaḥ | kiñca punardoṣatrayayuktaḥ | (1) aviśuddhiḥ | yathoktaṃ vede 

	he paśo tava pitā mātā bandhavaḥ sarve'pi tvāmanumanyatām‌ |
	idaṃ śarīraṃ tyaktvā divi janma lapsyase || iti |

yathoktamaśvamedhe |
	ṣaṭ‍ śatāni nihanyante paśūnām‌ | ṣaṭśatapaśavasatribhirūnāḥ |

	[yadi tato'pi] ūnāḥ, tadā na divi janma prāpayanti ānandādipañcavṛttyartham‌ | yadyanṛtaṃ brūyānmanuṣyaḥ, deā maharṣayaścāhurnaitatpāpamiti | evamādipāpamānuśravike hetāvasti | tasmādaviśuddhaḥ | (2) kṣayaḥ | yathoktaṃ vede |

	kālena samatītāni kālo hi duratikramaḥ || iti |
	
	yadāyaṃ dharmaḥ kṣīyate tadā dātā svargānnivartate | ataḥ kṣayayuktaḥ | (3) atiśayaḥ yathā āḍhyamavalokya daridraḥ khidyate | surūpaṃ vīkṣya kurūpaḥ prājñañca dṛṣṭvā mūrkhaḥ khidyate | tathā svarge'pi uttamavargaṃ dṛṣṭvā hīnavargaḥ khidyate | tasmādatiśayayuktaḥ | ete trayaḥ, pūrva[muktau] dvau, tābhyāṃ saha pañca doṣāḥ iti vedo na [duḥkhābhighātāya] hetuḥ |
	para āha | yathā cetko hetuḥ śreyān‌ | uttaramāha | etadvividhahetuviparītaḥ śreyāniti | yau dvau hetū āyurvedokto vedoktaśca | (1) aikāntikaḥ (2) ātyantikaḥ (3) viśuddhaḥ (4) akṣayaḥ (5) samānaḥ iti | tasmāt‌ pūrvadvayādutkṛṣṭaḥ | para āha | ayaṃ hetuḥ kuto labhyate | uttaramāha | vyaktāvyaktajñavijñānāditi | vyaktam‌, mahānahaṅkāraḥ pañca tanmātrāṇi pañca [karme]ndriyāṇi pañca buddhīndriyāṇi manaḥ pañca mahābhūtāni ca | etāni sapta vyaktamityākhyāyante | prakṛtikṛtatvāt‌ | avyaktaṃ pradhānaṃ mūlakāraṇam‌ | jñaḥ puruṣaḥ | etatpañcaviṃśatitattvaprameyajñānāṃ na vṛddhiḥ na kṣayaḥ duḥkhatrayādaikāntikamokṣaśca bhavati | yathoktā mokṣe gāthā | 
	pañcaviṃśatitattvajño yatra tatrāśrame vaset‌ |
	jaṭī muṇḍī śikhī vāpi mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 2 || iti

	para āha | kathaṃ mūlaprakṛtivikṛtipuruṣāṇāṃ pravibhāgaḥ | uttaramāha |

	<< mūlaprakṛtiravikṛtiḥ mahadādyāḥ prakṛtivikṛtayaḥ sapta |
	ṣoḍaśakastu vikāro na prakṛtirna vikṛtiḥ puruṣaḥ || 3 || >>

	Ch: mūlaprakṛtiravikṛtiḥ mahadādyāḥ prakṛtayo vikṛtayaśca |	
	ṣoḍaśako vikāra eva puruṣo na prakṛtiḥ vikṛtirvā || 

	mūlaprakṛtiḥ sarvajananī | anyasmādanutpattermūlaprakṛtiriti vyapadiśyate | mūlaprakṛtirmahadādīnāṃ janakatvānmūleti nāma labhate | avikṛtiḥ anyasmādanutpatteḥ | mahānahaṅkāraḥ pañca tanmātrāṇi, etāni sapta prakṛtayo vikṛtayaśca | mahān‌ mūlaprakṛtita utpanna iti vikṛtiḥ | pañca tanmātrāṇi janayatīti prakṛtiḥ | pañca tanmātrāṇi ahaṅkārādutpannāni iti vikṛtayaḥ | mahābhūtāni indriyāṇi ca utpādayantīti prakṛtayaḥ | [tatra] śabdatanmātramākāśaṃ śrotrendriyañca janayatīti prakṛtiḥ yāvadgandhatanmātraṃ pṛthivīṃ ghrāṇendriyañca janayatīti | evaṃ saptāpi prakṛtayo vikṛtayaśca | ṣoḍaśako vikāra eveti | ākāśādipañcamahābhūtāni śrotrādipañca[buddhī]ndriyāṇi jihvādipañcakarmendriyāṇi manaśca itīme ṣoḍaśa dharmā anyasmādevotpannatvād‌ anyānutpādakatvācca vikāra eva | puruṣo na prakṛtirvikṛtirveti | puruṣo'tra ātmā ucyate | jñānātmakatvāt‌ | sa ātmā notpādako nānyasmādutpannaḥ | pūrvatrayādbhinna iti na prakṛtirna vikṛtiḥ || 3 ||

	para āha | ime [vyaktāvyaktajñāḥ] trayaḥ padārthāḥ kena pramāṇena jñāpyante | loke pramāṇaṃ hi [prameya]jñāpanasamartham‌ | yathā tulāhastādīni dīrghaṃ hrasvaṃ guru laghu vā jñāpayanti | uttaramāha |

	<< dṛṣṭamanumānamāptavacanañca sarvapramāṇasiddhatvāt‌ |
	trividhaṃ pramāṇamiṣṭaṃ prameyasiddhiḥ pramāṇāddhi || 4 || >>

	Ch: dṛṣṭamanumānamāptavacanañca sarvaprameyāni jñāpayanti |
	ataḥ pramāṇaṃ trividhamiṣṭaṃ prameyasiddhiḥ pramāṇāt‌ [hi] ||

	asmin‌ śāstre trividhaṃ pramāṇaṃ sthāpitam‌ | (1) dṛṣṭam‌ (2) anumānam‌ (3) āptavacanañceti | dṛṣṭaṃ pramāṇam‌ indriyaviṣayābhyāmutpannaṃ [yad‌] etajjñānaprakāśitamavyabhicāri asandigdhañca; idamucyate dṛṣṭaṃ pramāṇam‌ | anumānaṃ pramāṇaṃ dṛṣṭapūrvakam‌ | tattrividhaṃ (1) pūrvavat‌ (2) śeṣavat‌ (3) sāmānyato dṛṣṭamiti | yo'yamarthaḥ pratyakṣānumānapramāṇābhyāṃ nādhigamyate | āptavacanena punarayamadhigamyate | yathā svargaḥ uttarāvatī (?) | [ayamarthaḥ] pratyakṣānumānābhyāṃ nādhigamyate | āptavacanena punaradhigamyate | āptavacanaṃ yathoktaṃ gāthāyām‌ |
	āgamo hyāptavacanamāptaṃ doṣakṣayā[dviduḥ] |
	kṣīṇadoṣo'nṛtaṃ vākyaṃ na brūyāddhetvasambhavāt‌ || iti |
	sarvaprameyāni jñāpayanti iti | yadavaśiṣṭaṃ pramāṇamavaśiṣṭaṃ prameyañca; [etatsarvam‌] asmāt‌ trividhāt‌ pramānānna bahirbhavati | upamānādi ṣaṭpramāṇānāmāptavacane'ntarbhāvāt‌ | prameyasiddhiḥ pramāṇāddhīti | prameyaiḥ pañcaviṃśatipadārthaiḥ sarvasaṅgrahāt‌ | siddhiḥ etatpañcaviṃśati[padārtha]vittiḥ | kathaṃ te prameyamiti nāma labhante | pramāṇasya gocaratvāt‌, prameyamiti siddhyati | pratyakṣānumānāptavacanaiḥ saṃkṣepeṇa siddhāstrayaḥ [vyaktāvyaktajñāḥ padārthāḥ] pañcaviṃśatidhā tu vistṛtāḥ || 4 ||

	para āha | uktāni trividhapramāṇāni | kiṃ pramāṇalakṣaṇam‌ | uttaramāha |
	
	<< prativiṣayādhyavasāyo dṛṣṭaṃ trividhamanumānamākhyātam‌ |
	talliṅgaliṅgipūrvakamāptaśrutirāptavacanantu || 5 || >>

	Ch: prativiṣayādhyavasāyo dṛṣṭamanumānaṃ trividhaṃ jñātam‌ |
	liṅgaliṅgipūrvakam; āptaśrutirāptavacanamucyate || 

	prativiṣayādhyavasāyo dṛṣṭamiti | śrotraṃ śabde'dhyavasāyaṃ janayati | yāvad‌ ghrāṇaṃ gandhe'dhyavasāyaḥ janayati | adhyavasāyakameva, na tu jñāpakam‌; idamucyate dṛṣṭaṃ pramāṇam‌ | anumānaṃ trividhaṃ jñātamiti | (1) pūrvavat‌ (2) śeṣavat‌ (3) sāmānyato dṛṣṭamiti | etattrividhaṃ jñānaṃ dṛṣṭapramāṇahetukamiti trividhasya prameyasya trividhasyādhvanaśca vibhedanakṣamam‌, idamanumānamucyate | yathā kaścit‌ kṛṣṇameghaṃ dṛṣṭvā jānīyād‌ vṛṣṭiravaśyaṃ [bhaviṣyati] iti | yathā nadyāṃ pūrṇaṃ nūtanaṃ paṅkilaṃ jalaṃ dṛṣṭvā jānīyādavaśyamupari vṛṣṭirabhūditi | yathā vā pāṭalipure āmrataruṃ puṣpitaṃ dṛṣṭvā kośaladeśe'pyevamiti jānīyāt‌ | liṅgaliṅgipūrvakamiti | liṅgaṃ liṅginā saṃyuktaṃ na viyuktam‌ | liṅgadarśanādanumitiḥ sādhyate | āptaśrutirāptavacanamucyate iti | yathā brahmaṇā manunā ca uktāścatvāro vedā dharmaśāstrañca || 5 ||

	para āha | trividhamanumānamuktam‌ | [tatra] kena pramāṇena kasya prameyasya siddhirbudhyate | uttaramāha |

	<< sāmānyatastu dṛṣṭādatīndriyāṇāṃ pratītiranumānāt‌ |

	para āha | yadi prakṛtipuruṣau nopalabhyeta | dvādaśānāṃ madhye kena nopalabhyete | samādhīyate | ekasmātkāraṇānnopalabhyete | kimekaṃ kāraṇam‌ | āryayā pradarśayati |
	
	<< saukṣmyāttadanupalabdhirnābhāvātkāryatastadupalabdhiḥ |
	mahadādi tacca kāryaṃ prakṛtivirūpaṃ sarūpañca || 8 || >>

	Ch: prakṛtiḥ saukṣmyānnopalabhyate, na kāraṇamupalabdhamasad‌ iti |
	mahadādi tasya kāryaṃ prakṛtivirūpaṃ sarūpañca || 

	prakṛtiḥ saukṣmyānnopalabhyate, na kāraṇamupalabdhamasaditi | iti | pradhānaṃ sadeva nopalabhyate | saukṣmyād‌ yathā dhūmādi[paramāṇavaḥ] saukṣmyānnopalabhyante | tathā pradhānamapi | na tu dvitīyaṃ śiraḥ tṛtīyo bāhurivātyantamasattvānnopalabhyate | para āha | yadi nopalabhyate | kathaṃ tarhi jñāyate astīti | ucyate | kāryaṃ pratītya svakāraṇaṃ dṛśyate | kāryaṃ pradhānakṛtamiti sāmānyato dṛṣṭānumānātpradhānamastīti jñāyate | para āha | kiṃ tatkāryam‌ | ucyate | mahadādi tasya kāryam‌ | pradhānānmahānutpadyate | mahata ahaṅkāraḥ | ahaṅkārātpañcatanmātrāṇi | pañcatanmātrebhyaḥ ṣoḍaśa [vikārā] utpadyante | mahadādi kāryaṃ triguṇaṃ dṛśyate | ataḥ pradhānaṃ triguṇamiti jñāyate | prakṛtivirūpaṃ sarūpañceti | kāryaṃ dvividhaṃ (1) prakṛtivirūpaṃ (2) prakṛtisarūpañceti | yathā ekasya dvau sutāvutpannau | ekaḥ pitṛsarūpaḥ aparastu [pitṛ]virūpaḥ | [evaṃ] etatkāraṇakaṃ kāryaṃ prakṛtisarūpaṃ virūpañca | iti paścādvistareṇa vakṣyate | īdṛśaṃ kāryamasmin‌ śāstre || 8 ||
