haṭhayogapradīpikā
(1)
prathamopadeśaḥ

śrīādināthāya namo'stu tasmai
yenopadiṣṭā haṭhayogavidyā  |
vibhrājate pronnatarājayogam
āroḍhumicchoradhirohiṇīva  || ḥẏড়_1.1 ||

praṇamya śrīguruṃ nāthaṃ svātmārāmeṇa yoginā  |
kevalaṃ rājayogāya haṭhavidyopadiśyate  || ḥẏড়_1.2 ||
bhrāntyā bahumatadhvānte rājayogamajānatām  |
haṭhapradīpikāṃ dhatte svātmārāmaḥ kṛpākaraḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.3 ||
haṭhavidyāṃ hi matsyendragorakṣādyā vijānate  |
svātmārāmo'thavā yogī jānīte tatprasādataḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.4 ||
śrīādināthamatsyendraśāvarānandabhairavāḥ  |
cauraṅgīmīnagorakṣavirūpākṣabileśayāḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.5 ||
manthāno bhairavo yogī siddhirbuddhaśca kanthaḍiḥ  |
koraṃṭakaḥ surānandaḥ siddhapādaśca carpaṭiḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.6 ||
kānerī pūjyapādaśca nityanātho nirañjanaḥ  |
kapālī bindunāthaśca kākacaṇḍīśvarāhvayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.7 ||
allāmaḥ prabhudevaśca ghoḍā colī ca ṭiṃṭiṇiḥ  |
bhānukī nāradevaśca khaṇḍaḥ kāpālikastathā  || ḥẏড়_1.8 ||
ityādayo mahāsiddhā haṭhayogaprabhāvataḥ  |
khaṇḍayitvā kāladaṇḍaṃ brahmāṇḍe vicaranti te  || ḥẏড়_1.9 ||
aśeṣatāpataptānāṃ samāśrayamaṭho haṭhaḥ  |
aśeṣayogayuktānāmādhārakamaṭho haṭhaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.10 ||
haṭhavidyā paraṃ gopyā yoginā siddhimicchatā  |
bhavedvīryavatī guptā nirvīryā tu prakāśitā  || ḥẏড়_1.11 ||
surājye dhārmike deśe subhikṣe nirupadrave  |
dhanuḥ pramāṇaparyantaṃ śilāgnijalavarjite  |
ekānte maṭhikāmadhye sthātavyaṃ haṭhayoginā  || ḥẏড়_1.12 ||

alpadvāramarandhragartavivaraṃ nātyuccanīcāyataṃ
samyaggomayasāndraliptamamalaṃ niḥśesajantūjjhitam  |
bāhye maṇḍapavedikūparuciraṃ prākārasaṃveṣṭitaṃ
proktaṃ yogamaṭhasya lakṣaṇamidaṃ siddhairhaṭhābhyāsibhiḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.13 ||

evaṃ vidhe maṭhe sthitvā sarvacintāvivarjitaḥ  |
gurūpadiṣṭamārgeṇa yogameva samabhyaset || ḥẏড়_1.14 ||
atyāhāraḥ prayāsaśca prajalpo niyamāgrahaḥ  |
janasaṅgaśca laulyaṃ ca ṣaḍbhiryogo vinaśyati  || ḥẏড়_1.15 ||
utsāhātsāhasāddhairyāttattvajñānāśca niścayāt |
janasaṅgaparityāgātṣaḍbhiryogaḥ prasiddhyati  || ḥẏড়_1.16 ||

atha yamaniyamāḥ

ahiṃsā satyamasteyaṃ brahmacaryaṃ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ  |
dayārjavaṃ mitāhāraḥ śaucaṃ caiva yamā daśa  || ḥẏড়_1.17 ||
tapaḥ santoṣa āstikyaṃ dānamīśvarapūjanam  |
siddhāntavākyaśravaṇaṃ hrīmatī ca tapo hutam  |
niyamā daśa samproktā yogaśāstraviśāradaiḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.18 ||

atha āsanam

haṭhasya prathamāṅgatvādāsanaṃ pūrvamucyate  |
kuryāttadāsanaṃ sthairyamārogyaṃ cāṅgalāghavam  || ḥẏড়_1.19 ||
vaśiṣṭhādyaiśca munibhirmatsyendrādyaiśca yogibhiḥ  |
aṅgīkṛtānyāsanāni kathyante kānicinmayā  || ḥẏড়_1.20 ||
jānūrvorantare samyakkṛtvā pādatale ubhe  |
ṛjukāyaḥ samāsīnaḥ svastikaṃ tatpracakṣate  || ḥẏড়_1.21 ||
savye dakṣiṇagulphaṃ tu pṛṣṭhapārśve niyojayet |
dakṣiṇe'pi tathā savyaṃ gomukhaṃ gomukhākṛtiḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.22 ||
ekaṃ pādaṃ tathaikasmin vinyaseduruṇi sthiram  |
itarasmiṃstathā coruṃ vīrāsanamitīritam  || ḥẏড়_1.23 ||
gudaṃ nirudhya gulphābhyāṃ vyutkrameṇa samāhitaḥ  |
kūrmāsanaṃ bhavedetaditi yogavido viduḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.24 ||
padmāsanaṃ tu saṃsthāpya jānūrvorantare karau  |
niveśya bhūmau saṃsthāpya vyomasthaṃ kukkuṭāsanam  || ḥẏড়_1.25 ||
kukkuṭāsanabandhastho dorbhyāṃ sambadya kandharām  |
bhavedkūrmavaduttāna etaduttānakūrmakam  || ḥẏড়_1.26 ||
pādāṅguṣṭhau tu pāṇibhyāṃ gṛhītvā śravaṇāvadhi  |
dhanurākarṣaṇaṃ kuryāddhanurāsanamucyate  || ḥẏড়_1.27 ||
vāmorumūlārpitadakṣapādaṃ
jānorbahirveṣṭitavāmapādam  |
pragṛhya tiṣṭhetparivartitāṅgaḥ
śrīmatysanāthoditamāsanaṃ syāt || ḥẏড়_1.28 ||
matsyendrapīṭhaṃ jaṭharapradīptiṃ
pracaṇḍarugmaṇḍalakhaṇḍanāstram  |
abhyāsataḥ kuṇḍalinīprabodhaṃ
candrasthiratvaṃ ca dadāti puṃsām  || ḥẏড়_1.29 ||
prasārya pādau bhuvi daṇḍarūpau
dorbhyāṃ padāgradvitayaṃ gṛhītvā  |
jānūparinyastalalāṭadeśo
vasedidaṃ paścimatānamāhuḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.30 ||
iti paścimatānamāsanāgryaṃ
pavanaṃ paścimavāhinaṃ karoti  |
udayaṃ jaṭharānalasya kuryād
udare kārśyamarogatāṃ ca puṃsām  || ḥẏড়_1.31 ||
dharāmavaṣṭabhya karadvayena
tatkūrparasthāpitanābhipārśvaḥ  |
uccāsano daṇḍavadutthitaḥ khe
māyūrametatpravadanti pīṭham  || ḥẏড়_1.32 ||
harati sakalarogānāśu gulmodarādīn
abhibhavati ca doṣānāsanaṃ śrīmayūram  |
bahu kadaśanabhuktaṃ bhasma kuryādaśeṣaṃ
janayati jaṭharāgniṃ jārayetkālakūṭam  || ḥẏড়_1.33 ||
uttānaṃ śabavadbhūmau śayanaṃ tacchavāsanam  |
śavāsanaṃ śrāntiharaṃ cittaviśrāntikārakam  || ḥẏড়_1.34 ||
caturaśītyāsanāni śivena kathitāni ca  |
tebhyaścatuṣkamādāya sārabhūtaṃ bravīmyaham  || ḥẏড়_1.35 ||
siddhaṃ padmaṃ tathā siṃhaṃ bhadraṃ veti catuṣṭayam  |
śreṣṭhaṃ tatrāpi ca sukhe tiṣṭhetsiddhāsane sadā  || ḥẏড়_1.36 ||

atha siddhāsanam
yonisthānakamaṅghrimūlaghaṭitaṃ kṛtvā dṛḍhaṃ vinyaset
meṇḍhre pādamathaikameva hṛdaye kṛtvā hanuṃ susthiram  |
sthāṇuḥ saṃyamitendriyo'caladṛśā paśyedbhruvorantaraṃ
hyetanmokṣakapāṭabhedajanakaṃ siddhāsanaṃ procyate  || ḥẏড়_1.37 ||

meṇḍhrādupari vinyasya savyaṃ gulphaṃ tathopari  |
gulphāntaraṃ ca nikṣipya siddhāsanamidaṃ bhavet || ḥẏড়_1.38 ||
etatsiddhāsanaṃ prāhuranye vajrāsanaṃ viduḥ  |
muktāsanaṃ vadantyeke prāhurguptāsanaṃ pare  || ḥẏড়_1.39 ||
yameṣviva mitāhāramahiṃsā niyameṣviva  |
mukhyaṃ sarvāsaneṣvekaṃ siddhāḥ siddhāsanaṃ viduḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.40 ||
caturaśītipīṭheṣu siddhameva sadābhyaset |
dvāsaptatisahasrāṇāṃ nāḍīnāṃ malaśodhanam  || ḥẏড়_1.41 ||
ātmadhyāyī mitāhārī yāvaddvādaśavatsaram  |
sadā siddhāsanābhyāsādyogī niṣpattimāpnuyāt || ḥẏড়_1.42 ||
kimanyairbahubhiḥ pīṭhaiḥ siddhe siddhāsane sati  |
prāṇānile sāvadhāne baddhe kevalakumbhake  |
utpadyate nirāyāsātsvayamevonmanī kalā  || ḥẏড়_1.43 ||
tathaikāsminneva dṛḍhe siddhe siddhāsane sati  |
bandhatrayamanāyāsātsvayamevopajāyate  || ḥẏড়_1.44 ||
nāsanaṃ siddhasadṛśaṃ na kumbhaḥ kevalopamaḥ  |
na khecarīsamā mudrā na nādasadṛśo layaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.45 ||

atha padmāsanam
vāmorūpari dakṣiṇaṃ ca caraṇaṃ saṃsthāpya vāmaṃ tathā
dakṣorūpari paścimena vidhinā dhṛtvā karābhyāṃ dṛḍham  |
aṅguṣṭhau hṛdaye nidhāya cibukaṃ nāsāgramālokayet
etadvyādhivināśakāri yamināṃ padmāsanaṃ procyate  || ḥẏড়_1.46 ||

uttānau caraṇau kṛtvā ūrusaṃsthau prayatnataḥ  |
ūrumadhye tathottānau pāṇī kṛtvā tato dṛśau  || ḥẏড়_1.47 ||
nāsāgre vinyasedrājadantamūle tu jihvayā  |
uttambhya cibukaṃ vakṣasyutthāpypavanaṃ śanaiḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.48 ||
idaṃ padmāsanaṃ proktaṃ sarvavyādhivināśanam  |
durlabhaṃ yena kenāpi dhīmatā labhyate bhuvi  || ḥẏড়_1.49 ||

kṛtvā sampuṭitau karau dṛḍhataraṃ baddhvā tu padmāsanaṃ
gāḍhaṃ vakṣasi sannidhāya cibukaṃ dhyāyaṃśca taccetasi  |
vāraṃ vāramapānamūrdhvamanilaṃ protsārayan pūritaṃ
nyañcan prāṇamupaiti bodhamatulaṃ śaktiprabhāvānnaraḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.50 ||

padmāsane sthito yogī nāḍīdvāreṇa pūritam  |
mārutaṃ dhārayedyastu sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.51 ||

atha siṃhāsanam
gulphau ca vṛṣaṇasyādhaḥ sīvanyāḥ pārśvayoḥ kṣipet |
dakṣiṇe savyagulphaṃ tu dakṣagulphaṃ tu savyake  || ḥẏড়_1.52 ||
hastau tu jānvoḥ saṃsthāpya svāṅgulīḥ samprasārya ca  |
vyāttavaktro nirīkṣeta nāsāgraṃ susamāhitaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.53 ||
siṃhāsanaṃ bhavedetatpūjitaṃ yogipuṅgavaiḥ  |
bandhatritayasandhānaṃ kurute cāsanottamam  || ḥẏড়_1.54 ||

atha bhadrāsanam
gulphau ca vṛṣaṇasyādhaḥ sīvanyāḥ pārśvayoḥ kṣipte  |
savyagulphaṃ tathā savye dakṣagulphaṃ tu dakṣiṇe  || ḥẏড়_1.55 ||
pārśvapādau ca pāṇibhyāṃ dṛḍhaṃ baddhvā suniścalam  |
bhadrāsanaṃ bhavedetatsarvavyādhivināśanam  |
gorakṣāsanamityāhuridaṃ vai siddhayoginaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.56 ||

evamāsanabandheṣu yogīndro vigataśramaḥ  |
abhyasennāḍikāśuddhiṃ mudrādipavanīkriyām  || ḥẏড়_1.57 ||
āsanaṃ kumbhakaṃ citraṃ mudrākhyaṃ karaṇaṃ tathā  |
atha nādānusandhānamabhyāsānukramo haṭhe  || ḥẏড়_1.58 ||
brahmacārī mitāhārī tyāgī yogaparāyaṇaḥ  |
abdādūrdhvaṃ bhavetsiddho nātra kāryā vicāraṇā  || ḥẏড়_1.59 ||
susnigdhamadhurāhāraścaturthāṃśavivarjitaḥ  |
bhujyate śivasamprītyai mitāhāraḥ sa ucyate  || ḥẏড়_1.60 ||

kaṭvāmlatīkṣṇalavaṇoṣṇaharītaśāka
sauvīratailatilasarṣapamadyamatsyān  |
ājādimāṃsadadhitakrakulatthakola
piṇyākahiṅgulaśunādyamapathyamāhuḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.61 ||

bhojanamahitaṃ vidyātpunarasyoṣṇīkṛtaṃ rūkṣam  |
atilavaṇamamlayuktaṃ kadaśanaśākotkaṃ varjyam  || ḥẏড়_1.62 ||
vahnistrīpathisevānāmādau varjanamācaret || ḥẏড়_1.63 ||
tathā hi gorakṣavacanam
varjayeddurjanaprāntaṃ vahnistrīpathisevanam  |
prātaḥsnānopavāsādi kāyakleśavidhiṃ tathā  || ḥẏড়_1.64 ||

godhūmaśāliyavaṣāṣṭikaśobhanānnaṃ
kṣīrājyakhaṇḍanavanītasiddhāmadhūni  |
śuṇṭhīpaṭolakaphalādikapañcaśākaṃ
mudgādidivyamudakaṃ ca yamīndrapathyam  || ḥẏড়_1.65 ||

puṣṭaṃ sumadhuraṃ snigdhaṃ gavyaṃ dhātuprapoṣaṇam  |
manobhilaṣitaṃ yogyaṃ yogī bhojanamācaret || ḥẏড়_1.66 ||
yuvo vṛddho'tivṛddho vā vyādhito durbalo'pi vā  |
abhyāsātsiddhimāpnoti sarvayogeṣvatandritaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.67 ||
kriyāyuktasya siddhiḥ syādakriyasya kathaṃ bhavet |
na śāstrapāṭhamātreṇa yogasiddhiḥ prajāyate  || ḥẏড়_1.68 ||
na veṣadhāraṇaṃ siddheḥ kāraṇaṃ na ca tatkathā  |
kriyaiva kāraṇaṃ siddheḥ satyametanna saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_1.69 ||
pīṭhāni kumbhakāścitrā divyāni karaṇāni ca  |
sarvāṇyapi haṭhābhyāse rājayogaphalāvadhi  || ḥẏড়_1.70 ||

iti haṭhapradīpikāyāṃ prathamopadeśaḥ  |

(2)
dvitīyopadeśaḥ

athāsane dṛdhe yogī vaśī hitamitāśanaḥ  |
gurūpadiṣṭamārgeṇa prāṇāyāmān samabhyaset || ḥẏড়_2.1 ||
cale vāte calaṃ cittaṃ niścale niścalaṃ bhavet || ḥẏড়_2.
yogī sthāṇutvamāpnoti tato vāyuṃ nirodhayet || ḥẏড়_2.2 ||
yāvadvāyuḥ sthito dehe tāvajjīvanamucyate  |
maraṇaṃ tasya niṣkrāntistato vāyuṃ nirodhayet || ḥẏড়_2.3 ||
malākalāsu nāḍīṣu māruto naiva madhyagaḥ  |
kathaṃ syādunmanībhāvaḥ kāryasiddhiḥ kathaṃ bhavet || ḥẏড়_2.4 ||
śuddhameti yadā sarvaṃ nāḍīcakraṃ malākulam  |
tadaiva jāyate yogī prāṇasaṃgrahaṇe kṣamaḥ  || ḥẏড়_2.5 ||
prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kuryānnityaṃ sāttvikayā dhiyā  |
yathā suṣumṇānāḍīsthā malāḥ śuddhiṃ prayānti ca  || ḥẏড়_2.6 ||
baddhapadmāsano yogī prāṇaṃ candreṇa pūrayet |
dhārayitvā yathāśakti bhūyaḥ sūryeṇa recayet || ḥẏড়_2.7 ||
prāṇaṃ sūryeṇa cākṛṣya pūrayedudaraṃ śanaiḥ  |
vidhivatkumbhakaṃ kṛtvā punaścandreṇa recayet || ḥẏড়_2.8 ||
yena tyajettena pītvā dhārayedatirodhataḥ  |
recayecca tato'nyena śanaireva na vegataḥ  || ḥẏড়_2.9 ||

prāṇaṃ cediḍayā pibenniyamitaṃ bhūyo'nyathā recayet
pītvā piṅgalayā samīraṇamatho baddhvā tyajedvāmayā  |
sūryacandramasoranena vidhinābhyāsaṃ sadā tanvatāṃ
śuddhā nāḍigaṇā bhavanti yamināṃ māsatrayādūrdhvataḥ  || ḥẏড়_2.10 ||

prātarmadhyandine sāyamardharātre ca kumbhakān  |
śanairaśītiparyantaṃ caturvāraṃ samabhyaset || ḥẏড়_2.11 ||
kanīyasi bhavedsveda kampo bhavati madhyame  |
uttame sthānamāpnoti tato vāyuṃ nibandhayet || ḥẏড়_2.12 ||
jalena śramajātena gātramardanamācaret |
dṛḍhatā laghutā caiva tena gātrasya jāyate  || ḥẏড়_2.13 ||
abhyāsakāle prathame śastaṃ kṣīrājyabhojanam  |
tato'bhyāse  dṛḍhībhūte na tādṛṅniyamagrahaḥ  || ḥẏড়_2.14 ||
yathā siṃho gajo vyāghro bhavedvaśyaḥ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ  |

tenaiṣā khecarī nāma mudrā siddhairnirūpitā  || ḥẏড়_3.41 ||
khecaryā mudritaṃ yena vivaraṃ lambikordhvataḥ  |
na tasya kṣarate binduḥ kāminyāḥ śleṣitasya ca  || ḥẏড়_3.42 ||
calito'pi yadā binduḥ samprāpto yonimaṇḍalam  |
vrajatyūrdhvaṃ hṛtaḥ śaktyā nibaddho yonimudrayā  || ḥẏড়_3.43 ||
ūrdhvajihvaḥ sthiro bhūtvā somapānaṃ karoti yaḥ  |
māsārdhena na sandeho mṛtyuṃ jayati yogavit || ḥẏড়_3.44 ||
nityaṃ somakalāpūrṇaṃ śarīraṃ yasya yoginaḥ  |
takṣakeṇāpi daṣṭasya viṣaṃ tasya na sarpati  || ḥẏড়_3.45 ||
indhanāni yathā vahnistailavarti ca dīpakaḥ  |
tathā somakalāpūrṇaṃ dehī dehaṃ na muñcati  || ḥẏড়_3.46 ||
gomāṃsaṃ bhakṣayennityaṃ pibedamaravāruṇīm  |
kulīnaṃ tamahaṃ manye cetare kulaghātakāḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.47 ||
gośabdenoditā jihvā tatpraveśo hi tāluni  |
gomāṃsabhakṣaṇaṃ tattu mahāpātakanāśanam  || ḥẏড়_3.48 ||
jihvāpraveśasambhūtavahninotpāditaḥ khalu  |
candrātsravati yaḥ sāraḥ sā syādamaravāruṇī  || ḥẏড়_3.49 ||

cumbantī yadi lambikāgramaniśaṃ jihvārasasyandinī
sakṣārā kaṭukāmladugdhasadṛśī madhvājyatulyā tathā  |
vyādhīnāṃ haraṇaṃ jarāntakaraṇaṃ śastrāgamodīraṇaṃ
tasya syādamaratvamaṣṭaguṇitaṃ siddhāṅganākarṣaṇam  || ḥẏড়_3.50 ||

mūrdhnaḥ ṣoḍaśapatrapadmagalitaṃ prāṇādavāptaṃ haṭhād
ūrdvhāsyo rasanāṃ niyamya vivare śaktiṃ parāṃ cintayan  |
utkallolakalājalaṃ ca vimalaṃ dhārāmayaṃ yaḥ piben
nirvyādhiḥ sa mṛṇālakomalavapuryogī ciraṃ jīvati  || ḥẏড়_3.51 ||

yatprāleyaṃ prahitasuṣiraṃ merumūrdhāntarasthaṃ
tasmiṃstattvaṃ pravadati sudhīstanmukhaṃ nimnagānām  |
candrātsāraḥ sravati vapuṣastena mṛtyurnarāṇāṃ
tadbadhnīyātsukaraṇamadho nānyathā kāyasiddhiḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.52 ||

suṣiraṃ jñānajanakaṃ pañcasrotaḥsamanvitam  |
tiṣṭhate khecarī mudrā tasmin śūnye nirañjane  || ḥẏড়_3.53 ||
ekaṃ sṛṣṭimayaṃ bījamekā mudrā ca khecarī  |
eko devo nirālamba ekāvasthā manonmanī  || ḥẏড়_3.54 ||

atha uḍḍīyānabandhaḥ
baddho yena suṣumṇāyāṃ prāṇastūḍḍīyate yataḥ  |
tasmāduḍḍīyanākhyo'yaṃ yogibhiḥ samudāhṛtaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.55 ||
uḍḍīnaṃ kurute yasmādaviśrāntaṃ mahākhagaḥ  |
uḍḍīyānaṃ tadeva syāttava bandho'bhidhīyate  || ḥẏড়_3.56 ||
udare paścimaṃ tānaṃ nābherūrdhvaṃ ca kārayet |
uḍḍīyāno hyasau bandho mṛtyumātaṅgakesarī  || ḥẏড়_3.57 ||
uḍḍīyānaṃ tu sahajaṃ guruṇā kathitaṃ sadā  |
abhyasetsatataṃ yastu vṛddho'pi taruṇāyate  || ḥẏড়_3.58 ||
nābherūrdhvamadhaścāpi tānaṃ kuryātprayatnataḥ  |
ṣaṇmāsamabhyasenmṛtyuṃ jayatyeva na saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.59 ||
sarveṣāmeva bandhānāmuttamo hyuḍḍīyānakaḥ  |
uḍḍiyāne dṛḍhe bandhe muktiḥ svābhāvikī bhavet || ḥẏড়_3.60 ||

atha mūlabandhaḥ
pārṣṇibhāgena sampīḍya yonimākuñcayedgudam  |
apānamūrdhvamākṛṣya mūlabandho'bhidhīyate  || ḥẏড়_3.61 ||
adhogatimapānaṃ vā ūrdhvagaṃ kurute balāt |
ākuñcanena taṃ prāhurmūlabandhaṃ hi yoginaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.62 ||
gudaṃ pārṣṇyā tu sampīḍya vāyumākuñcayedbalāt |
vāraṃ vāraṃ yathā cordhvaṃ samāyāti samīraṇaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.63 ||
prāṇāpānau nādabindū mūlabandhena caikatām  |
gatvā yogasya saṃsiddhiṃ yacchato nātra saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.64 ||
apānaprāṇayoraikyaṃ kṣayo mūtrapurīṣayoḥ  |
yuvā bhavati vṛddho'pi satataṃ mūlabandhanāt || ḥẏড়_3.65 ||
apāna ūrdhvage jāte prayāte vahnimaṇḍalam  |
tadānalaśikhā dīrghā jāyate vāyunāhatā  || ḥẏড়_3.66 ||
tato yāto vahnyapānau prāṇamuṣṇasvarūpakam  |
tenātyantapradīptastu jvalano dehajastathā  || ḥẏড়_3.67 ||
tena kuṇḍalinī suptā santaptā samprabudhyate  |
daṇḍāhatā bhujaṅgīva niśvasya ṛjutāṃ vrajet || ḥẏড়_3.68 ||
bilaṃ praviṣṭeva tato brahmanāḍyaṃ taraṃ vrajet |
tasmānnityaṃ mūlabandhaḥ kartavyo yogibhiḥ sadā  || ḥẏড়_3.69 ||

atha jalandharabandhaḥ
kaṇṭhamākuñcya hṛdaye sthāpayeccibukaṃ dṛḍham  |
bandho jālandharākhyo'yaṃ jarāmṛtyuvināśakaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.70 ||
badhnāti hi sirājālamadhogāmi nabhojalam  |
tato jālandharo bandhaḥ kaṇṭhaduḥkhaughanāśanaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.71 ||
jālandhare kṛte bandhe kaṇṭhasaṃkocalakṣaṇe  |
na pīyūṣaṃ patatyagnau na ca vāyuḥ prakupyati  || ḥẏড়_3.72 ||
kaṇṭhasaṃkocanenaiva dve nāḍyau stambhayeddṛḍham  |
madhyacakramidaṃ jñeyaṃ ṣoḍaśādhārabandhanam  || ḥẏড়_3.73 ||
mūlasthānaṃ samākuñcya uḍḍiyānaṃ tu kārayet |
iḍāṃ ca piṅgalāṃ baddhvā vāhayetpaścime pathi  || ḥẏড়_3.74 ||
anenaiva vidhānena prayāti pavano layam  |
tato na jāyate mṛtyurjarārogādikaṃ tathā  || ḥẏড়_3.75 ||
bandhatrayamidaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ mahāsiddhaiśca sevitam  |
sarveṣāṃ haṭhatantrāṇāṃ sādhanaṃ yogino viduḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.76 ||
yatkiṃcitsravate candrādamṛtaṃ divyarūpiṇaḥ  |
tatsarvaṃ grasate sūryastena piṇḍo jarāyutaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.77 ||

atha viparītakaraṇī mudrā
tatrāsti karaṇaṃ divyaṃ sūryasya mukhavañcanam  |
gurūpadeśato jñeyaṃ na tu śāstrārthakoṭibhiḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.78 ||
ūrdhvanābheradhastālorūrdhvaṃ bhānuradhaḥ śaśī  |
karaṇī viparītākhā guruvākyena labhyate  || ḥẏড়_3.79 ||
nityamabhyāsayuktasya jaṭharāgnivivardhanī  |
āhāro bahulastasya sampādyaḥ sādhakasya ca  || ḥẏড়_3.80 ||
alpāhāro yadi bhavedagnirdahati tatkṣaṇāt |
adhaḥśirāścordhvapādaḥ kṣaṇaṃ syātprathame dine  || ḥẏড়_3.81 ||
kṣaṇācca kiṃcidadhikamabhyasecca dine dine  |
valitaṃ palitaṃ caiva ṣaṇmāsordhvaṃ na dṛśyate  |
yāmamātraṃ tu yo nityamabhyasetsa tu kālajit || ḥẏড়_3.82 ||

atha vajrolī
svecchayā vartamāno'pi yogoktairniyamairvinā  |
vajrolīṃ yo vijānāti sa yogī siddhibhājanam  || ḥẏড়_3.83 ||
tatra vastudvayaṃ vakṣye durlabhaṃ yasya kasyacit |
kṣīraṃ caikaṃ dvitīyaṃ tu nārī ca vaśavartinī  || ḥẏড়_3.84 ||
mehanena śanaiḥ samyagūrdhvākuñcanamabhyaset |
puruṣo'pyathavā nārī vajrolīsiddhimāpnuyāt || ḥẏড়_3.85 ||
yatnataḥ śastanālena phūtkāraṃ vajrakandare  |
śanaiḥ śanaiḥ prakurvīta vāyusaṃcārakāraṇāt || ḥẏড়_3.86 ||
nārībhage padadbindumabhyāsenordhvamāharet |
calitaṃ ca nijaṃ bindumūrdhvamākṛṣya rakṣayet || ḥẏড়_3.87 ||
evaṃ saṃrakṣayedbinduṃ jayati yogavit |
maraṇaṃ bindupātena jīvanaṃ bindudhāraṇāt || ḥẏড়_3.88 ||
sugandho yogino dehe jāyate bindudhāraṇāt |
yāvadbinduḥ sthiro dehe tāvatkālabhayaṃ kutaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.89 ||
cittāyattaṃ nṝṇāṃ śukraṃ śukrāyattaṃ ca jīvitam  |
tasmācchukraṃ manaścaiva rakṣaṇīyaṃ prayatnataḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.90 ||
ṛtumatyā rajo'pyevaṃ nijaṃ binduṃ ca rakṣayet |
meḍhreṇākarṣayedūrdhvaṃ samyagabhyāsayogavit || ḥẏড়_3.91 ||

atha sahajoliḥ
sahajoliścāmarolirvajrolyā bheda ekataḥ  |
jale subhasma nikṣipya dagdhagomayasambhavam  || ḥẏড়_3.92 ||

sahajoliriyaṃ proktā śraddheyā yogibhiḥ sadā  |
ayaṃ śubhakaro yogo bhogayukto'pi muktidaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.94 ||
ayaṃ yogaḥ puṇyavatāṃ dhīrāṇāṃ tattvadarśinām  |
nirmatsarāṇāṃ vai sidhyenna tu matsaraśālinām  || ḥẏড়_3.95 ||

atha amarolī
pittolbaṇatvātprathamāmbudhārāṃ

niṣevyate śītalamadhyadhārā

amarīṃ yaḥ pibennityaṃ nasyaṃ kurvan dine dine  |
vajrolīmabhyasetsamyaksāmarolīti kathyate  || ḥẏড়_3.97 ||
abhyāsānniḥsṛtāṃ cāndrīṃ vibhūtyā saha miśrayet |
dhārayeduttamāṅgeṣu divyadṛṣṭiḥ prajāyate  || ḥẏড়_3.98 ||
puṃso binduṃ samākuñcya samyagabhyāsapāṭavāt |
yadi nārī rajo rakṣedvajrolyā sāpi yoginī  || ḥẏড়_3.99 ||
tasyāḥ kiṃcidrajo nāśaṃ na gacchati na saṃśayaḥ  |
tasyāḥ śarīre nādaśca bindutāmeva gacchati  || ḥẏড়_3.100 ||
sa bindustadrajaścaiva ekībhūya svadehagau  |
vajrolyabhyāsayogena sarvasiddhiṃ prayacchataḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.101 ||
rakṣedākuñcanādūrdhvaṃ yā rajaḥ sā hi yoginī  |
atītānāgataṃ vetti khecarī ca bhaveddhruvam  || ḥẏড়_3.102 ||
dehasiddhiṃ ca labhate vajrolyabhyāsayogataḥ  |
ayaṃ puṇyakaro yogo bhoge bhukte'pi muktidaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.103 ||

atha śakticālanam
kuṭilāṅgī kuṇḍalinī bhujaṅgī śaktirīśvarī  |
kuṇḍalyarundhatī caite śabdāḥ paryāyavācakāḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.104 ||
udghāṭayetkapāṭaṃ tu yathā kuṃcikayā haṭhāt |
kuṇḍalinyā tathā yogī mokṣadvāraṃ vibhedayet || ḥẏড়_3.105 ||
yena mārgeṇa gantavyaṃ brahmasthānaṃ nirāmayam  |
mukhenācchādya tadvāraṃ prasuptā parameśvarī  || ḥẏড়_3.106 ||
kandordhve kuṇḍalī śaktiḥ suptā mokṣāya yoginām  |
bandhanāya ca mūḍhānāṃ yastāṃ vetti sa yogavit || ḥẏড়_3.107 ||
kuṇḍalī kuṭilākārā sarpavatparikīrtitā  |
sā śaktiścālitā yena sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.108 ||
gaṅgāyamunayormadhye bālaraṇḍāṃ tapasvinīm  |
balātkāreṇa gṛhṇīyāttadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam  || ḥẏড়_3.109 ||
iḍā bhagavatī gaṅgā piṅgalā yamunā nadī  |
iḍāpiṅgalayormadhye bālaraṇḍā ca kuṇḍalī  || ḥẏড়_3.110 ||
pucche pragṛhya bhujaṅgīṃ suptāmudbodhayecca tām  |
nidrāṃ vihāya sā śaktirūrdhvamuttiṣṭhate haṭhāt || ḥẏড়_3.111 ||

avasthitā caiva phaṇāvatī sā
prātaśca sāyaṃ praharārdhamātram  |
prapūrya sūryātparidhānayuktyā
pragṛhya nityaṃ paricālanīyā  || ḥẏড়_3.112 ||

ūrdhvaṃ vitastimātraṃ tu vistāraṃ caturaṅgulam  |
mṛdulaṃ dhavalaṃ proktaṃ veṣṭitāmbaralakṣaṇam  || ḥẏড়_3.113 ||
sati vajrāsane pādau karābhyāṃ dhārayeddṛḍham  |
gulphadeśasamīpe ca kandaṃ tatra prapīḍayet || ḥẏড়_3.114 ||
vajrāsane sthito yogī cālayitvā ca kuṇḍalīm  |
kuryādanantaraṃ bhastrāṃ kuṇḍalīmāśu bodhayet || ḥẏড়_3.115 ||
bhānorākuñcanaṃ kuryātkuṇḍalīṃ cālayettataḥ  |
mṛtyuvaktragatasyāpi tasya mṛtyubhayaṃ kutaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.116 ||
muhūrtadvayaparyantaṃ nirbhayaṃ cālanādasau  |
ūrdhvamākṛṣyate kiṃcitsuṣumṇāyāṃ samudgatā  || ḥẏড়_3.117 ||
tena kuṇḍalinī tasyāḥ suṣumṇāyā mukhaṃ dhruvam  |
jahāti tasmātprāṇo'yaṃ suṣumṇāṃ vrajati svataḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.118 ||
tasmātsaṃcālayennityaṃ sukhasuptāmarundhatīm  |

yena saṃcālitā śaktiḥ sa yogī siddhibhājanam  |
kimatra bahunoktena kālaṃ jayati līlayā  || ḥẏড়_3.120 ||
brahmacaryaratasyaiva nityaṃ hitamitāśinaḥ  |
maṇḍalāddṛśyate siddhiḥ kuṇḍalyabhyāsayoginaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.121 ||
kuṇḍalīṃ cālayitvā tu bhastrāṃ kuryādviśeṣataḥ  |
evamabhyasyato nityaṃ yamino yamabhīḥ kutaḥ  || ḥẏড়_3.122 ||
dvāsaptatisahasrāṇāṃ nāḍīnāṃ malaśodhane  |
kutaḥ prakṣālanopāyaḥ kuṇḍalyabhyasanādṛte  || ḥẏড়_3.123 ||
iyaṃ tu madhyamā nāḍī dṛḍhābhyāsena yoginām  |
āsanaprāṇasaṃyāmamudrābhiḥ saralā bhavet || ḥẏড়_3.124 ||
abhyāse tu vinidrāṇāṃ mano dhṛtvā samādhinā  |
rudrāṇī vā parā mudrā bhadrāṃ siddhiṃ prayacchati  || ḥẏড়_3.125 ||
rājayogaṃ vinā pṛthvī rājayogaṃ vinā niśā  |

itaratra na kartavyā manovṛttirmanīṣiṇā  || ḥẏড়_3.127 ||

prāṇo mano dvayamidaṃ vilayaṃ nayedyo
mokṣaṃ sa gacchati naro na kathaṃcidanyaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.15 ||

jñātvā suṣumṇāsadbhedaṃ kṛtvā vāyuṃ ca madhyagam  |
sthitvā sadaiva susthāne brahmarandhre nirodhayet || ḥẏড়_4.16 ||
sūryacandramasau dhattaḥ kālaṃ rātrindivātmakam  |
bhoktrī suṣumnā kālasya guhyametadudāhṛtam  || ḥẏড়_4.17 ||
dvāsaptatisahasrāṇi nāḍīdvārāṇi pañjare  |
suṣumṇā śāmbhavī śaktiḥ śeṣāstveva nirarthakāḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.18 ||
vāyuḥ paricito yasmādagninā saha kuṇḍalīm  |
bodhayitvā suṣumṇāyāṃ praviśedanirodhataḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.19 ||
suṣumṇāvāhini prāṇe siddhyatyeva manonmanī  |
anyathā tvitarābhyāsāḥ prayāsāyaiva yoginām  || ḥẏড়_4.20 ||
pavano badhyate yena manastenaiva badhyate  |
manaśca badhyate yena pavanastena badhyate  || ḥẏড়_4.21 ||
hetudvayaṃ tu cittasya vāsanā ca samīraṇaḥ  |
tayorvinaṣṭa ekasmin tau dvāvapi vinaśyataḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.22 ||
mano yatra vilīyeta pavanastatra līyate  |
pavano līyate yatra manastatra vilīyate  || ḥẏড়_4.23 ||

dugdhāmbuvatsaṃmilitāvubhau tau
tulyakriyau mānasamārutau hi  |
yato maruttatra manaḥpravṛttir
yato manastatra marutpravṛttiḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.24 ||

tatraikanāśādaparasya nāśa
ekapravṛtteraparapravṛttiḥ  |
adhvastayoścendriyavargavṛttiḥ
pradhvastayormokṣapadasya siddhiḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.25 ||

rasasya manasaścaiva cañcalatvaṃ svabhāvataḥ  |
raso baddho mano baddhaṃ kiṃ na siddhyati bhūtale  || ḥẏড়_4.26 ||
mūrcchito harate vyādhīnmṛto jīvayati svayam  |
baddhaḥ khecaratāṃ dhatte raso vāyuśca pārvati  || ḥẏড়_4.27 ||
manaḥ sthairyaṃ sthiro vāyustato binduḥ sthiro bhavet |
bindusthairyātsadā sattvaṃ piṇḍasthairyaṃ prajāyate  || ḥẏড়_4.28 ||
indriyāṇāṃ mano nātho manonāthastu mārutaḥ  |
mārutasya layo nāthaḥ sa layo nādamāśritaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.29 ||
so'yamevāstu mokṣākhyo māstu vāpi matāntare  |
manaḥprāṇalaye kaścidānandaḥ sampravartate  || ḥẏড়_4.30 ||
pranaṣṭaśvāsaniśvāsaḥ pradhvastaviṣayagrahaḥ  |
niśceṣṭo nirvikāraśca layo jayati yoginām  || ḥẏড়_4.31 ||
ucchinnasarvasaṅkalpo niḥśeṣāśeṣaceṣṭitaḥ  |
svāvagamyo layaḥ ko'pi jāyate vāgagocaraḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.32 ||
yatra dṛṣṭirlayastatra bhūtendriyasanātanī  |
sā śaktirjīvabhūtānāṃ dve alakṣye layaṃ gate  || ḥẏড়_4.33 ||
layo laya iti prāhuḥ kīdṛśaṃ layalakṣaṇam  |
apunarvāsanotthānāllayo viṣayavismṛtiḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.34 ||
vedaśāstrapurāṇāni sāmānyagaṇikā iva  |
ekaiva śāmbhavī mudrā guptā kulavadhūriva  || ḥẏড়_4.35 ||

atha śāmbhavī
antarlakṣyaṃ bahirdṛṣṭirnimeṣonmeṣavarjitā  |
eṣā sā śāmbhavī mudrā vedaśāstreṣu gopitā  || ḥẏড়_4.36 ||

antarlakṣyavilīnacittapavano yogī yadā vartate
dṛṣṭyā niścalatārayā bahiradhaḥ paśyannapaśyannapi  |
mudreyaṃ khalu śāmbhavī bhavati sā labdhā prasādādguroḥ
śūnyāśūnyavilakṣaṇaṃ sphurati tattattvaṃ padaṃ śāmbhavam  || ḥẏড়_4.37 ||

śrīśāmbhavyāśca khecaryā avasthādhāmabhedataḥ  |
bhaveccittalayānandaḥ śūnye citsukharūpiṇi  || ḥẏড়_4.38 ||
tāre jyotiṣi saṃyojya kiṃcidunnamayedbhruvau  |
pūrvayogaṃ mano yuñjannunmanīkārakaḥ kṣaṇāt || ḥẏড়_4.39 ||
kecidāgamajālena kecinnigamasaṅkulaiḥ  |
kecittarkeṇa muhyanti naiva jānanti tārakam  || ḥẏড়_4.40 ||

ardhonmīlitalocanaḥ sthiramanā nāsāgradattekṣaṇaś
candrārkāvapi līnatāmupanayannispandabhāvena yaḥ  |
jyotīrūpamaśeṣabījamakhilaṃ dedīpyamānaṃ paraṃ
tattvaṃ tatpadameti vastu paramaṃ vācyaṃ kimatrādhikam  || ḥẏড়_4.41 ||

divā na pūjayelliṅgaṃ rātrau caiva na pūjayet |
sarvadā pūjayelliṅgaṃ divārātrinirodhataḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.42 ||

atha khecarī
savyadakṣiṇanāḍīstho madhye carati mārutaḥ  |
tiṣṭhate khecarī mudrā tasmin sthāne na saṃśayaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.43 ||
iḍāpiṅgalayormadhye śūnyaṃ caivānilaṃ graset |
tiṣṭhate khecarī mudrā tatra satyaṃ punaḥ punaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.44 ||
sūrcyācandramasormadhye nirālambāntare punaḥ  |
saṃsthitā vyomacakre yā sā mudrā nāma khecarī  || ḥẏড়_4.45 ||
somādyatroditā dhārā sākṣātsā śivavallabhā  |
pūrayedatulāṃ divyāṃ suṣumṇāṃ paścime mukhe  || ḥẏড়_4.46 ||
purastāccaiva pūryeta niścitā khecarī bhavet |
abhyastā khecarī mudrāpyunmanī samprajāyate  || ḥẏড়_4.47 ||
bhruvormadhye śivasthānaṃ manastatra vilīyate  |
jñātavyaṃ tatpadaṃ turyaṃ tatra kālo na vidyate  || ḥẏড়_4.48 ||
abhyasetkhecarīṃ tāvadyāvatsyādyoganidritaḥ  |
samprāptayoganidrasya kālo nāsti kadācana  || ḥẏড়_4.49 ||
nirālambaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiṃcidapi cintayet |
sabāhyābhyantaraṃ vyomni ghaṭavattiṣṭhati dhruvam  || ḥẏড়_4.50 ||
bāhyavāyuryathā līnastathā madhyo na saṃśayaḥ  |
svasthāne sthiratāmeti pavano manasā saha  || ḥẏড়_4.51 ||
evamabhyasyatastasya vāyumārge divāniśam  |
abhyāsājjīryate vāyurmanastatraiva līyate  || ḥẏড়_4.52 ||
amṛtaiḥ plāvayeddehamāpādatalamastakam  |
siddhyatyeva mahākāyo mahābalaparākramaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.53 ||
śaktimadhye manaḥ kṛtvā śaktiṃ mānasamadhyagām  |
manasā mana ālokya dhārayetparamaṃ padam  || ḥẏড়_4.54 ||
khamadhye kuru cātmānamātmamadhye ca khaṃ kuru  |
sarvaṃ ca khamayaṃ kṛtvā na kiṃcidapi cintayet || ḥẏড়_4.55 ||
antaḥ śūnyo bahiḥ śūnyaḥ śūnyaḥ kumbha ivāmbare  |
antaḥ pūrṇo bahiḥ pūrṇaḥ pūrṇaḥ kumbha ivārṇave  || ḥẏড়_4.56 ||
bāhyacintā na kartavyā tathaivāntaracintanam  |
sarvacintāṃ parityajya na kiṃcidapi cintayet || ḥẏড়_4.57 ||

saṅkalpamātrakalanaiva jagatsamagraṃ
saṅkalpamātrakalanaiva manovilāsaḥ  |
saṅkalpamātramatimutsṛja nirvikalpam
āśritya niścayamavāpnuhi rāma śāntim  || ḥẏড়_4.58 ||

karpūramanale yadvatsaindhavaṃ salile yathā  |
tathā sandhīyamānaṃ ca manastattve vilīyate  || ḥẏড়_4.59 ||
jñeyaṃ sarvaṃ pratītaṃ ca jñānaṃ ca mana ucyate  |
jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ samaṃ naṣṭaṃ nānyaḥ panthā dvitīyakaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.60 ||
manodṛśyamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiṃcitsacarācaram  |
manaso hyunmanībhāvāddvaitaṃ naivolabhyate  || ḥẏড়_4.61 ||
jñeyavastuparityāgādvilayaṃ yāti mānasam  |
manaso vilaye jāte kaivalyamavaśiṣyate  || ḥẏড়_4.62 ||
evaṃ nānāvidhopāyāḥ samyaksvānubhavānvitāḥ  |
samādhimārgāḥ kathitāḥ pūrvācāryairmahātmabhiḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.63 ||
suṣumṇāyai kuṇḍalinyai sudhāyai candrajanmane  |
manonmanyai namastubhyaṃ mahāśaktyai cidātmane  || ḥẏড়_4.64 ||
aśakyatattvabodhānāṃ mūḍhānāmapi saṃmatam  |
proktaṃ gorakṣanāthena nādopāsanamucyate  || ḥẏড়_4.65 ||

śrīādināthena sapādakoṭi
layaprakārāḥ kathitā jayanti  |
nādānusandhānakamekameva
manyāmahe mukhyatamaṃ layānām  || ḥẏড়_4.66 ||

muktāsane sthito yogī mudrāṃ sandhāya śāmbhavīm  |
śṛṇuyāddakṣiṇe karṇe nādamantāsthamekadhīḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.67 ||

śravaṇapuṭanayanayugala
ghrāṇamukhānāṃ nirodhanaṃ kāryam  |
śuddhasuṣumṇāsaraṇau
sphuṭamamalaḥ śrūyate nādaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.68 ||

ārambhaśca ghaṭaścaiva tathā paricayo'pi ca  |
niṣpattiḥ sarvayogeṣu syādavasthācatuṣṭayam  || ḥẏড়_4.69 ||

atha ārambhāvasthā
brahmagrantherbhavedbhedo hyānandaḥ śūnyasambhavaḥ  |
vicitraḥ kvaṇako dehe'nāhataḥ śrūyate dhvaniḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.70 ||
divyadehaśca tejasvī divyagandhastvarogavān  |
sampūrṇahṛdayaḥ śūnya ārambhe yogavān bhavet || ḥẏড়_4.71 ||

atha ghaṭāvasthā
dvitīyāyāṃ ghaṭīkṛtya vāyurbhavati madhyagaḥ  |
dṛḍhāsano bhavedyogī jñānī devasamastadā  || ḥẏড়_4.72 ||
viṣṇugranthestato bhedātparamānandasūcakaḥ  |
atiśūnye vimardaśca bherīśabdastadā bhavet || ḥẏড়_4.73 ||

atha paricayāvasthā
tṛtīyāyāṃ tu vijñeyo vihāyo mardaladhvaniḥ  |
mahāśūnyaṃ tadā yāti sarvasiddhisamāśrayam  || ḥẏড়_4.74 ||
cittānandaṃ tadā jitvā sahajānandasambhavaḥ  |
doṣaduḥkhajarāvyādhikṣudhānidrāvivarjitaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.75 ||

atha niṣpattyavasthā
rudragranthiṃ yadā bhittvā śarvapīṭhagato'nilaḥ  |
niṣpattau vaiṇavaḥ śabdaḥ kvaṇadvīṇākvaṇo bhavet || ḥẏড়_4.76 ||
ekībhūtaṃ tadā cittaṃ rājayogābhidhānakam  |
sṛṣṭisaṃhārakartāsau yogīśvarasamo bhavet || ḥẏড়_4.77 ||
astu vā māstu vā muktiratraivākhaṇḍitaṃ sukham  |
layodbhavamidaṃ saukhyaṃ rājayogādavāpyate  || ḥẏড়_4.78 ||
rājayogamajānantaḥ kevalaṃ haṭhakarmiṇaḥ  |
etānabhyāsino manye prayāsaphalavarjitān  || ḥẏড়_4.79 ||
unmanyavāptaye śīghraṃ bhrūdhyānaṃ mama saṃmatam  |
rājayogapadaṃ prāptuṃ sukhopāyo'lpacetasām  |
sadyaḥ pratyayasandhāyī jāyate nādajo layaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.80 ||

nādānusandhānasamādhibhājāṃ
yogīśvarāṇāṃ hṛdi vardhamānam  |
ānandamekaṃ vacasāmagamyaṃ
jānāti taṃ śrīgurunātha ekaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.81 ||

karṇau pidhāya hastābhyāṃ yaḥ śṛṇoti dhvaniṃ muniḥ  |
tatra cittaṃ sthirīkuryādyāvatsthirapadaṃ vrajet || ḥẏড়_4.82 ||
abhyasyamāno nādo'yaṃ bāhyamāvṛṇute dhvanim  |
pakṣādvikṣepamakhilaṃ jitvā yogī sukhī bhavet || ḥẏড়_4.83 ||
śrūyate prathamābhyāse nādo nānāvidho mahān  |
tato'bhyāse vardhamāne śrūyate sūkṣmasūkṣmakaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.84 ||
ādau jaladhijīmūtabherījharjharasambhavāḥ  |
madhye mardalaśaṅkhotthā ghaṇṭākāhalajāstathā  || ḥẏড়_4.85 ||
ante tu kiṅkiṇīvaṃśavīṇābhramaraniḥsvanāḥ  |
iti nānāvidhā nādāḥ śrūyante dehamadhyagāḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.86 ||
mahati śrūyamāṇe'pi meghabheryādike dhvanau  |
tatra sūkṣmātsūkṣmataraṃ nādameva parāmṛśet || ḥẏড়_4.87 ||
ghanamutsṛjya vā sūkṣme sūkṣmamutsṛjya vā ghane  |
ramamāṇamapi kṣiptaṃ mano nānyatra cālayet || ḥẏড়_4.88 ||
yatra kutrāpi vā nāde lagati prathamaṃ manaḥ  |
tatraiva susthirībhūya tena sārdhaṃ vilīyate  || ḥẏড়_4.89 ||
makarandaṃ piban bhṛṅgī gandhaṃ nāpekṣate yathā  |
nādāsaktaṃ tathā cittaṃ viṣayānnahi kāṅkṣate  || ḥẏড়_4.90 ||
manomattagajendrasya viṣayodyānacāriṇaḥ  |
samartho'yaṃ niyamane ninādaniśitāṅkuśaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.91 ||
baddhaṃ tu nādabandhena manaḥ santyaktacāpalam  |
prayāti sutarāṃ sthairyaṃ chinnapakṣaḥ khago yathā  || ḥẏড়_4.92 ||
sarvacintāṃ parityajya sāvadhānena cetasā  |
nāda evānusandheyo yogasāmrājyamicchatā  || ḥẏড়_4.93 ||
nādo'ntaraṅgasāraṅgabandhane vāgurāyate  |
antaraṅgakuraṅgasya vadhe vyādhāyate'pi ca  || ḥẏড়_4.94 ||
antaraṅgasya yamino vājinaḥ parighāyate  |
nādopāstirato nityamavadhāryā hi yoginā  || ḥẏড়_4.95 ||
baddhaṃ vimuktacāñcalyaṃ nādagandhakajāraṇāt |
manaḥpāradamāpnoti nirālambākhyakhe'ṭanam  || ḥẏড়_4.96 ||
nādaśravaṇataḥ kṣipramantaraṅgabhujaṅgamam  |
vismṛtaya sarvamekāgraḥ kutracinnahi dhāvati  || ḥẏড়_4.97 ||
kāṣṭhe pravartito vahniḥ kāṣṭhena saha śāmyati  |
nāde pravartitaṃ cittaṃ nādena saha līyate  || ḥẏড়_4.98 ||
ghaṇṭādinādasaktastabdhāntaḥkaraṇahariṇasya  |
praharaṇamapi sukaraṃ syāccharasandhānapravīṇaścet || ḥẏড়_4.99 ||
anāhatasya śabdasya dhvanirya upalabhyate  |
dhvanerantargataṃ jñeyaṃ jñeyasyāntargataṃ manaḥ  |
manastatra layaṃ yāti tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam  || ḥẏড়_4.100 ||
tāvadākāśasaṅkalpo yāvacchabdaḥ pravartate  |
niḥśabdaṃ tatparaṃ brahma paramāteti gīyate  || ḥẏড়_4.101 ||
yatkiṃcinnādarūpeṇa śrūyate śaktireva sā  |
yastattvānto nirākāraḥ sa eva parameśvaraḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.102 ||

iti nādānusandhānam

sarve haṭhalayopāyā rājayogasya siddhaye  |
rājayogasamārūḍhaḥ puruṣaḥ kālavañcakaḥ  || ḥẏড়_4.103 ||

unmanī kalpalatikā sadya eva pravartate  || ḥẏড়_4.104 ||
sadā nādānusandhānātkṣīyante pāpasaṃcayāḥ  |

sarvāvasthāvinirmuktaḥ sarvacintāvivarjitaḥ  |
