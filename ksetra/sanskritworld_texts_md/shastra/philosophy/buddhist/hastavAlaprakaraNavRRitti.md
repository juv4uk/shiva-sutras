hastavālaprakaraṇavṛttiḥ 

mañjuśrīye jñānasattvāya

namaḥ trailokye vyavahāramātre sati paramarthābhimānāttattvārthānavagāhibhiḥ sattvairvastusvabhāvavivekadvāreṇāviparyayajñānasaṃprāptaye śāstraracaneyam ||

rajjau sarpamanaskāro rajjuṃ dṛṣṭvā nirarthakaḥ |
tadaṃśān vīkṣya tatrāpi bhrāntā buddhirahāviva || 1 (6) ||

sarvāṇyāśritavastūni svarupe suvicarite |
āśritānyanyato yāvatsaṃvṛtijñānagocaraḥ || 2 (24) ||

niraṃśānāmacintyatvadaṇtyo'pyavastunā samaḥ |
bhrāntamatramataḥ prājñairna cintyaṃ paramārthataḥ || 3 (45) ||

bhrāntaṃ tadapya samyaktvadyathā bhanaṃ tathāsti na |
anarthakaṃ bhāsamānaṃ tatsadṛśātmakaṃ bhavet || 4 ||

sarvamevāśritaṃ yena vidyate sūksmabuddhinā |
tyajetsa buddhiman suṣthurāgādyahibhayaṃ yathā || 5 ||

laukikārthavicareṣu lokasiddhimanuvrajet |
kleśan sarvasaṃ tyaktumana yateta paramārthataḥ || 6 ||
