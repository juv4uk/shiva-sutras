mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāraḥ



om


namaḥ sarvabuddhabodhisatvebhyaḥ


prathamo'dhikāraḥ


arthajño'rthavibhāvanāṃ prakurute vācā padaiścāmalairduḥkhasyottaraṇāya duḥkhitajane kāruṇyatastanmayaḥ  |
dharmasyottamayānadeśitavidheḥ satveṣu tadgāmiṣu śliṣṭāmarthagatiṃ niruttaragataṃ pañcātmikāṃ darśayan  || 1.1 ||

ghaṭitamiva suvarṇaṃ vārijaṃ vā vibuddhaṃ sukṛtamiva subhojyaṃ bhujyamānaṃ kṣudhārtaiḥ  |
vidita iva sulekho ratnapeṭeva muktā vivṛta iha sa dharmaḥ prītimagryāṃ dadhāti  || 1.2 ||

yathā bimbaṃ bhūṣāprakṛtiguṇavaddarpaṇagataṃ viśiṣṭaṃ prāmodyaṃ janayati nṛṇāṃ darśanavaśāt |
tathā dharmaḥ sūktaprakṛtiguṇayukto'pi satataṃ vibhaktārthastuṣṭiṃ janayati viśiṣṭāmiha satām  || 1.3 ||

āghrāyamāṇakaṭukaṃ svādurasaṃ yathauṣadhaṃ tadvat |
dharma[rmo] dvayavyavasthā[stho] vyañjanato'rtho na ca[rthataśca] jñeyaḥ  || 1.4 ||

rājeva durārādho dharmo'yaṃ vipulagāḍhagambhīraḥ  |
ārādhitaśca tadvadvaraguṇadhanadāyako bhavati  || 1.5 ||

ratnaṃ jātyamanarthaṃ[rghaṃ]yathā'parīkṣakajanaṃ na toṣayati  |
dharmastathāyamabudhaṃ viparyayātteṣayati tadvat || 1.6 ||

ādāvavyākaraṇātsamapravṛtteragocarātsiddheḥ  |
bhāvābhāve'bhāvātpratipakṣatvādrutānyatvāt || 1.7 ||

pratyakṣacakṣuṣo buddhāḥ śāsanasya ca rakṣakāḥ  |
adhmamanyanāvṛtajñānā upekṣāto na yujyate  || 1.8 ||

vaikalyato virodhādanupāyatvāttthāpyanupadeśāt |
na śrāvakayānamidaṃ bhavati mahāyānadharmākhyam  || 1.9 ||

āśayasyopadeśasya prayogasya virodhataḥ  |
upastambhasya kālasya yathīnaṃ hīnameva tat || 1.10 ||

svake'vatārātsvasyaiva vinaye darśanādapi  |
audāryādapi gāmbhīryādaviruddhaiva dharmatā  || 1.11 ||

niśrito'niyato'vyāpī sāṃvṛtaḥ khedavānapi  |
bālāśrayo matastarkastasyāto viṣayo na tat || 1.12 ||

audāryādapi gāmbhīryātparipāko'vikalpanā  |
deśanā'to dvayasyāsmin sa copāyo niruttare  || 1.13 ||

tadasthānatrāso bhavati jagatāṃ dāhakaraṇo mahā'puṇyaskandhaprasavakaraṇāddīrdhasamayam  |
agotro'sanmitro'kṛtamatirapūrvā'citaśubhasrasatyasmin dharme patati mahato'rthādgata iha  || 1.14 ||

tadanyānyā[nyasyā?]bhāvātparamagahanatvādanugamātvicitrasyākhyānāddhruvakathanayogādbahumukhāt |
yathākhyānaṃ nārthādbhagavati ca bhāvātigahanātna dharme'smiṃsrāso bhavati viduṣāṃ yonivicayāt || 1.15 ||

śrutaṃ niśrityādau prabhavati manaskāra iha yo
manaskārājñānaṃ prabhavati ca tatvārthaviṣayam  |

tato dharmaprāptiḥ prabhavati ca tasminmatirato
yadā pratyātmaṃ sā kathamasati tasminvyavasitiḥ  || 1.16 ||

ahaṃ na boddhā na gabhīraboddhā buddhau gabhīraṃ kimatarkagamyam  |
kasmādgabhīrārthavidāṃ na mokṣa ityetaduttrāsapadaṃ na yuktam  || 1.17 ||

hīnādhimuktaḥ sunihīnadhātorhī naiḥ sahāyaiḥ parivāritasya  |
audāryagāmbhīryasudeśite'smin dharme'dhimuktiryadi nāsti siddham  || 1.18 ||

śrutānusāreṇa hi buddhimattaṃ labdhvā'śrute yaḥ prakarotyavajñām  |
śrute vicitre sati cāprameye śiṣṭe kuto niścayameti mūḍhaḥ  || 1.19 ||

yathārute'rthe parikalpyamāne svapratyayo hānimupaiti buddheḥ  |
svākhyātatāṃ ca kṣipati kṣatiṃ ca prāpnoti dharme pratighāvatīva[pratighātameva]  || 1.20 ||

manaḥ pradoṣaḥ prakṛtipraduṣṭo ['yathārute cāpi] hyayuktarūpaḥ  |
prāgeva saṃdehagatasya dharme tasmādupekṣaiva varaṃ hyadoṣā  || 1.21 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre mahāyānasiddhyadhikāraḥ prathamaḥ

dvitīyo'dhikāraḥ


śaraṇagamanaviśeṣasaṃgrahaślokaḥ  |


ratnāni yo hi śaraṇapragato'tra yāne jñeyaḥ sa eva paramaḥ śaraṇa[ṇaṃ] gatānām  |
sarvatragābhyupagamādhigamābhibhūtibhedaiścaturvidhamayārthaviśeṣaṇena  || 2.1 ||

yasmādādau duṣkara eṣa vyavasāyo duḥsādho'sau naikasahasrairapi kalpaiḥ  |
siddho yasmātsattvahitādhānamahārthastasmādagre yāna ihāgraśaraṇārthaḥ  || 2.2 ||

sarvān sattvāṃstārayituṃ yaḥ pratipanno yano jñāne sarvagate kauśalyayuktaḥ  |
yo nirvāṇe saṃsaraṇe'pyekaraso'sau [saṃsṛtiśāntyekaraso'sau] jñeyo dhīmāneṣa hi sarvatraga evam  || 2.3 ||

śaraṇagatimimāṃ gato mahārthāṃ guṇagaṇavṛddhimupaiti so'prameyām  |
sphurati jagadidaṃ kṛpāśayena prathayati cāpratimaṃ mahā[rdhaṃ]dharmam  || 2.4 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre śaraṇagamanādhikāro dvitīyaḥ

tṛtīyo'dhikāraḥ


gotraprabhedasaṃgrahaślokaḥ


sattvāgratvaṃ svabhāvaśca liṅgaṃ gotraprabhedatā  |
ādīnavo'nuśaṃsaśca dvidhaupamyaṃ caturvidhā  || 3.1 ||

dhātūnāmadhimukteśca pratipatteśca bhedataḥ  |
phalabhedopalabdheśca gotrāstitvaṃ nirūpyate  || 3.2 ||

udagratve'tha sarvatve mahārthatve'kṣayāya ca  |
śubhasya tatrimittatvātgotrāgratvaṃ vidhīyate  || 3.3 ||

prakṛtyā paripuṣṭaṃ ca āśrayaścāśritaṃ ca tat |
sadasaccaiva vijñeyaṃ guṇottāraṇatārthataḥ  || 3.4 ||

kāruṇyamadhimuktiśca kṣāntiścādiprayogataḥ  |
samācāraḥ subhasyāpi gotreliṅgaṃ nirupyate  || 3.5 ||

niyatāniyataṃ gotramahāryaṃ hāryameva ca  |
pratyairgotrabhedo'yaṃ samāsena caturvidhaḥ  || 3.6 ||

kleśābhyāsaḥ kumitratvaṃ vidhātaḥ paratantratā  |
gotrasyādīnavo jñeyaḥ samāsena caturvidhaḥ  || 3.7 ||

cirādapāyagamanamāśumokṣaśca tatra ca  |
tanuduḥkhopasaṃvittiḥ sodvegā sattvapācanā  || 3.8 ||

suvarṇagotravatjñeyamameyaśubhatāśrayaḥ  |
jñānanirmalatāyogaprabhāvāṇāṃ ca niśrayaḥ  || 3.9 ||

suratnagotravatjñeyaṃ mahābodhinimittataḥ  |
mahājñānasamādhyāryamahāsattvārthaniśrayāt || 3.10 ||

aikāntiko duścarite'sti kaścitkaścitsamudghātitaśukladharmā  |
amokṣabhāgīyaśubho'sti kaścinnihīnaśuklo'styapi hetuhīnaḥ  || 3.11 ||

gāmbhīryaudāryavāde parahitakaraṇāyodite dīrghadharme ajñātvaivādhimuktirbhavati suvipulā saṃprapattikṣamā ca  |
saṃpattiścāvasāne dvayagataparamā yadbhavatyeva teṣāṃ tajjñeyaṃ bodhisattvaprakṛtiguṇavatastatprapuṣṭācca gotrāt || 3.12 ||

suvipulaguṇabodhivṛkṣavṛddhyai dhanasukhaduḥkhaśamopalabdhaye ca  |
svaparahitasukhakriyā phalatvādbhavati samudagra[samūlamudagra]gotrametat || 3.13 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre gotrādhikārastṛtīyaḥ

caturtho'dhikāraḥ


cittotpādalakṣaṇe ślokaḥ  |


mahotsāhā mahārambhā mahārthāya mahodayā  |
cetanā bodhisattvānāṃ dvayārthā cittasaṃbhavaḥ  || 4.1 ||

cittotpādo'dhimokṣo'sau śuddhādhyāśayiko'paraḥ  |
vaipākyo bhūmiṣu matastathāvaraṇavarjitaḥ  || 4.2 ||

karuṇāmūla iṣṭo'sau sadāsattvahitāśayaḥ  |
dharmādhimokṣastajjñānaparyeṣṭyālambanastathā  || 4.3 ||

uttaracchandayāno'sau pratiṣṭhāśīlasaṃvṛtiḥ  |
utthāpanā vipakṣasya paripantho'dhivāsanā  || 4.4 ||

śubhavṛddhyanuśaṃso'bhau puṇyajñānamayaḥ sa hi  |
sadāpāramitāyoganiryāṇaśca sa kathyate  || 4.5 ||

bhūmiparyavasāno'sau pratisvaṃ tatprayogataḥ  |
vijñeyo bodhisattvānāṃ cittotpādaviniścayaḥ  || 4.6 ||

mitrabalādhetubalānmūlabalācchū tabalācchubhābhyāsāt |
adṛḍhadṛḍhodaya uktaścittotpādaḥ parākhyānāt || 4.7 ||

sūpāsitasaṃbuddhe susaṃbhṛtajñānapuṇyasaṃbhāre  |
dharmeṣu nirvikalpajñānaprasavātparamatāsya  || 4.8 ||

dharmeṣu ca satveṣu ca tatkṛtyeṣūttame ca buddhatve  |
samacittopā[ttopa]lambhātprāmodyaviśiṣṭatā tasya  || 4.9 ||

janmaudāryaṃ tasminnutsāhaḥ śuddhirāśayasyāpi  |
kauśalyaṃ pariśiṣṭe niryāṇaṃ caiva vijñeyam  || 4.10 ||

dharmādhimuktibījātpāramitāśreṣṭhamātṛto jātaḥ  |
dhyānamaye sukhagarbhe karuṇā saṃvardhikā dhātrī  || 4.11 ||

audāryaṃ vijñeyaṃ praṇidhānamahādaśābhinirhārāt |
utsāho boddhavyo duṣkaradīghāghikākhedāt || 4.12 ||

āsannabodhibodhāttadupāyajñānalābhataścāpi  |
āśayaśuddhirjñeyā kauśalyaṃ tvanyabhūmigatam  || 4.13 ||

niryāṇaṃ vijñeyaṃ yathāvyavasthānamanasikāreṇa  |
tatkalpanatājñānādavikalpanayā ca tasyaiva  || 4.14 ||

pṛthivīsama utpādaḥ kalyāṇasuvarṇasaṃnibhaścānyaḥ  |
śuklanavacandrasadṛśo vahniprakhyo'parocchrāyaḥ [jñeyaḥ]  || 4.15 ||

bhūyo mahānidhānavadanyo ratnākaro yathaivānyaḥ  |
sāgarasadṛśo jñeyo vajraprakhyo'calendranibhaḥ  || 4.16 ||

bhaiṣajyarājasadṛśo mahāsuhṛtsaṃnibho'paro jñeyaḥ  |
cintāmaṇiprakāśo dinakarasadṛśo'paro jñeyaḥ  || 4.17 ||

gandharvamadhuraghoṣavadanyo rājopamo'paro jñeyaḥ  |
koṣṭhāgāraprakhyo mahāpathasamastathaivānyaḥ  || 4.18 ||

yānasamo vijñeyo gandharvasamaśca vetasaga[cetasaḥ]prabhavaḥ  |
ānandaśabdasadṛśo mahānadīśrota[srotaḥ]sadṛśaśca  || 4.19 ||

meghasadṛśaśca kathataścittotpādo jinātmajānāṃ hi  |
tasmāttathā guṇāḍhyaṃ cittaṃ muditaiḥ samutpādyam  || 4.20 ||

parārthacittāttadupāyalābhato mahābhisaṃdhyarthasutatvadarśanāt |
mahārhacittodayavarjitā janāḥ śamaṃ gamiṣyanti vihāya tatsukham  || 4.21 ||

sahodayāccittavarasya dhīmataḥ susaṃvṛtaṃ cittamanantaduṣkṛtāt |
sukhena duḥkhena ca modate sadā śubhī kṛpāluśca vivardhana, dvayam  || 4.22 ||

yadānapekṣaḥ svaśarīrajīvite parārthamabhyeti paraṃ pariśramam  |
paropaghātena tathāvidhaḥ kathaṃ sa duṣkṛte karmaṇi saṃpravatsyati  || 4.23 ||

māyopamānvīkṣya sa sarvadharmānudhānayātrāmiva copapattīḥ  |
kleśācca duḥkhācca bibheti nāsau saṃpattikāle'tha vipattikāle  || 4.24 ||

svakā guṇāḥ sattvahitācca modaḥ saṃcintyajanmarddhivikurvitaṃ ca  |
vibhūṣaṇaṃ bhojanamagrabhūmiḥ krīḍāratirnityakṛpātmakānām  || 4.25 ||

parārthamudyogavataḥ kṛpātmano hyavīcirapyeti yato'sya ramyatām  |
kutaḥ punastrasyati tādṛśo bhavan parāśrayairduḥkhasamudbhavairbhave  || 4.26 ||

mahākṛpācāryasadoṣitātmanaḥ parasya duḥkhairupataptacetasaḥ  |
parasya kṛtye samupasthite punaḥ paraiḥ samādāpanato'tilajjanā  || 4.27 ||

śirasi vinihitoccasatvabhāraḥ śithilagatirnahi śobhate'grasattvaḥ  |
svaparavavidhabandhanātibaddhaḥ śataguṇamutsahamarhati prakartum  || 4.28 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre cittotpādādhikāraścaturthaḥ

pañcamo'dhikāraḥ


pratipattilakṣaṇe ślokaḥ  |


mahāśrayārambhaphalodayātmikā jinātmajānāṃ pratipattiriṣyate  |
sadā mahādānamahādhivāsanā mahārthasaṃpādanakṛtyakārikā  || 5.1 ||

paratralabdhvātmasamānacittatāṃ svato'dhi vā śreṣṭhatareṣṭatāṃ pare  |
tathātmano'nyārthaviśiṣṭasaṃjñinaḥ svakārthatā kā katamā parārthatā  || 5.2 ||

paratra loko na tathātinirdayaḥ pravartate tāpanakarmaṇāripau  |
yathā parārthaṃ bhṛśaduḥkhatāpane kṛpātmakaḥ svātmani saṃpravartate  || 5.3 ||

nikṛṣṭamadhyottamadharmatāsthite sudeśanāvarjanatā'vatāraṇā  |
vinītirarthe paripācanā śubhe tathāvavādasthitibuddhimuktayaḥ  || 5.4 ||

guṇairviśiṣṭaiḥ samudāgamastathā kulodayo vyākaraṇābhiṣiktatā  |
tathāgatajñānamanuttaraṃ padaṃ parārtha eṣa tryadhiko daśātmakaḥ  || 5.5 ||

janānurūpā'viparītadeśanā nirunnatā cāpyamamā vicakṣaṇā  |
kṣamā ca dāntā ca sudūragā'kṣayā jinātmajānāṃ pratipattiruttamā  || 5.6 ||

mahābhaye kāmijanaḥ pravartate cale viparyāsasukhe bhavapriyaḥ  |
pratisvamādhipraśame śamapriyaḥ sadā tu sarvādhiga[śa]me kṛpātmakaḥ  || 5.7 ||

jano vimūḍhaḥ svasukhārthamudyataḥ sadā tadaprāpya paraiti duḥkhatām  |
sadā tu dhīro hi parārthamudyato dvayārthamādhāya paraiti nirvṛtim  || 5.8 ||

yathā yathā hyakṣavicitragocare pravartate cāragato jinātmajaḥ  |
tathā tathā yuktasamānatāpadairhitāya sattveṣvabhisaṃskaroti tat || 5.9 ||

sadā'svatantrīkṛtadoṣacetane jane na saṃdoṣamupaiti buddhimān  |
akāmakāreṇa hi viprapattayo jane bhavantīti kṛpāvivṛddhitaḥ  || 5.10 ||

bhavagatisakalābhibhūyagantrī paramaśamānugatā prapattireva  |
vividhaguṇagaṇairvivardhamānā jagadupagu[gṛ?]hya sadā kṛpāśayena  || 5.11 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre pratipattyadhikāraḥ pañcamaḥ

ṣaṣṭho'dhikāraḥ


paramārthalakṣaṇavibhāge ślokaḥ  |


na sanna cāsanna tathā na cānyathā na jāyate vyeti na cāvahīyate  |
na vardhate nāpi viśudhyate punarviśudhyate tatparamārthalakṣaṇam  || 6.1 ||

na cātmadṛṣṭiḥ svayamātmalakṣaṇā na cāpi duḥsaṃsthitatā vilakṣaṇā  |
dvayānna cānyadbhrama eṣata[tū]ditastataśca mokṣo bhramamātrasaṃkṣayaḥ  || 6.2 ||

kathaṃ jano vibhramamātramāśritaḥ paraiti duḥkhaprakṛtiṃ na saṃtatām  |
avedako vedaka eva duḥkhito na duḥkhito dharmamayo na tanmayaḥ  || 6.3 ||

pratītyabhāvaprabhave kathaṃ janaḥ samakṣavṛttiḥ śrayate'nyakāritam  |
tamaḥ prakāraḥ katamo'yamīdṛśo yato'vipaśyansadasannirīkṣate  || 6.4 ||

ta cāntaraṃ kiṃcana vidyate'nayoḥ sadarthavṛttyā śamajanmanoriha  |
tathāpi janmakṣayato vidhīyate śamasya lābhaḥ śubhakarmakāriṇām  || 6.5 ||

saṃbhṛtya saṃbhāramanantapāraṃ jñānasya puṇyasya ca bodhisattvaḥ  |
dharmeṣu cintāsuviniśri[ści]tatvājjalpānvayāmarthagatiṃ paraiti  || 6.6 ||

arthānsa vijñāya ca jalpamātrān saṃtiṣṭhate tannibhacittamātre  |
pratyakṣatāmeti ca dharmadhātustasmādviyuktodvayalakṣaṇena  || 6.7 ||

nāstīti cittātparametya buddhyā cittasya nāstitvamupaiti tasmāt |
dvayasya nāstitvamupetya dhīmān saṃtiṣṭhate'tadgatidharmaghātau  || 6.8 ||

akalpanājñānabalena dhīmataḥ samānuyātena samantataḥ sadā  |
tadāśrayo gahvaradoṣasaṃcayo mahagadeneva viṣaṃ nirasyate  || 6.9 ||

munivihitasudharmasuvyavastho matimupadhāya samūladharmadhātau  |
smṛtima[ga]timavagamya kalpamātrāṃ vrajati guṇārṇavapāramāśudhīraḥ  || 6.10 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāre tatvādhikāraḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ

saptamo'dhikāraḥ


prabhāvalakṣaṇavibhāge ślokaḥ  |


utpattivākcittaśubhāśubhādhi tatsthānaniḥsārapadā parokṣam  |
jñānaṃ hi sarvatragasaprabhedeṣvavyāhataṃ dhīragataḥ prabhāvaḥ  || 7.1 ||

dhyānaṃ caturthaṃ suviśuddhametya niṣkalpanājñānaparigraheṇa  |
yathāvyavasthānamanaskriyātaḥ prabhāvasiddhiṃ paramāṃ paraiti  || 7.2 ||

yenāryadivyāpratimairvihārairbrāhmaiśca nityaṃ viharatyudāraiḥ  |
buddhāṃśca sattvāṃśca sa dikṣu gatvā saṃmānayatyānayate viśuddhim  || 7.3 ||

māyopamānpaśyati lokadhātūnsarvānsasattvānsavivartanāśān  |
saṃdarśayatyeva ca tānyatheṣṭaṃ vaśī vicitrairapi sa prakāraiḥ  || 7.4 ||

raśmipramokṣairbhṛśaduḥkhitāṃśca āpāyikānsvargagatānkaroti  |
mārānvayān kṣubdhavimānaśobhān saṃkampayaṃsrāsayate samārān  || 7.5 ||

samādhivikrīḍitamaprameyaṃ saṃdarśayatyagragaṇasyamadhye  |
sakarmajanmottamanirmitaiśca sattvārthamātiṣṭhati sarvakālam  || 7.6 ||

jñānavaśitvātsamupaiti śuddhiṃ kṣetraṃ yathākāmanidarśanāya

abuddhanāmeṣu[?] ca buddhanāma saṃśrāvaṇāttānkṣipate'nyadhātau  || 7.7 ||

śakto bhavatyeva ca satvapāke saṃjātapakṣaḥ śakuniryathaiva  |
buddhātpraśaṃsāṃ labhate'timātrāmādeyavākyo bhavati prajānām  || 7.8 ||

kimataḥ paramāścaryaṃ yadduḥkhaṃ saukhyamabhibhavati sarva  |

karuṇā bodhisatvānāṃ sukhādduḥkhāttadanvayāt |

karuṇā bodhisattvānāṃ samā jñeyā tadāśayāt |

maitrādribhāvanāgrā svacittato dharmato'dhimokṣācca  |

kṣetraśuddhiśca satvānāṃ pākaniṣpattireva ca  || 20.20 ||

samādhidhāraṇīnāṃ ca niṣpattiḥ paramaṃ phalaṃ  |
caturvidhaṃ phalaṃ hyetatcaturbhūmisamāśritam  || 20.21 ||

dharmatāṃ pratividhyeha śīlaskandhasya śodhanā  |
samādhiprajñāskandhasya tata ūrdhvaṃ viśodhanā  || 20.22 ||

vimuktimuktijñānasya tadanyāsu viśodhanā  |
caturvidhādāvaraṇātpratighātāvṛterapi  || 20.23 ||

aniṣpannāśca niṣpannā vijñeyāḥ sarvabhūmayaḥ  |
niṣpannā apyaniṣpannā niṣpannāśca punarmatāḥ  || 20.24 ||

niṣpattirvijñeyā yathāvyavasthānamanasikāreṇa  |
tatkalpanatājñānādavikalpanayā ca tasyaiva  || 20.25 ||

bhāvanā api niṣpattiracintyaṃ sarvabhūmiṣu  |
pratyātmavedanīyatvātbuddhānāṃ viṣayādapi  || 20.26 ||

adhimuktirhi sarvatra sālokā liṅgamiṣyate  |
alīnatvamadīnatvamaparapratyayātmatā  || 20.27 ||

prativedhaśca sarvatra sarvatra samacittatā  |
aneyānunayopāyajñānaṃ maṇḍalajanma ca  || 20.28 ||

nācchando na ca lubdhahrasvahṛdayo na krodhano nālaso nāmaitrīkarūṇāśayo na kumatiḥ kalpairvikalpairhataḥ  |
no vikṣiptamatiḥ sukhairna ca hato duḥkhairna vā [vyā]vartate satyaṃ mitramupāśritaḥ śrutaparaḥ pūjāparaḥ śāstari  || 20.29 ||

sarvaṃ puṇyasamuccayaṃ suvipulaṃ kṛtvānyasādhāraṇaṃ saṃbodhau pariṇāmayatyaharaharyo hyuttamopāyavit |

jātaḥ svāyatane sadā śubhakaraḥ krīḍatyabhijñāguṇaiḥ sarveṣāmuparisthito guṇanidhirjñeyaḥ sa buddhātmanaḥ  || 20.30 ||

śamathe vipaśyanāyāṃ ca dvayapañcātmako mataḥ  |
dhīmatāmanuśaṃso hi sarvathā sarvabhūmiṣu  || 20.31 ||

paśyatāṃ bodhimāsannāṃ satvārthasya ca sādhanaṃ  |
tīvra utpadyate modo muditā tena kathyate  || 20.32 ||

dauḥ śīlyābhogavaimalyādvimalā bhūmirucyate  |
mahādharmāvabhāsasya karaṇācca prabhākarī  || 20.33 ||

arcirbhūtā yato dharmā bodhipakṣāḥ pradāhakāḥ  |
arciṣmatīti tadyogātsā bhūmirdvayadāhataḥ  || 20.34 ||

satvānāṃ paripākaśca svacittasya ca rakṣaṇā  |
dhīmadbhirjīyate duḥkhaṃ durjayā tena kathyate  || 20.35 ||

ābhimukhyāddvyasyeha saṃsārasyāpi nirvṛteḥ  |
uktā hyabhimukhī bhūmiḥ prajñāpāramitāśrayāt || 20.36 ||

ekāyanapathaśleṣādbhūmirdūraṃgamā matā  |
dvyasaṃjñāvicalanādacalā ca nirucyate  || 20.37 ||

pratisaṃvinmatisādhutvādbhūmiḥ sādhumati matā  |
dharmameghā dvayavyāpterdharmākāśasya meghavat || 20.38 ||

vividhe śubhanirhāre ratyā viharaṇātsadā  |
sarvatra bodhisatvānāṃ vihārabhūmayo matāḥ  || 20.39 ||

bhūyo bhūyo'mitāsvāsu ūrdhvaṃgamanayogataḥ  |
bhūtāmitābhayārthāya ta eveṣṭā hi bhūmayaḥ  || 20.40 ||

bhūmilābhe[bho]'dhimukteśca cariteṣu ca vartanāt |
prativedhācca bhūmīnāṃ niṣpatteśca caturvidhaḥ  || 20.41 ||

mahāyāne'dhimuktānāṃ hīnayāne ca dehināṃ  |
dvayorāvarjanārthāya vinayāya ca deśitāḥ  |
caryāścatasro dhīrāṇāṃ yathāsūtrānusārataḥ  || 20.42 ||

anukampakasatveṣu saṃyogavigamāśaya  |
aviyogāśaya saukhyahitāśaya namo'stute  || 20.43 ||

sarvāvaraṇanirmukta sarvalokābhibhū mune  |
jñānena jñeyaṃ vyāptaṃ te muktacitta namo'stute  || 20.44 ||

aśeṣaṃ sarvasatvānāṃ sarvakleśavināśaka  |
kleśaprahāraka kliṣṭasānukrośa namo'stute  || 20.45 ||

anābhoga nirāsaṅga avyāghāta samāhita  |
sadaiva sarvapraśnānāṃ visarjaka namo'stu te  || 20.46 ||

āśraye'thāśrite deśye vākye jñāne ca deśike  |
avyāhatamate nityaṃ sudeśika namo'stute  || 20.47 ||

upetya vacanaisteṣāṃ carijña āgatau gatau  |
niḥ sāre caiva satvānāṃ svavavāda namo'stu te  || 20.48 ||

satpauruṣyaṃ prapadyante tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā sarvadehinaḥ  |
dṛṣṭamātrātprasādasya vidhāyaka namo'stu te  || 20.49 ||

ādānasthānasaṃtyāganirmāṇapariṇāmane  |
samādhijñānavaśitāmanuprāpta namo'stu te  || 20.50 ||

upāye śaraṇe śuddhau satvānāṃ vipravādane  |
mahāyāne ca niryāṇe mārabhañja namo'stu te  || 20.51 ||

jñānaprahāṇaniryāṇavighnakārakadeśika  |
svaparārthe'nyatīrthyānāṃ nirādhṛṣya namo'stu te  || 20.52 ||

vi[ni]gṛhyavaktā parṣatsu dvyasaṃkleśavarjita  |
nirārakṣa asaṃmoṣa gaṇakarṣa namo'stu te  || 20.53 ||

cāre vihāre sarvatra nāstyasarvajñaceṣṭitaṃ  |
sarvadā tava sarvajña bhūtārthika namo'stu te  || 20.54 ||

sarvasattvārthakṛtyeṣu kālaṃ tvaṃ nātivartase  |
abandhyakṛtya satatamasaṃmoṣaḥ namo'stu te  || 20.55 ||

sarvalokamahorātraṃ ṣaṭkṛtvaḥ pratyavekṣase  |
mahākaruṇayā yukta hitāśaya namo'stu te  || 20.56 ||

cāreṇādhigamenāpi jñānenāpi ca karmaṇā  |
sarvaśrāvakapratyekabuddhottama namo'stu te  || 20.57 ||

tribhiḥ kāyairmahābodhiṃ sarvākārāmupāgata  |
sarvatra sarvasatvānāṃ kāṅkṣāchida namo'stu te  || 20.58 ||

niravagraha nirdoṣa niṣkāluṣyānavasthita  |
āniṅkṣya sarvadharmeṣu niṣprapañca namo'stu te  || 20.59 ||

niṣpannaparamārthoṃ'si sarvabhūmiviniḥsṛtaḥ  |
sarvasatvāgratāṃ prāptaḥ sarvasatvavimocakaḥ  || 20.60 ||

akṣayairasamairyukto guṇairlokeṣu dṛśyase  |
maṇḍaleṣvapyadṛśyaśca sarvathā devamānuṣaiḥ  || 20.61 ||

mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāreṣu vyavadātasamayamahābodhisatvabhāṣite caryāpratiṣṭhādhikāro nāmaikaviṃśatitamo'dhikāraḥ
