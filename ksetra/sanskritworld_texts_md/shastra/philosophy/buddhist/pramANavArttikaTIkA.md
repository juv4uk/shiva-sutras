4 ||| punarityādi | na tu sāmānyenaiva vṛttita ityanantaramuktatvādayuktaṃ codyam | satyametadadhikaṃ tu parihāramabhidhātumupanyāsaḥ saṃketenaa viṣayīkṛtaḥ saṃketitastamāhuḥ śabdāḥ vyavahārāya sa smṛta iti saṃketaḥ | yasmin svalakṣaṇe saṃketaḥ kriyata itīṣyate | tata svalakṣaṇaṃ tadā vyavahārakāle nāsti na hi saṃketa |||
