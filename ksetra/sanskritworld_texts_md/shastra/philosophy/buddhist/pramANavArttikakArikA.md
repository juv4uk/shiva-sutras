pramāṇavārtikam

prathamaḥ paricchedaḥ
pramāṇasiddhiḥ

vidhūtakalpanājālagambhīrodāramūrtaye |
namaḥ samantabhadrāya samantaspharaṇatviṣe || 1.1 ||
prāyaḥ prākṛtasaktirapratibalaprajño janaḥ kevalam |
nānarthyeva subhāṣitaiḥ parigato vidveṣṭyapīrṣyāmalaiḥ |
tenāyaṃ na paropakāra iti naścintāpi cetaściraṃ sūktābhyāsavivardhitavyasanamityatrānubaddhaspṛham || 1.2 ||
pramāṇamavisaṃvādi jñānamarthakriyāsthitiḥ |
avisaṃvādanaṃ śābde'pyabhiprāyanivedanāt || 1.3 ||
vaktṛvyāapāraviṣayo yo'rtho buddhau prakāśate |
prāmāṇyaṃ tatra śabdasya nāthatattvanibandhanam || 1.4 ||
gṛhītagrahaṇānneṣṭaṃ sāṃvṛtaṃ dhīpramāṇatā |
pravṛttestatpradhānatvātheyopādeyavastuni || 1.5 ||
viṣayākārabhedācca dhiyo'dhigamabhedataḥ |
bhāvādevāsya tadbhāve svarūpasya svato gatiḥ || 1.6 ||
prāmāṇyaṃ vyavahāreṇa śāstraṃ mohanirvatanam |
ajñātārthaprakāśo vā svarūpādhigateḥ param || 1.7 ||
prāptaṃ sāmānyavijñānamavijñāte svalakṣaṇe |
yajjñānamityabhiprāyātsvalakṣaṇavicārataḥ || 1.8 ||
tadvatpramāṇaṃ bhagavānabhūtavinivṛttaye |
bhūtoktiḥ sādhanāpekṣā tato yuktā pramāṇatā || 1.9 ||
nityaṃ pramāṇaṃ naivāsti prāmāṇyādvastusadgateḥ |
jñeyānityatayā tasyā adhrauvyātkramajanmanām || 1.10 ||
nityādutpattiviśleṣādapekṣāyā ayogataḥ |
kathañcinnoparkāyatvātanitye'pyapramāṇatā || 1.11 ||
sthitvāpravṛttiḥ saṃsthānaviśeṣārthakriyādiṣu |
iṣṭasiddhirasiddhirvā dṛṣṭānte saṃśayo'thavā || 1.12 ||
siddhaṃ yādṛgadhiṣṭhātṛbhāvābhāvānuvṛttimat |
sanniveśādi tadyuktaṃ tasmādyadanumīyate || 1.13 ||
vastubhede prasiddhasya śabdasāmyādabhedinaḥ |
na yuktānumitiḥ pāṇḍudravyādiva hutāśane || 1.14 ||
anyathā kumbhakāreṇa mṛdvikārasya kasyacit |
ghaṭādeḥ karaṇātsidhyedvalmīkasyāpi tatkṛtiḥ || 1.15 ||
sādhyenānugamātkārye sāmānyenāpi sādhane |
sambandhibhedādbhedoktidoṣaḥ kāryasamo mataḥ || 1.16 ||
jātyantare prasiddhasya śabdasāmānyadarśanāt |
na yuktaṃ sādhanaṃ gotvādvāgādīnāṃ viṣāṇavat || 1.17 ||
vivakṣāparatantratvānna śabdāḥ santi kutra vā |
tadbhāvādarthasiddhau tu sarva sarvasya sidhyati || 1.18 ||
etena kāpilādīnāmacaitanyādi cintitam |
anityādeśca caitanyaṃ maraṇāttvagapohataḥ || 1.19 ||
vastusvarūpe siddhe'yaṃ nyāyaḥ siddhe viśeṣaṇam |
abādhakamasiddhāvapyākāśāśrayavaddhvaneḥ || 1.20 ||
asiddhāvapi śabdasya śiddhe vastuni sidhyati |
aulūkyasya yathā bauddhenoktaṃ mūrtyādisādhanam || 1.21 ||
tasyaiva vyabhicārādau śabde'pyavyabhicāriṇa |
doṣavatsādhanaṃ jñeyaṃ vastuno vastusiddhitaḥ || 1.22 ||
yathā tatkāraṇaṃ vastu tathaiva tadakāraṇam |
yadā tatkāraṇaṃ kena mataṃ neṣṭamakāraṇam || 1.23 ||
śastrauṣadhābhisambandhāccaitrasya vraṇarohaṇe |
asambaddhasya kiṃ sthāṇoḥ kāraṇatvaṃ na kalpyate || 1.24 ||
svabhāvabhedena vinā vyāpāro'pi na yujyate |
nityasyāvyatirekitvātsāmarthya ca duranvayam || 1.25 ||
yeṣu satsu bhavatyeva yattebhyo'nyasya kalpane |
taddhetutvena sarvatra hetunāmanavasthitiḥ || 1.26 ||
svabhāvapariṇāmena heturaṅkurajanmani |
bhūmyādistasya saṃskāre tadviśeṣasya darśanāt || 1.27 ||
yathā viśeṣeṇa vinā viṣayendriyasaṃhatiḥ |
buddherhetustathedaṃ cenna tatrāpi viśeṣataḥ || 1.28 ||
pṛthakpṛthagaśaktānāṃ svabhāvātiśaye'sati |
saṃhatāvapyasāmarthya syātsiddho'tiśayastataḥ || 1.29 ||
tasmātpṛthagaśakteṣu yeṣu sambhāvyate guṇaḥ |
saṃhatau hetutā teṣāṃ neśvarāderabhedataḥ || 1.30 ||
prāmāṇyañca parokṣārthajñānaṃ yatsādhanasya ca |
abhāvānnāstyanuṣṭhānamiti kecitpracakṣate || 1.31 ||
jñānavānmṛgyate kaścittaduktapratipattaye |
ajñopadeśakaraṇe vipralambhanaśaṅkibhiḥ || 1.32 ||
tasmādanuṣṭheyagataṃ jñānamasya vicāryatām |
kīṭasaṃkhyāparijñānaṃ tasya naḥ kvopayujyate || 1.33 ||
heyopādeyatattvasya sābhyupāyasya vedakaḥ |
yaḥ pramāṇamasāviṣṭo na tu savasya vedakaḥ || 1.34 ||
dūraṃ paśyatu vā mā vā tattvamiṣṭaṃ tu paśyatu |
pramāṇaṃ dūradarśī cedeta gṛdhrānupāsmahe || 1.35 ||
sādhanaṃ karuṇābhyāsātsā buddherdehasaṃśrayāt |
asiddhi'bhyāsa iti cennāśrayapratiṣedhataḥ || 1.36 ||
prāṇāpānendriyadhiyāṃ dehādeva na kevalāt |
sajātinirapekṣāṇāṃ janma janmaparigrahe || 1.37 ||
atiprasaṅgātya ddṛṣṭaṃ pratisandhānaśaktimat |
kimāsīttasya yannāsti paśyādyena na sandhimat || 1.38 ||
na sa kaścitpṛthivyāderaṃśo yatra na jantavaḥ |
saṃsvedajādyā jāyante sarva bījātmakaṃ tataḥ || 1.39 ||
tatsajātyanapekṣāṇāmakṣādīnāṃ samudbhave |
pariṇamo yathaikasya syātsarvasyāviśeṣataḥ || 1.40 ||
pratyekamupaghāte'pi nendriyāṇāṃ manomateḥ |
upaghāto'sti bhaṅge'syāsteṣāṃ bhaṅgaśca dṛśyate || 1.41 ||
tasmātsthityāśrayo buddherbuddhimeva samāśritaḥ |
kaścinnimittamakṣāṇāṃ tasmādakṣāṇi buddhitaḥ || 1.42 ||
yādṛśyākṣepikā sāsītpaścādapyastu tādṛśī |
tajjñānairupakāryatvāduktaṃ kāyāśritaṃ manaḥ || 1.43 ||
yadyapyakṣairvinā buddhirna tānyapi tayā vinā |
tathāpyanyo'nyahetutvaṃ tato'pyanyo'nyahetuke || 1.44 ||
nākramātkramiṇo bhāvo nāpyapekṣyāviśeṣiṇaḥ |
kramādbhavantī dhīḥ kāyātkramaṃ tasyāpi śaṃsati || 1.45 ||
pratikṣaṇamapūrvasya pūrvaḥ pūrvaḥ kṣaṇo bhavet |
tasya heturato heturdṛṣṭa evāstu sarvadā || 1.46 ||
cittāntarasya sandhāne ko virodho'ntyacetasaḥ |
tadvadapyarhataścittasandhānaṃ kuto matam || 1.47 ||
asiddhārthaḥ pramāaṇena kiṃ siddhānto'nugamyate |
hetorvaikalyatastaccetkiṃ tadevātra noditam || 1.48 ||
taddhīvadgrahaṇaprāptermanojñānaṃ na sendriyāt |
jñānotpādanasāmarthyabhedānna sakalādapi || 1.49 ||
acetanatvānnānyasmādhetvabhedātsahasthitiḥ |
akṣavadrūparasavadarthadvāreṇa vikriyā || 1.50 ||
sattopakāriṇī yasya nityaṃ tadanubandhataḥ |
sa hetuḥ saptamī tasmādutpādāditi cocyate || 1.51 ||
astūpakārako vāpi kadāciccittasantateḥ |
vahnayādivadghaṭādināṃ vinivṛttirna tāvatā || 1.52 ||
anivṛttiprasaṅśca dehe tiṣṭhati cetasaḥ |
tadbhāvabhāvādvaśyatvātprāṇāpānau tato na tat || 1.53 ||
preraṇākarṣaṇe vāyoḥ prayatnena vinā kutaḥ |
nirhrāsāatiśayāpattirnirhrāsātiśayāttayoḥ || 1.54 ||
tulyaḥ prasaṅgo'pi tayoḥ na tulyaṃ cittakāraṇe |
sthityāvedhakamanyacca yataḥ kāraṇamiṣyate || 1.55 ||
na doṣairviguṇo deho heturvartyādivadyadi |
mṛte śamīkṛte doṣe punarujjīvanaṃ bhavet || 1.56 ||
nivṛtte'pyanale kāṣṭhavikārāvinivṛttivat |
tasyānivṛttiriti cenna cikitsāprayogataḥ || 1.57 ||
apunarbhāvataḥ kiñcadvikārajananaṃ kvacit |
ciñcidviparyayādagniryathā kāṣṭhasuvarṇayoḥ || 1.58 ||
ādyasyālpo'pyasaṃhāryaḥ pratyāneyastu yatkṛtaḥ |
vikāraḥ syātpunarbhāvaḥ tasya hemnikharatvavat || 1.59 ||
durlabhatvātsamādhāturasādhyaṃ kiñcidīritam |
āyuḥkṣayādvā doṣe tu kevale nāstyasādhyatā || 1.60 ||
mṛte viṣādisaṃhārāttaṃddaśacchedato'pi vā |
vikārahetorvigame sa nocchavasiti sa nocchvasiti kiṃ punaḥ || 1.61 ||
upādānāvikāreṇa nopādeyasya vikriyā |
kartu śakyāvikāreṇa mṛdaḥ kuṇḍādike yathā || 1.62 ||
avikṛtya hi yadvastu yaḥ padārtho vikāryate |
upādānaṃ na tattasya yuktaṃ gogavayādivat || 1.63 ||
cetaḥśarīrayorevaṃ taddhetoḥ kāryajanmanaḥ |
sahakārātsahasthānamagnitāmradravatvavat || 1.64 ||
anāśrayātsadasatornāśrayaḥ sthitikāraṇam |
sataścedāśrayo nāsyāḥ sthāturavyatirekataḥ || 1.65 ||
vyatireke'pi taddhetustena bhāvasya kiṃ kṛtam |
abināśaprasaṅgaḥ sa nāśahetormato yadi || 1.66 ||
tulyaḥ prasaṅgastatrāpi kiṃ punaḥ sthitihetunā |
ā nāśakāgamātsthānaṃ tataścedvastudharmatā || 1.67 ||
nāśasya satyabādho'sāviti ki sthitihetunā |
yathā jalāderādhāra iti cettulyamatra ca || 1.68 ||
pratikṣaṇavināśe hi bhavānāṃ bhāvasantateḥ |
tathotpatteḥ sahetutvādāśrayo'yuktamanyathā || 1.69 ||
syādādhāro jalādīnāṃ gamanapratibandhataḥ |
agatīnāṃ kimādhārairguṇasāmānyakarmaṇām || 1.70 ||
etena samavāyaśca samavāyi ca kāraṇam |
vyavasthitatvaṃ jātyādernirastamanapāśrayāt || 1.71 ||
parato bhāvānāśaścettasya kiṃ sthitihetunā |
sa vinaśyedvināpyanyairaśaktāḥ sthitihetavaḥ || 1.72 ||
sthitimānnāśrayaḥ sarvaḥ sarvotpattai ca sāśrayaḥ |
tasmātsarvasya bhāvasya na vināśaḥ kadācana || 1.73 ||
svayaṃ vinaśvarātmā cettasya kaḥ sthāpakaḥ paraḥ |
svayaṃ na naśvarātmā cettasya kaḥ sthāpakaḥ paraḥ || 1.74 ||
buddhivyāpārabhedena nirhrāsātiśayāvapi |
prajñāderbhavato dehanirhrāsatiśayau vinā || 1.75 ||
idaṃ dīpaprabhādīnāmāśritānāṃ na vidyate |
syāttato'pi viśeṣo'sya na citte'nupakāriṇa || 1.76 ||
rāgādivṛddhiḥ puṣṭyādeḥ kadācitsukhaduḥkhajā |
tayośca dhātusāmyāderantararthasya sannidheḥ || 1.77 ||
etena sannipātādeḥ smṛtibhraṃśādayo gatāḥ |
vikārayati dhīreva hyantararthaviśeṣajā || 1.78 ||
śārdū laśoṇitādīnāṃ santānātiśaye kvacit |
mohādayaḥ sambhavanti śravaṇekṣaṇato yathā || 1.79 ||
tasmātsvasyaiva saṃskāraṃ niyamenānuvartate |
tannāntarīyakaṃ cittamataścittasamāśritam || 1.80 ||
yathā śrutādisaṃskāraḥ kṛtaścetasi cetasi |
kālena vyajyate'bhedātsyāddehe'pi tato guṇaḥ || 1.81 ||
ananyasattvaneyasya hīnasthānaparigrahaḥ |
ātmasnehavato duḥkhasukhatyāgāptivāñchayā || 1.82 ||
duḥkhe viparyāsamatiḥ tṛṣṇā cābandhakāraṇam ||
janmino yasya te na sto na sa janmādhigacchati || 1.83 ||
gatyāgatī na dṛṣṭe cedindriyāṇāmapāṭavāt |
adṛṣṭirmandanetrasya tanudhūmāgatiryathā || 1.84 ||
tanutvānmūrtamapi tu kiñcitkvacidaśaktimat |
jalavatsūtavaddhemni nādṛṣṭenāsadeva vā || 1.85 ||
pāṇyādikampe sarvasya kampaprāptervirodhinaḥ |
ekasmin karmaṇo'yogātsyātpṛthaksiddhiranyathā || 1.86 ||
ekasya cāvṛtau sarvasyāvṛtiḥ syādanāvṛtau |
dṛśyeta rakte caikasmin rāgo'raktasya vāgatiḥ || 1.87 ||
nāstyekasamudāyo'smādanekatve'pi pūrvavat |
aviśeṣādaṇutvācca na gatiścenna sidhyati || 1.88 ||
aviśeṣo viśiṣṭānāmaindriyatvamato'naṇuḥ |
etenāvaraṇādīnāmabhāvaśca nirākṛtaḥ || 1.89 ||
kathaṃ vā sūtahemādimiśraṃ taptopalādi vā |
dṛśyaṃ pṛthagaśaktānāmakṣādīnāṃ gatiḥ katham || 1.90 ||
saṃyogāccetsamāno'tra prasaṅgo hemasūtayoḥ |
dṛśyaḥ saṃyoga iti cetkuto'dṛśyāśraye gatiḥ || 1.91 ||
rasarūpādiyogaśca saṃyoga upacārataḥ |
iṣṭaścedbuddhibhedo'stu paṃktirdirgheti vā katham || 1.92 ||
saṃkhyāsaṃyogakarmāderapi tadvatsvarūpataḥ |
abhilāpācca bhedena rūpaṃ buddhau na bhāsate || 1.93 ||
śabdajñāne vikalpena vastubhedānusāriṇā |
guṇādiṣviva kalpyārthe naṣṭājāteṣu vā yathā || 1.94 ||
mato yadyupacāro'tra sa iṣṭo yannibandhanaḥ |
sa eva sarvabhāveṣu hetuḥ ki neṣyate tayoḥ || 1.95 ||
upacāro na sarvatra yadi bhinnaviśeṣaṇam |
mukhyamityeva ca kuto'bhinne bhinnārthateti cet || 1.96 ||
anarthāntarahetutve'pyaparyāyaḥ sitādiṣu |
saṃkhyādiyoginaḥ śabdāstatrāpyarthāntaraṃ yadi || 1.97 ||
guṇadravyāviśeṣaḥ syādbhinno vyāvṛttibhedataḥ |
syādanarthāntarārthatve'pyakarmādravyaśabdavat || 1.98 ||
vyatirekīva yaccāpi sūcyate bhāvavācibhiḥ |
saṃkhyāditadvataḥ śabdaistaddharmāntarabhedakam || 1.99 ||
śrutistanmātrajijñāsoranākṣiptākhilāparā |
bhinnaṃ dharmamivācaṣṭe yogo'ṅgulyā iti kvacit || 1.100 ||
yuktāṅgulīti sarveṣāmākṣepāddharmivācinī |
khyātaikārthābhidhāne'pi tathā bihitasaṃsthitiḥ || 1.101 ||
rūpādiśaktibhedānāmanākṣepeṇa vartate |
tatsamānaphalāhetuvyavacchede ghaṭaśrutiḥ || 1.102 ||
ato na rūpaṃ ghaṭa ityekādhikararaṇa śrutiḥ |
bhedo'yamīdṛśo jātisamudāyābhidhāyinoḥ || 1.103 ||
rūpādayo ghaṭasyeti tatsāmānyopasarjanāḥ |
tacchaktibhedāḥ khyāpyante vācyo'nyo'pi diśānayā || 1.104 ||
hetutve ca samastānāmekāṅgavikale'pi na |
pratyekamapi sāmarthye yugapadbahusambhavaḥ || 1.105 ||
nānekatvasya tulyatvātprāṇāpānau niyāmakau |
ekatve'pi bahuvyaktistaddhetornityasannidheḥ || 1.106 ||
nānekaheturiti cennāviśeṣātkramādapi |
naikaprāṇe'pyanekārthagrahaṇanniyamastataḥ || 1.107 ||
ekayānekavijñāne buddhyāstu sakṛdeva tat |
avirodhātkrameṇāpi mābhūttadaviśeṣataḥ || 1.108 ||
bahavaḥ kṣaṇikāḥ prāṇā asvajātīyakālikāḥ |
tādṛśāmeva cittānāṃ kalpyante yadi kāraṇam || 1.109 ||
kramavantaḥ kathaṃ te syuḥ kramavaddhetunā vinā |
pūrvasvajātihetutve na syādādyasya sambhavaḥ || 1.110 ||
taddhetustādṛśo nāsti sati vānekatā dhruvam |
prāṇānāṃ bhinnadeśatvātsakṛjjanma dhiyāmataḥ || 1.111 ||
yadyekakāliko'neko'pyekacaitanyakāraṇam |
ekasyāpi va vaikalye syānmandaśvasitādiṣu || 1.112 ||
atha heturyathābhāvaṃ jñāne'pi syādviśiṣṭatā |
na hi tattasya kārya yadyasya bhedānnna bhidyate || 1.113 ||
vijñānaṃ śaktiniyamādekamekasya kāraṇam |
anyārthāsaktiviguṇe jñāne cārthāntarāgrahāt || 1.114 ||
śarīrātsakṛdutpannā dhīḥ svajātyā niyamyate |
parataścetsamarthasya dehasya viratiḥ kutaḥ || 1.115 ||
anāśrayānnivṛtte syāccharīre cetasaḥ sthitiḥ |
kevalasyeti ceccittasantānasthitikāraṇam || 1.116 ||
taddhetuvṛttilābhāya nāṅgatāṃ yadi gacchati |
heturdehāntarotpattau pañcāyatanamaihikam || 1.117 ||
tadaṅgabhāvahetutvaniṣedhe'nupalambhanam |
aniścayakaraṃ proktamindriyādyapi śeṣavat || 1.118 ||
dṛṣṭā ca saktiḥ pūrveṣāmindriyāṇāṃ svajātiṣu |
vikāradarśanātsiddhamaparāparajanma ca || 1.119 ||
śarīrādyadi tajjanma prasaṅgaḥ pūrvavadbhavet |
cittāccettata evāstu janma dehāntarasya ca || 1.120 ||
tasmānna hetuvaikalyātsarveṣāmantyacetasām |
asandhirīdṛśaṃ tena śeṣavatsādhanaṃ matam || 1.121 ||
abhyāsena viśeṣe'pi laṅghanodakatāpavat |
svabhāvātikramo mā bhūditi cedāhitaḥ sa cet || 1.122 ||
punaryatnamapekṣeta yadi syāccāsthitāśrayaḥ |
viśeṣo naiva vardheta svabhāvaśca na tādṛśaḥ || 1.123 ||
tatropayuktaśaktīnāṃ viśeṣānuttarān prati |
sādhanānāmasāmārthyānnityaṃ cānāśrayasthiteḥ || 1.124 ||
viśeṣasyāsvabhāvatvādvṛddhāvapyāhito yadā |
nāpekṣeta punaryatnaṃ yatno'nyaḥ syādviśeṣakṛt || 1.125 ||
kāṣṭhapāradahemāderagnyāderiva cettasaḥ |
abhyāsajāḥ pravarttante svarasena kṛpādayaḥ || 1.126 ||
tasmātsa teṣāmutpannaḥ svabhāvo jāyate guṇaḥ |
taduttarottaro yatno viśeṣasya vidhāyakaḥ || 1.127 ||
yasmācca tulyajātīyapūrvabījapravṛddhayaḥ |
kṛpādibuddhayastāsāṃ satyabhyāse kutaḥ sthitiḥ || 1.128 ||
na caivaṃ laṅghanādeva laṅghanaṃ balayatnayoḥ |
