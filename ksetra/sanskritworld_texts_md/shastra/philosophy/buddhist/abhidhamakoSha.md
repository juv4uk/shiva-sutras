abhidharmakośabhāṣyam

prathamaṃ kośasthānam

oṃ namo buddhāya

yaḥ sarvathāsarvahatāndhakāraḥ saṃsārapaṅkājjagadujjahāra |
tasmai namaskṛtya yathārthaśāstre śāstraṃ pravakṣyāmyabhidharmakośam || 1.1 ||
prajñāmalā sānucarābhidharmaḥ tatprāptaye yāpi ca yacca śāstram |
tasyārthato'smin samanupraveśātsa cā śrayo'syetyabhidharmakośam || 1.2 ||
dharmāṇāṃ pravicayamantareṇa nāsti kleśānāṃ yata upaśāntaye'bhyupāyaḥ |
kleśaiśca bhramati bhavārṇave'tra lokastaddhetorata uditaḥ kilaiṣa śāstrā || 1.3 ||
sāsravānāsravā dharmāḥ saṃskṛtā mārgavarjitāḥ |
sāsravāḥ āsravāsteṣu yasmātsamanuśerate || 1.4 ||
anāsravā mārgasatyaṃ trividhaṃ cāpyasaṃskṛtam |
ākāśaṃ dvau nirodhau ca tatrākāśamanāvṛtiḥ || 1.5 ||
pratisaṃkhyānirodho yo visaṃyogaḥ pṛthakpṛthak |
utpādātyantavighno'nyo nirodho'pratisaṃkhyayā || 1.6 ||
te punaḥ saṃskṛtā dharmā rūpādiskandhapañcakam |
sa evādhvā kathāvastu saniḥsārāḥ savastukāḥ || 1.7 ||
ye sāsravā upādānaskandhāste saraṇā api |
duḥkhaṃ samudayo loko dṛṣṭisthānaṃ bhavaśca te || 1.8 ||
rūpaṃ pañcendriyāṇyarthāḥ pañcāvijñaptireva ca |
tadvijñānāśrayā rūpaprasādāścakṣurādayaḥ || 1.9 ||
rūpaṃ dvidhā viṃśatidhā śabdastvaṣṭavidhaḥ rasaḥ |
ṣoḍhā caturvidho gandhaḥ spṛśyamekādaśātmakam || 1.10 ||
vikṣiptācittakasyāpi yo'nubandhaḥ śubhāśubhaḥ |
mahābhūtānyupādāya sa hyavijñaptirucyate || 1.11 ||
bhūtāni pṛthividhāturaptejovāyudhātavaḥ |
dhṛtyādikarmasaṃsiddhā kharasnehoṣṇateraṇāḥ || 1.12 ||
pṛthivī varṇasaṃsthānamucyate lokasaṃjñayā |
āpastejaśca vāyustu dhātureva tathāpi ca || 1.13 ||
indriyārthāsta eveṣṭā daśāyatanadhātavaḥ |
vedanānubhavaḥ saṃjñā nimittodgrahaṇātmikā || 1.14 ||
caturbhyo'nye tu saṃskāraskandhaḥ ete punastrayaḥ |
dharmāyatanadhātvākhyāḥ sahāvijñaptyasaṃskṛtai || 1.15 ||
vijñānaṃ prativijñaptiḥ mana āyatanaṃ ca tat |
dhātavaḥ sapta ca matāḥ ṣaḍvijñānānyatho mahaḥ || 1.16 ||
ṣaṇṇāmanantarātītaṃ vijñānaṃ yaddhi tanmanaḥ |
ṣaṣṭhāśrayaprasiddhayarthaṃ dhatavo'ṣṭādaśa smṛtāḥ || 1.17 ||
sarvasaṃgraha ekena skandhenāyatanena ca |
dhātunā ca svabhāvena parabhāvaviyogataḥ || 1.18 ||
jātigocaravijñānasāmānyādekadhātutā |
dvitve'pi cakṣurādīnāṃ śobhārtha tu dvayodbhavaḥ || 1.19 ||
rāśyāyadvāragotrārthāḥ skandhāyatanadhātavaḥ |
mohendriyarūcitraidhāttistraḥ skandhādideśanāḥ || 1.20 ||
vivādamūlasaṃsārahetutvātkramakāraṇāt |
caittebhyo vedanāsaṃjñe pṛthakskandhau niveśitau || 1.21 ||
skandheṣva saṃskṛtaṃ noktamarthāyogātkramaḥ punaḥ |
yathaudārikasaṃklesabhājanādyarthadhātutaḥ || 1.22 ||
prākpañca vārttamānārthyātbhautikārthyāccatuṣṭayam |
dūrāśutaravṛttyānyatyathāsthānaṃ kramo'thavā || 1.23 ||
viśeṣaṇārthaṃ prādhānyabdahudharmāgrasaṃgrahāt |
ekamāyatanaṃ rūpamekaṃ dharmākhyamucyate || 1.24 ||
dharmaskandhasahasrāṇi yānyaśītiṃ jagau muniḥ |
tāni vāṅnāma vetyeṣāṃ rūpasaṃskārasaṃgrahaḥ || 1.25 ||
śāstrapramāṇā ityeke skandhādīnāṃ kathaikaśaḥ |
caritapratipakṣastu dharmaskandho'nuvarṇitaḥ || 1.26 ||
tathānye'pi yathāyogaṃ skandhāyatanadhātavaḥ |
pratipādyā yathokteṣu saṃpradhārya svalakṣaṇam || 1.27 ||
chidramākāśadhātvākhyamālokatamasī kila |
vijñānadhāturvijñānaṃ sāsravaṃ janmaniśrayāḥ || 1.28 ||
sanidarśana eko'tra rūpaṃ sapratighā daśa |
rūpiṇaḥ avyākṛtā aṣṭau ta evārūpaśabdakāḥ || 1.29 ||
tridhānye kāmadhātvāptāḥ sarve rūpe caturdaśa |
vinā gandharasaghrāṇajivhāvijñānadhātubhiḥ || 1.30 ||
ārūpyāptā manodharmamanovijñānadhātavaḥ |
sāsravānāsravā ete trayaḥ śeṣāstu sāsravāḥ || 1.31 ||
savitarkavicārā hi pañca vijñānadhātavaḥ |
antyāstrayastriprakārāḥ śeṣā ubhayavarjitāḥ || 1.32 ||
nirūpaṇānusmaraṇavikalpenāvikalpakāḥ |
tau prajñāmānasī vyagrā smṛtiḥ sarvaiva mānasī || 1.33 ||
sapta sālambanāścittadhātavaḥ ardhaṃ ca dharmataḥ |
navānupāttā te cāṣṭau śabdaśca anye nava dvidhā || 1.34 ||
spraṣṭavyaṃ dvividhaṃ śeṣā rūpiṇo nava bhautikāḥ |
dharmadhātvekadeśaśca saṃcitā daśa rūpiṇaḥ || 1.35 ||
chinatti chidyate caiva bāhyaṃ dhātu catuṣṭayam |
dahyate tulayatyevaṃ vivādo dagdhṛtulyayoḥ || 1.36 ||
vipākajaupacayikāḥ pañcādhyātmaṃ vipākajaḥ |
na śabdaḥ apratighā aṣṭau naiḥṣyandika vipākajāḥ || 1.37 ||
tridhānye dravyavānekaḥ kṣaṇikāḥ paścimāstrayaḥ |
cakṣurvijñānadhātvoḥ syātpṛthaklābhaḥ sahāpi ca || 1.38 ||
dvādaśādhyātmikāḥ hitvā rūpādīn dharmasaṃjñakaḥ |
sabhāgaḥ tatsabhāgāśca śeṣāḥ yo na svakarmakṛt || 1.39 ||
daśa bhāvanayā heyāḥ pañca ca antyāstrayastridhā |
na dṛṣṭiheyamakliṣṭaṃ na rūpaṃ nāpyaṣaṣṭhajam || 1.40 ||
cakṣuśca dharmadhātośca pradeśau dṛṣṭiḥ aṣṭadhā |
pañcavijñānasahajā dhīrna dṛṣṭiratīraṇāt || 1.41 ||
cakṣuḥ paśyati rūpāṇi sabhāgaṃ na tadāśritam |
vijñānaṃ dṛśyate rūpaṃ na kilāntaritaṃ yataḥ || 1.42 ||
ubhābhyāmapi cakṣurbhyāṃ paśyati vyaktadarśanāt |
cakṣuḥśrotramano'prāptaviṣayaṃ trayamanyathā || 1.43 ||
tribhirghrāṇādibhistulyaviṣayagrahaṇaṃ matam |
caramasyāśrayo'tītaḥ pañcānāṃ sahajaśca taiḥ || 1.44 ||
tadvikāravikāritvādāśrayāścakṣurādayaḥ |
ato'sādhāraṇatvāddhi vijñānaṃ tairnirucyate || 1.45 ||
na kāyasyādharaṃ cakṣuḥ ūrdhvaṃ rūpaṃ na cakṣuṣaḥ |
vijñānaṃ ca asya rūpaṃ tu kāyasyobhe ca sarvataḥ || 1.46 ||
tathā śrotraṃ trayāṇāṃ tu sarvameva svabhūmikam |
kāyavijñānamadharasvabhūmi aniyataṃ manaḥ || 1.47 ||
pañca bāhyā divijñeyāḥ nityā dharmā asaṃskṛtāḥ |
dharmārdhamindriyaṃ ye ca dvādaśādhyātmikāḥ smṛtāḥ || 1.48 ||

abhidharmakośabhāṣye dhātunirdeśo nāma prathamaṃ kośasthānaṃ samāptamiti |

ye dharmā hetuprabhavā hetusteṣāṃ tathāgato hyavadat |
teṣāṃ ca yo nirodha evaṃvādī mahāśramaṇaḥ ||

likhāpitamidaṃ śrīlāmāvākeneti |


dvitīyaṃ kośasthānam

caturṣvartheṣu pañcānāmādhipatyaṃ dvayoḥ kila |
caturṇṇāṃ pañcakāṣṭānāṃ saṃkleśavyavadānayoḥ || 2.1 ||
svārthopalabdhyādhipatyātsarvasya ca ṣaḍindriyam |
strītvapuṃstvādhipatyāttu kāyātstrīpuruṣendriye || 2.2 ||
nikāyasthitisaṃkleśavyavadānādhipatyataḥ |
jīvitaṃ vedanāḥ pañca śraddhādyāścendriyaṃ matāḥ || 2.3 ||
ājñāsyāmyākhyamājñākhyamājñātāvīndriyaṃ tathā |
uttarottarasaṃprāptinirvāṇādyādhipatyataḥ || 2.4 ||
cittāśrayastadvikalpaḥ sthitiḥ saṃkleśa eva ca |
saṃbhāro vyavadānaṃ ca yāvatā tāvadindriyam || 2.5 ||
pravṛtterāśrayotpattisthitipratyupabhogataḥ |
caturdaśa tathānyāni nivṛtterindriyāṇi vā || 2.6 ||
duḥkhendriyamaśātā yā kāyikī vedanā sukham |
śātā dhyāne tṛtīye tu caitasī sā sukhendriyam || 2.7 ||
anyatra sā saumanasyamaśātā caitasī punaḥ |
daurmanasyamupekṣā tu madhyā ubhayī avikalpanāt || 2.8 ||
dṛgbhāvanāśaikṣapathe nava trīṇi amalaṃ trayam |
rūpīṇi jīvitaṃ duḥkhe sāsravāṇi dvidhā nava || 2.9 ||
vipāko jīvitaṃ dvedhā dvādaśa antyāṣṭakādṛte |
daurmanasyācca tattvekaṃ savipākaṃ daśa dvidhā || 2.10 ||
mano'nyavittiśraddhādīni aṣṭakaṃ kuśalaṃ dvidhā |
daurmanasyaṃ mano'nyā ca vittistredhā anyadekadhā || 2.11 ||
kāmāptamamalaṃ hitvā rūpāptaṃ strīpumindriye |
duḥkhe ca hitvā ārūpyāptaṃ sukhe cāpohya rūpi ca || 2.12 ||
manovittitrayaṃ tredhā dviheyā durmanaskatā |
nava bhavanayā pañca tvaheyānyapi na trayam || 2.13 ||
kāmeṣvādau vipāko dve labhyate nopapādukaiḥ |
teḥ ṣaḍvā sapta vā aṣṭau vā ṣaḍrūpeṣu ekamuttare || 2.14 ||
nirodhayatyuparamānnārūpye jīvitaṃ manaḥ |
upekṣāṃ caiva rūpe'ṣṭau kāme daśa navāṣṭau vā || 2.15 ||
kramamṛtyau tu catvāri śubhe sarvatra pañca ca |
navāptirantyaphalayoḥ saptāṣṭanavabhirdvayoḥ || 2.16 ||
ekādaśabhirarhattvamuktaṃ tvekasya saṃbhavāt |
upekṣajīvitamanoyukto'vaśyaṃ trayānvitaḥ || 2.17 ||
caturbhiḥ sukhakāyābhyāṃ pañcabhiścakṣurādimān |
saumanasyī ca duḥkhī tu saptabhiḥ strīndriyādimān || 2.18 ||
aṣṭābhiḥ ekādaśabhistvājñājñāte ndriyānvitaḥ |
ājñāsyāmīndriyopetastrayodaśabhiranvitaḥ || 2.19 ||
sarvālpairniḥśubho'ṣṭābhirvinmanaḥkāyajīvitaiḥ |
yuktaḥ bālastathārūpye upekṣāyurmanaḥśubhaiḥ || 2.20 ||
bahubhiryukta ekānnaviṃśatyāmalavarjitaiḥ |
dviliṅgaḥ āryo rāgī ekaliṅgadvayamalavarjitaiḥ || 2.21 ||
kāme'ṣṭadravyako'śabdaḥ paramāṇuranindriyaḥ |
kāyendriyī navadravyaḥ daśadravyo'parendriyaḥ || 2.22 ||
cittaṃ caittāḥ sahāvaśyaṃ sarva saṃskṛtalakṣaṇaiḥ |
prāptyā vā pañcadhā caittā mahābhūmyādibhedataḥ || 2.23 ||
vedanā cetanā saṃjñā cchandaḥ sparśo matiḥ smṛtiḥ |
manaskāro'dhimokṣaśca samādhiḥ sarvacetasi || 2.24 ||
śraddhāpramādaḥ praśrabdhirupekṣā hrīrapatrapā |
mūladvayamahiṃsā ca vīryaṃ ca kuśale sadā || 2.25 ||
mohaḥ pramādaḥ kauśīdyamāśraddhayaṃ styānamuddhavaḥ |
kliṣṭe sadaiva akuśale tvāhrīkyamanapatrapā || 2.26 ||
krodhopanāhaśāṭhyerṣyāpradāsamrakṣamatsarāḥ |
māyāmadavihiṃsāśca parīttakleśabhūmikāḥ || 2.27 ||
savitarkavicāratvātkuśale kāmacetasi |
dvāṃviṃśatiścaitasikāḥ kaukṛtyamadhikaṃ kvacit || 2.28 ||
āveṇike tvakuśale dṛṣṭiyukte ca viṃśatiḥ |
kleśaiścaturbhiḥ krodhādyaiḥ kaukṛtyenaikaviṃśatiḥ || 2.29 ||
nivṛte'ṣṭādaśa anyatra dvādaśāvyākṛte matāḥ |
middhaṃ sarvāvirodhitvādyatra syādadhikaṃ hi tat || 2.30 ||
kaukṛtyamiddhākuśalānyādye dhyāne na santyataḥ |
dhyānāntare vitarkaśca vicāraścāpyataḥ param || 2.31 ||
ahrīragurutā avadye bhayādarśitva matrapā |
prema śraddhā gurutvaṃ hrīḥ te punaḥ kāmarūpayoḥ || 2.33 ||
vitarkacārā vaudāryasūkṣmate māna unnatiḥ |
madaḥ svadharme raktasya paryādānaṃ tu cetasaḥ || 2.33 ||
cittaṃ mano'tha vijñānamekārthaṃ cittacaitasāḥ |
sāśrayā lambanākārāḥ saṃprayuktāśca pañcadhā || 2.34 ||
viprayuktāstu saṃskārāḥ prāptyaprāptī sabhāgatā |
āsaṃjñikaṃ samāpattī jīvitaṃ lakṣaṇāni ca || 2.35 ||
nāmakāyādayaśceti prāptirlābhaḥ samanvayaḥ |
prāptyaprāptī svasaṃtāna patitānāṃ nirodhayoḥ || 2.36 ||
traiyadhvikānāṃ trividhā śubhādīnāṃ śubhādikā |
svadhātukā tadāptānāmanāptānāṃ caturvidhā || 2.37 ||
tridhā naśaikṣāśaikṣāṇāmaheyānāṃ dvidhā matā |
avyākṛtāptiḥ sahajā abhijñānairmāṇikādṛte || 2.38 ||
nivṛtasya ca rūpasya kāme rūpasya nāgrajā |
akliṣṭāvyākṛtāprāptiḥ sātītājātayostridhā || 2.39 ||
kāmādyāptāmalānāṃ ca mārgasyāprāptiriṣyate |
pṛthagjanatvaṃ tatprāptibhūsaṃcārādvihīyate || 2.40 ||
sabhāgatā sattvasāmyamāsaṃjñikamasaṃjñiṣu |
nirodhaścittacaittānāṃ vipākaḥ te bṛhatphalāḥ || 2.41 ||
tathāsaṃjñisamāpattiḥ dhyāne'ntye niḥsṛtīcchayā |
śubhā upapadyavedyaiva nāryasya ekādhvikāpyate || 2.42 ||
nirodhākhyā tathaiveyaṃ vihārārthaṃ bhavāgrajā |
śubhā dvivedyāniyatā ca āryasya āpyā prayogataḥ || 2.43 ||
bodhilabhyā muneḥ na prākcatustriṃśatkṣaṇāptitaḥ |
kāmarūpāśraye bhūte nirodhākhyādito nṛṣu || 2.44 ||
āyurjīvitamādhāra ūṣmavijñāyorhi yaḥ |
lakṣaṇāni punarjātirjarā sthitiranityatā || 2.45 ||
jātijātyādayasteṣāṃ te'ṣṭadharmaikavṛttayaḥ |
janyasya janikā jātirna hetupratyayairvinā || 2.46 ||
nāmakāyādayaḥ saṃjñāvākyākṣarasamuktayaḥ |
kāmarūpāptasattvākhyā niḥṣyandāvyākṛtāḥ tathā || 2.47 ||
sabhāgatā sā tu punarvipāko'pi āptayo dvidhā |
lakṣaṇāni ca niḥṣyandāḥ samāpattya samanvayāḥ || 2.48 ||
kāraṇaṃ sahabhūścaiva sabhāgaḥ saṃprayuktakaḥ |
sarvatrago vipākākhyaḥ ṣaḍvidho heturiṣyate || 2.49 ||
svato'nye kāraṇaṃ hetuḥ sahabhūrye mithaḥphalāḥ |
bhūtavaccittacittānuvartilakṣaṇalakṣyavat || 2.50 ||
caittā dvau saṃvarau teṣāṃ cetaso lakṣaṇāni ca |
cittānuvarttinaḥ kālaphalādiśubhatādibhiḥ || 2.51 ||
sabhāgahetuḥ sadṛśāḥ svanikāyabhuvaḥ agrajāḥ |
anyo'nyaṃ navabhūmistu mārgaḥ samaviśiṣṭayoḥ || 2.52 ||
prayogajāstayoreva śrutacintāmayādikāḥ |
saṃprayuktakahetustu cittacaittāḥ samāśrayāḥ || 2.53 ||
sarvatragākhyaḥ kliṣṭānāṃ svabhūmau pūrvasarvagāḥ |
vipākaheturaśubhāḥ kuśalāścaiva sāsravāḥ || 2.54 ||
sarvatragaḥ sabhāgaśca dvayadhvagau tryadhvagāstrayaḥ |
saṃskṛtaṃ savisaṃyoga phalaṃ nāsaṃskṛtasya te || 2.55 ||
vipākaphalamantyasya pūrvasyādhipataṃ phalam |
sabhāga sarvatragayorniṣyandaḥ pauruṣaṃ dvayoḥ || 2.56 ||
vipāko'vyākṛto dharmaḥ sattvākhyaḥ vyākṛtodbhavaḥ |
niḥṣyando hetusadṛśaḥ visaṃyogaḥ kṣayo dhiyā || 2.57 ||
yadvalājjāyate yattatphalaṃ puruṣakārajam |
apūrvaḥ saṃskṛtasyaiva saṃskṛto'dhipateḥ phalam || 2.58 ||
varttamānāḥ phalaṃ pañca gṛṇhanti dvau prayacchataḥ |
varttamānābhyatītau dvau eko'tītaḥ prayacchati || 2.59 ||
kliṣṭā vipākajāḥ śeṣāḥ prathamāryā yathākramam |
vipākaṃ sarvagaṃ hitvā tau sabhāgaṃ ca śeṣajāḥ || 2.60 ||
cittacaitāḥ tathānye'pi saṃprayuktakavarjitāḥ |
catvāraḥ pratyayā uktāḥ hetvākhyaḥ pañca hetavaḥ || 2.61 ||
cittacaittā acaramā utpannāḥ samanantaraḥ |
ālambanaṃ sarvadharmāḥ kāraṇākhyo'dhipaḥ smṛtaḥ || 2.62 ||
nirudhyamāne kāritraṃ dvau hetū kurutaḥ trayaḥ |
jāyamāne tato'nyau tu pratyayau tadviparyayāt || 2.63 ||
caturbhiścattacaittā hi samāpattidvayaṃ tribhiḥ |
dvābhyāmanye tu jāyante neśvarādeḥ kramādibhiḥ || 2.64 ||
dvidhā bhūtāni taddhetuḥ bhautikasya tu pañcadhā |
tridhā bhautikamanyonyaṃ bhūtānāmekadhaiva tat || 2.65 ||
kuśalākuśalaṃ kāme nivṛtānivṛtaṃ manaḥ |
rūpārūpyeṣvakuśalādanyatra anāsravaṃ dvidhā || 2.66 ||
kāme nava śubhāccittāccittāni aṣṭābhya eva tat |
daśabhyo'kuśalaṃ tasmāccatvāri nivṛtaṃ tathā || 2.67 ||
pañcabhyo'nivṛtaṃ tasmātsapta cittānyanantaram |
rūpe daśaikaṃ ca śubhātnavabhyastadanantaram || 2.68 ||
aṣṭābhyo nivṛtaṃ tasmātṣaṭtribhyo'nivṛtaṃ punaḥ |
tasmātṣaṭevāmārūpye tasya nītiḥ śubhātpunaḥ || 2.69 ||
nava cittāni tatṣaṇṇāṃ nivṛtātsapta tattathā |
caturbhyaḥ śaikṣamasmāttu pañca aśaikṣaṃ tu pañcakāt || 2.70 ||
tasmāccatvāri cittāni dvādaśaitāni viṃśatiḥ |

bhāvane darśane caiva sapta vargā yathākramam || 6.70 ||
anāsravāṇi bodhyaṅgamārgāṅgāni dvidhetare |
sakalāḥ prathame dhyāne anāgamye prītivarjitāḥ || 6.71 ||
dvitīye'nyatra saṃkalpātdvayostaddvayavarjitāḥ |
dhyānāntare ca śīlāṅgaistābhyāṃ ca triṣvarūpiṣu || 6.72 ||
kāmadhātau bhavāgre ca bodhimārgāṅgavarjitāḥ |
trisatyadarśane śīladharmāvetyaprasādayoḥ || 6.73 ||
lābho mārgābhisamaye buddhatatsaṃghayorapi |
dharmaḥ satyatrayaṃ bodhisattvapratyekabuddhayoḥ || 6.74 ||
mārgaśca dravyatastu dve śraddhā śīlaṃ ca nirmalāḥ |
noktā vimuktiḥ śaikṣāṅgaṃ baddhatvātsā punardvidhā || 6.75 ||
asaṃskṛtā kleśahānamadhimuktastu saṃskṛtā |
sāṅgaḥ saiva vimuktī dve jñānaṃ bodhiryathoditā || 6.76 ||
vimucyate jāyamānasaśaikṣaṃ cittamāvṛteḥ |
nirudhyamāno mārgastu prajahāti tadāvṛtim || 6.77 ||
asaṃskṛtaiva dhātvākhyā virāgo rāgasaṃkṣayaḥ |
prahāṇadhāturanyeṣāṃ nirodhākhyastu vastunaḥ || 6.78 ||
nirvidyate duḥkhahetukṣāntijñānaiḥ virajyate |
sarvairjahāti yaiḥ evaṃ catuṣkoṭikasaṃbhavaḥ || 6.79 ||

abhidharmakośe mārgapudgalanirdeśo nāma ṣaṣṭhaṃ kośasthānaṃ samāptamiti ||


saptamaṃ kośasthānam

namo buddhāya ||

nāmalā kṣāntayo jñānaṃ kṣayānutpādadhīrna dak |
tadanyobhayathāryā dhīḥ anyā jñānaṃ dṛśaśca ṣaṭ || 7.1 ||
sāsravānāsravaṃ jñānamādyaṃ saṃvṛtijñāpakam |
anāsravaṃ dvidhā dharmajñānamanvayameva ca || 7.2 ||
sāṃvṛtaṃ sarvaviṣayaṃ kāmaduḥkhādigocaram |
dharmākhyamanvayajñānaṃ tūrdhvaduḥkhādigocaram || 7.3 ||
te eva satyabhedena catvāri ete caturvidhe |
anutpādakṣayajñāne te punaḥ prathamodite || 7.4 ||
duḥkhahetvanvayajñāne caturbhyaḥ paracittavit |
bhūmyakṣapudgalotkrāntaṃ naṣṭājātaṃ na vetti tat || 7.5 ||
ta dharmānvayadhīpakṣyamanyo'nyaṃ darśanakṣaṇau |
śrāvako vetti khaṅgastrīn sarvānbuddho'prayogataḥ || 7.6 ||
kṣayajñānaṃ hi satyeṣu parijñātādiniścayaḥ |
na parijñeyamityādiranutpādamatirmatā || 7.7 ||
svabhāvapratipakṣābhyāmākārākāragocarāt |
prayogakṛtakṛtyatvahetūpacayato daśa || 7.8 ||
dharmajñānanirodhe yanmārge vā bhāvanāpathe |
tridhātupratipakṣastatkāmadhāto'stu nānvayam || 7.9 ||
dharmajñānānvayajñānaṃ ṣoḍaśākāramanyathā |
tathā ca sāṃvṛtaṃ svaiḥ svaiḥ satyākāraiścatuṣṭayam || 7.10 ||
tathā paramanojñānaṃ nirmalaṃ samalaṃ punaḥ |
jñeyasvalakṣaṇākāramekaikadravyagocaram || 7.11 ||
śeṣe caturdaśākāre śūnyānātmavivarjite |
nāmalaḥ ṣoḍaśabhyo'nya ākāraḥ anye'sti śāstrataḥ || 7.12 ||
dravyataḥ ṣoḍaśākārāḥ prajñākāraḥ tayā saha |
ākārayanti sālambāḥ sarvamākāryate tu sat || 7.13 ||
tridhādyaṃ kuśalānyanyāni ādyaṃ sarvāsu bhūmiṣu |
dharmākhyaṃ ṣaṭsu navasu tvanvayākhyaṃ tathaiva ṣaṭ || 7.14 ||
dhyāneṣvanyamanojñānaṃ kāmarūpāśrayaṃ ca tat |
kāmāśrayaṃ tu dharmākhyamanyattraidhātukāśrayam || 7.15 ||
smṛtyupasthānamekaṃ dhīrnirodhe paracittadhīḥ |
trīṇi catvāri śeṣāṇi dharmadhīgocaro nava || 7.16 ||
nava mārgānvayadhiyoḥ duḥkhahetudhiyordvayam |
caturṇāṃ daśa naikasya yojyā dharmāḥ punardaśa || 7.17 ||
traidhātukāmalā dharmā akṛtāśca dvidhā dvidhā |
sāṃvṛtaṃ svakalāpānyadekaṃ vidyādanātmataḥ || 7.18 ||
ekajñānānvito rāgī prathame'nāsravakṣaṇe |
dvitīye tribhiḥ ūrdhvastu caturṣvekaikavṛddhimān || 7.19 ||
yathotpannāni bhāvyante kṣāntijñānāni darśane |
anāgatāni tatraiva sāṃvṛtaṃ cānvayatraye || 7.20 ||
ato'bhisamayāntyākhyaṃ tadānutpattidharmakam |
svādhobhūmi nirodhe'ntyaṃ svasatyākāraṃ yātnikam || 7.21 ||
ṣoḍaśe ṣaṭsarāgasya vītarāgasya sapta tu |
sarāgabhāvanā mārge tadūrdhvaṃ saptabhāvanā || 7.22 ||
saptabhūmijayābhijñākopyāptyākīrṇabhāvite
ānantaryapatheṣūrdhvaṃ muktimārgāṣṭake'pi ca || 7.23 ||
śaikṣottāpanamuktau vā ṣaṭsaptajñānabhāvanā |
ānantaryapathe ṣaṇṇāṃ bhavāgravijaye tathā || 7.24 ||
navānāṃ tu kṣayajñāne akopyasya daśa bhāvanā |
tatsaṃcare'ntyamuktau ca proktaśeṣe'ṣṭabhāvanā || 7.25 ||
yadvairāgyāya yallābhastatra cādhaśca bhāvyate |
sāsravāśca kṣayajñāne labdhapūrvaṃ na bhāvyate || 7.26 ||
pratilambhaniṣevākhye śubhasaṃskṛtabhāvane |
pratipakṣavinirdhāvabhāvane sāsravasya tu || 7.27 ||
aṣṭādaśāveṇikāstu buddhadharmā balādayaḥ |
sthānāsthāne daśa jñānāni aṣṭau karmaphale nava || 7.28 ||
dhyānādyakṣādhimokṣeṣu dhātau ca pratipatsu tu |
daśa vā saṃvṛtijñānaṃ dvayoḥ ṣaṭdaśa vā kṣaye || 7.29 ||
prāṅinavisacyutotpādabaladhyāneṣu śeṣitam |
sarvabhūmiṣu kenāsya balamavyāhataṃ yataḥ || 7.30 ||
nārāyaṇabalaṃ kāye saṃdhiṣvanye daśādhikam |
hastyādisaptakabalaṃ spraṣṭavyāyatanaṃ ca tat || 7.31 ||
vaiśāradyaṃ caturdhā tu yathādyadaśame bale |
dvitīyasaptame caiva smṛtiprajñātmakaṃ trayam || 7.32 ||
mahākṛpā saṃvṛtidhīḥ saṃbhārākāragocaraiḥ |
samatvādādhimātryācca nānākaraṇamaṣṭadhā || 7.33 ||
saṃbhāradharmakāyābhyāṃ jagataścārthacaryayā |
samatā sarvabuddhānāṃ nāyurjātipramāṇataḥ || 7.34 ||
śiṣyasādhāraṇā anye dharmāḥ kecitpṛthagjanaiḥ |
araṇāpraṇidhijñānapratisaṃvidguṇādayaḥ || 7.35 ||
saṃvṛtijñānamaraṇā dhyāne'ntye akopyadharmaṇaḥ |
nṛjā anutpannakāmāptasavastukleśagocarāḥ || 7.36 ||
tathaiva praṇidhijñānaṃ sarvālambaṃ tu tattathā |
dharmārthayorniruktau ca pratibhāne ca saṃvidaḥ || 7.37 ||
tisro nāmāthavāgjñānamavivartyaṃ yathākramam |
caturthīyuktamuktābhilāpamārgavaśitvayoḥ || 7.38 ||
vāṅmārgālambanā cāsau nava jñānāni sarvabhūḥ |
daśa ṣaḍvārthasaṃvitsā sarvatra anye tu sāṃvṛtam || 7.39 ||
kāmadhyāneṣu dharme vitvāci prathamakāmayoḥ |
vikalābhirna tallābhī ṣaḍete prāntakoṭikāḥ || 7.40 ||
tatṣaḍvidhaṃ sarvabhūmyanulomitam |
vṛddhikāṣṭhāgataṃ tacca buddhānyasya prayogajāḥ || 7.41 ||
ṛddhiśrotramanaḥpūrvajanmacyutyudayakṣaye |
jñāta sākṣīkriyābhijñā ṣaḍvidhā muktimārgadhīḥ || 7.42 ||
catasraḥ saṃvṛtijñānaṃ cetasi jñānapañcakam |
kṣayābhijñā balaṃ yadvatpañca dhyānacatuṣṭaye || 7.43 ||
svādhobhūviṣayāḥ labhyā ucitāstu virāgataḥ |
tṛtīyā trīpyupasthānāni ādyaṃ śrotraddhircakṣuṣi || 7.44 ||
avyākṛte śrotracakṣurabhijñe itarāḥ śubhāḥ |
tisro vidyāḥ avidyāyāḥ pūrvāntādau nivarttanāt || 7.45 ||
aśaikṣyantyā tadākhye dve tatsaṃtānamudbhavāt |
iṣṭe śaikṣasya nokte tu vidye sāvidyasaṃtateḥ || 7.46 ||
ādyā tṛtīyā ṣaṣṭhī ca prātihāryāṇi śāsanam |
agyramavyabhicāritvāddhiteṣṭaphalayojanāt || 7.47 ||
ṛddhiḥ samādhiḥ gamanaṃ nirmāṇaṃ ca gatistridhā |
śāsturmanojavā anyeṣāṃ vāhinyapyādhimokṣikī || 7.48 ||
kāmāptaṃ nirmitaṃ bāhyaṃ caturāyatanaṃ dvidhā |
rūpāptaṃ dve tu nirmāṇacittaistāni caturdaśa || 7.49 ||
yathākramaṃ dhyānaphalaṃ dve yāvatpañca nordhvajam |
tallābho dhyānavatśuddhāttatsvataśca tato'pi te || 7.50 ||
svabhūmikena nirmāṇaṃ bhāṣaṇaṃ tvadhareṇa ca |
nirmātraiva sahāśāstuḥ adhiṣṭhāyānyavarttanāt || 7.51 ||
mṛtasyāpyastyadhiṣṭhānaṃ nāsthirasya apare tu na |
ādāvekamanekena jitāyāṃ tu viparyayāt || 7.52 ||
avyākṛtaṃ bhāvanājaṃ trividhaṃ tūpapattijam |
ṛddhirmantrauṣadhābhyāṃ ca karmajā ceti pañcadhā || 7.53 ||
divyaśrotrākṣiṇī rūpaprasādau dhyānabhūmikau |
sabhāgāvikale nityaṃ dūrasūkṣmādigocare || 7.54 ||
durasthamāvṛtaṃ sūkṣmaṃ sarvataśca na paśyati |
māṃsacakṣuryato rūpamato divyaṃ dṛgiṣyate ||
dvitrisāhasrakāsaṃkhyadṛśo'rhatkhaḍgadaiśikāḥ |
anyadapyupapattyāptaṃ taddṛśyo nāntarībhavaḥ || 7.55 ||
cetojñānaṃ tu tattredhā tarkavidyākṛtaṃ ca yat |
jānate nārakā ādau nṛṇāṃ notpattilabhikam || 7.56 ||

abhidharmakośe jñānanirdeśo nāma saptamaṃ kośasthānam ||


aṣṭamaṃ kośasthānam

oṃ namo buddhāya ||

dvidhā dhyānāni catvāri proktāstadupapattayaḥ |
samāpattiḥ śubhaikāgyraṃ pañcaskandhāstu sānugam || 8.1 ||
vicāraprītisukhavatpūrvapūrvāṅgavarjitam |
tathārūpyāḥ catuskandhāḥ adhobhūmivivekajāḥ || 8.2 ||
vibhūtarūpasaṃjñākhyāḥ saha sāmantakaistribhiḥ |
nārūpye rūpasadbhāvaḥ rūpotpattistu cittataḥ || 8.3 ||
ākāśānantyavijñānanatyākiṃcanyasaṃjñakāḥ |
tathāprayogātmāndyāttu nasaṃjñānāpyasaṃjñakaḥ || 8.4 ||
iti maulaṃ samāpattidravyamaṣṭavidhaṃ tridhā |
sapta āsvādanavacchuddhānāsravāṇi aṣṭamaṃ dvidhā || 8.5 ||
āsvādanāsaṃprayuktaṃ satṛṣṇaṃ laukikaṃ śubham |
śuddhakaṃ tattadāsvādyaṃ lokattaramanāsravam || 8.6 ||
pañcādye tarkacārau ca prītisaukhyasamādhayaḥ |
prītyādayaḥ prasādaśca dvitīye'ṅgacatuṣṭayam || 8.7 ||
tṛtīye pañca tūpekṣā smṛtiḥ prajñā sukhaṃ sthitiḥ |
catvāryante'sukhāduḥkhopekṣāsmṛtisamādhayaḥ || 8.8 ||
dravyato daśa caikaṃ ca prasrabdhi sukhamādyayoḥ |
śraddhā prasādaḥ prītistu saumanasyaṃ dvidhāgamāt || 8.9 ||
kliṣṭeṣva satprītisukhaṃ prasādaḥ saṃpradhīḥ smṛtiḥ |
upekṣāsmṛtiśuddhiśca kecitprasrabdhyupekṣaṇe || 8.10 ||
aṣṭāpakṣālamuktatvādāniñjaṃ tu caturthakam |
vitarkacārau śvāsau ca sukhādi ca catuṣṭayam || 8.11 ||
saumanasyasukhopekṣā upekṣāsumanaskate |
sukhopekṣe upekṣā pravido dhyānopapattiṣu || 8.12 ||
kāyākṣiśrotravijñānaṃ vijñaptyutthāpakaṃ ca yat |
dvitīyādau tadādyāptamakliṣṭāvyākṛtaṃ ca tat || 8.13 ||
atadvān labhate śuddhaṃ vairāgyeṇopapattitaḥ |
anāsravaṃ tu vairāgyātkliṣṭaṃ hānyupapattitaḥ || 8.14 ||
tṛtīyādyāvadūrdhvādho'nāsravānantaraṃ śubham |
utpadyate tathā śuddhātkliṭaṃ cāpi svabhūmikam || 8.15 ||
kliṣṭātsvaṃ śuddakaṃ kliṣṭamevaṃ cādharaśuddhakam |
cyutau tu śuddhakātkliṣṭaṃ sarvaṃ kliṣṭāttu nottaram || 8.16 ||
caturdhā śuddhakaṃ hānabhāgīyādi yathākramam |
kleśotpattisvabhūmyūrdhvānāsravānuguṇaṃ hi tat || 8.17 ||
dve trīṇi trīṇi caikaṃ ca hāna bhāgādyanantaram |
gatvāgamya dvidhā bhūmīraṣṭau śliṣṭai kalaṅghitāḥ || 8.18 ||
vyutkrāntakasamāpattirvisabhāgatṛtīyagā |
svādhobhūmyāśrayā eva dhyānārūpyāḥ vṛthādharam || 8.19 ||
āryākiṃcanyasāṃmukhyātbhavāgre tvāsravakṣayaḥ |
satṛṣṇāḥ svabhavālambāḥ dhyānaṃ sadviṣayaṃ śubham || 8.20 ||
na maulāḥ kuśalārūpyāḥ sāsravādharagocarāḥ |
anāsraveṇa hīyante kleśāḥ sāmantakena ca || 8.21 ||
aṣṭau sāmantakānyeṣāṃ śuddhāduḥkhāsukhāni hi |
ārya cādyaṃ tridhā kecitatarka dhyānamantaram || 8.22 ||
tridhā aduḥkhāsukhaṃtacca mahābrahmaphalaṃ ca tat |
savitarkavicāro'dhaḥsamādhiḥ parato'dvayaḥ || 8.23 ||
ānimittaḥ samākāraiḥ śūnyatānātmaśūnyataḥ |
pravartate apraṇihitaḥ satyākārairataḥ paraiḥ || 8.24 ||
śuddhāmalāḥ nirmalāstu te vimokṣamukhatrayam |
śūnyatāśunyatādyākhyāstrayo'parasamādhayaḥ || 8.25 ||
ālambete aśaikṣaṃ dvau śūnyataścāpyanityataḥ |
ānimittānimittastu śāntato'saṃkhyayā kṣayam || 8.26 ||
sāsravāḥ nṛṣu akopyasya saptasāmantavarjitāḥ |
samādhibhāvanā dhyānaṃ subhamādyaṃ sukhāya hi || 8.27 ||
darśanāyākṣyabhijñeṣṭā dhībhedāya prayogajāḥ |
vajropamo'ntye yo dhyāne sāsravakṣayabhāvanā || 8.28 ||
apramāṇāni catvāri vyāpādādivipakṣataḥ |
maitryadveṣaḥ api karuṇā muditā sumanaskatā || 8.29 ||
upekṣālobhaḥ ākāraḥ sukhitā duḥkhitā vata |
modantāmiti sattvācca kāmasattvāstu gocaraḥ || 8.30 ||
dhyānayormuditā anyāni ṣaṭsu kecittu pañcasu |
na taiḥ prahāṇaṃ nṛṣveva janyante tryanvito dhruvam || 8.31 ||
aṣṭau vimokṣāḥ prathamāvaśubhā dhyānayordvayoḥ |
tṛtīyo'ntye sa cālobhaḥ śubhārūpyāḥ samāhitāḥ || 8.32 ||
nirodhastu samāpattiḥ sūkṣmasūkṣmādanantaram |
svaśuddhakādharāryeṇa vyutthānaṃ cetasā tataḥ || 8.33 ||
kāmāptadṛśyaviṣayāḥ prathamāḥ ye tvarūpiṇaḥ |
te'nvayajñānapakṣordhvasvabhūduḥkhādigocarāḥ || 8.34 ||
abhibhvāyatanānyaṣṭau dvayamādyavimokṣavat |
dve dvitīyavatanyāni punaḥ śubhavimokṣavat || 8.35 ||
daśa kṛtsnāni alobhāṣṭau dhyāne'ntye gocaraḥ punaḥ |
kāmāḥ dve śuddhākārūpye svacatuḥskandhagocare || 8.36 ||
nirodha uktaḥ vairāgyaprayogāptaṃ tu śeṣitam |
tridhātvāśrayamārūpyasaṃjñaṃ śeṣaṃ manuṣyajam || 8.37 ||
hetukarmabālāddhātvorārupyotpādanaṃ dvayoḥ |
dhyānānāṃ rūpadhātau tu tābhyāṃ dharmatayāpi ca || 8.38 ||
saddharmo dvividhaḥ śāsturāgamādhigamātmakaḥ |
dhātārastasya vaktāraḥ pratipattāra eva ca || 8.39 ||
kāśmīravaibhāṣikanītisiddhaḥ prāyo mayāyaṃ kathito'bhidharmaḥ |
yaddurguhītaṃ tadihāsmadāgaḥ saddharmanītau munayaḥ pramāṇam || 8.40 ||
nimīlite śāstari lokacakṣuṣi kṣayaṃ gate sākṣijane ca bhūyasā |
adṛṣṭatattvairniravagrahaiḥ kṛtaṃ kutārkikaiḥ śāsanametadākulam || 8.41 ||
gate'tha śāntiṃ paramāṃ svayaṃbhuvi svayaṃbhuvaḥ śāsanadhūrdhareṣu ca |
jagatyanāthe gaṇaghātibhirmataiḥ niraṅkuśaiḥ svairamihādya caryate || 8.42 ||
iti kaṇṭhagataprāṇaṃ viditvā śāsanaṃ muneḥ |
balakālaṃ malānāṃ ca na pramādyaṃ mumukṣubhiḥ || 8.43 ||
