nāgārjuna kṛta madhyamakaśāstram | 


1 pratyayaparīkṣā nāma prathamaṃ prakaraṇam

anirodhamanutpādamanucchedamaśāśvatam |
anekārthamanānārthamanāgamamanirgamam || 1.1 ||
yaḥ pratītyasamutpādaṃ prapañcopaśamaṃ śivam |
deśayāmāsa saṃbuddhastaṃ vande vadatāṃ varam || 1.2 ||
na svato nāpi parato na dvābhyāṃ nāpyahetutaḥ |
utpannā jātu vidyante bhāvāḥ kkacana kecana || 1.3 ||
catvāraḥ pratyayā hetuścālambanamanantaram |
tathaivādhipateyaṃ ca pratyayo nāsti pañcamaḥ || 1.4 ||
na hi svabhāvo bhāvānāṃ pratyayādiṣu vidyate |
avidyamāne svabhāve parabhāvo na vidyate || 1.5 ||
kriyā na pratyayavatī nāpratyayavatī kriyā |
pratyayā nākriyāvantaḥ kriyāvantaśca santyuta || 1.6 ||
utpadyate pratītyemānitīme pratyayāḥ kila |
yāvannotpadyata ime tāvannāpratyayāḥ katham || 1.7 ||
naivāsato naiva sataḥ pratyayo'rthasya yujyate |
asataḥ pratyayaḥ kasya sataśca pratyayena kim || 1.8 ||
na sannāsanna sadasan dharmo nirvartate yadā |
kathaṃ nirvartako heturevaṃ sati hi yujyate || 1.9 ||
anālambana evāyaṃ san dharma upadiśyate |
athānālambane dharme kuta ālambanaṃ punaḥ || 1.10 ||
anutpanneṣu dharmeṣu nirodho nopapadyate |
nānantaramato yuktaṃ niruddhe pratyayaśca kaḥ || 1.11 ||
bhāvānāṃ niḥsvabhāvānāṃ na sattā vidyate yataḥ |
satīdamasmin bhavatītyetannaivopapadyate || 1.12 ||
na ca vyastasamasteṣu pratyayeṣvasti tatphalam |
pratyayebhyaḥ kathaṃ tacca bhavenna pratyayeṣu yat || 1.13 ||
athāsadapi tattebhyaḥ pratyayebhyaḥ pravartate |
apratyayebhyo'pi kasmātphalaṃ nābhipravartate || 1.14 ||
phalaṃ ca pratyayamayaṃ pratyayāścāsvayaṃmayāḥ |
phalamasvamayebhyo yattatpratyayamayaṃ katham || 1.15 ||
tasmānna pratyayamayaṃ nāpratyayamayaṃ phalam |
saṃvidyate phalābhāvātpratyayāpratyayāḥ kutaḥ || 1.16 ||

2 gatāgataparīkṣā dvitīyaṃ prakaraṇam |

gataṃ na gamyate tāvadagataṃ naiva gamyate |
gatāgatavinirmuktaṃ gamyamānaṃ na gamyate || 2.1 ||
ceṣṭā yatra gatistatra gamyamāne ca sā yataḥ |
na gate nāgate ceṣṭā gamyamāne gatistataḥ || 2.2 ||
gamyamānasya gamanaṃ kathaṃ nāmopapatsyate |
gamyamāne dvigamanaṃ yadā naivopapadyate || 2.3 ||
gamyamānasya gamanaṃ yasya tasya prasajyate |
ṛte gatergamyamānaṃ gamyamānaṃ hi gamyate || 2.4 ||
gamyamānasya gamane prasaktaṃ gamanadvayam |
yena tadgamyamānaṃ ca yaccātra gamanaṃ punaḥ || 2.5 ||
dvau gantārau prasajyete prasakte gamanadvaye |
gantāraṃ hi tiraskṛtya gamanaṃ nopapadyate || 2.6 ||
gantāraṃ cettiraskṛtya gamanaṃ nopapadyate |
gamane'sati gantātha kuta eva bhaviṣyati || 2.7 ||
gantā na gacchati tāvadagantā naiva gacchati |
anyo ganturagantuśca kastṛtīyo hi gacchati || 2.8 ||
gantā tāvadgacchatīti kathamevopapatsyate |
gamanena vinā gantā yadā naivopapadyate || 2.9 ||
pakṣo gantā gacchatīti yasya tasya prasajyate |
gamanena vinā gantā ganturgamanamicchataḥ || 2.10 ||
gamane dve prasajyete gantā yadyuta gacchati |
ganteti cocyate yena gantā san yacca gacchati || 2.11 ||
gate nārabhyate gantuṃ gataṃ nārabhyate'gate |
nārabhyate gamyamāne gantumārabhyate kuha || 2.12 ||
na pūrvaṃ gamanārambhādgamyamānaṃ na vā gatam |
yatrārabhyeta gamanamagate gamanaṃ kutaḥ || 2.13 ||
gataṃ kiṃ gamyamānaṃ kimagataṃ kiṃ vikalpyate |
adṛśyamāna ārambhe gamanasyaiva sarvathā || 2.14 ||
gantā na tiṣṭhati tāvadagantā naiva tiṣṭhati |
anyo ganturagantuśca kastṛtīyo'tha tiṣṭhati || 2.15 ||
gantā tāvattiṣṭhatīti kathamevopapatsyate |
gamanena vinā gantā yadā naivopapadyate || 2.16 ||
na tiṣṭhati gamyamānānna gatānnāgatādapi |
gamanaṃ saṃpravṛttiśca nivṛttiśca gateḥ samā || 2.17 ||
yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eveti na yujyate |
anya eva punargantā gateriti na yujyate || 2.18 ||
yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eva hi bhavedyadi |
ekībhāvaḥ prasajyeta kartuḥ karmaṇa eva ca || 2.19 ||
anya eva punargantā gateryadi vikalpyate |
gamanaṃ syādṛte ganturgantā syādgamanādṛte || 2.20 ||
ekībhāvena vā siddhirnānābhāvena vā yayoḥ |
na vidyate, tayoḥ siddhiḥ kathaṃ nu khalu vidyate || 2.21 ||
gatyā yayocyate gantā gatiṃ tāṃ sa na gacchati |
yasmānna gatipūrvo'sti kaścitkiṃciddhi gacchati || 2.22 ||
gatyā yayocyate gantā tato'nyāṃ sa na gacchati |
gatī dve nopapadyete yasmādeke pragacchati || 2.23 ||
sadbhūto gamanaṃ gantā triprakāraṃ na gacchati |
nāsadbhūto'pi gamanaṃ triprakāraṃ sa gacchati || 2.24 ||
gamanaṃ sadasadbhūtastriprakāraṃ na gacchati |
tasmādgatiśca gantā ca gantavyaṃ ca na vidyate || 2.25 ||

3 cakṣurādīndriyaparīkṣā tṛtīyaṃ prakaraṇam |

darśanaṃ śravaṇaṃ ghrāṇaṃ rasanaṃ sparśanaṃ manaḥ |
indriyāṇi ṣaḍeteṣāṃ draṣṭavyādīni gocaraḥ || 3.1 ||
svamātmānaṃ darśanaṃ hi tattameva na paśyati |
na paśyati yadātmānaṃ kathaṃ drakṣyati tatparān || 3.2 ||
na paryāpto'gnidṛṣṭānto darśanasya prasiddhaye |
sadarśanaḥ sa pratyukto gamyamānagatāgataiḥ || 3.3 ||
nāpaśyamānaṃ bhavati yadā kiṃcana darśanam |
darśanaṃ paśyatītyevaṃ kathametattu yujyate || 3.4 ||
paśyati darśanaṃ naiva naiva paśyatyadarśanam |
vyākhyāto darśanenaiva draṣṭā cāpyupagamyatām || 3.5 ||
tiraskṛtya draṣṭā nāstyatiraskṛtya ca darśanam |
draṣṭavyaṃ darśanaṃ caiva draṣṭaryasati te kutaḥ || 3.6 ||
pratītya mātāpitarau yathoktaḥ putrasaṃbhavaḥ |
cakṣūrūpe pratītyaivamukto vijñānasaṃbhavaḥ || 3.7 ||
draṣṭavyadarśanābhāvādvijñānādicatuṣṭayam |
nāstīti upādānādīni bhaviṣyanti punaḥ katham || 3.8 ||
vyākhyātaṃ śravaṇaṃ ghrāṇaṃ rasanaṃ sparśanaṃ manaḥ |
darśanenaiva jānīyācchrotṛśrotavyakādi ca || 3.9 ||

4 skandhaparīkṣā caturthaṃ prakaraṇam |

rūpakāraṇanirmuktaṃ na rūpamupalabhyate |
rūpeṇāpi na nirmuktaṃ dṛśyate rūpakāraṇam || 4.1 ||
rūpakāraṇanirmukte rūpe rūpaṃ prasajyate |
āhetukaṃ, na cāstyarthaḥ kaścidāhetukaḥ kvacit || 4.2 ||
rūpeṇa tu vinirmuktaṃ yadi syādrūpakāraṇam |
akāryakaṃ kāraṇaṃ syātnāstyakāryaṃ ca kāraṇam || 4.3 ||
rūpe satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate |
rūpe'satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate || 4.4 ||
niṣkāraṇaṃ punā rūpaṃ naiva naivopapadyate |
tasmātrūpagatān kāṃścinna vikalpān vikalpayet || 4.5 ||
na kāraṇasya sadṛśaṃ kāryamityupapadyate |
na kāraṇasyāsadṛśaṃ kāryamityupapadyate || 4.6 ||
vedanācittasaṃjñānāṃ saṃskārāṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ |
sarveṣāmeva bhāvānāṃ rūpeṇaiva samaḥ kramaḥ || 4.7 ||
vigrahe yaḥ parīhāraṃ kṛte śūnyatayā vadet |
sarvaṃ tasyāparihṛtaṃ samaṃ sādhyena jāyate || 4.8 ||
vyākhyāne ya upālambhaṃ kṛte śūnyatayā vadet |
sarvaṃ tasyānupālabdhaṃ samaṃ sādhyena jāyate || 4.9 ||

5 dhātuparīkṣā pañcamaṃ prakaraṇam |

nākāśaṃ vidyate kiṃcitpūrvamākāśalakṣaṇāt |
alakṣaṇaṃ prasajyeta syātpūrvaṃ yadi lakṣaṇāt || 5.1 ||
alakṣaṇo na kaścicca bhāvaḥ saṃvidyate kkacit |
asatyalakṣaṇe bhāve kramatāṃ kuha lakṣaṇam || 5.2 ||
nālakṣaṇe lakṣaṇasya pravṛttirna salakṣaṇe |
salakṣaṇālakṣaṇābhyāṃ nāpyanyatra pravartate || 5.3 ||
lakṣaṇāsaṃpravṛttau ca na lakṣyamupapadyate |
lakṣyasyānupapattau ca lakṣaṇasyāpyasaṃbhavaḥ || 5.4 ||
tasmānna vidyate lakṣyaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ naiva vidyate |
lakṣyalakṣaṇanirmukto naiva bhāvo'pi vidyate || 5.5 ||
avidyamāne bhāve ca kasyābhāvo bhaviṣyati |
bhāvābhāvavidharmā ca bhāvābhāvamavaiti kaḥ || 5.6 ||
tasmānna bhāvo nābhāvo na lakṣyaṃ nāpi lakṣaṇam |
ākāśamākāśasamā dhātavaḥ pañca ye pare || 5.7 ||
astitvaṃ ye tu paśyanti nāstitvaṃ cālpabuddhayaḥ |
bhāvānāṃ te na paśyanti draṣṭavyopaśamaṃ śivam || 5.8 ||

6 rāgaraktaparīkṣā ṣaṣṭhaṃ prakaraṇam |

rāgādyadi bhavetpūrvaṃ rakto rāgatiraskṛtaḥ |
taṃ pratītya bhavedrāgo rakte rāgo bhavetsati || 6.1 ||
rakte'sati punā rāgaḥ kuta eva bhaviṣyati |
sati vāsati vā rāge rakte'pyeṣa samaḥ kramaḥ || 6.2 ||
sahaiva punarudbhūtirna yuktā rāgaraktayoḥ |
bhavetāṃ rāgaraktau hi nirapekṣau parasparam || 6.3 ||
naikatve sahabhāvo'sti na tenaiva hi tatsaha |
pṛthaktve sahabhāvo'tha kuta eva bhaviṣyati || 6.4 ||
ekatve sahabhāvaścetsyātsahāyaṃ vināpi saḥ |
pṛthaktve sahabhāvaścetsyātsahāyaṃ vināpi saḥ || 6.5 ||
pṛthaktve sahabhāvaśca yadi kiṃ rāgaraktayoḥ |
siddhaḥ pṛthakpṛthagbhāvaḥ sahabhāvo yatastayoḥ || 6.6 ||
siddhaḥ pṛthakpṛthagbhāvo yadi vā rāgaraktayoḥ |
sahabhāvaṃ kimarthaṃ tu parikalpayase tayoḥ || 6.7 ||
pṛthaṅna sidhyatītyevaṃ sahabhāvaṃ vikāṅkṣasi |
sahabhāvaprasiddhyarthaṃ pṛthaktvaṃ bhūya icchasi || 6.8 ||
pṛthagbhāvāprasiddheśca sahabhāvo na sidhyati |
katamasmin pṛthagbhāve sahabhāvaṃ satīcchasi || 6.9 ||
evaṃ raktena rāgasya siddhirna saha nāsaha |
rāgavatsarvadharmāṇāṃ siddhirna saha nāsaha || 6.10 ||

7 saṃskṛtaparīkṣā saptamaṃ prakaraṇam |

yadi saṃskṛta utpādastatra yuktā trilakṣaṇī |
athāsaṃskṛta utpādaḥ kathaṃ saṃskṛtalakṣaṇam || 7.1 ||
utpādādyāstrayo vyastā nālaṃ lakṣaṇakarmaṇi |
saṃskṛtasya samastāḥ syurekatra kathamekadā || 7.2 ||
utpādasthitibhaṅgānāmanyatsaṃskṛtalakṣaṇam |
asti cedanavasthaivaṃ nāsti cette na saṃskṛtāḥ || 7.3 ||
utpādotpāda utpādo mūlotpādasya kevalam |
utpādotpādamutpādo maulo janayate punaḥ || 7.4 ||
utpādotpāda utpādo mūlotpādasya te yadi |
maulenājanitastaṃ te sa kathaṃ janayiṣyati || 7.5 ||
sa te maulena janito maulaṃ janayate yadi |
maulaḥ sa tenājanitastamutpādayate katham || 7.6 ||
ayamutpadyamānaste kāmamutpādayedimam |
yadīmamutpādayitumajātaḥ śaknuyādayam || 7.7 ||
pradīpaḥ svaparātmānau saṃprakāśayitā yathā |
utpādaḥ svaparātmānāvubhāvutpādayettathā || 7.8 ||
pradīpe nāndhakāro'sti yatra cāsau pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
kiṃ prakāśayati dīpaḥ prakāśo hi tamovadhaḥ || 7.9 ||
kathamutpadyamānena pradīpena tamo hatam |
notpadyamāno hi tamaḥ pradīpaḥ prāpnute yadā || 7.10 ||
aprāpyaiva pradīpena yadi vā nihataṃ tamaḥ |
ihasthaḥ sarvalokasthaṃ sa tamo nihaniṣyati || 7.11 ||
pradīpaḥ svaparātmānau saṃprakāśayate yadi |
tamo'pi svaparātmānau chādayiṣyatyasaṃśayam || 7.12 ||
anutpanno'yamutpādaḥ svātmānaṃ janayetkatham |
athotpanno janayate jāte kiṃ janyate punaḥ || 7.13 ||
notpadyamānaṃ notpannaṃ nānutpannaṃ kathaṃcana |
utpadyate tathākhyātaṃ gamyamānagatāgataiḥ || 7.14 ||
utpadyamānamutpattāvidaṃ na kramate yadā |
kathamutpadyamānaṃ tu pratītyotpattimucyate || 7.15 ||
pratītya yadyadbhavati tattacchāntaṃ svabhāvataḥ |
tasmādutpadyamānaṃ ca śāntamutpattireva ca || 7.16 ||
yadi kaścidanutpanno bhāvaḥ saṃvidyate kvacit |
utpadyeta sa kiṃ tasmin bhāva utpadyate'sati || 7.17 ||
utpadyamānamutpādo yadi cotpādayatyayam |
utpādayettamutpādamutpādaḥ katamaḥ punaḥ || 7.18 ||
anya utpādatyenaṃ yadyutpādo'navasthitiḥ |
athānutpāda utpannaḥ sarvamutpadyate tathā || 7.19 ||
sataśca tāvadutpattirasataśca na yujyate |
na sataścāsataśceti pūrvamevopapāditam || 7.20 ||
nirudhyamānasyotpattirna bhāvasyopapadyate |
yaścānirudhyamānastu sa bhāvo nopapadyate || 7.21 ||
na sthitabhāvastiṣṭhatyasthitabhāvo na tiṣṭhati |
na tiṣṭhati tiṣṭhamānaḥ ko'nutpannaśca tiṣṭhati || 7.22 ||
sthitirnirudhyamānasya na bhāvasyopapadyate |
yaścānirudhyamānastu sa bhāvo nopapadyate || 7.23 ||
jarāmaraṇadharmeṣu sarvabhāveṣu sarvadā |
tiṣṭhanti katame bhāvā ye jarāmaraṇaṃ vinā || 7.24 ||
sthityānyayā sthiteḥ sthānaṃ tayaiva ca na yujyate |
utpādasya yathotpādo nātmanā na parātmanā || 7.25 ||
nirudhyate nāniruddhaṃ na niruddhaṃ nirudhyate |
tathāpi nirudhyamānaṃ kimajātaṃ nirudhyate || 7.26 ||
sthitasya tāvadbhāvasya nirodho nopapadyate |
nāsthitasyāpi bhāvasya nirodha upapadyate || 7.27 ||
tayaivāvasthayāvasthā na hi saiva nirudhyate |
anyayāvasthayāvasthā na cānyaiva nirudhyate || 7.28 ||
yadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāmutpādo nopapadyate |
tadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nirodho nopapadyate || 7.29 ||
sataśca tāvadbhāvasya nirodho nopapadyate |
ekatve na hi bhāvaśca nābhāvaścopapadyate || 7.30 ||
asato'pi na bhāvasya nirodha upapadyate |
na dvitīyasya śirasacchedanaṃ vidyate yathā || 7.31 ||
na svātmanā nirodho'sti nirodho na parātmanā |
utpādasya yathotpādo nātmanā na parātmanā || 7.32 ||
utpādasthitibhaṅgānāmasiddhernāsti saṃskṛtam |
saṃskṛtasyāprasiddhau ca kathaṃ setsyatyasaṃskṛtam || 7.33 ||
yathā māyā yathā svapno gandharvanagaraṃ yathā |
tathotpādastathā sthānaṃ tathā bhaṅga udāhṛtam || 7.34 ||

8 karmakārakaparīkṣā aṣṭamaṃ prakaraṇam |

sadbhūtaḥ kārakaḥ karma sadbhūtaṃ na karotyayam |
kārako nāpyasadbhūtaḥ karmāsadbhūtamīhate || 8.1 ||
sadbhūtasya kriyā nāsti karma ca syādakartṛkam |
sadbhūtasya kriyā nāsti kartā ca syādakarmakaḥ || 8.2 ||
karoti yadyasadbhūto'sadbhūtaṃ karma kārakaḥ |
ahetukaṃ bhavetkarma kartā cāhetuko bhavet || 8.3 ||
hetāvasati kāryaṃ ca kāraṇaṃ ca na vidyate |
tadabhāve kriyā kartā karaṇaṃ ca na vidyate || 8.4 ||
dharmādharmau na vidyete kriyādīnāmasaṃbhave |
dharme cāsatyadharme ca phalaṃ tajjaṃ na vidyate || 8.5 ||
phale'sati na mokṣāya na svargāyopapadyate |
mārgaḥ sarvakriyāṇāṃ ca nairarthakyaṃ prasajyate || 8.6 ||
kārakaḥ sadasadbhūtaḥ sadasatkurute na tat |
parasparaviruddhaṃ hi saccāsaccaikataḥ kutaḥ || 8.7 ||
satā ca kriyate nāsannāsatā kriyate ca sat |
kartrā sarve prasajyante doṣāstatra ta eva hi || 8.8 ||
nāsadbhūtaṃ na sadbhūtaḥ sadasadbhūtameva vā |
karoti kārakaḥ karma pūrvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 8.9 ||
nāsadbhūto'pi sadbhūtaṃ sadasadbhūtameva vā |
karoti kārakaḥ karma purvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 8.10 ||
karoti sadasadbhūto na sannāsacca kārakaḥ |
karma tattu vijānīyātpūrvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 8.11 ||
pratītya kārakaḥ karma taṃ pratītya ca kārakam |
karma pravartate, nānyatpaśyāmaḥ siddhikāraṇam || 8.12 ||
evaṃ vidyādupādānaṃ vyutsargāditi karmaṇaḥ |
kartuśca karmakartṛbhyāṃ śeṣān bhāvān vibhāvayet || 8.13 ||

9 pūrvaparīkṣā navamaṃ prakaraṇam |

darśanaśravaṇādīni vedanādīni cāpyatha |
bhavanti yasya prāgebhyaḥ so'stītyeke vadantyuta || 9.1 ||
kathaṃ hyavidyamānasya darśanādi bhaviṣyati |
bhāvasya tasmātprāgebhyaḥ so'sti bhāvo vyavasthitaḥ || 9.2 ||
darśanaśravaṇādibhyo vedanādibhya eva ca |
yaḥ prāgvyavasthito bhāvaḥ kena prajñapyate'tha saḥ || 9.3 ||
vināpi darśanādīni yadi cāsau vyavasthitaḥ |
amūnyapi bhaviṣyanti vinā tena na saṃśayaḥ || 9.4 ||
ajyate kenacitkaścitkiṃcitkenacidajyate |
kutaḥ kiṃcidvinā kaścitkiṃcitkaṃcidvinā kutaḥ || 9.5 ||
sarvebhyo darśanādibhyaḥ kaścitpūrvo na vidyate |
ajyate darśanādīnāmanyena punaranyadā || 9.6 ||
sarvebhyo darśanādibhyo yadi pūrvo na vidyate |
ekaikasmātkathaṃ pūrvo darśanādeḥ sa vidyate || 9.7 ||
draṣṭā sa eva sa śrotā sa eva yadi vedakaḥ |
ekaikasmādbhavetpūrvamevaṃ caitanna yujyate || 9.8 ||
draṣṭānya eva śrotānyo vedako'nyaḥ punaryadi |

27 dṛṣṭiparīkṣā saptaviṃśatitamaṃ prakaraṇam |

abhūmatītamadhvānaṃ nābhūmiti ca dṛṣṭayaḥ |
yāstāḥ śāśvatalokādyāḥ pūrvāntaṃ samupāśritāḥ || 27.1 ||
dṛṣṭayo na bhaviṣyāmi kimanyo'nāgate'dhvani |
bhaviṣyāmīti cāntādyā aparāntaṃ samāśritāḥ || 27.2 ||
abhūmatītamadhvānamityetannopapadyate |
yo hi janmasu pūrveṣu sa eva na bhavatyayam || 27.3 ||
sa evātmeti tu bhavedupādānaṃ viśiṣyate |
upādānavinirmukta ātmā te katamaḥ punaḥ || 27.4 ||
upādānavinirmukto nāstyātmeti kṛte sati |
syādupādānamevātmā nāsti cātmeti vaḥ punaḥ || 27.5 ||
na copādānamevātmā vyeti tatsamudeti ca |
kathaṃ hi nāmopādānamupādātā bhaviṣyati || 27.6 ||
anyaḥ punarupādānādātmā naivopapadyate |
gṛhyate hyanupādāno yadyanyo na ca gṛhyate || 27.7 ||

ātmā nāstyanupādānaḥ nāpi nāstyeṣa niścayaḥ || 27.8 ||
nābhūmatītamadhvānamityetannopapadyate |
yo hi janmasu pūrveṣu tato'nyo na bhavatyayam || 27.9 ||
yadi hyayaṃ bhavedanyaḥ pratyākhyāyāpi taṃ bhavet |
tathaiva ca sa saṃtiṣṭhettatra jāyeta vāmṛtaḥ || 27.10 ||
ucchedaḥ karmaṇāṃ nāśastathānyakṛtakarmaṇām |
anyena paribhogaḥ syādevamādi prasajyate || 27.11 ||
nāpyabhūtvā samudbhūto doṣo hyatra prasajyate |
kṛtako vā bhavedātmā saṃbhūto vāpyahetukaḥ || 27.12 ||
evaṃ dṛṣṭiratīte yā nābhūmahamabhūmaham |
ubhayaṃ nobhayaṃ ceti naiṣā samupapadyate || 27.13 ||
adhvanyanāgate kiṃ nu bhaviṣyāmīti darśanam |
na bhaviṣyāmi cetyetadatītenādhvanā samam || 27.14 ||
sa devaḥ sa manuṣyaścedevaṃ bhavati śāśvatam |
anutpannaśca devaḥ syājjāyate na hi śāśvatam || 27.15 ||
devādanyo manuṣyaścedaśāśvatamato bhavet |
devādanyo manuṣyaścetsaṃtatirnopapadyate || 27.16 ||
divyo yadyekadeśaḥ syādekadeśaśca mānuṣaḥ |
aśāśvataṃ śāśvataṃ ca bhavetacca na yujyate || 27.17 ||
aśāśvataṃ śāśvataṃ ca prasiddhamubhayaṃ yadi |
siddhe na śāśvataṃ kāmaṃ naivāśāśvatamityapi || 27.18 ||
kutaścidāgataḥ kaścitkiṃcidnacchetpunaḥ kvacit |
yadi tasmādanādistu saṃsāraḥ syānna cāsti saḥ || 27.19 ||
nāsti cecchāśvataḥ kaścitko bhaviṣyatyaśāśvataḥ |
śāśvato'śāśvataścāpi dvābhyāmābhyāṃ tiraskṛtaḥ || 27.20 ||
