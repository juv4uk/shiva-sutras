śrīḥ

oṃ tatsadbrahmaṇe namaḥ |

atha rasaratnasamuccayaḥ |


rasotpattirnāma prathamo'dhyāyaḥ |

yasyānandabhavena maṅgalakalāsaṃbhāvitena sphura- dhāmnā siddharasāmṛtena karuṇāvīkṣāsudhāsindhunā  |
bhaktānāṃ prabhavaprasaṃhṛtijarārāgādirogāḥ kṣaṇā- cchāntiṃ yānti jagatpradhānabhiṣaje tasmai parasmai namaḥ  || 1.1 ||

ādimaścandrasenaśca laṅkeśaścaviśāradaḥ  |
kapālī mattamāṇḍavyau bhāskaraḥ śūrasenakaḥ  || 1.2 ||

ratnakośaśca śaṃbhuśca sāttviko naravāhanaḥ  |
indrado gomukhaścaiva kambalirvyāḍireva ca  || 1.3 ||

nāgārjunaḥ surānando nāgabodhiryaśodhanaḥ  |
khaṇḍaḥ kāpāliko  brahmā govindo lampako hariḥ  || 1.4 ||

saptaviṃśatisaṃkhyākā rasasiddhipradāyakāḥ  |
rasāṅkuśo bhairavaśca nandī svacchandabhairavaḥ  || 1.5 ||

manthānabhairavaścaiva kākacaṇḍīśvarastathā  |
vāsudeva ṛṣiḥ śṛṅgaḥ kriyātantrasamuccayī  || 1.6 ||

rasendratilako yogī bhālukī maithilāhvayaḥ  |
mahādevo narendraśca vāsudevo harīśvaraḥ  || 1.7 ||

eteṣāṃ kriyate'nyeṣāṃ tantrāṇyālokya saṃgrahaḥ  |
rasānāmatha siddhānāṃ cikitsārthopayoginām  || 1.8 ||

sūnunā siṃhaguptasya rasaratnasamuccayaḥ  |
rasoparasalohāni yantrādikaraṇāni ca  || 1.9 ||

śuddhyarthamapi lohānāṃ tantrādikaraṇāni ca  |
śuddhiḥ sattvaṃ drutirbhasma- karaṇaṃ ca pravakṣyate  || 1.10 ||

asti nīhāranilayo mahānuttaradiṅmukhe  |
uttuṅgaśṛṅgasaṃghāta- laṅghitābhro mahīdharaḥ  || 1.11 ||

viśrāmāya viyanmārga- vilaṅghanaghanaśramaḥ  |
avatīrṇa iva kṣoṇīṃ śaradambumucāṃ gaṇaḥ  || 1.12 ||

rāśirāśīviṣādhīśa- phaṇāphalakarociṣām  |
bhittvā bhuvamivottīrṇo yo vibhāti bhṛśonnataḥ  || 1.13 ||

jvaladauṣadhayo yasya nitambamaṇibhūmayaḥ  |
naktamuddāmataḍitāmanukurvanti vārmucām  || 1.14 ||

kaṭake saṃcarantīnāṃ yasya kinnarayoṣitām  |
pādeṣu dhāturāgeṇa lākṣākṛtyamanuṣṭhitam  || 1.15 ||

avataṃsitaśītāṃśurācchāditadigambaraḥ  |
yo guhādhigato lokairgirīśa iti gīyate  || 1.16 ||

nimīlitadṛśo nityaṃ munayo yasya sānuṣu  |
pratyakṣayanti giriśamavāṅmanasagocaram  || 1.17 ||

śilātalapratihatairyasya nirjharaśīkaraiḥ  |
ahanyapi nirīkṣante yakṣāstārāṅkitaṃ nabhaḥ  || 1.18 ||

nīhārapavanodreka- niḥsahā yatra puruṣāḥ  |
nijastrīṇāṃ niṣevante kucoṣmāṇaṃ nirantaram  || 1.19 ||

saṃcaran kaṭake yasya nidāghe'pi divākaraḥ  |
uddāmahimaruddhoṣmā na śītāṃśorvibhidyate  || 1.20 ||

guhāgṛheṣu kastūrī- mṛganābhisugandhiṣu  |
gāyanti yatra kinnaryo gaurīpariṇayotsavam  || 1.21 ||

cakāsti tatra jagatāmādidevo maheśvaraḥ  |
rasātmanā jagattrātuṃ jāto yasmānmahārasaḥ  || 1.22 ||

śatāśvamedhena kṛtena puṇyaṃ gokoṭibhiḥ svarṇasahasradānāt |
nṛṇāṃ bhavetsūtakadarśanena yatsarvatīrtheṣu kṛtābhiṣekāt || 1.23 ||

vidhāya rasaliṅgaṃ yo bhaktiyuktaḥ samarcayet |
jagattritayaliṅgānāṃ pūjāphalamavāpnuyāt || 1.24 ||

bhakṣaṇaṃ sparśanaṃ dānaṃ dhyānaṃ ca paripūjanam  |
pañcadhā rasapūjoktā mahāpātakanāśinī  || 1.25 ||

hanti bhakṣaṇamātreṇa pūrvajanmāghasaṃbhavam  |
rogasaṃghamaśeṣāṇāṃ narāṇāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 1.26 ||

pūrvajanmakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ sadyo naśyati dehinām  |
sugandhapiṣṭasūtena yadi śaṃbhurvilepitaḥ  || 1.27 ||

abhrakaṃ truṭimātraṃ yo rasasya parijārayet |
śatakratuphalaṃ tasya bhavedityabravīcchivaḥ  || 1.28 ||

yaśca nindati sūtendraṃ śaṃbhostejaḥ parātparam  |
sa patennarake ghore yāvatkalpavikalpanā  || 1.29 ||

rogibhyo yo rasaṃ datte śuddhipākasamanvitam  |
tulādānāśvamedhānāṃ phalaṃ prāpnoti śāśvatam  || 1.30 ||

siddhe rase kariṣyāmi nirdāridryagadaṃ jagat |
rasadhyānamidaṃ proktaṃ brahmahatyādipāpanut || 1.31 ||

abhragrāso hi sūtasya naivedyaṃ parikīrtitam  |
rasasyetyarcanaṃ kṛtvā prāpnuyātkratujaṃ phalam  || 1.32 ||

udare saṃsthite sūte yasyotkrāmati jīvitam  |
sa mukto duṣkṛtādghorātprayāti paramaṃ padam  || 1.33 ||

mūrchitvā harati rujaṃ bandhanamanubhūya muktido bhavati  |
amarīkaroti hi mṛtaḥ ko'nyaḥ karuṇākaraḥ sūtāt || 1.34 ||

suragurugodvijahiṃsāpāpakalāpodbhavaṃ kilāsādhyam  |
śvitraṃ tadapi ca śamayati yastasmātkaḥ pavitrataraḥ sūtāt || 1.35 ||

rasabandha eva dhanyaḥ prārambhe yasya satatamitikaraṇā  |
setsyati rase kariṣye mahīmahaṃ nirjarāmaraṇām  || 1.36 ||

sukṛtaphalaṃ tāvadidaṃ sukule yajjanma dhīśca tatrāpi  |
sāpi ca sakalamahītalatulanaphalā bhūtalaṃ ca suvidheyam  || 1.37 ||

bhūtalavidheyatāyāḥ phalamarthāste ca vividhabhogaphalāḥ  |
bhogāśca santi śarīre tadanityamato vṛthā sakalaṃ  || 1.38 ||

iti dhanaśarīrabhogānmatvānityānsadaiva yatanīyam  |
muktau sāca jñanāttaccābhyāsātsa ca sthire dehe  || 1.39 ||

tatsthairye na samarthaṃ rasāyanaṃ kimapi mūlalohādi  |
svayamasthirasvabhāvaṃ dāhyaṃ kledyaṃ ca śoṣyaṃ ca  || 1.40 ||

kāṣṭhauṣadhyo nāge nāgo vaṅge'tha vaṅgamapi śulbe  |
śulbaṃ tāre tāraṃ kanake kanakaṃ ca līyate sūte  || 1.41 ||

amṛtatvaṃ hi bhajante haramūrtau yogino yathā līnāḥ  |
tadvatkavalitagagane rasarāje hemalohādyāḥ  || 1.42 ||

paramātmanīva satataṃ bhavati layo yatra sarvasattvānām  |
eko'sau rasarājaḥ śarīramajarāmaraṃ kurute  || 1.43 ||

sthiradehe'bhyāsavaśātprāpya jñānaṃ guṇāṣṭakopetam  |
prāpnoti brahmapadaṃ na punarbhavavāsajanmaduḥkhāni  || 1.44 ||

ekāṃśena jagadyugapadavaṣṭabhyāvasthitaṃ paraṃ jyotiḥ  |
pādaistribhistadamṛtaṃ sulabhaṃ na viraktimātreṇa  || 1.45 ||

na hi dehena kathaṃcidvyādhijarāmaraṇaduḥkhavidhureṇa  |
kṣaṇabhaṅgureṇa sūkṣmaṃ tadbrahmopāsituṃ śakyam  || 1.46 ||

nāmāpi dehasiddhe ko gṛṇhīyādvinā śarīreṇa  |
yadyogagamyamamalaṃ manaso'pi na gocaraṃ tattvaṃ  || 1.47 ||

yajñāddānāttapaso vedādhyayanāddamātsadācārāt |
atyantabhūyasī kila yogavaśādātmasaṃvittiḥ  || 1.48 ||

bhrūyugamadhyagataṃ yacchikhividyutsūryavajjagadbhāsi  |
keṣāṃcitpuṇyadṛśāmunmīlati cinmayaṃ paraṃ jyotiḥ  || 1.49 ||

paramānandaikarasaṃ paramaṃ jyotiḥsvabhāvamavikalpam  |
vigalitasakalakleśaṃ jñeyaṃ śāntaṃ svasaṃvedyam  || 1.50 ||


tasminnādhāya manaḥ sphuradakhilaṃ cinmayaṃ jagatpaśyan  |
utsannakarmabandho brahmatvamihaiva cāpnoti  || 1.51 ||

rāgadveṣavimuktāḥ satyācārā mṛṣārahitāḥ  |
sarvatra nirviśeṣā bhavanti cidbrahmasaṃsparśāt || 1.52 ||

tiṣṭhantyaṇimādiyutā vilasaddehāḥ sadoditānandāḥ  |
brahmasvabhāvamamṛtaṃ saṃprāptāścaiva kṛtakṛtyāḥ  || 1.53 ||

āyatanaṃ vidyānāṃ mūlaṃ dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇām  |
śreyaḥ paraṃ kimanyaccharīramajarāmaraṃ vihāyaikam  || 1.54 ||

pratyakṣeṇa pramāṇena yo na jānāti sūtakam  |
adṛṣṭavigrahaṃ devaṃ kathaṃ jñāsyati cinmayam  || 1.55 ||

yajjarayā jarjaritaṃ kāsaśvāsādiduḥkhavivaśaṃ ca  |
yogyaṃ tanna samādhau pratihatabuddhīndriyaprasaram  || 1.56 ||

bālaḥ ṣoḍaśavarṣo viṣayarasāsvādalampaṭaḥ parataḥ  |
yātaviveko vṛddho martyaḥ kathamāpnuyānmuktim  || 1.57 ||

asminneva śarīre yeṣāṃ paramātmano na saṃvedaḥ  |
dehatyāgādūrdhvaṃ teṣāṃ tadbrahma dūrataram  || 1.58 ||

brahmādayo yatante tasmindivyāṃ tanuṃ samāśritya  |
jīvanmuktāścānye kalpāntasthāyino munayaḥ  || 1.59 ||

tasmājjīvanmuktiṃ samīhamānena yoginā prathamam  |
divyā tanurvidheyā haragaurīsṛṣṭisaṃyogāt || 1.60 ||

śaile'smiñśivayoḥ prītyā parasparajigīṣayā  |
saṃpravṛtte ca saṃbhoge trilokīkṣobhakāriṇi 61 ||

vinivārayituṃ vahniḥ saṃbhogaṃ preṣitaḥ suraiḥ  |
kāṅkṣamāṇaistayoḥ putraṃ tārakāsuramārakam  || 1.62 ||

kapotarūpiṇaṃ prāptaṃ himavatkandare'nalam  |
apakṣibhāvasaṃkṣubdhaṃ smaralīlāvilokinam  || 1.63 ||

taṃ dṛṣṭvā lajjitaḥ śaṃbhurvirataḥ suratāttadā  |
pracyutaścaramo dhāturgṛhītaḥ śūlapāṇinā  || 1.64 ||

prakṣipto vadane vahnergaṅgāyāmapi so'patat |
bahiḥ kṣiptastayā so'pi paridandahyamānayā  || 1.65 ||

saṃjātāstanmalādhānāddhātavaḥ siddhihetavaḥ  |
yāvadagnimukhādreto nyapatadbhūrisārataḥ  || 1.66 ||

śatayojananimnāṃstānkṛtvā kūpāṃstu pañca ca  |
tadāprabhṛti kūpasthaṃ tadretaḥ pañcadhābhavat || 1.67 ||

raso rasendraḥ sūtaśca pārado miśrakastathā  |
iti pañcavidho jātaḥ kṣetrabhedena śaṃbhujaḥ  || 1.68 ||

raso rakto vinirmuktaḥ sarvadoṣai rasāyanaḥ  |
saṃjātāstridaśāstena nīrujā nirjarāmarāḥ  || 1.69 ||

rasendro doṣanirmuktaḥ śyāvo rūkṣo'ticañcalaḥ  |
rasāyino'bhavaṃstena nāgā mṛtyujarojjhitāḥ  || 1.70 ||

devairnāgaiśca tau kūpau pūritau mṛdbhiraśmabhiḥ  |
tadāprabhṛti lokānāṃ tau jātāvatidurlabhau  || 1.71 ||

īṣatpītaśca rūkṣāṅgo doṣayuktaśca sūtakaḥ  |
daśāṣṭasaṃskṛtaiḥ siddho dehaṃ lohaṃ karoti saḥ  || 1.72 ||

athānykūpajaḥ ko'pi sa calaḥ śvetavarṇavān  |
pārado vividhairyogaiḥ sarvarogaharaḥ sa hi  || 1.73 ||

mayūracandrikāchāyaḥ sa raso miśrako mataḥ  |
so'pyaṣṭādaśasaṃskārayuktaścātīva siddhidaḥ  || 1.74 ||

trayaḥ sūtādayaḥ sūtāḥ sarvasiddhikarā api  |
nijakarmavinirmāṇaiḥ śaktimanto'timātrayā  || 1.75 ||

etāṃ rasasamutpattiṃ yo jānāti sa dhārmikaḥ  |
āyurārogyasaṃtānaṃ rasasiddhiṃ ca vindati  || 1.76 ||

rasanātsarvadhātūnāṃ rasa ityabhidhīyate  |
jarāruṅmṛtyunāśāya rasyate vā raso mataḥ  || 1.77 ||

rasoparasarājatvādrasendra iti kīrtitaḥ  |
dehalohamayīṃ siddhiṃ sūte sūtastataḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1.78 ||

rogapaṅkābdhimagnānāṃ pāradānācca pāradaḥ  |
sarvadhātugataṃ tejomiśritaṃ yatra tiṣṭhati  || 1.79 ||

tasmātsa miśrakaḥ prokto nānārūpaphalapradaḥ  |
evaṃbhūtasya sūtasya martyamṛtyugadacchidaḥ  |
prabhāvānmānuṣā jātā devatulyabalāyuṣaḥ  || 1.80 ||

tān dṛṣṭvābhyarthito rudraḥ śakreṇa tadanantaram  |
doṣaiśca kañcukābhiśca rasarājo niyojitaḥ  || 1.81 ||

tadāprabhṛti sūto'sau naiva sidhyatyasaṃskṛtaḥ  |
jalago jalarūpeṇa tvarito haṃsago bhavet || 1.82 ||

malago malarūpeṇa sadhūmo dhūmago bhavet |
anyā jīvagatirdaivī jīvo'ṇḍādiva niṣkramet || 1.83 ||

sa tāṃśca jīvayejjīvāṃstena jīvo rasaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
catasro gatayo dṛśyā adṛśyā pañcamī gatiḥ  || 1.84 ||

mantradhyānādinā tasya rudhyate pañcamī gatiḥ  || 1.85 ||

iti bhinnagatitvācca sūtarājyasya durlabhaḥ  |
saṃskārastasya bhiṣajā nipuṇena tu rakṣayet || 1.86 ||

prathame rajasi snātāṃ hayārūḍhāṃ svalaṃkṛtāṃ  |
vīkṣamāṇāṃ vadhūṃ dṛṣṭvā jighṛkṣuḥ kūpago rasaḥ  || 1.87 ||

udgacchati javātsāpi taṃ dṛṣṭvā yāti vegataḥ  |
anugacchati tāṃ sūtaḥ sīmānaṃ yojanonmitam  || 1.88 ||

pratyāyāti tataḥ kūpaṃ vegataḥ śivasaṃbhavaḥ  |
mārganirmitagarteṣu sthitaṃ gṛhṇanti pāradam  |
patito darade deśe gauravādvahnivaktrataḥ  || 1.89 ||

sa raso bhūtale līnastattaddeśanivāsinaḥ  |
tāṃ mṛdaṃ pātanāyantre kṣiptvā sūtaṃ haranti ca  || 1.90 ||

iti śrīvaidyapatisiṃhaguptasya sūnorvāgbhaṭācāryasya  |
kṛtau rasaratnasamuccaye rasotpattirnāma  |
prathamo'dhyāyaḥ  || 1 ||



atha dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ

[mahārasāḥ]

abhravaikrāntamākṣīkavimalādrijasasyakam  |
capalo rasakaśceti jñātvāṣṭau saṃgrahedrasān  || 2.1 ||

[devyā rajo bhavedgandho dhātuḥ śukraṃ tathābhrakamiti kṣepakaḥ]  || 2.2 ||

gaurītejaḥ paramamamṛtaṃ vātapittakṣayaghnaṃ prajñābodhi praśamitarujaṃ vṛṣyamāyuṣyamagryam  |
balyaṃ snigdhaṃ rucidamakaphaṃ dīpanaṃ śītavīryaṃ tattadyogaiḥ sakalagadahṛdvyoma sūtendrabandhi  || 2.3 ||

rājahastādadhastādyatsamānītaṃ ghanaṃ khaneḥ  |
bhavettaduktaphaladaṃ niḥsattvaṃ niṣphalaṃ param  || 2.4 ||

pinākaṃ nāgamaṇḍūkaṃ vajramityabhrakaṃ matam  |
śvetādivarṇabhedena pratyekaṃ taccaturvidham  || 2.5 ||

pinākaṃ pāvakottaptaṃ vimuñcati daloccayam  |
tatsevitaṃ malaṃ baddhvā mārayatyeva mānavam  || 2.6 ||

nāgābhraṃ nāgavatkuryāddhvaniṃ pāvakasaṃsthitam  |
tadbhuktaṃ kurute kuṣṭhaṃ maṇḍalākhyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ  || 2.7 ||

utplutyotplutya maṇḍūkaṃ dhmātaṃ patati cābhrakam  |
tatkuryādaśmarīrogamasādhyaṃ śastrato'nyathā  || 2.8 ||

vajrābhraṃ vahnisaṃtaptaṃ nirmuktāśeṣavaikṛtam  |
dehalohakaraṃ tacca sarvarogaharaṃ param  || 2.9 ||

śvetaṃ raktaṃ ca pītaṃ ca kṛṣṇamevaṃ caturvidham  |
śvetaṃ śvetakriyāsūktaṃ raktābhaṃ raktakarmaṇi  |
pītābhamabhrakaṃ yattu śreṣṭhaṃ tatpītakarmaṇi  || 2.10 ||

caturvidhaṃ varaṃ vyoma yadyapyuktaṃ rasāyane  |
tathāpi kṛṣṇavarṇābhraṃ koṭikoṭiguṇādhikam  || 2.11 ||

snigdhaṃ pṛthudalaṃ varṇasaṃyuktaṃ bhārato'dhikam  |
sukhanirmocyapatraṃ ca tadabhraṃ śastamīritam  || 2.12 ||

sacandrikaṃ ca kiṭṭābhaṃ vyoma na grāsayedrasaḥ  |
grasitaśca niyojyo'sau lohe caiva rasāyane  || 2.13 ||

niścandrikaṃ mṛtaṃ vyoma sevyaṃ sargadeṣu ca  |
sevitaṃ candrasaṃyuktaṃ mehaṃ mandānalaṃ caret || 2.14 ||

yairuktaṃ yuktinirmuktaiḥ patrābhrakarasāyanam  |
tairdṛṣṭaṃ kālakūṭākhyaṃ viṣaṃ jīvanahetave  || 2.15 ||

sattvārthaṃ sevanārthaṃ ca yojayecchodhitābhrakam  |
anyathā tvaguṇaṃ kṛtvā vikarotyeva niścitam  || 2.16 ||

prataptaṃ saptavārāṇi nikṣiptaṃ kāñjake'bhrakam  |
nirdoṣaṃ jāyate nūnaṃ prakṣiptaṃ vāpi gojale  || 2.17 ||

triphalākvathite cāpi gavāṃ dugdhe viśeṣataḥ  |
tato dhānyābhrakaṃ kṛtvā piṣṭvā matsyākṣikārasaiḥ  || 2.18 ||

cakrīṃ kṛtvā viśoṣyātha puṭedardhebhake puṭe  |
puṭedevaṃ hi ṣaḍvāraṃ paunarnavarasaiḥ saha  || 2.19 ||

kalāṃśaṭaṅkaṇenāpi saṃmardya kṛtacakrikam  |
ardhebhākhyapuṭaistadvatsaptavāraṃ puṭetkhalu  || 2.20 ||

evaṃ vāsārasenāpi taṇḍulīyarasena ca  |
prapuṭetsaptavārāṇi pūrvaproktavidhānataḥ  || 2.21 ||

evaṃ siddhaṃ ghanaṃ sarvayogeṣu viniyojayet || 2.22 ||

cūrṇābhraṃ śālisaṃyuktaṃ vastrabaddhaṃ hi kāñjike  |
niryātaṃ mardanādvastrāddhānyābhramiti kathyate  || 2.23 ||

dhānyābhraṃ kāsamardasya rasena parimarditam  |
puṭitaṃ daśavāreṇa mriyate nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 2.24 ||

tadvanmustārasenāpi tandulīyarasena ca  |
pītāmalakasaubhāgyapiṣṭaṃ cakrīkṛtābhrakam  || 2.25 ||

puṭitaṃ ṣaṣṭivārāṇi sindūrābhaṃ prajāyate  |
kṣayādyakhilarogaghnaṃ bhavedrogānupānataḥ  || 2.26 ||

vaṭamūlatvacaḥ kvāthaistāmbūlīpatrasārataḥ  |
vāsāmatsyākṣikābhyāṃ vā mīnākṣyā sakaṭillayā  || 2.27 ||

payasā vaṭavṛkṣasya marditaṃ puṭitaṃ ghanam  |

yonirogānaśeṣāṃśca viṣamāṃśca jvarānapi  |

atha gandhakaḥ -
gandhāśmagairikāsīsakāṅkṣītālaśilāñjanam  |

pārvatyuvāca -
gandhakasya tu māhātmyaṃ tadguhyaṃ vada me prabho  |
īśvara uvāca -
śvetadvīpe purā devi sarvaratnavibhūṣite  |
sarvakāmamaye ramye tīre kṣīrapayonidheḥ  || 3.2 ||

vidyādharādimukhyābhiraṅganābhiśca yoginām  |
siddhāṅganābhiḥ śreṣṭhābhistathaivāpsarasāṃ gaṇaiḥ  || 3.3 ||

vṛtā devāṅganābhistvaṃ kailāsaṃ punarāgatā  |
ūrmibhistadrajovastraṃ nītaṃ madhye payonidheḥ  || 3.7 ||

iti devagaṇaiḥ prītaiḥ purā proktaṃ sureśvari  |
tenāyaṃ gandhako nāma vikhyātaḥ kṣitimaṇḍale  || 3.11 ||

granthāntare caturdhā gandhako jñeyo varṇaiḥ śvetādibhiḥ khalu  |

tathā cāmalasāraḥ syādyo bhavetpītavarṇavān  |
śukapicchaḥ sa eva syācchreṣṭho rasarasāyane  || 3.14 ||

raktaśca śukatuṇḍākhyo dhātuvādavidhau varaḥ  |

gandhāśmātirasāyanaḥ sumadhuraḥ pāke kaṭūṣṇo mataḥ kaṇḍūkuṣṭhavisarpadadrudalano dīptānalaḥ pācanaḥ  |

chardihṛdrogayoścaiva tṛṣṇāmadyodbhavārśasām  |
udāvartātisārāṇāṃ grahaṇyartipravāhiṇoḥ  || 12.2 ||

visūcyā vahnimāndyasya mūtrakṛcchrāśmarujām  |
mehasya somarogasya piṭikānāṃ ca vidradheḥ  || 12.3 ||

vṛddhigulmādirogāṇāṃ śūlānāmudarasya ca  |
pāṇḍuśophavisarpāṇāṃ kuṣṭhaśvitranabhasvatām  || 12.4 ||

vātāsrasyāvṛtānāṃ ca vandhyānāṃ garbhiṇīrujām  |
sūtikābālarogāṇāmunmāde'pasmṛtāvapi  || 12.5 ||

netraroge karṇaroge nāsārogāsyarogayoḥ  |
śiraḥsaṃjātarogeṣu vraṇe bhaṅge bhagaṃdare  || 12.6 ||

granthyādau kṣudrarogeṣu guhyaroge viṣeṣu ca  |
jarāyāstvanapatyānāṃ bījapoṣaṇahetave  || 12.7 ||

paripāṭyānayā sarvaṃ rogāṇāṃ hi cikitsanam  |
rasalohaviṣairatra yogairvakṣye yathāgamam  || 12.8 ||

romāñcakampau vadane madhutvamujjṛmbhaṇaṃ mastakatodadāhau  |
vātajvarasyoktamidaṃ hi lakṣma bhuktottaraṃ syādyadi śaśvadeva  || 12.9 ||

virekaśoṣāsyakaṭutvatīvratāpapralāpabhramamūrchanāni  |
etāni pittajvaralakṣaṇāni vamiḥ satṛṣṇāṅgavidāhitā ca  || 12.10 ||

kāsaśvāsau mukhe jāḍyaṃ mādhuryaṃ bahunidratā  |
prasvedaḥ svalpadāhaśca śleṣmajajvaralakṣaṇam  || 12.11 ||

miśritaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ yattu dvayostriṣu bhavecca tat || 12.12 ||

vimarditābhyāṃ rasagandhakābhyāṃ nīreṇa kuryādiha golakaṃ tam  |
bhāṇḍe navīne viniveśya paścāttadgolakasyopari tāmrapātram  || 12.13 ||

sārdhaṃ muhūrtaṃ vinirudhya dhīmānuddīpayeddīptakṛśānunāsya  |
adhastataḥ sidhyati parpaṭīyaṃ navajvarāraṇyakṛśānumeghaḥ  || 12.14 ||

vilipya pūrvaṃ rasanāṃ ca tāludeśaṃ ca sindhūdbhavajīrakārdraiḥ  |
vallonmitāṃ cārdrakatoyamiśrāmenāṃ niyojya sthagayetpaṭena  || 12.15 ||

gharmodgamo yāvadataḥ paraṃ ca takraudanaṃ pathyamiha prayojyam  |
kuryāddinānāṃ tritayaṃ yadītthaṃ jvarasya śaṅkāpi tadā bhavetkim  || 12.16 ||

sūtārkagandhacapalājayapālatiktāpathyātrivṛcca viṣatindukajān samāṃśān  |
saṃbhāvya vajripayasā madhunā trivallastrailokyaḍambararaso'bhinavajvaraghnaḥ  || 12.17 ||

pādāṃśakaṃ sāraraviḥ samāṃśagandho vipakvaḥ svakaṣāyapiṣṭaḥ  |
rasaḥ kramānmāṣamito'nilādijvareṣu nāmnā kila meghanādaḥ  || 12.18 ||

daradajaladayuktaṃ śuddhasūtaṃ ca gandhaṃ praharamatha supiṣṭaṃ vallayugmaṃ ca dadyāt |
jvaragajaharisaṃjñaṃ śṛṅgaverodakena prathamajanitadāhī kṣīrabhaktena bhojyaḥ  || 12.19 ||

saṃtaptasīsabhāgaṃ ca pāradaṃ gandhakaṃ kaṇām  |
samabhāgaṃ pṛthaktatra melayecca yathāvidhi  || 12.20 ||

jambīrasya rase sarvaṃ mardayecca dinatrayam  |
meghanādakumāryośca rase cāpi dinatrayam  || 12.21 ||

dinadvayamajāmūtre gavāṃ mūtre dinatrayam  |
bhāvayecca yathāyogyaṃ tasminnetāni dāpayet || 12.22 ||

saindhavaṃ citrakaṃ bhāgaṃ sauvarcalalavaṇaṃ tathā  |
tena sammelanaṃ kṛtvā bhāvayecca punaḥ punaḥ  || 12.23 ||

anena vidhinā samyaksiddho bhavati tadrasaḥ  |
śarkarāghṛtasaṃyuktaṃ dadyādvallatrayaṃ rasam  || 12.24 ||

godhūmasyaudanaṃ pathyaṃ māṣasūpaṃ ca vāstukam  |
dhātrīphalasamāyuktaṃ sarvajvaravināśanam  |
dīpikārasa ityeṣa tantrajñaiḥ parikīrtitaḥ  || 12.25 ||

pāradaṃ rasakaṃ tālaṃ tutthaṃ gandhakaṭaṅkaṇam  |
sarvametatsamaṃ śuddhaṃ kāravallyā dravairdinam  || 12.26 ||

mardayettena kalkena tāmrapātrodaraṃ limpet |
aṅgulārdhārdhamānena taṃ pacetsikatāhvaye  || 12.27 ||

yantre yāvatsphuṭantyevaṃ vrīhayastasya pṛṣṭhataḥ  |
tataḥ suśītalaṃ grāhyaṃ tāmrapātrodarādbhiṣak || 12.28 ||

śītabhañjī raso nāma cūrṇayenmaricaiḥ samam  |
māṣaikaṃ parṇakhaṇḍena bhakṣayennāśayejjvaram  |
tridinairviṣamaṃ tīvramekadvitricaturthakam  || 12.29 ||

sūtatālaśilāstulyā mardayetkarkaṭīrase  |
tāmrapātre vinikṣipya tatkalkaṃ kajjalīkṛtam  || 12.30 ||

vipacedvālukāyantre yathoktavidhinā tataḥ  |
dadyānmaricacūrṇena māṣamātraṃ bhiṣagvaraḥ  || 12.31 ||

prapibeduṣṇatoyasya culukaṃ śītakajvare  |
śītabhañjī rasaḥ so'yaṃ śītajvaranivāraṇaḥ  || 12.32 ||

kūṣmāṇḍacūrṇatilajaiḥ praviśuddhatālaṃ gāḍhaṃ vimardya suṣavīsalilena tulyam  |
sūtena hiṅgulabhuvā sikatākhyayantre golaṃ vidhāya parivṛttakapālamadhye  || 12.33 ||

pattreṇa taṃ dinapateśca pidhāya ruddhvā saṃdhiṃ tayorguḍasudhākhaṭikāśivābhiḥ  |
vahnau pacenmṛduni pātraśiraḥsthaśālīvaivarṇyamātramavadhiṃ pravidhāya dhīmān  || 12.34 ||

vallaṃ tataḥ surasamiśramamuṣya dadyātsarpiḥ sitākaṇapayomadhu cānupeyam  |
jetuṃ jvarān praviṣamāniha vāntiśāntyai maulau suśītalajalasya dadīta dhārām  || 12.35 ||

athāmayāntaṃ rasarājamaulibhūṣāmaṇiṃ taṃ mṛtajīvanākhyam  |
sudhāraseneva rasena yena saṃjīvanaṃ syātsahasāturāṇām  || 12.36 ||

rasahiṅgulajepālairvṛddhyā dantyambumarditaiḥ  |
dinārdhena jvaraṃ hanyādguñjaikaṃ sitayā saha  || 12.37 ||

śuddhaṃ sūtaṃ viṣaṃ gandhaṃ dhūrtabījaṃ tribhiḥ samam  |
caturbhiśca samaṃ vyoṣaṃ cūrṇīkṛtya nidhāpayet || 12.38 ||

dantabhāṇḍe'tha vā śārṅge kāṣṭhe naiva kadācana  |
vātaśleṣmajvare deyaṃ dvaṃdvaje vā tridoṣaje  || 12.39 ||

rasena śṛṅgaverasya jambīrasyāthavā punaḥ  |
guñjādvayaṃ ca jīrṇe'smindadhibhaktaṃ prayojayet || 12.40 ||

ekadvitridinairhanyājjvarān doṣakrameṇa tu  |
mahājvarāṅkuśo nāma raso'yaṃ śambhunoditaḥ  || 12.41 ||

tālaṃ tāmrarajo rasaśca gaganaṃ gandhaśca nepālakaṃ dīnārapramitaṃ tadardhamuditaṃ ṭaṅkaṃ śilāmākṣikam  |
dīnāradvitayaṃ viṣasya śikhinaḥ piṣṭvā rasaiḥ pācito yaścintāmaṇivajjvaraughavijayī nāmnā tu mṛtyuṃjayaḥ  || 12.42 ||

tālaṃ tāmramayorajaśca capalā tutthābhrakaṃ kāntakaṃ nāgaṃ syācca samāṃśakaṃ sumṛditaṃ mūlaṃ ca paunarnavam  |
bhṛṅgīkāsaharīpunarnavāmahāmandārapattrodbhavaiḥ kalkaṃ vālukayantrapācitamidaṃ sarvajvarasyāntakṛt || 12.43 ||

tutthena tulyaḥ śivajaśca gandho jambīranīreṇa vimardanīyaḥ  |
dinatrayaṃ melaya tena tulyaṃ vyoṣaṃ tataḥ sidhyati candrasūryaḥ  || 12.44 ||

vallo vijetuṃ viṣamāvalambi dalena deyo bhujagākhyavallyāḥ  |
dugdhaṃ hitaṃ syādiha śṛṅgaverarasena śaityeṣu niṣevaṇīyaḥ  || 12.45 ||

takraṃ sagarbhājvaraśūlayostu drākṣāmbunā pathyamanantaroktam  |
rodhaṃ varāyāḥ salilena śūlaṃ jambīranīreṇa varājalena  || 12.46 ||

apasmṛtāvatra niyojanīyamabhyañjanaṃ bimbapayobhavābhyām  |
ghṛtaudanaṃ syādiha bhojanāya jambīranīreṇa nihanti gulmam  || 12.47 ||

hiṅgvamlikānimburasena deyaṃ plīhodare syādiha takrabhaktaḥ  |
stambhārthamasminsasitaṃ payaḥ syādguḍo niyojyo vamanapraśāntyai  || 12.48 ||

aśītiryasya varṣāṇi vasuvarṣāṇi yasya vā  |
viṣaṃ tasya na dātavyaṃ dattaṃ ceddoṣakārakam  || 12.49 ||

meghapāradagandhāśmaviṣavyoṣapaṭūni ca  |
jīrakadvayametāni samabhāgāni kārayet || 12.50 ||

sinduvārarasenāpi laśunasya rasena ca  |
apāmārgarasenāpi saptarātraṃ vimardayet || 12.51 ||

tatpakvaṃ vālukāyantre guñjāmātraṃ prayojayet |
sanāgavallīmaricaṃ tataḥ śītāmbu pāyayet || 12.52 ||

umāprasādano nāma rasaḥ śītajvarāpahaḥ  |
cāturthikaṃ trirātraṃ vā nāśayetkimutāparān  || 12.53 ||

ṭaṅkaṇaṃ rasagandhau ca samabhāgānprakalpayet |
nepālaṃ dviguṇaṃ dattvā mardayetkhallamadhyataḥ  || 12.54 ||

ślakṣṇatāṃ yāti tadyāvattāvattanmardayecchanaiḥ  |
saindhavaṃ maricaṃ śaṅkhaṃ ciñcākṣāraṃ samākṣikam  || 12.55 ||

tattulyametatkṛtvātha nimbūtoyena mardayet |
caṇapramāṇavaṭikāṃ bhakṣayeddivasatrayam  || 12.56 ||

aikāhikaṃ dvyāhikaṃ ca tryāhikaṃ ca caturthakam  |
sarvajvaravināśāya jvarāṅkuśa iti smṛtaḥ  || 12.57 ||

abhrakaṃ gandhakaṃ sūtaṃ tolakaikaṃ pṛthakpṛthak |
gṛhītvā viṣatolārdhaṃ tolārdhaṃ tintiḍīphalam  || 12.58 ||

etatsarvaṃ samaṃ kṛtvā mardayetkhallamadhyataḥ  |
ślakṣṇatāṃ yāti tadyāvattāvatsaṃmardayecchanaiḥ  || 12.59 ||

vistāre pariṇāhe ca gartāṃ kṛtvā ṣaḍaṅgulām  |
phaṇivallīdalānyantargartāyāṃ prakṣipennaraḥ  || 12.60 ||

parṇeṣu sūtakalkaṃ taṃ gartāyāṃ sthāpayeddṛḍham  |
kalkādupari tatparṇairgartāvaktraṃ prapūrayet || 12.61 ||

gartāyāṃ tu tato deyaṃ puṭamāraṇyakotpalaiḥ  |
svāṅgaśītalatāṃ jñātvā samākarṣettataḥ param  || 12.62 ||

sūtaliptadalaiḥ sārdhaṃ kalkaṃ khalle vimardayet |
tolārdhamamṛtaṃ kṣiptvā tolārdhaṃ tintiḍīphalam  || 12.63 ||

sthāpayetkhallitaṃ kalkaṃ yojayedguñjamātrayā  |
śṛṅgaverāmbhasā yuktaṃ tīkṣṇacitrakasaindhavaiḥ  || 12.64 ||

saṃnipāte tathā vāte tridoṣe viṣamajvare  |
agnimāndye grahiṇyāṃ ca tathā deyo'tisāriṇi  || 12.65 ||

bhojanaṃ dadhibhaktaṃ ca rase'smin saṃprayojayet |
vyādhyādikaṃ yathā kuryādudakaṃ ḍhālayettataḥ  || 12.66 ||

eṣa yogavaraḥ śrīmānprāṇināṃ prāṇadāyakaḥ  |
cintāmaṇiritikhyāto rasaḥ sarvāṅgasundaraḥ  || 12.67 ||

sūtendraṃ parimardya pañcapaṭubhiḥ kṣāraistribhistaṃ tataḥ piṇḍe hiṅgumahauṣadhāsurīmaye saṃsvedya dhānyodake  |
nirguṇḍyambuhutāśamanthatilaparṇyunmattabhṛṅgārdrakaṃ kāmātāgirikarṇikāplavadalāpañcāṅgulotthairjalaiḥ  || 12.68 ||

sūtendreṇa samairvimardya sahajaiḥ pittaistato bhāvayeddaṃṣṭricchāgalulāyamatsyaśikhināṃ sā saṃnipātāñjayet |
vikhyātā bhuvi lokanāthaguṭikā mārīcamātrā hitā syādasyāḥ sahitaṃ dadhīkṣuśakalaṃ vīryaṃ bhavecchītalam  || 12.69 ||

vajravaikrāntayorbhasma pratyekaṃ niṣkasammitam  |
śṛṅgīviṣaṃ dviniṣkaṃ ca triniṣkaṃ cūlikāpaṭu  || 12.70 ||

pañcaniṣko'gnijāraśca sarvamekatra melayet |
tāvadbhasma rasaṃ yāvanmardayeddivasatrayam  || 12.71 ||

śārṅgaṣṭādikavargasya kṣāranīreṇa bhāvayet |
trayoviṃśativārāṇi vimardya ca viśoṣya ca  || 12.72 ||

tato vimardya divasaṃ kṣipeddantakaraṇḍake  |
mṛtasaṃjīvanākhyo'yaṃ sūcikābharaṇo rasaḥ  || 12.73 ||

saṃnipātena tīvreṇa mumurṣorbhūgatasya ca  |
tāluni vṛścayitvātha rasamenaṃ vinikṣipet || 12.74 ||

sūcyātisūkṣmayā toyabhinnayātiprayatnataḥ  |
tatastailena taṃ liptvā nirvāte saṃniveśayet || 12.75 ||

tato'rdhapraharādūrdhvaṃ muktamūtrapurīṣakam  |
labdhasaṃjñaṃ pratāpāḍhyaṃ dolayantaṃ śiro muhuḥ  || 12.76 ||

āyuṣmantaṃ vijānīyādanyathā cānyathā khalu  |
tataḥ śītāmbusampūrṇe kaṭāhe taṃ niveśayet || 12.77 ||

tatra cotkvathitaṃ toyamapanīyāparaṃ kṣipet |
yācamānamamuṃ paścātpāyayetsasitaṃ payaḥ  || 12.78 ||

dadhi vā sitayopetaṃ nārikelajalaṃ tathā  |
rambhāphalāni dadyācca mriyate so'nyathā khalu  || 12.79 ||

labdhasaṃjñaṃ prabhāṣantaṃ yācamānaṃ phalādikam  |
tasmādākṛṣya tailāktaṃ tailaṃ piṣṭvāpanīya ca  || 12.80 ||

lepayedgandhakarpūrairā pādatalamastakam  |
ityādiśiśirairdravyaiḥ saptarātramupācaret || 12.81 ||

karṇākṣināsikāvaktre kṣipetpotāśrayaṃ muhuḥ  |
aṣṭame'hani samprāpte dardurīmūlajaṃ rasam  || 12.82 ||

sasitaṃ pāyayedvegamavatārayituṃ rasam  |
rase'vatārite paścādyatheṣṭaṃ bhojanaṃ dadhi  || 12.83 ||

śvāsocchvāsayutaṃ cānyairmuktajīvanalakṣaṇaiḥ  |
kaṭāhe jalasampūrṇe nikṣipedbodhalabdhaye  || 12.84 ||

labdhabodhaṃ tamākṛṣya pūrvavatsamupācaret |
jīvitvā yāvadāyuṣyaṃ mriyate tadanantaram  || 12.85 ||

saṃnipāte mahāghore majjantaṃ mṛtyusāgare  |
uddharettasya dharmasya brahmāpyantaṃ na vindati  || 12.86 ||

saṃnipātamahāmṛtyubhayanirmuktamānavaḥ  |
api sarvasvadānena prāṇācāryaṃ prapūjayet || 12.87 ||

anyathā narake tāvadyāvatkalpavikalpanā  |
ityājñā śāṃkarī jñeyā śambhunā parikīrtitā  || 12.88 ||

prakāśā naiva kartavyā rasottaraṇamūlikā  |
śāstraṃ vinā prayacchante mandā vittābhikāṅkṣayā  |
guruprasādamāsādya saṃnipāte prayujyatām  || 12.89 ||

śārṅgaṣṭā ca tathā vyāghrī karīrastilaparṇikā  |
indravāruṇikā mustā haridrāṅkolamūlikā  || 12.90 ||

apāmārgaḥ kaṇā svarṇaṃ kaṭutumbī ca tintiḍī  |
śārṅgaṣṭādikavargo'yaṃ saṃnipātaharaḥ param  || 12.91 ||

sūtaṃ gandhakatālakaṃ maṇiśilāṃ tāpyaṃ lavaṃ tutthakaṃ jepālaṃ viṣaṭaṅkaṇaṃ madhuphalaṃ kṛtvā samāṃśaṃ dṛḍham  |
kṛtvā kajjalikāṃ viṣolbaṇaphaṇeḥ pittaiśca saṃbhāvayetkṣiptvā sīsakakūpike rasavaraṃ sūcīmukhaṃ nāmataḥ  || 12.92 ||

brahmadvāri vikīrṇalohitalave guñjaikamātraṃ dadeddattvā sampuṭabaddhatandrikadhanurvāte saśākhāhime  |
kāsaṃ śvāsamarocakaṃ pralapanaṃ kampaṃ ca hikkāturaṃ mūkatvaṃ badhiratvamunmadamapasmāraṃ jayettatkṣaṇāt || 12.93 ||

rasagandhakatāmrābhraṃ lāṅgalīvahnirāmaṭham  |
vandhyāpaṭolanirguṇḍīsugandhānimbapallavāḥ  || 12.94 ||

pāṭhākṣāratrayaṃ kṣveḍaboladhattūrataṇḍulaiḥ  |
śṛṅgīmadhukasāraṃ ca jambīrāmlena mardayet || 12.95 ||

kuryāddhi niṣkamānena vaṭikā sā niyacchati  |
sasvedadāhābhinyāsaṃ saṃnipātagajāṃkuśaḥ  || 12.96 ||

sasārā vaiṣṇavī senā acalā kādi kaṅkaṇā  |
rāgarudropamopetā prauḍhā mastakaśālinī  || 12.97 ||

tribhāgaṃ tālakaṃ vidyādekabhāgaṃ tu pāradam  |
tadardhaṃ gandhakaṃ caiva tadardhaṃ tu manaḥśilā  || 12.98 ||

kāravallīdalarasairmardayettatpraharatrayam  |
pācito vālukāyantre cāturthikaharo rasaḥ  || 12.99 ||

syādrasena samāyukto gandhakaḥ sumanoharaḥ  |
hiyāvallitriguṇito nirguṇḍīrasamarditaḥ  || 12.100 ||

saptavārāṇi tadyojyamārdrakasvarasena tu  |
saṃtatādijvaraṃ hanyāccāturthikagajāṃkuśaḥ  || 12.101 ||

tāpyatālakajepālavatsanābhamanaḥśilāḥ  |
tāmragandhakasūtaṃ ca musalīrasamarditaḥ  |
mṛtyuṃjaya iti khyātaḥ kukkuṭīpuṭapācitaḥ  || 12.102 ||

valladvayaṃ prayuñjīta yatheṣṭaṃ dadhibhojanam  |
navajvaraṃ saṃnipātaṃ hanyādeṣa mahārasaḥ  || 12.103 ||

śuddhaṃ sūtaṃ viṣaṃ gandhaṃ maricaṃ ṭaṅkaṇaṃ kaṇām  |
mardayeddhūrtajadrāvairdinamekaṃ tu śoṣayet || 12.104 ||

pañcavaktro raso nāma dviguñjaḥ saṃnipātajit |
arkamūlakaṣāyaṃ ca satryūṣamanupāyayet |
dadhyodanaṃ hitaṃ tatra jalayogaṃ ca kārayet || 12.105 ||

rasagandhakatulyāṃśaṃ dhattūraphalajadravaiḥ  |
mardayeddinamekaṃ tu tattulyaṃ trikaṭu kṣipet |
unmattākhyo raso nāmnā nasye syātsaṃnipātajit || 12.106 ||

nistvagjepālajaṃ bījaṃ daśaniṣkaṃ pracūrṇayet |
maricaṃ pippalīṃ sūtaṃ pratiniṣkaṃ vimiśrayet || 12.107 ||

bhāvyaṃ jambīrajairdrāvaiḥ saptāhaṃ tatprayatnataḥ  |
saṃnipātaṃ nihantyāśu añjane yaḥ śivaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 12.108 ||

madanaphalaṃ viḍalavaṇaṃ sarṣapāḥ pratiniṣkadvayam  |
cūrṇayitvā triphalākvāthena saṭaṅkaṇaṃ pibet || 12.109 ||

kuṣṭhe jvare kāmalāyāṃ kaṇṭharoge hyajīrṇake  |
nasye ca girikarṇyutthabījaikaṃ śītavāriṇā  || 12.110 ||

pratyekaṃ rasagandhayordvipalayoḥ kṛtvā maṣīṃ śuddhayorramyāṃ mlecchalulāyalocanamanodhātrīprakuñcatrayam  |
pathyāyā badaratrikaṃ trikaṭu ṣaṭśāṇaṃ vacā dharmiṇī vellāmbhodharapattrakadviradakiñjalkāśvagandhāhvayam  || 12.111 ||

piṣṭvaitatsamasāramakhilaṃ karṣonmitaṃ nyasya tatpronmardyārdhakarañjakāmṛtayutaṃ sāgastikatryūṣaṇaiḥ  |
bhūdhātrīvijayāsaritpatiphalaṃ jvālāmukhīmārkavaiḥ pratyekaṃ vidadhīta niścalamatiḥ sapta kramādbhāvanāḥ  || 12.112 ||

pittairatho pañca vidhāya pañcabhiḥ karañjapattrāmṛtadhūpanaṃ tataḥ  |
dattvārdrakasya svarasena taṇḍulākṛtiṃ vidadhyādguṭikāṃ bhiṣagvaraḥ  || 12.113 ||

deyaikā saṃnipāte pratihataviṣaye mohanetraprasuptyoḥ syādgulme sājamodā pavanavikṛtiṣu tryūṣaṇena grahaṇyām  |
dātavyā jīrakeṇa dvipaturaganṛṇāṃ prāṇasaṃrakṣaṇāya kāyāmbhodhiretaṃ rasakasamarasaṃ vaidyanātho'bhyadhatta  || 12.114 ||

gandhakābhrasamaḥ sūto vārāhīrasamarditaḥ  |
pācito vālukāyantre triphalāvyoṣacitrakaiḥ  || 12.115 ||

trikṣāraṃ pañcalavaṇahiṅgugugguludīpyakaiḥ  |
sajīrakaiḥ sendrayavaiḥ pṛthagrasasamairyutaḥ  || 12.116 ||

māṣamātro'nupānena dvipalasyoṣṇavāriṇaḥ  |
abhinyāsānalabhraṃśagrahaṇīpāṇḍugulminām  || 12.117 ||

kuryātprāṇaparitrāṇamataḥ prāṇeśvaraḥ smṛtaḥ  |
vyādhivṛddhau prayogo'sya dvau vārau vaidyasaṃmataḥ  || 12.118 ||

rasāyovyoṣakaṅkuṣṭhaśilātālābhrahiṅgulān  |
kumbhyagnibhṛṅgamārītaṇḍulīyakamākṣikān  || 12.119 ||

hastiśuṇḍīyutāṃstulyāṃstadardhaśivagandhakān  |
tryahamārdrāmbunā piṣṭvā kūpīsthaṃ vālukāgninā  || 12.120 ||

jayājambīranirguṇḍīcāṅgerīvāri nikṣipet |
paktvā caturdaśāhāni piṣṭvārdrāktaṃ viśoṣayet || 12.121 ||
mṛtasaṃjīvanākhyo'yaṃ raso vallamito'śitaḥ  |
drāgjayedauṣadhaṃ saṃnipātādīn sakalān gadān  || 12.122 ||

rasabhāgo bhavedeko gandhako dviguṇo mataḥ  |
viṣatālakakaṅkuṣṭhaśilāhiṅgulalohakam  || 12.123 ||

vahnitrikaṭubhṛṅgāhvahemamākṣikamabhrakam  |
hastiśuṇḍī viṣaṃ kumbhī taṇḍulīyakatāmrakau  || 12.124 ||

eṣāṃ pratyekamekaikaṃ bhāgamādāya cūrṇayet |
ārdrakasya draveṇaiva mardayecca dinatrayam  || 12.125 ||

jambīrasya raso grāhyaḥ palatrayaparīkṣitaḥ  |
triphalāyāśca nirguṇḍyāḥ pratyekaṃ ca palatrayam  || 12.126 ||

rasasya palamātraṃ tu cāṅgeryāḥ parikīrtitam  |
kācakūpyāṃ vinikṣipya yantre kṣiptvā prayatnavān  || 12.127 ||

uddhṛtyārdrakaniryāsairmardayitvā viśoṣayet |
mṛtasaṃjīvano nāma raso'yaṃvidito bhuvi  |
guñjādvayaṃ dadītāsya saṃnipātāpanuttaye  || 12.128 ||

vaṅgaṃ nāgaṃ ca sūtaṃ ca nepālaṃ gandhakaṃ tathā  |
śulbaṃ viṣaṃ samāṃśena rasenārdreṇa mardayet || 12.129 ||

punarmardyeta nirguṇḍyāścāṅgeryā rasamarditaḥ  |
vallaprayogeṇa raso'yaṃ saṃnipātanut || 12.130 ||

gandhakaṃ ca rasaṃ śuddhaṃ pratyekaṃ karṣasammitam  |
ekatra kajjalīṃ kṛtvā tataḥ kurvīta golakam  || 12.131 ||

navabhāṇḍe vinikṣipya tāmrapātreṇa gopayet |
dṛḍhaṃ nirudhya tatpātramagnāvāropayettataḥ  || 12.132 ||

vrīhisphuṭanamātreṇa svāṅgaśītaṃ samuddharet |
navajvare prayuñjīta rasaṃ parpaṭikāhvayam  || 12.133 ||

ārdrakasya rasenaiva trivallaṃ tridinaṃ bhiṣak |
jvaritaṃ chādayedgāḍhaṃ yāvatsvedaḥ samudbhavet || 12.134 ||

takrabhaktaṃ bhavetpathyaṃ jvaramuktasya dehinaḥ  |
navajvarārirityeṣa rasaḥ paramadurlabhaḥ  |
vātajvare viśeṣeṇa rasaḥ sādhāraṇo mataḥ  || 12.135 ||

ṭaṅkaṇaṃ rasagandhau ca maricāni samāṃśakam  |
sarvaṃ jambīranīreṇa dināni trīṇi mardayet || 12.136 ||

saṃśoṣya śarkarāyuktaṃ matsyapittena bhāvayet |
bhāvitaṃ tadrasaṃ siddhamārdrakasvarasaistryaham  || 12.137 ||

vallaṃ vāratrayaṃ deyaṃ pānārthaṃ vāri śītalam  |
takrabhaktaṃ bhavetpathyaṃ vṛntākaphalasaṃyutam  |
sarvānnavajvarān hanti raso'yaṃ jalamañjarī  || 12.138 ||

kāntasya kaṇṭavedhyānāṃ pātrāṇāṃ bhasma kārayet |
tatsamaśca raso gandhaṣṭaṅkaṇo nimbavāriṇā  |
tataḥ saṃpeṣya tatkalkaṃ mardayettridinaṃ punaḥ  || 12.139 ||

rasatulyena matsyasya pittena paribhāvayet |
siddhaḥ kāntaraso hyeṣa prayojyo'bhinavajvare  |
śṛṅgaverānupānena mātrayā bhiṣaguttamaiḥ  || 12.140 ||

rasagandhau tathā vaṅgamabhrakaṃ samabhāgataḥ  |
melayitvātha vaṅgena samaṃ sūtaṃ vimardayet || 12.141 ||

tatraikīkṛtya gandhābhre peṣyaṃ jambīravāriṇā  |
sāmānyaṃ puṭamādadyātsaptadhā sādhitaṃ rasam  || 12.142 ||

kumāryā citrakeṇāpi bhāvayitvātha saptadhā  |
guḍena jīrakeṇāpi jvare jīrṇe prayojayet || 12.143 ||

kāse śvāse kumāryā ca triphalākvāthayogataḥ  |
unmādaṃ ca dhanurvātamamṛtākvāthayogataḥ  |
ityevaṃ rogatāpaghno rasaścandrodayābhidhaḥ  || 12.144 ||

nāgaṃ vaṅgaṃ rasaṃ tāmraṃ gandhakaṃ ṭaṅkaṇaṃ tathā  |
viṣaṃ ca nepālaṃ haritālaṃ samaṃ tathā  || 12.145 ||

vaṭakṣīreṇa saṃmardya sarvaṃ kuryāttu golakam  |
taṃ golakaṃ bhāṇḍamadhye pācayeddīpavahninā  || 12.146 ||

taṃ golaṃ śītalaṃ kṛtvā bhṛṅgarājena mardayet |
ārdrakasya rasenāpi mardayecca punaḥ punaḥ  || 12.147 ||

caṇapramāṇavaṭakān rasenārdrasya dāpayet |
guñjādvayapramāṇena jvaraṃ jīrṇaṃ haratyasau  || 12.148 ||

haraśca gandhakaṃ caiva kunaṭī ca samaṃ samam  |
mardyaṃ karkoṭikāyāśca rasena viniyojayet || 12.149 ||

navajvaramurāriḥ syādvallaṃ śarkarayā saha  |
taṇḍulīyarasenānupānaṃ śarkarayāpi vā  |
guñjādvayapramāṇena jvarānhanti navānhaṭhāt || 12.150 ||
