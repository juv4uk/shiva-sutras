1.10-11


śrībhairavaṃ praṇamyādau sarvajñaṃ trijagatprabhum  |
śrībhāratīṃ ca vighneśaṃ vande pratyūhaśāntaye  || 1.10 ||
brahmacaryaṃ tapaḥ kāryaṃ haviṣyānnasya bhojanam  |
kriyābhraṣṭe na sidhyanti taponaṣṭe phalanti na  || 1.11 ||

------------------

rasādhyāyaṭīkā:

iha rasakarmāṇi caturaśītiḥ guṭikābhedāścaturaśītiḥ añjanāni caturaśītiḥ || 1.10-11:1 ||
evaṃ te dvipañcāśadadhike dve śate bhedāḥ || 1.10-11:2 ||
tata eteṣāṃ bhedānāṃ madhyādyadaikā kācitkriyā prārabhyate tadā prārambhātpūrvaṃ māsamekaṃ brahmacaryarūpaṃ tapaḥ kartavyam || 1.10-11:3 ||
brahmacaryasyaiva sarvatapomūlatvāt || 1.10-11:4 ||
tadrahitasyāparatapasaḥ sarvasyāpi niṣphalatvāt || 1.10-11:5 ||
yadvā brahmacaryaṃ dhāryam || 1.10-11:6 ||
tathācāryādinā yathāyuktyā tapaḥ kāryam || 1.10-11:7 ||
dvayorapi sarvakarmaphalānāmādimūlatvāt || 1.10-11:8 ||
tathā haviṣyānnaṃ ghṛtaśarkarāmiśraṃ pāyasaṃ kṣīrabhojanaṃ kāryam || 1.10-11:9 ||
aparairāhārairdehaśuddherabhāvāt || 1.10-11:10 ||
prāṇāyāmasādhanābhāvācca || 1.10-11:11 ||
tata evaṃ karma pālayatā tatkarmaphalaṃ yāvannetavyam || 1.10-11:12 ||
yato rasādikā kriyāḥ sādhake bhraṣṭe yathoktabhojanamakurvati na sidhyanti || 1.10-11:13 ||
taponaṣṭe na ca phalanti || 1.10-11:14 ||
etacca granthānte svayaṃ spaṣṭayiṣyati || 1.10-11:15 ||

niyāmitasya nirodhakatvam || 1.12:10 ||
