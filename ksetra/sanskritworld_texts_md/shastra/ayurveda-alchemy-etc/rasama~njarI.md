 1
yadgaṇḍamaṇḍalagalanmadhuvāri binduḥ pānālasāti nibhṛtāṃ lalitālimālā |
yadguñjitena vinihanti navendranīla śaṅkāṃ sa vo gaṇapatiḥ śivamātanotu  || 1.1 ||
indīvarī bhavati yacca caraṇāravindadvandve purandarapuraḥsaradevatānām  |
vandārutā kalayatāṃ sukirīṭakoṭiḥ śrī śāradā bhavatu sā bhavapāradāya  || 1.2 ||
he bhasmāṅgaviraktirūpaguṇadaṃ taṃ preraṇādaṃ śivaṃ gaṅgābhūṣitaśekharaṃ smaraharaṃ śaktisvarūpaṃ prabho  |
tvāmīśaṃ karuṇārṇavaṃ śaraṇadaṃ vidyānidhiṃ nirguṇaṃ sūtendraṃ girijāpatiṃ śaśidharaṃ māṅgalyadevaṃ namaḥ  |
śrīvaidyanāthatanayaḥ sunayaḥ suśīlaḥ śrīśālinātha iti viśrutanāmadheyaḥ  |
tenāvalokya vidhivadvividhaprabandhānārambhate sukṛtinā rasamañjarīyam  || 1.3 ||
sanmadhuvrataṃ vṛndānāṃ satataṃ cittahāriṇī  |
anekarasapūrṇeyaṃ kriyate rasamañjarī  || 1.4 ||
harati sakalarogānmūrchito yo narāṇāṃ vitarati kila baddhaḥ khecaratvaṃ javena  |
sakalasuramunīndrairvanditaṃ śambhubījaṃ sa jayati bhavasindhuḥ pāradaḥ pārado'yam  || 1.5 ||
śuṣkendhanamahārāśiṃ yadvaddahati pāvakaḥ  |
tadvaddahati sūto'yaṃ rogān doṣatrayodbhavān  || 1.6 ||
tejo mṛgāṅkamaule soḍhuṃ yannaiva tejasāṃ puñjaiḥ  |
ajarāmaratāṃ vitarati kalpataruṃ ca raseśvaraṃ vande  || 1.7 ||
yo na vetti kṛpārāśiṃ rasahariharātmakam  |
vṛthā cikitsāṃ kurute sa vaidyo hāsyatāṃ vrajet || 1.8 ||
gurusevāṃ vinā karma yaḥ kuryānmūḍhacetasaḥ  |
sa yāti niṣphalatvaṃ hi svapnalabdhadhanaṃ yathā  || 1.9 ||
vidyāṃ gṛhītumicchanti cauryacchadmabalādinā  |
na teṣāṃ sidhyate kiṃcinmaṇimantrauṣadhādikam  || 1.10 ||
mantrasiddho mahāvīro niścalaḥ śivavatsalaḥ  |
devībhaktaḥ sadā dhīro devatāyāgatatparaḥ  || 1.11 ||
sarvaśāstrārthatattvajñaḥ kuśalo rasakarmaṇi  |
etallakṣaṇasaṃyukto rasavidyāgururbhavet || 1.12 ||
śiṣyo nijagurorbhaktaḥ satyavaktā dṛḍhavrataḥ  |
nirālasyaḥ svadharmajño devyārādhanatatparaḥ  || 1.13 ||
<pāradanāmāni>
śivabījaṃ sūtarājaḥ pāradaśca rasendrakaḥ  |
etāni rasanāmāni tathānyāni śive yathā  || 1.14 ||
antaḥsunīlo bahirujjvalo vā madhyāhṇasūryapratimaprakāśaḥ  |
śasto'tha dhūmraḥ paripāṇḍuraśca citro na yojyo rasakarmasiddhaye  || 1.15 ||
doṣamukto yadā sūtastadā mṛtyurujāpahaḥ  |
sākṣādamṛtamevaiṣa doṣayukto raso viṣam  || 1.16 ||
<pāradadoṣāḥ>
nāgo vaṅgo'gnicāpalyamasahyatvaṃ viṣaṃ giriḥ  |
malā hyete ca vijñeyā doṣāḥ pāradasaṃsthitāḥ  || 1.17 ||
jāḍyaṃ kuṣṭhaṃ mahādāhaṃ vīryanāśaṃ ca mūrcchanām  |
mṛtyuṃ sphoṭaṃ rogapuñjaṃ kurvantyete kramānnṛṇām  || 1.18 ||
<pāradaśodhana>
athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi pāradasya ca śodhanam  |
raso grāhyaḥ sunakṣatre palānāṃ śatamātrakam  || 1.19 ||
pañcāśatpañcaviṃśadvā daśa pañcaikameva vā  |
palādūnaṃ na kartavyaṃ rasasaṃskāramuttamam  || 1.20 ||
<pāradaśodhana (1)>
palatrayaṃ citrakasarṣapāṇāṃ kumārīkanyābṛhatīkaṣāyaiḥ  |
dinatrayaṃ marditasūtakastu vimucyate pañcamalādidoṣaiḥ  || 1.21 ||
<pāradaśodhana (2)>
iṣṭikārajanīcūrṇaiḥ ṣoḍaśāṃśaṃ rasasya ca  |
mardayettaṃ tathā khalve jambīrotthadravairdinam  || 1.22 ||
<sāmānyadoṣaśodhanam>
kāñjikaiḥ kṣālayetsūtaṃ nāgadoṣaṃ vimuñcati  |
viśālāṅkolacūrṇena vaṅgadoṣaṃ vimuñcati  || 1.23 ||
rājavṛkṣo malaṃ hanti pāvako hanti pāvakam  |
cāpalyaṃ kṛṣṇadhattūrastriphalā viṣanāśinī  || 1.24 ||
kaṭutrayaṃ giriṃ hanti asahyatvaṃ trikaṇṭakaḥ  |
pratidoṣaṃ palāṃśena tatra sūtaṃ sakāñjikam  || 1.25 ||
sarvadoṣavinirmuktaḥ saptakañcukavarjitaḥ  |
jāyate śuddhasūto'yaṃ yojayetsarvakarmasu  || 1.26 ||
<pāradaśodhanam (2)>
suvastragālitaṃ khalve sūtaṃ kṣiptvā vimardayet |
uddhṛtya cāranālena mṛdbhāṇḍe kṣālayetsudhīḥ  || 1.27 ||
rasasya daśamāṃśaṃ tu gandhaṃ dattvā vimardayet |
jambīrotthadravairyāmaṃ pātyaṃ pātanayantrake  || 1.28 ||
punarmardyaṃ punaḥ pātyaṃ saptavāraṃ viśuddhaye  |
yuktaṃ sarvasya sūtasya taptakhalve vimardanam  || 1.29 ||
<taptakhalva>
ajāśakṛttuṣāgniṃ tu bhūgarbhe tritayaṃ kṣipet |
tasyoparisthitaṃ khalvaṃ taptakhalvamidaṃ smṛtam  || 1.30 ||
<pāradaśodhanam>
kumāryāśca niśācūrṇairdinaṃ sūtaṃ vimardayet |
pātayetpātanāyantre samyakśuddho bhavedrasaḥ  || 1.31 ||
<pāradaśodhana>
śrīkhaṇḍadevadāruśca kākatuṇḍī jayādravaiḥ  |
karkoṭīmusalīkanyādravaṃ dattvā vimardayet || 1.32 ||
dinaikaṃ mardayetpaścācchuddhaṃ ca viniyojayet |
<pāradaśodhana>
athavā hiṅgulātsūtaṃ grāhayettannigadyate  || 1.33 ||
jambīranimbunīreṇa marditaṃ hiṅgulaṃ dinam  |
ūrdhvapātanayantreṇa grāhyaḥ syānnirmalo rasaḥ  || 1.34 ||
kañcukairnāgavaṅgādyairnirmukto rasakarmaṇi  |
vinā karmāṣṭakenaiva sūto'yaṃ sarvakarmakṛt || 1.35 ||
sarvasiddhamatametadīritaṃ sūtaśuddhikaramadbhutaṃ param  |
alpakarmavidhibhūrisiddhidaṃ dehalohakaraṇe hi śasyate  || 1.36 ||
saṃskārahīnaṃ khalu sūtarājaṃ yaḥ sevate tasya karoti bādhām  |
dehasya nāśaṃ vividhaṃ ca kuṣṭhaṃ kaṣṭaṃ ca rogāñjanayennarāṇām  || 1.37 ||

athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi rasajāraṇamuttamam  |
athājīrṇamabījaṃ ca sūtakaṃ yastu ghātayet || 2.1 ||
brahmahā sa durācārī mama drohī maheśvari  |
tasmātsarvaprayatnena jāritaṃ mārayedrasam  || 2.2 ||
<pāradabalijāraṇaṃ>
prakṣipya toyaṃ mṛtkuṇḍe tasyopari śarāvakam  |
saṃcūrṇamekhalāyuktaṃ sthāpayettasya cāntare  || 2.3 ||
rasaṃ kṣiptvā gandhakasya rajastasyopari kṣipet |
samaṃ bhāgaṃ tato dadyāccharāveṇa pidhāpayet || 2.4 ||
laghvīyasīṃ bhasmamudrāṃ tataḥ kuryādbhiṣagvaraḥ  |
āraṇyopalakaiḥ samyakcaturbhiḥ puṭamācaret || 2.5 ||
evaṃ punaḥ punargandhaṃ dattvā dattvā bhiṣagvaraḥ  |
samyakkurvīta sūtasya devi ṣaḍguṇajāraṇam  || 2.6 ||
<pāradasuvarṇajāraṇaṃ>
rasaḥ syātpaṭuśigrututthaiḥ sarājikairvyoṣaṇakaistrirātram  |
piṣṭastataḥ svinnatanu suvarṇamukhyānayaṃ khādati sarvadhātūn  || 2.7 ||
<pāradajāraṇa>
athavā biḍayogena śikhipittena lepitam  |
caretsuvarṇaṃ rasarāṭtaptakhalve yathāsukham  || 2.8 ||
nirdagdhaśaṃkhacūrṇaṃ ca ravikṣīreṇa saṃplutam  |
puṭitaṃ śataśo devi praśastaṃ jāraṇaṃ viduḥ  || 2.9 ||
svarṇābhrasarvalohāni yatheṣṭāni ca jārayet |
anekavidhinā sūtaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭyaṃśakādinā  |
dvātriṃśatṣoḍaśāṃśena jārayetkanakaṃ budhaḥ  || 2.10 ||
<rasamāraṇaṃ>
dvipalaṃ śuddhasūtaṃ ca sūtārdhaṃ śuddhagandhakam  |
kanyānīreṇa saṃmardya dinamekaṃ nirantaram  || 2.11 ||
ruddhvā tadbhūdhare yantre dinaikaṃ mārayetpuṭān  |
<rasamāraṇa (2)>
bhujaṅgavallīnīreṇa marditaṃ pāradaṃ dṛḍham  || 2.12 ||
karkoṭīkandamṛnmūṣāsampuṭasthaṃ puṭe gaje  |
bhasma tadyogavāhi syātsarvakarmasu yojayet || 2.13 ||
<rasamāraṇa (3)>
śvetāṅkolajaṭāvāri mardyaḥ sūto dinatrayam  |
puṭitaścāndhamūṣāyāṃ sūto bhasmatvamāpnuyāt || 2.14 ||
pratyahaṃ raktikāpañca bhakṣayenmadhu sarpiṣā  |
ko vā tasya guṇān vaktuṃ bhuvi śaknoti mānavaḥ  || 2.15 ||
<rasasindūranirmāṇa (1)>
palamatra rasaṃ śuddhaṃ tāvanmātraṃ sugandhakam  |
vidhivatkajjalīṃ kṛtvā nyagrodhāṅkuravāribhiḥ  || 2.16 ||
bhāvanātritayaṃ dattvā sthālīmadhye nidhāpayet |
viracya kavacīyantraṃ vālukābhiḥ prapūrayet || 2.17 ||
jāyate rasasindūraṃ taruṇāruṇasannibham  |
anupānaviśeṣeṇa karoti vividhān guṇān  || 2.18 ||
<rasasindūra (2)>
gandhakena samaḥ sūto nirguṇḍīrasamarditaḥ  |
pācito vālukāyantre raktaṃ bhasma prajāyate  || 2.19 ||
<rasasindūra>
sūtārddhaṃ gandhakaṃ śuddhaṃ mākṣikodbhūtasattvakam  |
gandhatulyaṃ vimardyātha dinaṃ nirguṇḍikādravaiḥ  || 2.20 ||
sthāpayedvālukāyantre kācakūpyāṃ vipācayet |
andhamūṣāgataṃ vātha vālukāyantrake dinam  || 2.21 ||
pakvaṃ saṃjāyate bhasma dāḍimīkusumopamam  |
<rasasindūra>
pṛthaksamaṃ samaṃ kṛtvā pāradaṃ gandhakaṃ tathā  || 2.22 ||
navasāraṃ dhūmasāraṃ sphaṭikīṃ yāmamātrake  |
nimbunīreṇa saṃmardya kācakupyāṃ vipācayet || 2.23 ||
mukhe pāṣāṇavaṭikāṃ dattvā mudrāṃ pralepayet |
saptabhirmṛttikāvastraiḥ pṛthaksaṃśoṣya veṣṭayet || 2.24 ||
sacchidrāyāṃ mṛdaḥ sthālyāṃ kūpikāṃ saṃniveśayet |
pūrayetsikatāpurairā galaṃ matimān bhiṣak || 2.25 ||
niveśya cullyāṃ dahanaṃ mandamadhyakharaṃ kramāt |
prajvālya dvādaśaṃ yāmaṃ svāṅgaśītalamuddharet || 2.26 ||
sphoṭayitvā punaḥ sthālīmūrdhvagaṃ gandhakaṃ tyajet |
adhasthaṃ rasasindūraṃ sarvakarmasu yojayet || 2.27 ||

sphoṭayetsvāṅgaśītaṃ tamūrdhvalagnaṃ tu taṃ tyajet |

<rasasindūra>

<rasasindūra>

<rasakarpūra>
ṭaṅkaṇaṃ madhu lākṣātha ūrṇā guñjāyuto rasaḥ  |
mardayedbhṛṅgajairdrāvairdinaikaṃ vā dhametpunaḥ  || 2.38 ||
dhmāto bhasmatvamāyāti śuddhaḥ karpūrasannibhaḥ  |
<rasakarpūra>
khaṭīṣṭigairikāvalmīmṛttikā saindhavaṃ samam  || 2.39 ||
bhāgadvayamito gandho rasabhāgadvayaṃ smṛtam  |

dagdhvātha haṇḍikāṃ dattvā dviraṣṭapraharaṃ pacet |

 3
<uparasāḥ>
gandhakaṃ vajravaikrāntaṃ gaganaṃ tālakaṃ śilām  |
kharparaṃ śikhitutthaṃ ca vimalā hemamākṣikam  || 3.1 ||
kāsīsaṃ kāntapāṣāṇaṃ varāṭāñjanahiṅgulam  |
kaṅkuṣṭhaṃ śaṃkhabhūnāgaṃ ṭaṅkaṇaṃ tu śilājatu  || 3.2 ||
ete uparasāḥ proktāḥ śodhyā drāvyāśca mārayet |

udumbaraśiphākvāthe pītaṃ garbhaṃ surakṣati  || 9.66 ||
mātuluṅgasya mūlāni madhukaṃ madhusaṃyutam  |
ghṛtena saha pātavyaṃ sukhaṃ nārī prasūyate  || 9.67 ||
tuṣāmbuparighṛṣṭena kandena parilepayet |
lāṅgalyāścaraṇau sūtiṃ kṣipramāpnoti garbhiṇī  || 9.68 ||
yāsāṃ puṣpāgamo nāsti ṛtukāle ca yoṣitām  |
tāsāṃ kuryāccikitseyaṃ punaḥ puṣpāgamo bhavet || 9.69 ||
pippalīṃ ca yavakṣāraṃ viḍaṅgaṃ manmathaphalam  |
tiktaṃ tu tumbinībījaṃ guṭikāṃ kārayedbhiṣak || 9.70 ||
muṇḍī ca kṣīrasaṃyuktā yonidvāre'ṅganā śubhā  |
tripañcasaptarātreṇa puṣpaṃ bhavati nānyathā  || 9.71 ||
bālatantraṃ pravakṣyāmi samāsādrāvaṇoditam  |
pūjādravyaṃ diśo bhāgaṃ mantraṃ tadgrāhyalakṣaṇam  || 9.72 ||
prathame divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
mandānāmnī samākhyātā yogiṇī tasya lakṣaṇam  || 9.73 ||
dvitīye divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
sunandā yoginī nāma prathamaṃ jāyate jvaraḥ  || 9.74 ||
saṃkoco hastapādānāmakṣirogo'tichardanam  |
sabhayatvaṃ kṛśatvaṃ ca tadgrasta iti lakṣaṇam  || 9.75 ||
tṛtīye divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
pūtanā yoginī nāma gātrabhaṅgo jvaro'ruciḥ  || 9.76 ||
pralāpaṃ kandharāśothacchardirityādilakṣaṇam  |
aparāhne ca vāruṇyāṃ pañcarātraṃ baliṃ kṣipet || 9.77 ||
caturthe divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
biḍālī nāma tadgraste cakṣuḥśūlaṃ jvaro'ruciḥ  || 9.78 ||
gātramāṭenamityādi vivarṇena baliṃ kṣipet |
pañcame divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  || 9.79 ||
nartakīti samākhyātā yoginī tasya lakṣaṇam  |
atha ṣaṣṭhadine māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  || 9.80 ||
yoginī śakunī nāma kāsaśvāso'rucirjvaraḥ  |
hastapādādisaṃkocaścakṣuḥpīḍeti lakṣaṇam  || 9.81 ||
pañcarātraṃ baliṃ tasyai vāruṇyāṃ diśi nikṣipet |
saptame divase māse varṣe śuṣkāśivā śiśum  || 9.82 ||
gṛhṇāti rodanaṃ kampo jvaraśoṣādilakṣaṇam  |
paścimāyāṃ diśi pañca balau datte śiśuḥ sukhī  || 9.83 ||
aṣṭame divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti jṛmbhakā  |
tayā gṛhītamātrasya prathamaṃ jāyate jvaraḥ  || 9.84 ||
śiraḥpīḍākṣirogaśca cakṣurutpāṭaceṣṭitam  |
dakṣiṇāṃ diśimāśritya baliṃ tasyai pradāpayet || 9.85 ||
navame divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
acintā yoginī nāma gātrabhaṅgaḥ śiro'kṣiruk || 9.86 ||
chardiḥ pralāpa ityādi tadgṛhītasya lakṣaṇam  |
uttarāṃ diśimāśritya baliṃ tasyai pradāpayet || 9.87 ||
daśame divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
nāmnā kāpālikā khyātā yoginī tasya lakṣaṇam  || 9.88 ||
rodanaṃ kampanaṃ chardirjvaro durbalatākṣiruk |
pūrvāṃ diśaṃ samāśritya pañcarātraṃ baliṃ kṣipet || 9.89 ||
ekādaśe dine māse varṣe gṛhṇāti lipsitā  |
gātrakampo jvarastīvrastadgṛhītasya lakṣaṇam  || 9.90 ||
bhānusaṃkhye dine māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
pītalī yoginī nāma rodanaṃ vedanā jvaraḥ  || 9.91 ||
nidrā kṣīṇasvaraḥ pīto vamanāhāraśūnyatā  |
uttarāṃ diśimāśritya saptarātraṃ baliṃ kṣipet || 9.92 ||
kāmasaṃkhye dine māse varṣe gṛhṇati bālakam  |
bhadrakālī jvaro nāma vāmahastasya kampam  || 9.93 ||
vedanāruciniḥśvāsāḥ kāyaḥ pīto viceṣṭitam  |
pūrvāṃ diśaḥ samāśritya baliṃ tasyai pradāpayet || 9.94 ||
purandaradine māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  |
tārā hi yoginī nāma jvaraḥ śoṣo'rucirbhṛśam  || 9.95 ||
cakṣuḥpīḍeṅgitaṃ tasyai paścime balimāharet |
pakṣe ca divase māse varṣe gṛhṇāti bālakam  || 9.96 ||
yoginī śarvarī nāma śvāsaḥ kāso'rucirjvaraḥ  |
tadgraste cihnamityādi dakṣiṇasyāṃ baliṃ kṣipet || 9.97 ||
vikāradivase māse varṣe gṛhṇati yoginī  |
kumārī nayanodvego jvaraśoṣādiceṣṭitam  || 9.98 ||
nairṛtīṃ diśimāśritya saptarātraṃ baliṃ kṣipet |
bālaṃ ca snapayetpaścācchāntitoyena mantravit || 9.99 ||
nadītīradvayākṛṣṭamṛdā devīsvarūpakam  |
kṛtvā pūjā ca kartavyā bhūpapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ  || 9.100 ||
vaṭakā laḍḍukāpūpā agrabhaktaṃ guḍaṃ dadhi  |
cāturvarṇyapatākāśca pradīptāḥ puṣpacandanam  || 9.101 ||
pūjayetsarvarogāṇāmaparāhne yathābali  |
sarvatra nāmabhedena balidānaṃ prajāyate  || 9.102 ||


 10
atha kālasya vijñānaṃ pravakṣyāmi yathāsukham  |
jīvitaṃ maraṇaṃ yogī yato jānāti niścayāt || 10.1 ||
kālagrahasya yasyedaṃ daṃṣṭrāsampuṭake jagat |
adyaiva vā prabhāte vā so'vaśyaṃ bhaviṣyati  || 10.2 ||
rasaṃ rasāyanaṃ yogaṃ kālaṃ jñātvā samācaret |
yasmājjñānaṃ vinā vyarthaṃ tattasmātprocyate'dhunā  || 10.3 ||
dūto raktakaṣāyakṛṣṇavasano dantī jarāmarditastailābhyaktaśarīrakāyudhakaro dīnāśrupūrṇānanaḥ  |
bhasmāṅgārakapālapāṃśumuśalaḥ sūryāstasūryodaye yaḥ sūryasvarasaṃsthito gadavatāṃ kālāya sa syādasau  || 10.4 ||
akasmāccittavikṛtirakasmātpuruṣottamaḥ  |
akasmādindriyotpattiḥ sannipātasya lakṣaṇam  || 10.5 ||
śarīraṃ śītalaṃ yasya prakṛtervikṛtirbhavet |
tatrāriṣṭaṃ samāsena vyāptaṃ tatra nibodha me  || 10.6 ||
duṣṭaśabdena ramate sādhuśabdena kupyati  |
yaścākasmānna śṛṇute taṃ gatāyuṣamādiśet || 10.7 ||
yo vā gandhaṃ na gṛhṇāti dīpe śānte ca mānavaḥ  |
divājyotīṃṣi yaścāpi jvalitāni ca paśyati  || 10.8 ||
candraṃ sūryaprabhaṃ paśyetsūryaṃ vā candravarcasam  |
taḍidvātoṣitānmeghānnirmale gagane carān  || 10.9 ||
vimānayānaprāsādairyaśca saṃkulamambaram  |
yaśca nīlaṃ mūrtimantamantarikṣaṃ ca paśyati  || 10.10 ||
yastūṣṇamiva gṛhṇāti śītamuṣṇaṃ ca śītavat |
saṃjātaḥ saṃśayo yasya taṃ vadanti gatāyuṣam  || 10.11 ||
viparītena gṛhṇāti bhāvānanyāṃśca yo naraḥ  |
dhūmanīhāravāsobhirāvṛtāmiva medinīm  || 10.12 ||
pradīptamiva lokaṃ ca yo'valuptamivāmbhasā  |
bhūmimaṣṭādaśākārāṃ lekhābhiryastu paśyati  || 10.13 ||
jyotsnādarśe hi toyeṣu chāyāṃ yaśca na paśyati  |
paśyatyekāṅgahīnāṃ ca vaikṛtaṃ cāpi paśyati  || 10.14 ||
śvakākakaṅkagṛdhrāṇāṃ prayātaṃ yakṣarākṣasām  |
piśācoraganāgānāṃ vikṛtāmapi yo naraḥ  || 10.15 ||
hrīśriyau yasya naśyetāṃ teja ojaḥ smṛtistathā  |
akasmājjṛmbhate yaśca sa parāsurasaṃśayam  || 10.16 ||
yasyādharoṣṭhaḥ patati sthitaścordhvaṃ tathottaram  |
pāśaśaṅkā bhavedyasya durlabhaṃ tasya jīvitam  || 10.17 ||
kuṭilā sphuṭitā vāpi suptā yasya ca nāsikā  |
avasphūryati magnā vā sa parāsurasaṃśayam  || 10.18 ||
svarūpaṃ paranetreṣu puttikāṃ yo na paśyati  |
yadā hi paṭudṛṣṭiśca tadā mṛtyuradūrataḥ  || 10.19 ||
karṇahīnaṃ yadātmānaṃ paśyatyātmā kathaṃcana  |
na sa jīvati loke'smin kālena kavalīkṛtaḥ  || 10.20 ||
rātrau dāho bhavedyasya divā śītaṃ ca jāyate  |
kaphapūritakaṇṭhasya mṛtyuścaiva na saṃśayaḥ  || 10.21 ||
caraṇau śītalau yasya śītalaṃ nābhimaṇḍalam  |
śirastāpo bhavedyasya tasya mṛtyurna saṃśayaḥ  || 10.22 ||
huṅkāraḥ śītalo yasya phutkāro vahṇisannibhaḥ  |
sadā dāho bhavedyasya tasya mṛtyurna saṃśayaḥ  || 10.23 ||
arundhatīṃ dhruvaṃ caiva viṣṇostrīṇi padāni ca  |
hīnāyudho na paśyanti caturthaṃ mātṛmaṇḍalam  || 10.24 ||
araśmibimbaṃ sūryasya vahneścaivāṃśuvarjitam  |
dṛṣṭvaikādaśamāsāstu naraścordhvaṃ na jīvati  || 10.25 ||
vātaṃ mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ yaḥ suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ tathā  |
pratyakṣamathavā svapne daśamāsaṃ na jīvati  || 10.26 ||
kvacitpaśyati yo dīptaṃ svarṇavatkānanaṃ naraḥ  |
virūpāṇi ca bhūtāni navamāsaṃ na jīvati  || 10.27 ||
sthūlāṅgo'pi kṛśaḥ kṛśo'pi sahasā sthūlatvamālambate śyāmo vā kanakaprabho yadi bhavedgauro'pi kṛṣṇacchaviḥ  |
dhīro dhīratayārthadharmanipuṇaḥ śāntopakārī pumānityevaṃ prakṛte tu śāntacalanaṃ māsyaṣṭame mṛtyudam  || 10.28 ||
pīḍā bhavetpāṇitale ca jihvāmūlaṃ samūlaṃ rudhiraṃ ca kṛṣṇam  |
vṛddhiṃ naraḥ kāmapi yanna dṛṣṭvā jīvenmanuṣyaḥ sa hi saptamāsān  || 10.29 ||
madhyāṅgulīnāṃ tritayaṃ viraktaṃ rogaṃ vinā śuṣyati yasya kaṇṭhaḥ  |
muhurmuhuḥ prasravaṇaṃ ca jāḍyaṃ ṣaṣṭhe ca māse pralayaṃ prayāti  || 10.30 ||
yasya na sphuraṇaṃ kiṃcidvidyate yasya karmaṇi  |
so'vaśyaṃ pañcame māsi skandhārūḍho gamiṣyati  || 10.31 ||
yasya na sphurate jyotiḥ pīḍite nayanadvaye  |
maraṇaṃ tasya nirdiṣṭaṃ caturthe māsi niścitam  || 10.32 ||
spandate vṛṣaṇo yasya na kiṃcidapi pīḍitaḥ  |
tṛtīye māsi so'vaśyaṃ yamaloke gamiṣyati  || 10.33 ||
tārā divā candraprabhaṃ niśānte yo vidyutaṃ paśyati caiva śvabhre  |
indrāyudhaṃ vā svayameva rātrau māsadvaye tasya vadanti nāśam  || 10.34 ||
yasya jānugataṃ marma na kiṃcidapi ceṣṭitam  |
māsānte maraṇaṃ tasya na kenāpi vilambyate  || 10.35 ||
kaniṣṭhāṅguliparva syātkṛṣṇaṃ ca madhyamaṃ yadā  |
gatāyuḥ procyate puṃsāmaṣṭādaśadināvadhiḥ  || 10.36 ||
ghṛte taile jale vāpi darpaṇe yasya dṛśyate  |
śirorahitamātmānaṃ pakṣamekaṃ sa jīvati  || 10.37 ||
śaityaṃ dadhyannapānāni yasya tāpakarāṇi ca  |
śītaraśmi bhaveccāruhāsaṃ cātha sunirmalam  || 10.38 ||
na vetti vai cāruhitaṃ na coṣṇaṃ vetti yo naraḥ  |
kālajñānena samproktaṃ pakṣamekaṃ sa jīvati  || 10.39 ||
snātamātrasya yasyaite trayaḥ śuṣyanti tatkṣaṇāt |
hṛdayaṃ hastapādau ca daśarātraṃ sa jīvati  || 10.40 ||
nāsāgraṃ rasanāgraṃ ca cakṣuścaivauṣṭhasampuṭam  |
yo na paśyetpurādṛṣṭaṃ saptarātraṃ sa jīvati  || 10.41 ||
dhārā bindusamā yasya patate ca mahītale  |
saptāhājjāyate mṛtyuḥ kālajñānena kathyate  || 10.42 ||
athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi chāyāpuruṣalakṣaṇam  |
yasya vijñānamātreṇa trikālajño bhavennaraḥ  || 10.43 ||
kālo dūrasthito'syāpi yenopāyena lakṣyate  |
taṃ vadanti samāsena yathoddiṣṭaṃ śivāgame  || 10.44 ||
ekānte vijane gatvā kṛtvādityaṃ svapṛṣṭhataḥ  |
saṃnirīkṣya nijacchāyāṃ kaṇṭhadeśasamāhitām  || 10.45 ||
tataścākāśamīkṣeta tataḥ paśyati śaṃkaram  |
aṣṭottaraśataṃ japtvā tato vai dṛśyate śubham  || 10.46 ||
śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaṃ nānārūpadharo haram  |
ṣaṇmāsābhyāsayogena bhūcarāṇāṃ patirbhavet || 10.47 ||
varṣadvayena he nātha kartā hartā svayaṃ prabhuḥ  |
trikālajñatvamāpnoti sa yogī nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 10.48 ||
yatkṛtābhyāsayogena nāsti kiṃcitsudurlabham  |
tadrūpaṃ kṛṣṇavarṇaṃ ca paśyati vyomni nirmale  || 10.49 ||
ṣaṇmāsānmṛtyumāpnoti sa yogī nātra saṃśayaḥ  |
pīto vyādhibhayaṃ rakto nīlo hatyāṃ vinirdiśet || 10.50 ||
nānāvarṇe svarūpe'sminudvego jāyate mahān  |
pādau gulphaṃ ca jaṭharaṃ vināśakṛśatā bhavet || 10.51 ||
ardhavarṣeṇa varṣe vā jīvanvarṣadvayena na vā  |
vināśo dakṣiṇe bāhau svabandhurmriyate dhruvam  || 10.52 ||
vāmabāhau tathā bhāryā vinaśyati na saṃśayaḥ  |
śiro dakṣiṇabāhubhyāṃ vināśo mṛtyumādiśet || 10.53 ||
aśiro māsamaraṇaṃ vinā jaṅghe divā nava  |
aṣṭabhiḥ skandhanāśena chāyāluptena tatkṣaṇāt || 10.54 ||
tīrthasnānena dānena tapasā sukṛtena vā  |
japena jñānayogena jāyate kālabandhanam  || 10.55 ||
rasāyanaṃ ca pūrvoktaṃ guṭikā mṛtajīvanī  |
naraiḥ sevyā yathoktaṃ ca paraṃ kālasya bandhanam  || 10.56 ||
rakṣaṇīyamato dehaṃ yato dharmādisādhanam  |
śarīraṃ nāśayantyete doṣā dhātumalāśrayāḥ  || 10.57 ||
vaidyanāthatanūjena śālināthena dhīmatā  |
śāstramālokya cākṛṣya racitā rasamañjarī  || 10.58 ||
