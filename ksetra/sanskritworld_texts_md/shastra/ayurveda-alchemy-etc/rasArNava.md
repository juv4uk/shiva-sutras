prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ
yasmin sarvaṃ yataḥ sarvaṃ yaḥ sarvaṃ sarvataśca yaḥ  |
yaśca sarvamayo nityaṃ tasmai sarvātmane namaḥ  || 1.1 ||
kailāsaśikhare ramye nānāratnavibhūṣite  |
nānādrumalatākīrṇe guptasambandhavarjite  || 1.2 ||
devadevaṃ sukhāsīnaṃ nīlakaṇṭhaṃ trilocanam  |
praṇamya śirasā devī pārvatī paripṛcchati  || 1.3 ||
śrīdevyuvāca
devadeva mahādeva kāla kāmāṅgadāhaka  |
kulakaulamahākaula- siddhakaulādināśana  || 1.4 ||
tvatprasādācchrutaṃ sarvamaśeṣamavadhāritam  |
yadi te'hamanugrāhyā yadyahaṃ tava vallabhā  || 1.5 ||
sūcitā sarvatantreṣu yā punarna prakāśitā  |
jīvanmuktiriyaṃ nātha kīdṛśī vaktumarhasi  || 1.6 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
sādhu sādhu mahābhāge sādhu parvatanandini  |
sādhu pṛṣṭaṃ tvayā devi bhaktānāṃ hitakāmyayā  || 1.7 ||
ajarāmaradehasya śivatādātmyavedanam  |
jīvanmuktirmahādevi devānāmapi durlabhā  || 1.8 ||
piṇḍapāte ca yo mokṣaḥ sa ca mokṣo nirarthakaḥ  |
piṇḍe tu patite devi gardabho'pi vimucyate  || 1.9 ||
yadi muktirbhagakṣobhe kiṃ na muñcanti gardabhāḥ  |
ajāśca vṛṣabhāścaiva kiṃna muktā gaṇāmbike  || 1.10 ||
tasmātsaṃrakṣayetpiṇḍaṃ rasaiścaiva rasāyanaiḥ  |
śukramūtrapurīṣāṇāṃ yadi muktirniṣevaṇāt || 1.11 ||
kiṃna muktā mahādevi śvānaśūkarajātayaḥ  |
ṣaḍdarśane'pi muktistu darśitā piṇḍapātane  || 1.12 ||
karāmalakavatsāpi pratyakṣaṃ nopalabhyate  |
akathyamapi deveśi sadbhāvaṃ kathayāmi te  || 1.13 ||
śūnyapāpo mantrayājī na piṇḍaṃ dhārayetkvacit |
devānāmapi deveśi durlabhaṃ piṇḍadhāraṇam  || 1.14 ||
kiṃ punarmānuṣāṇāṃ tu dharaṇītalavāsinām  |
dharme naṣṭe kuto dharmaḥ dharme naṣṭe kutaḥ kriyā  || 1.15 ||
kriyānaṣṭe kuto yogaḥ yoge naṣṭe kuto gatiḥ  |
gatinaṣṭe kuto mokṣo mokṣe naṣṭe na kiṃcana  || 1.16 ||
śrīdevyuvāca
jīvanmuktisvarūpaṃ tu devadeva śrutaṃ mayā  |
tatpratyupāyaṃ me brūhi yadyasti karuṇā mayi  || 1.17 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
karmayogena deveśi prāpyate piṇḍadhāraṇam  |
rasaśca pavanaśceti karmayogo dvidhā mataḥ  || 1.18 ||
mūrchito harati vyādhiṃ mṛto jīvayati svayam  |
baddhaḥ khecaratāṃ kuryātraso vāyuśca bhairavi  || 1.19 ||
jñānānmokṣaḥ sureśāni jñānaṃ pavanadhāraṇāt |
tatra devi sthiraṃ piṇḍaṃ yatra sthairye rasaḥ prabhuḥ  || 1.20 ||
acirājjāyate devi śarīramajarāmaram  |
manasaśca yathā dhyānaṃ rasayogādavāpyate  || 1.21 ||
satyaṃ sa labhate devi jñānaṃ vijñānapūrvakam  |
tasya mantrāśca sidhyanti yo'śnāti mṛtasūtakam  || 1.22 ||
yāvanna śaktipātastu na yāvatpāśakṛntanam  |
tāvattasya kuto buddhiḥ jāyate mṛtasūtake  || 1.23 ||
madyamāṃsaratā nityaṃ bhagaliṅgeṣu ye ratāḥ  |
teṣāṃ vinaṣṭabuddhīnāṃ rasajñānaṃ sudurlabham  || 1.24 ||
kulaśāsanahīnānāṃ saddarśanamakāṅkṣiṇām  |
na sidhyati raso devi pibanti mṛgatṛṣṇikām  || 1.25 ||
gomāṃsaṃ bhakṣayedyastu pibedamaravāruṇīm  |
kulīnaṃ tamahaṃ manye rasajñamapare'dhamāḥ  || 1.26 ||
na garbhaḥ sampradāyārthe raso garbho vidhīyate  |
tenāyaṃ labhate siddhiṃ na siddhiḥ sūtakaṃ vinā  || 1.27 ||
yāvanna harabījaṃ tu bhakṣayetpāradaṃ rasam  |
tāvattasya kuto muktiḥ kutaḥ piṇḍasya dhāraṇam  || 1.28 ||
madyamāṃsaratāprajñā mohitāḥ śivamāyayā  |
jalpanti ca vayaṃ muktā yāsyāmaḥ śivamandiram  || 1.29 ||
piṇḍadhāraṇayoge ca nispṛhā mandabuddhayaḥ  |
khaṇḍajñānena deveśi rañjitaṃ sacarācaram  || 1.30 ||
svadehe khecaratvaṃ ca śivatvaṃ yena labhyate  |
tādṛśe tu rasajñāne nityābhyāsaṃ kuru priye  || 1.31 ||
śrīdevyuvāca
avatāraṃ rasendrasya māhātmyaṃ tu sureśvara  |
śrotumicchāmi deveśa vaktumarhasi tattvataḥ  || 1.32 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
sādhu pṛṣṭaṃ mahābhāge guhyādguhyataraṃ tvayā  |
anugrahakaraṃ dhyānaṃ lokānāmupakārakam  || 1.33 ||
tvaṃ mātā sarvabhūtānāṃ pitā cāhaṃ sanātanaḥ  |
dvayośca yo raso devi mahāmaithunasambhavaḥ  || 1.34 ||
svairataḥ sambhavāddevi pāradaḥ kīrtito mahaḥ  |
pārado gadito yaśca parārthaṃ sādhakottamaiḥ  || 1.35 ||
sūto'yaṃ matsamo devi mama pratyaṅgasambhavaḥ  |
mama deharaso yasmātrasastenāyamucyate  || 1.36 ||
darśanātsparśanāttasya bhakṣaṇātsmaraṇādapi  |
pūjanācca pradānācca dṛśyate ṣaḍvidhaṃ phalam  || 1.37 ||
kedārādīni liṅgāni pṛthivyā yāni kāni ca  |
tāni dṛṣṭvā tu yatpuṇyaṃ tatpuṇyaṃ rasadarśanāt || 1.38 ||
candanāgurukarpūra- kuṅkumāntargato rasaḥ  |
mūrchitaḥ śivapūjāyāṃ śivasāṃnidhyasiddhaye  || 1.39 ||
bhakṣaṇātparameśāni hanti pāpatrayaṃ rasaḥ  |
tathā tāpatrayaṃ hanti rogān doṣatrayodbhavān  || 1.40 ||
durlabhaṃ brahmaniṣṇātaiḥ prāpyate paramaṃ padam  |
hṛdvyomakarṇikāntaḥstha- rasendrasya maheśvari  || 1.41 ||
smaraṇānmucyate pāpaiḥ sadyo janmāntarārjitaiḥ  || 1.42 ||
svayaṃbhūliṅgasāhasraiḥ yatphalaṃ samyagarcanāt |
tatphalaṃ koṭiguṇitaṃ rasaliṅgārcanādbhavet || 1.43 ||
adhamaḥ khagavādastu vilavādastu madhyamaḥ  |
uttamo mantravādastu rasavādo mahottamaḥ  || 1.44 ||
mantratantraparijñāne rasayogasya dūṣakāḥ  |
prayānti narakaṃ sarve chittvā sukṛtasaṃcayam  || 1.45 ||
rasavidyā parā vidyā trailokye'pi sudurlabhā  |
bhuktimuktikarī yasmāttasmāddeyā guṇānvitaiḥ  || 1.46 ||
astīti bhāṣate kaścitkaścinnāstīti bhāṣate  |
āstike tu bhavetsiddhiḥ tasya sidhyati bhūtale  || 1.47 ||
nāstikenānubhāvena nāsti nāstīti yo vadet |
tasya nāsti priye siddhirjanmakoṭiśatairapi  || 1.48 ||
brahmajñānena mukto'sau pāpī yo rasanindakaḥ  |
nāhaṃ trātā bhave tasya janmakoṭiśatairapi  || 1.49 ||
śvāno'yaṃ jāyate devi yāvatjanmasahasrakam  |
trikoṭijanmalakṣāṇi mārjāro jāyate rasāt |
rāsabho lakṣajanmāni lakṣajanmāni vāyasaḥ  || 1.50 ||
kṛmiko lakṣajanmāni kukkuṭo janmalakṣakam  |
gṛdhrako lakṣajanmāni yaḥ pāpī rasanindakaḥ  || 1.51 ||
ālāpaṃ gātrasaṃsparśaṃ yaḥ kuryādrasanindakaiḥ  |
yāvajjanmasahasraṃ tu sa bhavedduḥkhapīḍitaḥ  || 1.52 ||
rasavīryavipāke ca sūtakastvamṛtopamaḥ  |
tena janmajarāvyādhīn harate sūtakaḥ priye  || 1.53 ||
gurumārādhayetpūrvaṃ viśuddhenāntarātmanā  |
sampradāyaṃ prayacchanti gurau tuṣṭe marīcayaḥ  || 1.54 ||
gurusevāṃ vinā karma yaḥ kuryānmūḍhacetanaḥ  |
sa yāti niṣphalaṃ karma svapnalabdhaṃ dhanaṃ yathā  || 1.55 ||
yaḥ karma kurute dṛṣṭaṃ tasya lābhaḥ pade pade  |
kārayedrasavādaṃ tu tuṣṭena guruṇā priye  || 1.56 ||
siddhyupāyopadeśo'yamubhayorbhogamokṣadaḥ  |
rasārṇavaṃ mahātantramidaṃ paramadurlabham  || 1.57 ||
gopyaṃ guruprasādena labdhaṃ syātphalasiddhaye  |
labdhvātra rasakarmāṇi nāhaṃkāraṃ samācaret || 1.58 ||
anujñātaśca guruṇā labdhvā cājñāṃ rasāṅkuśīm  |
bhairavīṃ tanumāśritya sādhayedrasabhairavam  || 1.59 ||
evamuktā rasotpattiḥ māhātmyaṃ ca sureśvari  |
tanmamācakṣva deveśi kimanyacchrotumicchasi  || 1.60 ||


ḍvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ [ḍīkṣāvidhāna]
śrīdevyuvāca
rasopadeśadātā ca kathaṃ syādvada me prabho  |
śiṣyaścaiva kathaṃ deva rasānuṣṭhānatatparaḥ  || 2.1 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
nispṛho nirahaṃkāro lobhamāyāvivarjitaḥ  |
kulamārgarato nityaṃ gurupūjārataśca yaḥ  || 2.2 ||
dāntaḥ śiṣyopadeśajñaḥ śaktimān gatamatsaraḥ  |
dharmajñaḥ satyavākdakṣaḥ śīlavān guṇavān śuciḥ  || 2.3 ||
anekarasaśāstrajño rasamaṇḍapakovidaḥ  |
rasadīkṣāvidhānajño yantrauṣadhimahārasān  || 2.4 ||
rāgasaṃkhyāṃ bījakalāṃ dvaṃdvamelāpanaṃ viḍam  |
rañjanaṃ sāraṇaṃ tailaṃ dalāni krāmaṇāni ca  || 2.5 ||
varṇotkarṣaṃ mṛdutvaṃ ca jāraṇāṃ bālabaddhayoḥ  |
khecarīṃ bhūcarīṃ caiva yo vetti sa gururmataḥ  || 2.6 ||
deśakālakriyābhijño dayādākṣiṇyasaṃyutaḥ  |
lobhamāyāvinirmukto mantrānuṣṭhānatatparaḥ  || 2.7 ||
sāmudralakṣaṇopeto gambhīro guruvatsalaḥ  |
devāgniyoginīcakra- kulapūjārataḥ sadā  |
śiṣyo vinītastantrajñaḥ satyavādī dṛḍhavrataḥ  || 2.8 ||
ye narāḥ kumbhakuddāla- dhvajaśaṅkhādilāñchitaiḥ  |
karairadhiṣṭhitā devi yojyāste nidhisādhane  || 2.9 ||
balavanto mahāsattvāḥ kṛṣṇaraktavilocanāḥ  |
vakraghoṇāḥ sadā krūrāḥ praśastā bilasādhane  || 2.10 ||
nirmāṃsamūrdhapiṇḍīkān raktakeśān gatālasān  |
kaṭhinānuṣṇapādāṃśca dhātuvāde niyojayet || 2.11 ||
ādau parīkṣayeddevi sādhakān susamāhitān  |
brāhmaṇān kṣatriyān vaiśyān śūdrāṃścānukrameṇa tu  || 2.12 ||
jitendriyāḥ kleśasahā nityodyamasamanvitāḥ  |
śūrāśca kṛtavidyāśca praśastāḥ sādhakāḥ priye  || 2.13 ||
rasadīkṣāvihīnā ye proktalakṣaṇavarjitāḥ  |
viplāvakāḥ pāparatāḥ varjayettān prayatnataḥ  || 2.14 ||
duścāriṇī durācārā niṣṭhurā kalahapriyā  |
bahvāśinī ca duścittā koṭarākṣī ca nirdayā  |
rasanindākarī yā ca tāṃ nārīṃ parivarjayet || 2.15 ||
īdṛśībhirvarārohe sampūrṇaṃ kṣitimaṇḍalam  |
na tādṛśī bhavennārī yādṛśī rasabandhakī  || 2.16 ||
kākiṇī kīkaṇī nārī tathaiva kāñcikācinī  |
kṛṣṇapakṣe ṛtumatī sā nārī kākiṇī smṛtā  || 2.17 ||
ubhayapakṣe ṛtumatī sā nārī kīkaṇī matā  || 2.18 ||
śuklapakṣe ṛtumatī sā nārī kāñcikācinī  || 2.19 ||
navayauvanasampannā surūpā cāruhāsinī  |
sūkṣmakeśā tu yā nārī kṣīrāhārapriyā sadā  || 2.20 ||
priyālāpakarī nityaṃ śivaśāstrakathāpriyā  |
padmākāraṃ mukhaṃ yasyā dṛṣṭirindīvarākṛtiḥ  || 2.21 ||
daśanā vajrasadṛśāḥ pravālasadṛśo'dharaḥ  |
yasyāḥ payodharau devi tuṅgapīnau samāvubhau  || 2.22 ||
aśvatthapattrasadṛśī yonī yasyā bhagaḥ samaḥ  |
yatpādau māṃsalau snigdhau vartulāvartaromakau  || 2.23 ||
śyāmā ca madhye kṣāmā ca tanvī bhaktiparā śive  |
padminī sā tu vijñeyā prasannā mṛgalocanā  || 2.24 ||
pūrṇimāyāmamāyāṃ vā pakṣe pakṣe rajasvalā  |
ṣaḍbhedā kākiṇī nāmnā pūrvaproktā rasapradā  || 2.25 ||
yasya tuṣṭo mahādevastasya siddho rasāyane  |
tayaiva devadeveśi rasakarmāṇi kārayet || 2.26 ||
tasya hi nirmalā buddhirniścitā rasasādhane  |
tasmādguruśca śiṣyaśca pūrvoktaḥ siddhibhāgbhavet || 2.27 ||
śrīdevyuvāca
īdṛśairlakṣaṇaiḥ nārīṃ kutaḥ prāpnoti sādhakaḥ  |
yatra rūpaṃ mahādeva lakṣaṇaṃ nātra vidyate  || 2.28 ||
lakṣaṇaṃ vidyate yatra bhāvanā nātra vidyate  |
athavā rūpahīnāyā rūpaṃ kena pravartate  || 2.29 ||
lakṣaṇatritayaṃ kutra tvayā dṛṣṭaṃ maheśvara  |
tadetajjāyate yena tamupāyaṃ vada prabho  || 2.30 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
śṛṇu devi paraṃ guhyaṃ yatsurairapi durlabham  |
pituḥ sadāśivājjātaṃ janma yaccaṇḍikodare  || 2.31 ||
tāsāṃ buddhirbhaveddevi nirmalā rasasādhane  |
dāpayettvaritāmantraṃ japettaṃ darpavarjitā  || 2.32 ||
lakṣamantraṃ japedyā tu jāyate sā sulakṣaṇā  |
baddhvā tu khecarīṃ mudrāṃ japetpheṭkārabhairavīm  || 2.33 ||
mameyaṃ caṇḍikā mātā janma ca tripurāntakāt |
kālikāhaṃ samudbhūtā- smīdṛśaṃ saṃsmarettu sā  || 2.34 ||
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca trailokye kathayettu sā  |
sahāyāstādṛśāstasya yādṛśī bhavitavyatā  || 2.35 ||
śrīdevyuvāca
devadeva mahādeva samastajñānabhājana  |
rasadīkṣāṃ tu pṛcchāmi sādhakānāṃ hitāya vai  || 2.36 ||
śrībhairava uvāca
śṛṇu bhairavi yadbhāvamapūrvaṃ varṇayāmi te  |
rasadīkṣāvidhānaṃ tu tasmānnigaditaṃ śṛṇu  || 2.37 ||
yatra rājā nayaparo balavān dharmatatparaḥ  |
mantrī ca dharmatattvajño bhaktiśraddhāsamanvitaḥ  || 2.38 ||
tatredaṃ kārayetkarma rasabandhaṃ rasāyanam  |
janā māheśvarā yatra tatra sthāne tu kārayet || 2.39 ||
kārayedvijane sthāne paśuryatra na vidyate  |
sugupte suṣame sthāne sarvabādhāvivarjite  || 2.40 ||
prākāraparikhopete kapāṭārgalarakṣite  |
ekānte nirmale hṛdye nānāpuṣpadrumānvite  || 2.41 ||
haṃsakāraṇḍavākīrṇe cakravākopaśobhite  |
divyauṣadhigaṇopete sajale śyāmaśādvale  || 2.42 ||
kumudotpalakahlāra- kadalīṣaṇḍamaṇḍite  |
citrite bhavanodyāne kārayetparameśvari  || 2.43 ||
tanmadhye devadeveśi mattavāraṇasaṃyutam  |
vātāyanasamopetamūrdhvanirgāmidhūmakam  || 2.44 ||
raktapatākāsaṃyuktaṃ sajjopakaraṇaṃ tathā  |
pravibhaktauṣadhituṣā- kāṣṭhāraṇyotpalālayam  || 2.45 ||
samālikhitadigdevaṃ samarcitavināyakam  |
pratiṣṭhitamumeśābhyāṃ lokapālaiśca rakṣitam  || 2.46 ||
nirmāpayedekatalaṃ dvitalaṃ vāpi maṇḍapam  |
atha pakṣe site devi candratārābalānvite  || 2.47 ||
puṇye tithau muhūrte ca lagne saumyagrahekṣite  |
snātaḥ śuklāmbaradharaḥ śuklamālyānulepanaḥ  || 2.48 ||
madhusarpirdadhikṣīra- tilaiḥ saṃpūjya bālikāḥ  |
kapilāgomayālipte hiraṇyakalaśāvṛte  || 2.49 ||
yavasiddhārthakāstīrṇe gandhamālyopaśobhite  |
tatreṣṭikābhiḥ racite karapīṭhe sureśvari  || 2.50 ||
śilāpaṭṭaṃ samutkīrya śilāpaṭṭārgalaṃ priye  |
nyāsaṃ rasāṅkuśenaiva kṛtvāṅgulihṛdādiṣu  || 2.51 ||
rasaliṅgaṃ nyasettatra hemnā ca sahitaṃ priye  |
oṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ sūtarājasya mūlamantra udāhṛtaḥ  || 2.52 ||
tasmin rasāṅkuśīṃ devīṃ mūlenāvāhayetpriye  |
caturasre tu dikpālān pūjayitvā bahiḥ kramāt || 2.53 ||
nandinaṃ ca mahākālaṃ bhṛṅgirīṭaṃ mahābalam  |
kumbhakarṇaṃ ca sugrīvaṃ bhṛṅgīkaṃ ca dṛḍhāyudham  || 2.54 ||
dvāre caturdhā vinyasya pūjayeddakṣavāmayoḥ  |
śukraṃ pūrve'bhisaṃpūjya skandamāgneyagocare  || 2.55 ||
dakṣiṇasyāṃ tato rudraṃ pavanaṃ nairṛte tathā  |
śivaṃ paścimabhāge tu pāvakaṃ vāyave nyaset |
umāmuttarabhāge tu vyāpakaṃ ceśagocare  || 2.56 ||
lepikā kṣepikā caiva kṣārikā rañjikā tathā  |
lohaṭī bandhakārī ca bhūcarī mṛtyunāśinī  || 2.57 ||
vibhūtiḥ khecarī caiva daśa dūtyaḥ krameṇa ca  |
pūjyāstvaṣṭadale padme ūrdhvādhastu daleṣu ca  || 2.58 ||
mākṣiko vimalaḥ śailaścapalo rasakastathā  |
sasyako gandhatālau ca pūrvādikramayogataḥ  || 2.59 ||
aṣṭādaśabhujā rudrāḥ pañcavaktrāstryambakāḥ  |
candrārdhaśobhimukuṭā nīlagrīvā vṛṣadhvajāḥ  || 2.60 ||
svasvavarṇadharāḥ sarve'pyaṣṭavidyeśvarāstathā  |
pūjanīyā maheśāni dvitīye'ṣṭadalāmbuje  || 2.61 ||
karṇikāyāṃ tu pūrvādi- pūrvaṃ śakticatuṣṭayam  |
mālinī hemaśaktiśca parā śaktirbalā smṛtā  |
aparā vajraśaktiśca kāntijñeyaṃ parāparam  || 2.62 ||

vajradehaḥ sa siddhaḥ syātdivyastrījanavallabhaḥ  |
krīḍate khecarairbhogaiḥ svecchayā śivatāṃ vrajet || 12.337 ||
nānāvidhaphalaṃ ca syāttadrasairbījatailataḥ  |
tadbaddhaṃ pāradaṃ caiva guṭikāṃ śṛṇu sundari  || 12.338 ||
śuddhabaddharasendrastu gandhakaṃ tatra jārayet |
triguṇe gandhake jīrṇe tena hema tu kārayet || 12.339 ||
kārayedbhasma sūtaṃ tu kāñcanaṃ tatra sūtakam  |
tadbhasma sūtake jāryaṃ rasendrasya same samam  || 12.340 ||
tena sūtakajīrṇena vajraratnaṃ tu jārayet |
tadvajraṃ jāyate bhasma sindūrāruṇasaṃnibham  || 12.341 ||
tadbhasma jārayate sūte triguṇe tu surārcite  |
hāṭake sārayettaṃ tu guṭikāṃ tena kārayet || 12.342 ||
trilohāveṣṭitaṃ taṃ tu mukhe prakṣipya sādhakaḥ  |
naṣṭacchāyo bhavetso'yamadṛśyo devadānavaiḥ  || 12.343 ||
tatkṣaṇādvedhayeddevi sarvalohāni kāñcanam  |
bahuvarṣasahasrāṇi nirvalīpalito bhavet || 12.344 ||
śūlinaṃ śaktisaṃyuktaṃ ratnādiguṇabhūṣitam  |
vaktre kare ca bibhṛyātsarvāyudhanivāraṇāt || 12.345 ||
vyoma mākṣikasattvaṃ ca tārāmātraṃ surāyudham  |
vaikrāntaṃ tālakaṃ sūtaṃ ratnādiguṇabhūṣitam  || 12.346 ||
guṭikā sā varārohe madhuratrayasaṃyutā  |
vaktrasthā nāśayetsākṣātpalitaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ  |
śivaśaktiśca deveśi ratnādiśivagā yathā  || 12.347 ||
hema tāraṃ tathā bhānuṃ samabhāgāni kārayet |
strīrajo vyāghramadhyasthaṃ padmasūtreṇa veṣṭayet || 12.348 ||
secayettattathāveṣṭya guhyasthāne nidhāpayet |
raṇe rājakule dyūte divye kāmye jayo bhavet |
yanmukhe caiva tadgolaṃ sa sarvarañjako bhavet || 12.349 ||
vaikrāntābhrakakāntaṃ tu sasyakaṃ tu surāyudham  |
vibhītakādisambhūta- kāñcikasya samaṃ bhavet || 12.350 ||
rājāvartaṃ tataḥ sūte yojayetpādayogataḥ  |
kumārīrasasaṃghṛṣṭā kṛtaikā guṭikā śubhā  |
rogamṛtyujarā hanti vaktrasthā nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 12.351 ||
pañcatāraṃ varārohe sūtakaṃ dvayameva ca  |
trayo gaganabhāgāḥ syurekaikaṃ hemakāntayoḥ  || 12.352 ||
ardhaśulvavidhānena guṭikāmarasundari  |
akṣayo hyajaraścaiva bhavettena mahābalaḥ  |
sarvarogavinirmukto jīvedvaktre vidhāraṇāt || 12.353 ||
bhasmasūtapalaikaṃ ca mṛtakāntapalaṃ tathā  |
mākṣikasya palaṃ caiva śilājatupalaṃ punaḥ  || 12.354 ||
palamekaṃ viḍaṅgasya pathyācūrṇapalaṃ tathā  |
ekīkṛtyātha tatsarvaṃ madhvājyena tu peṣayet || 12.355 ||
guṭikāḥ kārayeddevi ṣaṣṭyadhikaśatatrayam  |
ekaikaṃ bhakṣayennityaṃ varṣamekaṃ nirantaram  || 12.356 ||
jīvedvarṣasahasrāṇi rudratulyo mahābalaḥ  || 12.357 ||
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi rasabhasmarasāyanam  |
vijñeyaṃ niṣparīhāraṃ sākṣāddivyauṣadhaṃ param  || 12.358 ||
āmalakyādi kāntaṃ ca pāradaṃ ca manaḥśilām  |
vākucīsaptabhāgaṃ ca kṣīriṇīrasapeṣitam  |
meghanādarasopetaṃ mūkamūṣāgataṃ puṭet || 12.359 ||
māṣaṃ trimāṣaṃ triguṇaṃ bhakṣayettu krameṇa tu  |
varṣatrayaparaṃ devi pādaniṣkārdhakaṃ kramāt || 12.360 ||
ṣaṭsaptāṣṭau ca varṣāṇi kramānniṣkapramāṇataḥ  |
bhuñjīta sa ca divyānnaṃ jarāvairūpyavarjitaḥ  || 12.361 ||
kiṃcitkāñcanasaṃyuktaṃ niṣkaṃ niṣkārdhameva ca  |
yo bhakṣayettribhirvarṣaiḥ sarvavyādhīn jayatyalam  || 12.362 ||
aṣṭavarṣe sahasrāyuḥ dvādaśe lakṣavedhakaḥ  |
ṣoḍaśe vatsare devi divyarūpaḥ sa jāyate  || 12.363 ||
śatapalamabhayānāmakṣadhātryostathaiva kvathitajalaśatāṣṭau bhāgamaṣṭāvaśeṣam  |
ghṛtamadhusitayāḍhyaṃ vyoṣacitraṃ daśāṃśaṃ rasaphalarasasiddhaṃ lohajīrṇaṃ mṛtaṃ ca  || 12.364 ||
girijatusamamabhraṃ kāntabhṛṅgaṃ viḍaṅgaṃ rasasahitasubhāvyaṃ taṇḍulairdivyamukhyaiḥ  |
ahimarakṛtakalkaṃ lohapātrasthamāṣaṃ tridinatanususiddhaṃ kalkametadvariṣṭham  || 12.365 ||
lihati śayanakāle vāmanetrāvasevī ghananibiḍasumadhyo mattamātaṃgadarpaḥ  |
vigatasakaladoṣaḥ sarvadigdivyacakṣuḥ madana iva sukāntiḥ kāminīnāṃ pravīraḥ  || 12.366 ||
jaladalavavapuṣmān kuñcitānīlakeśaḥ suragururiva śuddhaḥ satkaviścitrakārī  |
vṛṣabhagativiceṣṭaḥ snigdhagambhīraghoṣaḥ suragaja iva loke śrāntihantāśu nityam  || 12.367 ||
prabhavati khalu loke somatārārkajīvī kamalasadanasuśrīrnyāyaśāstrādivettā  |
sujanasamayapātā dharmadīkṣānumātā hariharamagabhīraḥ sūryasomābdhidhīraḥ  || 12.368 ||
kāntahemaravicandramabhrakaṃ golakaṃ nihitamiṅgudīphale  |
śailavārivarasiddhagolakaṃ sundaraṃ hyamararañjakaṃ śubham  || 12.369 ||
kāntahemaravicandramabhrakaṃ vajraratnamahirājagolakam  |
kṣiptamāmalakakāṣṭhakoṭare bhūmiśailanihitaṃ samuddhṛtam  |
śailatāṃ gatamathāhitaṃ mukhe vajrakāyakaramalpavāsaraiḥ  || 12.370 ||
tālahemavaraśulbasūtakaiḥ golakaṃ varaṇakāṣṭhayantritam  |
śailavārikṛtasundarīrasaṃ khecarīti guṭikā nigadyate  || 12.371 ||
śailāmbunikṣiptapalāśabījaṃ śailīkṛtaṃ kṣaudraghṛtena khādet |
triḥsaptarātraṃ dinamekamekaṃ sahasrajīvī vijayī naraḥ syāt || 12.372 ||
sūtakaṃ cābhrakaṃ caiva vajrīkṣīrasamanvitam  |
hāṭakena samāyuktaṃ guṭikā khecarī bhavet || 12.373 ||
karañjaphalamadhyasthaṃ sūtakaṃ tatra nikṣipet |
ghṛtaśailāmbumadhyasthaṃ sahasrāyuḥ prayacchati  || 12.374 ||
tinduke dvisahasrāyuḥ jambīre trisahasrakam  |
mātuluṅge ca nāraṅge catuḥpañcasahasrakam  || 12.375 ||
rambhāphale ṣaṭsahasraṃ panase saptasaṃkhyakam  |
vibhītakaphale caiva daśasāhasrasaṃkhyakam  || 12.376 ||
nārikele mahābhāge sahasrāṇi caturdaśa  |
triṃśatsahasraṃ padme ca lakṣamāmalake punaḥ  || 12.377 ||
abhrapattradrave kvāthamahorātraṃ śilodake  |
tasmāttu coddhṛtaṃ sūtaṃ mṛtyudāridryanāśanam  || 12.378 ||
sāraṇākramayogena navīnaṃ jāyate vapuḥ  |

dhamanātpatate sattvaṃ mukhe taddhārayennaraḥ  |
