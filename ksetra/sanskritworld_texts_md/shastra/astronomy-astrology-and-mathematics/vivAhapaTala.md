1 ajñātamadhyāntasamudbhavāya sarvātmane sarvagatāya tasmai|

1 praṇamya devāya manobhavāya khalīkṛtaṃ yena jagatsamagram||

2 prajāpatirmanmatha eva yasmātkāmādvinā na prabhavaḥ prajānām|

2 mahatsu sūkṣmeṣu ca so'sti devaḥ sarvātmakaḥ sarvagataśca yasmāt ||

27 vyādhyairmṛtyuraṇaṃgrahair[ū. asaṅgrahair] bahuvidhā dīkṣā pravāsāḥ śubhaiḥ||

28 kriye kumāreṣvanuraktacittā vihīnavittā gavi govratā c
