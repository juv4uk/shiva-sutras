[ācārādhyāya]

[1. upodghātaprakaraṇam]

yogīśvaraṃ yājñavalkyaṃ saṃpūjya munayo'bruvan  |
varṇāśrametarāṇāṃ no brūhi dharmānaśeṣataḥ  || 1.1 ||

mithilāsthaḥ sa yogīndraḥ kṣaṇaṃ dhyātvābravīnmunīn  |
yasmin deśe mṛgaḥ kṛṣṇastasmin dharmānnibodhata  || 1.2 ||

purāṇanyāyamīmāṃsā- dharmaśāstrāṅgamiśritāḥ  |
vedāḥ sthānāni vidyānāṃ dharmasya ca caturdaśa  || 1.3 ||

manvatriviṣṇuhārīta- yājñavalkyośano'ṅgirāḥ  |
yamāpastambasaṃvartāḥ kātyāyanabṛhaspatī  || 1.4 ||

parāśaravyāsaśaṅkha- likhitā dakṣagautamau  |
śātātapo vasiṣṭhaśca dharmaśāstraprayojakāḥ  || 1.5 ||

deśe kāla upāyena dravyaṃ śraddhāsamanvitam  |
pātre pradīyate yattatsakalaṃ dharmalakṣaṇam  || 1.6 ||

śrutiḥ smṛtiḥ sadācāraḥ svasya ca priyamātmanaḥ  |
samyaksaṃkalpajaḥ kāmo dharmamūlamidaṃ smṛtam  || 1.7 ||

ijyācāradamāhiṃsā- dānasvādhyāyakarmaṇām  |
ayaṃ tu paramo dharmo yadyogenātmadarśanam  || 1.8 ||

catvāro vedadharmajñāḥ parṣattraividyameva vā  |
sā brūte yaṃ sa dharmaḥ syādeko vādhyātmavittamaḥ  || 1.9 ||



[2. brahmacāriprakaraṇam]

brahmakṣatriyaviṭśūdrā varṇāstvādyāstrayo dvijāḥ  |
niṣekādyāḥ śmaśānāntāsteṣāṃ vai mantrataḥ kriyāḥ  || 1.10 ||

garbhādhānamṛtau puṃsaḥ savanaṃ spandanātpurā  |
ṣaṣṭhe'ṣṭame vā sīmanto māsyete jātakarma ca  || 1.11 ||

ahanyekādaśe nāma caturthe māsi niṣkramaḥ  |
ṣaṣṭhe'nnaprāśanaṃ māsi cūḍā kāryā yathākulam  || 1.12 ||
evamenaḥ śamaṃ yāti bījagarbhasamudbhavam  |
tūṣṇīmetāḥ kriyāḥ strīṇāṃ vivāhastu samantrakaḥ  || 1.13 ||

garbhāṣṭame'ṣṭame vābde brāhmaṇasyopanāyanam  |
rājñāmekādaśe saike viśāmeke yathākulam  || 1.14 ||

upanīya guruḥ śiṣyaṃ mahāvyāhṛtipūrvakam  |
vedamadhyāpayedenaṃ śaucācārāṃśca śikṣayet || 1.15 ||

divāsaṃdhyāsu karṇastha- brahmasūtrodaṅmukhaḥ  |
kuryānmūtrapurīṣe ca rātrau ceddakṣiṇāmukhaḥ  || 1.16 ||

gṛhītaśiśnaścotthāya mṛdbhirabhyuddhṛtairjalaiḥ  |
gandhalepakṣayakaraṃ śaucaṃ kuryādatandritaḥ  || 1.17 ||

antarjānu śucau deśa upaviṣṭa udaṅmukhaḥ  |
prāgvā brāhmeṇa tīrthena dvijo nityamupaspṛśet || 1.18 ||

kaniṣṭhādeśinyaṅguṣṭha- mūlānyagraṃ karasya ca  |
prajāpatipitṛbrahma- devatīrthānyanukramāt || 1.19 ||

triḥ prāśyāpo dvirunmṛjya khānyadbhiḥ samupaspṛśet |
adbhistu prakṛtisthābhirhīnābhiḥ phenabudbudaiḥ  || 1.20 ||

hṛtkaṇṭhatālugābhistu yathāsaṃkhyaṃ dvijātayaḥ  |
śudhyeran strī ca śūdraśca sakṛtspṛṣṭābhirantataḥ  || 1.21 ||

snānamabdaivatairmantrairmārjanaṃ prāṇasaṃyamaḥ  |
sūryasya cāpyupasthānaṃ gāyatryāḥ pratyahaṃ japaḥ  || 1.22 ||

gāyatrīṃ śirasā sārdhaṃ japedvyāhṛtipūrvikām  |
pratipraṇavasaṃyuktāṃ trirayaṃ prāṇasaṃyamaḥ  || 1.23 ||

prāṇānāyamya saṃprokṣya tṛcenābdaivatena tu  |
japannāsīta sāvitrīṃ pratyagātārakodayāt || 1.24 ||

saṃdhyāṃ prākprātarevaṃ hi tiṣṭhedāsūryadarśanāt |
agnikāryaṃ tataḥ kuryātsaṃdhyayorubhayorapi  || 1.25 ||

tato'bhivādayedvṛddhānasāvahamiti bruvan  |
guruṃ caivāpyupāsīta svādhyāyārthaṃ samāhitaḥ  || 1.26 ||

āhūtaścāpyadhīyīta labdhaṃ cāsmai nivedayet |
hitaṃ tasyācarennityaṃ manovākkāyakarmabhiḥ  || 1.27 ||

kṛtajñādrohimedhāvi- śucikalyānasūyakāḥ  |
adhyāpyā dharmataḥ sādhu śaktāptajñānavittadāḥ  || 1.28 ||

daṇḍājinopavītāni mekhalāṃ caiva dhārayet |
brāhmaṇeṣu caredbhaikṣamanindyeṣvātmavṛttaye  || 1.29 ||

ādimadhyāvasāneṣu bhavacchabdopalakṣitā  |
brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ bhaikṣacaryā yathākramam  || 1.30 ||

kṛtāgnikāryo bhuñjīta vāgyato gurvanujñayā  |
āpośānakriyāpūrvaṃ satkṛtyānnamakutsayan  || 1.31 ||
brahmacarye sthito naikamannamadyādanāpadi  |
brāhmaṇaḥ kāmamaśnīyācchrāddhe vratamapīḍayan  || 1.32 ||

madhumāṃsāñjanocchiṣṭa- śuktastrīprāṇihiṃsanam  |
bhāskarālokanāślīla- parivādādi varjayet || 1.33 ||

sa gururyaḥ kriyāḥ kṛtvā vedamasmai prayacchati  |
upanīya dadadvedamācāryaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 1.34 ||

ekadeśamupādhyāya ṛtvigyajñakṛducyate  |
ete mānyā yathāpūrvamebhyo mātā garīyasī  || 1.35 ||

prativedaṃ brahmacaryaṃ dvādaśābdāni pañca vā  |
grahaṇāntikamityeke keśāntaścaiva ṣoḍaśe  || 1.36 ||

āṣoḍaśādādvāviṃśāccaturviṃśācca vatsarāt |
brahmakṣatraviśāṃ kālaupanāyanikaḥ paraḥ  || 1.37 ||

ata ūrdhvaṃ patantyete sarvadharmabahiṣkṛtāḥ  |
sāvitrīpatitā vrātyā vrātyastomādṛte kratoḥ  || 1.38 ||

māturyadagre jāyante dvitīyaṃ mauñjibandhanāt |
brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśastasmādete dvijāḥ smṛtāḥ  || 1.39 ||

yajñānāṃ tapasāṃ caiva śubhānāṃ caiva karmaṇām  |
veda eva dvijātīnāṃ niḥśreyasakaraḥ paraḥ  || 1.40 ||

madhunā payasā caiva sa devāṃstarpayeddvijaḥ  |
pitṝnmadhughṛtābhyāṃ ca ṛco'dhīte ca yo'nvaham  || 1.41 ||

yajūṃṣi śaktito'dhīte yo'nvahaṃ sa ghṛtāmṛtaiḥ  |
prīṇāti devānājyena madhunā ca pitṝṃstathā  || 1.42 ||

sa tu somaghṛtairdevāṃstarpayedyo'nvahaṃ paṭhet |
sāmāni tṛptiṃ kuryācca pitṝṇāṃ madhusarpiṣā  || 1.43 ||

medasā tarpayeddevānatharvāṅgirasaḥ paṭhan  |
pitṝṃśca madhusarpirbhyāmanvahaṃ śaktito dvijaḥ  || 1.44 ||

vākovākyaṃ purāṇaṃ ca nārāśaṃsīśca gāthikāḥ  |
itihāsāṃstathā vidyāḥ śaktyādhīte hi yo'nvaham  || 1.45 ||

māṃsakṣīraudanamadhu- tarpaṇaṃ sa divaukasām  |
karoti tṛptiṃ kuryācca pitṝṇāṃ madhusarpiṣā  || 1.46 ||

te tṛptāstarpayantyenaṃ sarvakāmaphalaiḥ śubhaiḥ  |
yaṃ yaṃ kratumadhīte ca tasya tasyāpnuyātphalam  || 1.47 ||

trirvittapūrṇapṛthivī- dānasya phalamaśnute  |
tapasaśca parasyeha nityaṃ svādhyāyavān dvijaḥ  || 1.48 ||

naiṣṭhiko brahmacārī tu vasedācāryasaṃnidhau  |
tadabhāve'sya tanaye patnyāṃ vaiśvānare'pi vā  || 1.49 ||

anena vidhinā dehaṃ sādayan vijitendriyaḥ  |
brahmalokamavāpnoti na cehājāyate punaḥ  || 1.50 ||


[3. vivāhaprakaraṇam]

gurave tu varaṃ dattvā snāyādvā tadanujñayā  |
vedaṃ vratāni vā pāraṃ nītvā hyubhayameva vā  || 1.51 ||

aviplutabrahmacaryo lakṣaṇyāṃ striyamudvahet |
ananyapūrvikāṃ kāntāmasapiṇḍāṃ yavīyasīm  || 1.52 ||

arogiṇīṃ bhrātṛmatīmasamānārṣagotrajān  |
pañcamātsaptamādūrdhvaṃ mātṛtaḥ pitṛtastathā  || 1.53 ||

daśapūruṣavikhyātācchrotriyāṇāṃ mahākulāt |
sphītādapi na saṃcāri- rogadoṣasamanvitāt || 1.54 ||

etaireva guṇairyuktaḥ savarṇaḥ śrotriyo varaḥ  |
yatnātparīkṣitaḥ puṃstve yuvā dhīmān janapriyaḥ  || 1.55 ||

yaducyate dvijātīnāṃ śūdrāddāropasaṃgrahaḥ  |
naitanmama mataṃ yasmāttatrāyaṃ jāyate svayam  || 1.56 ||

tisro varṇānupūrvyeṇa dve tathaikā yathākramam  |
brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ bhāryā svā śūdrajanmanaḥ  || 1.57 ||

brāhmo vivāha āhūya dīyate śaktyalaṃkṛtā  |
tajjaḥ punātyubhayataḥ puruṣānekaviṃśatim  || 1.58 ||

yajñastha ṛtvije daiva ādāyārṣastu godvayam  |
caturdaśa prathamajaḥ punātyuttarajaśca ṣaṭ || 1.59 ||

ityuktvā caratāṃ dharmaṃ saha yā dīyate'rthine  |
sa kāyaḥ pāvayettajjaḥ ṣaṭṣaḍvaṃśyān sahātmanā  || 1.60 ||

āsuro draviṇādānādgāndharvaḥ samayānmithaḥ  |
rākṣaso yuddhaharaṇātpaiśācaḥ kanyakāchalāt || 1.61 ||

pāṇirgrāhyaḥ savarṇāsu gṛhṇīyātkṣatriyā śaram  |
vaiśyā pratodamādadyādvedane tvagrajanmanaḥ  || 1.62 ||

pitā pitāmaho bhrātā sakulyo jananī tathā  |
kanyāpradaḥ pūrvanāśe prakṛtisthaḥ paraḥ paraḥ  || 1.63 ||

aprayacchan samāpnoti bhrūṇahatyāmṛtāvṛtau  |
gamyaṃ tvabhāve dātṝṇāṃ kanyā kuryātsvayaṃvaram  || 1.64 ||

sakṛtpradīyate kanyā haraṃstāṃ coradaṇḍabhāk |
dattāmapi haretpūrvācchreyāṃścedvara āvrajet || 1.65 ||

anākhyāya dadaddoṣaṃ daṇḍya uttamasāhasam  |
aduṣṭāṃ tu tyajan daṇḍyo dūṣayaṃstu mṛṣā śatam  || 1.66 ||

akṣatā ca kṣatā caiva punarbhūḥ saṃskṛtā punaḥ  |
svairiṇī yā patiṃ hitvā savarṇaṃ kāmataḥ śrayet || 1.67 ||

aputrāṃ gurvanujñāto devaraḥ putrakāmyayā  |
sapiṇḍo vā sagotro vā ghṛtābhyakta ṛtāviyāt || 1.68 ||

āgarbhasaṃbhavādgacchetpatitastvanyathā bhavet |
anena vidhinā jātaḥ kṣetrajo'sya bhavetsutaḥ  || 1.69 ||

hṛtādhikārāṃ malināṃ piṇḍamātropajīvinām  |
paribhūtāmadhaḥśayyāṃ vāsayedvyabhicāriṇīm  || 1.70 ||

somaḥ śaucaṃ dadāvāsāṃ gandharvaśca śubhāṃ giram  |
pāvakaḥ sarvamedhyatvaṃ medhyā vai yoṣito hyataḥ  || 1.71 ||

vyabhicārādṛtau śuddhirgarbhe tyāgo vidhīyate  |
garbhabhartṛvadhādau ca tathā mahati pātake  || 1.72 ||

surāpī vyādhitā dhūrtā vandhyārthaghnyapriyaṃvadā  |
strīprasūścādhivettavyā puruṣadveṣiṇī tathā  || 1.73 ||

adhivinnā tu bhartavyā mahadeno'nyathā bhavet |
yatrānukūlyaṃ daṃpatyostrivargastatra vardhate  || 1.74 ||

mṛte jīvati vā patyau yā nānyamupagacchati  |
seha kīrtimavāpnoti modate comayā saha  || 1.75 ||

ājñāsaṃpādinīṃ dakṣāṃ vīrasūṃ priyavādinīm  |
tyajan dāpyastṛtīyāṃśamadravyo bharaṇaṃ striyāḥ  || 1.76 ||

strībhirbhartṛvacaḥ kāryameṣa dharmaḥ paraḥ striyāḥ  |
āśuddheḥ saṃpratīkṣyo hi mahāpātakadūṣitaḥ  || 1.77 ||

lokānantyaṃ divaḥ prāptiḥ putrapautraprapautrakaiḥ  |
yasmāttasmātstriyaḥ sevyāḥ kartavyāśca surakṣitāḥ  || 1.78 ||

ṣoḍaśartuniśāḥ strīṇāṃ tasmin yugmāsu saṃviśet |
brahmacāryeva parvāṇyādyāścatasrastu varjayet || 1.79 ||

evaṃ gacchan striyaṃ kṣāmāṃ maghāṃ mūlaṃ ca varjayet |
sustha indau sakṛtputraṃ lakṣaṇyaṃ janayetpumān  || 1.80 ||

yathākāmī bhavedvāpi strīṇāṃ varamanusmaran  |
svadāranirataścaiva striyo rakṣyā yataḥ smṛtāḥ  || 1.81 ||

bhartṛbhrātṛpitṛjñāti- śvaśrūśvaśuradevaraiḥ  |
bandhubhiśca striyaḥ pūjyā bhūṣaṇācchādanāśanaiḥ  || 1.82 ||

saṃyatopaskarā dakṣā hṛṣṭā vyayaparāṅmukhī  |
kuryācchvaśurayoḥ pāda- vandanaṃ bhartṛtatparā  || 1.83 ||

krīḍāṃ śarīrasaṃskāraṃ samājotsavadarśanam  |
hāsyaṃ paragṛhe yānaṃ tyajetproṣitabhartṛkā  || 1.84 ||

rakṣetkanyāṃ pitā vinnāṃ patiḥ putrāstu vārdhake  |
abhāve jñātayasteṣāṃ na svātantryaṃ kvacitstriyāḥ  || 1.85 ||

pitṛmātṛsutabhrātṛ- śvaśrūśvaśuramātulaiḥ  |
hīnā na syādvinā bhartrā garhaṇīyānyathā bhavet || 1.86 ||

patipriyahite yuktā svācārā vijitendriyā  |
seha kīrtimavāpnoti pretya cānuttamāṃ gatim  || 1.87 ||

satyāmanyāṃ savarṇāyāṃ dharmakāryaṃ na kārayet |
savarṇāsu vidhau dharmye jyeṣṭhayā na vinetarā  || 1.88 ||

dāhayitvāgnihotreṇa striyaṃ vṛttavatīṃ patiḥ  |
āharedvidhivaddārānagnīṃścaivāvilambayan  || 1.89 ||



[4. varṇajātivivekaprakaraṇam]

savarṇebhyaḥ savarṇāsu jāyante hi sajātayaḥ  |
anindyeṣu vivāheṣu putrāḥ saṃtānavardhanāḥ  || 1.90 ||

viprānmūrdhāvasikto hi kṣatriyāyāṃ viśaḥ striyām  |
ambaṣṭhaḥ śūdryāṃ niṣādo jātaḥ pāraśavo'pi vā  || 1.91 ||

vaiśyāśūdryostu rājanyānmāhiṣyograu sutau smṛtau  |
vaiśyāttu karaṇaḥ śūdryāṃ vinnāsveṣa vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1.92 ||

brāhmaṇyāṃ kṣatriyātsūto vaiśyādvaidehakastathā  |
śūdrājjātastu caṇḍālaḥ sarvadharmabahiṣkṛtaḥ  || 1.93 ||

kṣatriyā māgadhaṃ vaiśyācchūdrātkṣattārameva ca  |
śūdrādāyogavaṃ vaiśyā janayāmāsa vai sutam  || 1.94 ||

māhiṣyeṇa karaṇyāṃ tu rathakāraḥ prajāyate  |
asatsantastu vijñeyāḥ pratilomānulomajāḥ  || 1.95 ||

jātyutkarṣo yuge jñeyaḥ saptame pañcame'pi vā  |
vyatyaye karmaṇāṃ sāmyaṃ pūrvavaccādharottaram  || 1.96 ||



[5. gṛhasthadharmaprakaraṇam]

karma smārtaṃ vivāhāgnau kurvīta pratyahaṃ gṛhī  |
dāyakālāhṛte vāpi śrautaṃ vaitānikāgniṣu  || 1.97 ||

śarīracintāṃ nirvartya kṛtaśaucavidhirdvijaḥ  |
prātaḥsaṃdhyāmupāsīta dantadhāvanapūrvakam  || 1.98 ||

hutvāgnīn sūryadaivatyān japenmantrān samāhitaḥ  |
vedārthānadhigacchecca śāstrāṇi vividhāni ca  || 1.99 ||

upeyādīśvaraṃ caiva yogakṣemārthasiddhaye  |
snātvā devān pitṝṃścaiva tarpayedarcayettathā  || 1.100 ||

vedātharvapurāṇāni setihāsāni śaktitaḥ  |
japayajñaprasiddhyarthaṃ vidyāṃ cādhyātmikīṃ japet || 1.101 ||

balikarmasvadhāhoma- svādhyāyātithisatkriyāḥ  |
bhūtapitramarabrahma- manuṣyāṇāṃ mahāmakhāḥ  || 1.102 ||

devebhyaśca hutādannāccheṣādbhūtabaliṃ haret |
annaṃ bhūmau śvacāṇḍāla- vāyasebhyaśca nikṣipet || 1.103 ||

annaṃ pitṛmanuṣyebhyo deyamapyanvahaṃ jalam  |
svādhyāyaṃ satataṃ kuryānna pacedannamātmane  || 1.104 ||

bālasvavāsinīvṛddha- garbhiṇyāturakanyakāḥ  |
saṃbhojyātithibhṛtyāṃśca daṃpatyoḥ śeṣabhojanam  || 1.105 ||

āpośanenopariṣṭādadhastādaśnatā tathā  |
anagnamamṛtaṃ caiva kāryamannaṃ dvijanmanā  || 1.106 ||

atithitvena varṇānāṃ deyaṃ śaktyānupūrvaśaḥ  |
apraṇodyo'tithiḥ sāyamapi vāgbhūtṛṇodakaiḥ  || 1.107 ||

satkṛtya bhikṣave bhikṣā dātavyā savratāya ca  |
bhojayeccāgatān kāle sakhisaṃbandhibāndhavān  || 1.108 ||

mahokṣaṃ vā mahājaṃ vā śrotriyāyopakalpayet |
satkriyānvāsanaṃ svādu bhojanaṃ sūnṛtaṃ vacaḥ  || 1.109 ||

pratisaṃvatsaraṃ tvarghyāḥ snātakācāryapārthivāḥ  |
priyo vivāhyaśca tathā yajñaṃ pratyṛtvijaḥ punaḥ  || 1.110 ||

adhvanīno'tithirjñeyaḥ śrotriyo vedapāragaḥ  |
mānyāvetau gṛhasthasya brahmalokamabhīpsataḥ  || 1.111 ||

parapākarucirna syādanindyāmantraṇādṛte  |
vākpāṇipādacāpalyaṃ varjayeccātibhojanam  || 1.112 ||

atithiṃ śrotriyaṃ tṛptamāsīmantamanuvrajet |
ahaḥśeṣaṃ sahāsīta śiṣṭairiṣṭaiśca bandhubhiḥ  || 1.113 ||

upāsya paścimāṃ saṃdhyāṃ hutvāgnīṃstānupāsya ca  |
bhṛtyaiḥ parivṛto bhuktvā nātitṛptyātha saṃviśet || 1.114 ||

brāhme muhūrte cotthāya cintayedātmano hitam  |
dharmārthakāmān sve kāle yathāśakti na hāpayet || 1.115 ||

vidyākarmavayobandhu- vittairmānyā yathākramam  |
etaiḥ prabhūtaiḥ śūdro'pi vārdhake mānamarhati  || 1.116 ||

vṛddhabhārinṛpasnāta- strīrogivaracakriṇām  |
panthā deyo nṛpasteṣāṃ mānyaḥ snātaśca bhūpateḥ  || 1.117 ||

ijyādhyayanadānāni vaiśyasya kṣatriyasya ca  |
pratigraho'dhiko vipre yājanādhyāpane tathā  || 1.118 ||

pradhānaṃ kṣatriye karma prajānāṃ paripālanam  |

udakyāspṛṣṭasaṃghuṣṭaṃ paryāyānnaṃ ca varjayet |

karmaniṣṭhāstaponiṣṭhāḥ pañcāgnirbrahmacāriṇaḥ  |

tavāhaṃvādinaṃ klībaṃ nirhetiṃ parasaṃgatam  |

hiraṇyaṃ vyāpṛtānītaṃ bhāṇḍāgāreṣu nikṣipet |

dṛṣṭvā jyotirvido vaidyān dadyādgāṃ kāñcanaṃ mahīm  |

arakṣyamāṇāḥ kurvanti yatkiṃcitkilbiṣaṃ prajāḥ  |

arirmitramudāsīno'nantarastatparaḥ paraḥ  |

upāyāḥ sāma dānaṃ ca bhedo daṇḍastathaiva ca  |

yadā sasyaguṇopetaṃ pararāṣṭraṃ tadā vrajet |

yathā hyekena cakreṇa rathasya na gatirbhavet |

hiraṇyabhūmilābhebhyo mitralabdhirvarā yataḥ  |

svāmyamātyā jano durgaṃ kośo daṇḍastathaiva ca  |

sa netuṃ nyāyato'śakyo lubdhenākṛtabuddhinā  |

yathāśāstraṃ prayuktaḥ san sadevāsuramānavam  |

samyaktu daṇḍanaṃ rājñaḥ svargakīrtijayāvaham  || 1.357 ||

api bhrātā suto'rghyo vā śvaśuro mātulo'pi vā  |
nādaṇḍyo nāma rājño'sti dharmādvicalitaḥ svakāt || 1.358 ||

yo daṇḍyān daṇḍayedrājā samyagvadhyāṃśca ghātayet |
iṣṭaṃ syātkratubhistena samāptavaradakṣiṇaiḥ  || 1.359 ||

iti saṃcintya nṛpatiḥ kratutulyaphalaṃ pṛthak |
vyavahārān svayaṃ paśyetsabhyaiḥ parivṛto'nvaham  || 1.360 ||

kulāni jātīḥ śreṇīśca gaṇān jānapadānapi  |
svadharmāccalitān rājā vinīya sthāpayetpathi  || 1.361 ||

jālasūryamarīcisthaṃ trasareṇū rajaḥ smṛtam  |
te'ṣṭau likṣā tu tāstisro rājasarṣapa ucyate  || 1.362 ||

gaurastu te trayaḥ ṣaṭte yavo madhyastu te trayaḥ  |
kṛṣṇalaḥ pañca te māṣaste suvarṇastu ṣoḍaśa  || 1.363 ||

palaṃ suvarṇāścatvāraḥ pañca vāpi prakīrtitam  |
dve kṛṣṇale rūpyamāṣo dharaṇaṃ ṣoḍaśaiva te  || 1.364 ||

śatamānaṃ tu daśabhirdharaṇaiḥ palameva tu  |
niṣkaṃ suvarṇāścatvāraḥ kārṣikastāmrikaḥ paṇaḥ  || 1.365 ||

sāśītipaṇasāhasro daṇḍa uttamasāhasaḥ  |
tadardhaṃ madhyamaḥ proktastadardhamadhamaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1.366 ||

dhigdaṇḍastvatha vāgdaṇḍo dhanadaṇḍo vadhastathā  |
yojyā vyastāḥ samastā vā hyaparādhavaśādime  || 1.367 ||

jñātvāparādhaṃ deśaṃ ca kālaṃ balamathāpi vā  |
vayaḥ karma ca vittaṃ ca daṇḍaṃ daṇḍyeṣu pātayet || 1.368 ||



[Endof the ācārādhyāya]



[īī. vyavahārādhyāya]

[1. sādhāraṇavyavahāramātṛkāprakaraṇam]

vyavahārānnṛpaḥ paśyedvidvadbhirbrāhmaṇaiḥ saha  |
dharmaśāstrānusāreṇa krodhalobhavivarjitaḥ  || 2.1 ||

śrutādhyayanasaṃpannā dharmajñāḥ satyavādinaḥ  |
rājñā sabhāsadaḥ kāryā ripau mitre ca ye samāḥ  || 2.2 ||

apaśyatā kāryavaśādvyavahārānnṛpeṇa tu  |
sabhyaiḥ saha niyoktavyo brāhmaṇaḥ sarvadharmavit || 2.3 ||

rāgāllobhādbhayādvāpi smṛtyapetādikāriṇaḥ  |
sabhyāḥ pṛthakpṛthagdaṇḍyā vivādāddviguṇaṃ damam  || 2.4 ||

smṛtyācāravyapetena mārgeṇādharṣitaḥ paraiḥ  |
āvedayati cedrājñe vyavahārapadaṃ hi tat || 2.5 ||

pratyarthino'grato lekhyaṃ yathāveditamarthinā  |
samāmāsatadardhāhar- nāmajātyādicihnitam  || 2.6 ||

śrutārthasyottaraṃ lekhyaṃ pūrvāvedakasaṃnidhau  |
tato'rthī lekhayetsadyaḥ pratijñātārthasādhanam  || 2.7 ||

tatsiddhau siddhimāpnoti viparītamato'nyathā  |
catuṣpādvyavahāro'yaṃ vivādeṣūpadarśitaḥ  || 2.8 ||



[2. asādhāraṇavyavahāramātṛkāprakaraṇam]

abhiyogamanistīrya nainaṃ pratyabhiyojayet |
abhiyuktaṃ ca nānyena noktaṃ viprakṛtiṃ nayet || 2.9 ||

kuryātpratyabhiyogaṃ ca kalahe sāhaseṣu ca  |
ubhayoḥ pratibhūrgrāhyaḥ samarthaḥ kāryanirṇaye  || 2.10 ||

nihnave bhāvito dadyāddhanaṃ rājñe ca tatsamam  |
mithyābhiyogī dviguṇamabhiyogāddhanaṃ vahet || 2.11 ||

sāhasasteyapāruṣya- go'bhiśāpātyaye striyām  |
vivādayetsadya eva kālo'nyatrecchayā smṛtaḥ  || 2.12 ||

deśāddeśāntaraṃ yāti sṛkkiṇī parileḍhi ca  |
lalāṭaṃ svidyate cāsya mukhaṃ vaivarṇyameti ca  || 2.13 ||

pariśuṣyatskhaladvākyo viruddhaṃ bahu bhāṣite  |
vākcakṣuḥ pūjayati no tathauṣṭhau nirbhujatyapi  || 2.14 ||

svabhāvādvikṛtiṃ gacchenmanovākkāyakarmabhiḥ  |
abhiyoge'tha sākṣye vā duṣṭaḥ sa parikīrtitaḥ  || 2.15 ||

saṃdigdhārthaṃ svatantro yaḥ sādhayedyaśca niṣpatet |
na cāhūto vadetkiṃciddhīno daṇḍyaśca sa smṛtaḥ  || 2.16 ||

sākṣiṣūbhayataḥ satsu sākṣiṇaḥ pūrvavādinaḥ  |
pūrvapakṣe'dharībhūte bhavantyuttaravādinaḥ  || 2.17 ||

sapaṇaścedvivādaḥ syāttatra hīnaṃ tu dāpayet |
daṇḍaṃ ca svapaṇaṃ caiva dhanine dhanameva ca  || 2.18 ||
chalaṃ nirasya bhūtena vyavahārānnayennṛpaḥ  |
bhūtamapyanupanyastaṃ hīyate vyavahārataḥ  || 2.19 ||

nihnute likhitaṃ naikamekadeśe vibhāvitaḥ  |
dāpyaḥ sarvaṃ nṛpeṇārthaṃ na grāhyastvaniveditaḥ  || 2.20 ||

smṛtyorvirodhe nyāyastu balavān vyavahārataḥ  |
arthaśāstrāttu balavaddharmaśāstramiti sthitiḥ  || 2.21 ||

pramāṇaṃ likhitaṃ bhuktiḥ sākṣiṇaśceti kīrtitam  |
eṣāmanyatamābhāve divyānyatamamucyate  || 2.22 ||

sarveṣvarthavivādeṣu balavatyuttarākriyā  |
ādhau pratigrahe krīte pūrvā tu balavattarā  || 2.23 ||

paśyato'bruvato bhūmerhānirviṃśativārṣikī  |
pareṇa bhujyamānāyā dhanasya daśavārṣikī  || 2.24 ||

ādhisīmopanikṣepa- jaḍabāladhanairvinā  |
tathopanidhirājastrī- śrotriyāṇāṃ dhanairapi  || 2.25 ||

ādhyādīnāṃ vihartāraṃ dhanine dāpayeddhanam  |
daṇḍaṃ ca tatsamaṃ rājñe śaktyapekṣamathāpi vā  || 2.26 ||

āgamo'bhyadhiko bhogādvinā pūrvakramāgatāt |
āgame'pi balaṃ naiva bhuktiḥ stokāpi yatra no  || 2.27 ||

āgamastu kṛto yena so'bhiyuktastamuddharet |
na tatsutastatsuto vā bhuktistatra garīyasī  || 2.28 ||

yo'bhiyuktaḥ paretaḥ syāttasya rikthī tamuddharet |
na tatra kāraṇaṃ bhuktirāgamena vinākṛtā  || 2.29 ||

nṛpeṇādhikṛtāḥ pūgāḥ śreṇayo'tha kulāni ca  |
pūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ guru jñeyaṃ vyavahāravidhau nṛṇām  || 2.30 ||

balopādhivinirvṛttān vyavahārānnivartayet |
strīnaktamantarāgāra- bahiḥśatrukṛtāṃstathā  || 2.31 ||

mattonmattārtavyasani- bālabhītādiyojitaḥ  |
asaṃbaddhakṛtaścaiva vyavahāro na sidhyati  || 2.32 ||

pranaṣṭādhigataṃ deyaṃ nṛpeṇa dhanine dhanam  |
vibhāvayenna celliṅgaistatsamaṃ daṇḍamarhati  || 2.33 ||

rājā labdhvā nidhiṃ dadyāddvijebhyo'rdhaṃ dvijaḥ punaḥ  |
vidvānaśeṣamādadyātsa sarvasya prabhuryataḥ  || 2.34 ||

itareṇa nidhau labdhe rājā ṣaṣṭhāṃśamāharet |
aniveditavijñāto dāpyastaṃ daṇḍameva ca  || 2.35 ||

deyaṃ caurahṛtaṃ dravyaṃ rājñā jānapadāya tu  |
adadaddhi samāpnoti kilbiṣaṃ yasya tasya tat || 2.36 ||



[3. ṛṇādānaprakaraṇam]

aśītibhāgo vṛddhiḥ syānmāsi māsi sabandhake  |
varṇakramācchataṃ dvitri- catuṣpañcakamanyathā  || 2.37 ||

kāntāragāstu daśakaṃ sāmudrā viṃśakaṃ śatam  |
dadyurvā svakṛtāṃ vṛddhiṃ sarve sarvāsu jātiṣu  || 2.38 ||

saṃtatistu paśustrīṇāṃ rasasyāṣṭaguṇā parā  |
vastradhānyahiraṇyānāṃ catustridviguṇā parā  || 2.39 ||

prapannaṃ sādhayannarthaṃ na vācyo nṛpaterbhavet |
sādhyamāno nṛpaṃ gacchan daṇḍyo dāpyaśca taddhanam  || 2.40 ||

gṛhītānukramāddāpyo dhanināmadhamarṇikaḥ  |
dattvā tu brāhmaṇāyaiva nṛpatestadanantaram  || 2.41 ||

rājñādhamarṇiko dāpyaḥ sādhitāddaśakaṃ śatam  |
pañcakaṃ ca śataṃ dāpyaḥ prāptārtho hyuttamarṇikaḥ  || 2.42 ||

hīnajātiṃ parikṣīṇamṛṇārthaṃ karma kārayet |
brāhmaṇastu parikṣīṇaḥ śanairdāpyo yathodayam  || 2.43 ||

dīyamānaṃ na gṛhṇāti prayuktaṃ yaḥ svakaṃ dhanam  |
madhyasthasthāpitaṃ cetsyādvardhate na tataḥ param  || 2.44 ||

avibhaktaiḥ kuṭumbārthe yadṛṇaṃ tu kṛtaṃ bhavet |
dadyustadrikthinaḥ prete proṣite vā kuṭumbini  || 2.45 ||

na yoṣitpatiputrābhyāṃ na putreṇa kṛtaṃ pitā  |
dadyādṛte kuṭumbārthānna patiḥ strīkṛtaṃ tathā  || 2.46 ||

surākāmadyūtakṛtaṃ daṇḍaśulkāvaśiṣṭakam  |
vṛthādānaṃ tathaiveha putro dadyānna paitṛkam  || 2.47 ||

gopaśauṇḍikaśailūṣa- rajakavyādhayoṣitām  |
ṛṇaṃ dadyātpatisteṣāṃ yasmādvṛttistadāśrayā  || 2.48 ||

pratipannaṃ striyā deyaṃ patyā vā saha yatkṛtam  |
svayaṃkṛtaṃ vā yadṛṇaṃ nānyatstrī dātumarhati  || 2.49 ||

pitari proṣite prete vyasanābhiplute'pi vā  |
putrapautrairṛṇaṃ deyaṃ nihnave sākṣibhāvitam  || 2.50 ||

rikthagrāha ṛṇaṃ dāpyo yoṣidgrāhastathaiva ca  |
putro'nanyāśritadravyaḥ putrahīnasya rikthinaḥ  || 2.51 ||

bhrātṝṇāmatha daṃpatyoḥ pituḥ putrasya caiva hi  |
prātibhāvyamṛṇaṃ sākṣyamavibhakte na tu smṛtam  || 2.52 ||

darśane pratyaye dāne prātibhāvyaṃ vidhīyate  |
ādyau tu vitathe dāpyāvitarasya sutā api  || 2.53 ||

darśanapratibhūryatra mṛtaḥ prātyayiko'pi vā  |
na tatputrā ṛṇaṃ dadyurdadyurdānāya yaḥ sthitaḥ  || 2.54 ||

bahavaḥ syuryadi svāṃśairdadyuḥ pratibhuvo dhanam  |
ekacchāyāśriteṣveṣu dhanikasya yathāruci  || 2.55 ||

pratibhūrdāpito yattu prakāśaṃ dhanino dhanam  |
dviguṇaṃ pratidātavyamṛṇikaistasya tadbhavet || 2.56 ||

saṃtatiḥ strīpaśuṣveva dhānyaṃ triguṇameva ca  |
vastraṃ caturguṇaṃ proktaṃ rasaścāṣṭaguṇastathā  || 2.57 ||

ādhiḥ praṇaśyeddviguṇe dhane yadi na mokṣyate  |
kāle kālakṛto naśyetphalabhogyo na naśyati  || 2.58 ||

gopyādhibhoge no vṛddhiḥ sopakāre ca hāpite  |
naṣṭo deyo vinaṣṭaśca daivarājakṛtādṛte  || 2.59 ||

ādheḥ svīkaraṇātsiddhī rakṣyamāṇo'pyasāratām  |
yātaścedanya ādheyo dhanabhāgvā dhanī bhavet || 2.60 ||

caritrabandhakakṛtaṃ sa vṛddhyā dāpayeddhanam  |
satyaṃkārakṛtaṃ dravyaṃ dviguṇaṃ pratidāpayet || 2.61 ||

upasthitasya moktavya ādhiḥ steno'nyathā bhavet |
prayojake'sati dhanaṃ kule nyasyādhimāpnuyāt || 2.62 ||

tatkālakṛtamūlyo vā tatra tiṣṭhedavṛddhikaḥ  |
vinā dhāraṇakādvāpi vikrīṇīta sasākṣikam  || 2.63 ||

yadā tu dviguṇībhūtamṛṇamādhau tadā khalu  |
mocya ādhistadutpanne praviṣṭe dviguṇe dhane  || 2.64 ||



[4. upanidhiprakaraṇam]

vāsanasthamanākhyāya haste'nyasya yadarpyate  |
dravyaṃ tadaupanidhikaṃ pratideyaṃ tathaiva tat || 2.65 ||

na dāpyo'pahṛtaṃ taṃ tu rājadaivikataskaraiḥ  |
bhreṣaścenmārgite'adatte dāpyo daṇḍaṃ ca tatsamam  || 2.66 ||

ājīvan svecchayā daṇḍyo dāpyastaṃ cāpi sodayam  |
yācitānvāhitanyāsa- nikṣepādiṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ  || 2.67 ||



[5. sākṣiprakaraṇam]

tapasvino dānaśīlāḥ kulīnāḥ satyavādinaḥ  |
dharmapradhānā ṛjavaḥ putravanto dhanānvitāḥ  || 2.68 ||

tryavarāḥ sākṣiṇo jñeyāḥ śrautasmārtakriyāparāḥ  |
yathājāti yathāvarṇaṃ sarve sarveṣu vā smṛtāḥ  || 2.69 ||

strībālavṛddhakitava- mattonmattābhiśastakāḥ  |
raṅgāvatāripākhaṇḍi- kūṭakṛdvikalendriyāḥ  || 2.70 ||

patitāptārthasaṃbandhi- sahāyariputaskarāḥ  |
sāhasī dṛṣṭadoṣaśca nirdhūtādyāstvasākṣiṇaḥ  || 2.71 ||

ubhayānumataḥ sākṣī bhavatyeko'pi dharmavit |
sarvaḥ sākṣī saṃgrahaṇe cauryapāruṣyasāhase  || 2.72 ||

sākṣiṇaḥ śrāvayedvādi- prativādisamīpagān  |
ye pātakakṛtāṃ lokā mahāpātakināṃ tathā  || 2.73 ||

agnidānāṃ ca ye lokā ye ca strībālaghātinām  |
sa tān sarvānavāpnoti yaḥ sākṣyamanṛtaṃ vadet || 2.74 ||

sukṛtaṃ yattvayā kiṃcijjanmāntaraśataiḥ kṛtam  |
tatsarvaṃ tasya jānīhi yaṃ parājayase mṛṣā  || 2.75 ||

abruvan hi naraḥ sākṣyamṛṇaṃ sadaśabandhakam  |
rājñā sarvaṃ pradāpyaḥ syātṣaṭcatvāriṃśake'hani  || 2.76 ||

na dadāti hi yaḥ sākṣyaṃ jānannapi narādhamaḥ  |
sa kūṭasākṣiṇāṃ pāpaistulyo daṇḍena caiva hi  || 2.77 ||

dvaidhe bahūnāṃ vacanaṃ sameṣu guṇināṃ tathā  |
guṇidvaidhe tu vacanaṃ grāhyaṃ ye guṇavattamāḥ  || 2.78 ||

yasyocuḥ sākṣiṇaḥ satyāṃ pratijñāṃ sa jayī bhavet |
anyathā vādino yasya dhruvastasya parājayaḥ  || 2.79 ||

ukte'pi sākṣibhiḥ sākṣye yadyanye guṇavattamāḥ  |
dviguṇā vānyathā brūyuḥ kūṭāḥ syuḥ pūrvasākṣiṇaḥ  || 2.80 ||

pṛthakpṛthagdaṇḍanīyāḥ kūṭakṛtsākṣiṇastathā  |
vivādāddviguṇaṃ daṇḍaṃ vivāsyo brāhmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 2.81 ||

yaḥ sākṣyaṃ śrāvito'nyebhyo nihnute tattamovṛtaḥ  |
sa dāpyo'ṣṭaguṇaṃ daṇḍaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ tu vivāsayet || 2.82 ||

varṇināṃ hi vadho yatra tatra sākṣyanṛtaṃ vadet |
tatpāvanāya nirvāpyaścaruḥ sārasvato dvijaiḥ  || 2.83 ||



[6. lekhyaprakaraṇam]

yaḥ kaścidartho niṣṇātaḥ svarucyā tu parasparam  |
lekhyaṃ tu sākṣimatkāryaṃ tasmin dhanikapūrvakam  || 2.84 ||

samāmāsatadardhāhar- nāmajātisvagotrakaiḥ  |
sabrahmacārikātmīya- pitṛnāmādicihnitam  || 2.85 ||

samāpte'rthe ṛṇī nāma svahastena niveśayet |
mataṃ me'mukaputrasya yadatropari lekhitam  || 2.86 ||

sākṣiṇaśca svahastena pitṛnāmakapūrvakam  |
atrāhamamukaḥ sākṣī likheyuriti te samāḥ  || 2.87 ||

ubhayābhyarthitenaitanmayā hyamukasūnunā  |
likhitaṃ hyamukeneti lekhako'nte tato likhet || 2.88 ||

vināpi sākṣibhirlekhyaṃ svahastalikhitaṃ tu yat |
tatpramāṇaṃ smṛtaṃ lekhyaṃ balopadhikṛtādṛte  || 2.89 ||

ṛṇaṃ lekhyakṛtaṃ deyaṃ puruṣaistribhireva tu  |
ādhistu bhujyate tāvadyāvattanna pradīyate  || 2.90 ||

deśāntarasthe durlekhye naṣṭonmṛṣṭe hṛte tathā  |
bhinne dagdhe'thavā chinne lekhyamanyattu kārayet || 2.91 ||

saṃdigdhalekhyaśuddhiḥ syātsvahastalikhitādibhiḥ  |
yuktiprāptikriyācihna- saṃbandhāgamahetubhiḥ  || 2.92 ||

lekhyasya pṛṣṭhe'bhilikheddattvā dattvā rṇiko dhanam  |
dhanī vopagataṃ dadyātsvahastaparicihnitam  || 2.93 ||

dattvā rṇaṃ pāṭayellekhyaṃ śuddhyai vānyattu kārayet |
sākṣimacca bhavedyadvā taddātavyaṃ sasākṣikam  || 2.94 ||



[7. divyaprakaraṇam]

tulāgnyāpo viṣaṃ kośo divyānīha viśuddhaye  |
mahābhiyogeṣvetāni śīrṣakasthe'bhiyoktari  || 2.95 ||

rucyā vānyataraḥ kuryāditaro vartayecchiraḥ  |
vināpi śīrṣakātkuryānnṛpadrohe'tha pātake  || 2.96 ||

sacailaṃ snātamāhūya sūryodaya upoṣitam  |
kārayetsarvadivyāni nṛpabrāhmaṇasaṃnidhau  || 2.97 ||

tulā strībālavṛddhāndha- paṅgubrāhmaṇarogiṇām  |
agnirjalaṃ vā śūdrasya yavāḥ sapta viṣasya vā  || 2.98 ||

nāsahasrāddharetphālaṃ na viṣaṃ na tulāṃ tathā  |
nṛpārtheṣvabhiśāpe ca vaheyuḥ śucayaḥ sadā  || 2.99 ||

tulādhāraṇavidvadbhirabhiyuktastulāśritaḥ  |
pratimānasamībhūto rekhāṃ kṛtvāvatāritaḥ  || 2.100 ||

tvaṃ tule satyadhāmāsi purā devairvinirmitā  |
tatsatyaṃ vada kalyāṇi saṃśayānmāṃ vimocaya  || 2.101 ||

yadyasmi pāpakṛnmātastato māṃ tvamadho naya  |
śuddhaścedgamayordhvaṃ māṃ tulāmityabhimantrayet || 2.102 ||

karau vimṛditavrīherlakṣayitvā tato nyaset |
saptāśvatthasya patrāṇi tāvatsūtreṇa veṣṭayet || 2.103 ||

tvamagne sarvabhūtānāmantaścarasi pāvaka  |
sākṣivatpuṇyapāpebhyo brūhi satyaṃ kave mama  || 2.104 ||

tasyetyuktavato lauhaṃ pañcāśatpalikaṃ samam  |
agnivarṇaṃ nyasetpiṇḍaṃ hastayorubhayorapi  || 2.105 ||
sa tamādāya saptaiva maṇḍalāni śanairvrajet |
ṣoḍaśāṅgulakaṃ jñeyaṃ maṇḍalaṃ tāvadantaram  || 2.106 ||

muktvāgniṃ mṛditavrīhiradagdhaḥ śuddhimāpnuyāt |
antarā patite piṇḍe saṃdehe vā punarharet || 2.107 ||

satyena mābhirakṣa tvaṃ varuṇetyabhiśāpya kam  |
nābhidaghnodakasthasya gṛhītvorū jalaṃ vaśet || 2.108 ||

samakālamiṣuṃ muktamānīyānyo javī naraḥ  |
gate tasminnimagnāṅgaṃ paśyeccecchuddhimāpnuyāt || 2.109 ||

tvaṃ viṣa brahmaṇaḥ putraḥ satyadharme vyavasthitaḥ  |

upajīvyadrumāṇāṃ ca viṃśaterdviguṇo damaḥ  || 2.227 ||

caityaśmaśānasīmāsu puṇyasthāne surālaye  |
jātadrumāṇāṃ dviguṇo damo vṛkṣe ca viśrute  || 2.228 ||

gulmagucchakṣupalatā- pratānauṣadhivīrudhām  |
pūrvasmṛtādardhadaṇḍaḥ sthāneṣūkteṣu kartane  || 2.229 ||



[20. sāhasaprakaraṇam]

sāmānyadravyaprasabha- haraṇātsāhasaṃ smṛtam  |
tanmūlyāddviguṇo daṇḍo nihnave tu caturguṇaḥ  || 2.230 ||

yaḥ sāhasaṃ kārayati sa dāpyo dviguṇaṃ damam  |
yaścaivamuktvāhaṃ dātā kārayetsa caturguṇam  || 2.231 ||

arghyākṣepātikramakṛdbhrātṛbhāryāprahārakaḥ  |
saṃdiṣṭasyāpradātā ca samudragṛhabhedakṛt || 2.232 ||

sāmantakulikādīnāmapakārasya kārakaḥ  |
pañcāśatpaṇiko daṇḍa eṣāmiti viniścayaḥ  || 2.233 ||

svacchandavidhavāgāmī vikruṣṭe'nabhidhāvakaḥ  |
akāraṇe ca vikroṣṭā caṇḍālaścottamān spṛśet || 2.234 ||

śūdrapravrajitānāṃ ca daive pitrye ca bhojakaḥ  |
ayuktaṃ śapathaṃ kurvannayogyo yogyakarmakṛt || 2.235 ||

vṛṣakṣudrapaśūnāṃ ca puṃstvasya pratighātakṛt |
sādhāraṇasyāpalāpī dāsīgarbhavināśakṛt || 2.236 ||

pitṛputrasvasṛbhātṛ- daṃpatyācāryaśiṣyakāḥ  |
eṣāmapatitānyonya- tyāgī ca śatadaṇḍabhāk || 2.237 ||

vasānastrīn paṇān daṇḍyo nejakastu parāṃśukam  |
vikrayāvakrayādhānayāciteṣu paṇān daśa  || 2.238 ||
pitāputravirodhe tu sākṣiṇāṃ tripaṇo damaḥ  |
antare ca tayoryaḥ syāttasyāpyaṣṭaguṇo damaḥ  || 2.239 ||

tulāśāsanamānānāṃ kūṭakṛnnāṇakasya ca  |
ebhiśca vyavahartā yaḥ sa dāpyo damamuttamam  || 2.240 ||

akūṭaṃ kūṭakaṃ brūte kūṭaṃ yaścāpyakūṭakam  |
sa nāṇakaparīkṣī tu dāpya uttamasāhasam  || 2.241 ||

bhiṣaṅmithyācaran daṇḍyastiryakṣu prathamaṃ damam  |
mānuṣe madhyamaṃ rāja- puruṣeṣūttamaṃ damam  || 2.242 ||

abandhyaṃ yaśca badhnāti baddhaṃ yaśca pramuñcati  |
aprāptavyavahāraṃ ca sa dāpyo damamuttamam  || 2.243 ||

mānena tulayā vāpi yo'ṃśamaṣṭamakaṃ haret |
daṇḍaṃ sa dāpyo dviśataṃ vṛddhau hānau ca kalpitam  || 2.244 ||

bheṣajasnehalavaṇa- gandhadhānyaguḍādiṣu  |
paṇyeṣu prakṣipan hīnaṃ paṇān dāpyastu ṣoḍaśa  || 2.245 ||

mṛccarmamaṇisūtrāyaḥ- kāṣṭhavalkalavāsasām  |
ajātau jātikaraṇe vikreyāṣṭaguṇo damaḥ  || 2.246 ||

samudgaparivartaṃ ca sārabhāṇḍaṃ ca kṛtrimam  |
ādhānaṃ vikrayaṃ vāpi nayato daṇḍakalpanā  || 2.247 ||

bhinne paṇe ca pañcāśat- paṇe tu śatamucyate  |
dvipaṇe dviśato daṇḍo mūlyavṛddhau ca vṛddhimān  || 2.248 ||

saṃbhūya kurvatāmarghaṃ saṃbādhaṃ kāruśilpinām  |
arghasya hrāsaṃ vṛddhiṃ vā jānato dama uttamaḥ  || 2.249 ||

saṃbhūya vaṇijāṃ paṇyamanargheṇoparundhatām  |
vikrīṇatāṃ vā vihito daṇḍa uttamasāhasaḥ  || 2.250 ||

rājani sthāpyate yo'rghaḥ pratyahaṃ tena vikrayaḥ  |
krayo vā niḥsravastasmādvaṇijāṃ lābhakṛtsmṛtaḥ  || 2.251 ||

svadeśapaṇye tu śataṃ vaṇiggṛhṇīta pañcakam  |
daśakaṃ pāradeśye tu yaḥ sadyaḥ krayavikrayī  || 2.252 ||

paṇyasyopari saṃsthāpya vyayaṃ paṇyasamudbhavam  |
argho'nugrahakṛtkāryaḥ kreturvikretureva ca  || 2.253 ||



[21. vikrīyāsaṃpradānaprakaraṇam]

gṛhītamūlyaṃ yaḥ paṇyaṃ kreturnaiva prayacchati  |
sodayaṃ tasya dāpyo'sau diglābhaṃ vā digāgate  || 2.254 ||

vikrītamapi vikreyaṃ pūrvakretaryagṛhṇati  |
hāniścetkretṛdoṣeṇa kretureva hi sā bhavet || 2.255 ||

rājadaivopaghātena paṇye doṣamupāgate  |
hānirvikreturevāsau yācitasyāprayacchataḥ  || 2.256 ||

anyahaste ca vikrīya duṣṭaṃ vāduṣṭavadyadi  |
vikrīṇīte damastatra mūlyāttu dviguṇo bhavet || 2.257 ||

kṣayaṃ vṛddhiṃ ca vaṇijā paṇyānāmavijānatā  |
krītvā nānuśayaḥ kāryaḥ kurvan ṣaḍbhāgadaṇḍabhāk || 2.258 ||



[22 saṃbhūyasamutthānaprakaraṇam]

samavāyena vaṇijāṃ lābhārthaṃ karma kurvatām  |
lābhālābhau yathādravyaṃ yathā vā saṃvidā kṛtau  || 2.259 ||

pratiṣiddhamanādiṣṭaṃ pramādādyacca nāśitam  |
sa taddadyādviplavācca rakṣitāddaśamāṃśabhāk || 2.260 ||

arghaprakṣepaṇādviṃśaṃ bhāgaṃ śulkaṃ nṛpo haret |
vyāsiddhaṃ rājayogyaṃ ca vikrītaṃ rājagāmi tat || 2.261 ||

mithyā vadan parīmāṇaṃ śulkasthānādapāsaran  |
dāpyastvaṣṭaguṇaṃ yaśca savyājakrayavikrayī  || 2.262 ||

tarikaḥ sthalajaṃ śulkaṃ gṛhṇan dāpyaḥ paṇān daśa  |
brāhmaṇaprātiveśyānāmetadevānimantraṇe  || 2.263 ||

deśāntaragate prete dravyaṃ dāyādabāndhavāḥ  |
jñātayo vā hareyustad- āgatāstairvinā nṛpaḥ  || 2.264 ||

jihmaṃ tyajeyurnirlābhamaśakto'nyena kārayet |
anena vidhirākhyāta ṛtvikkarṣakakarmiṇām  || 2.265 ||



[23 steyaprakaraṇam]

grāhakairgṛhyate cauro loptreṇātha padena vā  |
pūrvakarmāparādhī ca tathā cāśuddhavāsakaḥ  || 2.266 ||

anye'pi śaṅkayā grāhyā jātināmādinihnavaiḥ  |
dyūtastrīpānasaktāśca śuṣkabhinnamukhasvarāḥ  || 2.267 ||

paradravyagṛhāṇāṃ ca pṛcchakā gūḍhacāriṇaḥ  |
nirāyā vyayavantaśca vinaṣṭadravyavikrayāḥ  || 2.268 ||

gṛhītaḥ śaṅkayā caurye nātmānaṃ cedviśodhayet |
dāpayitvā hṛtaṃ dravyaṃ cauradaṇḍena daṇḍayet || 2.269 ||

cauraṃ pradāpyāpahṛtaṃ ghātayedvividhairvadhaiḥ  |
sacihnaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ kṛtvā svarāṣṭrādvipravāsayet || 2.270 ||

ghātite'pahṛte doṣo grāmabharturanirgate  |
vivītabhartustu pathi cauroddharturavītake  || 2.271 ||

svasīṃni dadyādgrāmastu padaṃ vā yatra gacchati  |
pañcagrāmī bahiḥ krośāddaśagrāmyatha vā punaḥ  || 2.272 ||

bandigrāhāṃstathā vāji- kuñjarāṇāṃ ca hāriṇaḥ  |
prasahyaghātinaścaiva śūlānāropayennarān  || 2.273 ||

utkṣepakagranthibhedau karasaṃdaṃśahīnakau  |
kāryau dvitīyāparādhe karapādaikahīnakau  || 2.274 ||

kṣudramadhyamahādravya- haraṇe sārato damaḥ  |
deśakālavayaḥśakti saṃcintyaṃ daṇḍakarmaṇi  || 2.275 ||

bhaktāvakāśāgnyudaka- mantropakaraṇavyayān  |
dattvā caurasya vā hanturjānato dama uttamaḥ  || 2.276 ||

śastrāvapāte garbhasya pātane cottamo damaḥ  |
uttamo vādhamo vāpi puruṣastrīpramāpaṇe  || 2.277 ||

vipraduṣṭāṃ striyaṃ caiva puruṣaghnīmagarbhiṇīm  |
setubhedakarīṃ cāpsu śilāṃ baddhvā praveśayet || 2.278 ||

viṣāgnidāṃ patiguru- nijāpatyapramāpaṇīm  |
vikarṇakaranāsauṣṭhīṃ kṛtvā gobhiḥ pramāpayet || 2.279 ||

avijñātahatasyāśu kalahaṃ sutabāndhavāḥ  |
praṣṭavyā yoṣitaścāsya parapuṃsi ratāḥ pṛthak || 2.280 ||

strīdravyavṛttikāmo vā kena vāyaṃ gataḥ saha  |
mṛtyudeśasamāsannaṃ pṛcchedvāpi janaṃ śanaiḥ  || 2.281 ||

kṣetraveśmavanagrāma- vivītakhaladāhakāḥ  |
rājapatnyabhigāmī ca dagdhavyāstu kaṭāgninā  || 2.282 ||



[24 strīsaṃgrahaṇaprakaraṇam]

pumān saṃgrahaṇe grāhyaḥ keśākeśi parastriyā  |
sadyo vā kāmajaiścihnaiḥ pratipattau dvayostathā  || 2.283 ||

nīvīstanaprāvaraṇa- sakthikeśāvamarśanam  |
adeśakālasaṃbhāṣaṃ sahaikāsanameva ca  || 2.284 ||

strī niṣedhe śataṃ dadyāddviśataṃ tu damaṃ pumān  |
pratiṣedhe tayordaṇḍo yathā saṃgrahaṇe tathā  || 2.285 ||

sajātāvuttamo daṇḍa ānulomye tu madhyamaḥ  |
prātilomye vadhaḥ puṃso nāryāḥ karṇādikartanam  || 2.286 ||

alaṃkṛtāṃ haran kanyāmuttamaṃ hyanyathādhamam  |
daṇḍaṃ dadyātsavarṇāsu prātilomye vadhaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 2.287 ||

sakāmāsvanulomāsu na doṣastvanyathā damaḥ  |
dūṣaṇe tu karaccheda uttamāyāṃ vadhastathā  || 2.288 ||

śataṃ strīdūṣaṇe dadyāddve tu mithyābhiśaṃsane  |
paśūn gacchan śataṃ dāpyo hīnāṃ strīṃ gāṃ ca madhyamam  || 2.289 ||
avaruddhāsu dāsīsu bhujiṣyāsu tathaiva ca  |
gamyāsvapi pumān dāpyaḥ pañcāśatpaṇikaṃ damam  || 2.290 ||

prasahya dāsyabhigame daṇḍo daśapaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
bahūnāṃ yadyakāmāsau caturviṃśatikaḥ pṛthak || 2.291 ||

gṛhītavetanā veśyā necchantī dviguṇaṃ vahet |
agṛhīte samaṃ dāpyaḥ pumānapyevameva hi  || 2.292 ||

ayonau gacchato yoṣāṃ puruṣaṃ vābhimehataḥ  |
caturviṃśatiko daṇḍastathā pravrajitāgame  || 2.293 ||

antyābhigamane tvaṅkyaḥ kubandhena pravāsayet |
śūdrastathāntya eva syādantyasyāryāgame vadhaḥ  || 2.294 ||



[25 prakīrṇakaprakaraṇam]

ūnaṃ vābhyadhikaṃ vāpi likhedyo rājaśāsanam  |
pāradārikacauraṃ vā muñcato daṇḍa uttamaḥ  || 2.295 ||

abhakṣyeṇa dvijaṃ dūṣyo daṇḍya uttamasāhasam  |
madhyamaṃ kṣatriyaṃ vaiśyaṃ prathamaṃ śūdramardhikam  || 2.296 ||

kūṭasvarṇavyavahārī vimāṃsasya ca vikrayī  |
tryaṅgahīnastu kartavyo dāpyaścottamasāhasam  || 2.297 ||

catuṣpādakṛto doṣo nāpehīti prajalpataḥ  |
kāṣṭhaloṣṭeṣupāṣāṇa- bāhuyugyakṛtastathā  || 2.298 ||

chinnanasyena yānena tathā bhagnayugādinā  |
paścāccaivāpasaratā hiṃsane svāmyadoṣabhāk || 2.299 ||

śakto'pyamokṣayan svāmī daṃṣṭriṇāṃ śṛṅgiṇāṃ tathā  |
prathamaṃ sāhasaṃ dadyādvikruṣṭe dviguṇaṃ tathā  || 2.300 ||

jāraṃ cauretyabhivadan dāpyaḥ pañcaśataṃ damam  |
upajīvya dhanaṃ muñcaṃstadevāṣṭaguṇīkṛtam  || 2.301 ||

rājño'niṣṭapravaktāraṃ tasyaivākrośakāriṇam  |
tanmantrasya ca bhettāraṃ chittvā jihvāṃ pravāsayet || 2.302 ||

mṛtāṅgalagnavikreturgurostāḍayitustathā  |
rājayānāsanāroḍhurdaṇḍa uttamasāhasaḥ  || 2.303 ||

dvinetrabhedino rāja- dviṣṭādeśakṛtastathā  |
vipratvena ca śūdrasya jīvato'ṣṭaśato damaḥ  || 2.304 ||

durdṛṣṭāṃstu punardṛṣṭvā vyavahārānnṛpeṇa tu  |
sabhyāḥ sajayino daṇḍyā vivādāddviguṇaṃ damam  || 2.305 ||

yo manyetājito'smīti nyāyenāpi parājitaḥ  |
tamāyāntaṃ punarjitvā dāpayeddviguṇaṃ damam  || 2.306 ||

rājñānyāyena yo daṇḍo gṛhīto varuṇāya tam  |
nivedya dadyādviprebhyaḥ svayaṃ triṃśadguṇīkṛtam  || 2.307 ||

[Endof the vyavahārādhyāya]



[īīī. prāyaścittādhyāyah]

[1. āśaucaprakaraṇam]

ūnadvivarṣaṃ nikhanenna kuryādudakaṃ tataḥ  |
āśmaśānādanuvrajya itaro jñātibhirvṛtaḥ  || 3.1 ||

yamasūktaṃ tathā gāthā japadbhirlaukikāgninā  |
sa dagdhavya upetaścedāhitāgnyāvṛtārthavat || 3.2 ||

saptamāddaśamādvāpi jñātayo'bhyupayantyapaḥ  |
apa naḥ śośucadaghamanena pitṛdiṅmukhāḥ  || 3.3 ||

evaṃ mātāmahācārya- pretānāmudakakriyā  |
kāmodakaṃ sakhiprattā- svasrīyaśvaśurartvijām  || 3.4 ||

sakṛtprasiñcantyudakaṃ nāmagotreṇa vāgyatāḥ  |
na brahmacāriṇaḥ kuryurudakaṃ patitāstathā  || 3.5 ||

pākhaṇḍyanāśritāḥ stenā bhartṛghnyaḥ kāmagādikāḥ  |
surāpya ātmatyāginyo nāśaucodakabhājanāḥ  || 3.6 ||

kṛtodakān samuttīrṇānmṛduśādvalasaṃsthitān  |
snātānapavadeyustānitihāsaiḥ purātanaiḥ  || 3.7 ||

mānuṣye kadalīstambha- niḥsāre sāramārgaṇam  |
karoti yaḥ sa sammūḍho jalabudbudasaṃnibhe  || 3.8 ||

pañcadhā saṃbhṛtaḥ kāyo yadi pañcatvamāgataḥ  |
karmabhiḥ svaśarīrotthaistatra kā paridevanā  || 3.9 ||

gantrī vasumatī nāśamudadhirdaivatāni ca  |
phenaprakhyaḥ kathaṃ nāśaṃ martyaloko na yāsyati  || 3.10 ||

śleṣmāśru bāndhavairmuktaṃ preto bhuṅkte yato'vaśaḥ  |
ato na roditavyaṃ hi kriyāḥ kāryāḥ svaśaktitaḥ  || 3.11 ||

iti saṃśrutya gaccheyurgṛhaṃ bālapuraḥsarāḥ  |
vidaśya nimbapatrāṇi niyatā dvāri veśmanaḥ  || 3.12 ||

ācamyāgnyādi salilaṃ gomayaṃ gaurasarṣapān  |
praviśeyuḥ samālabhya kṛtvāśmani padaṃ śanaiḥ  || 3.13 ||

praveśanādikaṃ karma pretasaṃsparśināmapi  |
icchatāṃ tatkṣaṇācchuddhiḥ pareṣāṃ snānasaṃyamān  || 3.14 ||

ācāryapitṛupādhyāyānnirhṛtyāpi vratī vratī  |
saṃkaṭānnaṃ ca nāśnīyānna ca taiḥ saha saṃvaset || 3.15 ||

krītalabdhāśanā bhūmau svapeyuste pṛthakkṣitau  |
piṇḍayajñāvṛtā deyaṃ pretāyānnaṃ dinatrayam  || 3.16 ||

jalamekāhamākāśe sthāpyaṃ kṣīraṃ ca mṛnmaye  |
vaitānāupāsanāḥ kāryāḥ kriyāśca śruticodanāt || 3.17 ||

trirātraṃ daśarātraṃ vā śāvamāśaucamiṣyate  |
ūnadvivarṣa ubhayoḥ sūtakaṃ mātureva hi  || 3.18 ||

pitrostu sūtakaṃ mātustadasṛgdarśanāddhruvam  |
tadaharna praduṣyeta pūrveṣāṃ janmakāraṇāt || 3.19 ||

antarā janmamaraṇe śeṣāhobhirviśudhyati  |
garbhasrāve māsatulyā niśāḥ śuddhestu kāraṇam  || 3.20 ||

hatānāṃ nṛpagoviprairanvakṣaṃ cātmaghātinām  |
proṣite kālaśeṣaḥ syātpūrṇe dattvodakaṃ śuciḥ  || 3.21 ||

kṣatrasya dvādaśāhāni viśaḥ pañcadaśaiva tu  |
triṃśaddināni śūdrasya tadardhaṃ nyāyavartinaḥ  || 3.22 ||

ādantajanmanaḥ sadyā acūḍānnaiśikī smṛtā  |
trirātramāvratādeśāddaśarātramataḥ param  || 3.23 ||

ahastvadattakanyāsu bāleṣu ca viśodhanam  |
gurvantevāsyanūcānamātulaśrotriyeṣu ca  || 3.24 ||

anauraseṣu putreṣu bhāryāsvanyagatāsu ca  |
nivāsarājani prete tadahaḥ śuddhikāraṇam  || 3.25 ||

brāhmaṇenānugantavyo na śūdro na dvijaḥ kvacit |
anugamyāmbhasi snātvā spṛṣṭvāgniṃ ghṛtabhukśuciḥ  || 3.26 ||

mahīpatīnāṃ nāśaucaṃ hatānāṃ vidyutā tathā  |
gobrāhmaṇārthaṃ saṃgrāme yasya cecchati bhūmipaḥ  || 3.27 ||

ṛtvijāṃ dīkṣitānāṃ ca yajñiyaṃ karma kurvatām  |
satrivratibrahmacāri- dātṛbrahmavidāṃ tathā  || 3.28 ||

dāne vivāhe yajñe ca saṃgrāme deśaviplave  |
āpadyapi hi kaṣṭāyāṃ sadyaḥ śaucaṃ vidhīyate  || 3.29 ||

udakyāśucibhiḥ snāyātsaṃspṛṣṭastairupaspṛśet |
abliṅgāni japeccaiva gāyatrīṃ manasā sakṛt || 3.30 ||

kālo'gniḥ karma mṛdvāyurmano jñānaṃ tapo jalam  |
paścāttāpo nirāhāraḥ sarve'mī śuddhihetavaḥ  || 3.31 ||

akāryakāriṇāṃ dānaṃ vego nadyāśca śuddhikṛt |
śodhyasya mṛcca toyaṃ ca saṃnyāso vai dvijanmanām  || 3.32 ||

tapo vedavidāṃ kṣāntirviduṣāṃ varṣmaṇo jalam  |
japaḥ pracchannapānānāṃ manasaḥ satyamucyate  || 3.33 ||

bhūtātmanastapovidye buddherjñānaṃ viśodhanam  |
kṣetrajñasyeśvarajñānādviśuddhiḥ paramā matā  || 3.34 ||


[2. āpaddharmaprakaraṇam]

kṣātreṇa karmaṇā jīvedviśāṃ vāpyāpadi dvijaḥ  |
nistīrya tāmathātmānaṃ pāvayitvā nyasetpathi  || 3.35 ||

phalopalakṣaumasoma- manuṣyāpūpavīrudhaḥ  |
tilaudanarasakṣārān dadhi kṣīraṃ ghṛtaṃ jalam  || 3.36 ||

śastrāsavamadhūcchiṣṭaṃ madhu lākṣā ca barhiṣaḥ  |
mṛccarmapuṣpakutapa- keśatakraviṣakṣitiḥ  || 3.37 ||

kauśeyanīlalavaṇa- māṃsaikaśaphasīsakān  |
śakārdrauṣadhipiṇyāka- paśugandhāṃstathaiva ca  || 3.38 ||

vaiśyavṛttyāpi jīvanno vikrīṇīta kadācana  |
dharmārthaṃ vikrayaṃ neyāstilā dhānyena tatsamāḥ  || 3.39 ||

lākṣālavaṇamāṃsāni patanīyāni vikraye  |
pāyo dadhi ca madyaṃ ca hīnavarṇakarāṇi tu  || 3.40 ||

āpadgataḥ saṃpragṛhṇan bhuñjāno vā yatastataḥ  |
na lipyetainasā vipro jvalanārkasamo hi saḥ  || 3.41 ||

kṛṣiḥ śilpaṃ bhṛtirvidyā kusīdaṃ śakaṭaṃ giriḥ  |
sevānūpaṃ nṛpo bhaikṣamāpattau jīvanāni tu  || 3.42 ||

bubhukṣitastryahaṃ sthitvā dhānyamabrāhmaṇādharet |
pratigṛhya tadākhyeyamabhiyuktena dharmataḥ  || 3.43 ||

tasya vṛttaṃ kulaṃ śīlaṃ śrutamadhyayanaṃ tapaḥ  |
jñātvā rājā kuṭumbaṃ ca dharmyāṃ vṛttiṃ prakalpayet || 3.44 ||



[3. vānaprasthadharmaprakaraṇam]

sutavinyastapatnīkastayā vānugato vanam  |
vānaprastho brahmacārī sāgniḥ sopāsano vrajet || 3.45 ||

aphālakṛṣtenāgnīṃśca pitṝn devātithīnapi  |
bhṛtyāṃśca tarpayetśmaśru- jaṭālomabhṛdātmavān  || 3.46 ||

ahno māsasya ṣaṇṇāṃ vā tathā saṃvatsarasya vā  |
arthasya saṃcayaṃ kuryātkṛtamāśvayuje tyajet || 3.47 ||

dāntastriṣavaṇasnāyī nivṛttaśca pratigrahāt |
svādhyāyavān dānaśīlaḥ sarvasattvahite rataḥ  || 3.48 ||

dantolūkhalikaḥ kāla- pakvāśī vāśmakuṭṭakaḥ  |
śrautraṃ smārtaṃ phalasnehaiḥ karma kuryāttathā kriyāḥ  || 3.49 ||

cāndrāyaṇairnayetkālaṃ kṛcchrairvā vartayetsadā  |
pakṣe gate vāpyaśnīyānmāse vāhani vā gate  || 3.50 ||

svapyādbhūmau śucī rātrau divā saṃprapadairnayet |
sthānāsanavihārairvā yogābhyāsena vā tathā  || 3.51 ||

grīṣme pañcāgnimadhyastho varṣāsu sthaṇḍileśayaḥ  |
ārdravāsāstu hemante śaktyā vāpi tapaścaret || 3.52 ||

yaḥ kaṇṭakairvitudati candanairyaśca liṃpati  |
akruddho'parituṣṭaśca samastasya ca tasya ca  || 3.53 ||

agnīn vāpyātmasātkṛtvā vṛkṣāvāso mitāśanaḥ  |
vānaprasthagṛheṣveva yātrārthaṃ bhaikṣamācaret || 3.54 ||

grāmādāhṛtya vā grāsānaṣṭau bhuñjīta vāgyataḥ  |
vāyubhakṣaḥ prāgudīcīṃ gacchedvāvarṣmasaṃkṣayāt || 3.55 ||



[4. yatidharmaprakaraṇam]

vanādgṛhādvā kṛtveṣṭiṃ sārvavedasadakṣiṇām  |
prājāpatyāṃ tadante tānagnīnāropya cātmani  || 3.56 ||

adhītavedo japakṛtputravānannado'gnimān  |
śaktyā ca yajñakṛnmokṣe manaḥ kuryāttu nānyathā  || 3.57 ||

sarvabhūtahitaḥ śāntastridaṇḍī sakamaṇḍaluḥ  |
ekārāmaḥ parivrajya bhikṣārthī grāmamāśrayet || 3.58 ||

apramattaścaredbhaikṣaṃ sāyāhne'nabhilakṣitaḥ  |
rahite bhikṣukairgrāme yātrāmātramalolupaḥ  || 3.59 ||

yatipātrāṇi mṛdveṇu- dārvalābumayāni ca  |
salilaṃ śuddhireteṣāṃ govālaiścāvagharṣaṇam  || 3.60 ||

saṃnirudhyendriyagrāmaṃ rāgadveṣau prahāya ca  |
bhayaṃ hitvā ca bhūtānāmamṛtībhavati dvijaḥ  || 3.61 ||

kartavyāśayaśuddhistu bhikṣukeṇa viśeṣataḥ  |
jñānotpattinimittatvātsvātantryakaraṇāya ca  || 3.62 ||

avekṣyā garbhavāsāśca karmajā gatayastathā  |
ādhayo vyādhayaḥ kleśā jarā rūpaviparyayaḥ  || 3.63 ||

bhavo jātisahasreṣu priyāpriyaviparyayaḥ  |
dhyānayogena saṃpaśyetsūkṣma ātmātmani sthitaḥ  || 3.64 ||

nāśramaḥ kāraṇaṃ dharme kriyamāṇo bhavedhi saḥ  |
ato yadātmano'pathyaṃ pareṣāṃ na tadācaret || 3.65 ||

satyamasteyamakrodho hrīḥ śaucaṃ dhīrdhṛtirdamaḥ  |
saṃyatendriyatā vidyā dharmaḥ sarva udāhṛtaḥ  || 3.66 ||

niḥsaranti yathā loha- piṇḍāttaptātsphuliṅgakāḥ  |
sakāśādātmanastadvadātmānaḥ prabhavanti hi  || 3.67 ||

tatrātmā hi svayaṃ kiṃcitkarma kiṃcitsvabhāvataḥ  |
karoti kiṃcidabhyāsāddharmādharmobhayātmakam  || 3.68 ||
nimittamakṣaraḥ kartā boddhā guṇī vaśī  |
ajaḥ śarīragrahaṇātsa jāta iti kīrtyate  || 3.69 ||

sargādau sa yathākāśaṃ vāyuṃ jyotirjalaṃ mahīm  |
sṛjatyekottaraguṇāṃstathādatte bhavannapi  || 3.70 ||

āhutyāpyāyate sūryaḥ sūryādvṛṣṭirathauṣadhiḥ  |
tadannaṃ rasarūpeṇa śukratvamadhigacchati  || 3.71 ||

strīpuṃsayostu saṃyoge viśuddhe śukraśoṇite  |
pañcadhātūn svayaṃ ṣaṣṭha ādatte yugapatprabhuḥ  || 3.72 ||

indriyāṇi manaḥ prāṇo jñānamāyuḥ sukhaṃ dhṛtiḥ  |
dhāraṇā preraṇaṃ duḥkhamicchāhaṃkāra eva ca  || 3.73 ||

prayatna ākṛtirvarṇaḥ svaradveṣau bhavābhavau  |
tasyaitadātmajaṃ sarvamanāderādimicchataḥ  || 3.74 ||

prathame māsi saṃkleda- bhūto dhātuvimūrcchitaḥ  |
māsyarbudaṃ dvitīye tu tṛtīye'ṅgendriyairyutaḥ  || 3.75 ||

ākāśāllāghavaṃ saukṣmyaṃ śabdaṃ śrotraṃ balādikam  |
vāyośca sparśanaṃ ceṣṭāṃ vyūhanaṃ raukṣyameva ca  || 3.76 ||

pittāttu darśanaṃ paktimauṣṇyaṃ rūpaṃ prakāśitām  |
rasāttu rasanaṃ śaityaṃ snehaṃ kledaṃ samārdavam  || 3.77 ||

bhūmergandhaṃ tathā ghrāṇaṃ gauravaṃ mūrtimeva ca  |
ātmā gṛhṇātyajaḥ sarvaṃ tṛtīye spandate tataḥ  || 3.78 ||

dauhṛdasyāpradānena garbho doṣamavāpnuyāt |
vairūpyaṃ maraṇaṃ vāpi tasmātkāryaṃ priyaṃ striyāḥ  || 3.79 ||

sthairyaṃ caturthe tvaṅgānāṃ pañcame śoṇitodbhavaḥ  |
ṣaṣṭhe balasya varṇasya nakharomṇāṃ ca saṃbhavaḥ  || 3.80 ||

manaścaitanyayukto'sau nāḍīsnāyuśirāyutaḥ  |
saptame cāṣṭame caiva tvaṅmāṃsasmṛtimānapi  || 3.81 ||

punardhātrīṃ punargharmamojastasya pradhāvati  |
aṣṭame māsyato garbho jātaḥ prāṇairviyujyate  || 3.82 ||

navame daśame vāpi prabalaiḥ sūtimārutaiḥ  |
niḥsāryate bāṇa iva yantracchidreṇa sajvaraḥ  || 3.83 ||

tasya ṣoḍhā śarīrāṇi śaṭtvaco dhārayanti ca  |
saḍaṅgāni tathāsthnāṃ ca saha ṣaṣṭyā śatatrayam  || 3.84 ||

sthālaiḥ saha catuḥṣaṣṭirdantā vai viṃśatirnakhāḥ  |
pāṇipādaśalākāśca teṣāṃ sthānacatuṣṭayam  || 3.85 ||

ṣaṣṭyaṅgulīnāṃ dve pārṣṇyorgulpheṣu ca catuṣṭayam  |
catvāryaratnikāsthīni jaṅghayostāvadeva tu  || 3.86 ||

dve dve jānukapoloru- phalakāṃsasamudbhave  |
akṣatālūṣake śroṇī- phalake ca vinirdiśet || 3.87 ||
bhagāsthyekaṃ tathā pṛṣṭhe catvāriṃśacca pañca ca  |
grīvā pañcadaśāsthiḥ syājjatrvekaikaṃ tathā hanuḥ  || 3.88 ||

tanmūle dve lalāṭākṣi- gaṇḍe nāsā ghanāsthikā  |
pārśvakāḥ sthālakaiḥ sārdhamarbudaiśca dvisaptatiḥ  || 3.89 ||

dvau śaṅkhakau kapālāni catvāri śirasastathā  |
uraḥ saptadaśāsthīni puruṣasyāsthisaṃgrahaḥ  || 3.90 ||

gandharūparasasparśa- śabdāśca viṣayāḥ smṛtāḥ  |
nāsikā locane jihvā tvakśrotraṃ ca indriyāṇi ca  || 3.91 ||

hastau pāyurupasthaṃ ca jihvā pādau ca pañca vai  |
karmendriyāṇi jānīyānmanaścaivobhayātmakam  || 3.92 ||

nābhirojo gudaṃ śukraṃ śoṇitaṃ śaṅkhakau tathā  |
mūrdhāṃsakaṇṭhahṛdayaṃ prāṇasyāyatanāni tu  || 3.93 ||

vapā vasāvahananaṃ nābhiḥ kloma yakṛtplihā  |
kṣudrāntraṃ vṛkkakau bastiḥ purīṣādhānameva ca  || 3.94 ||

āmāśayo'tha hṛdayaṃ sthūlāntraṃ guda eva ca  |
udaraṃ ca gudau koṣṭhyau vistāro'yamudāhṛtaḥ  || 3.95 ||

kanīnike cākṣikūṭe śaṣkulī karṇapatrakau  |
karṇau śaṅkhau bhruvau danta- veṣṭāvoṣṭhau kakundare  || 3.96 ||

vaṅkṣaṇau vṛṣaṇau vṛkkau śleṣmasaṃghātajau stanau  |
upajihvāsphijau bāhū jaṅghoruṣu ca piṇḍikā  || 3.97 ||

tālūdaraṃ bastiśīrṣaṃ cibuke galaśuṇḍike  |
avaṭaścaivametāni sthānānyatra śarīrake  || 3.98 ||

akṣikarṇacatuṣkaṃ ca paddhastahṛdayāni ca  |
nava chidrāṇi tānyeva prāṇasyāyatanāni tu  || 3.99 ||

śirāḥ śatāni saptaiva nava snāyuśatāni ca  |
dhamanīnāṃ śate dve tu pañca peśīśatāni ca  || 3.100 ||

ekonatriṃśallakṣāṇi tathā nava śatāni ca  |
ṣaṭpañcāśacca jānīta śirā dhamanisaṃjñitāḥ  || 3.101 ||

trayo lakṣāstu vijñeyāḥ śmaśrukeśāḥ śarīriṇām  |
saptottaraṃ marmaśataṃ dve ca saṃdhiśate tathā  || 3.102 ||

romṇāṃ koṭyastu pañcāśaccatasraḥ koṭya eva ca  |
saptaṣaṣṭistathā lakṣāḥ sārdhāḥ svedāyanaiḥ saha  || 3.103 ||

vāyavīyairvigaṇyante vibhaktāḥ paramāṇavaḥ  |
yadyapyeko'nuvettyeṣāṃ bhāvanāṃ caiva saṃsthitim  || 3.104 ||

rasasya nava vijñeyā jalasyāñjalayo daśa  |
saptaiva tu purīṣasya raktasyāṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ  || 3.105 ||

ṣaṭśleṣmā pañca pittaṃ tu catvāro mūtrameva ca  |
vasā trayo dvau tu medo majjaikordhvaṃ tu mastake  || 3.106 ||
śleṣmaujasastāvadeva retasastāvadeva tu  |
ityetadasthiraṃ varṣma yasya mokṣāya kṛtyasau  || 3.107 ||

dvāsaptatisahasrāṇi hṛdayādabhiniḥsṛtāḥ  |
hitāhitā nāma nāḍyastāsāṃ madhye śaśiprabham  || 3.108 ||

maṇḍalaṃ tasya madhyastha ātmā dīpa ivācalaḥ  |
sa jñeyastaṃ viditveha punarājāyate na tu  || 3.109 ||

jñeyaṃ cāraṇyakamahaṃ yadādityādavāptavān  |
yogaśāstraṃ ca matproktaṃ jñeyaṃ yogamabhīpsatā  || 3.110 ||

ananyaviṣayaṃ kṛtvā manobuddhismṛtīndriyam  |
dhyeya ātmā sthito yo'sau hṛdaye dīpavatprabhuḥ  || 3.111 ||

yathāvidhānena paṭhan sāmagāyamavicyutam  |
sāvadhānastadabhyāsātparaṃ brahmādhigacchati  || 3.112 ||

aparāntakamullopyaṃ madrakaṃ prakarīṃ tathā  |
auveṇakaṃ sarobindumuttaraṃ gītakāni ca  || 3.113 ||

ṛggāthā pāṇikā dakṣa- vihitā brahmagītikā  |
geyametattadabhyāsa- karaṇānmokṣasaṃjñitam  || 3.114 ||

vīṇāvādanatattvajñaḥ śrutijātiviśāradaḥ  |
tālajñaścāprayāsena mokṣamārgaṃ niyacchati  || 3.115 ||

gītajño yadi yogena nāpnoti paramaṃ padam  |
rudrasyānucaro bhūtvā tenaiva saha modate  || 3.116 ||

anādirātmā kathitastasyādistu śarīrakam  |
ātmanastu jagatsarvaṃ jagataścātmasaṃbhavaḥ  || 3.117 ||

kathametadvimuhyāmaḥ sadevāsuramānavam  |
jagadudbhūtamātmā ca kathaṃ tasmin vadasva naḥ  || 3.118 ||

mohajālamapāsyeha puruṣo dṛśyate hi yaḥ  |
sahasrakarapannetraḥ sūryavarcāḥ sahasrakaḥ  || 3.119 ||

sa ātmā caiva yajñaśca viśvarūpaḥ prajāpatiḥ  |
virājaḥ so'nnarūpeṇa yajñatvamupagacchati  || 3.120 ||

yo dravyadevatātyāga- saṃbhūto rasa uttamaḥ  |
devān saṃtarpya sa raso yajamānaṃ phalena ca  || 3.121 ||

saṃyojya vāyunā somaṃ nīyate raśmibhistataḥ  |
ṛgyajuḥ sāmavihitaṃ sauraṃ dhāmopanīyate  || 3.122 ||

khamaṇḍalādasau sūryaḥ sṛjatyamṛtamuttamam  |
yajjanma sarvabhūtānāmaśanānaśanātmanām  || 3.123 ||

tasmādannātpunaryajñaḥ punarannaṃ punaḥ kratuḥ  |
evametadanādyantaṃ cakraṃ saṃparivartate  || 3.124 ||

anādirātmā saṃbhūtirvidyate nāntarātmanaḥ  |
samavāyī tu puruṣo mohecchādveṣakarmajaḥ  || 3.125 ||
sahasrātmā mayā yo va ādideva udāhṛtaḥ  |
mukhabāhūrupajjāḥ syustasya varṇā yathākramam  || 3.126 ||

pṛthivī pādatastasya śiraso dyaurajāyata  |
nastaḥ prāṇā diśaḥ śrotrātsparśādvāyurmukhācchikhī  || 3.127 ||

manasaścandramā jātaścakṣuṣaśca divākaraḥ  |
jaghanādantarikṣaṃ ca jagacca sacarācaram  || 3.128 ||

yadyevaṃ sa kathaṃ brahman pāpayoniṣu jāyate  |
īśvaraḥ sa kathaṃ bhāvairaniṣṭaiḥ saṃprayujyate  || 3.129 ||

karaṇairanvitasyāpi pūrvaṃ jñānaṃ kathaṃ ca na  |
vetti sarvagatāṃ kasmātsarvago'pi na vedanām  || 3.130 ||

antyapakṣisthāvaratāṃ manovākkāyakarmajaiḥ  |
doṣaiḥ prayāti jīvo'yaṃ bhavaṃ yoniśateṣu ca  || 3.131 ||

anantāśca yathā bhāvāḥ śarīreṣu śarīriṇām  |
rūpāṇyapi tathaiveha sarvayoniṣu dehinām  || 3.132 ||

vipākaḥ karmaṇāṃ pretya keṣāṃcidiha jāyate  |
iha vāmutra vaikeṣāṃ bhāvastatra prayojanam  || 3.133 ||

paradravyāṇyabhidhyāyaṃstathāniṣṭāni cintayan  |
vitathābhiniveśī ca jāyate'nyāsu yoniṣu  || 3.134 ||

puruṣo'nṛtavādī ca piśunaḥ paruṣastathā  |
anibaddhapralāpī ca mṛgapakṣiṣu jāyate  || 3.135 ||

adattādānanirataḥ paradāropasevakaḥ  |
hiṃsakaścāvidhānena sthāvareṣvabhijāyate  || 3.136 ||

ātmajñaḥ śaucavān dāntastapasvī vijitendriyaḥ  |
dharmakṛdvedavidyāvitsāttviko devayonitām  || 3.137 ||

asatkāryarato'dhīra ārambhī viṣayī ca yaḥ  |
sa rājaso manuṣyeṣu mṛto janmādhigacchati  || 3.138 ||

nidrāluḥ krūrakṛllubdho nāstiko yācakastathā  |
pramādavān bhinnavṛtto bhavettiryakṣu tāmasaḥ  || 3.139 ||

rajasā tamasā caivaṃ samāviṣṭo bhramanniha  |
bhāvairaniṣṭaiḥ saṃyuktaḥ saṃsāraṃ pratipadyate  || 3.140 ||

malino hi yathā ādarśo rūpālokasya na kṣamaḥ  |
tathāvipakvakaraṇa ātmajñānasya na kṣamaḥ  || 3.141 ||

kaṭvervārau yathāpakve madhuraḥ san raso'pi na  |
prāpyate hyātmani tathā nāpakvakaraṇe jñatā  || 3.142 ||

sarvāśrayāṃ nije dehe dehī vindati vedanām  |
yogī muktaśca sarvāsāṃ yo na cāpnoti vedanām  || 3.143 ||

ākāśamekaṃ hi yathā ghaṭādiṣu pṛthagbhavet |
tathātmā eko hyanekaśca jalādhāreṣvivāṃśumān  || 3.144 ||
brahmakhānilatejāṃsi jalaṃ bhūśceti dhātavaḥ  |
ime lokā eṣa cātmā tasmācca sacarācaram  || 3.145 ||

mṛddaṇḍacakrasaṃyogātkumbhakāro yathā ghaṭam  |
karoti tṛṇamṛtkāṣṭhairgṛhaṃ vā gṛhakārakaḥ  || 3.146 ||

hemamātramupādāya rūpaṃ vā hemakārakaḥ  |
nijalālāsamāyogātkośaṃ vā kośakārakaḥ  || 3.147 ||

kāraṇānyevamādāya tāsu tāsviha yoniṣu  |
sṛjatyātmānamātmā ca saṃbhūya karaṇāni ca  || 3.148 ||

mahābhūtāni satyāni yathātmāpi tathaiva hi  |
ko'nyathaikena netreṇa dṛṣṭamanyena paśyati  || 3.149 ||

vācaṃ vā ko vijānāti punaḥ saṃśrutya saṃśrutām  |
atītārthasmṛtiḥ kasya ko vā svapnasya kārakaḥ  || 3.150 ||

jātirūpavayovṛtta- vidyādibhirahaṃkṛtaḥ  |
śabdādiviṣayodyogaṃ karmaṇā manasā girā  || 3.151 ||

sa saṃdigdhamatiḥ karma- phalamasti na veti vā  |
viplutaḥ siddhamātmānamasiddho'pi hi manyate  || 3.152 ||

mama dārāḥ sutāmātyā ahameṣāmiti sthitiḥ  |
hitāhiteṣu bhāveṣu viparītamatiḥ sadā  || 3.153 ||

jñeyajñe prakṛtau caiva vikāre cāviśeṣavān  |
anāśakānalāghāta- jalaprapatanodyamī  || 3.154 ||

evaṃvṛtto'vinītātmā vitathābhiniveśavān  |
karmaṇā dveṣamohābhyāmicchayā caiva badhyate  || 3.155 ||

ācāryopāsanaṃ veda- śāstrārtheṣu vivekitā  |
tatkarmaṇāmanuṣṭhānaṃ saṅgaḥ sadbhirgiraḥ śubhāḥ  || 3.156 ||

stryālokālambhavigamaḥ sarvabhūtātmadarśanam  |
tyāgaḥ parigrahāṇāṃ ca jīrṇakāṣāyadhāraṇam  || 3.157 ||

viṣayendriyasaṃrodhastandrālasyavivarjanam  |
śarīraparisaṃkhyānaṃ pravṛttiṣvaghadarśanam  || 3.158 ||

nīrajastamasā sattva- śuddhirniḥspṛhatā śamaḥ  |
etairupāyaiḥ saṃśuddhaḥ sattvayogyamṛtī bhavet || 3.159 ||

tattvasmṛterupasthānātsattvayogātparikṣayāt |
karmaṇāṃ saṃnikarṣācca satāṃ yogaḥ pravartate  || 3.160 ||

śarīrasaṃkṣaye yasya manaḥ sattvasthamīśvaram  |
aviplutamatiḥ samyaksa jātisaṃsmaratāmiyāt || 3.161 ||

yathā hi bharato varṇairvarṇayatyātmanastanum  |
nānārūpāṇi kurvāṇastathātmā karmajāstanūḥ  || 3.162 ||

kālakarmātmabījānāṃ doṣairmātustathaiva ca  |
garbhasya vaikṛtaṃ dṛṣṭamaṅgahīnādi janmanaḥ  || 3.163 ||
ahaṃkāreṇa manasā gatyā karmaphalena ca  |

ākāśapavanajyotir- jalabhūtimiraistathā  || 3.172 ||

ahaṃkāraḥ smṛtirmedhā dveṣo buddhiḥ sukhaṃ dhṛtiḥ  |

pitṛyāno'javīthyāśca yadagastyasya cāntaram  |
tenāgnihotriṇo yānti svargakāmā divaṃ prati  || 3.184 ||

ye ca dānaparāḥ samyagaṣṭābhiśca guṇairyutāḥ  |
te'pi tenaiva mārgeṇa satyavrataparāyaṇāḥ  || 3.185 ||

tatrāṣṭāśītisāhasra- munayo gṛhamedhinaḥ  |
punarāvartino bīja- bhūtā dharmapravartakāḥ  || 3.186 ||

saptarṣināgavīthyantardevalokaṃ samāśritāḥ  |
tāvanta eva munayaḥ sarvārambhavivarjitāḥ  || 3.187 ||

tatastān puruṣo'bhyetya mānaso brahmalaukikān  |
karoti punarāvṛttisteṣāmiha na vidyate  || 3.194 ||

yajñena tapasā dānairye hi svargajito narāḥ  |
dhūmaṃ niśāṃ kṛṣṇapakṣaṃ dakṣiṇāyanameva ca  || 3.195 ||

pitṛlokaṃ candramasaṃ vāyuṃ vṛṣṭiṃ jalaṃ mahīm  |
kramātte saṃbhavantīha punareva vrajanti ca  || 3.196 ||

etadyo na vijānāti mārgadvitayamātmavān  |

ūrusthottānacaraṇaḥ savye nyasyottaraṃ karam  |

saṃnirudhyendriyagrāmaṃ nātinīcocchritāsanaḥ  |

tato dhyeyaḥ sthito yo'sau hṛdaye dīpavatprabhuḥ  |

antardhānaṃ smṛtiḥ kāntirdṛṣṭiḥ śrotrajñatā tathā  |
nijaṃ śarīramutsṛjya parakāyapraveśanam  || 3.202 ||

arthānāṃ chandataḥ sṛṣṭiryogasiddherhi lakṣaṇam  |
siddhe yoge tyajan dehamamṛtatvāya kalpate  || 3.203 ||

atha vāpyabhyasan vedaṃ nyastakarmā vane vasan  |
ayācitāśī mitabhukparāṃ siddhimavāpnuyāt || 3.204 ||

nyāyāgatadhanastattva- jñānaniṣṭho'tithipriyaḥ  |

tato niṣkalmaṣībhūtāḥ kule mahati bhoginaḥ  |
jāyante vidyayopetā dhanadhānyasamanvitāḥ  || 3.218 ||

vihitasyānanuṣṭhānānninditasya ca sevanāt |

prāyaścittamakurvāṇāḥ pāpeṣu niratā narāḥ  |

mahāpātakajairghorairupapātakajaistathā  |

gurūṇāmadhyadhikṣepo vedanindā suhṛdvadhaḥ  |
brahmahatyāsamaṃ jñeyamadhītasya ca nāśanam  || 3.228 ||

niṣiddhabhakṣaṇaṃ jaihmyamutkarṣe ca vaco'nṛtam  |

anāhitāgnitāpaṇya- vikrayaḥ paridevanam  || 3.234 ||

bhṛtādadhyayanādānaṃ bhṛtakādhyāpanaṃ tathā  |
pāradāryaṃ pārivittyaṃ vārdhuṣyaṃ lavaṇakriyā  || 3.235 ||

strīśūdraviṭkṣatravadho ninditārthopajīvanam  |

dhānyakupyapaśusteyamayājyānāṃ ca yājanam  |

kanyāsaṃdūṣaṇaṃ caiva parivindakayājanam  |

asacchāstrādhigamanamākareṣvadhikāritā  |
bhāryāyā vikrayaścaiṣāmekaikamupapātakam  || 3.242 ||

śiraḥkapālī dhvajavān bhikṣāśī karma vedayan  |
brahmahā dvādaśābdāni mitabhukśuddhimāpnuyāt || 3.243 ||

brāhmaṇasya paritrāṇādgavāṃ dvādaśakasya ca  |

lomabhyaḥ svāhetyevaṃ hi lomaprabhṛti vai tanum  |
majjāntāṃ juhuyādvāpi mantrairebhiryathākramam  || 3.247 ||

saṃgrāme vā hato lakṣya- bhūtaḥ śuddhimavāpnuyāt |
mṛtakalpaḥ prahārārto jīvannapi viśudhyati  || 3.248 ||

araṇye niyato japtvā trirvai vedasya saṃhitām  |
śudhyeta vā mitāśitvātpratisrotaḥ sarasvatīm  || 3.249 ||

pātre dhanaṃ vā paryāptaṃ dattvā śuddhimavāpnuyāt |
ādātuśca viśuddhyarthamiṣṭairvaiśvānarī smṛtā  || 3.250 ||

yāgasthakṣatraviḍghātī caredbrahmahaṇi vratam  |
garbhahā ca yathāvarṇaṃ tathātreyīniṣūdakaḥ  || 3.251 ||

caredvratamahatvāpi ghātārthaṃ cetsamāgataḥ  |
dviguṇaṃ savanasthe tu brāhmaṇe vratamādiśet || 3.252 ||

surāmbughṛtagomūtra- payasāmagnisaṃnibham  |
surāpo'nyatamaṃ pītvā maraṇācchuddhimṛcchati  || 3.253 ||

vālavāsā jaṭī vāpi brahmahatyāvrataṃ caret |
piṇyākaṃ vā kaṇān vāpi bhakṣayettrisamā niśi  || 3.254 ||

ajñānāttu surāṃ pītvā reto viṇmūtrameva ca  |
punaḥ saṃskāramarhanti trayo varṇā dvijātayaḥ  || 3.255 ||

patilokaṃ na sā yāti brāhmaṇī yā surāṃ pibet |
ihaiva sā śunī gṛdhrī sūkarī copajāyate  || 3.256 ||
brāhmaṇasvarṇahārī tu rājñe musalamarpayet |
svakarma vyākhyāyaṃstena hato mukto'pi vāśuciḥ  || 3.257 ||

anivedya nṛpe śudhyetsurāpavratamācaran  |
ātmatulyaṃ suvarṇaṃ vā dadyādvā vipratuṣṭikṛt || 3.258 ||

tapte'yaḥśayane sārdhamāyasyā yoṣitā svapet |

cāndrāyaṇaṃ vā trīnmāsānabhyasedvedasaṃhitām  || 3.260 ||

ebhistu saṃvasedyo vai vatsaraṃ so'pi tatsamaḥ  |
kanyāṃ samudvahedeṣāṃ sopavāsāmakiṃcanām  || 3.261 ||

cāndrāyaṇaṃ caretsarvānavakṛṣṭānnihatya tu  |
śūdro'dhikārahīnopi kālenānena śudhyati  || 3.262 ||

pañcagavyaṃ pibedgoghno māsamāsīta saṃyataḥ  |
goṣṭheśayo go'nugāmī gopradānena śudhyati  || 3.263 ||

kṛcchraṃ caivātikṛcchraṃ ca caredvāpi samāhitaḥ  |

upapātakaśuddhiḥ syādevaṃ cāndrāyaṇena vā  |

ṛṣabhaikasahasrā gā dadyātkṣatravadhe pumān  |
brahmahatyāvrataṃ vāpi vatsaratritayaṃ caret || 3.266 ||

mārjāragodhānakula- maṇḍūkāṃśca patatriṇaḥ  |

gaje nīlavṛṣāḥ pañca śuke vatso dvihāyanaḥ  |

haṃsaśyenakapikravyājjalasthalaśikhaṇḍinaḥ  |

urageṣvāyaso daṇḍaḥ paṇḍake trapu sīsakam  |

phalapuṣpānnarasaja- sattvaghāte ghṛtāśanam  |

puṃścalīvānarakharairdaṣṭaśvoṣṭrādivāyasaiḥ  |

yanme'dya reta ityābhyāṃ skannaṃ reto'bhimantrayet |

mayi teja iti chāyāṃ svāṃ dṛṣṭvāmbugatāṃ japet |

avakīrṇī bhavedgatvā brahmacārī tu yoṣitam  |
gardabhaṃ paśumālabhya nairṛtaṃ sa viśudhyati  || 3.280 ||

bhaikṣāgnikārye tyaktvā tu saptarātramanāturaḥ  |
kāmāvakīrṇa ityābhyāṃ juhuyādāhutidvayam  || 3.281 ||

upasthānaṃ tataḥ kuryātsaṃ mā siṃcantvanena tu  |

pratikūlaṃ guroḥ kṛtvā prasādyaiva viśudhyati  |

kriyamāṇopakāre tu mṛte vipre na pātakam  |

mithyābhiśaṃsino doṣo dviḥ samo bhūtavādinaḥ  |

aniyukto bhrātṛjāyāṃ gacchaṃścāndrāyaṇaṃ caret |
trirātrānte ghṛtaṃ prāśya gatvodakyāṃ viśudhyati  || 3.287 ||

trīn kṛcchrānācaredvrātya- yājako'bhicarannapi  |
vedaplāvī yavāśyabdaṃ tyaktvā ca śaraṇāgatam  || 3.288 ||

goṣṭhe vasan brahmacārī māsamekaṃ payovratam  |
gāyatrījapyanirataḥ śudhyate'satpratigrahāt || 3.289 ||

prāṇāyāmī jale snātvā kharayānoṣṭrayānagaḥ  |
nagnaḥ snātvā ca bhuktvā ca gatvā caiva divā striyam  || 3.290 ||

guruṃ huṃkṛtya tvaṃkṛtya vipraṃ nirjitya vādataḥ  |
baddhvā vā vāsasā kṣipraṃ prasādyopavaseddinam  || 3.291 ||

vipradaṇḍodyame kṛcchrastvatikṛcchro nipātane  |
kṛcchrātikṛcchro'sṛkpāte kṛcchro'bhyantaraśoṇite  || 3.292 ||

deśaṃ kālaṃ vayaḥ śaktiṃ pāpaṃ cāvekṣya yatnataḥ  |
prāyaścittaṃ prakalpyaṃ syādyatra coktā na niṣkṛtiḥ  || 3.293 ||
dāṣīkumbhaṃ bahirgrāmānninayeran svabāndhavāḥ  |
patitasya bahiḥ kuryuḥ sarvakāryeṣu caiva tam  || 3.294 ||

caritavrata āyāte ninayerannavaṃ ghaṭam  |
jugupseranna cāpyenaṃ saṃvaseyuśca sarvaśaḥ  || 3.295 ||

patitānāmeṣa eva vidhiḥ strīṇāṃ prakīrtitaḥ  |
vāso gṛhāntake deyamannaṃ vāsaḥ sarakṣaṇam  || 3.296 ||

nīcābhigamanaṃ garbha- pātanaṃ bhartṛhiṃsanam  |
viśeṣapatanīyāni strīmāmetānyapi dhruvam  || 3.297 ||

śaraṇāgatabālastrī- hiṃsakān saṃvasenna tu  |
cīrṇavratānapi sataḥ kṛtaghnasahitānimān  || 3.298 ||

ghaṭe'pavarjite jñāti- madhyastho yavasaṃ gavām  |
sa dadyātprathamaṃ gobhiḥ satkṛtasya hi satkriyā  || 3.299 ||

vikhyātadoṣaḥ kurvīta parṣado'numataṃ vratam  |

anabhikhyātadoṣastu rahasyaṃ vratamācaret || 3.300 ||

trirātropoṣito japtvā brahmahā tvaghamarṣaṇam  |
antarjale viśudhyeta dattvā gāṃ ca payasvinām  || 3.301 ||

lomabhyaḥ svāhetyatha vā divasaṃ mārutāśanaḥ  |
jale sthitvābhijuhuyāccatvāriṃśadghṛtāhutīḥ  || 3.302 ||

trirātropoṣito hutvā kūṣmāṇḍībhirghṛtaṃ śuciḥ  |

sahasraśīrṣājāpī tu mucyate gurutalpagaḥ  |
gaurdeyā karmaṇo'syānte pṛthagebhiḥ payasvinī  || 3.304 ||

prāṇāyāmaśataṃ kāryaṃ sarvapāpāpanuttaye  |

oṃkārābhiṣṭutaṃ soma- salilaṃ pāvanaṃ pibet |

niśāyāṃ vā divā vāpi yadajñānakṛtaṃ bhavet |
traikālyasaṃdhyākaraṇāttatsarvaṃ vipraṇaśyati  || 3.307 ||

na spṛśantīha pāpāni mahāpātakajānyapi  || 3.310 ||

vāyubhakṣo divā tiṣṭhan rātriṃ nītvāpsu sūryadṛk |
japtvā sahasraṃ gāyatryāḥ śudhyedbrahmavadhādṛte  || 3.311 ||

brahmacaryaṃ dayā kṣāntirdānaṃ satyamakalkatā  |

snānaṃ maunopavāsejyā- svādhyāyopasthanigrahāḥ  |
niyamā guruśuśrūṣā śaucākrodhāpramādatā  || 3.313 ||

yathākathaṃcittriguṇaḥ prājāpatyo'yamucyate  |

eṣāṃ trirātramabhyāsādekaikasya yathākramam  |

tithivṛddhyā caretpiṇḍān śukle śikhyaṇḍasammitān  |

yathākathaṃcitpiṇḍānāṃ catvāriṃśacchatadvayam  |

kṛcchrakṛddharmakāmastu mahatīṃ śriyamāpnuyāt |
