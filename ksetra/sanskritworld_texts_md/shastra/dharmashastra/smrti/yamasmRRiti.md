adhyāya 1

athāto yamadharmasya prāyaścittaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ  |
caturṇāmapi varṇānāṃ prāyaścittaṃ prakalpayet || 1.1 ||
brāhmaṇastu śunā daṣṭo jambūkena vṛkeṇa vā  |
udite grahanakṣatre dṛṣṭvā sadyaḥ śucirbhavet || 1.2 ||
jalāgnibandhanabhraṣṭāḥ pravrajyānāśakacyutāḥ  |
viṣaprapannagātrāśca śastrāghātahatāśca ye  || 1.3 ||
navaite pratyavasitāḥ sarvadharmabahiṣkṛtāḥ  |
cāndrāyaṇena śudhyanti taptakṛcchradvayena ca  || 1.4 ||
ubhayāvasitāḥ pāpā ye śāmaśabalācyutāḥ  |
indudvayena śudhyanti dattvā dhenuṃ tathā vṛṣam  || 1.5 ||
gobrāhmaṇahataṃ dagdhaṃ mṛtamudbandhanena tu  |
pāśaṃ chittvā tatastasya taptakṛcchradvayaṃ caret || 1.6 ||
kṛmibhirbrahmasaṃyuktaṃ makṣikaiścopaghātitam  |
kṛcchrārdhaṃ saṃprakurvīta śaktyā dadyāttu dakṣiṇām  || 1.7 ||
cāṇḍālabhāṇḍasaṃspṛṣṭaṃ pītvā bhūmigataṃ jalam  |
gomūtrayāvakāhāraḥ ṣaḍrātreṇa viśudhyati  || 1.8 ||
cāṇḍālaghaṭabhāṇḍasthaṃ yastoyaṃ pibati dvijaḥ  |
tatkṣanātkṣipate yastu prājāpatyena śudhyati  || 1.9 ||
yadi na kṣipate toyaṃ śarīre yasya jīryati  |
prājāpatyaṃ na dātavyaṃ kṛcchraṃ sāṃtapanādikam  || 1.10 ||
caretsāṃtapanaṃ vipraḥ prājāpatyaṃ tu kṣattriyaḥ  |
tadardhaṃ tu caredvaiśyaḥ pādaṃ śūdrasya dāpayet || 1.11 ||
cāṇḍālānnaṃ bhakṣayitvā tadvatsalilameva ca  |
māsaṃ kṛcchraṃ caredvipraścāndrāyaṇamathāpi vā  || 1.12 ||
gomūtraṃ gomayaṃ kṣīraṃ dadhi sarpiḥ kuśodakam  |
ekarātropavāsaśca kṛcchraṃ sāṃtapanaṃ smṛtam  || 1.13 ||
cāṇḍālamūrtikā ye ca ye ca saṃkīrṇayonayaḥ  |
teṣāṃ dattvā ca bhuktvā ca taptakṛcchraṃ samācaret || 1.14 ||
cāṇḍālikāsu nārīṣu dvijo maithunakārakaḥ  |
kṛtvā'ghamarṣaṇaṃ pakṣaṃ śudhyate ca payovratāt || 1.15 ||

iti śrīyāmye dharmaśāstre prathamo dhyāyaḥ ||


adhyāya 2

naṭāṃ [ṭīṃ] śailūṣikāṃ caiva rajakīṃ veṇujīvinīm  |
gatvā cāndrāyaṇaṃ kuryāttathā carmopajīvinīm  || 2.1 ||
kāpālikānnabhoktṝṇāṃ tanayāgāmināṃ tathā  |
ajñānātkṛcchramuddiṣṭaṃ jñātvā caiva vratadvayam  || 2.2 ||
surāyāḥ saṃprapānena gomāṃsabhakṣaṇe kṛte  |
taptakṛcchraṃ caredvipro mauñjīhomena śudhyati  || 2.3 ||
gokṣattriyaṃ tathā vaiśyaṃ śūdraṃ cāpyanulomajam  |
jñātvā viśeṣeṇa tataścareccāndrāyaṇaṃ vratam  || 2.4 ||
kukkuṭāṇḍakamātraṃ tu grāsaṃ ca parikalpayet |
anyathābhāvadoṣeṇa navame'ti ca śudhyahni  || 2.5 ||
ekaikaṃ vardhayedgrāsaṃ śukle kṛṣṇe ca hrāsayet |
amāyāṃ tu na bhuñjīta eṣa cāndrāyaṇo vidhiḥ  || 2.6 ||
prāyaścittamupakramya kartā yadi vipadyate  |
pūtastadaharedvāpi iha loke paratra ca  || 2.7 ||
yāvadekaḥ pṛthagbhāvyaḥ prāyaścittaṃ na sevate  |
apraśastā na te spṛśyāste sarve'pi vigarhitāḥ  || 2.8 ||
abhojyāścāpratigrāhyā asaṃpaṅktyāvivāhikāḥ  |
pūyante tu vrate cīrṇe sarve te rikthabhāginaḥ  || 2.9 ||

iti śrīyāmye dharmaśāstre dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ ||


adhyāya 3

ūnaikādaśavarṣasya pañcavarṣātparasya ca  |
prāyaścittaṃ caredbhrāta pitā vā'nyo'pi bāndhavaḥ  || 3.1 ||
ato bālatarasyāpi nāparādho na pātakam  |
rājadaṇḍo na tasyāsti prāyaścittaṃ na vidyate  || 3.2 ||
aśītyadhikavarṣāṇi bālo vā'pyūnaṣoḍaśaḥ  |
prāyaścittārdhamarhanti striyo vyādhita eva ca  || 3.3 ||
pitṛvyabhrātṛbhāryāṃ ca bhaginīṃ mātureva ca  |
śvaśrūṃ pitṛṣvasāraṃ ca taptakṛcchraṃ samācaret || 3.4 ||
rājñīmācāryaśiṣyāṃ vā upādhyāyasya yoṣitaḥ  |
etā gatvā striyo mohātṣaṇmāsaṃ kṛcchramācaret || 3.5 ||
dvau māsau bhakṣya bhojyaṃ ca dvau māsau yāvakena tu  |
dvau māsau pañcagavyena ṣaṇmāsaṃ kṛcchramācaret || 3.6 ||
mātaraṃ gurupatnīṃ ca svasāraṃ duhitāṃ tathā  |
gatvā tu praviśedagniṃ nānyā śuddhirvidhīyate  || 3.7 ||
astaṃ gate yadā sūrye cāṇḍālamṛtumatstriyaḥ  |
saṃspṛśettu yadā kaścitprāyaścittaṃ kathaṃ bhavet || 3.8 ||
jātarūpyaṃ suvarṇaṃ tu divā''hṛtaṃ ca yajjalaṃ  |
tena snātvā ca pītvā ca gāmālabhya viśudhyati  || 3.9 ||
dāsanāpitagopāla- kulamitrārdhasīriṇaḥ  |
ete śūdrāstu bhojyānnā yaścā''tmānaṃ nivedayet || 3.10 ||
asacchūdreṣu annādyaṃ ye bhuñjantyabudhā dvijāḥ  |
prāyaścittaṃ tathā prāptaṃ careccāndrāyaṇavratam  || 3.11 ||
yaḥ karotyekarātreṇa vṛṣalīsevanaṃ dvijaḥ  |
tadbhakṣaṇe japennityaṃ tribhirvarṣairvyapohati  || 3.12 ||
vṛṣalīṃ yastu gṛhṇāti brāhmaṇo madamohitaḥ  |
sadā sūtakitā tasya brahmahatyā dine dine  || 3.13 ||
vṛṣalīgamanaṃ caiva māsamekaṃ nirantaram  |
iha janmani śūdratvaṃ punaḥ śvāno bhaviṣyati  || 3.14 ||
vṛṣalīphenapītasya niḥśvāsopagatasya ca  |
tasyāṃ caiva prasūtasya niṣkṛtirna vidhīyate  || 3.15 ||
agre māhiṣakaṃ dṛṣṭvā madhye ca vṛṣalīpatim  |
ante vārdhuṣikaṃ dṛṣṭvā nirāśāḥ pitaro gatāḥ  || 3.16 ||
mahiṣītyucyate bhāryā sā caiva vyabhicāriṇī  |
tān doṣān kṣamate yastu sa vai māhiṣakaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 3.17 ||
piturgehe tu yā kanyā paśyatyasaṃskṛtā rajaḥ  |
bhrūṇahatyā pitustasyāḥ kanyā sā vṛṣalī smṛtā  || 3.18 ||
yastāṃ vivāhayetkanyāṃ brāhmaṇo madamohitaḥ  |
asaṃbhaṣyo hyapāṅkteyaḥ sa vipro vṛṣalīpatiḥ  || 3.19 ||
prāpte dvādaśame varṣe kanyāṃ yo na prayacchati  |
māsi māsi rajastasyāḥ pitā pibati śoṇitam  || 3.20 ||
aṣṭavarṣā bhavedgaurī navavarṣā ca rohiṇī  |
daśavarṣā bhavetkanyā ata ūrdhvaṃ rajasvalā  || 3.21 ||
mātā caiva pitā caiva jyeṣṭhabhrātā tathaiva ca  |
trayaste narakaṃ yānti dṛṣṭvā kanyāṃ rajasvalām  || 3.22 ||
samarghaṃ dhanamutsṛjya maha[hā]rghaṃ yaḥ prayacchati  |
sa vai vārdhuṣiko jñeyo brahmavādiṣu garhitaḥ  || 3.23 ||
śukrakṣayakarā vandhyā tyājyaiti parikīrtitā  |
tasyāstu yo bhavedbhartā taṃ tu vidyādajāvikam  || 3.24 ||
dūrācchrāntaṃ bhayagrastaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ gṛhamāgatam  |
anarcayitvā yo bhuṅkte tatkṣaṇe'sau vidhīyate  || 3.25 ||
ajāviko māhiṣaśca tathā ca vṛṣalīpatiḥ  |
tṛṇāgreṇāpi saṃspṛṣṭvā savāsā jalamāviśet || 3.26 ||
yāvaduṣṇaṃ bhavedannaṃ yāvadbhuñjanti vāgyatāḥ  |
pitarastāvadaśnanti yāvannoktā havirguṇāḥ  || 3.27 ||
havirguṇā na vaktavyāḥ pitaro yāntyatarpitāḥ  |
pitṛbhistarpitaiḥ paścādvaktavyaṃ śobhanaṃ haviḥ  || 3.28 ||
yāvato grasate grāsān havyakavyeṣvamantritaḥ  |
tāvato grasate pretya dīptān grāsānayomayān  || 3.29 ||
āsaneśvāsanaṃ dadyānna tu haste kadācana  |
hasteśvāsanadāne ca nirāśāḥ pitaro gatāḥ  || 3.30 ||
āsane pādamārūḍho vastrasyārdhamadhaḥ kṛtam  |
mukhena dhamitaṃ bhuṅkte dvijaścāndrāyaṇaṃ caret || 3.31 ||
aṅgulyāṃ yaḥ pavitrāṇi kṛtvā gandhān samarca[rpa]yet |
pitṝṇāṃ nopatiṣṭheta rākṣasairvipralupyati  || 3.32 ||
hasangrāsaṃ ca yo bhuṅkte saśabdaṃ seṅgitaṃ tathā  |
lehitaṃ vartitaṃ caiva ṣaḍete paṅktidūṣakāḥ  || 3.33 ||
śvitrī kuṣṭḥī tathā śūlī kunakhī śyāvadantakaḥ  |
rogī hīnātiriktāṅgaḥ piśuno matsarī tathā  || 3.34 ||
durbhago hi tathā ṣaṇḍhaḥ pākhaṇḍī vedanindakaḥ  |
haitukaḥ śūdrayājī ca ayājyānāṃ ca yājakaḥ  || 3.35 ||
nityaṃ pratigrahe lubdho yācako viṣayātmakaḥ  |
śyāvadanto'tha vaidyaśca asadālāpakastathā  || 3.36 ||
ete śrāddhe ca dāne ca varjanīyāḥ prayatnataḥ  |
tathā devalakaścaiva bhṛtako vedavikrayī  || 3.37 ||
ete varjyāḥ prayatnena evameva yamo'bravīt |
nirāśāḥ pitarastasya bhavanti ṛṇabhāginaḥ  || 3.38 ||
atha cenmatra[ntra]vidyukto vaiṣṇavo jñānavān hi saḥ  |
havyakavye niyoktavya iti prāha svayaṃ yamaḥ  || 3.39 ||
tasmātsarvaprayatnena śrāddhe yajñe ca karmaṇi  |
adūṣyaṃ caiva viprendraṃ yojanīyaṃ[yettu] prayatnataḥ  || 3.40 ||
tathaiva mantravidyuktaḥ śārīraiḥ paṅktidūṣanaiḥ  |
varjitaṃ ca yamaḥ prāha paṅktipāvana eva saḥ  || 3.41 ||
nirmatsaraḥ sadācāraḥ śrotriyo brahmavidyuvā  |
vidyāvinayasaṃpannaḥ pātrabhūto dvijottamaḥ  || 3.42 ||
vedāntavijjyeṣṭhasāmā alubdho veda tatparaḥ  |
yojanīyaḥ prayatnena daive pitrye ca karmaṇi  |
yadattaṃ ca hutaṃ tasmai hyanantaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 3.43 ||
ucchiṣṭocchiṣṭasaṃspṛṣṭaḥ śunā śūdreṇa vā dvijaḥ  |
upoṣya rajanīmekāṃ pañcagavyena śudhyati  || 3.44 ||
ucchiṣṭabhājanaṃ yena vipreṇa cānnavarjitam  |
spṛṣṭaṃ tena pramādācca prājāpatyaṃ samācaret || 3.45 ||
ucchiṣṭocchiṣṭasaṃspṛṣṭo brāhmaṇo brāhmaṇena hi  |
daśarudrīṃ japetpaścādgāyatryā śodhanaṃ param  || 3.46 ||
ucchiṣṭocchiṣṭasaṃspṛṣṭaḥ kṣattriyo vaiśya eva ca  |
pramādocchiṣṭasaṃspṛṣṭaḥ śūdreṇa tu yadā dvijaḥ  || 3.47 ||
upoṣya rajanīmekāṃ pañcagavyena śudhyati  |
śvānakukkuṭamārjārāḥ kāko vā spṛśate yadi  || 3.48 ||
ucchiṣṭaṃ taṃ dvijaṃ yastu ahorātreṇa śudhyati  |
pañcagavyena śuddhiḥ syādityāha bhagavān yamaḥ  || 3.49 ||
rajasvalāṃ spṛśedyastu trirātraṃ tatra kārayet |
upoṣya dvijasaṃskāraṃ pañcagavyena śudhyati  || 3.50 ||
udakyā dṛṣṭipātena śrutaśabdena caiva hi  |
snānaṃ devārcanaṃ dānaṃ havanaṃ ca praṇaśyati  || 3.51 ||
raktavastrasya vikretā lākṣārajakameva ca  |
veṇujīvanakaivarta- takṣacarmopajīvinaḥ  || 3.52 ||
eteṣāṃ sparśanātpāpaṃ tathā caiva tu mohitaḥ  |
pratigrahācca vipro vai narakaṃ pratigacchati  || 3.53 ||
udakyāḥ sparśane caiva brāhmaṇo vai pramādataḥ  |
ṣaḍrātropoṣitaḥ snātvā pañcagavyena śudhyati  || 3.54 ||
sūtake vartamāne'pi dāsavargasya kā kriyā  |
svāmitulyaṃ bhavettasya sūtakaṃ tu praśasyate  || 3.55 ||
yanna kārayate tattannānyaṃ pratyabravīdyamaḥ  |
vivāhotsavayajñeṣu kārye caivamupasthite  || 3.56 ||
rajaḥ paśyati yā nārī tasya kālasya kā kriyā  |
vipule ca jale snātvā śuklavāsāstvalaṅkṛtā  || 3.57 ||
āpo hiṣṭḥetyṛgabhiṣiktā''yaṃ gauriti vā ṛcaḥ[cā]  |
pūjānte homayetpaścādghṛtāhutyā śatāṣṭakaṃ  || 3.58 ||
gāyatryā vyāhṛtibhiśca tataḥ karma samācaret |
yāvaddvijā na cārcyante annadānahiraṇyakaiḥ  || 3.59 ||
tāvaccīrṇavratasyāpi tatpāpaṃ na praṇaśyati  || 3.60 ||
yaddehakaṃ kākabalākacillām- edhyena liptaṃ tu bhavetkadācit |
śrotre mukhe vā parimastake vā jñā[snā]nena lepopahatasya śuddhiḥ  || 3.61 ||
abhakṣyāṇāmapeyānāmalehyānāṃ ca bhakṣaṇe  |
retomūtrapurīṣāṇāṃ prāyaścittaṃ kathaṃ bhavet || 3.62 ||
padmodumbarabilvānāṃ kuśāśvatthapalāśayoḥ  |
eteṣāmudakaṃ pītvā pañcagavyena śudhyati  || 3.63 ||
strīṇāṃ rajasvalānāṃ ca sparśaścaiva bhavedyadi  |
caturṇāmapi varṇanāṃ prāyaścittaṃ kathaṃ bhavet || 3.64 ||
spṛṣṭvā rajasvalā'nyonyaṃ sagotrā cānyagotrakā  |
kāmādakāmato vā'pi trirātrāccuddhiriṣyate  || 3.65 ||
spṛṣṭvā rajasvalā'nyonyaṃ brāhmaṇī kṣattriyā tathā  |
ardhakṛcchraṃ caretpūrvā pādakṛcchraṃ tathottarā  || 3.66 ||
spṛṣṭvā rajasvalā'nyonyaṃ brāhmaṇī vaiśyinī tathā  |
pādahīnaṃ caretpūrvā pādamekaṃ tathottarā  || 3.67 ||
spṛṣṭvā rajasvalā'nyonyaṃ brāhmaṇī śūdriṇī tathā  |
kṛcchreṇa śudhyate śūdrī brāhmī dānena śudhyati  || 3.68 ||
vipraḥ spṛṣṭo niśāyāṃ tūdakyayā patitena vā  |
divā''nītena toyena snāpayedagnisaṃnidhau  || 3.69 ||
divā caivārkasaṃspṛṣṭaṃ rātrau nakṣatradarśanāt |
saṃdhyayorubhayorvā'pi pavitraṃ sarvadā jalam  || 3.70 ||

iti śrīyāmye dharmaśāstre tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ ||


adhyāya 4

khātaṃ vāpī tathā kūpa- pāṣāṇe śastraghātite  |
yaṣṭyā tu ghātite caiva mṛtpiṇḍānyeva sādhayet || 4.1 ||
govadhe caiva yatpāpaṃ balīvardasya caiva hi  |
prāyaścittaṃ bhavettatra striyā vā puruṣasya vā  || 4.2 ||
khāte ca patitā yā gauḥ kūpe vā cāvaṭe'pi vā  |
āśā[śai]vālakuḍe[ṇḍe] vā'pi śastraghātena caiva hi  || 4.3 ||
yaṣṭyā tu patitā yā gaurbalīvardo mṛto'pi vā  |
vatso vatsataro vā'pi prāyaścittī bhavennaraḥ  || 4.4 ||
nārī vā'pi kumāro vā prāyaścittādviśudhyati  |
pāpī prakhyāpayetpāpaṃ dattvā dhenuṃ tathā vṛṣam  || 4.5 ||
pracchannapāpino ye syuḥ kṛtaghnā duṣṭacāriṇaḥ  |
narakeṣu ca pacyante yāvadābhūtasaṃplavam  || 4.6 ||
tasmācca pāpinā grāhyaṃ prāyaścittaṃ yathā tathā  |
pramādācca hatā yena kapilā vā tathetarā  || 4.7 ||
yathā brahmavadhe pāpaṃ kapilāyā vadhe tathā  |
balīvarde'pi ca tathā prāyaścittaṃ samaṃ smṛtam  || 4.8 ||
rodhane bandhane caiva mṛtpiṇḍanavagomaye  |
utkṛṣṭenāpi gohantā prāyaścittena śudhyati  || 4.9 ||
muṣṭyā vā nihatā yā gauḥ śakaṭe dhā[vā]ripaṅkayoḥ  |
govartapatitā gāvaḥ śvanadyāmuttare'pi vā  || 4.10 ||
etatte kathitaṃ sarvaṃ gavāṃ ca [vi]ghātamuttamam  |
yatra yatra mriyedgauśca prāyaścittaṃ pṛthakpṛthak || 4.11 ||
vane ca patitā yā gauḥ pāmaratrāṭa [sa]śaṅktitā  |
mṛtā caiva yadā sā gauḥ prāyaścittī bhavecca saḥ  || 4.12 ||
preṣitaḥ puruṣo vā'pi prāyaścittaṃ ca yatsmṛtam  |
ābdikaṃ caiva śūdrasya vaiśyasya dviguṇaṃ bhavet || 4.13 ||
triguṇaṃ kṣattriyasyaiva viprasyaiva caturguṇam  |
goṣṭhe nivasanaṃ kāryaṃ goghno'hamiti vācayet || 4.14 ||
kaṣṭena vartamāno'pi kālenaiva śucirbhavet |
gavāṃ madhye vasedrātrau divā gā vai(?) hyanuvrajet || 4.15 ||
na strīṇāṃ vapanaṃ kuryānna ca govrajanaṃ smṛtam  |
na ca goṣṭhe vasedrātrau na kuryādvaidikīṃ śrutim  || 4.16 ||
sarvān keśān samucchritya cchedayedaṅguladvayam  |
eṣa e[vame]va tu nārīṇāṃ śiromuṇḍāpanaṃ smṛtam  || 4.17 ||
sūtake mṛtake caiva vidhiṃ prabrūhi no yama  |
jātake vartamāne'pi mṛtakaṃ ca yadā bhavet || 4.18 ||
ko vidhiḥ sa vinirdiṣṭaḥ kathayasva yathā tatham  |
evamukto hi bhagavān yamaḥ prāha yathā tatham  || 4.19 ||
jātake naiva mṛtakaṃ kṣayaṃ yāti na saṃśayaḥ  |
pūrvavratamanirdiṣṭaṃ mayā ca sūtakaṃ bhavet || 4.20 ||
sūtakena na lipyeta iti prāha svayaṃ yamaḥ  |
sūtakena na lipyeta vrataṃ saṃpūrṇatāṃ vrajet || 4.21 ||
śrāddhaṃ dānaṃ tapo yajño devatārādhanaṃ tathā  |
brahmahā ca surāpaśca svarṇasteyī gurudruhaḥ  || 4.22 ||
saṃsargī pañcamo jñeyastatsamo nātra saṃśayaḥ  |
eteṣu dvādaśābdaṃ ca prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate  || 4.23 ||
