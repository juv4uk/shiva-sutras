kātyāyanasmṛtisāroddhāraḥ
[rājaguṇāḥ]

vinītaḥ śāstrasaṃpannaḥ kośaśauryasamanvitaḥ  |
brahmaṇyo dānaśīlaḥ syātsatyadharmaparo nṛpaḥ  || 001 ||

stambhopatāpapaiśunya- cāpalakrodhavarjitaḥ  |
pragalbhaḥ sannatodagraḥ saṃbhāṣī priyadarśanaḥ  || 002 ||

vaśyendriyaṃ jitātmānaṃ dhṛtadaṇḍaṃ vikāriṣu  |
parīkṣyakāriṇaṃ dhīramatyantaṃ śrīrniṣevate  || 003 ||


[rājadharmāḥ]

śauryavidyārthabāhulyātprabhutvācca viśeṣataḥ  |
sadā cittaṃ narendrāṇāṃ mohamāyāti kāraṇāt || 004 ||

tasmāccittaṃ praboddhavyaṃ rājadharme sadā dvijaiḥ  |
pavitraṃ paramaṃ puṇyaṃ smṛtivākyaṃ na laṅghayet || 005 ||

vedadhvaniprabhāveṇa devāḥ svarganivāsinaḥ  |
te'pi tatra pramodante tṛptāstu dvijapūjanāt || 006 ||

tasmādyatnena kartavyā dvijapūjā sadā nṛpaiḥ  |
tena bhūyo'pi śakratvaṃ narendratvaṃ punaḥ punaḥ  || 007 ||

surādhyakṣaścyutaḥ svargānnṛparūpeṇa tiṣṭhati  |
kartavyaṃ tena tannityaṃ yena tattvaṃ samāpnuyāt || 008 ||

ātmīye saṃsthitā dharme nṛpāḥ śakratvamāpnuyuḥ  |
avīcivāsino ye tu vyapetācāriṇaḥ sadā  || 009 ||

gacchetsamyagavijñāya vaśaṃ krodhasya yo nṛpaḥ  |
vasetsa narake ghore kalpārdhaṃ tu na saṃśayaḥ  || 010 ||

etaireva guṇairyuktamamātyaṃ kāryacintakam   |
brāhmaṇaṃ tu prakurvīta nṛpabhaktaṃ kulodvaham  || 011 ||

mantriṇo yatra sabhyāśca vaidyāśca priyavādinaḥ  |
rājyāddharmātsukhāttatra kṣipraṃ hīyeta pārthivaḥ  || 012 ||

na tasya vacane kopameteṣāṃ tu pravartayet |
yasmādetaiḥ sadā vācyaṃ nyāyyaṃ supariniṣṭhitam  || 013 ||

yatra karmāṇi nṛpatiḥ svayaṃ paśyati dharmataḥ  |
tatra sādhusamācārā nivaseyuḥ sukhaṃ prajāḥ  || 014 ||

prajānāṃ rakṣaṇaṃ nityaṃ kaṇṭakānāṃ ca śodhanam  |
dvijānāṃ pūjanaṃ caiva etadarthaṃ kṛto nṛpaḥ  || 015 ||

bhūsvāmī tu smṛto rājā nānyadravyasya sarvadā  |
tatphalasya hi ṣaḍbhāgaṃ prāpnuyānnānyathaiva tu  || 016 ||

bhūtānāṃ tannivāsitvātsvāmitvaṃ tena kīrtitam  |
tatkriyā baliṣaḍbhāgaṃ śubhāśubhanimittajam  || 017 ||

evaṃ pravartate yastu lobhaṃ tyaktvā narādhipaḥ  |
tasya putrāḥ prajāyante rāṣṭraṃ kośaśca vardhate  || 018 ||

anyāyena hi yo rāṣṭrātkaraṃ daṇḍaṃ ca pārthivaḥ  |
sasyabhāgaṃ ca śulkaṃ cāpyādadīta sa pāpabhāk || 019 ||

arthaśāstroktamutsṛjya dharmaśāstroktamāvrajet || 020 ||

duṣṭasyāpi narendrasya tadrāṣṭraṃ na vināśayet |
na prajānumato yasmādanyāyeṣu pravartate  || 021 ||

akleśenārthine yastu rājā samyaṅnivedayet |
tattārayatyanantaṃ syāddharmārthaṃ dānamīdṛśam  || 022 ||

nyāyenākramya yallabdhaṃ ripuṃ nirjitya pārthivaiḥ  |
tacchuddhaṃ tatpradeyaṃ tannānyathopahṛtaṃ kvacit || 023 ||

rājā purohitaṃ kuryāduditaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ hitam   |
kṛtādhyayanasaṃpannamalubdhaṃ satyavādinam  || 024 ||


[vyavahāralakṣaṇādi]

prayatnasādhye vicchinne dharmākhye nyāyavistare  |
sādhyamūlastu yo vādo vyavahāraḥ sa ucyate  || 025 ||

vi nānārthe'va saṃdehe haraṇaṃ hāra ucyate  |
nānāsaṃdehaharaṇādvyavahāra iti smṛtaḥ  || 026 ||

na rājā tu viśitvena dhanalobhena vā punaḥ  |
svayaṃ kāryāṇi kurvīta narāṇāmavivādinām  || 027 ||

utpādayati yo hiṃsāṃ deyaṃ vā na prayacchati  |
yācamānaya dauḥśīlyādākṛṣyo'sau nṛpājñayā  || 028 ||

dvipade sādhyabhedāttu padāṣṭādaśatāṃ gate  |
aṣṭādaśa kriyābhedādbhinnānyaṣṭasahasraśaḥ  || 029 ||

sādhyavādasya mūlaṃ syādvādinā yanniveditam  |
deyāpradānaṃ hiṃsā cetyutthānadvayamucyate  || 030 ||

pūrvapakṣaścottaraṃ ca pratyākalitameva ca  |
kriyāpādaśca tenāyaṃ catuṣpātsamudāhṛtaḥ  || 031 ||

dharmaśāstrārthaśāstre tu skandhadvayamudāhṛtam  |
jayaścaivāvasāyaśca dve phale samudāhṛte  || 032 ||

śāstreṇa ninditaṃ tvartha- mukhyo rājñā pracoditaḥ   |
āvedayati yaḥ pūrvaṃ stobhakaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 033 ||

nṛpeṇaiva niyukto yaḥ padadoṣamavekṣitum  |
nṛpasya sūcayejjñātvā sūcakaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 034 ||


[dharmavyavahāracaritrarājaśāsanādīṇāṃ balābalavicāraḥ]

doṣakārī tu kartṛtvaṃ dhanasvāmī svakaṃ dhanam  |
vivāde prāpnuyādyatra dharmeṇaiva sa nirṇayaḥ  || 035 ||

smṛtiśāstraṃ tu yatkiṃcitprathitaṃ dharmasādhakaiḥ  |
kāryāṇāṃ nirṇayārthe tu vyavahāraḥ smṛto hi saḥ  || 036 ||

yadyadācaryate yena dharmyaṃ vādharmyameva vā  |
deśasyācaraṇānnityaṃ caritraṃ tatprakīrtitam  || 037 ||

nyāyaśāstrāvirodhena deśadṛṣṭestathaiva ca  |
yaṃ dharmaṃ sthāpayedrājā nyāyyaṃ tadrājaśāsanam  || 038 ||

yuktiyuktaṃ tu kāryaṃ syāddivyaṃ yatra vivarjitam  |
dharmastu vyavahāreṇa bādhyate tatra nānyathā  || 039 ||

pratilomaprasūteṣu tathā durganivāsiṣu  |
viruddhaṃ niyataṃ prāhustaṃ dharmaṃ na vicālayet || 040 ||

nirṇayaṃ tu yadā kuryāttena dharmeṇa pārthivaḥ  |
vyavahāraścaritreṇa tadā tenaiva bādhyate  || 041 ||

viruddhaṃ nyāyato yattu caritraṃ kalpyate nṛpaiḥ   |
evaṃ tatra nirasyeta caritraṃ tu nṛpājñayā  || 042 ||

anena vidhinā yuktaṃ bādhakaṃ yadyaduttaram  |
anyathābādhanaṃ yatra tatra dharmo vihanyate  || 043 ||

asvargyā lokanāśāya parānīkabhayāvahā  |
āyurbījaharī rājñāṃ sati vākye svayaṃ kṛtiḥ  || 044 ||

tasmācchāstrānusāreṇa rājā kāryāṇi sādhayet |
vākyābhāve tu sarveṣāṃ deśadṛṣṭena sannayet || 045 ||

yasya deśasya yo dharmaḥ pravṛttaḥ sārvakālikaḥ  |
śrutismṛtyavirodhena deśadṛṣṭaḥ sa ucyate  || 046 ||

deśapattanagoṣṭheṣu puragrāmeṣu vāsinām  |
teṣāṃ svasamayairdharma- śāstrato'nyeṣu taiḥ saha  || 047 ||

deśasyānumatenaiva vyavasthā yā nirūpitā  |
likhitā tu sadā dhāryā mudritā rājamudrayā  || 048 ||

śāstravadyatnato rakṣyā tāṃ nirīkṣya vinirṇayet |
naigamasthaistu yatkāryaṃ likhitaṃ yadvyavasthitam  || 049 ||

tasmāttatsaṃpravarteta nānyathaiva pravartayet |
pramāṇadeśadṛṣṭaṃ tu yadevamiti niścitam  || 050 ||

aprvṛttaṃ kṛtaṃ yatra śrutismṛtyanumoditam   |
nānyathā tatpunaḥ kāryaṃ nyāyāpetaṃ vivarjayet || 051 ||


[dharmādhikaraṇam]

dharmaśāstravicāreṇa mūlasāravivecanam  |
yatrādhikriyate sthāne dharmādhikaraṇaṃ hi tat || 052 ||

prātarutthāya nṛpatiḥ śaucaṃ kṛtvā samāhitaḥ  |
guruṃ jyotirvidaṃ vaidyān devān viprān purohitān  || 053 ||

yathārhametān saṃpūjya supuṣpābharaṇāmbaraiḥ  |
abhivandya ca gurvādīn sumukhāṃ praviśetsabhām  || 054 ||

vinītaveṣo nṛpatiḥ sabhāṃ gatvā samāhitaḥ  |
āsīnaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ sthitvā paśyetkāryāṇi kāryiṇām  |
saha traividyavṛddhaiśca mantrajñaiścaiva mantribhiḥ  || 055 ||

saprāḍvivākaḥ sāmātyaḥ sabrāhmaṇapurohitaḥ  |
sasabhyaḥ prekṣako rājā svarge tiṣṭhati dharmataḥ  || 056 ||

saha sabhyaiḥ sthirairyuktaiḥ prājñairmaulairdvijottamaiḥ  |
dharmaśāstrārthakuśalairarthaśāstraviśāradaiḥ  || 057 ||

kulaśīlavayovṛtta- vittavadbhiramatsaraiḥ  |
vaṇigbhiḥ syātkatipayaiḥ kulabhūtairadhiṣṭhitam  || 058 ||

śrotāro vaṇijastatra kartavyā nyāyadarśinaḥ   || 059 ||

[kāryadarśanakālaḥ]

sabhāsthāneṣu pūrvāhṇe kāryāṇāṃ nirṇayaṃ nṛpaḥ  |
kuryācchāstrapraṇītena mārgeṇāmitrakarṣaṇaḥ  || 060 ||

divasasyāṣṭamaṃ bhāgaṃ muktvā kālatrayaṃ tu yat |
sa kālo vyavahārāṇāṃ śāstradṛṣṭaḥ paraḥ smṛtaḥ  || 061 ||

ādyādahno'ṣṭabhāgādyadūrdhvaṃ bhāgatrayaṃ bhavet |
sa kālo vyavahārasya śāstre dṛṣṭo manīṣibhiḥ  || 062 ||


[prāḍvivākaḥ]

yadā kuryānna nṛpatiḥ svayaṃ kāryavinirṇayam  |
tadā tatra niyuñjīta brāhmaṇaṃ śāstrapāragam  || 063 ||

dakṣaṃ kulīnamadhyasthamanudvegakaraṃ sthiram  |
paratra bhīruṃ dharmiṣṭhamudyuktaṃ krodhavarjitam  || 064 ||

akrūro madhuraḥ snigdhaḥ kṣamāyāto vicakṣaṇaḥ  |
utsāhavānalubdhaśca vāde yojyo nṛpeṇa tu  || 065 ||

ekaśāstramadhīyāno na vidyātkāryaniścayam  |
tasmādbahvāgamaḥ kāryo vivādeṣūttamo nṛpaiḥ  || 066 ||

brāhmaṇo yatra na syāttu kṣatriyaṃ tatra yojayet  |
vaiśyaṃ vā dharmaśāstrajñaṃ śūdraṃ yatnena varjayet || 067 ||

ato'nyairyatkṛtaṃ kāryamanyāyena kṛtaṃ tu tat |
niyuktairapi vijñeyaṃ daivādyadyapi śāstrataḥ  || 068 ||

vyavahārāśritaṃ praśnaṃ pṛcchati prāṅiti sthitiḥ  |
vivecayati yastasmin prāḍvivākastataḥ smṛtaḥ  || 069 ||

anirṇīte tu yadyarthe saṃbhāṣeta raho'rthinā  |
prāḍvivāko'tha daṇḍyaḥ syātsabhyāścaiva viśeṣataḥ  || 070 ||


[sabhyāḥ]

alubdhā dhanavantaśca dharmajñāḥ satyavādinaḥ  |
sarvaśāstrapravīṇāśca sabhyāḥ kāryā dvijottamāḥ  || 071 ||

nyāyaśāstramatikramya sabhyairyatra viniścitam  |
tatra dharmo hyadharmeṇa hato hanti na saṃśayaḥ  || 072 ||

yatra dharmo hyadharmeṇa satyaṃ yatrānṛtena ca  |
hanyate prekṣamāṇānāṃ hatāstatra sabhāsadaḥ  || 073 ||

adharmataḥ pravṛttaṃ tu nopekṣeran sabhāsadaḥ  |
upekṣamāṇāḥ sanṛpā narakaṃ yāntyadhomukhāḥ  || 074 ||

anyāyenāpi taṃ yāntaṃ ye'nuyānti sabhāsadaḥ   |
te'pi tadbhāginastasmādbodhanīyaḥ sa tairnṛpaḥ  || 075 ||

nyāyamārgādapetaṃ tu jñātvā cittaṃ mahīpateḥ  |
vaktavyaṃ tatpriyaṃ tatra na sabhyaḥ kilviṣī bhavet || 076 ||

sabhyenāvaśyavaktavyaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ vacaḥ  |
śṛṇoti yadi no rājā syāttu sabhyastato'naghaḥ  || 077 ||

adharmāya yadā rājā niyuñjīta vivādinām  |
vijñāpya nṛpatiṃ sabhyastadā kāryaṃ nivartayet || 078 ||

snehādajñānato vāpi lobhādvā mohato'pi vā  |
tatra sabhyo'nyathāvādī daṇḍyo'sabhyaḥ smṛto hi saḥ  || 079 ||

kāryasya nirṇayaṃ samyagjñātvā sabhyastato vadet |
anyathā naiva vaktavyaṃ vaktā dviguṇadaṇḍabhāk || 080 ||

sabhyadoṣāttu yannaṣṭaṃ deyaṃ sabhyena tattadā  |
kāryaṃ tu kāryiṇāmeva niścitaṃ na vicālayet || 081 ||


[kāryanirṇetṝṇāṃ gurulāghavam]

kulāni śreṇayaścaiva gaṇastvadhikṛto nṛpaḥ  |
pratiṣṭhā vyavahārāṇāṃ gurvebhyastūttarottaram  || 082 ||

tapasvināṃ tu kāryāṇi traividyaireva kārayet  |
māyāyogavidāṃ caiva na svayaṃ kopakāraṇāt || 083 ||

samyagvijñānasaṃpanno nopadeśaṃ prakalpayet |
utkṛṣṭajātiśīlānāṃ gurvācāryatapasvinām  || 084 ||

gotrasthitistu yā teṣāṃ kramādāyāti dharmataḥ  |
kuladharmaṃ tu taṃ prāhuḥ pālayettaṃ tathaiva tu  || 085 ||


[praśnaprakāraḥ]

kāle kāryārthinaṃ pṛcchetpraṇataṃ purataḥ sthitam  |
kiṃ kāryaṃ kā ca te pīḍā mā bhaiṣīrbrūhi mānava  || 086 ||

kena kasmin kadā kasmātpṛcchedevaṃ sabhāgataḥ  |
evaṃ pṛṣṭaḥ sa yadbrūyāttatsabhyairbrāhmaṇaḥ sahaḥ  || 087 ||

vimṛśya kāryaṃ nyāyyaṃ cedāhvānārthamataḥ param  |
mudrāṃ vā nikṣipettasmin puruṣaṃ vā samādiśet || 088 ||


[pratinidhiḥ]

samarpito'rthinā yo'nyaḥ paro dharmādhikāriṇi  |
prativādī sa vijñeyaḥ pratipannaśca yaḥ svayam  || 089 ||

adhikāro'bhiyuktasya netarasyāstyasaṅgateḥ   |
itaro'pyabhiyuktena pratirodhikṛto mataḥ  || 090 ||

arthinā saṃniyukto vā pratyarthiprahito'pi vā  |
yo yasyārthe vivadate tayorjayaparājayau  || 091 ||

dāsāḥ karmakarāḥ śiṣyā niyuktā bāndhavāstathā  |
vādino na ca daṇḍyāḥ syuḥ yastvato'nyaḥ sa daṇḍabhāk || 092 ||

brahmahatyāsurāpāna- steyagurvaṅganāgame  |
anyeṣu cātipāpeṣu prativādī na dīyate  || 093 ||

manuṣyamāraṇe steye paradārābhimarśane  |
abhakṣyabhakṣaṇe caiva kanyāharaṇadūṣaṇe  || 094 ||

pāruṣye kūṭakaraṇe nṛpadrohe tathaiva ca  |
prativādī na dātavyaḥ kartā tu vivadetsvayam  || 095 ||


[āhvānaṃ]

dharmotsukānabhyudaye rogiṇo'tha jaḍānapi  |
asvasthamattonmattārta- striyo nāhvānayennṛpaḥ  || 096 ||
na hīnapakṣāṃ yuvatiṃ kule jātāṃ prasūtikām   |
sarvavarṇottamāṃ kanyāṃ tā jñātiprabhukāḥ smṛtāḥ  || 097 ||

tadadhīnakuṭumbinyaḥ svairiṇyo gaṇikāśca yāḥ  |
niṣkulā yāśca patitāstāsāmāhvānamiṣyate  || 098 ||

saśastro'nuttarīyo vā muktakeśaḥ sahāsanaḥ  |
vāmahastena vā vādaṃ vadan daṇḍamavāpnuyāt || 099 ||

āhūtastvavamanyeta yaḥ śakto rājaśāsanam  |
tasya kuryānnṛpo daṇḍaṃ vidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā  || 100 ||

hīne karmāṇi pañcāśan- madhyame dviśatāvaraḥ  |
gurukāryeṣu daṇḍaḥ syānnityaṃ pañcaśatāvaraḥ  || 101 ||

kalpito yasya yo daṇḍastvaparādhasya yatnataḥ  |
paṇānāṃ grahaṇaṃ tu syāttanmūlyaṃ vātha rājani  || 102 ||


[āsedhaḥ]

utpādayati yo hiṃsāṃ deyaṃ vā na prayacchati  |
yācamānāya dauḥśīlyādākṛṣyo'sau nṛpājñayā  || 103 ||

āvedya tu nṛpe kāryamasaṃdigdhe pratiśrute  |
tadāsedhaṃ prayuñjīta yāvadāhvānadarśanam  || 104 ||

āsedhayogya āsiddha utkrāman daṇḍamarhati   || 105 ||

[anāsedhyāḥ]

yastvindriyanirodhena vyāhārocchvasanādibhiḥ  |
āsedhayedanāsedhyaṃ sa daṇḍyo na tvatikramī  || 106 ||

vṛkṣaparvatamārūḍhā hastyaśvarathanausthitāḥ  |
viṣamasthāśca te sarve nāsedhyāḥ kāryasādhakaiḥ  || 107 ||

vyādhyārtā vyasanasthāśca yajamānāstathaiva ca  |
anuttīrṇāśca nāsedhyā mattonmattajaḍāstathā  || 108 ||

na karṣako bījakāle senākāle tu sainikaḥ  |
pratijñāya prayātaśca kṛtakālaśca nāntarā  || 109 ||

udyuktaḥ karṣakaḥ sasye toyasyāgamane tathā  |
ārambhātsaṃgrahaṃ yāvattatkālaṃ na vivādayet |
āsedhayaṃstvanāsedhyaṃ rajñā śāsya iti sthitiḥ  || 110 ||

abhiyuktaśca ruddhaśca tiṣṭheyuśca nṛpājñayā  |
na tasyānyena kartavyamabhiyuktaṃ vidurbudhāḥ  || 111 ||

ekāhadvyāhādyapekṣaṃ deśakālādyapekṣayā  |
dūtāya sādhite kārye tena bhaktaṃ pradāpayet || 112 ||

deśakālavayaḥśaktyādy- apekṣaṃ bhojanaṃ smṛtam   |
ākārakasya sarvatra iti tattvavido viduḥ  || 113 ||


[pratibhūtvenāgrāhyāḥ]

na svāmī na ca vai śatruḥ svāminādhikṛtastathā  |
niruddho daṇḍitaścaiva saṃśayasthāśca na kvacit || 114 ||

naiva rikthī na riktaśca na caivātyantavāsinaḥ  |
rājakāryaniyuktaśca ye ca pravrajitā narāḥ  || 115 ||

nāśakto dhanine dātuṃ daṇḍaṃ rājñe ca tatsamam  |
jīvan vāpi pitā yasya tathaivecchāpravartakaḥ  |
nāvijñāto grahītavyaḥ pratibhūtvakriyāṃ prati  || 116 ||

atha cetpratibhūrnāsti vādayogyasya vādinaḥ  |
sa rakṣito dinasyānte dadyāddūtāya vetanam  || 117 ||

dvijātiḥ pratibhūhīno rakṣyaḥ syādbāhyacāribhiḥ  |
śūdrādīn pratibhūhīnān bandhayennigaḍena tu  || 118 ||

atikrame'payāte ca daṇḍayettaṃ paṇāṣṭakam  |
nityakarmāparodhastu kāryaḥ sarvavarṇinām  || 119 ||

grahītagrahaṇo nyāye na pravartyo mahībhṛtā  |
tasya vā tatsamarpyaṃ syātsthāpayedvā parasya tat || 120 ||


[abhiyoktrādīnāmuktikramaḥ]

tatrābhiyoktā prāgbrūyādabhiyuktastvanantaram  |
tayorante sadasyāstu prāḍvivākastataḥ param  || 121 ||

yasya syādadhikā pīḍā kāryaṃ vāpyadhikaṃ bhavet |
pūrvapakṣo bhavettasya na yaḥ pūrvaṃ nivedayet || 122 ||

yasya vārthagatā pīḍā śārīrī vādhikā bhavet |
tasyārthivādo dātavyo na yaḥ pūrvaṃ nivedayet || 123 ||


[pratijñāsvarūpam]

niveśya kālaṃ varṣaṃ ca māsaṃ pakṣaṃ tirthi tathā  |
velāṃ pradeśaṃ viṣayaṃ sthānaṃ jātyākṛtī vayaḥ  || 124 ||

sādhyapramāṇaṃ dravyaṃ ca saṃkhyāṃ nāma tathātmanaḥ  |
rājñāṃ ca kramaśo nāma nivāsaṃ sādhyanāma ca  || 125 ||

kramātpitṝṇāṃ nāmāni pīḍāṃ cāhartṛdāyakau  |
kṣamāliṅgāni cānyāni pakṣaṃ saṃkīrtya kalpayet || 126 ||

deśaścaiva tathā sthānaṃ saṃniveśastathaiva ca  |
jātiḥ saṃjñā nivāsaśca pramāṇaṃ kṣetranāma ca  || 127 ||

pitṛpaitāmahaṃ caiva pūrvarājānukīrtanam  |
sthāvareṣu vivādeṣu daśaitāni niveśayet || 128 ||

rāgādīnāṃ yadekena kopitaḥ karaṇe vadet |
tadomiti likhetsarvaṃ vādinaḥ phalakādiṣu  || 129 ||

adhikān śodhayedarthānnyūnāṃśca pratipūrayet |
bhūmau niveśayettāvadyāvatpakṣaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ  || 130 ||

pūrvapakṣaṃ svabhāvoktaṃ prāḍvivāko'bhilekhayet |
pāṇḍulekhena phalake tataḥ patre viśodhitam  || 131 ||

anyaduktaṃ likhedanyadyo'rthipratyarthināṃ vacaḥ  |
cauravacchāsayettaṃ tu dhārmikaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ  || 132 ||

sollekhanaṃ vā labhate tryahaṃ saptāhameva vā  |
matirutpadyate yāvadvivāde vaktumicchataḥ  || 133 ||

yasmātkāryasamārambhāccirāttena viniścayaḥ   |
tasmātna labhate kālamabhiyuktastu kālabhāk || 134 ||

matirnotsahate yatra vivāde kāryamicchatoḥ  |
dātavyastatra kālaḥ syādarthipratyarthinorapi  || 135 ||


[pratijñādoṣāḥ : pūrvapakṣadoṣāḥ]

yaśca rāṣṭraviruddhaśca yaśca rājñā vivarjitaḥ  |
anekapadasaṃkīrṇaḥ pūrvapakṣo na siddhyati  || 136 ||

bahupratijñaṃ yatkāryaṃ vyavahāreṣu niścitam  |
kāmaṃ tadapi gṛhṇīyādrājā tattvabubhutsayā  || 137 ||

deśakālavihīnaśca dravyasaṃkhyāvivarjitaḥ  |
sādhyapramāṇahīnaśca pakṣo'nādeya iṣyate  || 138 ||

nyāyasthaṃ necchate kartumanyāyaṃ vā karotyayam  |
na lekhayati yattvevaṃ tasya pakṣo na sidhyati  || 139 ||

aprasiddhaṃ nirābādhaṃ nirarthaṃ niṣprayojanam  |
asādhyaṃ vā viruddhaṃ vā pakṣābhāsaṃ vivarjayet || 140 ||

pratijñādoṣanirmuktaṃ sādhyaṃ satkāraṇānvitam   |
niścitaṃ lokasiddhaṃ ca pakṣaṃ pakṣavido viduḥ  || 141 ||

svalpākṣaraḥ prabhūtārtho niḥsaṃdigdho nirākulaḥ  |
virodhikāraṇairmukto virodhipratiṣedhakaḥ  || 142 ||

yadā tvevaṃ vidhaḥ pakṣaḥ kalpitaḥ pūrvavādinā  |
dadyāttatpakṣasaṃbaddhaṃ prativādī tadottaram  || 143 ||

śrāvyamāṇo'rthinā yatra yo hyartho na vighātitaḥ  |
dānakāle'thavā tūṣṇīṃ sthitaḥ so'rtho'numoditaḥ  || 144 ||


[uttaraṃ sadyo dātavyaṃ kālāntareṇa vā dātavyam]

śrutvā lekhyagataṃ tvarthaṃ pratyarthī kāraṇādyadi  |
kālaṃ vivāde yāceta tasya deyo na saṃśayaḥ  || 145 ||

sadyo vaikāhapañcāha- tryahaṃ vā gurulāghavāt |
labhetāsau tripakṣaṃ vā saptāhaṃ vā ṛṇādiṣu  || 146 ||

kālaṃ śaktiṃ viditvā tu kāryāṇāṃ ca balābalam  |
alpaṃ vā bahu vā kālaṃ dadyātpratyarthine prabhuḥ  || 147 ||

dinaṃ māsārdhamāsau vā ṛtuḥ saṃvatsaro'pi vā   |
kriyāsthityanurūpastu deyaṃ kālaḥ pareṇa tu  || 148 ||

vyapaiti gauravaṃ yatra vināśastyāga eva vā  |
kālaṃ tatra na kurvīta kāryamātyayikaṃ hi tat || 149 ||

dhenāvanaḍuhi kṣetre strīṣu prajanane tathā  |
nyāse yācitake datte tathaiva krayavikraye  || 150 ||

kanyāyā dūṣaṇe steye kalahe sāhase nidhau  |
upadhau kauṭasākṣye ca sadya eva vivādayet || 151 ||

sāhasasteyapāruṣya- go'bhiśāpe tathātyaye  |
bhūmau vivādayetkṣipramakāle'pi bṛhaspatiḥ  || 152 ||

sadyaḥ kṛteṣu kāryeṣu sadya eva vivādayet |
kālātīteṣu vā kālaṃ dadyātpratyarthine prabhuḥ  || 153 ||

sadyaḥ kṛte sadya eva māsātīte dinaṃ bhavet |
ṣaḍābdike trirātraṃ syātsaptāhaṃ dvādaśābdike  || 154 ||

viṃśatyabde daśāhaṃ tu māsārdhaṃ vā labheta saḥ  |
māsaṃ triṃśatsamātīte tripakṣaṃ parato bhavet || 155 ||

kālaṃ saṃvatsarādarvāksvayameva yathepsitam  |
saṃvatsaraṃ jaḍonmatta- manaske vyādhipīḍite  || 156 ||

digantaraprapanne vā ajñātārthe ca vastuni   |
mūlaṃ vā sākṣiṇo vātha paradeśe sthitā yadā  || 157 ||

tatra kālo bhavetpuṃsāmā svadeśasamāgamāt |
datte'pi kāle deyaṃ syātpunaḥ kāryasya gauravāt || 158 ||

pūrvapakṣaśrutārthastu pratyarthī tadanantaram  |
pūrvapakṣārthasaṃbandhaṃ pratipakṣaṃ nivedayet || 159 ||

ācāradravyadāneṣṭa- kṛtyopasthānanirṇaye  |
nopasthito yadā kaścicchalaṃ tatra na kārayet || 160 ||

daivarājakṛto doṣastasmin kāle yadā bhavet |
abādhatyāgamātreṇa na bhavetsa parājitaḥ  || 161 ||

daivarājakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ sākṣibhiḥ pratipādayet |
jaihmyena vartamānasya daṇḍo dāpyastu taddhanam  || 162 ||

abhiyukto'bhiyoktāramabhiyuñjīta karhicit |
anyatra daṇḍapāruṣya- steyasaṃgrahaṇātyayāt || 163 ||

yāvan yasmin samācāraḥ pāraṃparyakramāgataḥ  |
taṃ pratīkṣya yathānyāyamuttaraṃ dāpayennṛpaḥ  || 164 ||


[caturvidhamuttaram]

satyaṃ mithyottaraṃ caiva pratyavaskandanaṃ tathā  |
pūrvanyāyavidhiścaivamuttaraṃ syāccaturvidham  || 165 ||

śrutvā bhāṣārthamanyastu yadā taṃ pratiṣedhati  |
arthataḥ śabdato vāpi mithyā tajjñeyamuttaraṃ  || 166 ||

abhiyukto'bhiyogasya yadi kuryāttu nihnavam   |
mithyā tattu vijānīyāduttaraṃ vyavahārataḥ  || 167 ||

sādhyasya satyavacanaṃ pratipattirudāhṛtā  || 168 ||

mithyaitannābhijānāmi tadā tatra na saṃnidhiḥ  |
ajātaścāsmi tatkāla iti mithyā caturvidham  || 169 ||

yo'rthinārthaḥ samuddiṣṭaḥ pratyarthī yadi taṃ tathā  |
prapadya kāraṇaṃ brūyādādharyaṃ gururabravīt || 170 ||

ācāreṇāvasanno'pi punarlekhayate yadi  |
so'bhidheyo jitaḥ pūrvaṃ prāṅnyāyastu sa ucyate  || 171 ||

vibhāvayāmi kulikaiḥ sākṣibhirlikhitena vā  |
jitaścaiva mayāyaṃ prākprāṅnyāyastriprakārakaḥ  || 172 ||


[uttarābhāsā uttaradoṣā vā]

aprasiddhaṃ viruddhaṃ yadatyalpamatibhūri ca  |
saṃdigdhāsaṃbhavāvyaktamanyārthaṃ cātidoṣavat || 173 ||

avyāpakaṃ vyastapadaṃ nigūḍhārthaṃ tathākulam  |
vyākhyāgamyamasāraṃ ca nottaraṃ śasyate budhaiḥ  || 174 ||

yadvyastapadamavyāpi nigūḍhārthaṃ tathākulam  |
vyākhyāgamyamasāraṃ ca nottaraṃ svārthasiddhaye  || 175 ||

cihnākārasahasraṃ tu samayaṃ cāvijānatā  |
bhāṣāntareṇa vā proktamaprasiddhaṃ taduttaram  || 176 ||

pratidattaṃ mayā bālye pratidattaṃ mayā na hi  |
yadevamāha vijñeyaṃ viruddhaṃ tadihottaraṃ  || 177 ||

jitaḥ purā mayāyaṃ ca tvarthe'sminniti bhāṣitum  |
purā mayāyamiti yattadūnaṃ cottaraṃ smṛtam  || 178 ||

gṛhītamiti vācye tu kāryaṃ tena kṛtaṃ mayā  |
purā gṛhītaṃ yaddravyamiti yaccātibhūri tat || 179 ||

deyaṃ mayeti vaktavye mayādeyamitīdṛśam  |
saṃdigdhamuttaraṃ jñeyaṃ vyavahāre budhaistadā  || 180 ||

balābalena caitena sāhasaṃ sthāpitaṃ purā  |
anuktametanmanyante tadanyārthamitīritam  || 181 ||

asmai dattaṃ mayā sārdhaṃ sahasramiti bhāṣite  |
pratidattaṃ tadardhaṃ yattadihāvyāpakaṃ smṛtam  || 182 ||

pūrvavādī kriyāṃ yāvatsamyaṅnaiva niveśayet |
mayā gṛhītaṃ pūrvaṃ no tadvyastapadamucyate  || 183 ||

tatkiṃ tāmarasaṃ kaścidagṛhītaṃ pradāsyati  |
nigūḍhārthaṃ tu tatproktamuttaraṃ vyavahārataḥ  || 184 ||

kiṃ tenaiva sadā deyaṃ mayā deyaṃ bhavediti  |
etadakulamityuktamuttaraṃ tadvido viduḥ  || 185 ||

kākasya dantā no santi santītyādi yaduttaram  |
asāramiti tattvena samyaṅnottaramiṣyate  || 186 ||

prastutādalpamavyaktaṃ nyūnādhikamasaṅgatam  |
avyāpyasāraṃ saṃdigdhaṃ pratipakṣaṃ na laṅghayet || 187 ||

saṃdigdhamanyatprakṛtādatyalpamatibhūri ca  |
pakṣaikadeśavyāpyeva tattu naivottaraṃ bhavet || 188 ||

pakṣaikadeśe yatsatyamekadeśe ca kāraṇam  |
mithyā caivaikadeśe ca saṅkarāttadanuttaram  || 189 ||

na caikasmin vivāde tu kriyā syādvādinordvayoḥ  |
na cārthasiddhirubhayorna caikatra kriyādvayam  || 190 ||


[vādahānikarāṇi]

prapadya kāraṇaṃ pūrvamanyadgurutaraṃ yadi  |
prativākyagataṃ brūyātsādhyate taddhi netarat || 191 ||

yathārthamuttaraṃ dadyādayacchantaṃ ca dāpayet |
sāmabhedādibhirmārgairyāvatso'rthaḥ samutthitaḥ  || 192 ||

mohādvā yadi vā śāṭhyādyannoktaṃ pūrvavādinā  |
uttarāntargataṃ cāpi tadgrāhyamubhayorapi  || 193 ||

upāyaiścodyamānastu na dadyāduttaraṃ tu yaḥ  |
atikrānte saptarātre jito'sau dātumarhati  || 194 ||

śrāvayitvā yathākāryaṃ tyajedanyadvadedasau  |
anyapakṣāśrayastena kṛto vādī sa hīyate  || 195 ||

na mayābhihitaṃ kāryamabhiyujya paraṃ vadet |
vibruvaṃśca bhavedevaṃ hīnaṃ tamapi nirdiśet || 196 ||

lekhayitvā tu yo vākyaṃṃ hīnaṃ vāpyadhikaṃ punaḥ  |
vadedvādī sa hīyeta nābhiyogaṃ tu so'rhati  || 197 ||

sabhyāśca sākṣiṇaścaiva kriyā jñeyā manīṣibhiḥ  |
tāṃ kriyāṃ dveṣṭi yo mohātkriyādveṣī sa ucyate  || 198 ||

āhvānādanupasthānātsadya eva prahīyate  || 199 ||

brūhītyukto'pi na brūyātsadyo bandhanamarhati  |
dvitīye'hani durbuddhervidyāttasya parājayam  || 200 ||

vyājenaiva tu yatrāsau dīrghakālamabhīpsati  |
sāpadeśaṃ tu tadvidyādvādahānikaraṃ smṛtam  || 201 ||

anyavādī paṇān pañca kriyādveṣī paṇān daśa  |
nopasthātā daśa dvau ca ṣoḍaśaiva niruttaraḥ  |
āhūtaprapalāyī ca paṇān grāhyastu viṃśatim  || 202 ||

trirāhūtamanāyāntamāhūtaprapalāyinam  |
pañcarātramatikrāntaṃ vinayettaṃ mahīpatiḥ  || 203 ||

śrāvitavyavahārāṇāmekaṃ yatra prabhedayet |
vādinaṃ lobhayeccaiva hīnaṃ tamiti nirdiśet || 204 ||

bhayaṃ karoti bhedaṃ vā bhīṣaṇaṃ vā nirodhanam  |
etāni vādinorarthasya vyavahāre sa hīyate  || 205 ||

doṣānurūpaṃ saṃgrāhyaḥ punarvādo na vidyate  |
ubhayorlikhite vācye prārabdhe kāryaniścaye  |
ayuktaṃ tatra yo brūyāttasmādarthātsa hīyate  || 206 ||

sākṣiṇo yastu nirdiśya kāmato na vivādayet |
sa vādī hīyate tasmāttriṃśadrātrātpareṇa tu  || 207 ||

palāyanānuttaratvādanyapakṣāśrayeṇa ca  |
hīnasya gṛhyate vādo na svavākyajitasya tu  || 208 ||

yo hīnavākyena jitastasyoddhāraṃ vidurbudhāḥ  |
svavākyahīno yastu syāttasyoddhāro na vidyate  || 209 ||

āvedya pragṛhītārthāḥ praśamaṃ yānti ye mithaḥ  |
sarve dviguṇadaṇḍyāḥ syuḥ vipralambhānnṛpasya te  || 210 ||


[kriyāpādaḥ]

kāraṇātpūrvapakṣo'pi hyuttaratvaṃ prapadyate  |
ataḥ kriyā tadā proktā pūrvapakṣaprasādhinī  || 211 ||

śodhite likhite samyagiti nirdoṣa uttare  |
pratyarthino'rthino vāpi kriyākaraṇamiṣyate  || 212 ||

vādinā yadabhipretaṃ svayaṃ sādhayituṃ sphuṭam  |
tatsādhyaṃ sādhanaṃ yena tatsādhyaṃ sādhyate'khilam  || 213 ||


[pramāṇāni, teṣāṃ ca balābalādivicāraḥ]



likhitaṃ sākṣiṇo bhuktiḥ pramāṇaṃ trividhaṃ viduḥ  |
leśoddeśastu yuktiḥ syāddivyānīha viṣādayaḥ  || 214 ||

pūrvavāde'pi likhite yathākṣaramaśeṣataḥ  |
arthī tṛtīyapāde tu kriyayā pratipādayet || 215 ||

kāryaṃ hi sādhyamityuktaṃ sādhanaṃ tu kriyocyate  |
dvibhedā sā punarjñeyā daivikī mānuṣī tathā  |
mānuṣī likhyasākṣyādirvadhādirdaivikī matā  || 216 ||

saṃbhave sākṣiṇāṃ prājño daivikīṃ varjayetkriyāṃ  |
saṃbhave tu prayuñjāno daivikīṃ hīyate tataḥ  || 217 ||

yadyeko mānuṣīṃ brūyādanyo brūyāttu daivikīm  |
mānuṣīṃ tatra gṛhṇīyānna tu daivīṃ kriyāṃ nṛpaḥ  || 218 ||

yadyekadeśavyāptāpi kriyā vidyeta mānuṣī   |
sā grāhyā na tu pūrṇāpi daivikī vadatāṃ nṛṇām  || 219 ||

pañcaprakāraṃ daivaṃ syānmānuṣaṃ trividhaṃ smṛtam  || 220 ||

kriyāṃ balavatīṃ muktvā durbalāṃ yo'valambate  |
sa jaye'vadhṛte sabhyaiḥ punastāṃ nāpnuyātkriyām  || 221 ||

sārabhūtaṃ padaṃ muktvā asārāṇi bahūnyapi  |
saṃsādhayetkriyā yā tu tāṃ jahyātsāravarjitām  |
pakṣadvayaṃ sādhayedyā tāṃ jahyāddūrataḥ kriyām  || 222 ||

kriyā na daivikī proktā vidyāmāneṣu sākṣiṣu  |
lekhye ca sati vādeṣu na divyaṃ na ca sākṣiṇaḥ  || 223 ||

kālena hīyate lekhyaṃ dūṣitaṃ nyāyatastathā  |
alekhyasākṣike daivīṃ vyavahāre vinirdiśet |
daivasādhye pauruṣeyīṃ na lekhyaṃ vā prayojayet || 224 ||

pūgaśreṇigaṇādīnāṃ yā sthitiḥ parikīrtitā  |
tasyāstu sādhanaṃ lekhyaṃ na divyaṃ na ca sākṣiṇaḥ  || 225 ||

dvāramārgakriyābhoga- jalavāhādike tathā  |
bhuktireva hi gurvī syānna lekhyaṃ na ca sākṣiṇaḥ  || 226 ||

dattādatte'tha bhṛtyānāṃ svāminā nirṇaye sati  |
vikrayādānasaṃbandhe krītvā dhanamayacchati  || 227 ||

dyūte samāhvaye caiva vivāde samupasthite   |
sākṣiṇaḥ sādhanaṃ proktaṃ na divyaṃ na ca lekhyakam  || 228 ||

prakrānte sāhase vāde pāruṣye daṇḍavācike  |
balodbhūteṣu kāryeṣu sākṣiṇo divyameva vā  || 229 ||

gūḍhasāhasikānāṃ tu prāptaṃ divyaiḥ parīkṣaṇam  |
yukticihneṅgitākāra- vākcakṣuśceṣṭitairnṛṇām  || 230 ||

uttameṣu ca sarveṣu sāhaseṣu vicārayet |
sadbhāvaṃ divyadṛṣṭena satsu sākṣiṣu vai bhṛguḥ  || 231 ||

samatvaṃ sākṣiṇāṃ yatra divyaistatrāpi śodhayet |
prāṇāntikavivādeṣu vidyāmāneṣu sākṣiṣu  |
divyamālambate vādī na pṛcchettatra sākṣiṇaḥ  || 232 ||

ṛṇe lekhyaṃ sākṣiṇo vā yuktileśādayo'pi vā  |
daivikī vā kriyā proktā prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā  || 233 ||

codanā pratikālaṃ ca yuktileśastathaiva ca  |
tṛtīyaḥ śapathaḥ proktaḥ tairṛṇaṃ sādhayetkramāt || 234 ||

abhīkṣṇaṃ codyamāno'pi pratihanyānna tadvacaḥ  |
triḥ catuḥ pañcakṛtvo vā parato'rthaṃ samācaret || 235 ||

codanāpratighāte tu yuktileśaiḥ samanviyāt |
deśakālārthasaṃbandha- parimāṇakriyādibhiḥ  || 236 ||

yuktiṣvapyasamarthāsu śapathaireva niṇayet |
arthakālabalāpekṣairagnyambusukṛtādibhiḥ  || 237 ||

yatra syātsopadhaṃ lekhyaṃ tadrājñaḥ śrāvitaṃ yadi  |
divyena śodhayettatra rājā dharmāsanasthitaḥ  || 238 ||

vākpāruṣye ca bhūmau ca divyaṃ na parikalpayet || 239 ||

sthāvareṣu vivādeṣu divyāni paridhārayet |
sākṣibhirlikhitenārthe bhuktyā caiva prasādhayet || 240 ||

pramāṇairhetunā vāpi divyenaiva tu niścayam  |
sarveṣveva vivādeṣu sadā kuryānnarādhipaḥ  || 241 ||

likhitaṃ sākṣiṇo bhuktiḥ pramāṇaṃ trividhaṃ smṛtam  |
anumānaṃ vidurhetuṃ tarkaṃ caiva manīṣiṇaḥ  || 242 ||

pūrvābhāve pareṇaiva nānyathaiva kadācana  |
pramāṇairvādinirdiṣṭairbhuktyā likhitasākṣibhiḥ  || 243 ||

na kaścidabhiyoktāraṃ divyeṣu viniyojayet |
abhiyuktāya dātavyaṃ divyaṃ divyaviśāradaiḥ  || 244 ||

mithyoktau sa catuṣpātsyātpratyavaskandane tathā  |
prāṅnyāye sa ca vijñeyo dvipātsaṃpratipattiṣu  || 245 ||

parājayaśca dvividhaḥ paroktaḥ svokta eva ca  |
paroktaḥ syāddaśavidhaḥ svokta ekavidhaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 246 ||

vivādāntarasaṃkrāntiḥ pūrvottaraviruddhatā  |
dūṣaṇaṃ svakriyotpatteḥ paravākyopapādanam  || 247 ||

anirdeśaśca deśasya nirdeśo'deśakālayoḥ  |
sākṣiṇāmupajāpaśca vidveṣo vacanasya ca  || 248 ||


[lekhyam]

lekhyaṃ tu dvividhaṃ proktaṃ svahastānyakṛtaṃ tathā  |
asākṣimatsākṣimacca siddhirdeśasthitestayoḥ  || 249 ||

grāhakeṇa svahastena likhitaṃ sākṣivarajitam  |
svahastalekhyaṃ vijñeyaṃ pramāṇaṃ tatsmṛtaṃ budhaiḥ  || 250 ||

utpattijātisaṃjñāṃ ca dhanasaṃkhyāṃ ca lekhayet |
smaratyevaṃ prayuktasya naśyedarthastvalekhitaḥ  || 251 ||

lekhyaṃ tu sākṣimatkāryamaviluptākṣarakramam  |
deśācārasthitiyutaṃ samagraṃ sarvavastuṣu  || 252 ||

varṇavākyakriyāyuktamasaṃdigdhaṃ sphuṭākṣaram  |
ahīnakramacihnaṃ ca lekhyaṃ tatsiddhimāpnuyāt || 253 ||

cātruvidyapuraśreṇī- gaṇapaurādikasthitiḥ  |
tatsidhyarthaṃ tu yallekhyaṃ tadbhavetsthitipatrakam  || 254 ||

abhiśāpe samuttīrṇe prāyaścitte kṛte janaiḥ  |
viśuddhipatrakaṃ jñeyaṃ tebhyaḥ sākṣisamanvitam  || 255 ||

uttameṣu samasteṣu abhiśāpe samāgate  |
vṛttānuvādalekhyaṃ yattajjñeyaṃ sandhipatrakam  || 256 ||

sīmāvivāde nirṇīte sīmāpatraṃ vidhīyate  || 257 ||

rājñaḥ svahastasaṃyuktaṃ samudrācihnitaṃ tathā  |
rājakīyaṃ smṛtaṃ lekhyaṃ sarveṣvartheṣu sākṣimat || 258 ||

arthipratyarthivākyāni pratijñā sākṣivāktathā  |
nirṇayaśca yathā tasya yathā cāvadhṛtaṃ svayam  || 259 ||

etadyathākṣaraṃ lekhye yathāpūrvaṃ niveśayet |
abhiyoktṛabhiyuktānāṃ vacanaṃ prāṅniveśayet || 260 ||

sabhyānāṃ prāḍvivākasya kulānāṃ vā tataḥ param  |
niścayaṃ smṛtiśāstrasya mataṃ tatraiva lekhayet || 261 ||

siddhenārthena saṃyojyo vādī satkārapūrvakam  |
lekhyaṃ svahastasaṃyuktaṃ tasmai dadyāttu pārthivaḥ  || 262 ||

sabhāsadaśca ye tatra smṛtiśāstravidaḥ sthitāḥ  |
yathālekhyavidhhau tadvatsvahastaṃ tatra dāpayet || 263 ||

anena vidhinā lekhyaṃ paścātkāraṃ vidurbudhāḥ  |
nirastā tu kriyā yatra pramāṇenaiva vādinā  |
paścātkāro bhavettatra na sarvāsu vidhīyate  || 264 ||

anyavādīādihīnebhya itareṣāṃ pradīyate  |
vṛttānuvādasaṃsiddhaṃ tacca syājjayapatrakam   || 265 ||

[lekhyaparīkṣā]

rājājñayā samāhūya yathānyāyaṃ vicārayet |
lekhyācāreṇa likhitaṃ sākṣyācāreṇa sākṣiṇaḥ  || 266 ||

varṇavākyakriyāyuktamasaṃdigdhaṃ sphuṭākṣaram  |
ahīnakramacihnaṃ ca lekhyaṃ tatsiddhimāpnuyāt || 267 ||

deśācārayutaṃ varṣamāsapakṣādivṛddhimat |
ṛṇisākṣilekhakānāṃ hastāṅgaṃ lekhyamucyate  || 268 ||

sthānabhraṣṭāstvapaṅktisthāḥ saṃdigdhā lakṣaṇacyutāḥ  |
yadā tu saṃsthitā varṇāḥ kūṭalekhyaṃ tadā bhavet || 269 ||

deśācāraviruddhaṃ yatsaṃdigdhaṃ kramavarjitam  |
kṛtamasvāminā yacca sādhyahīnaṃ ca duṣyati  || 270 ||

mattenopādhibhītena tathonmattena pīḍitaiḥ  |
strībhirbālāsvatantraiśca kṛtaṃ lekhyaṃ na sidhyati  || 271 ||

khyāpitaṃ ceddvitīye'hni na kaścidvinivartayet |
tathā tatsyātpramāṇaṃ tu mattonmattakṛtādṛte  || 272 ||

sākṣidoṣādbhavedduṣṭaṃ patraṃ vai lekhakasya vā  |
dhanikasyopadhādoṣāttathā dhāraṇikasya vā   || 273 ||

duṣṭairduṣṭaṃ bhavellekhyaṃ śuddhaṃ śuddhairvinirdiśet |
tatpatramupadhāduṣṭaiḥ sākṣilekhakakārakaiḥ  || 274 ||

pramāṇasya hi ye doṣā vaktavyāste vivādinā  |
gūḍhāstu prakaṭāḥ sabhyaiḥ kāle śāstrapradarśanāt || 275 ||

sākṣilekhakakartāraḥ kūṭatāṃ yānti te yathā  |
tathā doṣāḥ prayoktavyā duṣṭairlekhyaṃ praduṣyāta  || 276 ||

na lekhakena likhitaṃ na dṛṣṭaṃ sākṣibhistathā  |
evaṃ pratyarthinokte tu kūṭalekhyaṃ prakīrtitam  || 277 ||

nātathyena pramāṇaṃ tu doṣeṇaiva tu dūṣayet |
mithyābhiyoge daṇḍyaḥ syātsādhyārthāccāpi hīyate  || 278 ||

evaṃ duṣṭaṃ nṛpasthāne yasmiṃstaddhi vicāryate  |
vimṛśya brāhmaṇaiḥ sārdhaṃ patradoṣānnirūpayet || 279 ||

yena te kūṭatāṃ yānti sākṣilekhakakārakāḥ  |
tena duṣṭaṃ bhavellekhyaṃ śuddhaiḥ śuddhiṃ vinirdiśet || 280 ||

dhanikena svahastena likhitaṃ sākṣivarjitam   |
bhavetkūṭaṃ na cetkartā kṛtaṃ hīti vibhāvayet || 281 ||

dattaṃ lekhye svahastaṃ tu ṛṇiko yadi nihnate  |
patrasthaiḥ sākṣibhirvācyo lekhakasya matena vā  || 282 ||

kṛtākṛtavivādeṣu sākṣibhiḥ patranirṇayaḥ  |
dūṣite patrake vādī tadārūḍhāṃstu nirdiśet || 283 ||

trividhasyāpi lekhyasya bhrāntiḥ sañjāyate nṛṇām  |
ṛṇisākṣilekhakānāṃ hastoktyā sādhayettataḥ  || 284 ||

atha pañcatvamāpanno lekhakaḥ saha sākṣibhiḥ  |
tatsvahastādibhisteṣāṃ viśudhyettu na saṃśayaḥ  || 285 ||

ṛṇisvahastasaṃdehe jīvato vā mṛtasya vā  |
tatsvahastakṛtairanyaiḥ patraistallekhyanirṇayaḥ  || 286 ||

samudre'pi lekhye mṛtāḥ sarve'pi te sthitāḥ (?)  |
likhitaṃ tatpramāṇaṃ tu mṛteṣvapi hi teṣu ca  || 287 ||

pratyakṣamanumānena na kadācitprabādhyate  |
tasmāllekhyasya duṣṭasya vacobhiḥ sākṣiṇāṃ bhavet || 288 ||

nirṇayaḥ svadhanārthaṃ hi patraṃ dūṣayati svayam   |
likhitaṃ likhitenaiva sākṣimatsākṣibhirharet || 289 ||

kūṭoktau sākṣiṇāṃ vākyāllekhakasya ca patrakam  |
nayecchuddhiṃ na yaḥ kūṭaṃ sa dāpyo damamuttamam  || 290 ||

āḍhyasya nikaṭasthasya yacchaktena na yācitam  |
śuddharṇaśaṅkayā tattu lekhyaṃ durbalatāmiyāt || 291 ||

lekhyaṃ triṃśatsamātītamadṛṣṭāśrāvitaṃ ca yat |
na tatsiddhimavāpnoti tiṣṭhatsvapi hi sākṣiṣu  || 292 ||

prayukte śāntalābhe tu likhitaṃ yo na darśayet |
naiva yāceta ṛṇikaṃ na tatsiddhimavāpnuyāt || 293 ||

paścātkāranibaddhaṃ yattadyatnena vicārayet |
yadi syādyuktiyuktaṃ tu pramāṇaṃ likhitaṃ tadā  || 294 ||

anyathā dūrataḥ kāryaṃ punareva vinirṇayet |
atathyaṃ tathyabhāvena sthāpitaṃ jñānavibhramāt |
nivartyaṃ tatpramāṇaṃ syādyatnenāpi kṛtaṃ nṛpaiḥ  || 295 ||

mudrāśuddhaṃ kriyāśuddhaṃ bhuktiśuddhaṃ sacihnakam  |
rājñaḥ svahastasaṃśuddhaṃ śuddhimāyāti śāsanam  || 296 ||

nirdoṣaṃ prathitaṃ yattu lekhyaṃ tatsiddhimāpnuyāt || 297 ||

dṛṣṭe patre sphuṭān doṣānnoktavānṛṇiko yadi  |
tato viṃśativarṣāṇi sthitaṃ patraṃ sthiraṃ bhavet  || 298 ||

śaktasya saṃnidhāvarthe yena lekhyena bhujyate  |
varṣāṇi viṃśatiṃ yāvattatpatraṃ doṣavarjitam  || 299 ||

atha viṃśativarṣāṇi ādhirbhuktaḥ suniścitam  |
tena lekhyena tatsiddhirlekhyadoṣavivarjitā  || 300 ||

sīmāvivāde nirṇīte sīmāpatraṃ vidhīyate  |
tasya doṣāḥ pravaktavyā yāvadvarṣāṇi viṃśatiḥ  || 301 ||

ādhānasahitaṃ yatra ṛṇaṃ lekhye niveśitam  |
mṛtasākṣi pramāṇaṃ tu svalpabhogeṣu tadviduḥ  || 302 ||

prāptaṃ vānena cetkiñciddānaṃ cāpyanirūpitam  |
vināpi mudrayā lekhyaṃ pramāṇaṃ mṛtasākṣikam  || 303 ||

yadi labdhaṃ bhavetkiñcitprajñaptirvā kṛtā bhavet |
pramāṇameva likhitaṃ mṛtā yadyapi sākṣiṇaḥ  || 304 ||

darśitaṃ pratikālaṃ yadgrāhitaṃ smāritaṃ tathā   |
lekhyaṃ sidhyati sarvatra mṛteṣvapi ca sākṣiṣu  || 305 ||

na divyaiḥ sākṣibhirvāpi hīyate likhitaṃ kvacit |
lekhyadharmaḥ sadā śreṣṭho hyato nānyena hīyate  || 306 ||

tadyuktapratilekhyena tadviśiṣṭena vā sadā  |
lekhyakriyā nirasyeta nirasyānyena na kvacit || 307 ||

darpaṇasthaṃ yathā bimbamasatsadiva dṛśyate  |
tathā lekhyasya bimbāni kurvanti kuśalā janāḥ  || 308 ||

dravyaṃ gṛhītvā yallekhyaṃ parasmai saṃpradīyate  |
channamanyena cārūḍhaṃ saṃyataṃ cānyaveśmani  || 309 ||

datte vṛtte'tha vā dravye kvacillikhitapūrvake  |
eṣa eva vidhirjñeyo lekhyaśuddhivinirṇaye  || 310 ||

sthāvare vikrayādhāne lekhyaṃ kūṭaṃ karoti yaḥ  |
sa samyagbhāvitaḥ kāryo jihvāpāṇyaṅghrivarjitaḥ  || 311 ||

malairyadbheditaṃ dagdhaṃ chidritaṃ vītameva vā  |
tadanyatkārayellekhyaṃ svedenollikhitaṃ tathā  || 312 ||


[bhuktiḥ]

likhitaṃ sākṣiṇo bhuktiḥ pramāṇatrayamiṣyate  |
pramāṇeṣu smṛtā bhukteḥ sallekhasamatā nṛṇām  || 313 ||

rathyānirgamanadvāra- jalavāhādisaṃśaye  |
bhuktireva tu gurvī syātpramāṇeṣviti niścayaḥ  || 314 ||

anumānādguruḥ sakṣī sākṣibhyo likhitaṃ guru  |
avyāhatā tripuruṣī bhuktirebhyo garīyasī  || 315 ||

nopabhoge balaṃ kāryamāhartrā tatsutena vā  |
paśustrīpuruṣādīnāmiti dharmo vyavasthitaḥ  || 316 ||

bhuktistu dvividhā proktā sāgamānāgamā tathā  |
tripuruṣī yā svatantrā sā cedalpā tu sāgamā  || 317 ||

mukhyā paitāmahī bhuktiḥ paitṛkī cāpi saṃmatā  |
tribhiretairavicchinnā sthirā ṣaṣṭyābdikī matā  || 318 ||

sāgamena tu bhuktena samyagbhuktaṃ yadā tu yat |
āhartā labhate tattu nāpahāryaṃ tu tatkvacit || 319 ||

pranaṣṭāgamalekhyena bhogārūḍhena vādinā  |
kālaḥ pramāṇaṃ dānaṃ ca kīrtanīyāni saṃsadi  || 320 ||

smārtakāle kriyā bhūmeḥ sāgamā bhuktiriṣyate  |
asmārte'nugamābhāvātkramāttripuruṣāgatā  || 321 ||

ādau tu kāraṇaṃ madhye bhuktistu sāgamā (?)   |
kāraṇaṃ bhuktirevaikā saṃtatā yā tripauruṣī  || 322 ||

āhartā bhuktiyukto'pi lekhyadoṣān viśodhayet |
tatsuto bhuktidoṣāṃstu lekhyadoṣāṃstu nāpnuyāt || 323 ||

yenopāttaṃ hi yaddravyaṃ so'bhiyuktastaduddharet |
cirakālopabhoge'pi bhuktistasyaiva neṣyate  || 324 ||

cirantanamavijñātaṃ bhogaṃ lobhānna cālayet || 325 ||

pitrā bhuktaṃ tu yaddravyaṃ bhuktyācāreṇa dharmataḥ  |
tasmin prete na vācyo'sau bhuktyā prāptaṃ hi tasya tat || 326 ||

tribhireva tu yā bhuktā puruṣairbhū yathāvidhi  |
lekhyābhāve'pi tāṃ tatra caturthaḥ samavāpnuyāt || 327 ||

yathā kṣīraṃ janayati dadhi kālādrasānvitam  |
dānahetustathā kālādbhogastripuruṣāgataḥ  || 328 ||

bhuktirbalavatī śāstre saṃtatā yā cirantanī  |
vicchinnāpi sā jñeyā yā tu pūrvaprasādhitā  || 329 ||

na bhogaṃ kalpayetstrīṣu devarājadhaneṣu ca  |
bālaśrotriyavitte ca mātṛtaḥ pitṛtaḥ kramāt || 330 ||

brahmacarī caretkaścidavrataṃ ṣaṭtriṃśadābdikam   |
arthārthī cānyaviṣaye dīrghakālaṃ vasennaraḥ  || 331 ||

samāvṛtto'vratī kuryātsvadhanānveṣaṇaṃ tataḥ  |
pañcāśadābdiko bhogastaddhanasyāpahārakaḥ  || 332 ||

pravivedaṃ dvādaśābdaḥ kālo vidyārthināṃ smṛtaḥ  |
śilpavidyārthināṃ caiva grahaṇāntaḥ prakīrtitaḥ  || 333 ||

suhṛdbhirbandhubhiścaiṣāṃ yatsvaṃ bhuktamapaśyatām  |
nṛpāparādhināṃ caiva na tatkālena hīyate  || 334 ||

sanābhibhirbāndhavaiśca yadbhuktaṃ svajanaistathā  |
bhogāttatra na siddhiḥ syādbhogamanyatra kalpayet || 335 ||


[yuktiḥ]

arthinābhyarthito yastu vighātaṃ na prayojayet |
tricatuḥpañcakṛtvo vā parastadṛṇī bhavet(?)  || 336 ||

dānaṃ prajñāpanā bhedaḥ saṃpralobhakriyā ca yā  |
cittāpanayanaṃ caiva hetavo hi vibhāvakāḥ  || 337 ||

eṣāmanyatamo yatra vādinā bhāvito bhavet |
mūlakriyā tu tatra syādbhāvite vādinihnave  || 338 ||


[sākṣiṇaḥ]

na kālaharaṇaṃ kāryaṃ rājñā sākṣiprabhāṣaṇe  |
mahān doṣo bhavetkālāddharmavyāvṛttilakṣaṇaḥ  || 339 ||

upasthitān parīkṣeta sākṣiṇo nṛpatiḥ svayam   |
sākṣibhirbhāṣitaṃ vākyaṃ sabhyaiḥ saha parīkṣayet || 340 ||

samyakkriyāparijñāne deyaḥ kālastu sākṣiṇām  |
saṃdigdhaṃ yatra sākṣyaṃ syātsadyaḥ spaṣṭaṃ vivādayet || 341 ||

sabhāntaḥ sākṣiṇaḥ sarvānarthipratyarthisaṃnidhau  |
prāṅvivāko niyuñjīta vidhinānena sāntvayan  || 342 ||

yaddvayoranayorvettha kārye'smiṃśceṣṭitaṃ mithaḥ  |
tadbrūta sarvaṃ satyena yuṣmākaṃ hyatra sākṣitā  || 343 ||

devabrāhmanasānnidhye sākṣyaṃ pṛcchedṛtaṃ dvijān  |
udaṅmukhān prāṅmukhān vā pūrvāhṇe vai śuciḥ śucīn  || 344 ||

āhūya sākṣiṇaḥ pṛcchenniyamya śapathairbhṛśam  |
samastān viditācārān vijñātārthān pṛthakpṛthak || 345 ||

arthipratyarthisāṃnidhyādanubhūtaṃ tu yadbhavet |
tadgrāhyaṃ sākṣiṇo vākyamanyathā na bṛhaspatiḥ  || 346 ||

prakhyātakulaśīlāśca lobhamohavivarjitāḥ  |
āptāḥ śuddhā viśiṣṭā ye teṣāṃ sākṣyamasaṃśayam  || 347 ||

vibhāvyo vādinā yādṛksadṛśaireva bhāvayet |
notkṛṣṭaścāvakṛṣṭastu sākṣibhirbhāvayetsadā  || 348 ||

liṅginaḥ śreṇipūgāśca vaṇigvrātāstathāpare  |
samūhasthāśca ye cānye vargāstānabravīdbhṛguḥ  || 349 ||

dāsacāraṇamallānāṃ hastyaśvāyudhajīvinām  |
pratyekaikaṃ samūhānāṃ nāyakā vargiṇastathā  |
teṣāṃ vādaḥ svavargeṣu vargiṇasteṣu sākṣiṇaḥ  || 350 ||

strīṇāṃ sākṣyaṃ striyaḥ kuryurdvijānāṃ sadṛśā dvijāḥ  |
śūdrāśca santaḥ śūdrāṇāmantyānāmantyayonayaḥ  || 351 ||

aśakya āgamo yatra videśaprativāsinām  |
traividyaprahitaṃ tatra lekhyasākṣyaṃ pravādayet || 352 ||

abhyantarastu nikṣepe sākṣyameko'pi vācyate  |
arthinā prahitaḥ sākṣī bhavatyeko'pi dūtakaḥ  || 353 ||

saṃskṛtaṃ yena yatpaṇyaṃ tattenaiva vibhāvayet |
eka eva pramāṇaṃ sa vivāde tatra kīrtitaḥ  || 354 ||

lekhakaḥ prāṅvivākaśca sabhyāścaivānupūrvaśaḥ  |
nṛpe paśyati yatkāryaṃ sākṣiṇaḥ samudāhṛtāḥ  || 355 ||

anye punaranirdiṣṭāḥ sākṣiṇaḥ samudāhṛtāḥ  |
grāmaśca prāṅvivākaśca rājā ca vyavahāriṇām  || 356 ||

kāryeṣvabhyantaro yaśca arthinā prahitaśca yaḥ  |
kulyāḥ kulavivādeṣu bhaveyuste'pi sākṣiṇaḥ  || 357 ||

rikthabhāgavivāde tu saṃdehe samupasthite  |
kulyānāṃ vacanaṃ tatra pramāṇaṃ tadviparyaye  || 358 ||

sākṣiṇāṃ likhitānāṃ tu nirdiṣṭānāṃ ca vādinā  |
teṣāmeko'nyathāvādī bhedātsarve na sākṣiṇaḥ  || 359 ||

anyena hi kṛtaḥ sākṣī naivānyastaṃ vivādayet |
tadabhāve niyukto vā bāndhavo vā vivādayet || 360 ||

tadvṛttijīvino ye ca tatsevāhitakāriṇaḥ  |
tadbandhusuhṛdo bhṛtyā āptāste tu na sākṣiṇaḥ  || 361 ||

mātṛṣvasṛsutāścaiva sodaryāsutamātulāḥ  |
ete sanābhayastūktāḥ sākṣyaṃ teṣu na yojayet || 362 ||

kulyāḥ saṃbandhinaścaiva vivāhyo bhaginīpatiḥ  |
pitā bandhuḥ pitṛvyaśca śvaśuro guravastathā  || 363 ||

nagaragrāmadeśeṣu niyuktā ye padeṣu ca  |
vallabhāśca na pṛccheyurbhaktāste rājapūruṣāḥ  || 364 ||

ṛṇādiṣu parīkṣeta sākṣiṇaḥ sthirakarmasu  |
sāhasātyayike caiva parīkṣā kutracitsmṛtā  || 365 ||

vyāghāteṣu nṛpājñāyāḥ saṃgrahe sāhaseṣu ca  |
steyapāruṣyayoścaiva na parīkṣeta sākṣiṇaḥ  || 366 ||

antarveśmani rātrau ca bahirgrāmācca yadbhavet |
eteṣvevābhiyogaścenna parīkṣeta sākṣiṇaḥ  || 367 ||

na sākṣyaṃ sākṣibhirvācyamapṛṣṭairarthinā sadā  |
na sākṣyaṃ teṣu vidyeta svayamātmani yojayet || 368 ||

lekhyārūḍhaścottaraśca sākṣī mārgadvayānvitaḥ  || 369 ||

atha svahastenārūḍhastiṣṭhaṃścaikaḥ sa eva tu  |
na cetpratyabhijānīyāttatsvahastaiḥ prasādhayet || 370 ||

arthinā svayamānīto yo lekhye saṃniveśyate  |
sa sākṣī likhito nāma smāritaḥ patrakādṛte  || 371 ||

yastu kāryaprasiddhyarthaṃ dṛṣṭvā kāryaṃ punaḥ punaḥ  |
smāryate hyarthinā sākṣī sa smārita ihocyate  || 372 ||

prayojanārthamānītaḥ prasaṅgādāgataśca yaḥ  |
dvau sākṣiṇau tvalikhitau pūrvapakṣasya sādhakau  || 373 ||

arthinā svārthasiddyarthaṃ pratyarthivacanaṃ sphuṭam  |
yaḥ śrāvitaḥ sthito gūḍho gūḍhasākṣī sa ucyate  || 374 ||

sākṣiṇāmapi yaḥ sākṣyamuparyupari bhāṣate  |
śravaṇācchrāvaṇādvāpi sa sākṣyuttarasaṃjñitaḥ  || 375 ||

ullapyaṃ yasya viśrambhātkāryaṃ vā viniveditam   |
gūḍhacārī sa vijñeyaḥ kāryamadhyagatastathā  || 376 ||

arthī yatra vipannaḥ syāttatra sākṣī mṛtāntaraḥ  |
pratyarthī ca mṛto yatra tatrāpyevaṃ prakalpyate  || 377 ||


[sākṣidoṣodbhāvanam]

lekhyadoṣāstu ye kecitsākṣiṇāṃ caiva ye smṛtāḥ  |
vādakāle tu vaktavyāḥ paścāduktānna dūṣayet || 378 ||

ukterarthe sākṣiṇo yastu dūṣayetprāgdūṣitān  |
na ca tatkāraṇaṃ brūyātprāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 379 ||

nātathyena pramāṇaṃ tu doṣeṇaiva tu dūṣayet |
mithyābhiyoge daṇḍyaḥ syātsādhyārthāccāpi hīyate  || 380 ||

pratyarthinārthinā vāpi sākṣidūṣaṇasādhane  |
prastutārthopayogitvādvyavahārāntaraṃ na ca  || 381 ||

sākṣidoṣāḥ pravaktavyāḥ saṃsadi prativādinā  |
patre vilikhya tān sarvān vācyaḥ pratyuttaraṃ tataḥ  || 382 ||

pratipattau tu sākṣitvamarhanti na kadācana  |
ato'nyathā bhāvanīyāḥ kriyayā prativādinā  || 383 ||

abhāvayan dhanaṃ dāpyaḥ pratyarthī sākṣiṇaḥ sphuṭam  |
bhāvitāḥ sākṣiṇaḥ sarve sākṣidharmanirākṛtāḥ  || 384 ||

ākāro'ṅgitaceṣṭābhistasya bhāvaṃ vibhāvayet |
prativādī bhaveddhīnaḥ so'numānena lakṣyate  || 385 ||

kampaḥ svedo'tha vaikalyamoṣṭhaśoṣābhimarśane  |
bhūlekhanaṃ sthānahānistiryagūrdhvanirīkṣaṇam  |
svarabhedaśca duṣṭasya cihnānyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ  || 386 ||

sabhāntaḥsthaistu vaktavyaṃ sākṣyaṃ nānyatra sākṣibhiḥ  |
sarvasākṣyeṣvayaṃ dharmo hyanyatra sthāvareṣu tu  || 387 ||

arthipratyarthisāṃnidhye sādhyārthasya ca saṃnidhau  |
pratyakṣaṃ deśayetsakṣyaṃ parokṣaṃ na kathaṃcana  || 388 ||

arthasyopari vaktavyaṃ tayorapi vinā kvacit |
catuṣpadeṣvayaṃ dharmo dvipadasthāvareṣu ca  || 389 ||

taulyagaṇimameyānāmabhāve'pi vivādayet |
kriyākāreṣu sarveṣu sākṣitvaṃ na tato'nyathā  || 390 ||

vadhe cetprāṇināṃ sākṣyaṃ vādayecchivasaṃnidhau  |
tadabhāve tu cihnasya nānyathaiva pravādayet || 391 ||

svabhāvoktaṃ vacasteṣāṃ grāhyaṃ yaddoṣavarjitam  |
ukte tu sākṣiṇo rājñā na praṣṭavyāḥ punaḥ punaḥ  || 392 ||

svabhāvenaiva yadbrūyustadgrāhyaṃ vyāvahārikam  |
ato yadanyadvibrūyurdharmārthaṃ tadapārthakam  || 393 ||

samavetaistu yaddṛṣṭaṃ vaktavyaṃ tattathaiva tu  |
vibhinnaikaikakāryaṃ yadvaktavyaṃ tatpṛthakpṛthak || 394 ||

bhinnakāle tu yatkāryaṃ vijñātaṃ tatra sākṣibhiḥ  |
ekaikaṃ vādayettatra bhinnakālaṃ tu tadbhṛguḥ  || 395 ||

ṛṇādiṣu vivādeṣu sthiraprāyeṣu niścitam  |
ūne vāpyadhike vārthe prokte sādhyaṃ na sidhyati  || 396 ||

sādhyārthāṃśe'pi gadite sākṣibhiḥ sakalaṃ bhavet |
strīsaṅge sāhase caurye yatsādhyaṃ parikalpitam  || 397 ||

ūnādhikaṃ tu yatra syāttatsākṣyaṃ tatra varjayet |
sākṣī tatra na daṇḍyaḥ syādabruvan daṇḍamarhati  || 398 ||

deśaṃ kālaṃ dhanaṃ saṃkhyāṃ rūpaṃ jātyākṛtī vayaḥ  |
visaṃvadedyatra sākṣye tadanuktaṃ vidurbudhāḥ  || 399 ||

nirdiṣṭeṣvarthajāteṣu sākṣī cetsākṣya āgate  |
na brūyādakṣarasamaṃ na tannigaditaṃ bhavet || 400 ||

ūnamabhyadhikaṃ vārthaṃ vibrūyuryatra sākṣiṇaḥ  |
tadapyayuktaṃ vijñeyameṣa sākṣiviniścayaḥ  || 401 ||


[sākṣiṇāṃ doṣā daṇḍāśca]

apṛṣṭaḥ sarvavacane pṛṣṭasyākathane tathā  |
sākṣiṇaḥ saṃniroddhavyā garhyā daṇḍyāśca dharmataḥ  || 402 ||

vākpāruṣye chale vāde dapyāḥ syurtriśataṃ damam  |
ṛṇādivādeṣu dhanaṃ te syurdāpyā ṛṇaṃ tathā  || 403 ||

yaḥ sākṣī naiva nirdiṣṭā nāhūto nāpi darśitaḥ  |
brūyānmithyeti tathyaṃ vā daṇḍyaḥ so'pi narādhamaḥ  || 404 ||

sākṣī sākṣyaṃ na cedbrūyātsamadaṇḍaṃ vahedṛṇam  |
ato'nyeṣu vivādeṣu triśataṃ daṇḍamarhati  || 405 ||

uktvānyathā bruvāṇāśca daṇḍyāḥ syurvākchalānvitāḥ  || 406 ||

yena kāryasya lobhena nirdiṣṭāḥ kūṭasākṣiṇaḥ  |
gṛhītvā tasya sarvasvaṃ kuryānnirviṣayaṃ tataḥ  || 407 ||

yatra vai bhāvitaṃ kāryaṃ sākṣibhirvādinā bhavet |
prativādī yadā tatra bhāvayetkāryamanyathā  |
bahubhiśca kulīnairvā pūrvāḥ syuḥ kūṭasākṣiṇaḥ  || 408 ||

yadā śuddhā kriyā nyāyāttadā tadvākyaśodhanam  |
śuddhācca vākyādyaḥ śuddhaḥ sa śuddho'rtha iti sthitiḥ  || 409 ||

saptāhāttu pratīyeta yatra sākṣyanṛtaṃ vadet |
rogo'gnirjñātimaraṇaṃ dvisaptāhāttrisapta vā  |
ṣaṭcatvāriṃśake vāpi dravyajātyādibhedataḥ  || 410 ||


[divyāni teṣāṃ ca vivādapadaviṣayiṇī vyavasthā]

na kaścidabhiyoktāraṃ divyeṣu viniyojayet |
abhiyuktāya dātavyaṃ divyaṃ divyaviśāradaiḥ  || 411 ||

pārthivaiḥ śaṅkitānāṃ tu tulādīni niyojayet |
ātmaśuddhividhāne ca na śirastatra kalpayet || 412 ||

lokāpavādaduṣṭānāṃ śaṅkitānāṃ ca dasyubhiḥ  |
tulādīni niyojyāni na śirastatra vai bhṛguḥ  || 413 ||

na śaṅkāsu śiraḥ kośe kalpayettu kadācana  |
aśirāṃsi ca divyāni rājabhṛtyeṣu dāpayet || 414 ||

śaṅkāviśvāsasaṃdhāne vibhāge rikthināṃ sadā  |
kriyāsamūhakartṛtve kośameva pradāpayet || 415 ||

dattasyāpahnavo yatra pramāṇaṃ tatra kalpayet |
steyasāhasayordivyaṃ svalpe'pyarthe pradāpayet || 416 ||

sarvadravyapramāṇaṃ tu jñātvā hema prakalpayet |
hemapramāṇayuktaṃ tu tadā divyaṃ niyojayet || 417 ||

jñātvā saṃkhyāṃ suvarṇānāṃ śatanāśe viṣaṃ smṛtam  |
aśītestu vināśe vai dadyāccaiva hutāśanam  || 418 ||

ṣaṣṭyā nāśe jalaṃ deyaṃ catvāriṃśati vai ghaṭam  |
viṃśaddaśavināśe vai kośapānaṃ vidhīyate  || 419 ||

pañcādhikasya vā nāśe tadardhārdhasya tandulāḥ  |
tadardhārdhasya nāśe tu spṛśetputrādimastakam  || 420 ||

tadardhārdhasya nāśe tu laukikāśca kriyāḥ smṛtāḥ  |
evaṃ vicārayan rājā dharmārthābhyāṃ na hīyate  || 421 ||


[divyānāmarthipratyarthijātiśilpānusāriṇyo vyavasthāḥ]

rājanye'gniṃ ghaṭaṃ vipre vaiśye toyaṃ niyojayet |
sarveṣu sarvadivyaṃ vā viṣaṃ varṃjya dviyottame  || 422 ||

gorakṣakān vāṇijakāṃstathā kārukuśīlavān  |
preṣyān vārdhuṣikāṃścaiva grāhayecśūdravaddvijān  || 423 ||

na lohaśilpināmagniṃ salilaṃ nāmbusevinām  |
mantrayogavidāṃ caiva viṣaṃ dadyācca na kvacit |
taṇḍulairna niyuñjīta vratinaṃ mukharogiṇam  || 424 ||

kuṣṭhināṃ varjayedagniṃ salilaṃ śvāsakāsinām  |
pittaśleṣmavatāṃ nityaṃ viṣaṃ tu parivarjayet || 425 ||

madyapastrīvyasanināṃ kitavānāṃ tathaiva ca  |
kośaḥ prājñairna dātavyo ye ca nāstikavṛttayaḥ  || 426 ||

mātāpitṛdvijaguru- bālastrīrājaghātinām  |
mahāpātakayuktānāṃ nāstikānāṃ viśeṣataḥ  || 427 ||

liṅgināṃ praśaṭhānāṃ tu mantrayogakriyāvidām  |
varṇasaṃkarajātānāṃ pāpābhyāsapravartinām  || 428 ||

eteṣvevābhiyogeṣu nindyeṣveva ca yatnataḥ  |
divyaṃ prakalpyennaiva rājā dharmaparāyaṇaḥ  || 429 ||

etaireva niyuktānāṃ sādhūnāṃ divyamarhati  |
necchanti sādhavo yatra tatra śodhyāḥ svakairnaraiḥ  || 430 ||

mahāpātakayukteṣu nāstikeṣu viśeṣataḥ  |
na deyaṃ teṣu divyaṃ tu pāpābhyāsarateṣu ca  || 431 ||

eṣu vādeṣu divyāni pratiṣiddhāni yatnataḥ  |
kārayetsajjanaistāni nābhiśastaṃ tyajenmanuḥ  || 432 ||

aspṛśyādhamadāsānāṃ mlecchānāṃ pāpakāriṇām  |
prātilomyapasūtānāṃ niścayo na tu rājani  |
tatprasiddhāni divyāni saṃśaye teṣu nirdiśet || 433 ||

[divyadeśāḥ]

indrasthāne'bhiśastānāṃ mahāpātakināṃ nṛṇām  |
nṛpadrohe pravṛttānāṃ rājadvāre prayojayet || 434 ||

prātilomyaprasūtānāṃ divyaṃ deyaṃ catuṣpathe  |
ato'nyeṣu sabhāmadhye divyaṃ deyaṃ vidurbudhāḥ  || 435 ||

kāladeśavirodhe tu yathāyuktaṃ prakalpayet |
anyena hārayeddivyaṃ vidhireṣa viparyaye  || 436 ||

adeśakāladattāni bahirvāsakṛtāni ca  |
vyabhicāraṃ sadārtheṣu kurvantīha na saṃśayaḥ  || 437 ||

sādhayettatpunaḥ sādhyaṃ vyāghāte sādhanasya hi  |
dattānyapi yathoktāni rājā divyāni varjayet |
mūrkhairlubdhaiśca duṣṭaiśca punardeyāni tāni vai  || 438 ||

tasmādyathoktavidhinā divyaṃ deyaṃ viśāradaiḥ  |
ayathoktaprayuktaṃ tu na śaktaṃ tasya sādhane  || 439 ||

śikyacchede tulābhaṅge tathā vāpi guṇasya vā  |
śuddhestu saṃśaye caiva parīkṣeta punarnaram  || 440 ||


[agnidivyavidhiḥ]

praskhalatyabhiyuktaścetsthānādanyatra dahyate  |
na dagdhaṃ tu vidurdevāstasya bhūyo'pi dāpayet || 441 ||


[udakadivyavidhiḥ]

śarāṃstvanāyasairagraiḥ prakurvīta viśuddhaye  |
veṇakāṇḍamayāṃścaiva kṣeptā ca sudṛḍhaṃ kṣipet || 442 ||

kṣipte tu majjanaṃ kāryaṃ gamanaṃ samakālikam  |
gamane tvāgamaḥ kāryaḥ pumānanyo jale viśet || 443 ||

śiromānaṃ tu dṛśyeta na karṇau nāpi nāsikā  |
apsu praveśane yasya śuddhaṃ tamapi nirdiśet || 444 ||

nimajjyotplavate yastu dṛṣṭaścetprāṇibhirnaraḥ  |
punastatra nimajjetsa deśacihnavibhāvite  || 445 ||


[viṣadivyavidhiḥ]

ajāśṛṅganibhaṃ śyāmaṃ supīnaṃ śṛṅgasaṃbhavam  |
bhaṅge ca śṛṅgaverābhaṃ khyātaṃ tacśṛṅgiṇāṃ viṣam  || 446 ||

raktaṃ tadasitaṃ kuryātkaṭinaṃ caiva tallakṣaṇāt |
anena vidhinā jñeyaṃ divyaṃ divyaviśāradaiḥ  || 447 ||

vatsanābhanibhaṃ pītaṃ varṇajñānena niścayaḥ  |
śuktiśaṅkhākṛtirbhaṅge vidyāttadvatsanābhakam  || 448 ||

madhukṣīrasamāyuktaṃ svacchaṃ kurvīta tatkṣaṇāt |
bāhyamevaṃ samākhyātaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ dharmasādhakaiḥ  || 449 ||

pūrvāhṇe śītale deśe viṣaṃ dadyāttu dehinām  |
ghṛtena yojitaṃ ślakṣṇaṃ piṣṭaṃ triṃśadguṇena tu  || 450 ||

viṣasya palaṣaḍbhāgādbhāgo viṃśatimastu yaḥ  |
tamaṣṭabhāgahīnaṃ śodhye deyaṃ ghṛtāplutam  || 451 ||


[kośadivyavidhiḥ]

svalpe'parādhe devānāṃ snāpayitvāyudhodakam  |
pāyyo vikāre cāśuddho niyamyaḥ śuciranyathā  || 452 ||


[taṇḍulavidhiḥ]

devatāsnānapānīya- divye taṇḍulabhakṣaṇe  |
śuddhaniṣṭhīvanācśuddho niyamyo'śuciranyathā  || 453 ||

avaṣṭambhābhiyuktasya viśuddhasyāpi kośataḥ  |
sadaṇḍamabhiyogaṃ ca dāpayedabhiyojakam  |
divyena śuddhaṃ puruṣaṃ satkuryāddhārmiko nṛpaḥ  || 454 ||

śoṇitaṃ dṛśyate yatra hanuvālaṃ ca sīdati  |
gātraṃ ca kampate yasya tamaśuddhaṃ vinirdiśet || 455 ||

atha daivavisaṃvādāttrisaptāhāttu dāpayet |
abhiyuktaṃ tu yatnena tamarthaṃ daṇḍameva ca  || 456 ||

tasyaikasya na sarvasya janasya yadi tadbhavet |
rogo'gnirjñātimaraṇamṛṇaṃ dāpyo damaṃ ca saḥ  || 457 ||

kṣayātisāravisphoṭāstālvasthiparipīḍanam  |
netraruggalarogaśca tathonmādaḥ prajāyate  |
śirorugbhujabhaṅgaśca daivikā vyādhayo nṛṇām  || 458 ||

śatārdhaṃ dāpayecśuddhamaśuddho daṇḍabhāgbhavet || 459 ||

viṣe toye hutāśe ca tulākośe ca taṇḍule  |
taptamāṣakadivye ca kramāddaṇḍaṃ prakalpayet || 460 ||

sahasraṃ ṣaṭśataṃ caiva tathā pañca śatāni ca  |
catustridvyekamevaṃ ca hīnaṃ hīneṣu kalpayet || 461 ||


[śapathavidhiḥ]

yatropadiśyate karma karturaṅgaṃ na tūcyate  |
dakṣiṇastatra vijñeyaḥ karmaṇāṃ pāragaḥ karaḥ  || 462 ||

ācaturdaśakādahno yasya no rājadaivikam  |
vyasanaṃ jāyate ghoraṃ sa jñeyaḥ śapathe śuciḥ  || 463 ||


[unmattāsvatantrādikṛtānāṃ vicāraḥ]

unmattenaiva mattena tathā bhāvāntareṇa vā  |
yaddattaṃ yatkṛtaṃ vātha pramāṇaṃ naiva tadbhavet || 464 ||

asvatantrakṛtaṃ kāryaṃ tasya svāmī nivartayet |
na bhartrā vivadetānyo bhītonmattakṛtādṛte  || 465 ||

pitāsvatantraḥ pitṛmān bhrātā bhātṛvya eva vā  |
kaniṣṭho vāvibhaktasvo dāsaḥ karmakarastathā  || 466 ||

na kṣetragṛhadāsānāṃ dānādhamanavikrayāḥ  |
asvatantrakṛtāḥ siddhiṃ prāpnuyurnānuvarṇitāḥ  || 467 ||

pramāṇaṃ sarva evaite paṇyānāṃ krayavikraye  |
yadi saṃvyavahāraṃ te kurvanto'pyanumoditāḥ  || 468 ||

kṣetrādīṇāṃ tathaiva syurbhrātā bhrātṛsutaḥ sutaḥ  |
nisṛṣṭāḥ kṛtyakaraṇe guruṇā yadi gacchatā  || 469 ||

nisṛṣṭārthastu yo yasmin tasminnarthe prabhustu saḥ  |
tadbhartā tatkṛtaṃ kāryaṃ nānyathā kartumarhati  || 470 ||

sutasya sutadārāṇāṃ vaśitvaṃ tvanuśāsane  |
vikraye caiva dāne ca vaśitvaṃ na sute pituḥ  || 471 ||


[nirṇayakṛtyam]

śuddhistu śāstratattvajñaiścikitsā samudāhṛtā  |
prāyaścittaṃ ca daṇḍaṃ ca tābhyāṃ sā dvividhā smṛtā  || 472 ||

anekārthābhiyoge'pi yāvatsaṃsādhayeddhanī  |
sākṣibhistāvadevāsau labhate sādhitaṃ dhanam  || 473 ||

sarvāpalāpaṃ yaḥ kṛtvā mitho'lpamapi saṃvadet |
sarvameva tu dāpyaḥ syādabhiyukto bṛhaspatiḥ  || 474 ||

evaṃ dharmāsanasthena samenaiva vivādinā  |
kāryāṇāṃ nirṇayo dṛśyo brāhmaṇaiḥ saha nānyathā  || 475 ||

vyavahārān svayaṃ dṛṣṭvā śrutvā vā prāṅvivākataḥ  |
jayapatraṃ tato dadyātparijñānāya pārthivaḥ  || 476 ||


[daṇḍavidhiḥ]

rājā tu svāmine vipraṃ sāntvenaiva pradāpayet |
deśācāreṇa cānyāṃstu duṣṭān saṃpīḍya dāpayet || 477 ||

rikthinaṃ suhṛdaṃ vāpi cchalenaiva pradāpayet |
vaṇijaḥ karṣakāṃścāpi śilpinaścābravīdbhṛguḥ  || 478 ||

dhanadānāsahaṃ buddhvā svādhīnaṃ karma kārayet |
aśaktau bandhanāgāraṃ praveśyo brāhmaṇādṛte  || 479 ||

karṣakān kṣatraviśśūdrān samīhānāṃstu dāpayet || 480 ||

ācāryasya piturmāturbāndhavānāṃ tathaiva ca  |
eteṣāmaparādheṣu daṇḍo naiva vidhīyate  || 481 ||

prāṇātyaye tu yatra syādakāryakaraṇaṃ kṛtam  |
daṇḍastatra tu naiva syādeṣa dharmo bhṛgusmṛtaḥ  || 482 ||

na jātu brāhmaṇaṃ hanyātsarvapāpeṣvavasthitam  |
rāṣṭrāccainaṃ bahiḥ kuryātsamagradhanamakṣatam  || 483 ||

caturṇāmapi varṇānāṃ prāyaścittamakurvatām  |
śarīradhanasaṃyuktaṃ daṇḍaṃ dharmyaṃ prakalpayet || 484 ||

yena doṣeṇa śūdrasya daṇḍo bhavati dharmataḥ  |
tena cetkṣatraviprāṇāṃ dviguṇo dviguṇo bhavet || 485 ||

pravrajyāvasitaṃ śūdraṃ japahomaparāyaṇam  |
vadhena śāsayetpāpaṃ daṇḍyo vā dviguṇaṃ damam  || 486 ||

sarveṣu cāparādheṣu puṃso yo'rthadamaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
tadardhaṃ yoṣito dadyurvadhe puṃso'ṅga kartanam  || 487 ||

nāsvatantrāḥ striyo grāhyāḥ pumāṃstatrāparādhyati  |
prabhuṇā śāsanīyāstā rājā tu puruṣaṃ nayet || 488 ||

proṣitasvāmikā nārī prāpitā yadyapi grahe  |
tāvatsā bandhane sthāpyā yāvatpratyāgataḥ prabhuḥ  || 489 ||

kalpito yasya yo daṇḍastvaparādhasya yatnataḥ  |
paṇānāṃ grahaṇaṃ tu syāttanmūlyaṃ vātha rājani  || 490 ||

māṣapādo dvipādo vā daṇḍo yatra pravartitaḥ  |
anirdiṣṭaṃ tu vijñeyaṃ māṣakaṃ tu prakalpayet || 491 ||

yatrokto māṣakairdaṇḍo rājataṃ tatra nirdiśeta  |
kṛṣṇalaiścoktameva syāduktadaṇḍaviniścayaḥ  || 492 ||

māṣo viṃśatibhāgastu jñeyaḥ kārṣāpaṇasya tu  |
kākaṇī tu caturbhāgā māṣakasya paṇasya ca  || 493 ||

pañcanadyāḥ pradeśe tu saṃjñeyaṃ vyavahārikī  |
kārṣāpaṇoṇḍikā jñeyāstāścatasrastu dhānakaḥ  |
te dvādaśa suvarṇāstu dīnāraścitrakaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 494 ||


[punarnyāyaḥ]

asatsaditi yaḥ pakṣaḥ sabhyairevāvadhāryate  |
tīritaḥ so'nuśiṣṭastu sākṣivākyātprakīrtitaḥ  || 495 ||

kulādibhirniścite'pi santoṣaṃ na gatastu yaḥ  |
vicārya tatkṛtaṃ rājā kukṛtaṃ punaruddharet || 496 ||


[ṛṇādāne vṛddhivicāraḥ]

na strībhyo dāsabālebhyaḥ prayacchetkvaciduddhṛtam  |
dātā na labhate tattu tebhyo dadyāttu yadvasu  || 497 ||

ṛṇikena tu yā vṛddhiradhikā saṃprakalpitā  |
āpatkālakṛtā nityaṃ dātavyā kāritā tu sā  |
anyathā kāritā vṛddhirna dātavyā kathaṃcana  || 498 ||

ekāntenaiva vṛddhiṃ tu śodhayedyatra carṇikam  |
pratikālaṃ dadātyeva śikhāvṛddhistu sā smṛtā  || 499 ||

gṛhāttoṣaḥ phalaṃ kṣetrādbhogalābhaḥ prakīrtitaḥ  || 500 ||

ādhibhogastvaśeṣo yo vṛddhistu parikalpitaḥ  |
prayogo yatra caivaṃ syādādhibhogaḥ sa ucyate  || 501 ||


[ākṛtavṛddhiḥ]

yo yācitakamādāya tamadattvā diśaṃ vrajet |
ūrdhvaṃ saṃvatsarāttasya taddhanaṃ vṛddhimāpnuyāt || 502 ||

kṛtvoddhāramadattvā yo yācitastu diśaṃ vrajet |
ūrdhvaṃ māsatrayāttasya taddhanaṃ vṛddhimāpnuyāt || 503 ||

svadeśe'pi sthito yastu na dadyādyācitaḥ kvacit |
taṃ tato'kāritāṃ vṛddhimanicchantaṃ ca dāpayet || 504 ||

prītidattaṃ na vardheta yāvanna pratiyācitam  |
yācyamānamadattaṃ cedvardhate pañcakaṃ śatam  || 505 ||

nikṣiptaṃ vṛddhiśeṣaṃ ca krayavikrayameva ca  |
yācyamānamadattaṃ cedvardhate pañcakaṃ śatam  || 506 ||

paṇyaṃ gṛhītvā yo mūlyamadattvaiva diśaṃ vrajet |
ṛtutrayasyāpariṣṭāttaddhanaṃ vṛddhimāpnuyāt || 507 ||

carmasasyāsavadyūte paṇyamūlye ca sarvadā  |
strīśulkeṣu na vṛddhiḥ syātprātibhāvyāgateṣu ca  || 508 ||


[vṛddheḥ parimāṇaṃ]

grāhyaṃ syāddviguṇaṃ dravyaṃ prayuktaṃ dhanināṃ sadā  |
labhate cenna dviguṇaṃ punarvṛddhiṃ prakalpayet || 509 ||

maṇimuktāpravālānāṃ suvarṇarajatasya ca  |
tiṣṭhati dviguṇā vṛddhiḥ phālakaiṭāvikasya ca  || 510 ||

tailānāṃ caiva sarveṣāṃ madyānāmatha sarpiṣām  |
vṛddhiraṣṭaguṇā jñeyā guḍasya lavaṇasya ca  || 511 ||

kupyaṃ pañcaguṇaṃ bhūmistathaivāṣṭaguṇā matā  |
sadya eveti vacanātsadya eva pradīyate  || 512 ||


[ṛṇoddharaṇaṃ]
[anekarṇasamavāye vidhiḥ]

ekāhe likhitaṃ yattu tattu kuryādṛṇaṃ samam  |
grahaṇaṃ rakṣaṇaṃ lābhamanyathā tu yathākramam  || 513 ||

nānāṛṇasamavāye tu yadyatpūrvakṛtaṃ bhavet |
tattadevāgrato deyaṃ rājñaḥ syācśrotriyādanu  || 514 ||

yasya dravyeṇa yatpaṇyaṃ sādhitaṃ yo vibhāvayet |
taddravyamṛṇikenaiva dātavyaṃ tasya nānyathā  || 515 ||


[ādhiḥ]

dravyaṃ gṛhītvā vṛddhyarthaṃ bhogayogyaṃ dadāti cet |
jaṅgamaṃ sthāvaraṃ vāpi bhogyādhiḥ sa tu kathyate  |
mūlyaṃ tadādhikaṃ dattvā svakṣetrādikamāpnuyāt || 516 ||

ādhimekaṃ dvayoryastu kuryātkā pratipadbhavet |
tayoḥ pūrvakṛtaṃ grāhyaṃ tatkartā coradaṇḍabhāk || 517 ||

ādhānaṃ vikrayo dānaṃ lekhyasākṣyakṛtaṃ yadā  |
ekakriyāviruddhaṃ tu lekhyaṃ tatrāpahārakam  || 518 ||

anirdiṣṭaṃ ca nirdiṣṭamekatra ca vilekhitam  |
viśeṣalikhitaṃ jyāya iti kātyāyano'bravīt || 519 ||

yo'vidyamānaṃ prathamamanirdiṣṭasvarūpakam  |
ākāśabhūtamādadhyādanirdiṣṭaṃ ca tadbhavet |
yadyattadāsya vidyeta tadādiṣṭaṃ vinirdiśet || 520 ||

yastu sarvasvamādiśya prākpaścānnāmacihnitam  |
ādadhyāttatkathaṃ na syāccihnitaṃ balavattaram  || 521 ||

maryādācihnitaṃ kṣetraṃ grāmaṃ vāpi yadā bhavet |
grāmādayaśca likhyante tadā siddhimavāpnuyāt || 522 ||

ādhīkṛtaṃ tu yatkiṃcidvinaṣṭaṃ daivarājataḥ  |
tatra ṛṇaṃ sodayaṃ dāpyo dhanināmadhamarṇakaḥ  || 523 ||

na ceddhanikadoṣeṇa nipatedvā mriyeta vā  |
ādhimanyaṃ sa dāpyaḥ syādṛṇānmucyeta narṇikaḥ  || 524 ||

akāmamananujñātamadhiṃ yaḥ karma kārayet |
bhoktā karmaphalaṃ dāpyo vṛddhiṃ vā labhate na saḥ  || 525 ||

yastvādhiṃ karma kurvāṇaṃ vācā daṇḍena karmabhiḥ  |
pīḍayedbhatsayeccaiva prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 526 ||

balādakāmaṃ yatrādhimanisṛṣṭaṃ praveśayet |
prāpnuyātsāhasaṃ pūrvamādhātā cādhimāpnuyāt || 527 ||

ādhiṃ duṣṭena lekhyena bhuṅkte yamṛṇikāddhanī  |
nṛpo damaṃ dāpayitvā ādhikekhyaṃ vināśayet || 528 ||

ādhātā yatra na syāttu dhanī bandhaṃ nivedayet |
rājñastataḥ sa vikhyāto vikreya iti dhāraṇā  |
savṛddhikaṃ gṛhītvā tu śeṣaṃ rājan yathārpayet || 529 ||


[pratibhūvidhānam]

dānopasthānavādeṣu viśvāsaśapathāya ca  |
lagnakaṃ kārayedevaṃ yathāyogaṃ viparyaye  || 530 ||

darśanapratibhūryastaṃ deśe kāle na darśayet |
nibandhamāvahettatra daivarājakṛtādṛte  || 531 ||

naṣṭasyānveṣaṇārthaṃ tu deyaṃ pakṣatrayaṃ param  |
yadyasau darśayettatra moktavyaḥ pratibhūrbhavet || 532 ||

kāle vyatīte pratibhūryadi taṃ naiva darśayet |
sa tamarthaṃ pradāpyaḥ syātprete caivaṃ vidhīyate  || 533 ||

gṛhītvā bandhakaṃ yatra darśane'sya sthito bhavet |
vinā pitrā dhanaṃ tasmāddāpyaḥ syāttadṛṇaṃ sutaḥ  || 534 ||

yo yasya pratibhūstiṣṭheddarśanāyeha mānavaḥ  |
adarśayan sa taṃ tasmai prayacchetsvadhanādṛṇam  || 535 ||

ādyau tu vitathe dāpyau tatkālāveditaṃ dhanam  |
uttarau tu visaṃvāde tau vinā tatsutau tathā  || 536 ||

ekacchāyāśrite sarvaṃ dadyāttu proṣite sutaḥ  |
mṛte pitari pitṛaṃśaṃ pararṇaṃ na bṛhaspatiḥ  || 537 ||

ekacchāyāpraviṣṭānāṃ dāpyo tastatra dṛśyate  |
proṣite tatsutaḥ sarvaṃ pitraṃśaṃ tu mṛte sutaḥ  || 538 ||

prātibhāvyaṃ tu yo dadyātpīḍitaḥ pratibhāvitaḥ  |
tripakṣātparataḥ so'rthaṃ dviguṇaṃ labdhumarhati  || 539 ||

yasyārthe yena yaddattaṃ vidhinābhyarthitena tu  |
sākṣibhirbhāvitenaiva pratibhūstatsamāpnuyāt || 540 ||

satyaṃkāravisaṃvāde dviguṇaṃ pratidāpayet |
akurvatastu taddhāni satyaṃkāraprayojanam  || 541 ||


[pitrādibhiḥ kṛtamṛṇaṃ kena pratideyam]

kuṭumbārthamaśaktena gṛhītaṃ vyādhitena vā  |
upaplavanimitte ca vidyādāpatkṛte tu tat || 542 ||

kanyāvaivāhikaṃ caiva pretakārye ca yatkṛtam  |
etatsarvaṃ pradātavyaṃ kuṭumbena kṛtaṃ prabhoḥ  || 543 ||

ṛṇaṃ putrakṛtaṃ pitrā na deyamiti dharmataḥ  |
deyaṃ pratiśrutaṃ yatsyātyacca syādanumoditam  || 544 ||

proṣitasyāmatenāpi kuṭumbārthamṛṇaṃ kṛtam  |
dāsastrīmātṛśiṣyairvā dadyātputreṇa vā bhṛguḥ  || 545 ||

bhartrā putreṇa vā sārdhaṃ kevalenātmanā kṛtam  |
ṛṇamevaṃvidhaṃ deyaṃ nānyathā tatkṛtaṃ striyā  || 546 ||

martukāmena yā bhartrā proktā deyamṛṇaṃ tvayā  |
aprapannāpi sā dāpyā dhanaṃ yadyāśritaṃ striyām  || 547 ||

vidyamāneapi rogārte svadeśātproṣite'pi vā  |
viṃśātsaṃvatsarāddeyamṛṇaṃ pitṛkṛtaṃ sutaiḥ  || 548 ||

vyādhitonmattavṛddhānāṃ tathā dīrghapravāsinām  |
ṛṇamevaṃvidhaṃ putrāñjīvatāmapi dāpayet || 549 ||

sāṃnidhye'pi pituḥ putrairṛṇaṃ deyaṃ vibhāvitam  |
jātyandhapatitonmatta- kṣayaśvitrādirogiṇaḥ  || 550 ||

pitṝṇāṃ sūnubhirjātairdānenaivādhamādṛṇāt |
vimokṣastu yatastasmādicchanti pitaraḥ sutān  || 551 ||

nāprāptavyavahāreṇa pitaryuparate kvacit |
kāle tu vidhinā deyaṃ vaseyurnarake'nyathā  || 552 ||

aprāptavyavahāraścetsvatantro'pīha narṇabhāk |
svātantryaṃ hi smṛtaṃ jyeṣṭhe jyaiṣṭhe guṇavayaḥkṛtam  || 553 ||

yaddṛṣṭaṃ dattaśeṣaṃ vā deyaṃ paitāmahaṃ tu tat |
sadoṣaṃ vyāhataṃ pitrā naiva deyamṛṇaṃ kvacit || 554 ||

pitrā dṛṣṭamṛṇaṃ yattu kramāyātaṃ pitāmahāt |
nirdoṣaṃ noddhṛtaṃ putrairdeyaṃ pautraistu tadbhṛguḥ  || 555 ||

paitāmahaṃ tu yatputrairna dattaṃ rogibhiḥ sthitaiḥ  |
tasmādevaṃvidhaṃ pautrairdeyaṃ paitāmahaṃ samam  || 556 ||

ṛṇaṃ tu dāpayetputraṃ yadi syānnirupadravaḥ  |
draviṇārhaśca dhuryaśca nānyathā dāpayetsutam  || 557 ||

yaddeyaṃ pitṛbhirnityaṃ tadabhāve tu taddhanāt |
taddhanaṃ putraputrairvā deyaṃ tatsvāmine tadā  || 558 ||

pitrarṇe vidyamāne tu na ca putro dhanaṃ haret |
deyaṃ taddhanike dravyaṃ mṛte gṛhṇaṃstu dāpyate  || 559 ||

putrābhāve tu dātavyamṛṇaṃ pautreṇa yatnataḥ  |
caturthena na dātavyaṃ tasmāttadvinirvartate  || 560 ||

prātibhāvyāgataṃ pautrairdātavyaṃ na tu tatkvacit |
putreṇāpi samaṃ deyamṛṇaṃ sarvatra paitṛkam  || 561 ||

rikthahartrā ṛṇaṃ deyaṃ tadabhāve ca yoṣitaḥ  |
putraiśca tadabhāve'nyai rikthabhāgbhiryathākramam  || 562 ||

yāvanna paitṛkaṃ dravyaṃ vidyamānaṃ labhetsutaḥ  |
susamṛddo'pi dāpyaḥ syāttāvannaivādhamarṇikaḥ  || 563 ||

likhitaṃ muktakaṃ vāpi deyaṃ yattu pratiśrutam  |
parapūrvastriyai yattu vidyātkāmakṛtaṃ nṛṇām  || 564 ||

yatra hiṃsāṃ samutpādya krodhāddravyaṃ vināśya vā  |
uktaṃ tuṣṭikaraṃ yattu vidyādkrodhakṛtaṃ tu tat || 565 ||

svasthenārtena vā deyaṃ bhāvitaṃ dharmakāraṇāt |
adattvā tu mṛte dāpyastatsuto nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 566 ||

nirdhanairanapatyaistu yatkṛtaṃ śauṇḍikādibhiḥ  |
tatstrīṇāmupabhoktā tu dadyāttadṛṇameva hi  || 567 ||

śauṇḍikavyādhajanaka- gopanāvikayoṣitām  |
adhiṣṭhātā ṛṇaṃ dāpyastāsāṃ bhartṛkriyāsu tat || 568 ||

na ca bhāryākṛtamṛṇaṃ kathaṃcitpatyurābhavet |
āpatkṛtādṛte puṃsāṃ kuṭumbārthe hi vistaraḥ  || 569 ||

anyatra rajakavyādha- gopaśauṇḍikayoṣitām  |
teṣāṃ tu tatparā vṛttiḥ kuṭumbaṃ ca tadāśrayam  || 570 ||

amatenaiva putrasya pradhanā yānyamāśrayet |
putreṇaivāpahāryaṃ tad- dhanaṃ duhitṛbhirvinā  || 571 ||

ṛṇārthamāharettantuṃ na sukhārthaṃ kadācana  |
ayukte kāraṇe yasmātpitarau tu na dāpayet || 572 ||

yā svaputraṃ tu jahyātstrī samarthamapi putriṇī  |
āhṛtya strīdhanaṃ tatra pitryarṇaṃ śodhayenmanuḥ  || 573 ||

bālaputrādhikārthā ca bhartāraṃ yānyamāśritā  |
āśritastadṛṇaṃ dadyādbālaputrāvidhiḥ smṛtaḥ  || 574 ||

dīrghapravāsinirbandhu- jaḍonmattārtaliṅginām  |
jīvatāmapi dātavyaṃ tatstrīdravyasamāśritaiḥ  || 575 ||

vyasanābhiplute putre bālo vā yatna dṛśyate  |
dravyahṛddāpyate tatra tasyābhāve purandhrihṛt || 576 ||

pūrvaṃ dadyāddhanagrāhaḥ putrastasmādanantaram  |
yoṣidgrāhaḥ sutābhāve putro vātyantanirdhanaḥ  || 577 ||

deyaṃ bhāryākṛtamṛṇaṃ bhartrā putreṇa mātṛkam  |
bharturarthe kṛtaṃ yatsyādabhidhāya gate diśam  || 578 ||

deyaṃ putrakṛtaṃ tatsyādyacca syādanuvarṇitam  |
kṛtāsaṃvāditaṃ yacca śrutvā caivānucoditam  || 579 ||


[adharmaṇikasyāvarodhādinā dhanoddhāravicāra]

dhāryo'varuddhastvṛṇikaḥ prakāśaṃ janasaṃsadi  |
yāvanna dadyāddeyaṃ ca deśācārasthitiryathā  || 580 ||

viṇmūtraśaṅkā yasya syāddhāryamāṇasya dehinaḥ  |
pṛṣṭhato vānugantavyo nibaddhaṃ vā samutsṛjet || 581 ||

sa kṛtapratibhūścaiva moktavyaḥ syāddine dine  |
āhārakāle rātrau ca nibandhe pratibhūḥ sthitaḥ  || 582 ||

yo darśanapratibhuvaṃ nādhigacchenna cāśrayet |
sa cārake niroddhavyaḥ sthāpyo vāvedya rakṣiṇaḥ  || 583 ||

na cārake niroddhavya āryaḥ prātyayikaḥ śuciḥ  |
so'nibaddhaḥ pramoktavyo nibaddhaḥ śapathena vā  || 584 ||

pīḍanenoparodhena sādhayedṛṇikaṃ dhanī  |
karmaṇā vyavahāreṇa sāntvenādau vibhāvitaḥ  || 585 ||

ādadītārthamevaṃ tu vyājenācaritena ca  |
karmaṇā kṣatraviśśūdrān samahīnāṃśca dāpayet || 586 ||

rājānaṃ svāminaṃ vipraṃ sāntvenaiva pradāpayet |
rikthinaṃ suhṛdaṃ vāpi cchalenaiva prasādhayet || 587 ||

vaṇijaḥ karṣakāścaiva śilpinaścābravīdbhṛguḥ  |
deśācāreṇa dāpyāḥ syurduṣṭān saṃpīḍya dāpayet || 588 ||

pīḍayettu dhanī yatra ṛṇikaṃ nyāyavādinam  |
tasmādarthātsa hīyeta tatsamaṃ cāpnuyāddamam  || 589 ||

yadi hyādāvanādiṣṭamaśubhaṃ karma kārayet |
prāpnuyātsāhasaṃ pūrvamṛṇānmucyeta carṇikaḥ  || 590 ||

uddhārādikamādāya svāmine na dadāti yaḥ  |
sa tasya dāso bhṛtyaḥ strī paśurvā jāyate gṛhe  || 591 ||


[upanidhiḥ]

trayaproṣitanikṣipta- bandhānvāhitayācitam  |
vaiśyavṛttyarpitaṃ caiva so'rthastūpanidhiḥ smṛtaḥ  || 592 ||

nikṣiptaṃ yasya yatkiṃcittatprayatnena pālayet |
daivarājakṛtādanyo vināśastasya kīrtyate  || 593 ||

yasya doṣeṇa yatkiṃcidvināśyeta hriyeta vā  |
taddravyaṃ sodayaṃ dāpyo daivarājakṛtādvinā  || 594 ||

yācitānantaraṃ nāśe daivarājakṛte'pi saḥ  |
grahītā pratidāpyaḥ syānmūlyamātraṃ na saṃśayaḥ  || 595 ||

nyāsādikaṃ paradravyaṃ prabhakṣitamupekṣitam  |
ajñānanāśitaṃ caiva yena dāpyaḥ sa eva tat || 596 ||

bhakṣitaṃ sodayaṃ dāpyaḥ samaṃ dāpya upekṣitam  |
kiṃcinnyūnaṃ pradāpyaḥ syāddravyamajñānanāśitam  || 597 ||

arājadaivikenāpi nikṣiptaṃ yatra nāśitam  |
grahītuḥ saha bhāṇḍena dāturnaṣṭaṃ taducyate  || 598 ||

jñātvā dravyaviyogaṃ tu dātā yatra vinikṣipet |
sarvopāyavināśe'pi grahītā naiva dāpyate  || 599 ||

grāhakasya hi yaddoṣānnaṣṭaṃ tu grāhakasya tat |
tasminnaṣṭe hṛte vāpi grahītā mūlyamāharet || 600 ||

grāhyastūpanidhiḥ kāle kālahīnaṃ tu varjayet |
kālahīnaṃ dadaddaṇḍaṃ dviguṇaṃ ca pradāpyate  || 601 ||

sarveṣūpanidhiṣvete vidhayaḥ parikīrtitāḥ  || 602 ||

yaiśca saṃskriyate nyāso divasaiḥ pariniścitaiḥ  |
tadūrdhvaṃ sthāpayecśilpī dāpyo daivahate'pi tat || 603 ||

nyāsadoṣādvināśaḥ syācśilpinaṃ tanna dāpayet |
dāpayecśilpidoṣāttatsaṃskārārthaṃ yadarpitam  || 604 ||

svalpenāpi ca yatkarma naṣṭaṃ cedbhṛtakasya tat |
paryāptaṃ ditsatastasya vinaśyettadagṛhṇataḥ  || 605 ||

yadi tatkāryamuddiśya kālaṃ pariniyamya vā  |
yācito'rdhakṛte tasminnaprāpte na tu dāpyate  || 606 ||

prāptakāle kṛte kārye na dadyādyācito'pi san  |
tasminnaṣṭe vāpi grahītā mūlyamāharet || 607 ||

yācyamāno na dadyādvā dāpyastatsodayaṃ bhavet || 608 ||

atha kāryavipattistu tasyaiva svāmino bhavet |
aprāpte vai sa kāle tu dāpyastvardhakṛte'pi tat || 609 ||

yo yācitakamādāya na dadyātpratiyācitaḥ  |
sa nigṛhya balāddāpyo daṇḍyaśca na dadāti yaḥ  || 610 ||

anumārgeṇa kāryeṣu anyasmin vacanānmama  |
dadyāstvamiti yo dattaḥ sa ihānvādhirucyate  || 611 ||


[asvāmivikrayaḥ]

asvāmivikrayaṃ dānamādhiṃ ca vinivartayet || 612 ||

abhiyoktā dhanaṃ kuryātprathamaṃ jñātibhiḥ svakam  |
paścādātmaviśudhyarthaṃ krayaṃ ketā svabandhubhiḥ  || 613 ||

nāṣṭikastu prakurvīta taddhanaṃ jñātṛbhiḥ svakam  |
adattatyaktavikrītaṃ kṛtvā svaṃ labhate dhanam  || 614 ||

prakāśaṃ vā krayaṃ kuryānmūlaṃ vāpi samarpayet |
mūlānayanakālastu deyo yojanasaṃkhyayā  || 615 ||

prakāśaṃ ca krayaṃ kuryātsādhubhirjñātibhiḥ svakaiḥ  |
na tatrānyā kriyā proktā daivikī na ca mānuṣī  || 616 ||

yadā mūlamupanyasya punarvādī krayaṃ vadet |
āharenmūlamevāsau na krayeṇa prayojanam  || 617 ||

asamāhāryamūlastu krayameva viśodhayet |
viśodhite kraye rājñā na vaktavyaḥ sa kiṃcana  || 618 ||

anupasthāpayanmūlaṃ krayaṃ vāpyaviśodhayan  |
yathābhiyogaṃ dhanine dhanaṃ dāpyo damaṃ ca saḥ  || 619 ||

yadi svaṃ naiva kurute jñātibhirnāṣṭiko dhanam  |
prasaṅgavinivṛttyarthaṃ coravaddaṇḍamarhati  || 620 ||

vaniṅvīthīparigataṃ vijñātaṃ rājapuruṣaiḥ  |
avijñātāśrayātkrītaṃ vikretā yatra vā mṛtaḥ  || 621 ||

svāmī datvārdhamūlyaṃ tu pragṛhṇīta svaka dhanam  |
ardhaṃ dvayorapahṛtaṃ tatra syādvyavahārataḥ  || 622 ||

avijñātakrayo doṣastathā cāparipālanam  |
etaddvayaṃ samākhyātaṃ dravyahānikaraṃ buddhaiḥ  || 623 ||


[sambhūyasamutthānam]

samavetāstu ye kecicśalpino vaṇijo'pi vā  |
avibhajya pṛthagbhūtaiḥ prāptaṃ tatra phalaṃ samam  || 624 ||

bhāṇḍapiṇḍavyayoddhāra- bhārasārārthavīkṣaṇam  |
kuryuste'vyabhicāreṇa samayena vyavasthitāḥ  || 625 ||

prayogaṃ kurvate ye tu hemadhānyarasādinā  |
samanyūnādhikairaṃśairlābhasteṣāṃ tathāvidhaḥ  || 626 ||

bahūnāṃ saṃmato yastu dadyādeko dhanaṃ naraḥ  |
ṛṇaṃ ca kārayedvāpi sarvaireva kṛtaṃ bhavet || 627 ||

jñātisaṃbandhisuhṛdāmṛṇaṃ deyaṃ sabandhakam  |
anyeṣāṃ lagnakopetaṃ lekhyasākṣiyutaṃ tathā  || 628 ||

svecchādeyaṃ hiraṇyaṃ tu rasā dhānyaṃ ca sāvidhi  |
deśasthityā pradātavyaṃ grahītavyaṃ tathaiva ca  || 629 ||

samavetaistu yaddattaṃ prārthanīyaṃ tathaiva tat |
na ca yāceta yaḥ kaścillābhātsa parihīyate  || 630 ||

corataḥ salilādagnerdravyaṃ yastu samāharet |
tasyāṃśo daśamo deyaḥ sarvavādeṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ  || 631 ||

śikṣakābhijñakuśalā ācāryaśceti śilpinaḥ  |
ekadvitricaturbhāgān hareyuste yathottaram  || 632 ||

pararāṣṭrāddhanaṃ yatsyāccauraiḥ svāmyājñayāhṛtam  |
rājño daśāṃśamuddhṛtya vibhajeran yathāvidhi  || 633 ||

corāṇāṃ mukhyabhūtastu caturo'ṃśāṃstato haret |
śūro'ṃśāṃstrīn samartho dvau śoṣāstvekaikameva ca  || 634 ||

teṣāṃ cetprasṛtānāṃ yo grahaṇaṃ samavāpnuyāt |
tanmokṣaṇārthaṃ yaddattaṃ vaheyuste yathāṃśataḥ  || 635 ||

nartakānāmeṣa eva dharmaḥ sadbhirudāhṛtaḥ  |
tālajño labhate hyardhaṃ gāyanāstu samāṃśinaḥ  |
pramukhā dvyaṃśamarhanti so'yaṃ saṃbhūya kurvatām  || 636 ||

vaṇijāṃ karṣakāṇāṃ ca corāṇāṃ śilpināṃ tathā  |
aniyamyāṃśakartṝṇāṃ sarveṣāmeṣa nirṇayaḥ  || 637 ||


[dattānapākarma dattāpradānikaṃ vā]

vikrayaṃ caiva dānaṃ ca na neyāḥ syuranicchavaḥ  |
dārāḥ putrāśca sarvasvamātmanaiva tu yojayet || 638 ||

āpatkāle tu kartavyaṃ dānaṃ vikraya eva vā  |
anyathā na pravarteta iti śāstraviniścayaḥ  || 639 ||

sarvasvagṛhavarjaṃ tu kuṭumbabharaṇādhikam  |
yaddravyaṃ tatsvakaṃ deyamadeyaṃ syādato'nyathā  || 640 ||

ataśca sutadārāṇāṃ vaśitvaṃ tvanuśāsane  |
vikraye caiva dāne ca vaśitvaṃ na sute pituḥ  || 641 ||

svecchayā yaḥ pratiśrutya brāhmaṇāya pratigraham  |
na dadyādṛṇava dāpyaḥ prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 642 ||

pratiśrutasyādānena dattasyācchādanena ca  |
kalpakoṭiśataṃ martyastiryagyonau ca jāyate  || 643 ||

avijñātopalabdhyarthaṃ dānaṃ yatra nirūpitam  |
upalabdhikriyālabdhaṃ sā bhṛtiḥ parikīrtitā  || 644 ||

bhayatrāṇāya rakṣārthaṃ tathā kāryaprasādhanāt |
anena vidhinā labdhaṃ vidyātpratyupakārataḥ  || 645 ||

prāṇasaṃśayamāpannaṃ yo māmuttārayeditaḥ  |
sarvasvaṃ tasya dāsyāmītyukte'pi na tathā bhavet || 646 ||

kāmakrodhāsvatantrārta- klībonmattapramohitaiḥ  |
vyatyāsaparihāsācca yaddattaṃ tatpunarharet || 647 ||

yā tu kāryasya siddhyarthamutkocā syātpratiśrutā  |
tasminnapi pasiddhe'rthe na deyā syātkathaṃcana  || 648 ||

atha prāgeva dattā syātpratidāpyastathā balāt |
daṇḍaṃ caikādaśaguṇamāhurgārgīyamānavāḥ  || 649 ||

stenasāhasikodvṛtta- pārajāyikaśaṃsanāt |
darśanādvṛttanaṣṭasya tathāsatyapravartanāt || 650 ||

prāptametaistu yatkiṃcittadutkocākhyamucyate  |
na dātā tatra daṇḍyaḥ syānmadhyasthaścaiva doṣabhāk || 651 ||

niyukto yastu kāryeṣu sa cedutkocamāpnuyāt |
sa dāpyastaddhanaṃ kṛtsnaṃ damaścaikādaśādhikam  || 652 ||

aniyuktastu kāryārthamutkocaṃ yamavāpnuyāt |
kṛtapratyupakārārthastasya doṣo na vidyate  || 653 ||

svasthenārtena vā dattaṃ śrāvritaṃ dharmakāraṇāt |
adattvā tu mṛte dāpyastatsuto nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 654 ||

yogādhamanavikrītaṃ yogadānapatigraham  |
yasya vāpyupadhiṃ paśyettatsarvaṃ vinivartayet || 655 ||

bhṛtāvaniścitāyāṃ tu daśabhāgamavāpnuyāt |
lābhagovīryasasyānāṃ vaṇiggopakṛṣīvalāḥ  || 656 ||


[vetanasyānapākarma]

karmārambhaṃ tu yaḥ kṛtvā siddhaṃ naiva tu kārayet |
balātkārayitavyo'sau akurvan daṇḍamarhati  || 657 ||

vighnayan vāhako dāpyaḥ prasthāne dviguṇāṃ bhṛtim  || 658 ||

na tu dāpyo hṛtaṃ corairdagdhamūḍhaṃ jalena vā  || 659 ||
tyajetpathi sahāyaṃ yaḥ śrāntaṃ rogārtameva vā  |
prāpnuyātsāhasaṃ pūrvaṃ grāme tryahamapālayan  || 660 ||

yadā tu pathi tadbhāṇḍamāsidhyeta hriyeta vā  |
yāvānadhvā gatastena prāpnuyāttāvatīṃ bhṛtim  || 661 ||

hastyaśvagokharoṣṭrādīn gṛhītvā bhāṭakena yaḥ  |
nārpayetkṛtakṛtyārthaḥ sa tu dāpyaḥ sabhāṭakam  || 662 ||

gṛhavāryāpaṇādīṇi gṛhītvā bhāṭakena yaḥ  |
svāmine nārpayedyāvattāvaddāpyaḥ sabhāṭakam  || 663 ||


[svāmipālavivādaḥ]

kṣetrārāmavivīteṣu gṛheṣu paśuvāṭiṣu  |
grahaṇaṃ tatpraviṣṭānāṃ tāḍanaṃ vā bṛhaspatiḥ  || 664 ||

adhamottamamadhyānāṃ paśūnāṃ caiva tāḍane  |
svāmī tu vivadedyatra daṇḍaṃ tatra prakalpayet || 665 ||

ajāteṣveva sasyeṣu kuryādāvaraṇaṃ mahat |
duḥkheneha nivāryante labdhasvādurasā mṛgāḥ  || 666 ||

dāpayetpaṇapādaṃ gāṃ dvau pādau mahiṣīṃ tathā  |
tathājāvikavatsānāṃ pādo daṇḍaḥ prakīrtitaḥ  || 667 ||


[samayasyānapākarma saṃvidvyatikramo vā]

samūhināṃ tu yo dharmastena dharmeṇa te sadā  |
prakuryuḥ sarvakarmāṇi svadharmeṣu vyavasthitāḥ  || 668 ||

avirodhena dharmasya nirgataṃ rājaśāsanam  |
tasyaivācaraṇaṃ pūrvaṃ kartavyaṃ tu nṛpājñayā  || 669 ||

rājapravartitān dharmān yo naro nānupālayet |
garhyaḥ sa pāpo daṇḍyaśca lopayan rājaśāsanam  || 670 ||

yuktiyuktaṃ ca yo hanyādvakturyo'navakāśadaḥ  |
ayuktaṃ caiva yo brūte sa dāpyaḥ pūrvasāhasam  || 671 ||

sāhasī bhedakārī ca gaṇadravyavināśakaḥ  |
ucchedyāḥ sarva evaite vikhyāpyaivaṃ nṛpe bhṛguḥ  || 672 ||

ekapātre ca vā paṅktyāṃ saṃbhoktā yasya yo bhavet |
akurvaṃstattathā daṇḍyastasya doṣamadarśayan  || 673 ||

gaṇamuddiśya yatkiṃcitkṛtvarṇaṃ bhakṣitaṃ bhavet |
ātmārthaṃ viniyuktaṃ vā deyaṃ taireva tadbhavet || 674 ||

gaṇānāṃ śreṇivargāṇāṃ gatāḥ syurye tu madhyatām  |
prāktanasya dhanarṇasya samāṃśāḥ sarva eva te  || 675 ||

tathaiva bhojyavaibhājya- dānadharmakriyāsu ca  |
samūhastho'ṃśabhāgī syātpragatastvaṃśabhāṅna tu  || 676 ||

yattaiḥ prāptaṃ rakṣitaṃ vā gaṇārthe vā ṛṇaṃ kṛtam  |
rājaprasādalabdhaṃ ca sarveṣāmeva tatsamam  || 677 ||


[naigamādisaṃjñālakṣaṇam]

nānāpaurasamūhastu naigamaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  |
nānāyudhadharā vrātāḥ samavetāḥ prakīrtitāḥ  || 678 ||

samūho vaṇijādīnāṃ pūgaḥ saṃparikīrtitaḥ  |
pravrajyāvasitā ye tu pāṣaṇḍāḥ parikīrtitāḥ  || 679 ||

brāhmaṇānāṃ samūhastu gaṇaḥ saṃparikīrtitaḥ  |
śilpopajīvino ye tu śilpinaḥ parikīrtitāḥ  || 680 ||

ārhatasaugatānāṃ tu samūhaḥ saṅgha ucyate  |
cāṇḍālaśvapacādīnāṃ samūho gulma ucyate  || 681 ||

gaṇapāṣaṇḍapūgāśca vrātāśca śreṇayastathā  |
samūhasthāśca ye cānye vargākhyāste bṛhaspatiḥ  || 682 ||


[krayakikrayānuśayaḥ krīsvānuśayo vikrīye saṃpradānaṃ vā]

krītvā prāptaṃ na gṛhṇīyādyo na dadyādadūṣitam  |
sa mūlyāddaśamaṃ bhāgaṃ dattvā svadravyamāpnuyāt || 683 ||

aprāpte'rthakriyākāle kṛte naiva pradāpayet |
evaṃ dharmo daśāhāttu parato'nuśayo na tu  || 684 ||

bhūmerdaśāhe vikreturāyastatkretureva ca  |
dvādaśāhaḥ sapiṇḍānāmapi cālpamataḥ param  || 685 ||

krītvānuśayavān paṇyaṃ tyajeddohyādi yo naraḥ  |
aduṣṭameva kāle tu sa mūlyāddaśamaṃ vahet || 686 ||

krītvā gacchannanuśayaṃ krayī hastamupāgate  |
ṣaḍbhāgaṃ tatra mūlyasya dattvā krītaṃ tyajedbudhaḥ  || 687 ||

avijñātaṃ tu yatkrītaṃ duṣṭaṃ paścādvibhāvitam  |
krītaṃ tatsvāmine deyaṃ kāle cedanyathā na tu  || 688 ||

nirdoṣaṃ darśayitvā tu yaḥ sadoṣaṃ prayacchati  |
mūlyaṃ taddviguṇaṃ dāpyo vinayaṃ tāvadeva ca  || 689 ||

upahanyeta vā paṇyaṃ dahyetāpahriyeta vā  |
vikretureva so'nartho vikrīyāsaṃprayacchataḥ  || 690 ||

dīyamānaṃ na gṛhṇāti krīta paṇyaṃ ca yaḥ krayī  |
vikrītaṃ ca tadanyatra vikretā nāparādhruyāt || 691 ||

mattonmattena vikrītaṃ hīnamūlyaṃ bhayena vā  |
asvatantreṇa mugdhena tyājyaṃ tasya punarbhavet || 692 ||

tryahaṃ dohyaṃ parīkṣeta patrcāhadvāhyameva tu   |
muktāvajrapravālānāṃ saptāhaṃ syātpravīkṣaṇam  || 693 ||

dvipadāmardhamāsaṃ tu puṃsāṃ taddviguṇaṃ striyāḥ  |
daśāhaṃ sarvabījānāmekāhaṃ lohavāsasām  || 694 ||

ato'rvākpaṇyadoṣastu yadi saṃjāyate kvacit |
vikretuḥ pratideyaṃ tatkretā mūlyamavāpnuyāt || 695 ||

paribhuktaṃ tu yadvāsaḥ kliṣṭarūpaṃ malīmasam  |
sadoṣamapi tatkrītaṃ viketurna bhavetpunaḥ  || 696 ||

sādhāraṇaṃ tu yatkrītaṃ naiko dadyānnarādhamaḥ  |
nādadyānna ca gṛhṇīyādvikrīyācca na caiva hi  || 697 ||

krītvā mūlyena yatpaṇyaṃ duṣkrītaṃ manyate krayī  |
vikretuḥ pratideyaṃ tattasminnevāhnyavīkṣitam  || 698 ||

dvitīye'hni dadatkretā mūlyāttryaṃśāṃśamāharet |
dviguṇaṃ tṛtīye'hni parataḥ kretureva tat || 699 ||

dravyasvaṃ pañcadhā kṛtvā tribhāgo mūlyamucyate  |
lābhaścaturtho bhāgaḥ syātpañcamaḥ satyamucyate  || 700 ||

sandhiśca parivṛttiśca viṣamā vā tribhogataḥ  |
ājñayāpi krayaścāpi daśābdaṃ vinivartayet || 701 ||

jñātyādīnananujñāpya samīpasthānaninditān  |
krayavikrayadharmo'pi bhūmernāstīti nirṇayaḥ  || 702 ||

svagrāme daśarātraṃ syādanyagrāme tripakṣakam  |
rāṣṭrāntareṣu ṣaṇmāsaṃ bhāṣābhede tu vatsaram  || 703 ||

palāyite tu karade karapratibhuvā saha  |
karārthaṃ karadakṣetraṃ vikrīṇīyuḥ sabhāsadaḥ  || 704 ||

samavetaistu sāmantairabhijñaiḥ pāpabhīrubhiḥ  |
kṣetrārāmagṛhādīnāṃ dvipadāṃ ca catuṣpadām  || 705 ||

kalpitaṃ mūlyamityāhurbhāgaṃ kṛtvā tadaṣṭadhā  |
ekabhāgātiriktaṃ vā hīnaṃ vānucitaṃ smṛtam  || 706 ||

samāḥ śatamatīte'pi sarvaṃ tadvinivartate  |
krayavikrayaṇe krayyaṃ yanmūlyaṃ dharmato'rhati  || 707 ||

tatturye pañcame ṣaṣṭe saptame'ṃśe'ṣṭame'pi vā  |
hīne yadi vinirvṛtte krayavikrāyaṇe sati  || 708 ||

hīnamūlyaṃ tu tatsarvaṃ kṛtamapyakṛtaṃ bhavet |
uktādalpatare hīne krayo naiva praduṣyati  || 709 ||

tenāpyaṃśena hīyeta mūlyataḥ krayavikraye  |
katamapyakṛtaṃ prāhuranye dharmavido janāḥ  || 710 ||

ardhādhike krayaḥ sidhyeduktalābho daśādhikaḥ  |
avakrayastribhāgena sadya eva rucikrayaḥ  || 711 ||

mūlyātsvalpapradāne'pi krayasiddhiḥ kṛtā bhavet |
cakravṛddyāṃ pradātavyaṃ deyaṃ tatsamayādṛte  || 712 ||


[abhyupetyāśuśrūṣā]

yastu na grāhayecśilpaṃ karmāṇyanyāni kārayet |
prāpnuyātsāhasaṃ pūrvaṃ tasmācśiṣyo nivartate  || 713 ||

śikṣito'pi śritaṃ kāmamantevāsī samācaret |
tatra karma ca yatkuryādācāryasyaiva tatphalam  || 714 ||

svatantrasyātmano dānāddāsatvaṃ dāravadbhṛguḥ  |
triṣu varṇeṣu vijñeyaṃ dāsyaṃ viprasya na kvacit || 715 ||

varṇānāmanulāmyena dāsyaṃ na pratilomataḥ  |
rājanyavaiśyaśūdrāṇāṃ tyajatāṃ hi svatantratām  || 716 ||

samavarṇo'pi vipraṃ tu dāsatvaṃ naiva kārayet |
brāhmaṇasya hi dāsatvānnṛpatejo vihanyate  || 717 ||

kṣatraviśśūdradharmastu samavarṇe kadācana  |
kārayeddāsakarmāṇi brāhmaṇaṃ na bṛhaspatiḥ  || 718 ||

śīlādhyayanasaṃpanne tadūnaṃ karma kāmataḥ  |
tatrāpi nāśubhaṃ kiṃcitprakurvīta dvijottamaḥ  || 719 ||

viṇmūtronmārjanaṃ caiva nagnatvaparimardanam  |
prāyo dāsīsutāḥ kuryurgavādigrahaṇaṃ ca yat || 720 ||

pravrajyāvasitā yatra trayo varṇā dvijādayaḥ  |
nirvāsaṃ kārayedvipraṃ dāsatvaṃ kṣatraviḍnṛpaḥ  || 721 ||

śūdraṃ tu kārayeddāsaṃ krītamakrītameva vā  |
dāsyāyaiva hi sṛṣṭaḥ sa svayameva svayaṃ bhuvā  || 722 ||

svadāsīṃ yastu saṃgacchetprasūtā ca bhavettataḥ  |
avekṣya bījaṃ kāryā syānna dāsī sānvayā tu sā  || 723 ||

dāsasya tu dhanaṃ yatsyātsvāmī tasya prabhuḥ smṛtaḥ  |
prakāśaṃ vikrayādyattu na svāmī dhanamarhati  || 724 ||

dāsenoḍhā svadāsī yā sāpi dāsītvamāpnuyāt |
yasmādbhartā prabhustasyāḥ svāmyadhīnaḥ prabhuryataḥ  || 725 ||

ādadyādbrāhmaṇīṃ yastu cikrīṇīta tathaiva ca  |
rājñā tadakṛtaṃ kāryaṃ daṇḍyā syuḥ sarva eva te  || 726 ||

kāmāttu saṃśritāṃ yastu dāsīṃ kuryātkulastriyam  |
saṃkrāmayeta vānyatra daṇḍyastaccākṛtaṃ bhavet || 727 ||

bāladhātrīmadāsīṃ ca dāsīmiva bhunakti yaḥ  |
paricārakapatnīṃ vā prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 728 ||

vikrośamānāṃ yo bhaktāṃ dāsīṃ vikretumicchati  |
anāpadisthaḥ śaktaḥ san prāpnuyāddviśataṃ damam  || 729 ||

tavāhamiti cātmānaṃ yo'svatantraḥ prayacchati  |
na sa taṃ prāpnuyātkāmaṃ pūrvasvāmī labheta tam  || 730 ||

pravrajyāvasito dāso moktavyaśca na kenacit |
anākālabhṛto dāsyānmucyate goyugaṃ dadat || 731 ||


[sīmāvivādaḥ]

ādhikyaṃ nyūnatā cāṃśe astināstitvameva ca  |
abhogabhuktiḥ sīmā ca ṣaṭbhūvādasya hetavaḥ  || 732 ||

tasmin bhogaḥ prayoktavyaḥ sarvasākṣiṣu tiṣṭhati  |
lekhyārūḍhaścetaraśca sākṣī mārgadvayānvitaḥ  || 733 ||

kṣetravāstutaḍāgeṣu kūpopavanasetuṣu  |
dvayorvivāde sāmantaḥ pratyayaḥ sarvavastuṣu  || 734 ||

sāmantabhāve'sāmantaiḥ kuryātkṣetrādinirṇayam  |
grāmasīmāsu ca tathā tadvannagaradeśayoḥ  || 735 ||

grāmo grāmasya sāmantaḥ kṣetraṃ kṣetrasya kīrtitam  |
gṛhaṃ gṛhasya nirdiṣṭa samantātparirabhya hi  || 736 ||

teṣāmabhāve sāmanta- maulavṛddhoddhṛtādayaḥ  |
sthāvare ṣaṭprakāre'pi nātra kāryā vicāraṇā  || 737 ||

saṃsaktāstvatha sāmantāstatsaṃsaktāstathottarāḥ  |
saṃsaktasaktasaṃsaktāḥ padmākārāḥ prakīrtitāḥ  || 738 ||

svārthasiddhau praduṣṭeṣu sāmanteṣvarthagauravāt |
tatsaṃsaktaistu kartavya uddhāro nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 739 ||

saṃsaktasaktadoṣe tu tatsaṃsaktāḥ prakīrtitāḥ  |
kartavyā na praduṣṭāstu rājñā dharmaṃ vijānatā  || 740 ||

nājñānena hi mucyante sāmantā nirṇayaṃ prati  |
ajñānoktau daṇḍayitvā punaḥ sīmāṃ vicārayet |
kīrtite yadi bhedaḥ syāddaṇḍyāstūttamasāhasam  || 741 ||

tyaktvā duṣṭāṃstu sāmantānanyānmaulādibhiḥ saha  |
saṃmiśraya kārayetsīmāmevaṃ dharmavido viduḥ  || 742 ||

ye tatra pūrvaṃ sāmantāḥ paścāddeśāntaraṃ gatāḥ  |
tanmūlatvāttu te maulā ṛṣibhiḥ saṃprakīrtitāḥ  || 743 ||

niṣpādyamānaṃ yairdṛṣṭaṃ tatkāryaṃ nṛguṇānvitaiḥ  |
vṛddhā vā yadi vāvṛddhāste vṛddhāḥ parikīrtitāḥ  || 744 ||

upaśravaṇasaṃbhoga- kāryākhyānopacihnitāḥ  |
uddharanti tato yasmāduddhṛtāste tataḥ smṛtaḥ  || 745 ||

sāmantāḥ sādhanaṃ pūrvamaniṣṭoktau guṇānvitāḥ  |
dviguṇāstūttarā jñeyā tato'nye triguṇā matāḥ  || 746 ||

eko yadvannayetsīmāmubhayorīpsitaḥ kvacit |
mastake kṣitimāropya raktavāsāḥ samāhitāḥ  || 747 ||

bhayavarjitabhūpena sarvābhāve svayaṃkṛtā  || 748 ||

kṣetrakūpataḍāgānāṃ kedārārāmayorapi  |
gṛhaprāsādāvasatha- nṛpadevagṛheṣu ca  || 749 ||

bahūnāṃ tu gṛhītānāṃ na sarve nirṇayaṃ yadi  |
kuryurbhayādvā lobhādvā dāpyāstūttamasāhasam  || 750 ||

sīmācaṅkramaṇe kośe pādasparśe tathaiva ca  |
tripakṣapakṣasaptāhaṃ daivarājikamiṣyate  || 751 ||

mekhalābhramaniṣkāsa- gavākṣānnoparodhayet |
praṇālīṃ gṛhavāstuṃ ca pīḍayan daṇḍabhāgbhavet || 752 ||

niveśasamayādūrdhvaṃ naite yojyāḥ kadācana  |
dṛṣṭipātaṃ praṇālīṃ ca na kuryātparaveśmamu  || 753 ||

viṇmūtrodakavapraṃ ca vahniśvabhraniveśanam  |
aratnidvayamutsṛjya parakuḍyānniveśayet || 754 ||

sarve janāḥ sadā yena prayānti sa catuṣpathaḥ  |
aniruddho yathākālaṃ rājamārgaḥ sa ucyate  || 755 ||

na tatra ropayetkiṃcinnopahanyāttu kenacit |
guruācāryanṛpādīnāṃ mārgādānāttu daṇḍabhāk || 756 ||

yastatra saṃkaraśvabhrān vṛkṣāropaṇameva ca  |
kāmātpurīṣaṃ kuryācca tasya daṇḍastu māṣakaḥ  || 757 ||

taṭākodyānatīrthāni yo'medhyena vināśayet |
amedhyaṃ śodhayitvā tu daṇḍayetpūrvasāhasam  || 758 ||

dūṣayetsiddhatīrthāni sthāpitāni mahātmabhiḥ  |
puṇyāni pāvanīyāni prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 759 ||

sīmāmadhye tu jātānāṃ vṛkṣāṇāṃ kṣetrayordvayoḥ  |
phalaṃ puṣpaṃ ca sāmānyaṃ kṣetrasvāmiṣu nirdiśet || 760 ||

anyakṣetre tu jātānāṃ śākhā yatrānyasaṃśritāḥ  |
svāminaṃ taṃ vijānīyādyasya kṣetreṣu saṃśritāḥ  || 761 ||

asvāmyanumatenaiva saṃskāraṃ kurute tu yaḥ  |
gṛhodyānataṭākānāṃ saṃskartā labhate na tu  || 762 ||

vyayaṃ svāmini cāyāte na nivedya nṛpe yadi  |
athāvedya prayuktastu tadgataṃ labhate vyayam  || 763 ||

aśaktito na dadyāccetkhilārtho yatkṛto vyayaḥ  |
tadaṣṭabhāgahīnaṃ tu karṣakaḥ phalamāpnuyāt |
varṣāṇyaṣṭau sa bhoktā syātparataḥ svāmine tu tat || 764 ||

aśaktapretanaṣṭeṣu kṣetrikeṣvanivāritaḥ  |
kṣetraṃ cedvikṛṣetkaścidaśnuvīta sa tatphalam  || 765 ||

vikṛṣyamāṇe kṣetre ca kṣetrikaḥ punarāvrajet |
śīlopacāraṃ tatsarvaṃ dattvā kṣetramavāpnuyāt || 766 ||

tadaṣṭabhāgāpacayādyāvatsapta gatāḥ samāḥ  |
samāpte'ṣṭame varṣe bhuktakṣetraṃ labheta saḥ  || 767 ||


[vākpāruṣyam]

huṅkāraḥ kāsanaṃ caiva loke yacca vigarhitam  |
anukuryādanubrūyādvākpāruṣyaṃ taducyate  || 768 ||

niṣṭhurāślīlatīvratvāttadapi trividhaṃ smṛtam  |
ākṣepo niṣṭhuraṃ jñeyamaślīlaṃ nyaṅgasaṃjñitam  |
patanīyairupākrośaistīvramāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ  || 769 ||

yattvasatsaṃjñitairaṅgaiḥ paramākṣipati kvacit |
abhūtairvātha bhūtairvā niṣṭhurā vāksmṛtā budhaiḥ  || 770 ||

nyagbhāvakaraṇaṃ vācā krodhāttu kurute yadā  |
vṛttadeśakulādīnāmaślīlā sā budhaiḥ smṛtā  || 771 ||

mahāpātakayoktrī ca rāgadveṣakarī ca yā  |
jātibhraṃśakārī vātha tīvrā sā prathitā tu vāk || 772 ||

yo'guṇān kīrtayetkrodhānniguṇo vā guṇajñatām  |
anyasaṃjñānuyogī vā vāgduṣṭaṃ taṃ naraṃ viduḥ  || 773 ||

aduṣṭasyaiva yo doṣān kīrtayeddoṣakāraṇāt |
anyāpadeśavādī ca vāgduṣṭaṃ taṃ naraṃ viduḥ  || 774 ||

mohātpramādātsaṅgharṣātprītyā coktaṃ mayeti yat |
nāhamevaṃ punarvakṣye daṇḍārdhaṃ tasya kalpayet || 775 ||

yatra syātparihārārthaṃ patitastena kīrtanam  |
vacanāttatra na syāttu doṣo yatra vibhāvayet || 776 ||

anyathā tulyadoṣaḥ syānmithyoktau tūttamaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
mahatā praṇidhānena vāgduṣṭaṃ sādhayennaram  || 777 ||

atathyaṃ śrāvitaṃ rājā prayatnena vicārayet |
anṛtākhyānaśīlānāṃ jihvācchedo viśodhanam  || 778 ||


[daṇḍapāruṣyam]

hetuādibhirna paśyecceddaṇḍapāruṣyakāraṇam  |
tatra sākṣikṛtaṃ caiva divyaṃ vā viniyojayet || 779 ||

ābhīṣaṇena daṇḍena praharedyastu mānavaḥ  |
pūrvaṃ cāpīḍito vātha sa daṇḍyaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  || 780 ||

karṇauṣṭhaghrāṇapādākṣi- jihvāśiśnakarasya ca  |
chedane cottamo daṇḍo bhedane madhyamo bhṛguḥ  || 781 ||

manuṣyāṇāṃ paśūnāṃ ca duḥkhāya prahate sati  |
yathā yathā bhavedduḥkhaṃ daṇḍaṃ kuryāttathā tathā  || 782 ||

aspṛśyadhūrtadāsānāṃ mlecchānāṃ pāpakāriṇām  |
pratilomaprasūtānāṃ tāḍanaṃ nārthato damaḥ  || 783 ||

chardimūtrapurīṣādyairāpādyaḥ sa caturguṇaḥ  |
ṣaḍguṇaḥ kāyamadhye syānmūrdhni tvaṣṭaguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 784 ||

udgūraṇe tu hastasya kāryo dvādaśako damaḥ  |
sa eva dviguṇaḥ proktaḥ pātaneṣu svajātiṣu  || 785 ||

vākpāruṣye yathaivoktāḥ prātilomyānulomataḥ  |
tathaiva daṇḍapāruṣye pātyā daṇḍā yathākramam  || 786 ||

dehendriyavināśe tu yathā daṇḍaṃ prakalpayet |
tathā tuṣṭikaraṃ deyaṃ samutthānaṃ ca paṇḍitaiḥ  |
samutthānavyayaṃ cāsau dadyādāvraṇaropaṇāt || 787 ||

vāgdaṇḍastāḍanaṃ caiva yeṣūktamaparādhiṣu  |
hṛtaṃ bhagnaṃ pradāpyāste śodhyaṃ niḥsvaistu karmaṇā  || 788 ||

śrāntāṃstṛṣārtān kṣudhitānakāle vāhayennaraḥ  |
kharagomahiṣoṣṭrādīn prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 789 ||

dvipaṇo dvādaśapaṇo vadhe tu mṛgapakṣiṇām  |
sarpamārjāranakula- śvasūkaravadhe nṛṇām  || 790 ||

gokumārīdevapaśu- mukṣāṇaṃ vṛṣabhaṃ tathā  |
vāhayan sāhasaṃ pūrvaṃ prāpnuyāduttamaṃ vadhaḥ  || 791 ||

pramāpaṇe prāṇabhṛtāṃ dadyāttatpratirūpakam  |
tasyānurūpaṃ mūlyaṃ vā dadyādityabravīnmanuḥ  || 792 ||

vanaspatīnāṃ sarveṣāmupabhogo yathā yathā  |
tathā tathā damaḥ kāryo hiṃsāyāmiti dhāraṇā  || 793 ||

śiṣyaṃ krodhena hanyāccedācāryo latayā vinā  |
yenātyarthaṃ bhavetpīḍā vādaḥ syācśiṣyataḥ pituḥ  || 794 ||


[sāhasam]

sahasā yatkṛtaṃ karma tatsāhasamudāhṛtam  || 795 ||

sānvayastvapahāro yaḥ prasahya haraṇaṃ ca yat |
sāhasaṃ ca bhavedevaṃ steyamuktaṃ vinihnavaḥ  || 796 ||

vinā cihnaistu yatkāryaṃ sāhasākhyaṃ pravartate  |
śapathaiḥ sa viśodhyaḥ syātsarvavādeṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ  || 797 ||

ekaṃ cedvahavo hanyuḥ saṃrabdhāḥ puruṣaṃ narāḥ  |
marmaghāto tu yasteṣāṃ sa ghātaka iti smṛtaḥ  || 798 ||

vyāpādanena tatkārī vadhaṃ citramavāpnuyāt |
vināśahetumāyāntaṃ hanyādevāvicārayan  || 799 ||

udyatānāṃ tu pāpānāṃ hanturdoṣo na vidyate  |
nivṛttāstu yadārambhādgrahaṇaṃ na vadhaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 800 ||

ātatāyini cotkṛṣṭe tapaḥsvādhyāyajanmataḥ  |
vadhastatra tu naiva syātpāpe hīne vadho bhṛguḥ  || 801 ||

udyatāsiviṣāgniśca cāpodyatakarastathā  |
ātharvaṇena hantā ca piśunaścaiva rājani  || 802 ||

bhāryātikramakārī ca randhrānveṣaṇatatparaḥ  |
evamādyān vijānīyātsarvānevātatāyinaḥ  || 803 ||

yaśovṛttaharān pāpānāhurdharmārthahārakān  |
anākṣāritapūrvo yastvaparādhe pravartate  |
prāṇadravyāpahāre ca taṃ vidyādātatāyinam  || 804 ||

nakhināṃ śṛṇgiṇāṃ caiva daṃṣṭriṇāṃ cātatāyinām  |
hastyaśvānāṃ tathānyeṣāṃ vadhe hantā na doṣabhāk || 805 ||

garbhasya pātane steno brāhmaṇyāṃ śastrapātena  |
aduṣṭāṃ yoṣitaṃ hatvā hantvyo brāhmaṇo'pi hi  || 806 ||

kṣataṃ bhaṅgopamardau ca kuryāddravyeṣu yo naraḥ  |
prāpnuyātsāhasaṃ pūrvaṃ dravyabhāksvāmyudāhṛtaḥ  || 807 ||

haredbhindyāddahedvāpi devānāṃ pratimāṃ yadi  |
taggṛhaṃ caiva yo bhindyātprāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 808 ||

prākāraṃ bhedayedyastu pātayecchātayettathā  |
badhnīyādambhaso mārgaṃ prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 809 ||


[steyam]

pracchannaṃ vā prakāśaṃ vā niśāyāmatha vā divā  |
yatparadravyaharaṇaṃ steyaṃ tatparikīrtitam  || 810 ||

anyahastātparibhraṣṭamakāmāduddhṛtaṃ bhuvi  |
caureṇa vā parikṣiptaṃ loptraṃ yatnātparīkṣayet || 811 ||

tulāmānapratimāna- pratirūpakalakṣitaiḥ  |
carannalakṣitairvāpi prāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 812 ||

gṛhe tu muṣitaṃ rājā cauragrāhāṃstu dāpayet |
ārakṣakāṃśca dikpālān yadi cauro na labhyate  || 813 ||

grāmāntare hṛtaṃ dravyaṃ grāmādhyakṣaṃ pradāpayet |
vivīte svāminā deyaṃ cauroddhartā vivītake  || 814 ||

svadeśe yasya yatkiṃciddhṛtaṃ deyaṃ nṛpeṇa tu  |
gṛhṇīyāttatsvayaṃ naṣṭaṃ prāptamanviṣya pārthivaḥ  || 815 ||

caurairhṛtaṃ prayatnena svarūpaṃ pratipādayet |
tadabhāve tu mūlyaṃ syādanyathā kilviṣī nṛpaḥ  || 816 ||

labdhe'pi caure yadi tu moṣastasmānna labhyate  |
dadyāttamatha vā cauraṃ dāpayettu yatheṣṭataḥ  || 817 ||

tasmiṃśceddāpyamānānāṃ bhaveddoṣe tu saṃśayaḥ  |
muṣitaḥ śapathaṃ dāpyo bandhubhirvā viśodhayet || 818 ||

yasmādapahṛtāllabdhaṃ dravyātsvalpaṃ tu svāminā  |
tacśeṣamāpnuyāttasmātpratyaye svāminā kṛte  || 819 ||

svadeśaghātino ye syustathā mārganirodhakāḥ  |
teṣāṃ sarvasvamādāya rājā śūle niveśayet || 820 ||

acorāddāpitaṃ dravyaṃ caurānveṣaṇatatparaiḥ  |
upalabdhe labheraṃste dviguṇaṃ tatra dāpayet || 821 ||

yena yena paradrohaṃ karotyaṅgena taskaraḥ  |
chindyādaṅgaṃ nṛpastasya na karoti yathā punaḥ  || 822:1 ||

trapuṣe vāruke dve tu pañcāmraṃ pañcadāḍimam  |
kharjūrabadarādīnāṃ muṣṭiṃ gṛhṇanna duṣyati  || 822:2 ||

mānavāḥ sadya evāhuḥ sahoḍhānāṃ pravāsanam  |
gautamānāmaniṣṭaṃ yatprāṇyucchedadvigarhitam  || 823 ||

sahoḍhamasahoḍhaṃ vā tattvāgamitasāhasam  |
pragṛhyācchinnamāvedya sarvasvairviprayojayet || 824 ||

ayaḥsandānaguptāstu mandabhaktā balānvitāḥ  |
kuryuḥ karmāṇi nṛpaterāmṛtyoriti kauśikaḥ  || 825 ||

paradeśāddhṛtaṃ dravyaṃ vaideśyena yadā bhavet |
gṛhītvā tasya taddravyamadaṇḍaṃ taṃ visarjayet || 826 ||

corāṇāṃ bhaktadā ye syustathāgnyudakadāyakāḥ  |
kretāraścaiva bhāṇḍānāṃ pratigrāhiṇa eva ca  |
samadaṇḍāḥ smṛtā hyete ye ca pracchādayanti tān  || 827 ||

avidvān yājako vā syātpravaktā cānavasthitaḥ  |
tau ubhau coradaṇḍena vinīya sthāpayetpathi  || 828 ||


[strīsaṃgrahaṇam]

dūtopacārayuktaścedavelāsthānasaṃsthitiḥ  |
kṇṭhakeśāṇcalagrāhaḥ karṇanāsākarādiṣu  |
ekasthānāsanāhārāḥ saṃgraho navadhā smṛtaḥ  || 829 ||

strīṣu vṛttopabhogaḥ syātprasahya puruṣo yadā  |
vadhe tatra pravarteta kāryātikramaṇaṃ hi tat || 830 ||

kāmārtā svairiṇī yā tu svayameva prakāmayet |
rājādeśena moktavyā vikhyāpya janasaṃnidhau  || 831 ||

ārambhakṛtsahāyaśca tathā mārgānudeśakaḥ  |
āśrayaḥ śastradātā ca bhaktadātā vikarmiṇām  || 832 ||

yuddhopadeśakaścaiva tadvināśapradarśakaḥ  |
upekṣākāryayuktaśca doṣavaktranumokakaḥ  || 833 ||

aniṣeddhākṣamo yaḥ syātsarve tatkāryakāriṇaḥ  |
yathāśaktyanurūpaṃ tu daṇḍameṣāṃ prakalpayet || 834 ||


[strīpuṃdharmaḥ]

patyā cāpyaviyoginyā śuśrūṣyo'gnirvinītayā  |
saubhāgyavadavvaidhavya- kāmyayā bhartṛbhaktayā  || 835 ||

matiśuśrūṣayaiva strī sarvān kāmān samaśnute  |
divaḥ punarihāyātā sukhānāṃ śevadhirbhavet || 836 ||

mṛte bhartari yā sādhvī brahmacarye vyavasthitā  |
sārundhatīsamācārā brahmaloke mahīyate  || 837 ||


[dāyavibhāgaḥ]

sakalaṃ dravyajātaṃ yadbhāgairgṛhṇanti tatsamaiḥ  |
pitaro bhrātaraścaiva vibhāgo dharmya ucyate  || 838 ||

paitāmahaṃ samānaṃ syātpituḥ putrasya cobhayoḥ  |
svayaṃ copārjite pitrā na putraḥ svāmyamarhati  || 839 ||

paitāmahaṃ ca pitryaṃ ca yaccānyatsvayamarjitam  |
dāyādānāṃ vibhāge tu sarvametadvibhajyate  || 840 ||

dṛśyamānaṃ vibhajyeta gṛhaṃ kṣetraṃ catuṣpadam  |
gūḍhadravyābhiśaṅkāyāṃ pratyayastatra kīrtitaḥ  || 841 ||

gṛhopaskaravāhyāśca dohyābharaṇakarmiṇaḥ  |
dṛśyamānā vibhajyante kośaṃ gūḍhe'bravīdbhṛguḥ  || 842 ||

jīvadvibhāge tu pitā naikaṃ putraṃ viśeṣayet |
nirbhājayenna caivaikamakasmātkāraṇaṃ vinā  || 843 ||

saṃprāptavyavahārāṇāṃ vibhāgaśca vidhīyate  |
puṃsāṃ ca ṣoḍaśe varṣe jāyate vyavahāritā  || 844 ||

aprāptavyavahārāṇāṃ ca dhanaṃ vyayavivarjitam  |
nyaseyurbandhumitreṣu proṣitānāṃ tathaiva ca  || 845:1 ||

proṣitasya tu yo bhāgo rakṣeyuḥ sarva eva tam  |
bālaputre mṛte rikthaṃ rakṣyaṃ tattantubandhubhiḥ  |
paugaṇḍāḥ paratastaṃ tu vibhajeran yathāṃśataḥ  || 845:2 ||

bhrātrā pitṛvyamātṛbhyāṃ kuṭumbārthamṛṇaṃ kṛtam  |
vibhāgakāle deyaṃ tad- rikthibhiḥ sarvameva tu  || 846 ||

tadṛṇaṃ dhanine deyaṃ nānyathaiva pradāpayet |
bhāvitaṃ cetpramāṇena virodhātparato yadā  || 847 ||

dharmārthaṃ prītidattaṃ ca yadṛṇaṃ syānniyojitam  |
taddṛśyamānaṃ vibhajenna dānaṃ paitṛkāddhanāt || 848 ||

pitryaṃ pitryarṇasaṃśuddhamātmīyaṃ cātmanā kṛtam  |
ṛṇamevaṃvidhaṃ śodhyaṃ vibhāge bandhubhiḥ saha  || 849 ||

ṛṇaṃ prītipradānaṃ ca dattvā śeṣaṃ vibhājayet || 850 ||

dvyaṃśaharo'rdhaharo vā putravittārjanātpitā  |
mātāpi pitari prete putratulyāṃśabhāginī  || 851 ||

yathā yathā vibhāgāptaṃ dhanaṃ yāgārthatāmiyāt |
tathā tathā vidhātavyaṃ vidvadbhirbhāgagauravam  || 852 ||

loke rikthavibhāge'pi na kaścitprabhutāmiyāt |
bhoga eva tu kartavyo na dānaṃ na ca vikrayaḥ  || 853 ||

vibhaktā avibhaktā vā dāyādāḥ sthāvare samāḥ  |
eko hyanīśaḥ sarvatra dānādhamanavikraye  || 854 ||

avibhakte'nuje prete tatsutaṃ rikthabhāginam  |
kurvīta jīvanaṃ yena labdhaṃ naiva pitāmahāt || 855 ||

labhetāṃśaṃ sa pitryaṃ tu pitṛvyāttasya vā sutāt |
sa evāṃśastu sarveṣā bhrātṝṇāṃ nyāyato bhavet |
labheta tatsuto vāpi nivṛttiḥ parato bhavet || 856 ||

utpanne caurase putre caturthāṃśaharāḥ sutāḥ  |
savarṇā asavarṇāstu grāsācchādanabhājanāḥ  || 857 ||

kanyakānāṃ tvadattānāṃ caturto bhāga iṣyate  |
putrāṇāṃ tu trayo bhāgāḥ sāmyaṃ tvalpadhane smṛtam  || 858 ||

kṣetrikasya matenāpi phalamutpādayettu yaḥ  |
tasyeha bhāginau tau tu na phalaṃ hi vinaikataḥ  || 859 ||

klībaṃ vihāya patitaṃ yā punarlabhate patim  |
tasyāṃ paunarbhavo jāto vyaktamutpādakasya saḥ  || 860 ||

na mūtraṃ phenilaṃ yasya viṣṭhā cāpsu nimajjati  |
meḍhraśconmādaśukrābhyāṃ hīnaḥ klībaḥ sa ucyate  || 861 ||

akramoḍhāsutaścaiva sagotrādyastu jāyate  |
pravrajyāvasitaścaiva na rikthaṃ teṣu cārhati  || 862 ||

akramoḍhāsutastvṛkthī savarṇaśca yadā pituḥ  |
asavarṇaprasūtaśca kramoḍhāyāṃ ca yo bhavet || 863 ||

pratilomaprasūtā yā tasyāḥ putro na rikthabhāk |
grāsācchādanamatyantaṃ deyaṃ tadbandhubhirmatam  || 864 ||

bandhūnāmapyabhāve tu pitṛdravyaṃ tadāpnuyāt |
apitryaṃ draviṇaṃ prāptaṃ dāpanīyā na bāndhavāḥ  || 865 ||


[avibhājyāni]

svaśaktyapahṛtaṃ naṣṭaṃ svayamāptaṃ ca yadbhavet |
etatsarvaṃ pitā putrairvibhāge naiva dāpyate  || 866 ||

parabhaktopayogena vidyā prāptān yatastu yā  |
tayā prāptaṃ dhanaṃ yattu vidyāprāptaṃ taducyate  || 867 ||

upanyaste tu yallabdhaṃ vidyayā paṇapūrvakam  |
vidyādhanaṃ tu tadvidyādvibhāge na vibhajyate  || 868 ||

śiṣyādārtvijyataḥ praśnātsaṃdigdhapraśnanirṇayāt |
svajñānaśaṃsanādvādāllabdhaṃ prādhyayanācca yat |
vidyādhanaṃ tu tatprāhurvibhāge na vibhajyate  || 869 ||

śilpiṣvapi hi dharmo'yaṃ mūlyācyaccādhikaṃ bhavet || 870 ||

paraṃ nirasya yallabdhaṃ vidyāto dyūtapūrvakam  |
vidyādhanaṃ tu tadvidyānna vibhājyaṃ bṛhaspatiḥ  || 871 ||

vidyāpratijñayā labdhaṃ śiṣyādāptaṃ ca yadbhavet |
ṛtviṅnyāyena yallabdhametadvidyādhanaṃ bhṛguḥ  || 872 ||

vidyābalakṛtaṃ caiva yājyataḥ śiṣyatastathā  |
etadvidyādhanaṃ prāhuḥ sāmānyaṃ yadato'nyathā  || 873 ||

kule vinītavidyānāṃ bhrātṝṇāṃ pitṛto'pi vā  |
śauryaprāptaṃ tu yadvittaṃ vibhājyaṃ tadbṛhaspatiḥ  || 874 ||

nāvidyānāṃ tu vaidyena deyaṃ vidyādhanātkvacit |
samavidyādhikānāṃ tu deyaṃ vaidyena taddhanam  || 875 ||

āruhya saṃśayaṃ yatra prasabhaṃ karma kurvate  |
tasmin karmaṇi tuṣṭena prasādaḥ svāminā kṛtaḥ  |
tatra labdhaṃ tu yatkiñcitdhanaṃ śauryeṇa tadbhavet || 876 ||

śauryaprāptaṃ vidyayā ca strīdhanaṃ caiva yatsmṛtam  |
etatsarvaṃ vibhāge tu vibhājyaṃ naiva rikthibhiḥ  || 877 ||

dhvajāhṛtaṃ bhavedyattu vibhājyaṃ naiva tatsmṛtam  |
saṃgrāmādāhṛtaṃ yattu vidrāvya dviṣatāṃ valam  |
svāmyarthe jīvitaṃ tyaktvā taddhvajāhṛtamucyate  || 878 ||

yallabdhaṃ dānakāle tu svajātyā kanyayā saha  |
kanyāgataṃ tu tadvittaṃ śuddhaṃ vṛddhikaraṃ smṛtam  || 879 ||

vaivāhikaṃ tu tadvidyādbhāryayā yatsahāgatam  |
dhanamevaṃvidhaṃ sarvaṃ vijñeyaṃ dharmasādhakam  || 880 ||

vivāhakāle yatkiṃcidvarāyoddiśya dīyate  |
kanyāyāstaddhanaṃ sarvamavibhājyaṃ ca bandhubhiḥ  || 881 ||

dhanaṃ patraniviṣṭaṃ tu dharmārthaṃ ca nirūpitam  |
udakaṃ caiva dāsaśca nibandho yaḥ kramāgataḥ  || 882 ||

dhṛtaṃ vastramalaṃkāro nānurūpaṃ tu yadbhavet |
yathā kālopayogyāni tathā yojyāni bandhubhiḥ  || 883 ||

gopracāraśca rakṣā ca vastraṃ yaccāṅgayojitam  |
prayojyaṃ na vibhajyeta dharmārthaṃ ca bṛhaspatiḥ  || 884:1 ||

deśasya jāteḥ saṅghasya dharmo grāmasya yo bhṛguḥ  |
uditaḥ syātsa tenaiva dāyabhāgaṃ prakalpayet || 884:2 ||


[pracchāditarikthasya punarvibhāgaḥ]

pracchāditaṃ yadi dhanaṃ punarāsādya tatsamam  |
bhajeran bhrātṛbhiḥ sārdhamabhāve hi pituḥ sutāḥ  || 885 ||

anyonyāpahṛtaṃ dravyaṃ durvibhaktaṃ ca yadbhavet |
paścātprāptaṃ vibhajyeta samabhāgena tadbhṛguḥ  || 886 ||

vibhaktenaiva yatprāptaṃ dhanaṃ tasyaiva tadbhavet |
hṛtaṃ naṣṭaṃ ca yallabdhaṃ prāguktaṃ ca punarbhajet || 887 ||

bandhunāpahṛtaṃ dravyaṃ balānnaiva pradāpayet |
bandhūnāmavibhaktānāṃ bhogaṃ naiva pradāpayet || 888 ||

kṣetraṃ sādhāraṇaṃ tyaktvā yo'nyadeśaṃ samāśritaḥ  |
tadvaṃśyasyāgatasyāṃśaḥ pradātavyo na saṃśayaḥ  || 889 ||

tṛtīyaḥ pañcamo vāpi saptamaścāpi yo bhavet |
janmanāmaparijñāne labhetāṃśaṃ kramāgatam  || 890 ||

yaṃ paraṃparayā maulāḥ sāmantāḥ svāminaṃ viduḥ  |
tadanvayasyāgatasya dātavyā gotajairmahī  || 891 ||

vibhaktāḥ pitṛvittāccedakatra prativāsinaḥ (?)  |
vibhajeyuḥ punardvyaṃśaṃ sa labhetodayo yataḥ  || 892 ||


[vibhaktacihnādi]

vaseyurdaśa varṣāṇi pṛthagdharmāḥ pṛthakkriyāḥ  |
bhrātaraste'pi vijñeyā vibhaktāḥ paitṛkāddhanāt || 893 ||


[strīdhanalakṣaṇaṃ strīdhanaprakārāśca]

adhyagnyadhyāvāhanikaṃ dattaṃ ca prītitaḥ striyaiḥ  |
bhrātṛmātṛpitṛprāptaṃ ṣaḍvidhaṃ strīdhanaṃ smṛtam  || 894 ||

vivāhakāle yatstrībhyo dīyate hyagnisaṃnidhau  |
tadadhyagnikṛtaṃ sadbhiḥ strīdhanaṃ parikīrtitam  || 895 ||

yatpunarlabhate nārī nīyamānā piturgṛhāt |
adhyāvahanikaṃ caiva strīdhanaṃ tadudāhṛtam  || 896 ||

prītyā dattaṃ tu yatkiṃcitśvaśrvā vā śvaśureṇa vā  |
pādavandanikaṃ caiva prītidattaṃ taducyate  || 897 ||

gṛhopaskaravāhyānāṃ dohyābharaṇakarmiṇām  |
mūlyaṃ labdhaṃ tu yatkiṃcicśulkaṃ tatparikīrtitam  || 898 ||

vivāhātparato yattu labdhaṃ bhartṛkulātstriyā  |
anvādheyaṃ taduktaṃ tu labhdaṃ bandhukulāttathā  || 899 ||

ūrdhvaṃ labdhaṃ tu yatkiṃcitsaṃskārātprītitaḥ striyā  |
bhartuḥ pitroḥ sakāśādvā anvādheyaṃ tu tadbhṛguḥ  || 900 ||

ūḍhayā kanyayā vāpi bhartuḥ pitṛgṛhe'pi vā  |
bhrātuḥ sakāśātpitrorvā labdhaṃ saudāyikaṃ smṛtam  || 901 ||


[strīdhane svāmyādivicāraḥ]

pitṛmātṛpatibhrātṛ- jñātibhiḥ strīdhanaṃ striyai  |
yathāśaktyā dvisāhasrāddātavyaṃ sthāvarādṛte  || 902 ||

yattu sopādhikaṃ dattaṃ yacca yogavaśena vā  |
pitrā bhrātrātha vā patyā na tatstrīdhanamiṣyate  || 903 ||

prāptaṃ śilpaistu yadvittaṃ prītyā caiva yadanyataḥ  |
bhartuḥ svāmyaṃ tadā tatra śeṣaṃ tu strīdhanaṃ smṛtam  || 904 ||

saudāyikaṃ dhanaṃ prāpya strīṇāṃ svātantryamiṣyate  |
yasmāttadānṛśasyārthaṃ tairdattamupajīvanam  || 905 ||

saudāyike sadā strīṇāṃ svātantryaṃ parikīrtitam  |
vikraye caiva dāne ca yatheṣṭaṃ sthāvareṣvapi  || 906 ||

bhartṛdāyaṃ mṛte patyau vinyasetstrī yatheṣṭataḥ  |
vidyamāne tu saṃrakṣetkṣapayettatkule'nyathā  || 907 ||

atha cetsa dvibhāryaḥ syānna ca tāṃ bhajate punaḥ  |
prītyā nisṛṣṭamapi cetpratidāpyaḥ sa tadbalāt || 908 ||

grāsācchādanavāsānāmācchedo yatra yoṣitaḥ  |
tatra svamādadīta strī vibhāgaṃ rikthināṃ tathā  || 909 ||

likhitasyeti dharmo'yaṃ prāpte bhartṛkule vaset |
vyādhitā pretakāle tu gacchedbandhujanaṃ tataḥ  || 910 ||

na bhartā naiva ca suto na pitā bhrātaro na ca  |
ādāne vā visarge vā strīdhane prabhaviṣṇavaḥ  || 911 ||

yadi hyekataro'pyeṣāṃ strīdhanaṃ bhakṣayedbalāt |
savṛddhikaṃ pradāpyaḥ syāddaṇḍaṃ caiva samāpnuyāt || 912 ||

tadeva yadyanujñāpya bhakṣayetprītipūrvakam  |
mūlyameva pradāpyaḥ syādyadyasau dhanavān bhavet || 913 ||

vyādhitaṃ vyasanasthaṃ ca dhanikairvopapīḍitam  |
jñātvā nisṛṣṭaṃ yatprītyā dadyādātmecchayā tu saḥ  || 914 ||

jīvantyāḥ patiputrāstu devarāḥ pitṛbāndhavāḥ  |
anīśāḥ strīdhanasyoktā daṇḍyāstvapaharanti ye  || 915 ||

bhartrā pratiśrutaṃ deyamṛṇavatstrīdhanaṃ sutaiḥ  |
tiṣṭhedbhartṛkule yā tu na sā pitṛkule vaset || 916 ||


[mṛtāyāḥ striyā dhanādhikāriṇaḥ]

bhaginyo bāndhavaiḥ sārdhaṃ vibhajeran sabhartṛkāḥ  |
strīdhanasyeti dharmo'yaṃ vibhāgastu prakalpitaḥ  || 917 ||

duhitṝṇāmabhāve tu rikthaṃ putreṣu tadbhavet |
bandhudattaṃ tu bandhūnāmabhāve bhrtṛgāmi tat || 918 ||

pitṛbhyāṃ caiva yaddattaṃ duhituḥ sthāvaraṃ dhanam  |
aprajāyāmatātāyāṃ bhrātṛgāmi tu sarvadā  || 919 ||

āsurādiṣu yallabdhaṃ strīdhanaṃ paitṛkaṃ striyā  |
abhāve tadapatyānāṃ mātāpitrostadiṣyate  || 920 ||


[aputradhane patnyādayo dhanādhikāriṇaḥ]

aputrā śayanaṃ bhartuḥ pālayantī gurau sthitā  |
bhuñjītāmaraṇātkṣāntā dāyādā ūrdhvamāpnuyuḥ  || 921 ||

svaryāte svāmini strī tu grāsācchādanabhāginī  |
avibhakte dhanāṃśe tu prāpnotyāmaraṇāntikam  || 922 ||

bhoktumarhati klṛptāṃśaṃ guruśuśrūṣaṇe ratā  |
na kuryādyadi śuśrūṣāṃ cailapiṇḍe niyojyet || 923 ||

mṛte bhartari bhartṛaṃśaṃ labheta kulapālikā  |
yāvajjīvaṃ na hi svāmyaṃ dānādhamanavikraye  || 924 ||

vratopavāsaniratā brahmacarye vyavasthitā  |
damadānaratā nityamaputrāpi divaṃ vrajet || 925 ||

patnī bharturdhanaharī yā syādavyabhicāriṇī  |
tadabhāve tu duhitā yadyanūḍhā bhavettadā  || 926 ||

aputrasyātha kulajā patnī duhitaro'pi vā  |
tadabhāve pitā mātā bhrātā putrāśca kīrtitāḥ  || 927 ||

vibhakte saṃsthite dravyaṃ putrābhāve pitā haret |
bhrātā vā jananī vātha mātā vā tatpituḥ kramāt |
apacārakriyyayuktā nirlajjā vārthanāśikā  || 928 ||

vyabhicāraratā yā ca strī dhanaṃ sā na cārhati  || 929 ||

nārī khalvananujñātā pitrā bhartrā sutena vā  |
viphalaṃ tadbhavettasyā yatkarotyaurdhvadehikam  || 930 ||

adāyikaṃ rājagāmi yoṣidbhṛtyordhvadehikam  |
apāsya śrotriyadravyaṃ śrotriyebhyastadarpayet || 931 ||

saṃsṛṣṭānāṃ tu saṃsṛṣṭāḥ pṛthaksthānāṃ pṛthaksthitāḥ  |
abhāve'rthaharā jñeyā nirbījānyonyabhāginaḥ  || 932 ||


[dyūtasamāhvayau]

dyūtaṃ naiva tu seveta krodhalobhavivardhakam  |
asādhujananaṃ krūraṃ narāṇāṃ dravyanāśanam  || 933 ||

dhruvaṃ dyūtātkaliryasmādviṣaṃ sarpamukhādiva  |
tasmādrājā nivarteta viṣaye vyasanaṃ hi tat || 934 ||

varteta cetprakāśaṃ tu dvārāvasthitatoraṇam  |
asaṃmohārthamāryāṇāṃ kārayettatkarapadam  || 935 ||

sabhikaḥ kārayeddyūtaṃ deyaṃ dadyātsvayaṃ nṛpe  |
daśakaṃ tu śate vṛddhiṃ gṛhṇīyācca parājayāt || 936 ||

jeturdadyātsvakaṃ dravyaṃ jitādgrāhyaṃ tripakṣakam  |
sadyo vā sabhikenaiva kitāvāttu na saṃśayaḥ  || 937 ||

ekarūpā dvirūpā vā dyūte yasyākṣadevinaḥ  |
dṛśyate ca jayastasya yasmin rakṣā vyavasthitā  || 938 ||

atha vā kitavo rājñe dattvā bhāgaṃ yathoditam  |
prakāśaṃ devanaṃ kuryādevaṃ doṣo na vidyate  || 939 ||

prasahya dāpayeddeyaṃ tasmin sthāne na cānyathā  |
jitaṃ vai sabhikastatra sabhikapratyayā kriyā  || 940 ||

anabhijño jito mocyo'mocyo'bhijño jito rahaḥ  |
sarvasve vijite'bhijñe na sarvasvaṃ pradāpayet || 941 ||

vigrahe'tha jaye lābhe karaṇe kūṭadevinām  |
pramāṇaṃ sabhikastatra śuciśca sabhiko yadi  || 942 ||

mlecchaśvapākadhūrtānāṃ kitavānāṃ tapasminām  |
tatkṛtācārametṝṇāṃ niścayo na tu rājani  || 943 ||


[prakīrṇakam]

pūrvoktāduktaśeṣaṃ syādadhikāracyutaṃ ca yat |
āhṛtya paratantrārhta- nibaddhamasamañjasam  || 944 ||

dṛṣṭāntatvena śāstrānte punaruktakriyāsthitam  |
anena vidhinā yacca vākyaṃ tatsyātprakīrṇakam  || 945 ||

rājadharmān svadharmāṃśca saṃdigdhānāṃ ca bhāṣaṇam  |
pūrvoktāduktaśeṣaṃ ca sarvaṃ tatsyātprakīrṇakam  || 946 ||

sadbhāgakaraśulkaṃ ca garte deyaṃ tathaiva ca  |
saṃgrāmacaurabhedī ca paradārābhimardanam  || 947 ||

gobrāhmaṇajighāṃsā ca śasyavyāghātakṛttathā  |
etān daśāparādhāṃstu nṛpatiḥ svayamanviṣet || 948 ||

niṣkṛtīnāmakaraṇamājñāsedhavyatikramaḥ  |
varṇāśramavilopaśca prarṇasaṅkaralopanam  || 949 ||

nidhirniṣphalavittaṃ ca daridrasya dhanāgamaḥ  |
etāṃścāraiḥ suviditān svayaṃ rājā nivārayet || 950 ||

anāmnā tāni kāryāṇi kriyāvādāṃśca vādinām  |
prakṛtīnāṃ prakopaśca saṅketaśca parasparam  || 951 ||

aśāstravihitaṃ yacca prajāyāṃ saṃpravartate  |
upāyaiḥ sāmabhedādyairetāni śamaye nṛpaḥ  || 952 ||

mitrādiṣu prayuñjīta vāgdaṇḍaṃ dhiktapasvini  |
yathoktaṃ tasya tatkuryādanuktaṃ sādhu kalpitam  || 953 ||

pramāṇena tu kūṭena mudrayā vāpi kūṭayā  |
kāryaṃ tu sādhayedyo vai sa dāpyo damamuttamam  || 954 ||

rājakrīḍāsu ye saktā rājavṛttyupajīvinaḥ  |
apriyasya ca yo vaktā vadhaṃ teṣāṃ pravartayet || 955 ||

pratirūpasya kartāraḥ prekṣakāḥ prakarāśca ye  |
rājārthamoṣakāścaiva prāpnuyurvividhaṃ vadham  || 956 ||

pravrajyāvasitaṃ śūdraṃ japahomaparaṃ tathā  |
vadhena śāsayetpāpaṃ daṇḍyo vā dviguṇaṃ damam  || 957 ||

sacihnamapi pāpaṃ tu pṛcchetpāpasya kāraṇam  |
tadā daṇḍaṃ prakalpeta doṣamāropya yatnataḥ  || 958 ||

sadvṛttānāṃ tu sarveṣāmaparādho yadā bhavet |
avaśenaiva daivāttu tatra daṇḍaṃ na kalpayet || 959 ||

samyagdaṇḍapraṇetāro nṛpāḥ pūjyāḥ surairapi  |
ārambhe pradhamaṃ dadyātpravṛttau madhyamaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
yasya yo vihito daṇḍaḥ paryāptasya sa vai bhavet || 960 ||

rājāno mantriṇaścaiva viśeṣādevamāpnuyuḥ  |
aśāsanāttu pāpānāṃ natānāṃ daṇḍadhāraṇāt || 961 ||

paratantrāśca ye keciddāsatvaṃ ye ca saṃsthitāḥ  |
anāthāste tu nirdiṣṭāsteṣāṃ daṇḍastu tāḍanam  || 962 ||

tāḍanaṃ vandhanaṃ caiva tathaiva ca viḍambanam  |
eṣa daṇḍo hi dāsasya nārthadaṇḍo vidhīyate  || 963 ||

suvarṇaśatamekaṃ tu vadhārho daṇḍamarhati  |
aṅgacchede tadardhaṃ tu vivāse pañcaviṃśatim  || 964 ||

kulīnāryaviśiṣṭteṣu nikṛṣṭeṣvanusārataḥ  |
sarvasvaṃ vā nigṛhyaitān purātśīghraṃ pravāsayet || 965 ||

nirdhanā bandhane sthāpyā vadhaṃ naiva pravartayet |
sarveṣāṃ pāpayuktānāṃ viśeṣārthaśca śāstrataḥ  || 966 ||

vadhāṅgacchedārhavipro niḥsaṅge bandhane viśet |
tadakarmaviyuto'sau vṛttastasya damo hi saḥ  || 967 ||

kūṭasākṣyapi nirvāsyo vikhyāpyo'satpratigrahī  |
aṅgacchedī viyojyaḥ syātsvadharme bandhanena tu  || 968 ||

etaiḥ samāparādhānāṃ tatrāpyevaṃ prakalpayet |
bālavṛddhāturastrīṇāṃ na daṇḍastāḍanaṃ damaḥ  || 969 ||

strīdhanaṃ dāpayeddaṇḍaṃ dhārmikaḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ  |
nirdhanā prāptadoṣā strī tāḍanaṃ daṇḍamarhati  || 970 ||

anyāyopārjitaṃ nyastaṃ koṣe koṣaṃ niveśayet |
kāryārthe kāryanāśaḥ syādbuddhimānnopapātayet || 971 ||

dattvā dhanaṃ tadviprebhyaḥ sarvaṃ daṇḍasamutthitam  |
putre rājyaṃ samāsajya kurvīta prāyaṇaṃ vane  || 972 ||

evaṃ caretsadā yukto rājā dharmeṣu pārthivaḥ  |
hiteṣu caiva lokasya sarvān bhṛtyānniyojayet || 973 ||
