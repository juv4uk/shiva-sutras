1,vyavahārakāṇḍam
1,1

dharmapradhānāḥ puruṣāḥ pūrvamāsannahiṃsakāḥ  |
lobhadveṣābhibhūtānāṃ vyavahāraḥ pravartitaḥ  || 1,1.1 ||
prayaccheccedbhṛtiṃ svāmī bhṛtyānāṃ karma kurvatām  |
na kurvanti ca bhṛtyāścettatra vādaḥ pravartate  || 1,1.2 ||
hiṃsāṃ vā kurute kaściddeyaṃ vā na prayacchati  |
dve hi sthāne vivādasya tayorbahutarā gatiḥ  || 1,1.3 ||
yato dravyaṃ vinikrīya ṛṇārthaṃ caiva gṛhyate  |
tanmūlyamuttamarṇena vyavahāra iti smṛtaḥ  || 1,1.4 ||

[1,1.1 rājaguṇāḥ]
guṇadharmānato rājñaḥ kathayāmyanupūrvaśaḥ  |
dhanikarṇikasaṃdigdhau pratibhūlekhyasākṣiṇaḥ  || 1,1.5 ||
vicārayati yaḥ samyaktasyotpattiṃ nibodhata  |
somāgnyarkānilendrāṇāṃ vittāpattyoryamasya ca  || 1,1.6 ||
tejomātraṃ samuddhṛtya rājño mūrtirhi nirmitā  |
tasya savāṇi bhūtāni carāṇi sthāvarāṇi ca  || 1,1.7 ||
bhayādbhogāya kalpante svadharmānna calanti ca  |
nārājake kṛṣivaṇik- kusīdaparipālanam  || 1,1.8 ||
tasmādvarṇāśramāṇāṃ tu netāsau nirmitaḥ purā  |

[1,1.2 vyavahārapadāni]
dvipado vyavahāraḥ syātdhanahiṃsāsamudbhavaḥ  || 1,1.9 ||
dvisaptako'rthamūlastu hiṃsāmūlaścaturvidhaḥ  |
pāruṣye dve vadhaścaiva parastrīsaṃgrahastathā  || 1,1.10 ||
kusīdanidhideyādyaṃ saṃbhūyotthānameva ca  |
bhṛtyadānamaśuśrūṣā bhūvādo'svāmivikriyaḥ  || 1,1.11 ||
krayavikrayānuśayaḥ samayātikramastathā  |
strīpuṃsayogaḥ steyaṃ ca dāyabhāgo'kṣadevanam  || 1,1.12 ||
etānyarthasamutthāni padāni tu caturdaśa  |
punarevaṃ prabhinnāni kriyābhedādanekadahā  || 1,1.13 ||
pāruṣye dve sāhasaṃ ca parastrīsaṃgrahastathā  |
hiṃsodbhavapadānyevaṃ catvāryāha bṛhaspatiḥ  || 1,1.14 ||
hīnamadhyottamatvena prabhinnāni pṛthakpṛthak |
viśeṣa eṣāṃ nirdiṣṭaścaturṇāmapy anukramāt || 1,1.15 ||
padānyaṣṭādaśaitāni dharmaśāstroditāni tu  |
mūlaṃ sarvavivādānāṃ ye viduste parīkṣakāḥ  || 1,1.16 ||
pūrvapakṣaḥ smṛtaḥ pādo dvitīyastūttarastathā  |
kriyāpādastathā vācyaścaturtho nirṇayastathā  || 1,1.17 ||

[1,1.3 dharmādicatuṣṭayabalābalam]
dharmeṇa vyavahāreṇa cāritreṇa nṛpājñayā  |
catuṣprakāro'bhihitaḥ saṃdigdhe'rthe vinirṇayaḥ  || 1,1.18 ||
śāstraṃ kevalamāśritya kriyate yatra nirṇayaḥ  |
vyavahāraḥ sa vijñeyo dharmastenāpi vardhate  || 1,1.19 ||
deśasthityānumānena naigamānumatena ca  |
kriyate nirṇayastatra vyavahārastu bādhyate  || 1,1.20 ||
vihāya caritācāraṃ yatra kuryātpunarnṛpaḥ  |
nirṇayaṃ sā tu rājājñā caritaṃ bādhyate tayā  || 1,1.21 ||
dharmaśāstrānusāreṇa sāmātyaḥ sa purohitaḥ  |
vyavahārānnṛpaḥ paśyetprajāsaṃrakṣaṇāya ca    |
krodhalobhavihīnastu  satyavādī jitendriyaḥ  || 1,1.22 ||

[1,1.4 dharmādhikaraṇam]
saptaprakṛtikaṃ yattu vijigīṣorareśca yat |
caturdaśakamevedaṃ maṇḍalaṃ paricakṣate  || 1,1.23 ||
catvāraḥ pṛthivīpālāḥ pṛthaṅmitraiḥ sahāṣṭakam  |
amātyādibhirete ca jagatyakṣarasaṃhitāḥ  || 1,1.24 ||
prātarutthāya nṛpatiḥ śaucaṃ kṛtvā vidhānataḥ  |
gurūn jyotirvido vaidyān devān viprān purohitān  || 1,1.25 ||
yathārhametān saṃpūjya supuṣpābharaṇāmbaraiḥ  |
abhinandya ca gurvādīn sumukhaḥ praviśetsabhām  || 1,1.26 ||
rājā kāryāṇi saṃpaśyetsadbhireva tribhirvṛtaḥ  |
sabhāmeva praviśyāgryāmāsīnaḥ sthita eva vā  || 1,1.27 ||

[1,1.5 durgalakṣaṇam]
ātmadārārthalokānāṃ saṃcitānāṃ tu guptaye  |
nṛpatiḥ kārayeddurgaṃ prākāradvayasaṃyutam  || 1,1.28 ||
bhūpānāmindhanarasairvetraśaṣpānnavāhanaiḥ  |
yantrāyudhaiśca vividhaiḥ snigdhaiḥ śūrairnarairyutam  || 1,1.29 ||
vedavidyāvido viprān kṣatriyānagnihotriṇaḥ  |
āhṛtya sthāpayettatra teṣāṃ vṛttiṃ prakalpayet || 1,1.30 ||
anācchedyāḥ karāstebhyaḥ pradeyā gṛhabhūmayaḥ  |
muktā bhāvyāśca nṛpaterlekhayitvā svaśāsane  || 1,1.31 ||
nityaṃ naimittikaṃ kāmyaṃ śāntikaṃ pauṣṭikaṃ tathā  |
paurāṇāṃ karma kuryuste saṃdigdhavinayaṃ tathā  || 1,1.32 ||
samā nimnonnatā vāpi yatra bhūmiryathāvidhā  |
śālāṭṭaparikhādyāśca kartavyāśca tathāvidhāḥ  || 1,1.33 ||
samantāttatra veśmāni kuryuḥ prakṛtayastataḥ  |
dvijavaiśyavaṇicchilpi- kārukā rakṣakāstathā  || 1,1.34 ||
shalāvasthānaniṣkāśa- bhramaśvabhracatuṣpathān  |
samājavikrayasthāna- govrajāṃścaiva kalpayet || 1,1.35 ||
guṇavāniti yaḥ proktaḥ khyāpito janasaṃsadi  |
kathaṃ tenaiva vaktreṇa nirguṇaḥ parikathyate  || 1,1.36 ||
tasmātprabhutvaṃ vṛttiṃ ca nirdoṣasya na cālayet |
anavasthāprasaṅgaḥ syānnaśyetopagrahastathā  || 1,1.37 ||

[1,1.6 prajāpālanalakṣaṇam][p.7]
samyaṅniviṣṭadeśastu kṛtadurgastu śāstrataḥ  |
kaṇṭakoddharaṇe nityamātiṣṭhedbalamuttamam  || 1,1.38 ||
tatprajāpālanaṃ proktaṃ trividhaṃ nyāyavedibhiḥ  |
paracakrāccaurabhayādbalino'nyāyavartinaḥ  || 1,1.39 ||
parānīkastenabhayamupāyaiḥ śamayennṛpaḥ  |
balavatparibhūtānāṃ pratyahaṃ nyāyadarśanaiḥ  || 1,1.40 ||
yadadhīte yadyajate yajjuhoti yadarcati  |
tasya ṣaḍbhāgabhāgrājā samyagbhavati rakṣaṇāt || 1,1.41 ||
rakṣan dharmeṇa bhūtāni rājā vadhyāṃśca ghātayan  |
yajate'haraharyajñaiḥ sahasraśatadakṣiṇaiḥ  || 1,1.42 ||
daśāṣṭaṣaṣṭhaṃ nṛpaterbhāgaṃ dadyātkṛṣīvalam  |
khilādvarṣāvasantācca kṛṣyamāṇādyathākramam  || 1,1.43 ||
deśasthityā baliṃ dadyurbhūtaṃ ṣaṇmāsavārṣikam  |
eṣa dharmaḥ samākhyātaḥ kīnāśānāṃ purātanaḥ  || 1,1.44 ||

[1,1.7 sabhāniveśanaprakāraḥ]
audakaṃ pārvataṃ vārkṣyamairaṇaṃ dhānvanaṃ tathā  |
durgamadhye gṛhaṃ kuryājjalavṛkṣāvṛtaṃ pṛthak || 1,1.45 ||
prāgdiśi prāṅmukhīṃ tasya lakṣaṇyāṃ kalpayetsabhām  |
mālyadhūpāsanopetāṃ bījaratnasamanvitām  || 1,1.46 ||
pratimālekhyadevaiśca yuktāmagnyambunā tathā  |
lakṣaṇyāṃ vāstuśāstrokta- lakṣaṇena tu lakṣitām  || 1,1.47 ||
bhadrāsanamadhiṣṭhāya saṃvītāṅgaḥ samāhitaḥ  |
praṇamya lokapālebhyaḥ kāryadarśanamārabhet || 1,1.48 ||
vipro dharmadrumasyādiḥ skandhaśākhe mahīpatiḥ  |
sacivāḥ patrapuṣpāṇi phalaṃ nyāyena pālanam  || 1,1.49 ||
yaśo vittaṃ phalaraso bhogopagrahapūjanam  |
ajeyatvaṃ lokapaṅktiḥ svarge sthānaṃ ca śāśvatam  || 1,1.50 ||
vodotvaitānnyāyarasān samo bhūtvā vivādanam  |
tyaktalobhādikaṃ rājā dharmaṃ kuryādvinirṇayam  || 1,1.51 ||
rājā vṛttivivādānāṃ svayameva pradarśanam  |
śāstradṛṣṭena mārgeṇa sa vidvadbhiḥ prasevyate  || 1,1.52 ||
tasmānnyāyena rājā tu samyagyatnena pālayet |
tasmādarthaṃ ca rājyaṃ ca [yaśaśca] vipulaṃ labhet || 1,1.53 ||
satyaṃ devāḥ samāsena manuṣyāstvanṛtaṃ viduḥ  |
ihaiva tasya devatvaṃ yasya satye sthitā matiḥ  || 1,1.54 ||
paśvājyyartvigādīnāṃ saṃyogājjāyate'dhvaraḥ  |
yathā saṃbadhyate tena vyavahārastathocyate  || 1,1.55 ||
prāḍvivākasadasyānāmupajīvya matāni tu  |
tadyuktiyogādyo'rtheṣu nirṇaye na sa daṇḍabhāk || 1,1.56 ||

[1,1.8 sabhāprabhedāḥ]
pratiṣṭhitāpratiṣṭhā ca mudritā śāsitā tathā  |
caturvidhā sabhā proktā sabhyāścaiva tathāvidhāḥ  || 1,1.57 ||
pratiṣṭhitā pure grāme calā nāmāpratiṣṭhitā  |
mudritā adhyakṣasaṃyuktā rājayuktā ca śāsitā  || 1,1.58 ||
nyāyān paśyetkṛtamatiḥ sā sabhādhvarasaṃmitā  |

[1,1.9 sabhyāḥ]
lokavedāṅgadharmajñāḥ sapta pañca trayo'pi vā  |
yatropaviṣṭā viprāgryāḥ sā yajñasadṛśī sabhā  || 1,1.59 ||
kuryādalagnakau rakṣedarthiprathyarthinau sadā  |
etaddaśāṅgaṃ karaṇaṃ yasyāmadhyāsta pārthivaḥ  || 1,1.60 ||
dvisasyāṣṭamaṃ bhāgaṃ muktvā kālaṃ susaṃviśet |
sa kālo vyavahārāṇāṃ śāstradṛṣṭaḥ paraḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1,1.61 ||
sādhukarmakriyāyuktāḥ satyadharmaparāyaṇāḥ  |
akrodhalobhāḥ śāstrajñāḥ sabhyāḥ kāryā mahībhujā  || 1,1.62 ||
sapta pañca trayo vā sabhāsado bhavanti  || 1,1.63 ||
deśācārānabhijñā ye nāstikāḥ śāstravarjitāḥ  |
unmattakruddhalubdhārtā na praṣṭavyā vinirṇaye  || 1,1.64 ||
rājā kāryāṇi saṃpaśyetprāḍvivāko'tha vā dvijaḥ  |
nyāyāṅgānyagrataḥ kṛtvā sabhyaśāstramate sthitaḥ  || 1,1.65 ||
balena caturaṅgena yato rañjayate prajāḥ  |
dīpyamānaḥ svavapuṣā tena rājābhidhīyate  || 1,1.66 ||
ekastvanekadhā prokto vyavahāro manīṣibhiḥ  |
tasya nirṇayakṛdrājā brāhmaṇaśca bahuśrutaḥ  || 1,1.67 ||
vyavahārāśritaṃ praśnaṃ pṛcchati prāḍiti śrutiḥ  |
vivadettatra yastasmin prāḍvivākastu sa smṛtaḥ  || 1,1.68 ||
vivāde pṛcchati praśnaṃ pratipraśnaṃ tathaiva ca  |
priyapūrvaṃ prāgvadati prāḍvivākastataḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1,1.69 ||
saprāḍvivākaḥ sāmātyaḥ sabrāhmaṇapurohitaḥ  |
sasabhyaḥ prekṣako rājā svarge tiṣṭhati dharmataḥ  || 1,1.70 ||
sarvaśāstrārthavettāramalubdhaṃ nyāyabhāṣiṇam  |
vipraṃ prājñaṃ kramāyātamamātyaṃ sthāpayeddvijam  || 1,1.71 ||
dvijān vihāya yaḥ paśyetkāryāṇi vṛṣalaiḥ saha  |
tasya prakṣarate rāṣṭraṃ balaṃ kośaṃ ca naśyati  || 1,1.72 ||
ye cāraṇyacarāsteṣāmaraṇye karaṇaṃ bhavet |
senāyāṃ sainikānāṃ tu sārtheṣu baṇijāṃ tathā  || 1,1.73 ||
kīnāśāḥ kārukā mallāḥ kusīdaśreṇivartakāḥ  |
liṅginastaskarāścaiva svena dharmeṇa nirṇayaḥ  || 1,1.74 ||
kulāni śreṇayaścaiva gaṇāstvadhikṛto nṛpaḥ  |
pratiṣṭhā vyavahārāṇāṃ gurvebhyastūttarottaram  || 1,1.75 ||
tapasvināṃ tu kāryāṇi traividyaireva kārayet |
māyāyogavidāṃ caiva na svayaṃ kopakāraṇāt || 1,1.76 ||
adaṇḍyān daṇḍayan rājā daṇḍyāṃścaivāpyadaṇḍayan  |
ayaśo mahadāpnoti narakaṃ caiva gacchati  || 1,1.77 ||
api bhrātā suto'rghyo vā śvaśuro māturo'pi vā  |
nādaṇḍyo nāma rājño'sti dharmādvicalitaḥ svakāt || 1,1.78 ||
yatra vipro na vidvān syātkṣatriyaṃ tatra yojayet |
vaiśyaṃ vā dharmaśāstrajñaṃ śūdraṃ yatnena varjayet || 1,1.79 ||
dharmakarmavihīnastu brāhmairliṅgairvivarjitaḥ  |
bravīti brāhmaṇo'smīti tamāhurbrāhmaṇabruvam  || 1,1.80 ||
śabdābhidhānatattvajñau gaṇanākuśalau śucī  |
nānālipijñau kartavyau rājñā gaṇakalekhakau  || 1,1.81 ||
akāraṇe rakṣaṇe ca sākṣyarthipravādinām  |
sabhyādhīnaḥ satyavādī kartavyastu sa pūruṣaḥ  || 1,1.82 ||
etaddaśāṅgakaraṇaṃ yasyāmadhyāsya pārthivaḥ  |
nyhāyaṃ paśyetkṛtamatiḥ sā sabhādhvarasaṃmitā  || 1,1.83 ||
eṣāṃ mūrdhā nṛpo'ṅgānāṃ mukhaṃ cādhikṛtaḥ smṛtaḥ  |
bāhū sabhyāḥ smṛtirhastau jaṅghe gaṇakalekhakau  || 1,1.84 ||
hemāgnyambudṛśau hṛcca pādau svapuruṣastathā  || 1,1.85 ||
hiraṇyamagnimudakaṃ dharmaśāstrāṇi caiva hi  |
tanmadhye sthāpayedrājā puṇyāni ca hitāni ca  || 1,1.86 ||
ādityacandradevādi dikpālān tatra kalpayet |
hemāgnyambusvapuruṣāḥ sādhanāṅgāni vai daśa  || 1,1.87 ||
daśānāmapi caiteṣāṃ karma proktaṃ pṛthakpṛthak |
vaktādhyakṣo nṛpaḥ śāstā sabhyaḥ kāryaparīkṣakaḥ  || 1,1.88 ||
smṛtirvinirṇayaṃ brūte jayadānaṃ damaṃ tathā  |
śapathārthe hiraṇyāgnī ambu tṛṣitajantuṣu  || 1,1.89 ||
gaṇako gaṇayedarthaṃ likhennyāyaṃ ca lekhakaḥ  |
partyarthisabhyānayanaṃ sākṣiṇaṃ ca svapūruṣaḥ  || 1,1.90 ||
vāgdaṇḍaścaiva dhigdaṇḍo viprādhīnau tu tāūbhau  |
arthadaṇḍavadhāvuktau rājāyatāvubhāvapi  || 1,1.91 ||
rājñā ye viditāḥ samyakkulaśreṇigaṇādayaḥ  |
sāhasanyāyavarjyāni kuryuḥ kāryāṇi te nṛṇām  || 1,1.92 ||
kulaśreṇigaṇādhyakṣāḥ proktā nirṇayakārakāḥ  |
vicārya śreṇibhiḥ kāryaṃ kulairyanna vicāritam  || 1,1.93 ||
gaṇaiśca śreṇyavijñātaṃ gaṇājñātaṃ niyuktakaiḥ  |
kulādibhyo'dhikāḥ sabhyāstebhyo'dhyakṣaḥ smṛto'dhikaḥ  || 1,1.94 ||
sarveṣāmadhiko rājā dharmaṃ yatnena niścitam  |
uttamādhamamadhyānāṃ vivādānāṃ vicāraṇāt || 1,1.95 ||
uparyupari buddhīnāṃ carantīśvarabuddhayaḥ  |
ajñānatimiropetān saṃdehapaṭalānvitān  || 1,1.96 ||
nirāmayān yaḥ kurute śāstrāñjanaśalākayā  |
iha kīrtiṃ rājapūjāṃ labhate svargatiṃ ca saḥ  || 1,1.97 ||
lobhadveṣādikaṃ tyaktvā yaḥ kuryātkāryanirṇayam  |
śāstroditena vidhinā tasya yajñaphalaṃ bhavet || 1,1.98 ||
adharmataḥ pravṛttaṃ tu nopekṣeran sabhāsadaḥ  |
upekṣamāṇāste bhūpā narakaṃ yāntyadhomukhāḥ  || 1,1.99 ||
nyāyamārgādapetaṃ tu jñātvā cittaṃ mahīpateḥ  |
vaktavyaṃ tvapriyaṃ tatra na sabhyaḥ kilbiṣī tataḥ  || 1,1.100 ||
sabhyena tāvadvaktavyaṃ dharmārthasahitaṃ vacaḥ  |
śṛṇoti yadi no rājā syāttu sabhyastato'naghaḥ  || 1,1.101 ||
anirṇīteṣu yadyevaṃ saṃbhāṣeta raho'rthinā  |
prāḍvivāko'pi daṇḍyaḥ syātsabhyāścaiva viśeṣataḥ  || 1,1.102 ||
snetāccājñānato vāpi mohādvā lobhato'pi vā  |
yatra sabhyo'nyathāvādī daṇḍyo'sabhyaḥ smṛto hi saḥ  || 1,1.103 ||
lekhyaṃ yatra na vidyeta na sākṣī na ca bhuktayaḥ  |
pramāṇāni na santyekaṃ pramāṇaṃ tatra pārthivaḥ  || 1,1.104 ||
niścetuṃ ye na śakyāḥ syurvādāḥ saṃdigdharūpiṇaḥ  |
teṣāṃ nṛpaḥ pramāṇaṃ syātsa sarvasya prabhuryataḥ  || 1,1.105 ||
vyavahārān svayaṃ paśyetsabhyaiḥ parivṛto'nvaham  || 1,1.106 ||
anyāyavādinaḥ sabhyāstathaivotkocajīvinaḥ  |
viśvaste vañcakāścaiva nirvāsyāḥ sarva eva te  || 1,1.107 ||
niyukto vāniyukto vā śāstrajño vaktumarhati  |
yattena sadasi proktaṃ sa dharmo nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 1,1.108 ||
pūrvāmukhastūpaviśedrājā sabhyā udaṅmukhāḥ  |
gaṇakaḥ paścimā yastu lekhako dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ  || 1,1.109 ||
yathā yamaḥ priyadveṣyau prāpte kāle niyacchati  |
tathā rājñā niyantavyāḥ prajāstaddhi yamavratam  || 1,1.110 ||
dharmaśāstrārthaśāstrābhyāmavirodhena pārthivaḥ  |
samīkṣamāṇo nipuṇaṃ vyavahāragatiṃ nayet || 1,1.111 ||
nyāyaśāstramatikramya sabhyairatra tu niścitam  |
tatra dharmo hato hanti sarvāneva na saṃśayaḥ  || 1,1.112 ||
dhāryaṃ manvādikaṃ śāstraṃ nārthaśāstraṃ kathaṃcana  |
dvayorvirodhe kartavyaṃ dharmaśāstroditaṃ vacaḥ  || 1,1.113 ||
kevalaṃ śāstramāśritya na kartavyo vinirṇayaḥ  |
yuktihīne vicāre tu dharmahāniḥ prajāyate  || 1,1.114 ||
pūrvāhṇe tāmadhiṣṭhāya vṛddhāmātyānujīvibhiḥ  |

dhenāvanaḍuhi kṣetre strīṣu prajanane tathā  |
nyāse yācitake datte tathaiva krayavikraye  || 1,3.12 ||
prāṅnyāye kāraṇoktau ca pratyarthī nirdiśetkriyām  |
mithyoktau pūrvavādī tu pratipattau na saṃbhavet || 1,3.13 ||
anuktvā kāraṇaṃ yatra pakṣaṃ vādī prapadyate  |
pratipattistu sā jñeyā kāraṇaṃ tūttaraṃ pṛthak || 1,3.14 ||
sarvālāpaṃ tu yaḥ kṛtvā mitho'lpamapi saṃvadet |
sarvameva tu dāpyaḥ syādabhiyukto bṛhaspatiḥ  || 1,3.15 ||
vākpāruṣye ca bhūmyau ca divyaṃ tu parivarjayet |
vikrayādānasaṃbandhe kriyādānamanicchati  || 1,3.16 ||

[1,3.2 caturvidhamuttaram]
abhiyukto'bhiyogasya yadi kuryāttu nihnavam  |
mithyā tattu vijānīyāduttaraṃ vyavahārataḥ  || 1,3.17 ||
śrutvābhiyogaṃ pratyarthī yadi tatpratipadyate  |
sā tu saṃpratipattistu śāstravidbhirudāhṛtā  || 1,3.18 ||
arthinābhihito yo'rthaḥ pratyarthī yadi taṃ tathā  |
prapadya kāraṇaṃ brūyātpratyavaskandanaṃ hi tat || 1,3.19 ||
yo'rthinārthaḥ prabhāṣyeta pratyarthī yadi taṃ tathā  |
prapadya kāraṇaṃ brūyādādharyaṃ manurabravīt || 1,3.20 ||
ācāreṇāvasanno'pi punarlekhayate yadi  |
sa vineyo jitaḥ pūrvaṃ prāṅnyāyastu sa ucyate  || 1,3.21 ||
tathye tathyaṃ prayuñjīta mithyāyāṃ cāpi lekhayet |
kāraṇaṃ kāraṇopete prāgjaye tu jayaṃ tathā  || 1,3.22 ||
bhayadṛṣṭodbhavā mithyā garhitā śāstravedibhiḥ  |
satyā saṃpratipattistu dharmyā sā parikīrtitā  || 1,3.23 ||
prāṅnyāyakaraṇe tathyaṃ ślāghyaṃ sadbhirudāhṛtam  |
viparītamadharmyaṃ syātpratyarthī hānimāpnuyāt || 1,3.24 ||
ahaṃpūurvikayā yātāvarthipratyarthinau yadā  |
vādo varṇānupūrvyeṇa grāhyaḥ pīḍāmavekṣya ca  || 1,3.25 ||
ekakāle samānīte pratyarthī sabhyasaṃnidhau  |
pūrvapakṣākṣarasamaṃ lekhayeduttaraṃ tataḥ  || 1,3.26 ||
pratyarthavidhirākhyātaḥ saṃgatārthaprapādane  |
caturvidhasyāpyadhunā yattadgrāhyaṃ taducyate  || 1,3.27 ||
prastutādanyanmadhyasthaṃ nyūnādhikamasaṃgatam  |
avācyasāraṃ saṃdigdhaṃ pratipakṣaṃ na lakṣayet || 1,3.28 ||
bhayaṃ karoti bhedaṃ vā bhīṣaṇaṃ vā nirodhanam  |
etāni vādino'rthasya vyavahāre sa hīyate  || 1,3.29 ||
ṛtvigādirniyuktastu samau saṃparikīrtitau  |
yajñe svāmyāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ hāniṃ vāde'tha vā jayaṃ  || 1,3.30 ||
pūrvottare'bhilikhite yatra vādī pramīyate  |
pratyarthī vā sutastābhyāṃ vyavahāraṃ viśodhayet || 1,3.31 ||
anirṇīte vivāde tu vipralabdho bhavennṛpaḥ [p.41]  |
jayadānaṃ samaṃ na syāttasmātkāryāṇi nirṇayet || 1,3.32 ||
sākṣiṇastu samuddiśya yastu tānna vivādayet |
triṃsadrātrāttripakṣādvā tasya hāniḥ prajāyate  || 1,3.33 ||
āhūtaprapalāyī ca maunī sākṣiparājitaḥ  |
svavākyapratipannaśca hīnavādī caturvidhaḥ  || 1,3.34 ||
prapalāyī tripakṣeṇa maunī vā saptabhirdinaiḥ  |
sākṣibhinnastatkṣaṇena pratipannaśca hīyate  || 1,3.35 ||
niveditasya akathanamanupasthānameva ca  |
pakṣārthidoṣau maunaṃ ca hīyamānasya lakṣaṇam  || 1,3.36 ||
mahāpāpopapāpābhyāṃ pātakenātha saṃsadi  |
yo'bhiśastastatkṣamate saṃyuktaṃ taṃ vidurjanāḥ  || 1,3.37 ||
tasmādyatnena kartavyaṃ budhenātmaviśodhanam  |
yadyadgurutaraṃ kāryaṃ tattatpūrvaṃ viśodhayet || 1,3.38 ||
mahāpāpābhiśasto yaḥ pātakāttartumicchati  |
pūrvamaṅgīkṛtaṃ tena jito'sau daṇḍamarhati  || 1,3.39 ||
ācārakaraṇe divye kṛtvopasthānaniścayam  |
nopasthito yadā kaścicchalaṃ tatra na kārayet || 1,3.40 ||
daivarājakṛto doṣastatkāle tu yadā bhavet |
avadhityāgamātreṇa na bhavetsa parājitaḥ  || 1,3.41 ||
purvottare saṃniviṣṭe vicāre saṃpravartite  |
praśamaṃ ye mitho yānti dāpyaste dviguṇaṃ damam  || 1,3.42 ||
pūrvottarārthe likhite prakrānte kāryanirṇaye  |
dvayoḥ saṃtaptayoḥ sandhiḥ syādayaḥkhaṇḍayoriva  || 1,3.43 ||

[1,3.3 sandhivicāraḥ]
sākṣisabhyavikalpastu bhavettatrobhayorapi [p.43]  |
dolāyamānayoḥ sandhiḥ prakuryātāṃ vicakṣaṇaiḥ  || 1,3.44 ||
pramāṇasamatā yatra bhedaḥ śāstracaritrayoḥ  |
tatra rājāmayā sandhirubhayorapi śasyate  || 1,3.45 ||
yatra sāṃśāyiko dharmo vyavahāraśca pārthive  |
sandhistatra tu kartavyo'yasoḥ saṃtaptayoryathā  || 1,3.46 ||
samaḥ sandhistadā kāryo viṣamastu nivartate  |
dharmārthopagrahaḥ kīrtiḥ bhavetsāmyena bhūbhṛtaḥ  |
na kliśyante sākṣisabhyā airaṃ ca vinivartate  || 1,3.47 ||
nigrahānugrahaṃ daṇḍaṃ dharmaṃ prāpya yaśo'yaśaḥ  |
vigrahājjāyate nṛṇāṃ punardoṣastathaiva ca  || 1,3.48 ||
tasmātkulagaṇādhyakṣā dharmajñāḥ samadṛṣṭayaḥ  |
adveṣalobhā yadbrūyustatkartavyaṃ vijānatā  || 1,3.49 ||

[1,4. kriyāpādaḥ]
[1,4.]
śodhite likhite samyagiti nirdoṣa uttare  |
pratyarthino'rthino vāpi kriyā kāraṇamiṣyate  || 1,4.1 ||
ye tu tiṣṭhanti karaṇe teṣāṃ sabhyairvibhāvanā  |
kathayitvottaraṃ samyagdātavyaikasya vādinaḥ  || 1,4.2 ||

[1,4.1 pramāṇānāṃ balābalam]
pratijñāṃ bhāvayedvādī pratyarthī kāraṇaṃ tathā  |
prāgvṛttavādī vijayaṃ jayapattreṇa bhāvayet || 1,4.3 ||
pūrvapāde vilikhitaṃ yathākṣaramaśeṣataḥ  |
arthī tṛtīyapāde tu kriyayā pratipādayet || 1,4.4 ||
śrutvā pūrvottaraṃ sabhyairnirdiṣṭā yasya bhāvanā  |
vibhāvayetpratijñātaṃ so'khilaṃ likhitādinā  || 1,4.5 ||
dviprakārā kriyā proktā mānuṣīi daivikī tathā  |
ekaikānekadhā bhinnā ṛṣibhistattvavedibhiḥ  || 1,4.6 ||
sākṣiṇo likhitaṃ bhuktirmānuṣaṃ trividhaṃ smṛtam  |
dhaṭādyā dharmajāntā tu daivī navavidhā kriyā  || 1,4.7 ||
sākṣilekhyānumānaṃ ca mānuṣī trividhā kriyā  |
sākṣī dvādaśa bhedastu likhitaṃ tvaṣṭadhā smṛtam  || 1,4.8 ||
anumānaṃ tridhā proktaṃ navadhā daivikī kriyā  |
prathame vā tṛtīye vā pramāṇaṃ daivamānuṣam  || 1,4.9 ||
uttare syāccaturthe tu sasākṣijayapatrakam  |
ṛṇādikeṣu kāryeṣu kalpayenmānuṣīṃ kriyām  || 1,4.10 ||
prāṅnyāye pratyavaskande pratyarthī sādhayetsvayam  |
uttarārthaṃ pratijñārthamarthī mithyottare punaḥ  || 1,4.11 ||
kriyā na daivikī proktā vidyamāneṣu sākṣiṣu [p.47]  |
lekhye ca sati vādeṣu na syāddivyaṃ na sākṣiṇaḥ  || 1,4.12 ||
vākpāruṣye mahīvāde niṣiddhā daivikī kriyā  |
pradātavyā prayatnena sāhaseṣu caturṣvapi  || 1,4.13 ||
nṛpadrohe sāhase ca kalpayeddaivikīṃ kriyām  || 1,4.14 ||
maṇimuktāpravālānāṃ kūṭahṛtpāśahārakaḥ  |
hiṃsako'nyāṅganāsevī parīkṣyaḥ śapathaiḥ sadā  || 1,4.15 ||
mahāpāpābhiśāpeṣu nikṣepe haraṇe tathā  |
divyaiḥ kāryaṃ parīkṣeta rājā satsvapi sākṣiṣu  || 1,4.16 ||
likhite sākṣivāde ca saṃdigdhirjāyate yadi  |
anumāne ca saṃbhrānte tatra divyaṃ viśodhanam  || 1,4.17 ||
dyūte samāhvaye caiva vivāde samupasthite  |
sākṣiṇaḥ sādhanaṃ proktaṃ na divyaṃ na ca lekhakam  || 1,4.18 ||
yathālābhopapannaistairnirṇayaṃ kārayennṛpaḥ  |
prakānte sāhase vāde pāruṣye daṇḍavācike  |
balodbhūteṣu kāryeṣu sākṣiṇo divyameva ca  || 1,4.19 ||
ṛṇe lekhyaṃ sākṣiṇo vā yuktileśādayo'pi vā  |
daivikī vā kriyā proktā prajānāṃ hitakāmyayā  || 1,4.20 ||
cirantanopāṃśukṛte ciranaṣṭeṣu sākṣiṣu  |
praduṣṭeṣvanumāneṣu divyaiḥ kāryaṃ viśodhayet || 1,4.21 ||

[1,5. sākṣiṇaḥ]
[1,5.]
nava sapta ca pañca syuścatvārastraya eva vā  |
ubhau vā śrotriyau khyātau naikaṃ pṛcchetkadā cana  || 1,5.1 ||
dyūtakaḥ śaṭikāgrāhī kāryamadhyagatastathā  |
eka eva pramāṇaṃ syānnṛpo'dhyakṣastathaiva ca  || 1,5.2 ||
stenāḥ sāhasikāḥ ṣaṇḍāḥ kitavāḥ sūcakāstathā  |
na sākṣiṇaste duṣṭātvātteṣu sākṣiyaṃ na vidyate  || 1,5.3 ||

[1,5.1 sākṣibhedāḥ]
likhito likhito gūḍhaḥ smāritaḥ kulyadūtakau  |
yadṛcchaścottaraścaiva kāryamadhyagato'paraḥ  || 1,5.4 ||
nṛpo'dhyakṣastathā grāmaḥ sākṣī dvādaśadhā smṛtaḥ  |
prabhedameṣāṃ vakṣyāmi yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ  || 1,5.5 ||
jātināmādilikhitaṃ yena svaṃ pitryameva ca  |
nivāsaśca vijñeyaḥ sākṣī likhitasaṃjñakaḥ  || 1,5.6 ||
arthinā ca kriyā bhedaistasya kṛtvā ṛṇādikam  |
pratyakṣaṃ likhyate yastu lekhitaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 1,5.7 ||
kuḍyavyavahito yastu śrāvyate ṛṇabhāṣitam  |
vinihnuto yathābhūtaṃ gūḍhaḥ sākṣī sa ucyate  || 1,5.8 ||
āhūya yaḥ kṛtaḥ sākṣī ṛṇanyāsakriyādike [p.51]  |
smāryate ca muhuryaśca smāritaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 1,5.9 ||
vibhāgadāne vipaṇe jñātiryaścopayujyate  |
dvayoḥ samāno dharmajñaḥ kulyaḥ sa parikīrtitaḥ  || 1,5.10 ||
arthipratyarthivacanaṃ śṛṇuyātpreṣitastu yaḥ  |
ubhayoḥ saṃmataḥ sādhuḥ dūtakaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 1,5.11 ||
kriyamāṇe tu kartavye yaḥ kaścitsvayamāgataḥ  |
atra sākṣī tvamasmākamukto yādṛcchikastu saḥ  || 1,5.12 ||
yatra sākṣī diśaṃ gacchenmumūrṣurvā yathākramam  |
anyaṃ saṃśrāvayettaṃ tu vidyāduttarasākṣiṇam  || 1,5.13 ||
sākṣiṇāmapi yaḥ sākṣyamuparyupari bhāṣatām  |
śravaṇācchravaṇādvāpi sa sākṣyuttarasaṃjñitaḥ  || 1,5.14 ||
ubhābhyāṃ yasya viśvastaṃ kāryaṃ cāpi niveditam  |
gūḍhadhārī sa vijñeyaḥ kāryamadhyāgatastathā  || 1,5.15 ||
arthipratyarthinorvākyaṃ yacchrutaṃ bhūbhṛtā svayam  |
sa eva tatra sākṣī syādvisaṃvāde dvayorapi  || 1,5.16 ||
nirṇīte vyavahāre tu punarnyāyo yadā bhavet |
adhyakṣaḥ sabhyasahitaḥ sākṣī syāttatra nānyathā  || 1,5.17 ||
vyuṣitaṃ chāditaṃ yatra sīmāyāṃ ca samantataḥ [p.53]  |
sa kṛtto'pi bhavetsākṣī grāmastatra na saṃśayaḥ  || 1,5.18 ||
likhitau dvau tathā gūḍhau tricatuḥpañca lekhitāḥ  |
yadṛccha smāritāḥ kulyāstathā cottarasākṣiṇaḥ  || 1,5.19 ||
dūtakaḥ svaṭikāgrāhī- kāryamadhyagatastathā  |
eka eva pramāṇaṃ syānnṛpo'dhyakṣastathaiva ca  || 1,5.20 ||

[1,5.2 sākṣidoṣakathanaṃ duṣṭānāṃ daṇḍaśca]
sākṣiṇo'rthasamuddiṣṭān yastu doṣeṇa dūṣayet |
aduṣṭaṃ dūṣayan vādī tatsamaṃ daṇḍamarhati  || 1,5.21 ||
lekhyadoṣāstu ye kecitsākṣiṇāṃ caiva ye smṛtāḥ  |
vādakāle tu vaktavyāḥ paścāduktānna dūṣayet || 1,5.22 ||
sākṣidoṣāḥ prayoktavyāḥ saṃsadi prativādinā  |
patre vilikhya tān sarvān vācyaṃ pratyuttaraṃ tataḥ  || 1,5.23 ||
pratipattau na sākṣitvamarhanti tu kadā cana  |
ato'nyathā bhāvanīyāḥ kriyāyāṃ prativādinā  || 1,5.24 ||
abhāvayan damaṃ dāpyaḥ pratyarthī sākṣiṇā sphuṭam  |
bhāvitāḥ sākṣiṇaḥ sarve sākṣidharmanirākṛtāḥ  || 1,5.25 ||
pratyarthino'rthino vāpi sākṣidūṣaṇasādhane  |
prastutārthopayogena vyavahārāntaraṃ na ca  || 1,5.26 ||
jitaḥ sa vinayaṃ dāpyaḥ śāstradṛṣṭena karmaṇā  |
yadi vādī nirākāṅkṣaḥ sākṣī satye vyavasthitaḥ  || 1,5.27 ||
ukte'rthe sākṣiṇo yastu dūṣayan prāgadūṣitān  |
na ca tatkāraṇaṃ brūyātprāpnuyātpūrvasāhasam  || 1,5.28 ||
lekhyaṃ vā sākṣiṇo vāpi vivāde yasya dūṣitāḥ  |
tasya kāryaṃ na śodhyaṃ tu yāvattanna viśodhayet || 1,5.29 ||
sākṣibhirgaditaiḥ sabhyaiḥ prakrānte nirṇaye tu yaḥ  |
punarvivādaṃ kurute rājā tatra vicārayet || 1,5.30 ||
sākṣisaṃdūṣaṇe kāryaṃ pūrvaṃ sākṣiviśodhanam [p.55]  |
śuddheṣu sākṣiṣu tataḥ paścātkāryaṃ viśodhayet || 1,5.31 ||
satyapraśaṃsāvacanairanṛtasyāpavarjanaiḥ  |
sabhyaiḥ saṃbodhanīyāstu dharmaśāstrārthavedibhiḥ  || 1,5.32 ||
ā janmanaścā maraṇātsukṛtaṃ yattvayārjitam  |
tatsarvaṃ nāśamāyāti vitathasyābhiśaṃsanāt || 1,5.33 ||
kūṭasabhyaḥ kūṭasākṣī brahmahā ca samāḥ smṛtāḥ  |
bhrūṇahā mitrahā caiṣāṃ nādhikaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ  || 1,5.34 ||
evaṃ viditvā yaḥ sākṣī sa yathārthaṃ vadettataḥ  |
teneha kīrtimāpnoti paratra ca śubhāṃ gatim  || 1,5.35 ||
puruṣāḥ santi lobhādye kāryaṃ prabrūyuranyathā  |
santi cānye durātmānḥ kūṭalekhyakṛto narāḥ  || 1,5.36 ||
praṣṭavyāḥ sākṣiṇo ye tu varjyāścaiva narādhamāḥ  |
tānahaṃ kathayiṣyāmi sāṃprataṃ śāstracotitān  || 1,5.37 ||
śrautasmārtakriyāyuktāḥ lobhadveṣavivarjitāḥ  |
kulīnāḥ sākṣiṇo'nindyāstapodānadayānvitāḥ  || 1,5.38 ||

[1,5.3 asākṣiṇaḥ]
mātuḥ pitā pitṛvyaśca bhāryāyā bhrātṛmātarau  |
bhrātā sakhā ca jāmātā sarvavādeṣvasākṣiṇaḥ  || 1,5.39 ||
parastrīpānasaktāśca kitavāḥ pūrvadūṣitāḥ  |
unmattārtāḥ sāhasikā nāstikāśca na sākṣiṇaḥ  || 1,5.40 ||
santo'pi na pramāṇaṃ syurmṛte dhanini sākṣiṇaḥ  |
putre tu śrāvitā ye syuḥ svayamāsannamṛtyunā  || 1,5.41 ||
vihāyopānaduṣṇīṣaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ pāṇimuddharet |
hiraṇyaṃ gośakṛddarbhān samādāya ṛtaṃ vadet || 1,5.42 ||
upasthitāḥ parīkṣyāḥ syuḥ svarvarṇeṅgitādibhiḥ  |
sakṛtpramādāparādhi- vipraṃ vyāpadi pīḍitam  |
bhaṭādibhirvadhyamānaṃ rakṣeduktvānṛtānyapi  || 1,5.43 ||
yasyāśeṣaḥ pratijñārthaḥ sākṣibhiḥ prativarṇitaḥ [p.57]  |
sa jayī syādanyathā tu sādhyārthaṃ na samāpnuyāt || 1,5.44 ||
āhūto yatra nāgacchetsākṣī rogavivarjitaḥ  |
ṛṇaṃ damaṃ ca dāpyaḥ syāttripakṣātparatastu saḥ  || 1,5.45 ||

[1,5.4 sākṣyuktibalāblavicāraḥ]
sākṣidvaidhe prabhūtāḥ syurgrāhyāḥ sāmye guṇānvitāḥ  |
guṇidvaidhe kriyāyuktāstatsāmye śucimattarāḥ  || 1,5.46 ||
apṛṣṭāḥ satyavacane praśnasyākathane tathā  |
sākṣiṇaḥ sanniroddhvyā garhyā daṇḍyāśca dharmataḥ  || 1,5.47 ||
deśakālavayodravya- saṃjñājātipramāṇataḥ  |
anyūnaṃ cennigaditaṃ siddhaṃ sādhyaṃ vinirdiśet || 1,5.48 ||
deśakālavayodravya- pramāṇākṛtijātiṣu  |
yatra vipratipattiḥ syātsākṣyaṃ tadapi nānyathā  || 1,5.49 ||
nirdiṣṭeṣvarthajāteṣu sākṣī cetsākṣya āgate  |
na brūyādakṣasrasamaṃ na tannigaditaṃ bhavet || 1,5.50 ||
pūrvapakṣe pratijñātamaśeṣaṃ pratibhāvayet |
ūnādhikaṃ tu yatroktaṃ na tannigaditaṃ bhavet || 1,5.51 ||
ūnamabhyadhikaṃ vārthaṃ vibrūyuryatra sākṣiṇaḥ  |
tadarthānuktavijñeyameṣa sākṣividhiḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1,5.52 ||
sādhyārthāṃśe nigadite sākṣibhiḥ sakalaṃ bhavet |
strīsaṅge sāhase caurye yatsādhyaṃ parikalpyate  || 1,5.53 ||

jñātvā samyagdhanaṃ hṛtvā hantavyāḥ vividhairvadhaiḥ  || 1,23.10 ||
[1,23.1 sāhasikāḥ daṇḍyāḥ]

[1,23.2 saṃbhūyapraharaṇanirṇayaḥ]
