mātṛkā 1

[vyavahāraḥ]

dharmaikatānāḥ puruṣā yadāsan satyavādinaḥ  |
tadā na vyavahāro'bhūnna dveṣo nāpi matsaraḥ  || 1.1 ||
naṣṭe dharme manuṣyeṣu vyavahāraḥ pravartate  |
draṣṭā ca vyavahārāṇāṃ rājā daṇḍadharaḥ kṛtaḥ  || 1.2 ||
likhitaṃ sākṣiṇaścātra dvau vidhī saṃprakīrtitau  |
saṃdigdhārthaviśuddhyarthaṃ dvayorvivadamānayoḥ  || 1.3 ||
sottaro'nuttaraścaiva sa vijñeyo dvilakṣaṇaḥ  |
sottaro'bhyadhiko yatra vilekhāpūrvakaḥ paṇaḥ  || 1.4 ||
vivāde sottarapaṇe dvayoryastatra hīyate  |
sa paṇaṃ svakṛtaṃ dāpyo vinayaṃ ca parājaye  || 1.5 ||
sārastu vyavahārāṇāṃ pratijñā samudāhṛtā  |
taddhānau hīyate vādī taraṃstāmuttaro bhavet || 1.6 ||
kulāni śreṇayaścaiva gaṇāścādhikṛto nṛpaḥ  |
pratiṣṭhā vyavahārāṇāṃ gurvebhyastūttarottaram  || 1.7 ||
sa catuṣpāccatuḥsthānaścatuḥsādhana eva ca  |
caturhitaścaturvyāpī catuṣkārī ca kīrtyate  || 1.8 ||
aṣṭāṅgo'ṣṭādaśapadaḥ śataśākhastathaiva ca  |
triyonirdvyabhiyogaśca dvidvāro dvigatistathā  || 1.9 ||
dharmaśca vyavahāraśca caritraṃ rājaśāsanam  |
catuṣpādvyavahāro'yamuttaraḥ pūrvabādhakaḥ  || 1.10 ||
tatra satye sthito dharmo vyavahārastu sākṣiṣu  |
caritraṃ pustakaraṇe rājājñāyāṃ tu śāsanam  || 1.11 ||
sāmādyupāyasādhyatvāccatuḥsādhana ucyate  |
caturṇāmāśramāṇāṃ ca rakṣaṇātsa caturhitaḥ  || 1.12 ||
kartṝnatho sākṣiṇaśca sabhyān rājānameva ca  |
vyāpnoti pādaśo yasmāccaturvyāpī tataḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1.13 ||
dharmasyārthasya yaśaso lokapaktestathaiva ca  |
caturṇāṃ karaṇādeṣāṃ catuṣkārī prakīrtitaḥ  || 1.14 ||
rājā sapuruṣaḥ sabhyāḥ śāstraṃ gaṇakalekhakau  |
hiraṇyamagnirudakamaṣṭāṅgaḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  || 1.15 ||
ṛṇādānaṃ hyupanidhiḥ saṃbhūyotthānameva ca  |
dattasya punarādānamaśuśrūṣābhyupetya ca  || 1.16 ||
vetanasyānapākarma tathaivāsvāmivikrayaḥ  |
vikrīyāsaṃpradānaṃ ca krītvānuśaya eva ca  || 1.17 ||
samayasyānapākarma vivādaḥ kṣetrajastathā  |
strīpuṃsayośca saṃbandho dāyabhāgo'tha sāhasam  || 1.18 ||
vākpāruṣyaṃ tathaivoktaṃ daṇḍapāruṣyameva ca  |
dyūtaṃ prakīrṇakaṃ caivetyaṣṭādaśapadaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 1.19 ||
eṣāmeva prabhedo'nyaḥ śatamaṣṭottaraṃ smṛtam  |
kriyābhedānmanuṣyāṇāṃ śataśākho nigadyate  || 1.20 ||
kāmātkrodhācca lobhācca tribhyo yasmātpravartate  |
triyoniḥ kīrtyate tena trayametadvivādakṛt || 1.21 ||
dvyabhiyogastu vijñeyaḥ śaṅkātattvābhiyogataḥ  |
śaṅkāsatāṃ tu saṃsargāttattvaṃ hoḍhādidarśanāt || 1.22 ||
pakṣadvayābhisaṃbandhāddvidvāraḥ samudāhṛtaḥ  |
pūrvavādastayoḥ pakṣaḥ pratipakṣastaduttaram  || 1.23 ||
bhūtacchalānusāritvāddvigatiḥ sa udāhṛtaḥ  |
bhūtaṃ tattvārthasaṃyuktaṃ pramādābhihitaṃ chalam  || 1.24 ||
tatra śiṣṭaṃ chalaṃ rājā marṣayeddharmasādhanaḥ  |
bhūtameva prapadyeta dharmamūlā yataḥ śriyaḥ  || 1.25 ||
dharmeṇoddharato rājño vyavahārān kṛtātmanaḥ  |
saṃbhavanti guṇāḥ sapta sapta vahnerivārciṣaḥ  || 1.26 ||
dharmaścārthaśca kīrtiśca lokapaktirupagrahaḥ  |
prajābhyo bahumānaśca svarge sthānaṃ ca śāśvatam  || 1.27 ||
tasmāddharmāsanaṃ prāpya rājā vigatamatsaraḥ  |
samaḥ syātsarvabhūteṣu bibhradvaivasvataṃ vratam  || 1.28 ||
dharmaśāstraṃ puraskṛtya prāḍvivākamate sthitaḥ  |
samāhitamatiḥ paśyedvyavahārānanukramāt || 1.29 ||
āgamaḥ prathamaṃ kāryo vyavahārapadaṃ tataḥ  |
vivitsā nirṇayaścaiva darśanaṃ syāccaturvidham  || 1.30 ||
dharmaśāstrārthaśāstrābhyāmavirodhena mārgataḥ  |
samīkṣamāṇo nipuṇaṃ vyavahāragatiṃ nayet || 1.31 ||
yathā mṛgasya viddhasya vyādho mṛgapadaṃ nayet |
kakṣe śoṇitaleśena tathā dharmapadaṃ nayet || 1.32 ||
yatra vipratipattiḥ syāddharmaśāstrārthaśāstrayoḥ  |
arthaśāstroktamutsṛjya dharmaśāstroktamācāret || 1.33 ||
dharmaśāstravirodhe tu yuktiyukto'pi dharmataḥ  |
vyavahāro hi balavān dharmastenāvahīyate  || 1.34 ||
sūkṣmo hi bhagavān dharmaḥ parokṣo durvicāraṇaḥ  |
ataḥ pratyakṣamārgeṇa vyavahāragatiṃ nayet || 1.35 ||
yātyacauro'pi cauratvaṃ cauraścāyātyacauratām  |
acauraścauratāṃ prāpto māṇḍavyo vyavahārataḥ  || 1.36 ||
strīṣu rātrau bahirgrāmādantarveśmanyarātiṣu  |
vyavahāraḥ kṛto'pyeṣu punaḥ kartavyatāmiyāt || 1.37 ||
gahanatvādvivādānāmasāmarthyātsmṛterapi  |
ṛṇādiṣu haretkālaṃ kāmaṃ tattvabubhutsayā  || 1.38 ||
gobhūhiraṇyastrīsteya- pāruṣyātyayikeṣu ca  |
sāhaseṣvabhiśāpe ca sadya eva vivādayet || 1.39 ||
anāvedya tu yo rājñe saṃdigdhe'rthe pravartate  |
prasahya sa vineyaḥ syātsa cāsyārtho na sidhyati  || 1.40 ||
vaktavye'rthe na tiṣṭhantamutkrāmantaṃ ca tadvacaḥ  |
āsedhayedvivādārthī yāvadāhvānadarśanam  || 1.41 ||
sthānāsedhaḥ kālakṛtaḥ pravāsātkarmaṇastathā  |
caturvidhaḥ syādāsedho nāsiddhastaṃ vilaṅghayet || 1.42 ||
nadīsaṃtārakāntāra- durdeśopaplavādiṣu  |
āsiddhastaṃ parāsedhamutkrāmannāparādhnuyāt || 1.43 ||
āsedhakāla āsiddha āsedhaṃ yo vyatikramet |
sa vineyo'nyathā kurvannāseddhā daṇḍabhāgbhavet || 1.44 ||
nirveṣṭukāmo rogārto yiyakṣurvyasane sthitaḥ  |
abhiyuktastathānyena rājakāryodyatastathā  || 1.45 ||
gavāṃ pracāre gopālāḥ sasyabandhe kṛṣīvalāḥ  |
śilpinaḥ cāpi tatkālamāyudhīyāśca vigrahe  || 1.46 ||
aprāptavyavahāraśca dūto dānonmukho vratī  |
viṣamasthaśca nāsedhyo na cainānāhvayennṛpaḥ  || 1.47 ||
nābhiyukto'bhiyuñjīta tamatīrtvārthamanyataḥ  |
na cābhiyuktamanyena na viddhaṃ veddhumarhati  || 1.48 ||
yamarthamabhiyuñjīta na taṃ viprakṛtiṃ nayet |
nānyatpakṣāntaraṃ gacchedgacchan pūrvātsa hīyate  || 1.49 ||
na ca mithyābhiyuñjīta doṣo mithyābhiyoginaḥ  |
yastatra vinayaḥ proktaḥ so'bhiyoktāramāvrajet || 1.50 ||
sāpadeśaṃ haran kālamabruvaṃścāpi saṃsadi  |
uktvā vaco vibruvaṃśca hīyamānasya lakṣaṇam  || 1.51 ||
palāyate ya āhūtaḥ prāptaśca vivadenna yaḥ  |
vineyaḥ sa bhavedrājñā hīna eva sa vādataḥ  || 1.52 ||
nirṇiktavyavahāreṣu pramāṇamaphalaṃ bhavet |
likhitaṃ sākṣiṇo vāpi pūrvamāveditaṃ na cet || 1.53 ||
yathā pakveṣu dhānyeṣu niṣphalāḥ prāvṛṣo guṇāḥ  |
nirṇiktavyavahārāṇāṃ pramāṇamaphalaṃ tathā  || 1.54 ||
abhūtamapyabhihitaṃ prāptakālaṃ parīkṣyate  |
yattu pramādānnocyeta tadbhūtamapi hīyate  || 1.55 ||
tīritaṃ cānuśiṣṭaṃ ca yo manyeta vidharmataḥ  |
dviguṇaṃ daṇḍamāsthāya tatkāryaṃ punaruddharet || 1.56 ||
durdṛṣṭe vyavahāre tu sabhyāstaṃ daṇḍamāpnuyuḥ  |
na hi jātu vinā daṇḍaṃ kaścinmārge'vatiṣṭhate  || 1.57 ||
rāgādajñānato vāpi lobhādvā yo'nyathā vadet |
sabhyo'sabhyaḥ sa vijñeyastaṃ rājā vinayedbhṛśam  || 1.58 ||
kiṃtu rājñā viśeṣeṇa svadharmamanurakṣatā  |
manuṣyacittavaicitryātparīkṣyā sādhvasādhutā  || 1.59 ||
puruṣāḥ santi ye lobhātprabrūyuḥ sākṣyamanyathā  |
santi cānye durātmānaḥ kūṭalekhyakṛto janāḥ  || 1.60 ||
ataḥ parīkṣyamubhayametadrājñā viśeṣataḥ  |
lekhyācāreṇa likhitaṃ sākṣyācāreṇa sākṣiṇaḥ  || 1.61 ||
asatyāḥ satyasaṃkāśāḥ satyāścāsatyadarśanāḥ  |
dṛśyante vividhā bhāvāstasmādyuktaṃ parīkṣaṇam  || 1.62 ||
talavaddṛśyate vyoma khadyoto havyavāḍiva  |
na talaṃ vidyate vyomni na khadyote hutāśanaḥ  || 1.63 ||
tasmātpratyakṣadṛṣṭo'pi yuktamarthaḥ parīkṣitum  |
parīkṣya jñāpayanarthānna dharmātparihīyate  || 1.64 ||
evaṃ paśyan sadā rājā vyavahārān samāhitaḥ  |
vitatyeha yaśo dīptaṃ bradhnasyāpnoti viṣṭapam  || 1.65 ||

[bhāṣā]

suniścitabalādhānastvarthī svārthapracoditaḥ  |
lekhayetpūrvapakṣaṃ tu kṛtakāryaviniścayaḥ  || 2.1 ||
pūrvapakṣaśrutārthastu pratyarthī tadanantaram  |
pūrvapakṣārthasaṃbandhaṃ pratipakṣaṃ niveśayet || 2.2 ||
śvo lekhanaṃ vā sa labhettryahaṃ saptāhameva vā  |
arthī tṛtīyapāde tu yuktaṃ sadyo dhruvaṃ jayī  || 2.3 ||
mithyā saṃpratipattirvā pratyavaskandameva vā  |
prāṅnyāyavidhisādhyaṃ vā uttaraṃ syāccaturvidham  || 2.4 ||
mithyaitannābhijānāmi tadā tatra na saṃnidhiḥ  |
ajātaścāsmi tatkāla evaṃ mithyā caturvidhā  || 2.5 ||
mithyā ca viparītaṃ ca punaḥ śabdasamāgamam  |
pūrvapakṣārthasaṃbandhamuttaraṃ syāccaturvidham  || 2.6 ||
bhāṣāyā uttaraṃ yāvatpratyarthī na niveśayet |
arthī tu lekhayettāvadyāvadvastu vivakṣitam  || 2.7 ||
anyārthamarthahīnaṃ ca pramāṇāgamavarjitam  |
lekhyaṃ hīnādhikaṃ bhraṣṭaṃ bhāṣādoṣāstūdāhṛtāḥ  || 2.8 ||
labdhavyaṃ yena yadyasmātsa tattasmādavāpnuyāt |
na tvanyo'nyadathānyasmādityanyārthamidaṃ tridhā  || 2.9 ||
manasāhamapi dhyātastvanmitreṇeha śatruvat |
ato'nyathā mahākṣāntyā tvamihāvedito mayā  || 2.10 ||
dravyapramāṇahīnaṃ yatphalopāśrayavarjitam  |
pramāṇavarjitaṃ nāma lekhyadoṣaṃ tadutsṛjet || 2.11 ||
āgamavarjitaṃ doṣaṃ pūrvapāde vivarjayet |
ekasya bahubhiḥ sārdhaṃ purarāṣṭravirodhakam  || 2.12 ||
bindumātrāpadavarṇeṣvekāvidhiṣṭayā (?)  |
hīnādhikā bhavedvyarthā tāṃ yatnena vivarjayet || 2.13 ||
bhraṣṭaṃ tu duḥsthitaṃ yatsyājjalatailādibhirhatam  |
bhāṣāyāṃ tadapi spaṣṭaṃ vispaṣṭārthaṃ vivarjayet || 2.14 ||
satyā bhāṣā na bhavati yadyapi syātpratiṣṭhitā  |
bahiścedbhraśyate dharmānniyatādvyavahārikāt || 2.15 ||
gandhamādanasaṃsthasya mayāsyāsīttadarpitam  |
vyavahārikadharmasya bāhyametanna sidhyati  || 2.16 ||
anyākṣaraniveśena anyārthagamanena ca  |
ākulaṃ ca kriyādānaṃ kriyā caivākulā bhavet || 2.17 ||
rāgādīnāṃ yadekena kopitaḥ karaṇe vadet |
tadādau tu likhetsarvaṃ vādinaḥ phalakādiṣu  || 2.18 ||
nirākulāvabodhāya dharmasthaiḥ suvicāritam  |
tasmādanyadvyapohyaṃ syādvādinaḥ phalakādiṣu  || 2.19 ||
vādibhyāmabhyanujñātaṃ śeṣaṃ ca phalake sthitam  |
sasākṣikaṃ likheyuste pratipattiṃ ca vādinoḥ  || 2.20 ||
vādibhyāṃ likhitāccheṣaṃ yatpunarvādinā smṛtam  |
tatpratyākalitaṃ nāma svapāde tasya likhyate  || 2.21 ||
arthinā saṃniyukto vā pratyarthiprahito'pi vā  |
yo yasyārthe vivadate tayorjayaparājayau  || 2.22 ||
yo na bhrātā na ca pitā na putro na niyogakṛt |
parārthavādī daṇḍyaḥ syādvyavahāre'pi vibruvan  || 2.23 ||
pūrvavādaṃ parityajya yo'nyamālambate punaḥ  |
vādasaṃkramaṇājjñeyo hīnavādī sa vai naraḥ  || 2.24 ||
sarveṣvapi vivādeṣu vākchale nāpahīyate  |
paśustrībhūmyṛṇādāne śāsyo'pyarthānna hīyate  || 2.25 ||
abhiyukto'bhiyogasya yadi kuryādapahnavam  |
abhiyoktā diśeddeśyaṃ pratyavaskandito na cet || 2.26 ||
pūrvapāde hi likhitaṃ yathākṣaramaśeṣataḥ  |
arthī tṛtīyapāde tu kriyayā pratipādayet || 2.27 ||
kriyāpi dvividhā proktā mānuṣī daivikī tathā  |
mānuṣī lekhyasākṣibhyāṃ dhaṭādirdaivikī smṛtā  || 2.28 ||
divā kṛte kāryavidhau grāmeṣu nagareṣu vā  |
saṃbhave sākṣiṇāṃ caiva divyā na bhavati kriyā  || 2.29 ||
araṇye nirjane rātrāvantarveśmani sāhase  |
nyāsasyāpahnave caiva divyā saṃbhavati kriyā  || 2.30 ||
kāraṇapratipattyā ca pūrvapakṣe virodhite  |
abhiyuktena vai bhāvyaṃ vijñeyaṃ pūrvapakṣavat || 2.31 ||
palāyate ya āhūto maunī sākṣiparājitaḥ  |
svayamabhyupapannaśca avasannaścaturvidhaḥ  || 2.32 ||
anyavādī kriyādveṣī nopasthātā niruttaraḥ  |
āhūtaprapalāyī ca hīnaḥ pañcavidhaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 2.33 ||
maṇayaḥ padmarāgādyā dīnārādi hiraṇmayam  |
muktāvidrumaśaṅkhādyāḥ praduṣṭāḥ svāmigāminaḥ  || 2.34 ||
gandhamālyamadattaṃ tu bhūṣaṇaṃ vāsa eva vā  |
pāduketi rājoktaṃ tadākrāman vadhamarhati  || 2.35 ||
paṇyamūlyaṃ bhṛtirnyāso daṇḍo yaccāvahārakam  |
vṛthādānākṣikapaṇā vardhante nāvivakṣitāḥ  || 2.36 ||
mithyābhiyogino ye syurdvijānāṃ śūdrayonayaḥ  |
teṣāṃ jihvāṃ samutkṛtya rājā śūle vidhāpayet || 2.37 ||
ājñā lekhaḥ paṭṭakaḥ śāsanaṃ vā ādhiḥ pattraṃ vikrayo vā krayo vā  |
rājñe kuryātpūrvamāvedanaṃ yastasya jñeyaḥ pūrvapakṣaḥ vidhijñaiḥ  || 2.38 ||
sākṣikadūṣaṇe kāryaṃ pūrvasākṣiviśodhanam  |
śuddheṣu sākṣiṣu tataḥ paścātsākṣyaṃ viśodhayet || 2.39 ||
sākṣisabhyāvasannānāṃ dūṣaṇe darśanaṃ punaḥ  |
svacaryāvasitānāṃ tu nāsti paunarbhavo vidhiḥ  || 2.40 ||
svayamabhyupapanno'pi svacaryāvasito'pi san  |
kriyāvasanno'pyarheta paraṃ sabhyāvadhāraṇam  || 2.41 ||
pakṣānutsārya kāryastu sabhyaiḥ kāryaviniścayaḥ  |
anutsāritanirṇikte virodhaḥ pretya ceha ca  || 2.42 ||
sabhaireva jitaḥ paścādrājñā śāsyaḥ svaśāstrataḥ  |
jayine cāpi deyaṃ syādyathāvajjayapatrakam  || 2.43 ||
vyavahāramukhaṃ caitatpūrvamuktaṃ svayaṃbhuvā  |
mukhaśuddhau hi śuddhiḥ syādvyavahārasya nānyathā  || 2.44 ||

mātṛkā 3

[sabhā]

niyuktena tu vaktavyamapakṣapatitaṃ vacaḥ  || 3.1 ||
yuktarūpaṃ bruvan sabhyo nāpnuyāddveṣakilbiṣe  |
bruvāṇastvanyathā sabhyastadevobhayamāpnuyāt || 3.2 ||
rājā tu dhārmikān sabhyānniyuñjyātsuparīkṣitān  |
vyavahāradhuraṃ voḍhuṃ ye śaktāḥ sadgavā iva  || 3.3 ||
dharmaśāstrārthakuśalāḥ kulīnāḥ satyavādinaḥ  |
samāḥ śatrau ca mitre ca nṛpateḥ syuḥ sabhāsadaḥ  || 3.4 ||
tatpratiṣṭhaḥ smṛto dharmo dharmamūlaśca pārthivaḥ  |
saha sadbhirato rājā vyavahārān viśodhayet || 3.5 ||
śuddheṣu vyavahāreṣu śuddhiṃ yānti sabhāsadaḥ  |
śuddhiśca teṣāṃ dharmāddhi dharmameva vadettataḥ  || 3.6 ||
yatra dharmo hyadharmeṇa satyaṃ yatrānṛtena ca  |
hanyate prekṣamāṇānāṃ hatāstatra sabhāsadaḥ  || 3.7 ||
viddho dharmo hyadharmeṇa sabhāṃ yatropatiṣṭhate  |
na cedviśalyaḥ kriyate viddhāstatra sabhāsadaḥ  || 3.8 ||
sabhā vā na praveṣṭavyā vaktavyaṃ vā samañjasam  |
abruvan vibruvan vāpi naro bhavati kilbiṣī  || 3.9 ||
ye tu sabhyāḥ sabhāṃ gatvā tūṣṇīṃ dhyāyanta āsate  |
yathāprāptaṃ na bruvate sarve te'nṛtavādinaḥ  || 3.10 ||
pādo'dharmasya kartāraṃ pādaḥ sākṣiṇamṛcchati  |
pādaḥ sabhāsadaḥ sarvān pādo rājānamṛcchati  || 3.11 ||
rājā bhavatyanenāstu mucyante ca sabhāsadaḥ  |
eno gacchati kartāraṃ nindārho yatra nindyate  || 3.12 ||
andho matsyānivāśnāti nirapekṣaḥ sakaṇṭakān  |
parokṣamarthavaikalyādbhāṣate yaḥ sabhāṃ gataḥ  || 3.13 ||

pratidānaṃ tathaivāsya pratyayaḥ syādviparyaye  || nar_2.03 ||
yācyamānastu yo dātrā nikṣepaṃ na prayacchati  |
daṇḍyaḥ sa rājñā dāpyaśca naṣṭe dāpyaśca tatsamam  || nar_2.04 ||
yaścārthaṃ sādhayettena nikṣepturananujñayā  |
tatrāpi daṇḍyaḥ sa bhavettacca sodayamāvahet || nar_2.05 ||
grahītuḥ saha yo'rthena naṣṭo naṣṭaḥ sa dāyinaḥ  |
daivarājakṛte tadvanna cettajjihmakāritam  || nar_2.06 ||
eṣa eva vidhirdṛṣṭo yācitānvāhitādiṣu  |
śilpiṣūpanidhau nyāse pratinyāse tathaiva ca  || nar_2.07 ||
pratigṛhṇāti pogaṇḍaṃ yaśca sapradhanaṃ naraḥ  |
tasyāpyeṣa bhaveddharmaḥ ṣaḍete vidhayaḥ samāḥ  || nar_2.08 ||

3.saṃbhūyasamutthānam

vaṇikprabhṛtayo yatra karma saṃbhūya kurvate  |
tatsaṃbhūyasamutthānaṃ vyavahārapadaṃ smṛtam  || nar_3.01 ||
phalahetorupāyena karma saṃbhūya kurvatām  |
ādhārabhūtaḥ prakṣepastenottiṣṭheyuraṃśataḥ  || nar_3.02 ||
samo'tirikto hīno vā yatrāṃśo yasya yādṛśaḥ  |
kṣayavyayau tathā vṛddhistasya tatra tathāvidhāḥ  || nar_3.03 ||
bhāṇḍapiṇḍavyayoddhāra- bhārasārānvavekṣaṇam  |
kuryuste'vyabhicāreṇa samaye sve vyavasthitāḥ  || nar_3.04 ||
pramādānnāśitaṃ dāpyaḥ pratiṣiddhakṛtaṃ ca yat |
asaṃdiṣṭaśca yatkuryātsarvaiḥ saṃbhūyakāribhiḥ  || nar_3.05 ||
daivataskararājotthe vyasane samupasthite  |
yastatsvaśaktyā saṃrakṣettasyāṃśo daśamaḥ smṛtaḥ  || nar_3.06 ||
ekasya cetsyādvyasanaṃ dāyādo'sya tadāpnuyāt |
anyo vāsati dāyāde śaktāścetsarva eva vā  || nar_3.07 ||
ṛtvijāṃ vyasane'pyevamanyastatkarma nistaret |
labheta dakṣiṇābhāgaṃ sa tasmātsaṃprakalpitam  || nar_3.08 ||
ṛtvigyājyamaduṣṭaṃ yastyajedanapakāriṇam  |
aduṣṭaṃ va rtvijaṃ yājyo vineyau tāvubhāvapi  || nar_3.09 ||
ṛtviktu trividho dṛṣṭaḥ pūrvajuṣṭaḥ svayaṃkṛtaḥ  |
yadṛcchayā ca yaḥ kuryādārtvijyaṃ prītipūrvakam  || nar_3.10 ||
kramāgateṣveṣa dharmo vṛteṣvṛtvikṣu ca svayam  |
yādṛcchike tu saṃyājye tattyāge nāsti kilbiṣam  || nar_3.11 ||
śulkasthānaṃ vaṇikprāptaḥ śulkaṃ dadyādyathopagam  |
na tadvyatiharedrājñāṃ balireṣa prakalpitaḥ  || nar_3.12 ||
śulkasthānaṃ pariharanna kāle krayavikrayī  |
mithyoktvā ca parīmāṇaṃ dāpyo'ṣṭaguṇamatyayam  || nar_3.13 ||
kaściccetsaṃcaran deśātpreyādabhyāgato vaṇik |
rājāsya bhāṇḍaṃ tadrakṣetyāvaddāyādadarśanam  || nar_3.14 ||
dāyāde'sati bandhubhyo jñātibhyo vā tadarpayet |
tadabhāve suguptaṃ taddhārayeddaśatīḥ samāḥ  || nar_3.15 ||
asvāmikamadāyādaṃ daśavarṣasthitaṃ tataḥ  |
rājā tadātmasātkuryādevaṃ dharmo na hīyate  || nar_3.16 ||

4.dattāpradānikam

dattvā dravyamasamyagyaḥ punarādātumicchati  |
dattāpradānikaṃ nāma tadvivādapadaṃ smṛtam  || nar_4.01 ||
adeyamatha deyaṃ ca dattaṃ cādattameva ca  |
vyavahāreṣu vijñeyo dānamārgaścaturvidhaḥ  || nar_4.02 ||
tatra hyaṣṭāvadeyāni deyamekavidhaṃ smṛtam  |
dattaṃ saptavidhaṃ vidyādadattaṃ ṣoḍaśātmakam  || nar_4.03 ||
anvāhitaṃ yācitakamādhiḥ sādhāraṇaṃ ca yat |
nikṣepaḥ putradāraṃ ca sarvasvaṃ cānvaye sati  || nar_4.04 ||
āpatsvapi hi kaṣṭāsu vartamānena dehinā  |
adeyānyāhurācāryā yaccānyasmai pratiśrutam  || nar_4.05 ||
kuṭumbabharaṇāddravyaṃ yatkiṃcidatiricyate  |
taddeyamupahṛtyānyaddadaddoṣamavāpnuyāt || nar_4.06 ||
paṇyamūlyaṃ bhṛtistuṣṭyā snehātpratyupakārataḥ  |
strīśulkānugrahārthaṃ ca dattaṃ dānavido viduḥ  || nar_4.07 ||
adattaṃ tu bhayakrodha- śokavegarujānvitaiḥ  |
tathotkocaparīhāsa- vyatyāsacchalayogataḥ  || nar_4.08 ||
bālamūḍhāsvatantrārta- mattonmattāpavarjitam  |
kartā mamāyaṃ karmeti pratilābhecchayā ca yat || nar_4.09 ||
apātre pātramityukte kārye cādharmasaṃhite  |
yaddattaṃ syādavijñānādadattaṃ tadapi smṛtam  || nar_4.10 ||
gṛhṇātyadattaṃ yo lobhādyaścādeyaṃ prayacchati  |
adattādāyako daṇḍyastathādeyasya dāyakaḥ  || nar_4.11 ||

5.abhyupetyāśuśrūṣā

abhyupetya tu śuśrūṣāṃ yastāṃ na pratipadyate  |
aśuśrūṣābhyupetyaitadvivādapadamucyate  || nar_5.01 ||
śuśrūṣakaḥ pañcavidhaḥ śāstre dṛṣṭo manīṣibhiḥ  |
caturvidhaḥ karmakarasteṣāṃ dāsāstripañcakāḥ  || nar_5.02 ||
śiṣyāntevāsibhṛtakāścaturthastvadhikarmakṛt |
ete karmakarāḥ proktā dāsāstu gṛhajādayaḥ  || nar_5.03 ||
sāmānyamasvatantratvameṣāmāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ  |
jātikarmakṛtastūkto viśeṣo vṛttireva ca  || nar_5.04 ||
karmāpi dvividhaṃ jñeyamaśubhaṃ śubhameva ca  |
aśubhaṃ dāsakarmoktaṃ śubhaṃ karmakṛtāṃ smṛtam  || nar_5.05 ||
gṛhadvārāśucisthāna- rathyāvaskaraśodhanam  |
guhyāṅgasparśanocchiṣṭa- viṇmūtragrahaṇojjhanam  || nar_5.06 ||
iṣṭataḥ svāminaścāṅgairupasthānamathāntataḥ  |
aśubhaṃ karma vijñeyaṃ śubhamanyadataḥ param  || nar_5.07 ||
ā vidyāgrahaṇācchiṣyaḥ śuśrūṣetprayato gurum  |
tadvṛttirgurudāreṣu guruputre tathaiva ca  || nar_5.08 ||
brahmacārī caredbhaikṣamadhaḥśāyyanalaṅkṛtaḥ  |
jaghanyaśāyī sarveṣāṃ pūrvotthāyī gurorgṛhe  || nar_5.09 ||
nāsaṃdiṣṭaḥ pratiṣṭheta tiṣṭhedvāpi guruṃ kvacit |
saṃdiṣṭaḥ karma kurvīta śaktaścedavicārayan  || nar_5.10 ||
yathākālamadhīyīta yāvanna vimanā guruḥ  |
āsīno'dho guroḥ kūrce phalake vā samāhitaḥ  || nar_5.11 ||
anuśāsyaśca guruṇā na cedanuvidhīyate  |
avadhenāthavā hanyātrajjvā veṇudalena vā  || nar_5.12 ||
bhṛśaṃ na tāḍayedenaṃ nottamāṅge na vakṣasi  |
anuśāsyātha viśvāsyaḥ śāsyo rājñānyathā guruḥ  || nar_5.13 ||
samāvṛttaśca gurave pradāya gurudakṣiṇām  |
pratīyātsvagṛhāneṣā śiṣyavṛttirudāhṛtā  || nar_5.14 ||
svaśilpamicchannāhartuṃ bāndhavānāmanujñayā  |
ācāryasya vasedante kālaṃ kṛtvā suniścitam  || nar_5.15 ||
ācāryaḥ śikṣayedenaṃ svagṛhāddattabhojanam  |
na cānyatkārayetkarma putravaccainamācaret || nar_5.16 ||
śikṣayantamaduṣṭaṃ ca yastvācāryaṃ parityajet |
balādvāsayitavyaḥ syādvadhabandhau ca so'rhati  || nar_5.17 ||
śikṣito'pi kṛtaṃ kālamantevāsī samāpnuyāt |
tatra karma ca yatkuryādācāryasyaiva tatphalam  || nar_5.18 ||
gṛhītaśilpaḥ samaye kṛtvācāryaṃ pradakṣiṇam  |
śaktitaścānumānyainamantevāsī nivartayet || nar_5.19 ||
bhṛtakastrividho jñeya uttamo madhyamo'dhamaḥ  |
śaktibhaktyanurūpā syādeṣāṃ karmāśrayā bhṛtiḥ  || nar_5.20 ||
uttamastvāyudhīyo'tra madhyamastu kṛṣīvalaḥ  |
adhamo bhāravāhaḥ syādityevaṃ trividho bhṛtaḥ  || nar_5.21 ||
artheṣvadhikṛto yaḥ syātkuṭumbasya tathopari  |
so'dhikarmakaro jñeyaḥ sa ca kauṭumbikaḥ smṛtaḥ  || nar_5.22 ||
śubhakarmakarāstvete catvāraḥ samudāhṛtāḥ  |
jaghanyakarmabhājastu śeṣā dāsāstripañcakāḥ  || nar_5.23 ||
gṛhajātastathā krīto labdho dāyādupāgataḥ  |
anākālabhṛtastadvadādhattaḥ svāminā ca yaḥ  || nar_5.24 ||
mokṣito mahataścarṇātprāpto yuddhātpaṇe jitaḥ  |
tavāhamityupagataḥ pravrajyāvasitaḥ kṛtaḥ  || nar_5.25 ||
bhaktadāsaśca vijñeyastathaiva vaḍavābhṛtaḥ  |
vikretā cātmanaḥ śāstre dāsāḥ pañcadaśā smṛtāḥ  || nar_5.26 ||
tatra pūrvaścaturvargo dāsatvānna vimucyate  |
prasādādsvāmino'nyatra dāsyameṣāṃ kramāgatam  || nar_5.27 ||
yaścaiṣāṃ svāminaṃ kaścinmokṣayetprāṇasaṃśayāt |
dāsatvātsa vimucyeta putrabhāgaṃ labheta ca  || nar_5.28 ||
anākālabhṛto dāsyānmucyate goyugaṃ dadat |
saṃbhakṣitaṃ yaddurbhikṣe na tacchudhyeta karmaṇā  || nar_5.29 ||
ādhatto'pi dhanaṃ dattvā svāmī yadyenamuddharet |
athopagamayedenaṃ sa vikrītādanantaraḥ  || nar_5.30 ||
dattvā tu sodayamṛṇamṛṇī dāsyātpramucyate  |
kṛtakālābhyupagamātkṛtako'pi vimucyate  || nar_5.31 ||
tavāhamityupagato yuddhaprāptaḥ paṇe jitaḥ  |
pratiśīrṣapradānena mucyate tulyakarmaṇā  || nar_5.32 ||
rājña eva tu dāsaḥ syātpravrajyāvasito naraḥ  |
na tasya pratimokṣo'sti na viśuddhiḥ kathaṃcana  || nar_5.33 ||
bhaktasyopekṣaṇātsadyo bhaktadāsaḥ pramucyate  |
nigrahādvaḍavāyāśca mucyate vaḍavābhṛtaḥ  || nar_5.34 ||
vikrīṇīte ya ātmānaṃ svatantraḥ sannarādhamaḥ  |
sa jaghanyatarasteṣāṃ naiva dāsyātpramucyate  || nar_5.35 ||
caurāpahṛtavikrītā ye ca dāsīkṛtā balāt |
rājñā mokṣayitavyāste dāsatvaṃ teṣu neṣyate  || nar_5.36 ||
varṇānāṃ prātilomyena dāsatvaṃ na vidhīyate  |
svadharmatyāgino'nyatra dāravaddāsatā matā  || nar_5.37 ||
tavāhamiti cātmānaṃ yo'svatantraḥ prayacchati  |
na sa taṃ prāpnuyātkāmaṃ pūrvasvāmī labheta tam  || nar_5.38 ||
adhanāstraya evoktā bhāryā dāsastathā sutaḥ  |
yatte samadhigacchanti yasya te tasya taddhanam  || nar_5.39 ||
svadāsamicchedyaḥ kartumadāsaṃ prītamānasaḥ  |
skandhādādāya tasyāpi bhindyātkumbhaṃ sahāmbhasā  || nar_5.40 ||
akṣatābhiḥ sapuṣpābhirmūrdhanyenamavākiret |
adāsa iti coktvā triḥ prāṅmukhaṃ tamathotsṛjet || nar_5.41 ||
tataḥprabhṛti vaktavyaḥ svāmyanugrahapālitaḥ  |
bhojyānnaḥ pratigṛhyaśca bhavatyabhimataśca saḥ  || nar_5.42 ||

6.vetanasyānapākarma

bhṛtānāṃ vetanasyokto dānādānavidhikramaḥ  |
vetanasyānapākarma tadvivādapadaṃ smṛtam  || nar_6.1 ||
bhṛtāya vetanaṃ dadyātkarmasvāmī yathākramam  |
ādau madhye'vasāne vā karmaṇo yadviniścitam  || nar_6.2 ||
bhṛtāvaniścitāyāṃ tu daśabhāgaṃ samāpnuyuḥ  |
lābhagobījasasyānāṃ vaṇiggopakṛṣībalāḥ  || nar_6.3 ||
karmopakaraṇaṃ caiṣāṃ kriyāṃ prati yadāhṛtam  |
āptabhāvena kurvīta na jihmena samācaret || nar_6.4 ||
karmākurvan pratiśrutya kāryo dattvā bhṛtiṃ balāt |
bhṛtiṃ gṛhītvākurvāṇo dviguṇāṃ bhṛtimāvahet || nar_6.5 ||
kāle'pūrṇe tyajetkarma bhṛtināśo'sya cārhati  |
svāmidoṣādapākrāman yāvatkṛtamavāpnuyāt || nar_6.6 ||
bhṛtiṣaḍbhāgamābhāṣya pathi yugyakṛtaṃ tyajan  |
adadatkārayitvā tu karmaivaṃ sodayāṃ bhṛtim  || nar_6.7 ||
anayan bhāṭayitvā tu bhāṇḍavān yānavāhane  |
dāpyo bhṛticaturbhāgaṃ samamardhapathe tyajan  || nar_6.8 ||
anayan vāhako'pyevaṃ bhṛtihānimavāpnuyāt |
dviguṇāṃ tu bhṛtiṃ dāpyaḥ prasthāne vighnamācaran  || nar_6.9 ||
bhāṇḍaṃ vyasanamāgacchedyadi vāhakadoṣataḥ  |
dāpyo yattatra naṣṭaṃ syāddaivarājakṛtādṛte  || nar_6.10 ||
gavāṃ śatādvatsatarī dhenuḥ syāddviśatādbhṛtiḥ  |
prati samvatsaraṃ gope sadohaścāṣṭame'hani  || nar_6.11 ||
upānayetgā gopāya pratyahaṃ rajanīkṣaye  |
cīṛṇāḥ pītāśca tā gopaḥ sāyāhne pratyupānayet || nar_6.12 ||
syāccedgovyasanaṃ gopo vyāyacchettatra śaktitaḥ  |
aśaktastūrṇamāgamya svāmine tannivedayet || nar_6.13 ||
avyāyacchannavikrośan svāmine cānivedayan  |
voḍhumarhati gopastāṃ vinayaṃ cāpi rājani  || nar_6.14 ||
naṣṭavinaṣṭaṃ kṛmibhiḥ śvahataṃ viṣame mṛtam  |
hīnaṃ puruṣakāreṇa gopāyaiva nipātayet || nar_6.15 ||
ajāvike tathāruddhe vṛkaiḥ pāle tvanāyati  |
yatprasahya vṛko hanyātpāle tatkilbiṣaṃ bhavet || nar_6.16 ||
tāsāṃ caivāniruddhānāṃ carantīnāṃ mitho vane  |
yāmutpatya vṛko hanyānna pālastatra kilbiṣī  || nar_6.17 ||
vighuṣya tu hṛtaṃ caurairna pālo dātumarhati  |
yadi deśe ca kāle ca svāminaḥ svasya śaṃsati  || nar_6.18 ||
etena sarvapālānāṃ vivādaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ  |
mṛteṣu ca viśuddhiḥ syātpālasyāṅkādidarśanāt || nar_6.19 ||
śulkaṃ gṛhītvā paṇyastrī necchantī dvistadāvahet |
aprayacchaṃstadā śulkamanubhūya pumān striyam  || nar_6.20 ||
ayonau kramate yastu bahubhirvāpi vāsayet |
śulkamaṣṭaguṇaṃ dāpyo vinayastāvadeva ca  || nar_6.21 ||
parājire gṛhaṃ kṛtvā stomaṃ dattvā vasettu yaḥ  |
sa tadgṛhītvā nirgacchettṛṇakāṣṭheṣṭakādikam  || nar_6.22 ||
stomavāhīni bhāṇḍāni pūrṇakālānyupānayet |
grahīturābhavedbhagnaṃ naṣṭaṃ cānyatra saṃplavāt || nar_6.23 ||

7.asvāmivikrayaḥ

nikṣiptaṃ vā paradravyaṃ naṣṭaṃ labdhvāpahṛtya vā  |
vikrīyate'samakṣaṃ yadvijñeyo'svāmivikrayaḥ  || nar_7.1 ||
dravyamasvāmivikrītaṃ prāpya svāmī samāpnuyāt |
prakāśaṃ krayataḥ śuddhiḥ kretuḥ steyaṃ rahaḥ krayāt || nar_7.2 ||
asvāmyanumatāddāsādasataśca janādrahaḥ  |
hīnamūlyamavelāyāṃ krīṇaṃstaddoṣabhāgbhavet || nar_7.3 ||
na gūhetāgamaṃ kretā śuddhistasya tadāgamāt |
viparyaye tulyadoṣaḥ steyadaṇḍaṃ ca so'rhati  || nar_7.4 ||
vikretā svāmine'rthaṃ ca kreturmūlyaṃ ca tatkṛtam  |
dadyāddaṇḍaṃ tathā rājñe vidhirasvāmivikraye  || nar_7.5 ||

pareṇa nihitaṃ labdhvā rājanyupaharennidhim  |
rājagāmī nidhiḥ sarvaḥ sarveṣāṃ brāhmaṇādṛte  || nar_7.6 ||
brāhmaṇo'pi nidhiṃ labdhvā kṣipraṃ rājñe nivedayet |
tena dattaṃ ca bhūñjīta stenaḥ syādanivedayan  || nar_7.7 ||
svamapyarthaṃ tathā naṣṭaṃ labdhvā rājñe nivedayet |
gṛhṇīyāttatra taṃ śuddhamaśuddhaṃ syāttato'nyathā  || nar_7.8 ||

8.krītānuśayaḥ

vikrīya paṇyaṃ mūlyena kreturyanna pradīyate  |
vikrīyāsaṃpradānaṃ tadvivādapadamucyate  || nar_8.1 ||
loke'smin dvividhaṃ dravyaṃ jaṅgamaṃ sthāvaraṃ tathā  |
krayavikrayadharmeṣu sarvaṃ tatpaṇyamucyate  || nar_8.2 ||
ṣaḍvidhastasya tu budhairdānādānavidhiḥ smṛtaḥ  |
gaṇimaṃ tulimaṃ meyaṃ kriyayā rūpataḥ śriyā  || nar_8.3 ||
vikrīya paṇyaṃ mūlyena kreturyo na prayacchati  |
sthāvarasya kṣayaṃ dāpyo jaṅgamasya kriyāphalam  || nar_8.4 ||
arghaścedapahīyeta sodayaṃ paṇyamāvahet |
sthāyināmeṣa niyamo diglābho digvicāriṇām  || nar_8.5 ||
upahanyeta vā paṇyaṃ dahyetāpahriyeta vā  |
vikretureva so'nartho vikrīyāsamprayacchataḥ  || nar_8.6 ||
nirdoṣaṃ darśayitvā tu sadoṣaṃ yaḥ prayacchati  |
mūlyaṃ taddviguṇaṃ dāpyo vinayaṃ tāvadeva ca  || nar_8.7 ||
tathānyahaste vikrīya yo'nyasmai saṃprayacchati  |
so'pi taddviguṇaṃ dāpyo vineyastāvadeva ca  || nar_8.8 ||
dīyamānaṃ na gṛhṇāti krītaṃ paṇyaṃ ca yaḥ krayī  |
vikrīṇānastadanyatra vikretā nāparādhnuyāt || nar_8.9 ||
dattamūlyasya paṇyasya vidhireṣa prakīrtitaḥ  |
adatte'nyatra samayānna vikreturatikramaḥ  || nar_8.10 ||
lābhārthe vaṇijāṃ sarva- paṇyeṣu krayavikrayaḥ  |
sa ca lābho'rghamāsādya mahān bhavati vā na vā  || nar_8.11 ||
tasmāddeśe ca kāle ca vaṇigarghaṃ parākramet |
na jihmena pravarteta śreyānevaṃ vaṇikpathaḥ  || nar_8.12 ||

9.vikrīyāsaṃpradānam

krītvā mūlyena yaḥ paṇyaṃ kretā na bahu manyate  |
krītvānuśaya ityetadvivādapadamucyate  || nar_9.1 ||
krītvā mūlyena yatpaṇyaṃ duṣkrītaṃ manyate krayī  |
vikretuḥ pratideyaṃ tattasminnevāhnyavikṣatam  || nar_9.2 ||
dvitīye'hni dadatkretā mūlyāttriṃśāṃśamāvahet |
dviguṇaṃ tattṛtīye'hni parataḥ kretureva tat || nar_9.3 ||
kretā paṇyaṃ parīkṣeta prāksvayaṃ guṇadoṣataḥ  |
parīkṣyābhimataṃ krītaṃ vikreturna bhavetpunaḥ  || nar_9.4 ||
tryahāddohyaṃ parīkṣeta pañcāhādvāhyameva tu  |
muktāvajrapravālānāṃ saptāhaṃ syātparīkṣaṇam  || nar_9.5 ||
dvipadāmardhamāsaṃ syātpuṃsāṃ taddviguṇaṃ striyāḥ  |
daśāhaṃ sarvabījānāmekāhaṃ lohavāsasām  || nar_9.6 ||
paribhuktaṃ ca yadvāsaḥ kliṣṭarūpaṃ malīmasam  |
sadoṣamapi vikrītaṃ vikreturna bhavetpunaḥ  || nar_9.7 ||
mūlyāṣṭabhāgo hīyeta sakṛddhautasya vāsasaḥ  |
dviḥ pādastristribhāgastu catuḥkṛtvo'rdhameva ca  || nar_9.8 ||
ardhakṣayāttu parataḥ pādāṃśāpacayaḥ kramāt |
yāvatkṣīṇadaśaṃ jīrṇaṃ jīrṇasyāniyamaḥ kṣaye  || nar_9.9 ||
lohānāmapi sarveṣāṃ heturagnikriyāvidhau  |
kṣayaḥ saṃskriyamāṇānāṃ teṣāṃ dṛṣṭo'gnisaṃgamāt || nar_9.10 ||
suvarṇasya kṣayo nāsti rajate dvipalaṃ śatam  |
śatamaṣṭapalaṃ jñeyaṃ kṣayassyāttrapusīsayoḥ  || nar_9.11 ||
tāmre pañcapalaṃ vidyādvikārā ye ca tanmayāḥ  |
taddhātūnāmanekatvādayaso'niyamaḥ kṣaye  || nar_9.12 ||
tāntavasya ca saṃskāre kṣayavṛddhī udāhṛte  |
sūtrakārpāsikorṇānāṃ vṛddhirdaśapalaṃ śatam  || nar_9.13 ||
sthūlasūtravatāṃ teṣāṃ madhyānāṃ pañcakaṃ śatam  |
tripalaṃ tu susūkṣmāṇāmantaḥkṣaya udāhṛtaḥ  || nar_9.14 ||
triṃśāṃśo romaviddhasya kṣayaḥ karmakṛtasya tu  |
kauṣeyavalkalānāṃ tu naiva vṛddhirna ca kṣayaḥ  || nar_9.15 ||

krītvā nānuśayaṃ kuryādvaṇikpaṇyavicakṣaṇaḥ  |
vṛddhikṣayau tu jānīyātpaṇyānāmāgamaṃ tathā  || nar_9.16 ||

10.samayasyānapākarma

pāṣaṇḍanaigamādīnāṃ sthitiḥ samaya ucyate  |
samayasyānapākarma tadvivādapadaṃ smṛtam  || nar_10.1 ||
pāṣaṇḍanaigamaśreṇī- pūgavrātagaṇādiṣu  |
saṃrakṣetsamayaṃ rājā durge janapade tathā  || nar_10.2 ||
yo dharmaḥ karma yaccaiṣāmupasthānavidhiśca yaḥ  |
yaccaiṣāṃ vṛttyupādānamanumanyeta tattathā  || nar_10.3 ||
pratikūlaṃ ca yadrājñaḥ prakṛtyavamataṃ ca yat |
bādhakaṃ ca yadarthānāṃ tattebhyo vinivartayet || nar_10.4 ||
mithaḥ saṃghātakaraṇamahitaṃ śastradhāraṇam  |
parasparopaghātaṃ ca teṣāṃ rājā na marṣayet || nar_10.5 ||
pṛthaggaṇāṃśca ye bhindyuste vineyā viśeṣataḥ  |
āvaheyurbhayaṃ ghoraṃ vyādhivatte hyupekṣitāḥ  || nar_10.6 ||
doṣavatkaraṇaṃ yatsyādanāmnāyaprakalpitam  |
pravṛttamapi tadrājā śreyaskāmo nivartayet || nar_10.7 ||

11.kṣetrajavivādaḥ

setukedāramaryādā- vikṛṣṭākṛṣṭaniścayāḥ  |
kṣetrādhikārā yatra syurvivādaḥ kṣetrajastu saḥ  || nar_11.1 ||
kṣetrasīmāvirodheṣu sāmantebhyo viniścayaḥ  |
nagaragrāmagaṇino ye ca vṛddhatamā narāḥ  || nar_11.2 ||
grāmasīmāsu ca bahirye syustatkṛṣijīvinaḥ  |
gopaśākunikavyādhā ye cānye vanagocarāḥ  || nar_11.3 ||
samunnayeyuste sīmāṃ lakṣaṇairupalakṣitām  |
tuṣāṅgārakapālaiśca kumbhairāyatanairdrumaiḥ  || nar_11.4 ||
abhijñātaiśca valmīka- sthalanimnonnatādibhiḥ  |
kedārārāmamārgaiśca purāṇaiḥ setubhistathā  || nar_11.5 ||
nimnagāpahṛtotsṛṣṭa- naṣṭacihnāsu bhūmiṣu  |
tatpradeśānumānācca pramāṇairbhogadarśanaiḥ  || nar_11.6 ||
atha cedanṛtaṃ brūyuḥ sāmantāstadviniścaye  |
sarve pṛthakpṛthagdaṇḍyā rājñā madhyamasāhasam  || nar_11.7 ||
gaṇavṛddhādayastvanye daṇḍaṃ dāpyāḥ pṛthakpṛthak |
vineyāḥ prathamena syuḥ sāhasenānṛte sthitāḥ  || nar_11.8 ||
naikaḥ samunnayetsīmāṃ naraḥ pratyayavānapi  |
gurutvādasya dharmasya kriyaiṣā bahuṣu sthitā  || nar_11.9 ||
ekaścedunnayetsīmāṃ sopavāsaḥ samāhitaḥ  |
raktamālyāmbaradharaḥ kṣitimāropya mūrdhani  || nar_11.10 ||
yadā ca na syurjñātāraḥ sīmāyā na ca lakṣaṇam  |
tadā rājā dvayoḥ sīmāmuddharediṣṭataḥ svayam  || nar_11.11 ||
etenaiva gṛhodyāna- nipānāyatanādiṣu  |
vivādavidhirākhyātastathā grāmāntareṣu ca  || nar_11.12 ||
avaskarasthalaśvabhra- bhramasyandanikādibhiḥ  |
catuṣpathasurasthāna- rathyāmārgānna rodhayet || nar_11.13 ||
parakṣetrasya madhye tu seturna pratiṣidhyate  |
mahāguṇo'lpabādhaśca vṛddhiriṣṭā kṣaye sati  || nar_11.14 ||
setustu dvididho jñeyaḥ kheyo bandhyastathaiva ca  |
toyapravartanān kheyo bandhyaḥ syāttannivartanāt || nar_11.15 ||
nāntareṇodakaṃ sasyaṃ naśyedabhyudakena tu  |
ya evānudake doṣaḥ sa evābhyudake smṛtaḥ  || nar_11.16 ||
pūrvapravṛttamutsannamapṛṣṭvā svāminaṃ tu yaḥ  |
setuṃ pravartayetkaścinna sa tatphalabhāgbhavet || nar_11.17 ||
mṛte tu svāmini punastadvaṃśye vāpi mānave  |
rājānamāmantrya tataḥ prakuryātsetukarma tat || nar_11.18 ||
ato'nyathā kleśabhāksyānmṛgavyādhānudarśanāt |
iṣavastasya naśyanti yo viddhamanuvidhyati  || nar_11.19 ||
aśaktapretanaṣṭeṣu kṣetrikeṣvanivāritaḥ  |
kṣetraṃ cedvikṛṣetkaścidaśnuvīta sa tatphalam  || nar_11.20 ||
vikṛṣyamāṇe kṣetre cetkṣetrikaḥ punarāvrajet |
khilopacāraṃ tatsarvaṃ dattvā svakṣetramāpnuyāt || nar_11.21 ||
tadaṣṭabhāgāpacayādyāvatsapta gatāḥ samāḥ  |
saṃprāpte tvaṣṭame varṣe bhuktaṃ kṣetraṃ labheta saḥ  || nar_11.22 ||
saṃvatsareṇārdhakhilaṃ khilaṃ tadvatsaraistribhiḥ  |
pañcavarṣāvasannaṃ tu syātkṣetramaṭavīsamam  || nar_11.23 ||
kṣetraṃ tripuruṣaṃ yatsyādgṛhaṃ vā syātkramāgatam  |
rājaprasādādanyatra na tadbhogaḥ paraṃ nayet || nar_11.24 ||
utkramya tu vṛtiṃ yatra sasyaghāto gavādibhiḥ  |
pālaḥ śāsyo bhavettatra na cecchaktyā nivārayet || nar_11.25 ||
samūlasasyanāśe tu tatsvāmī samamāpnuyāt |
vadhena pālo mucyeta daṇḍaṃ svāmini pātayet || nar_11.26 ||
gauḥ prasūtā daśāhātca mahokṣājāvikuñjarāḥ  |
nivāryāstu prayatnena teṣāṃ svāmī na daṇḍabhāk || nar_11.27 ||
māṣaṃ gāṃ dāpayeddaṇḍaṃ dvau māṣau mahiṣīṃ tathā  |
ajāvike savatse tu daṇḍaḥ syādardhamāṣakaḥ  || nar_11.28 ||
adaṇḍyā hastino'śvāśca prajāpālā hi te smṛtāḥ  |
adaṇḍyā garbhiṇī gauśca sūtikā cābhisāriṇī  || nar_11.29 ||

proktastu dvirniṣaṇṇānāṃ vasantyāṃ tu caturguṇam  |
pratyakṣacārakāṇāṃ tu cauradaṇḍaḥ smṛṭastathā  || nar_11.30 ||
yā naṣṭāḥ pāladoṣeṇa gāvaḥ kṣetraṃ samāśritāḥ  |
na tatra gomino daṇḍaḥ pālastaṃ daṇḍamarhati  || nar_11.31 ||
rājagrāhagṛhīto vā vajrāśanihato'pi vā  |
atha sarpeṇa daṣṭo vā giryagrātpatito'pi vā  || nar_11.32 ||
siṃhavyāghrahato vāpi vyādhibhiḥ caiva pātitaḥ  |
na tatra doṣaḥ pālasya na ca doṣo'sti gominām  || nar_11.33 ||
gobhistu bhakṣitaṃ dhānyaṃ yo naraḥ pratimārgati  |
sāmantasya śado deyo dhānyaṃ yattatra vāpitam  |
gavatraṃ gomine deyaṃ dhānyaṃ tatkarṣikasya tu  || nar_11.34 ||
grāmopānte ca yatkṣetraṃ vivītānte mahāpathe  |
anāvṛte cettannāśe na pālasya vyatikramaḥ  || nar_11.35 ||
