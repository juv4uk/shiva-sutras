ācārakāṇḍam

cāturvarṇyācāram


athāto himaśailāgre devadāruvanālaye  |
vyāsamekāgramāsīnamapṛcchannṛṣayaḥ purā (ī,1, p. 37)  || 1.1 ||

mānuṣāṇāṃ hitaṃ dharmaṃ vartamāne kalau yuge  |
śaucācāraṃ yathāvacca vada satyavatīsuta  || 1.2 ||

tacchrutvā ṛṣivākyaṃ tu saśiṣyo'gnyarkasaṃnibhaḥ  |
pratyuvāca mahātejāḥ śrutismṛtiviśāradaḥ (ī,1, p. 65)  || 1.3 ||

na cāhaṃ sarvatattvajñaḥ kathaṃ dharmaṃ vadāmyahaṃ  |
asmatpitaiva praṣṭavya iti vyāsaḥ suto'bravīt || 1.4 ||

tatasta ṛṣayaḥ sarve dharmatattvārthakāṅkṣiṇaḥ  |
ṛṣiṃ vyāsaṃ puraskṛtya gatā badarikāśramam (ī,1, p. 68)  || 1.5 ||

nānāpuṣpalatākīrṇaṃ phalavṛṣkairalaṃkṛtam  |
nadīprasravaṇopetaṃ puṇyatīrthopaśobhitaṃ (ī,1, p. 70)  || 1.6 ||

mṛgapakṣininādāḍhyaṃ devatāyatanāvṛtam  |
yakṣagandharvasiddhaiśca nṛtyagītairalaṃkṛtam  || 1.7 ||

tasminnṛṣisabhāmadhye śaktiputraṃ parāśaram  |
sukhāsīnaṃ mahātejā munimukhyagaṇāvṛtam (ī,1, p. 73)  || 1.8 ||

kṛtāñjalipuṭo bhūtvā vyāsastu ṛṣibhiḥ saha  |
pradakṣiṇābhivādaiśca stutibhiḥ samapūjayat || 1.9 ||

tataḥ saṃtuṣṭahṛdayaḥ parāśaramahāmuniḥ  |
āha susvāgataṃ brūhītyāsīno munipuṅgavaḥ (ī,1, p. 75)  || 1.10 ||

kuśalaṃ samyagityuktvā vyāsaḥ pṛcchatyanantaram  |
yadi jānāsi bhaktiṃ me snehādvā bhaktavatsala (ī,1, p. 77)  || 1.11 ||

dharmaṃ kathaya me tāta anugrāhyo hyahaṃ tava  |
śrutā me mānavā dharmā vāsiṣṭhā kāśyapāstathā (ī,1, p. 78)  || 1.12 ||

gargeyā gautamīyāśca tathā cośanasā smṛtāḥ  |
atrerviṣṇośca saṃvartāddakṣādaṅgirasastathā  || 1.13 ||

śātātapācca hārītādyājñavalkyāttathaiva ca  |
āpastambakṛtā dharmā śaṅkhasya likhitasya ca  || 1.14 ||

kātyāyanakṛtāścaiva tathā prācetasānmuneḥ  |
śrutā hyete bhavatproktāḥ śrutyarthā me na vismṛtāḥ (ī,1, p. 79)  || 1.15 ||

asminmanvantare dharmāḥ kṛtatretādike yuge  |
sarve dharmāḥ kṛte jātāḥ sarve naṣṭāḥ kalau yuge (ī,1, p. 80)  || 1.16 ||

cāturvarṇyasamācāraṃ kiṃcitsādhāraṇaṃ vada  |
caturṇāmapi varṇānāṃ kartavyaṃ dharmakovidaiḥ (ī,1, p. 87)  || 1.17 ||

brūhi dharmasvarūpajña sūkṣmaṃ sthūlaṃ ca vistarāt |
vyāsavākyāvasāne tu munimukhyaḥ parāśaraḥ (ī,1, p. 92)  || 1.18 ||

dharmasya nirṇayaṃ prāha sūkṣmaṃ sthūlaṃ ca vistarāt |
śṛṇu putra pravakṣyāmi śṛṇvantu munayastathā (ī,1, p. 93)  || 1.19 ||

kalpe kalpe kṣayotpattyā brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ  |
śrutismṛtisadācāra- nirṇetāraśca sarvadā  || 1.20 ||

na kaścidvedakartā ca vedaṃ smṛtvā caturmukhaḥ  |
tathaiva dharmān smarati manuḥ kalpāntare'ntare (ī,1, p. 112)  || 1.21 ||

anye kṛtayuge dharmāstretāyāṃ dvāpare yuge  |
anye kaliyuge nṝṇāṃ yugarūpānusārataḥ (ī,1, p. 117)  || 1.22 ||

tapaḥ paraṃ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ jñānamucyate  |
dvāpare yajñamevāhuḥ dānameva kalau yuge (ī,1, p. 119)  || 1.23 ||

kṛte tu mānavā dharmāstretāyāṃ gautamāḥ smṛtāḥ  |
dvāpare śāṅkhalikhitāḥ kalau pārāśarāḥ smṛtāḥ (ī,1, p. 122)  || 1.24 ||

tyajeddeśaṃ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ grāmamutsṛjet |
dvāpare kulamekaṃ tu kartāraṃ tu kalau yuge  || 1.25 ||

kṛte saṃbhāṣaṇādeva tretāyāṃ sparśanena ca  |
dvāpare tvannamādāya kalau patati karmaṇā (ī,1, p. 123)  || 1.26 ||
kṛte tātkālikaḥ śāpastretāyāṃ daśabhirdinaiḥ  |
dvāpare caikamāsena kalau saṃvatsareṇa tu  || 1.27 ||

abhigamya kṛte dānaṃ tretāsvāhūya dīyate  |
dvāpare yācamānāya sevayā dīyate kalau  || 1.28 ||

abhigamyottamaṃ dānamāhūyaiva tu madhyamam  |
adhamaṃ yācamānāya sevādānaṃ tu niṣphalam (ī,1, p. 124)  || 1.29 ||

jito dharmo hyadharmeṇa satyaṃ caivānṛtena ca  |
jitāścoraiśca rājānaḥ strībhiśca puruṣāḥ kalau  || 1.30 ||

sīdanti cāgnihotrāṇi gurupūjā praṇaśyati  |
kumāryaśca prasūyante asmin kaliyuge sadā (ī,1, p. 125)  || 1.31 ||

kṛte tvasthigatāḥ prāṇāstretāyāṃ māṃsamāśritāḥ  |
dvāpare rudhiraṃ caiva kalau tvannādiṣu sthitāḥ (ī,1, p. 126)  || 1.32 ||

yuge yuge ca ye dharmāstatra tatra ca ye dvijāḥ  |
teṣāṃ nindā na kartavyā yugarūpā hi te dvijāḥ (ī,1, p. 128)  || 1.33 ||

yuge yuge tu sāmarthyaṃ śeṣaṃ munivibhāṣitam  |
parāśareṇa cāpyuktaṃ prāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate (ī,1, p. 131)  || 1.34 ||

ahamadyaiva tatsarvamanusmṛtya bravīmi vaḥ  |
cāturvarṇyasamācāraṃ śṛṇvantu ṛṣipuṅgavāḥ (ī,1, p. 140)  || 1.35 ||

parāśaramataṃ puṇyaṃ pavitraṃ pāpanāśanam  |
cintitaṃ brāhmaṇārthāya dharmasaṃsthāpanāya ca (ī,1, p. 141)  || 1.36 ||

caturṇāmapi varṇānāmācāro dharmapālakaḥ  |
ācārabhraṣṭadehānāṃ bhaveddharmaḥ parāṅmukhaḥ (ī,1, p. 143)  || 1.37 ||

ṣaṭkarmābhirato nityaṃ devatātithipūjakaḥ  |
hutaśeṣaṃ tu bhuñjāno brāhmaṇo nāvasīdati (ī,1, p. 145)  || 1.38 ||

saṃdhyāsnānaṃ japo homo devatātithipūjanam  |
ātithyaṃ vaiśvadevaṃ ca ṣaṭkarmāṇi dine dine (ī,1, p. 218)  || 1.39 ||

iṣṭo vā yadi vā dveṣyo mūrkhaḥ paṇḍita eva vā  |
saṃprāpto vaiśvadevānte so'tithiḥ svargasaṃgramaḥ (ī,1, p. 395)  || 1.40 ||

dūrādhvopagataṃ śrāntaṃ vaiśvadeva upasthitam  |
atithiṃ taṃ vijānīyānnātithiḥ pūrvamāgataḥ (ī,1, p. 396)  || 1.41 ||

naikagrāmīṇamatithiṃ saṃgṛhṇīta kadācana  |
anityamāgato yasmāttasmādatithirucyate (ī,1, p. 398)  || 1.42 ||

atithiṃ tatra saṃprāptaṃ pūjayetsvāgatādinā  |
arghyāsanapradānena pādaprakṣālanena ca (ī,1, p. 399)  || 1.43 ||

śraddhayā cānnadānena priyapraśnottareṇa ca  |
gacchantaṃ cānuyānena prītimutpādayedgṛhī (ī,1, p. 400)  || 1.44 ||

atithiryasya bhagnāśo gṛhātpratinivartate  |
pitarastasya nāśnanti daśa varṣāṇi pañca ca (ī,1, p. 401)  || 1.45 ||

kāṣṭhabhārasahasreṇa ghṛtakumbhaśatena ca  |
atithiryasya bhagnāśastasya homo nirarthakaḥ (ī,1, p. 402)  || 1.46 ||

sukṣetre vāpayedbījaṃ supātre nikṣipeddhanam  |
sukṣetre ca supātre ca hyuptaṃ dattaṃ na naśyati (ī,1, p. 403)  || 1.47 ||

na pṛcchedgotracaraṇe na svādhyāyaṃ śrutaṃ tathā  |
hṛdaye kalpayeddevaṃ sarvadevamayo hi saḥ (ī,1, p. 404)  || 1.48 ||

apūrvaḥ suvratī vipro hyapūrvaścātitihiryathā  |
vedābhyāsarato nityaṃ tāvapūrvau dine dine (ī,1, p. 406)  || 1.49 ||

vaiśvadeve tu saṃprāpte bhikṣuke gṛhamāgate  |
uddhṛtya vaiśvadevārthaṃ bhikṣukaṃ tu visarjayet(ī,1, p. 408)  || 1.50 ||

yatiśca brahmacārī ca pakvānnasvāmināvubhau  |
tayorannamadattvā tu bhuktvā cāndrāyaṇaṃ caret || 1.51 ||

dadyācca bhikṣātritayaṃ parivrāḍbrahmacāriṇām  |
icchayā ca tato dadyādvibhave satyavāritam (ī,1, p. 409)  || 1.52 ||

yatihaste jalaṃ dadyādbhaikṣaṃ dadyātpunarjalam  |
tadbhaikṣyaṃ meruṇā tulyaṃ tajjalaṃ sāgaropamam  || 1.53 ||

yasya chatraṃ hayaścaiva kuñjarārohamṛddhimat |
aindraṃ sthānamupāsīta tasmāttaṃ na vicārayet(ī,1, p. 410)  || 1.54 ||

vaiśvadevakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ śakto bhikṣurvyapohitum  |
na hi bhikṣukṛtān doṣān vaiśvadevo vyapohati (ī,1, p. 411)  || 1.55 ||

akṛtvā vaiśvadevaṃ tu bhuñjate ye dvijādhamāḥ  |
sarve te niṣphalā jñeyāḥ patanti narake'śucau (ī,1, p. 412)  || 1.56 ||

vaiśvadevavihīnā ye ātithyena bahiṣkṛtāḥ  |
sarve te narakaṃ yānti kākayoniṃ vrajanti ca  || 1.57 ||

pāpo vā yadi caṇḍālo vipraghnaḥ pitṛghātakaḥ  |
vaiśvadeve tu saṃprāptaḥ so'tithiḥ svargasaṃkramaḥ  || 1.58 ||

yo veṣṭitaśirā bhuṅkte yo bhuṅkte dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ  |
vāmapāde karaṃ nyasya tadvai rakṣāṃsi bhuñjate (ī,1, p. 413)  || 1.59 ||

avratā hyanadhīyānā yatra bhaikṣacarā dvijāḥ  |
taṃ grāmaṃ daṇḍayedrājā corabhaktaprado hi saḥ (ī,1, p. 445)  || 1.60 ||

kṣatriyo hi prajā rakṣañśastrapāṇiḥ pradaṇḍavān  |
nirjitya parasainyāni kṣitiṃ dharmeṇa pālayet || 1.61 ||

puṣpaṃ puṣpaṃ vicinuyānmūlacchedaṃ na kārayet |
mālākāra ivārāme na yathāṅgārakārakaḥ  || 1.62 ||

lābhakarma tathā ratnaṃ gavāṃ ca paripālanam  |
kṛṣikarma ca vāṇijyaṃ vaiśyavṛttirudāhṛtā (ī,1, p. 476)  || 1.63 ||

śūdrasya dvijaśuśrūṣā paramo dharma ucyate  |
anyathā kurute kiṃcittadbhavettasya niṣphalam (ī,1, p. 480)  || 1.64 ||

lavaṇaṃ madhutailaṃ ca dadhitakraṃ ghṛtaṃ payaḥ  |
na duṣyecchūdrajātīnāṃ kuryātsarveṣu vikrayam (ī,1, p. 484)  || 1.65 ||

vikrīṇanmadhyamāṃsāni hyabhakṣasya ca bhakṣaṇam  |
kurvannagamyā gamanaṃ śūdraḥ patati tatkṣaṇāt(ī,1, p. 486)  || 1.66 ||

kapilākṣīrapānena brāhmaṇīgamanena ca  |
vedākṣaravicāreṇa śūdraścāṇḍālatāṃ vrajet || 1.67 ||

ataḥ paraṃ gṛhasthasya karmācāraṃ kalau yuge  |
dharmaṃ sādhāraṇaṃ śaktyā cāturvarṇyāśramāgatam (ī,2, p. 1)  || 2.1 ||

taṃ pravakṣyāmyahaṃ pūrvaṃ parāśaravaco yathā  |
ṣaṭkarmasahito vipraḥ kṛṣikarma ca kārayet(ī,2, p. 3)  || 2.2 ||

kṣudhitaṃ tṛṣitaṃ śrāntaṃ balīvardaṃ na yojayet |
hīnāṅgaṃ vyādhitaṃ klībaṃ vṛṣaṃ vipro na vāhayet(ī,2, p. 6)  || 2.3 ||

sthirāṅgaṃ nīrujaṃ tṛptaṃ sunardaṃ ṣaṇḍhavarjitam  |
vāhayeddivasasyārdhaṃ paścātsnānaṃ samācaret || 2.4 ||

japyaṃ devārcanaṃ homaṃ svādhyāyaṃ caivamabhyaset |
ekadvitricaturviprān bhojayetsnātakān dvijaḥ (ī,2, p. 8)  || 2.5 ||

svayaṃkṛṣṭe tathā kṣetre dhānyaiśca svayamarjitaiḥ  |
nirvapetpañca yajñāṃśca kratudīkṣāṃ ca kārayet(ī,2, p. 9)  || 2.6 ||

tilā rasā na vikreyā vikreyā dhānyatatsamāḥ  |
viprasyaivaṃvidhā vṛttistṛṇakāṣṭhādivikrayaḥ  || 2.7 ||

brāhmaṇaścetkṛṣiṃ kuryāttanmahādoṣamāpnuyāt |
saṃvatsareṇa yatpāpaṃ matsyaghātī samāpnuyāt(ī,2, p. 13)  || 2.8 ||

ayomukhena kāṣṭhena tadekāhena lāṅgalī  |
pāśako matsyaghātī ca vyādhaḥ śākunikastathā  || 2.9 ||

adātā karṣakaścaiva sarve te samabhāginaḥ  |
vṛkṣaṃ chittvā mahīṃ bhittvā hatvā ca kṛmikīṭakān  || 2.10 ||

karṣakaḥ khalayajñena sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate  |
yo na dadyāddvijātibhyo rāśimūlamupāgataḥ (ī,2, p. 14)  || 2.11 ||

sa cauraḥ sa ca pāpiṣṭho brahmaghnaṃ taṃ vinirdiśet |
rājñe dattvā tu ṣaḍbhāgaṃ devānāṃ caikaviṃśakam (ī,2, p. 15)  || 2.12 ||

viprāṇāṃ triṃśakaṃ bhāgaṃ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate  |
kṣatriyo'pi kṛṣiṃ kṛtvā devān viprāṃśca pūjayet || 2.13 ||

vaiśyaḥ śūdrastathā kuryātkṛṣivāṇijyaśilpakam  |
vikarma kurvate śūdrā dvijaśuśrūṣayojjhitāḥ (ī,2, p. 16)  || 2.14 ||

bhavantyalpāyuṣaste vai nirayaṃ yāntyasaṃśayaṃ  |
caturṇāmapi varṇānāmeṣa dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ  || 2.15 ||





ataḥ śuddhiṃ pravakṣyāmi janane maraṇe tathā  |
dinatrayeṇa śudhyanti brāhmaṇāḥ pretasūtake (ī,2, p. 204)  || 3.1 ||

kṣatriyo dvādaśāhena vaiśyaḥ pañcadaśāhakaiḥ  |
śūdraḥ śudhyati māsena parāśaravaco yathā  || 3.2 ||

upāsane tu viprāṇāmaṅgaśuddhiśca jāyate  |
brāhmaṇānāṃ prasūtau tu dehasparśo vidhīyate (ī,2, p. 212)  || 3.3 ||

jātau vipro daśāhena dvādaśāhena bhūmipaḥ  |
vaiśyaḥ pañcadaśāhena śūdro māsena śudhyati (ī,2, p. 213)  || 3.4 ||

ekāhācchudhyate vipro yo'gnivedasamanvitaḥ  |
tryahātkevalavedastu dvihīno daśabhirdinaiḥ (ī,2, p. 215)  || 3.5 ||

janmakarmaparibhraṣṭaḥ saṃdhyopāsanavarjitaḥ  |
nāmadhārakaviprastu daśāhaṃ sūtakī bhavet(ī,2, p. 217)  || 3.6 ||

ekapiṇḍāstu dāyādāḥ pṛthagdāraniketanāḥ  |
janmanyapi vipattau ca teṣāṃ tatsūtakaṃ bhavet(ī,2, p. 219)  || 3.7 ||

tāvattatsūtakaṃ gotre caturthapuruṣeṇa tu  |
dāyādvicchedamāpnoti pañcamo vātmavaṃśajaḥ (ī,2, p. 221)  || 3.8 ||

caturthe daśarātraṃ syātṣaṇṇiśāḥ puṃsi pañcame  |
ṣaṣṭhe caturahācchuddhiḥ saptame tu dinatrayāt(ī,2, p. 222)  || 3.9 ||

bhṛgvagnimaraṇe caiva deśāntaramṛte tathā  |
bāle prete ca saṃnyaste sadyaḥ śaucaṃ vidhīyate (ī,2, p. 223)  || 3.10 ||

deśāntaramṛtaḥ kaścitsagotraḥ śrūyate yadi  |
na trirātramahorātraṃ sadyaḥ snātvā śucirbhavet(ī,2, p. 232)  || 3.11 ||

deśāntaragato vipraḥ prayāsātkālakāritāt |
dehanāśamanuprāptastithirna jñāyate yadi (ī,2, p. 236)  || 3.12 ||

kṛṣṇāṣṭamī tvamāvāsyā kṛṣṇā caikādaśī ca yā  |
udakaṃ piṇḍadānaṃ ca tatra śrāddhaṃ ca kārayet || 3.13 ||

ajātadantā ye bālā ye ca garbhādviniḥsṛtāḥ  |
na teṣāmagnisaṃskāro nāśaucaṃ nodakakriyā (ī,2, p. 237)  || 3.14 ||

yadi garbho vipadyeta sravate vāpi yoṣitaḥ  |
yāvanmāsaṃ sthito garbho dinaṃ tāvattu sūtakam (ī,2, p. 238)  || 3.15 ||

ā caturthādbhavetsrāvaḥ pātaḥ pañcamaṣaṣṭhayoḥ  |
ata ūrdhvaṃ prasūtiḥ syāddaśāhaṃ sūtakaṃ bhavet(ī,2, p. 240)  || 3.16 ||

dantajāte'nujāte ca kṛtacūḍe ca saṃsthite  |
agnisaṃskaraṇe teṣāṃ trirātramaśucirbhavet(ī,2, p. 241)  || 3.16|1 ||

ā dantajanmanaḥ sadya ā cūḍānnaiṣikī smṛtā  |
trirātramā vratādeśāddaśarātramataḥ param (ī,2, p. 243)  || 3.17 ||

brahmacārī gṛhe yeṣāṃ hūyate ca hutāśanaḥ  |
saṃparkaṃ na ca kurvanti na teṣāṃ sūtakaṃ bhavet(ī,2, p. 253)  || 3.18 ||

saṃparkādduṣyate vipro janane maraṇe tathā  |
saṃparkācca nivṛttasya na pretaṃ naiva sūtakam (ī,2, p. 254)  || 3.19 ||

śilpinaḥ kārukā vaidyā dāsīdāsāśca nāpitāḥ  |
rājānaḥ śrotriyāścaiva sadyaḥ śaucāḥ prakīrtitāḥ  || 3.20 ||

savrataḥ satrapūtaśca āhitāgniśca yo dvijaḥ  |
rājñaśca sūtakaṃ nāsti yasya cecchati pārthivaḥ (ī,2, p. 255)  || 3.21 ||

udyato nidhane dāne ārto vipro nimantritaḥ  |
tadaiva ṛṣibhirdṛṣṭaṃ yathā kālena śudhyati  || 3.22 ||

prasave gṛhamedhī tu na kuryātsaṃkaraṃ yadi  |
daśāhācchudhyate mātā tvavagāhya pitā śuciḥ (ī,2, p. 259)  || 3.23 ||

sarveṣāṃ śāvamāśaucaṃ mātā pitrostu sūtakam  |
sūtakaṃ mātureva syādupaspṛśya pitā śuciḥ  || 3.24 ||

yadi patnyāṃ prasūtāyāṃ saṃparkaṃ kurute dvijaḥ  |
sūtakaṃ tu bhavettasya yadi vipraḥ ṣaḍaṅgavit(ī,2, p. 260)  || 3.25 ||

saṃparkājjāyate doṣo nānyo doṣo'sti vai dvije  |
tasmātsarvaprayatnena saṃparkaṃ varjayedbudhaḥ (ī,2, p. 261)  || 3.26 ||

vivāhotsavayajñeṣu tvantarā mṛtasūtake  |
pūrvasaṃkalpitaṃ dravyaṃ dīyamānaṃ na duṣyati (ī,2, p. 262)  || 3.27 ||

antarā daśāhasya punarmaraṇajanmanī  |

ūrdhvocchiṣṭamadhocchiṣṭamantarikṣamṛtau tathā  |
kṛcchratrayaṃ prakurvīta aśaucamaraṇe tathā (īī,2, p. 46)  || 12.62(61) ||

kṛcchraṃ devyayutaṃ caiva prāṇāyāmaśatadvayam  |
puṇyatīrthe'nārdraśiraḥ snānaṃ dvādaśasaṃkhyayā  || 12.63(62) ||

dviyojane tīrthayātrā kṛcchramekaṃ prakalpitam  |
gṛhasthaḥ kāmataḥ kuryādretasaḥ skhalanaṃ bhuvi (īī,2, p. 48)  || 12.64(63) ||

sahasraṃ tu japeddevyāḥ prāṇāyāmaistribhiḥ saha  |
caturvidyopapannastu vidhivadbrahmaghātake (īī,2, p. 50)  || 12.65(64) ||

samudrasetugamanaṃ prāyaścittaṃ vinirdiśet |
setubandhapathe bhikṣāṃ cāturvarṇyātsamācaret || 12.66(65) ||

varjayitvā vikarmasthāṃśchatropānadvivarjitaḥ  |
ahaṃ duṣkṛtakarmā vai mahāpātakakārakaḥ (īī,2, p. 51)  || 12.67(66) ||

gṛhadvāreṣu tiṣṭhāmi bhikṣārthī brahmaghātakaḥ  |
gokuleṣu vaseccaiva grāmeṣu nagareṣu vā  || 12.68(67) ||

tapo vaneṣu tīrtheṣu nadīprasravaṇeṣu vā  |
eteṣu khyāpayannenaḥ puṇyaṃ gatvā tu sāgaram (īī,2, p. 52)  || 12.69(68) ||

daśayojanavistīrṇaṃ śatayojanamāyatam  |
rāmacandrasamādiṣṭa- nalasaṃcayasaṃcitam  || 12.70(69) ||

setuṃ dṛṣṭvā samudrasya brahmahatyāṃ vyapohati  |
setuṃ dṛṣṭvā viśuddhātmā tvavagāheta sāgaram  || 12.71(70) ||

yajeta vāśvamedhena rājā tu pṛthivīpatiḥ  |
punaḥ pratyāgate veśma vāsārthamupasarpati (īī,2, p. 54)  || 12.72(71) ||

saputraḥ saha bhṛtyaiśca kuryādbrāhmaṇabhojanam  |
gāścaivaikaśataṃ dadyāccaturvidhyeṣu dakṣiṇām  || 12.73(72) ||

brāhmaṇānāṃ prasādena brahmahā tu vimucyate  |
savanasthāṃ striyaṃ hatvā brahmahatyāvrataṃ caret(īī,2, p. 76)  || 12.74(73) ||

madyapaśca dvijaḥ kuryānnadīṃ gatvā samudragām  |
cāndrāyaṇe tataścīrṇe kuryādbrāhmaṇabhojanam (īī,2, p. 77)  || 12.75(74) ||

anaḍutsahitāṃ gāṃ ca dadyādvipreṣu dakṣiṇām  |
surāpānaṃ sakṛtkṛtvā agnivarṇaṃ surāṃ pibet(īī,2, p. 80)  || 12.76(75) ||

sa pāvayedathātmānamihaloke paratra ca  |
apahṛtya suvarṇaṃ tu brāhmaṇasya tataḥ svayam (īī,2, p. 84)  || 12.77(76) ||

gacchenmusalamādāya rājābhyāśaṃ vadhāya tu  |
tataḥ śuddhimavāpnoti rājñāsau mukta eva ca  || 12.78(77) ||

kāmatastu kṛtaṃ yatsyānnānyathā vadhamarhati  |
āsanācchayanādyānātsaṃbhāṣātsaha bhojanāt(īī,2, p. 90)  || 12.79(78) ||

saṃkrāmanti hi pāpāni tailabindurivāmbhasi  |
cāndrāyaṇaṃ yāvakaṃ tu tulāpuruṣa eva ca (īī,2, p. 91)  || 12.80(79) ||

gavāṃ caivānugamanaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam  |
etatpārāśaraṃ śāstraṃ ślokānāṃ śatapañcakaṃ  |
dvinavatyā samāyuktaṃ dharmaśāstrasya saṃgrahaḥ (īī,2, p. 287; Bī: 534)  ||
12.[81(80)] ||

yathādhyayanakarmāṇi dharmaśāstramidaṃ tathā  |
adhyetavyaṃ prayatnena niyataṃ svargagāminā (īī,2, p. 288)  || 12.[82](81) ||
