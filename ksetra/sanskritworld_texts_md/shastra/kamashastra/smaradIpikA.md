harakopānalenaiva bhasmībhūyākarotsmaraḥ  |
arddhaṃ nārīśarīraṃ hi yaśca tasmai namo'stu te  || 1 ||
samyagārādhitaḥ kāmaḥ sugandhikusumādibhiḥ  |
vidadhāti varastrīṇāṃ mānagranthivimocanam  || 2 ||
smarannirjitya rudreṇa paścāduddīpitaḥ smaraḥ  |
tena tannāmadheyena nirmitā smaradīpikā  || 3 ||
anekakāmaśāstrāṇāṃ sāramākṛṣya yatnataḥ  |
bālavyutpattaye strīṇāṃ cittasantoṣaṇāya ca  |
prabodhāya varastrīṇāṃ tuṣṭyai ratisukhāya ca  || 4 ||
gargeṇābhāṣitāṃ samyagvakṣyāmi smaradīpikām  |
yasya vijñānamātreṇa mūrkho'pi ratiraṅgadhīḥ  || 5 ||
bālikā taruṇī prauḍhā vṛddhā ceti viśeṣataḥ  |
jñātavyo hyanvitaḥ kāmo dhruvaṃ śṛṅgāramicchatā  || 6 ||
kāmaśāstrasya tattvajñā jāyante sundarīpriyāḥ  |
kāmaśāstramajānanto ramante paśuvatstriyam  || 7 ||
nānānibandhaiḥ suratopacāraiḥ krīḍāsukhaṃ janmaphalaṃ narāṇām  |
kiṃ saurabheyīśatamadhyavartī vṛṣo'pi saṃbhogasukhaṃ na bhuṅkte  || 8 ||
svanārīrakṣaṇaṃ puṃsāṃ paranāryanurañjanam  |
bandhabhedeṅgitajñānametatphalamudāhṛtam  || 9 ||
yena saṃvatsaro dṛṣṭaḥ sakṛtkāmaḥ susevitaḥ  |
tena sarvamidaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ punarāvartitaṃ jagat || 10 ||
prathamaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ puṃsāṃ strīṇāṃ ca tadanantaram  |
dhvajasya lakṣaṇaṃ proktaṃ bhagalakṣaṇasaṃyutam  || 11 ||
kāmasthānāni saṃlakṣya punaḥ samyakpracālanam  |
punaḥ ṣoḍaśa bandhāśca tathaivādhomukhāśca ṣaṭ || 12 ||
dvau bandhau sundarīṇāṃ ca paścānmukharataṃ tathā  |
bāhyaṃ rataṃ tataḥ kuryādrataṃ deśaviśeṣajam  || 13 ||
iṅgitasya parijñānaṃ dūtyāśca tadanantaram  |
tathāṣṭanāyikāyāśca mantrauṣadhisutodayaḥ  || 14 ||
śaśo mṛgo vṛṣaścaiva caturthastu hayastathā  |
kathayāmi kramātpuṃsāmetajjāticatuṣṭayam  || 15 ||
mṛducapalasuśīlaḥ komalāṅgaḥ suveṣaḥ sakalaguṇanidhānaṃ satyavādī śaśo'sau  || 16 ||
vadati madhuravāṇīṃ nṛtyagītānuraktaḥ  |
dvijasuragurubhakto bandhuyukto dhanāḍhyaḥ  || 17 ||
strījito gāyanaścaiva nārīsattvaparaḥ sukhī  |
ṣaḍaṅgulo bhavenmeḍhraḥ śrīmāṃśca śaśako mataḥ  || 18 ||
vadati madhuravāṇīṃ raktanetraḥ suśīlaḥ calamatiratibhīruḥ śīghragāmī mṛgo'sau  || 19 ||
udarakaṭikṛśaḥ syāddīrghabimbādharauṣṭho daśanavadanadīrgho dīrghabāhuḥ pratāpī  || 20 ||
alpabhugdhārmikaścaiva satyavādī priyaṃvadaḥ  |
aṣṭāṅgulo bhavenmeḍhro rūpayukto mṛgo mataḥ  || 21 ||
vṛṣo yathā udarakaṭikṛśāsyaḥ śīghragāmī natāṃsaḥ kanakaruciradehaḥ kaṣṭavādī vṛṣo'sau  || 22 ||
vyasanakṛpaṇabuddhiḥ strīvaśaḥ strīvilāso bahuguṇabahutejāḥ dīrghanetro'bhimānī  || 23 ||
upakāraparo nityaṃ strīvaśī śleṣmalastathā  |
lubdhaśca kṛpaṇaścaiva mithyāvādī ca nirbhayaḥ  |
daśāṅgulaśarīrastu medasvī vṛṣabho mataḥ  || 24 ||
hayo yathā udarakaṭikṛśāsyo dīrghakaṇṭhādharoṣṭhaḥ  |
daśanavadananetraṃ tasya dīrgho'pi nābhiḥ  || 25 ||
lubdhaśca kṛpaṇaścaiva mithyāvādī ca nirbhayaḥ  || 26 ||
dvādaśāṅgulameḍhrastu kuśalo'pi hayo mataḥ  || 27 ||
iti puruṣalakṣaṇāni catvāri  || 28 ||
atha strīṇāṃ jāticatuṣṭayaprakaraṇam  |
padminī citriṇī caiva śaṅkhinī hastinī tathā  |
pratyekaṃ ca varastrīṇāṃ khyātaṃ jāticatuṣṭayam  || 29 ||
padminī yathā bhavati kamalanetrā nāsikādūrdhvarandhrā aviralakucayugmā dīrghakeśā kṛśāṅgī  || 30 ||
mṛdugamanasuśīlā nṛtyagītānuraktā sakalaguṇasuveṣā padminī padmagandhā  || 31 ||
śaśivadanā bimbauṣṭhī tanvī tāmranakhī tathā  |
mandagā lajjitā śyāmā raktaprāntavilocanā  || 32 ||
gāyanī suratāḍhyā ca pārāvatakalasvanā  |
svalpāhārā sukeśī ca padmagandhā ca padminī  || 33 ||
citriṇī yathā bhavati vipulakeśā nātidīrghā na kharvā tilakusumasunāsā snigdhadehotpalākṣī  || 34 ||
kaṭhinaghanakucāḍhyā sundarī bandhaśīlā nikhilaguṇavicitrā citriṇī citraveṣā  || 35 ||
gaurāṅgī tyaktalajjā ca bāhyasaṃbhogasaṃratā  |
uttānaśāyinī coṣṇā pārāvatakalasvanā  || 36 ||
snigdhāṅgī māṃsagandhā ca svalpakāmā kṛśodarī  |
dhūrtā gurunitambā ca citriṇī śrīphalastanī  || 37 ||
dīrghākṛtidīrghasuvarṇanetrā dīrghādharā dīrghanitambabimbā  |
leṣātapālaṅkṛtakaṇṭhadeśā puṃsaikacittā khalu śaṅkhinī syāt || 38 ||
śobhanā komalā coṣṇā dīrghā dīrghaśiroruhā  |
mṛdvaṅgī kṣāragandhā ca nātisthūlā na durbalā  || 39 ||
gaurāṅgī tīkṣṇanāsā ca pīnastanī vicakṣaṇā  |
viśālajaghanā krūrā suratāḍhyā ca śaṅkhinī  || 40 ||
hastinī yathā sthūlākṛtiḥ sthūlapayodharā ca sthūlādharā sthūlanitambabimbā  |
kāmotsukā gāḍharatipriyā ca madhye ca puṣṭā kariṇī matā ca  || 41 ||
sthūlā kaṭhinakucā krūrā nātyuṣṇā nātiśītalā  |
gaurāṅgī kṣīṇanāsā ca pīnastanavilakṣaṇā  || 42 ||
viśālajaghanā krūrā phullanāsātiśītalā  |
kharvā ca kharvanāsā ca bahulomā ca kāmukī  || 43 ||
madagandhatanurnityaṃ mattamātaṅgagāminī  |
lubdhā pīnastanī kruddhā hastinī sā prakīrtitā  || 44 ||
padminī pikavāṇī ca snigdhavāṇī ca citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī krūravāṇī ca meghavāṇī ca hastinī  || 45 ||
padminī pādaśobhā ca keśaśobhā ca citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī mukhaśobhā ca kaṭiśobhā ca hastinī  || 46 ||
padminī padmagandhā ca kṣīragandhā ca citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī mīnagandhā ca madagandhā ca hastinī  || 47 ||
padminī padmanidrā ca dīrghanidrā ca citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī ghoranidrā ca gajanidrā ca hastinī  || 48 ||
padminī padmabandhena nāgabandhena citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī mīnabandhena gajabandhena hastinī  || 49 ||
padminī svalpabhogā ca laghubhogā ca citriṇī  |
śaṅkhinī bahubhogā ca gajabhogā ca hastinī  || 50 ||
śaśakaḥ padminīṃ caiva citriṇīṃ ca mṛgastathā  || 51 ||
śaṅkhinīṃ vṛṣabhaścaiva hastinīṃ tu hayastathā  |
ramate tulyabhāvena tadā samarataṃ bhavet || 52 ||
uccanīcātinīcaṃ ca tathātyuccaṃ ca varjitam  || 53 ||
khyātaṃ samarataṃ caiva tadvirahatyanyathā striyaḥ  || 54 ||
iti strīṇāṃ jāticatuṣṭayam  |
atha dhvajalakṣaṇaprakaraṇam  |
musalaṃ raṅgavīraṃ ca dvividhaṃ dhvajalakṣaṇam  |
sthūlaṃ musalamityāhurdīrghaṃ tadraṅgavīrakam  || 55 ||
nātihrasvaṃ nātidīrghaṃ sthūlaṃ sthūlāntikaṃ varam  |
kṣatraśīrṣe prasannaṃ ca liṅgāni syuḥ śubhāni ṣaṭ || 56 ||
atha bhagalakṣaṇaprakaraṇam  |
kūrmapṛṣṭhā gajaskandhā padmanābhisamā tathā  |
alomā mṛduvistīrṇā ṣaḍete subhagā bhagāḥ  || 57 ||
śītalaṃ coṣṇamatyuṣṇaṃ gojihvāsadṛśaṃ kharam  |
ityuktaṃ kāmaśāstrajñairbhagacihnacatuṣṭayam  || 58 ||
śītalaṃ sukhadaṃ proktamuṣṇaṃ ca madhyamaṃ smṛtam  |
atyuṣṇamasukhaṃ caiva kharaṃ prāṇaharaṃ smṛtam  || 59 ||
atha kāmacālanaprakaraṇam  |
aṅguṣṭhe caraṇe gulphe bhage nābhau kuce hṛdi  |
kakṣe kaṇṭhe'dhare netre kapole ca śrutāvapi  |
śīrṣe sarvaśarīre tu vasetkāmastithikramāt || 60 ||
savye puṃsaḥ striyo vāme kṛṣṇe śukle viparyayaḥ  |
ūrddhvaṃ pratipadādau ca kṛṣṇe cādhaḥ pracakṣate  || 61 ||
evaṃ caiva vasetkāmaḥ sthāne caiva viśeṣataḥ  |
pāde jaṅghe ūrudeśe stane kakṣe gale śrutau  || 62 ||
nakhakṣataṃ pradātavyaṃ bhage nābhau ca mardanam  |
gaṇḍe netre lalāṭe ca cumbanaṃ kāmukairiha  |
hṛdaye tāḍanaṃ hastāddantenādharapīḍanam  || 63 ||
ātmāsyenduśaradvisaptatithayaḥ khyātaṃ nalinyā rate paulastyābdhirasāṣṭabhāskaratithau prītā bhaveccitriṇī  |
rudrānaṅgagaṇeśaśambhutithayaḥ syuḥ śaṅkhinībhuktaye śeṣāḥ syuḥ suratotsaveṣu kariṇāṃ jātāṃ striyaḥ prītaye  || 64 ||
aṅguṣṭhe caraṇe ca gulphanicaye jānudvaye bāstike nābhau vakṣasi kakṣayornigaditā kaṇṭhe kapole'dhare  |
netre karṇayuge lalāṭaphalake dhautaṃ ca vāmabhruvāmūrdhvādhaścalanakrameṇa tithayaścāndrīkalāpakṣaye  || 65 ||
sīmante nayane'dhare ca galake kakṣātaṭe cūcuke nābhau śroṇitaṭe manobhavagṛhe jaṅghātaṭe piṇḍake  |
gulphe pādatale tadaṅgulitale'ṅguṣṭhe ca tiṣṭhatyasau vṛddhakṣīṇasamaṃ tadā śaśikalāpakṣadvaye yoṣitām  || 66 ||
maulau kuntalakarṣaṇaṃ nayanayorācumbanaṃ gaṇḍayoḥ dantenādharapīḍanaṃ hṛdi hatiṃ muṣṭyā ca nābhau śanaiḥ  |
kakṣākaṇṭhakapolamaṇḍalakucaśroṇīṣu deyā nakhāḥ sīmante śikharaṃ nakhairurasijau gṛhṇīta gāḍhaṃ tataḥ  || 67 ||
kurvāṇo viratiṃ manobhavagṛhe mātaṅgalīlāyitaṃ jānvaṅguṣṭhapadorugulphahananaṃ cānyonyataḥ kāminām  |
ityevaṃ gaditaṃ pradeśakalanādindoḥ kalāropaṇaṃ kartavyaṃ ca naraistu strīṣu rabhasāduktaḥ prabodhādhikaḥ  || 68 ||
iti kāmukasaṃghātaiḥ kartavyaṃ kāmacālanam  |
dravatyanena kāmo hi ghṛtabhāṇḍamivāgninā  || 69 ||
āliṅganaṃ cumbanaṃ ca dantakṣatanakhakṣate  |
mardanaṃ nakhacāṭuścādharapānaṃ kucagrahaḥ  || 70 ||
cumbanaṃ saṃpraharaṇaṃ nārīkṣobhaṇameva ca  |
etadbāhyarataṃ kuryātkāmaprakaṭahetave  || 71 ||
adhare gaṇḍayorbhāle kapole galake hṛdi  |
stane ca kakṣayorhaste nābhimūle bhagasthale  || 72 ||
kukṣau jaghanayormūle hṛdayornetrayormukhe  |
kaṭyāṃ ca cumbanaṃ dantakṣataṃ netre tu varjitam  || 73 ||
bhage mardanamāpūrya tato maithunamācaret |
kṛtvā vivastrāṃ kāntāṃ tu meḍhraṃ tatra praveśayet || 74 ||
vakṣasā stanayugmaṃ tu sampīḍya bāhubandhanam  |
anyonyaṃ dṛḍhamākramya kuryādāliṅganaṃ striyaḥ  || 75 ||
rasikena rasajñena satataṃ ratimicchatā  |
gāḍhamāliṅganaṃ deyaṃ tatsakhyā hyanurāgataḥ  || 76 ||
gale vakṣaḥsthale kukṣau kaṇṭhapārśve bhage tathā  |
jaghanorunitambeṣu pṛṣṭhe haste śiraḥsu ca  || 77 ||
kaṇṭhamūle kapole ca nakhakṣatamathācaret || 78 ||
kakṣākaṇṭhakapoleṣu nābhiśroṇikucau tathā  |
bhagaskandhau karṇamūlau trayodaśa nakhālayaḥ  || 79 ||
sthāneṣveteṣu kukṣau ca nakhacāṭuṃ samācaret |
romāñcahetusukhadaṃ madanoddīpanaṃ matam  || 80 ||
itthaṃ ca manmathotpanne keliṃ kuryāttathaiva ca  |
stanayoryugayoścaiva sanābhikabhagasthale  || 81 ||
jaghanorunitambeṣu kāmuko muhurarpayet || 82 ||
bhujayoḥ stanayoścaiva bhage nābhau tathaiva ca  |
ṣaṭsu sthāneṣu vidhinā mardanaṃ parikīrtitam  || 83 ||
kakṣākaṇṭhakapoleṣu nābhau śroṇyāṃ payodhare  |
karṇamūle tathā skandhe cumbanaṃ dvādaśasthale  || 84 ||
gaṇḍauṣṭhaṃ ca tathā vaktraṃ kakṣā nābhirbhagastathā  |
kucau ca bāhumūlaṃ ca dvādaśaitāni cumbanam  || 85 ||
gaṇḍau netre tathā vaktraṃ kakṣā nābhirbhagastathā  |
kucau ca bāhumūlaṃ ca sthānānyetāni cumbane  || 86 ||
adharauṣṭhe ca sampīḍya nāyako nāyikādharam  |
pūrṇacandranibhaṃ peyānnāryāśca sarasaṃ mukham  || 87 ||
payodharauṣṭhajihvāyā dvayoreva kucāgrayoḥ  |
pañcasthānasya vai pānaṃ kuryātkṛśavicakṣaṇaḥ  || 88 ||
oṣṭhena cauṣṭhagrahaṇapūrvakaṃ keśakarṣaṇam  |
śiraḥpradeśe kṛtvā tāmupaviṣṭāṃ ca kārayet || 89 ||
yathā kokilaśabdaḥ syādyathā pārāvatadhvaniḥ  |
yathā haṃsamayūrādeḥ kalarāvaṃ tathā caret || 90 ||
muṣṭāmuṣṭinitambe ca kuryāccaiva parasparam  |
upahastenābalāyāstāḍayejjaghanadvaye  || 91 ||
bhagagarbhasya madhye tu nāḍikā dhvajarūpiṇī  |
pūrṇā kāmajalairnityaṃ vāmasthā mṛdupīvarā  |
marditā cāṅgulībhyāṃ tu janayetkāmajaṃ jalam  || 92 ||
tatra ekaviṃśatiprakāraḥ  |
teṣāṃ nāmāni kākapadaḥ viparītakaḥ nāgarikaḥ ratipāśaḥ keyūraḥ priyatoṣaṇaṃ samapadaḥ ekapadaḥ ūrdhvasaṃpuṭaḥ saṃpuṭakaḥ manmathapriyaḥ ratisundaraḥ urupīḍanaḥ smaracakraṃ nāgapāśakaḥ gaganakaḥ vaṃśadārakaḥ kanakakṣayaḥ nāgaraḥ kuliśanāmakaḥ kāmasundara iti  |
yathākramaṃ teṣāṃ lakṣaṇānyapyāha  |
strīpādau skandhayugmasthau kṣiptvā dhvajaṃ bhagaṃ laghu  |
kāmayetkāmuko nārīṃ bandhaḥ kākapado hi saḥ  || 93 ||
pādamekaṃ kare dhṛtvā dvitīyaṃ skandhasaṃsthitam  |
nārīṃ kāmayate kāmī bandhaḥ syādviparītakaḥ  || 94 ||
ūrumūlopari nyasya yoṣidūruṃ naro yadi  |
grīvāṃ dhṛtvā karābhyāṃ tu bandho nāgariko mataḥ  || 95 ||
pīḍayedūruyugmena kāmukaṃ yadi kāminī  |
ratipāśastadā khyātaḥ kāminīnāṃ manoharaḥ  || 96 ||
strīṇāṃ jaṅghāntarāviṣṭo gāḍhāliṅgya ca sundarīm  |
ramate vihvalaḥ kāmī bandhaḥ keyūrasaṃjñakaḥ  || 97 ||
narīpādau svahastena hṛdaye dhārayedyadi  |
stanārpitakaraḥ kāmī kāmayetpriyatoṣaṇam  || 98 ||
yoṣitpādau hṛdi nyasya karābhyāṃ dhārayetkucau  |
yatheṣṭaṃ tāḍayedyonau bandhaḥ samapadaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 99 ||
pādamekaṃ kare dhṛtvā dvitīyaṃ skandhasaṃsthitam  |
stanārpitakaraḥ kāmī bandhastvekapado hi saḥ  || 100 ||
samprasārya striyāḥ pādau śayyārpitakaphoṇikaḥ  |
bhage liṅgasya saṃyogādramate sampuṭo hi saḥ  || 101 ||
strīpādau saralīkṛtya bhūmau kuñcitajānukaḥ  |
stanalagno rametkāmī bandhaḥ sampuṭako mataḥ  || 102 ||
svajaṅghādvayabāhye ca vā yoṣitpadadvayam  |
stanau dhṛtvā rametkāmī bandhaḥ syānmanmathapriyaḥ  || 103 ||
nārīpādadvayaṃ kāmī dhārayetkucamaṇḍale  |
dhṛtvā kaṇṭhaṃ ramennārīṃ bandho'yaṃ ratisundaraḥ  || 104 ||
striyā ūruyugaṃ dhṛtvā karābhyāṃ pīḍayetpunaḥ  |
kuryācca nirbhayāṃ kāmī bandhaḥ syādūrupīḍanaḥ  || 105 ||
dhṛtvā vāmakareṇoruṃ pādaṃ cāpi śiraḥsthitam  |
ramate sudṛḍhaḥ kāmī smaracakraṃ prakīrtitam  || 106 ||
svajānudvayamadhyābhyāṃ hastābhyāṃ dhārayetstriyam  |
ramenniḥśaṅkitaḥ kāmī bandhaḥ syānnāgapāśakaḥ  || 107 ||
samāśliṣya yadā kāntaḥ kāminyā jaghanadvayam  |
ūrdhvagaṃ ramate dorbhyāṃ bandho gaganakaḥ smṛtaḥ  || 108 ||
nārīpādadvayaṃ caiva skandhe yaḥ parivartanam  |
kṛtvā kānto ramedāśu bandho'yaṃ vaṃśadārakaḥ  || 109 ||
mukhaṃ mukhe yadā bāhyaṃ bāhye jaṅghe'pi jaṅghayoḥ  |
vakṣo vakṣaḥsthale dattvā bhavetsa kanakakṣayaḥ  || 110 ||
sundarīṃ ca rametkāntaḥ kaṭyāsaktabhujadvayām  |
hṛdi tatkāntahastāṃ ca bandho'yaṃ nāgaro mataḥ  || 111 ||
strīpādadvayamāliṅgya vimukhākṣiptaliṅgakaḥ  |
yonimāpīḍayetkāmī bandhaḥ kuliśanāmakaḥ  || 112 ||
svajaṅghādvayabāhyena dhārayetkucamaṇḍalam  |
dhṛtvā kaṇṭhaṃ rametkāmī bandhaḥ syātkāmasundaraḥ  || 113 ||
ityādikaṇṭhavinyāsādgāḍhaṃ sampādayetsukham  |
tasmādvidagdhasaṃghātaiḥ kāryā bandhāḥ prayatnataḥ  || 114 ||
striyamānatapūrvāṅgīṃ pādāhitakaradvayām  |
dhṛtodaro rametkāmī bandho'yaṃ paśunāmakaḥ  || 115 ||
