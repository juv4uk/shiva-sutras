puṣkara uvāca
śāntātītaṃ gaṇaṃ hutvā śāntimāpnoti mānavaḥ   |
bhaiṣajyaṃ ca gaṇaṃ hutvā sarvān rogān vyapohati  || 2,127.1 ||
triṣaptīyaṃ gaṇaṃ hutvā sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate  |
kvacinnāapnoti ca bhayaṃ hutvā caivābhayaṃ gaṇam  || 2,127.2 ||
na parājāyate rāma gaṇaṃ hutvāparājitam  |
āyuṣyaṃ ca gaṇaṃ hutvā cāpamṛtyūṃ vyapohati  || 2,127.3 ||

svastimāpnoti sarvatra hutvā svastyayanaṃ gaṇaṃ  |
śreyasā yogamāpnoti śarmavarmagaṇaṃ tathā  || 2,127.4 ||
vāstoṣpatyagaṇaṃ hutvā vāstudoṣān vyapohati  |
tathā raudragaṇaṃ hutvā sarvān doṣān vyapohati ||
etairdaśagaṇairhomo hyaṣṭādaśasu śāntiṣu  || 2,127.5 ||
vaiṣṇavī śāntiraindrī ca brāhmī raudrī tathaiva ca  |
vāyavyā vāruṇī caiva kauberī bhārgavī tathā  || 2,127.6 ||
prājāpatyā tathā tvāṣṭrī kaumārī vahnidevatā  |
mārudgaṇā ca gāndharvī śāntairnairṛtakī tathā  || 2,127.7 ||
śāntirāṅgirasī yāmyā pārthivī ca bhṛgūttama  |

etāstu śāntayaḥ proktāḥ sarvakarmahitāḥ sadā  || 2,127.8 ||
yastvā mṛtyuriti hyetajjaptaṃ mṛtyuvināśanaṃ  |

hutvā ca mātṛnāmāni kāmānevamavāpnuyāt || 2,127.9 ||
suparṇastveti hutvā ca bhujagairnaiva bādhyate  |

yasyedaṃ bhūmyāmiti ca bhūmikāmo japetsadā  || 2,127.10 ||

pṛthivyāmuttamo'sīti hutaṃ śraiṣṭhyapradaṃ sadā  |
audumbaraṃ yo na iti tathā vīryavivardhanam  || 2,127.11 ||
indreṇa dattamityetatsarvabādhāvināśanam  |
hiraṇyavarṇā iti ca snānaṃ pāpaharaṃ bhavet || 2,127.12 ||
asitasyeti sarvāṇi viṣaghnāni bhṛgūttama  |
sarasvatīti mantrāṃśca viṣaghnānnirviśedbudhaḥ  || 2,127.13 ||
śarabhādīni sarvāṇi piśācakṣapaṇāni ca  |

imā devīti mantraśca sarvaśāntikaraḥ paraḥ  || 2,127.14 ||
devā maruta ityetatsarvakāmakaraṃ bhavet |
yamasya lokādityetadduḥsvapnaśamanaṃ paraṃ  || 2,127.15 ||
agnerva iti cāpyetatkathitaṃ manyunāśanam  |
ūrdhvo bhavati vijñeyaḥ kṛtvā sthānakaraḥ paraḥ  || 2,127.16 ||
indraṃ vayaṃ vaṇijamiti paṇyalābhakaraṃ param  |
[Bhattacharyya̱ baṇijaṃ]
kāmo me rājña ityetatstrīṇāṃ saubhāgyavardhanam  || 2,127.17 ||
bhadrāya karṇamityetatkarṇaprasyandane japet |
bhadrāsyakṣirodhe jātā karṇaprasyandane tathā  || 2,127.18 ||
tubyameva jarimannāyuṣyaṃ tu hutaṃ bhavet |
āyātu mitra ityetanmitralabdhikaraṃ hutam  || 2,127.19 ||
āśāsānamidaṃ janā miśradhānyena homayet |
ādhipatyamavāpnoti sarvatra manujottama  || 2,127.20 ||
agne gobhirna ityetadgavāṃ vṛddhikaraṃ param  |
dvādaśāhaṃ tu juhuyātparākeṇa viśeṣataḥ  || 2,127.21 ||

śānto agniritītyetadgrāmasvāsthyakaraṃ bhavet |
tasmādgrāmānmṛdā kṛtvā vedisāmyaṃ samaśnute  || 2,127.22 ||
vidmāsya sarvā cittāni rāṣṭrasvīkaraṇāni tu  |
tribhyo rudrebhya ityetadvāstoṣpatamudāhṛtam  || 2,127.23 ||
dhruvaṃ dhruveṇeti hutaṃ sthānalābhakaraṃ bhavet |
acyutā dyaustathā rāma kathitaṃ sthānalābhadam  || 2,127.24 ||
payo deveṣviti hutaṃ rāyaspoṣakaraṃ param  |
yunakrasīreti śunā kṛṣilābhakaraṃ bhavet || 2,127.25 ||

ayaṃ te yonirityetatputralābhakaraṃ bhavet |
śunaṃ vatsān tathā hyetadgavāṃ vṛddhikaraṃ hutam  || 2,127.26 ||
... meti kathitaṃ sarvatśraiṣṭhyakārakam  |

ahaṃ te bhaga ityetadbhavetsaubhāgyavardhanaṃ  || 2,127.27 ||
ye te pāśāstathāpyetadbandhanānmokṣakārakam  |
yo na sva iti cāpyetacchatrunāśakaraṃ param  || 2,127.28 ||
sapatnahanamiti tathā nātra kāryā vicāraṇā  |
tvamuttamamitītyetadyaśaso vardhanaṃ param  || 2,127.29 ||
yathā mṛgo gopayasi strīṇāṃ saubhāgyavardhanam  |
ā no agna ityetacca kathitaṃ patilābhadam  || 2,127.30 ||
yena vehadiṣaṃ caiva garbhalābhakaraṃ bhavet |
imaṃ tapasvinniti tathā bhavetsaubhāgyavardhanam  || 2,127.31 ||
yatpṛthivyā anāvṛttaṃ hutametadyathāvidhi  |
[Bhattacharyya typo̱ pṛvivyā]
kṛtvā tu śaṃsanaṃ jñeyaṃ nātra kāryā vicāraṇā  || 2,127.32 ||
......  |
śivaḥ śivābhirityetadbhavecchreyaskaraṃ param  || 2,127.33 ||
kṛtyādūṣaṇa ityetatkṛtyādūṣaṇamucyate  |
bṛhaspatiḥ paripātu pathi svastyayanaṃ bhavet || 2,127.34 ||
mano nvedamabhayadaṃ pathi svastyayanaṃ bhavet |
ayaṃ no agniradhyakṣo bhavedagniprasādataḥ  || 2,127.35 ||
saṃvatsaraṃ tu śirasā dhārayedyo hutāśanam ||
[Bhattacharyya̱ hūtāśanam]
mantrametajjapennityamāgneyāśāmukhaḥ sthitaḥ ||
anagnijvalanaṃ kuryādrāma saṃvatsare gate  || 2,127.36 ||
dūṣyā dūṣīttyetajjapenmantramanuttamam  |
kuryātpratisarābandhaṃ sarvadoṣanibarhaṇam  || 2,127.37 ||
prāṇasūktañca kathitaṃ tathā prāṇavivardhanam  |
muñcāmi tveti kathitamapamṛtyuvimokṣaṇam  || 2,127.38 ||
atharvaśiraso'dhyetā sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate  |
paramaṃ pāvanaṃ taddhi sarvakalmaṣanāśanam  || 2,127.39 ||
evamete śubhā mantrāḥ prativedaṃ mayā tava  |
samāsātkathitā rāma samuddhṛtya pradhānataḥ  || 2,127.40 ||
ekaikasya ca mantrasya viniyogāḥ sahasraśaḥ  |
[Bhattacharyya̱ viniyogaḥ]
kathitā bhṛguśārdūla purāṇaiḥ sumahātmabhiḥ  || 2,127.41 ||
na te śakyā mahābhāga vaktuṃ varṣaśatairapi  |
prādhānyena tu mantrāṇāṃ kiñcitkarma taveritam  || 2,127.42 ||
home yatra na nirdiṣṭaṃ mayā dravyaṃ purā tava  |
havīṃṣi tatra vakṣyāmi tāni me gadataḥ śṛṇu  || 2,127.43 ||
vṛkṣāṇāṃ yajñiyānān tu samidhaḥ prathamaṃ haviḥ  |
ājyañca vrīhayaścaiva tathā vai gaurasarṣapāḥ  || 2,127.44 ||
akṣatāni tilāścaiva dadhi kṣīrañca bhārgava  |

darbhāstathaiva dūrvāśca bilvāni kamalāni ca  || 2,127.45 ||
śāntipuṣṭikarāṇyāhurdravyāṇyetāni bhārgava  |
tailendhanāni dharmajña rājikāṃ rudhiraṃ viṣam  |
samidhaḥ kaṇṭakopetā abhicāreṣu yojayet || 2,127.46 ||
ārṣaṃ vai daivataṃ chandastvavijñāya bhṛgūttama  |
mantrasya tena mantreṇa japyahomau na kārayet || 2,127.47 ||

chandasi brāhmaṇe sūkte yadavyaktaṃ pradṛśyate  |
vidvadbhiḥ saha niścitya tadyajñamavatārayet || 2,127.48 ||
sambhārā ye yathā yatra yāni dravyāṇi yo vidhiḥ  |
śākhāṃ prati tathā tatra tatpramāṇamiti sthitiḥ  || 2,127.49 ||
yaḥ svasūtramatikramya parasūtreṇa vartate  |
apramāṇamṛṣiṃ kṛtvā so'pyadharmeṇa yujyate  || 2,127.50 ||
tasmātsarvaprayatnena svasūtraṃ na vilaṅghayet |
prāktantrottaratantre dve svaśākhāpratyaye dvija  |
sarvakarmasu kartavye madhye karma vidhīyate  || 2,127.51 ||
ātharvaṇo'yaṃ kathito vidhiste saṃkṣepato bhārgavāṃśamukhya  |
ataḥ paraṃ kiṃ kathayāmi tubhyaṃ tanme vadasvāyatalohitākṣa  || 2,127.52 ||
