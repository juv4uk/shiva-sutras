śrīmaitrāyaṇīyamānavagṛhyasūtrabhāṣyam |


prathamaḥ puruṣaḥ |




prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

atha mānavagṛhyasūtraprārambhaḥ |
oṃ upanayanaprabhṛti vratacārī syāt || 1,1.1 ||

mārgavāsāḥ saṃhatakeśo bhākṣācāryavṛttiḥ saśalkadaṇḍaḥ sapta |
muñjāṃ mekhalāṃ dhārayedācāryasyāpratikūlaḥ sarvakārī || 1,1.2 ||

yadenamupeyāttadasmai dadyādbahūnāṃ yena saṃyuktaḥ || 1,1.3 ||

nāsya śayyāmāviśet || 1,1.4 ||

na savasrayet || 1,1.5 ||

na rathamārohet || 1,1.6 ||

nānṛtaṃ vadet || 1,1.7 ||

na muṣitāṃstriyaṃ prekṣeta || 1,1.8 ||

na vihārārtho jalpet || 1,1.9 ||

na rucyarthaṃ kiṃcana dhārayīta || 1,1.10 ||

sarvāṇi sāṃ sparśikāni strībhyo varjjayet || 1,1.11 ||

na madhumāṃse prāśnīyātkṣāralavaṇe ca || 1,1.12 ||

na snāyādudakaṃ vā bhyāveyāt || 1,1.13 ||

yadi snāyāddaṇḍa ivāpsu plaveta || 1,1.14 ||

prāgastamayānniṣkramya samidhāvahārerddhariṇyau brahmavarcasakāma iti śrutiḥ || 1,1.15 ||

imaṃ stomamarhata ityagniṃ parisamuhya paryukṣya paristīryaidhosyedhiṣīmahīti samidhamādadhāti, samidasi samedhiṣīmahīti dvitīyām || 1,1.16 ||

apo adyānvacāriṣamityupatiṣṭhate || 1,1. 17 ||

yadagre tapasā tapo brahmacaryamupeyamasi |
priyāḥ śrutasya bhūyāsmāyuṣmantaḥ sumedhasa iti mukhaṃ vimṛṣṭe || 1,1.18 ||

bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devā iti śrotre abhimṛśati || 1,1.19 ||

bhadraṃ paśyemākṣābhiryajatrā iti cakṣuṣī || 1,1.20 ||

sthirai raṅgai stuṣṭuvāṃ sastanūbhirvyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyu rityaṅgāni || 1,1.21 ||

iha dhṛtiriha svadhṛtiriti hṛdayadeśamārabhya japati || 1,1.22 ||

rucaṃ no dhehīti pṛthivīmārabhate || 1,1.23 ||

tryāyuṣaṃ jamadagneḥ kaśyapasya tryāyuṣamagastyasya tryāyuṣam |
yaddevānāṃ tryāyuṣaṃ tanme astu tryāyuṣamiti bhasmānāṅgāni saṃspṛśyāpohiṣṭhīyābhirmārjāyate || 1,1.24 ||

iti prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ||1||

dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

oṃ atha saṃdhyāmupāste || 1,2.1 ||

prāgastamayānniṣkramyottarato grāmasya purastādvā śucau deśe niṣedyopaspṛśyāpāmañjaliṃpūrayitvā pradakṣiṇamāvṛtya āyāhiviraje devyakṣare brahmasaṃmite |
gāyatrīṃ chandasāṃ mātaridaṃ brahma juṣasva me' ityāvāhayati || 1,2.2 ||

ādyojo'sīti japitvā kaste yunaktīti yojayitvoṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svastatsaviturityaṣṭau kṛtvaḥ prayuṅkta ityāmnātāḥ kāmā ādevo yātīti triṣṭubhaṃ rājavanyasya yuñjata iti jagatiṃ vaiśamya || 1,2.3 ||

udutyaṃ jātavedasamiti dve nigadya kaste vimuñjatīti vimucyodakāñjalimutsṛjati || 1,2.4 ||

evaṃ prātastiṣṭhan || 1,2.5 ||

etena dharmeṇa dvādaśacaturviṃśatiṃ ṣaṭtriśatamaṣṭācatvāriṃ śataṃ vā varṣāṇi yo brāhmaṇo rājanyo vaiśyo vā drahmacaryaṃ carati muṇḍaḥ śikhājaṭaḥ sarvajaṭo vā malajñurabalaḥ kṛśaḥ snātvā sa sarvaṃ vindate yatkiṃcinmanasecchatīti || 1,2.6 ||

etena dharmeṇa sādhvadhīte || 1,2.7 ||

chandasyarthānbudhvā snīsyan gāṃ kārayet || 1,2.8 ||

ācāryamarhayocchrotriyaḥ || 1,2.9 ||

anyo vedapāṭhī na tasya snānam || 1,2.10 ||

āpo hiṣṭheti tisṛbhirhiraṇyavarṇāḥ śucaya iti dvābhyāṃ snātvāhate vāsasī paridhatte || 1,2.11 ||

vasvasi vasumantaṃ mā kuru sauvarcasāya mā tejase brahmavarcasāya paridadhāmīti paridadhāti || 1,2.12 ||

yathā dyauśca pṛthivī ca na bibhīto na riṣyataḥ |
evaṃ me prāṇa mā riṣa ityāṅkte || 1,2.13 ||

hiraṇyamāvadhnīte || 1,2.1 ||

chatraṃ dhārayate daṇḍaṃ mālāṃ gandham || 1,2.15 ||

pratiṣṭhe sthodaivate dyāvāpṛthivī mā mā saṃtāptamityupānahau || 1,2.16 ||

dvivastro'ta urdhvaṃ bhavati tasmācchobhanaṃ vāso bhartavyamiti śrutiḥ || 1,2.17 ||

āmantryagurūn gurubandhūṃśca svān gṛhānvrajet || 1,2.18 ||

pratiṣiddhamaparayā dvārā niṣkramaṇaṃ malavadvāsasā saha saṃvastraṇaṃ rajaḥ sunāsinyā saha śayyā gurorduruktavacanamasthāne śayanaṃ smayanaṃ saraṇaṃ sthānaṃ yānaṃ gānaṃ tasya cekṣaṇam || 1,2.19 ||

paurṇamāsyāmamāvāsyāyāṃ vāgneyena paśunā yajeta || 1,2.20 ||

tasya pavirbhakṣayitvā yathāsukhamata ūrdhvaṃ madhumāṃse prāśnīyātkṣāralavaṇe ca || 1,2.21 ||

iti dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ||

tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

yamevaṃ vidvāṃsamabhyudiyādvābhyasta miyādvā pratibudhya japet

punarmā maitvindriyaṃ punarāyuḥ punarbhagaḥ

punardraviṇā maitu māṃ punarvāhyaṇa maitu mām

athoyatheme dhiṣṇyāso agnayo yathāsthānaṃ

kalpayantā mihaivetyabhyuditaḥ || 1,3.1 ||

punarmātmā punarāyuraitu punaḥ prāṇaḥ punarākūtiraitu vaiśvānaro vāvṛdhāno vareṇāntastiṣṭhato me mano amṛtasya ketuḥ ityabhyasta mitaḥ || 1,3.2 ||

ubhāveva vābhyudito japedubhāveva vābhyastamitaḥ || 1,3.3 ||

yadyācaraṇīyānvācaredanākrośedabhojyasyavānna maśnīyādakṣi vāspandetkarṇo vākrośedagniṃ vā citimārohetsmaśānaṃ vā gacchedyupaṃ vospṛśedretaso vā skandedetābhyāmeva mantrābhyāmāhutirjuhuyādapi vājyalipte samidhe vādadhyādapi vā mantrāveva japet || 1,3.4 ||

evamadharmamācaryāsthūlam || 1,3.5 ||

sthūle veṣaṇayā viharedavastro lomatvagācchādo'gnimārohetsaṃgrāme vā dhātayedapi vāgnimindhānaṃ tapasā'tmānamupayojayīta || 1,3.6 ||

iti tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

caturthaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

varṣāsu śravaṇena svādhyāyānupākurute || 1,4.1 ||

sa juheti

apvānāmāsi tasyāstejoṣṭraṃ gamayem |
ahamiddhi pituḥ parimedhāmṛtasya jagrabha sūrya ivājani svāhā |

sarasvatīnāmāsi sarasvannāmāsi |
yuktirnāmāsi yojo nāmāsi |
matirnāmāsi mane nāmāsi ||
tasyāstejoṣṭrīṃ gameyam |
tasya tejoṣṭraṃ gamayemiti sarvatrānuṣajati || 1,4.2 ||

yuje svāhā prayuje svāhodyujesvāhetyetairattevāsināṃ yogamicchanniti || 1,4.3 ||

prāksviṣṭakṛtotha japati |
ṛtaṃ vadiṣyāmi satyaṃ vadiṣyāmi tanmāmavatu tadvaktāramavatvavatu mā bhavatu vaktāram |
vāṅme manasi pratiṣṭhinā, mano me vaici pratiṣṭhitamāvirāyurmayi dhehi vedasya vāṇīḥ stha |
oṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svastatsavituriti || 1,4.4 ||

darbhapāṇistriḥ sāvitrī madhīte |
triṃścādito'nuvākān |
ko vā yunaktīti ca |
upākurmahe'dhyāyānupatiṣṭhantu chandāsīti ca || 1,4.5 ||

tasyānadhyāyāḥ samūhanvāto valīkakṣāraprabhṛti varṣaṃ na vidyotamāne na stanayatīti śrutirākālikaṃ devatumulaṃ vidyuddhanvolkātyakṣarāḥ śabdāḥ |
ācāreṇānye || 1,4.6 ||

arddhapañcamānmāsānadhītyotsṛjati pañcārddhaṣaṣṭhānvā || 1,4.7 ||

atha japati ṛta mavādiṣaṃ tanmāvītttadvaktāramāvīdāvīnmāmāvīdvaktāram |
vādhme manasi pratiṣṭhitā mano me vāci pratiṣṭhitaṃ māvirāyurmayi dhehi |
vedasya vāṇīḥ stha |
oṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svastatsavituriti || 1,4.8 ||

darbhapāṇistriḥ sāvitrīmadhīte |
trīṃścādito'muvākān ko vo vimuñcatīti vimucyotsṛjāmahedhyāyānpratīśvasantu chandāsīti ca || 1,4.9 ||

pratipadaṃ pakṣiṇīṃ rātrīṃ nādhīyīta nāta ūrdhvamabhreṣu || 1,4.10 ||

ākāliko vidyutsnanayitnuvarṣeṣu || 1,4.11 ||

gonāmeṣu mantrabrāhmaṇakalpayitṛmedhamahāvratāṣṭāpadīṃ vaiṣuvatāni divādhīyīta vaiṣuvattamārdrapāṇiḥ || 1,4.12 ||

rudānna naktaṃ na bhuktā na grāme || 1,4.13 ||

śukriyasya pravargyakalpe niyamo vyākhyātastrayoviṃśaṃ tu saṃmīlya || 1,4.14 ||

gavāṃ tu na sakāśe gonāmāni gārbhiṇīnāmasakāśe'ṣṭāpadīṃreto mūtramiti ca || 1,4.15 ||

śunīśīryasya ca saurye cakṣuṣkāmasya cakṣurno dhehi cakṣuṣa iti sūryo'po'vagāhata iti ca ādityasairyayāmyāni ṣaḍṛcāni divādhīyīta || 1,4.16 ||

upākṛtyotsṛjya ca tryahaṃ pañcarātrameke || 1,4.17 ||

vedārambhaṇe samāptau cākālam || 1,4.18 ||

iti caturthaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

pañcamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

athāto'ntarakalpaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ || 1,5.1 ||

darbhamayaṃ vāsaḥ paridhāyacamyāpāṃ naptra iti tīre japitvāpo'vagāhya oṃbhūrbhuvaḥ svastatsavituriti || 1,5.2 ||

darbhapāṇistriḥ sāvitrīmadhīte trīṃ śvādito'nuvākān || 1,5.3 ||

āpo devīrhaviṣmatīrimā nigrābhyo stha mahitrīṇāmavo'stu agnerarāyurasi devī rāpo apāṃ napāddevīrāpo madhumatīragnaye svāhā rātrīṃ rātrīmityaṣṭau || 1,5.4 ||

yā oṣadhayaḥ samānyāyanti punantu mā pitaro'gnermanve saśevṛdhamadhidhāḥ kayā naścitra ābhuvadūtīti tisraḥ || 1,5.5 ||

tacchaṃyorā vṛṇīmaha iti mārjayitvā vāsāṃsyutsṛjāyācāryānpitṛdharmeṇa tarpayanti || 1,5.6 ||

śrāddhakalpena śeṣo vyākhyātaḥ || 1,5.7 ||

iti pañcamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

ṣaṣṭhaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

athātogniṃ pravartayanti || 1,6.1 ||

uttarato grāmasya purastādvā śucau deśe vedyākṛtiṃ kṛtvāhavanīyasthāne saptacchandāṃsi pratiṣṭhāpya viṣṭharā darbhamuṣṭhīnvā dakṣiṇāgnisthāne praugākṛtiṃ kauśitaṃ khātvā paścādutkaramapāṃ pūrayitvā

gārhapatyasthāne'gniṃ praṇīya yuñjānaḥ prathamaṃ mana ityaṣṭau dutvā |
ñakūtamagniṃ prayujaṃ svāheti ṣaḍjuhoti viśvo devasya netu riti saptamīm || 1,6.2 ||

yajñiyānāṃ samidhāṃ trīṃ strīn samitpūlānupakalpya prāksviṣṭakṛtastiṣṭhanto vyāhṛtipūrvakaṃ khaṇḍilasyādītastribhi ranuvākairekaikena svāhākārāntābhirādadhati || 1,6.3 ||

āpodiṣṭīyābhiḥ kausitānmārjayitvā dhānābhirbrābmaṇānsvasti vācayanti dhānābhirbrāhmaṇānsvasti vācayanti || 1,6.4 ||

iti mānave ṣaṣṭhaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ||6||

saptamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

athopaniṣadarhāḥ |
brahmacārī sucariti medhāvī karmakṛddhanadaḥ priyo vidhāṃ vā vidyāyānveṣyan || 1,7.1 ||

tāni tīrthāni brahmaṇaḥ || 1,7.2 ||

bhāryāṃ vindate || 1,7.3 ||

kṛttikā svāti pūrvairiti varayet || 1,7.4 ||

rohiṇīmṛgaśiraḥ śravaṇāśraviṣṭhottarāṇītyupayame tathodvāhe yadvā puṇyoktam || 1,7.5 ||

pañca vivāhakārakāṇi bhavanti vittaṃ rūpaṃ vidyā prajñā bāndhava iti || 1,7.6 ||

ekālābhe vittaṃ visṛjeddvitīyālābhe rūpaṃ tṛtīyālābhe vidyāṃ prajñāyāṃ bāndhava iti ca vivahante || 1,7.7 ||

bandhumatīṃ kanyāmaspṛṣṭamaithunā samānavarṇāsamānapravarāṃ yavīyasīṃ nagnikāṃ śreṣṭhām || 1,7.8 ||

vijñānamasyāḥ kuryādaṣṭau loṣṭhānāharesītā loṣṭhaṃ vediloṣṭhaṃ dūrvāloṣṭhaṃ gomayaloṣṭhaṃ phalavato vṛkṣasyadhastālloṣṭhaṃ śmaśānaloṣṭha madhvaloṣṭhabhirīṇaloṣṭhamiti || 1,7.9 ||

devāgāre sthāpayitvātha kanyāṃ grāhayetyadi smaśānaloṣṭhaṃ gṛhṇīyādadhvaloṣṭhabhiriṇaloṣṭhaṃ vā no payamet || 1,7.10 ||

saṃjuṣṭāṃ dharmeṇopayaccheta brāhmeṇa śaulkena vā || 1,7.11 ||

śatamiti rathaṃ dadyātgomithunaṃ vā || 1,7.12 ||

iti saptamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |

aṣṭamaḥ khaṇḍa

paścādagneścatvāryāsanānyupakalpayīta || 1,8.1 ||

teṣūpaviśanti purastatpratyaṅmukho dātā paścātprāṅmukhaḥ pratigrahītā dāturuttarataḥ pratyaṅmukhī kanyā dakṣiṇata udaṅmukho mantrakāraḥ || 1,8.2 ||

teṣāṃ madhye prāktūlāndarbhānastīryā kāṃsyamakṣatodakena pūrayitvā avidhavāsmai prayacchati || 1,8.3 ||

tatra hiraṇyam || 1,8.4 ||

aṣṭau maṅgalyānyāvedayati || 1,8.5 ||

maṅgalyānyuktā dadāmi pratigṛhṇāmīti trirbrahmadeyā pitā bhrātā vā dadyat || 1,8.6 ||

sahiraṇyānañjalīnāvapati dhanāya tvetidātā putrebhyastveti pratigrahītā tasmai pratyāvapati || 1,8.7 ||

caturvyatihṛtya dadāti || 1,8.8 ||

sāvitreṇa kanyāṃ pratigṛhya prajāpataya iti ca ka idaṃ kasmā adāditi sarvatrānuṣajati kāmaitatta itiyantam || 1,8.9 ||

samānā vā ākūtānīti saha japantyāntādanuvākasya || 1,8.10 ||

khe rathasya khe nasaḥ yugasya śatakrato apālamindrastriḥ pūrtyavakṛṇotsūryatvaca miti tenodakāṃsyena kanyāmabhiṣiñcet || 1,8.11 ||

iti aṣṭamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ||8||

navamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

ṣaḍadhyārhā bhavantyṛtvigācāryo vivāhyo rājā snātakaḥ priyaśceti || 1,9.1 ||

aprākaraṇikānvā parisaṃvatsarādarhayanti || 1,9.2 ||

prākaraṇikāḥ kartāraḥ sadasyāśca vṛtāḥ || 1,9.3 ||

na jīvatpitṛkor'dhyaṃ pratigṛhṇīyāditiśrutirathavā pratigṛhṇīyāt || 1,9.4 ||

athainamarhayanti || 1,9. 5  ||

kāṃsye camase vā dadhi madhu cānīya varṣīyasā pidhāyācamanīyaprathamaiḥ pratipadyante || 1,9.6 ||

virājo dohosi virājo dohosi virājo dohamaśīya mayi dohaḥ padyāyai virājaḥ kalpatāmityekaika māhrimāṇaṃ pratīkṣate || 1,9.7 ||

sāvitreṇa viṣṭaraṃ pratigṛhya ahaṃ varṣma sadṛśānāmudyatāmiva sūryaḥ idaṃ tamabhitiṣṭhāmi yo mā kaścābhidāsatīti japati || 1,9.8 ||

rāṣṭrabhṛdasītyācārya āsandīmanumantrayate || 1,9.9 ||

mā tvā doṣa ityadhastātpādayorviṣṭaramupakarṣati || 1,9.10 ||

viṣṭara āsīnāyai kaikaṃ triḥ prāha || 1,9.11 ||

naiva bho ityāha nama ārṣeyāyeti śrutiḥ spṛśatyarghyam || 1,9.12 ||

pādyena pādau prakṣālya sāvitreṇa madhuparkaṃ pratigṛhya pratiṣṭhāpyavasāyya namo rudrāya pātrasade namo rudrāya pātrasada iti prādeśenādhyadhi pratidiśaṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ

sarvato'bhyuddiśati || 1,9.13 ||

madhuvātā ṛtāyata iti tisṛbhiraṅgulyā pradakṣiṇaṃ pratyṛcaṃ trirāyauti || 1,9.14 ||

amṛtopastaraṇamasītyupastarati || 1,9.15 ||

satyaṃ yaśaḥ śrīrmayi śrīḥśrayatāmiti madhuparkaṃ triḥ prāśnāti || 1,9.16 ||

amṛtāpidhānamasītyācāmati || 1,9.17 ||

suhṛde'vaśiṣṭaṃ prayacchati || 1,9.18 ||

asi pāṇirgāṃ prāha || 1,9.19 ||

hato me pāpmānaṃ me hata oṃkuruta iti preṣyati || 1,9.20 ||

caturo brāhmaṇānnānāgotrān bhojayate || 1,9.21 ||

paśvaṅgaṃ pāyasaṃ vā kārayennāmāṃso madhuparka iti śrutiḥ || 1,9.22 ||

yadyutsṛjenmātā rudrāṇāṃ duhitā vasūnāṃ svasādityānāmamṛtasya nābhiḥ |

pranuvocaṃ cikituṣe janāya māgā manāgā maditiṃ vaṅiṣṭa |
bhurbhuvaḥ svarotmutsṛjatu tṛṇānyattu || 1,9.23 ||

athālaṃkāraṇamalaṃkaraṇamasi sarvasmā alaṃ me bhūyāsam || 1,9.24 ||

praṇāpānau me tarpaya udīnarūpe me tarpaya sucakṣā aha makṣibhyāṃ bhūyāsaṃ suvarcā mukhena suśrutkarṇābhyāṃ bhūyāsāmiti yatāliṅgamaṅgāni saṃspṛśati || 1,9.25 ||

atha gandhotsadane vāsasī || 1,9.26 ||

paridhāsye yaśo dhāsye dīrghāyutvāya jaradaṣṭirastu

śataṃ jīvemaśaradaḥ purūcī rīyaspoṣamabhisaṃvyayiṣye

yaśasā mādyāvā pṛthivī yaśasendrā bṛhaspatī

yaśo bhagaśca māriṣadyaśo mā prati mucyatāmaṃ

ityahataṃvāsaḥ paridhatte || 1,9.27 ||

kumāryāḥ pramadane bhagamaryamaṇaṃ pūṣaṇaṃ tvaṣṭāramiti yajati || 1,9.28 || |

prāksviṣṭakṛtaścatasro avidhavā nandīrupavādayanti || 1,9.29 ||

abhyantare kautuke devapatnīrjayati || 1,9.30 ||
