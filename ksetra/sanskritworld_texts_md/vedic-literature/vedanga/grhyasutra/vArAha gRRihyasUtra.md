vārāhagṛhyasūtra, 1

vārāhagṛhyasūtra, 2
prāṅmukhamudaṅmukhaṃ vā sūtikālayaṃ kalpayitvā dhruvaṃ prapadye śubhaṃ prapadya āśāṃ prapadya iti kāle prapādayet || 2.1
reto mūtramiti cyāvanībhyāṃ dakṣiṇaṃ kukṣimabhimṛśet |
śrāvayedvā || 2.2
putraṃ jātamanvakṣaṃ snātaṃ na mātopahanyādā mantraprayogāt || 2.3
agnerabhyāhitasya parisamūḍhasya paristīrṇasya paścādahate vāsasi kumāraṃ prākśirasamuttānaṃ saṃveśya palāśasya madhyamaṃ parṇaṃ praveṣṭya tenāsya karṇāvājapetbhūstvayi dadhānīti dakṣiṇe |
bhuvastvayi dadhānīti savye |
svastvayi dadhānīti dakṣiṇe |
bhūrbhuvaḥ svastvayi dadhānīti savye || 2.4
athainamabhimantrayete |
aśmā bhava paraśurbhava hiraṇyamastṛtaṃ bhava |
aṅgādaṅgātsambhavasi hṛdayādadhijāyase |
ātmā vai putranāmāsi sa jīva śaradaḥ śatam |
iti || 2.5
yatra śete tadabhimṛśetveda te bhūmi hṛdayaṃ divi candramasi śritam |
vedāmṛtasya devā māhaṃ putryamaghaṃ rudam |
iti || 2.6
ājyaṃ saṃskṛtya brahmāṇamāmantrya samidhamādhāyāghārāvāghāryājyabhāgau hutvā vyāhṛtibhiścatasra ājyāhutīrjuhuyāt |
jayābhyātānānāṃ rāṣṭrabhṛtaścaike || 2.7
kāṃsye camase vāhūtisampātānavanīya tasmin suvarṇaṃ saṃnighṛṣya vyāhṛtibhiḥ kumāraṃ catuḥ prāśayet |
atyantameke suvarṇaprāśanamudake nighṛṣyā dvādaśavarṣatāyāḥ || 2.8
iṣaṃ pinva |
ūrjaṃ pinveti stanau prakṣālya pradhāpayet |
dakṣiṇaṃ pūrvaṃ savyaṃ paścāt || 2.9
sviṣṭakṛte hutvā prāyaścittāhutīśca samidhamādhāya paryukṣati || 2.10
eṣa karmānto bahirdvāre'gnirnityaḥ |
kaṇasarṣapayavānāṃ homaḥ |
vyāhṛtibhirjuhuyāt || 2.11
apratirathaṃ japet |
indro bhūtasyeti ṣaḍarcaṃ ca || 2.12
sūtikālayaṃ yathākālaṃ samantādudakena pariṣiñcet || 2.13


vārāhagṛhyasūtra, 3
evameva daśamyāṃ kṛtvā pitā mātā ca putrasya nāma dadhyatāṃ ghoṣavadādyantarantasthaṃ dīrghābhiniṣṭānāntaṃ kṛtaṃ na taddhitaṃ dvyakṣaraṃ caturakṣaraṃ vā tyaktvā piturnāmadheyātnakṣatradevateṣṭanāmāno vā || 3.1
dvināmā tu brāhmaṇaḥ || 3.2
nāmaiva kanyāyāḥ akāravyavadhānamākārāntamayugmākṣaraṃ nadīnakṣatracandrasūryapūṣadevadattarakṣitāvarjam || 3.3
navanītena pāṇī pralipya somasya tvā dyumnenetyenamabhimṛśet || 3.4
sarveṣu kumārakarmasvāgneyaḥ sthālīpākaḥ prājāpatyo vā || 3.5
sarvatrānādeśe'gniḥ puṃsāmaryamā strīṇām || 3.6
saṃvatsaraṃ mātāpitarau na māṃsamaśnīyātām || 3.7
putrasya jātadante yajetāgniṃ gavā paśunā vā || 3.8
viproṣitaḥ pratyetya putrasya mūrdhānaṃ trirājighretpaśūnāṃ tvā hiṃkāreṇābhijighrāmīti || 3.9
jātakarmavaddhastāṅguliṃ praveṣṭya tenāsya karṇāvājapet || 3.10
athainamabhimantrayate aśmā bhaveti || 3.11
agnidhanvantarī putravratī chāgameṣābhyāmiṣṭvā dīrghāṇāṃ vyāhṛtibhiḥ kumāraṃ catuḥ prāśayet |
āyurdā deveti ca || 3.12
kumārakarmāṇi śukla udagayane puṇye nakṣatre navamīvarjam |
sarva ṛtavo vivāhe'māghacaitrau māsau parihāpyottaraṃ ca naidāgham || 3.13
anvārambhayitvā havanam || 3.14


vārāhagṛhyasūtra, 4
tṛtīyavarṣasya jaṭāḥ kurvanti |
yathā vā kulakalpaḥ || 4.1
agnimupasamādhāya parisamuhya paristīrya paryukṣya dakṣiṇato'gnerbrahmāṇamupaveśyottarata udapātraṃ śamīśamakavat || 4.2
athainamabhimantrayate hiraṇyavarṇāḥ śucaya iti catasṛbhiḥ |
yā oṣadhaya ityanuvākena |
śaṃ no devīrabhiṣṭaya āpo bhavantu pītaye |
śaṃ yorabhisravantu naḥ |
priyāḥ śaṃ na āpo dhanvanyāḥ śaṃ naḥ santu nūpyāḥ |
śaṃ naḥ samudryā āpaḥ śamu naḥ santu kūpyāḥ |
śaṃ no mitraḥ śaṃ varuṇaḥ śaṃ no bhavatvaryamā |
śaṃ na indraścāgniśca śaṃ no viṣṇururukramaḥ |
iti |
tāsāmudakārthān kurvīta paryukṣaṇe'bhyundane snāpane ca || 4.3
ājyaṃ saṃskṛtya brahmāṇamāmantrya samidhamādhāyāghārāvāghāryājyabhāgau hutvā agnā āyūṃṣi pavasa iti saptabhiḥ sapta hutvā || 4.4
āyurdā deveti ca |
ye keśinaḥ prathame sattramāsata yebhirāvṛtaṃ yadidaṃ virājati |
tebhyo juhomyāyuṣe dīrghāyutvāya svastaye |
iti || 4.5
vyāhṛtibhiśca || 4.6
uktaḥ karmāntaḥ pūrveṇa || 4.7
śītena vāya udakenedhi |
uṣṇena vāya udakenedhīti taptā itarābhiḥ saṃsṛjya ārdradānava stha jīvadānavasthondatīriṣamāvadetyapo'bhimantrya dakṣiṇaṃ keśāntamabhyundyāt |
aditiḥ keśān vapatvāpa undantu jīvase |
dīrghāyutvāya svastaye |
iti || 4.8
dakṣiṇasmin keśānte darbhamūrdhvāgraṃ nidadhāti || 4.9
oṣadhe trāyasvainamiti darbhamantardadhāti || 4.10
svadhite mainaṃ hiṃsīriti kṣureṇābhinidadhāti || 4.11
yenāvapatsavitā kṣureṇa somasya rājño varuṇasya vidvān |
tena brahmāṇo vapatedamasyāyuṣmānayaṃ jaradaṣṭiryathāsat |
ahamasau |
iti pravapati || 4.12
dakṣiṇato mātānyā vāvidhavānaḍuhena gomayenābhūmigatān keśān parigṛhṇīyāt || 4.13
mā te keśānanugādvarca etattathā dhātā dadhātu te |
tubhyamindro varuṇo bṛhaspatiḥ savitā varca ādadhuḥ |
iti prapatato'numantrayate || 4.14
tena dharmeṇa punarapo'bhimantryāparaṃ keśāntamabhyundyāduttaraṃ ca || 4.15
anyau tu pravapanau yena pūṣā bṛhaspateragnerindrasya cāyuṣe'vapat |
tena te vapāmyāyuṣe dīrghāyutvāya svastaye |
iti paścāt |
yena bhūyaścaratyayaṃ jyokca paśyasi sūryam |
tena te vapāmyāyuṣe dīrghāyutvāya suślokyāya suvarcase |
ityuttarataḥ || 4.16
yatkṣureṇa parcayatā supeśasā vaptarvapasi keśān |
śundha śiro māsyāyuḥ pramoṣīḥ |
iti lohāyasaṃ kṣuraṃ keśavāpāya prayacchati || 4.17
yathārthaṃ keśayatnān kurvanti dakṣiṇataḥ kapardā vasiṣṭhānāmubhayato'tribhārgavakāśyapānāṃ pañcacūḍā āṅgirasaḥ śikhino'nye || 4.18
vājimeke maṅgalārtham || 4.19
tryāyuṣaṃ kaśyapasya jamadagnestryāyuṣamagastyasya tryāyuṣam |
yaddevānāṃ tryāyuṣaṃ tanme astu śatāyuṣam |
iti śiraḥ saṃmṛśati || 4.20
parigṛhya gomayena keśānuttarapūrvasyāṃ gṛhasya mūṣyāmantarā gehātpaladaṃ ca nidadhyāt |
arikte vā vapane |
uptvāya keśāntān varuṇāya rājño bṛhaspatiḥ savitā viṣṇurindraḥ |
tebhyo nidhānaṃ mahadanvavindannantarā dyāvāpṛthivī apa svaḥ || 4.21
iti |
kartre varaṃ dadāti || 4.22
pakṣmaguṇaṃ tilapiśitaṃ ca keśavāpāya prayacchati || 4.23
saṃvatsaraṃ mātā nāmlāya dhārayet |
roṣāya nāśnīyāt |
lavaṇavarjaṃ tūṣṇīm || 4.24
kanyāyā āhutivarjam || 4.25
viduṣo brāhmaṇānarthasiddhiṃ vācayet || 4.26
evamuttareṣu || 4.27


vārāhagṛhyasūtra, 5
garbhāṣṭameṣu brāhmaṇamupanayet |
ṣaṣṭhe saptame pañcame vā || 5.1
tato garbhaikādaśeṣu kṣatriyaṃ garbhadvādaśeṣu vaiśyam || 5.2
prākṣoḍaśādvarṣādbrāhmaṇasyāpatitā sāvitrī dvāviṃśātkṣatriyasya caturviṃśādvaiśyasya |
ata ūrdhvaṃ patitasāvitrikā bhavanti |
nainān yājayeyurnādhyāpayeyurna vivaheyurna vivāhayeyuḥ || 5.3
abhyantaraṃ jaṭākaraṇaṃ bahirupanayanam || 5.4
ukto'gnisaṃskāro brahmaṇaśca || 5.5
kumāraṃ paryuptinaṃ snātamabhyaktaśirasamupasparśanakalpe nopaspṛṣṭamagnerdakṣiṇato'vasthāpya dadhikrāvṇo akāriṣamiti dadhnaḥ kumāraṃ triḥ prāśayet || 5.6
iyaṃ duruktātparibādhamānā varṇaṃ pavitraṃ punatī na āgāt |
prāṇāpānābhyāṃ balamābhajantī śivā devī subhagā mekhaleyam |
ṛtasya goptrī tapasastarutrī ghnatī rakṣaḥ sahamānā arātīḥ |
sā mā samantamanuparyehi bhadre dhartāraste subhage mekhale mā riṣāma |
iti mauñjīṃ triguṇāṃ triḥ parivītāṃ mekhalāmābadhnīte |
maurvīṃ dhanurjyāṃ kṣatriyasya śāṇīṃ vaiśyasya || 5.7
upavītamasi yajñasya tvopavītenopavyayāmīti yajñopavītam || 5.8
yā akṛtan yā atanvan yā avāyan yā avāharan |
yāścāgnā devīrantānabhito'tanvata tāstvā devīrjarase saṃvyayantām |
āyuṣmānayaṃ paridhatta vāsaḥ paridhatta varcaḥ |
śatāyuṣaṃ kṛṇuhi dīrghamāyuḥ |
śataṃ ca jīva śaradaḥ purūcīrvasūni cāyyo vibhajāya jīyān |
ityahataṃ vāsa ācchādya mitrasya cakṣurdharaṇaṃ balīyastejo yaśasvi sthaviraṃ ca dhṛṣṇu |
anāhanasyaṃ vasanaṃ cariṣṇu parīdaṃ vājyaṃ vājinaṃ dadhe'ham |
iti kṛṣṇājinaṃ ca || 5.9
ājyaṃ saṃskṛtya brahmāṇamāmantrya samidhamādhāyāghārāvāghāryājyabhāgau hutvāṣṭau jaṭākaraṇīyān juhuyāt || 5.10
vyāhṛtibhiśca || 5.11
uktaḥ karmāntaḥ pūrveṇa || 5.12
kālāya vāṃ gotrāya vāṃ jaitrāya vāmaudbhetrāya vāmannādyāya vāmavanenijedityudakenāñjaliṃ pūrayitvā sukṛtāya vāmiti pāṇī prakṣālya idamahaṃ duryamanyā niṣplāvayāmītyācamya niṣṭhīvati || 5.13
bhrātṛvyāṇāṃ sapatnānāmahaṃ bhūyāsamuttama iti dvitīyam || 5.14
prātarjitaṃ bhagamugraṃ huvema vayaṃ putramaditeryo vidhartā |
ādhraścidyaṃ mantamānasturaścidrājā cidyaṃ bhagaṃ bhakṣīmahītyāha |
ityādityamupatiṣṭheta || 5.15
brahmacaryamupāgāmupa mā hvayasveti brūyāt || 5.16
ehi brahmopehi brahma brahma tvā sa brahmasantamupanayāmyahamasāviti || 5.17
athāsyābhivādanīyaṃ nāma gṛhṇāti || 5.18
devasya te savituḥ prasave'śvinorbāhubhyāṃ pūṣṇo hastābhyāṃ hastaṃ gṛhṇāmyahamasāvityasya hastaṃ dakṣiṇena dakṣiṇamuttānamabhīvāṅguṣṭhamabhīva lomāni gṛhṇīyāt || 5.19
māmevānvetu te mano māmevāpi tvamanvihi |
agnau dhṛtamiva dīpyatāṃ hṛdayaṃ tava yanmayi |
ityenaṃ prekṣamāṇaṃ samīkṣate || 5.20
pṛṣṭhato'sya pāṇimanvavahṛtya hṛdayadeśamanvārabhya japetprāṇānāṃ granthirasi sa mā visrasaditi |
brahmaṇo granthirasīti nābhideśam || 5.21
gaṇānāṃ tvā gaṇapatiṃ havāmahe kaviṃ kavīnāmupamaśravastamam |
jyeṣṭharājaṃ brahmāṇaṃ brahmaṇaspata ā naḥ kṛṇvannūtibhiḥ sīda sādanam |
iti pradakṣiṇamagniṃ pariṇayet || 5.22
paścādagnerdarbheṣūpaviśati dakṣiṇataśca brahmacārī || 5.23
adhīhi bho ityupaviśya japati || 5.24
prabhujya dakṣiṇaṃ jānuṃ pāṇī saṃdhāya darbhahastāvoṃ ityuktvā vyāhṛtīḥ sāvitrīṃ cānubrūyāt |
evaṃ kāṇḍānuvacaneṣu || 5.25
tatsaviturvareṇyamiti gāyatrīṃ brāhmaṇāya |
ā devo yātu savitā suratna iti triṣṭubhaṃ kṣatriyāya |
yuñjate mana iti jagatīṃ vaiśyāya |
paccho'rdharcaśaḥ sarvāmantataḥ || 5.26
pālāśaṃ daṇḍaṃ brāhmaṇāya prayacchati naiyagrodhaṃ kṣatriyāyāśvatthaṃ vaiśyāya |
suśravaḥ suśravasaṃ māṃ kuru |
yathā tvaṃ suśravaḥ suśravā asyevamahaṃ suśravaḥ suśravā bhūyāsam |
yathā tvaṃ devānāṃ vedasya nidhigopo'syevamahaṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ brahmaṇo nidhigopo bhūyāsamiti pratigṛhṇāti || 5.27
ūrdhvakapālo brāhmaṇasya kamaṇḍaluḥ parimaṇḍalaḥ kṣatriyasya nicalkalo vaiśyasya |
