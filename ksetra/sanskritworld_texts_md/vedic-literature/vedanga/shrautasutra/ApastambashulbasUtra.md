āpastambaśulbasūtram

vihārayogān vyākhyāsyāmaḥ || 1.1 ||
yāvadāyāmaṃ pramāṇam || 1.2  ||
tadardhamabhyasyāparasmiṃstṛtīye ṣaḍbhāgone lakṣaṇaṃ karoti || 1.2  ||
pṛṣṭhyāntayorantau niyamya lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇāpāyamya nimitaṃ karoti || 1.2  ||
evamuttarato viparyasyetarataḥ sa samādhiḥ  || 1.2  ||
tannimitto nirhrāso vivṛddhirvā  || 1.2  ||
āyāmaṃ vābhyasyāgantucaturthamāyāmaścākṣṇayā rajjustiryaṅbhānīśeṣaḥ | vyākhyātaṃ viharaṇam  || 1.3  ||
dīrghasyākṣṇayārajjuḥ pārśvamānī tiryaṅmānī ca yatpṛthagbhute kurutastadubhayaṃ karoti  || 1.4  ||
tābhirjñeyābhiruktaṃ viharaṇam  || 1.4  ||
caturaśrasyākṣṇayārajjurdvistavatīṃ bhūmiṃ karoti | samasya dvikaraṇī  || 1.5 ||
pramāṇaṃ tṛtīyena vardhayettaccaturthenātmacatustriṃśonena saviśeṣaḥ  || 1.6 ||
athāparam  || 1.7  ||
pramāṇamātrīṃ rajjumubhayataḥ pāśāṃ karoti  || 1.7  ||
madhye lakṣaṇamardhamadhyamayośca pṛṣṭhyāyāṃ rajjumāyamya pāśayorlakṣaṇeṣviti śaṅkūnnihatyupāntyayoḥ pāśau pratimucya madhyamena lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇāpāyamya śaṅkuṃ nimittaṃ karoti | madhyame pāśau pratimucya, uparyupari nimittaṃ madhyamena lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇamaṃsamāyacchet | unmucya pūrvasmādaparasmin pratimucya madhyamenaiva lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇāṃ śroṇimāyacchet | evamuttarau śroṇyaṃsau  || 1.7  ||


athāparoyogaḥ  || 2.1  ||
pṛṣṭhyāntayormadhye ca śaṅkūnnihatyārdhe'rdhe tadviśeṣamabhyasya lakṣaṇaṃ kṛtvārdhamagamayet | antyayoḥ pāśau kṛtvā madhyame saviśeṣaṃ pratimucya pūrvasminnitaraṃ lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇamaṅkamāyacchet | unmucya pūrvasmādaparasmin pratimucya lakṣaṇenaiva dakṣiṇāṃ śroṇimāyacchet | evamuttarau śroṇyaṃsau  || 2.1  ||
pramāṇaṃ tiryagdvikaraṇyāyāmastasyākṣṇayārajjustrikaraṇī  || 2.2 ||
tṛtīyakaraṇyetena vyākhyātā | vibhāgastu navadhā  || 2.3 ||
tulyayoścaturaśrayoruktaḥ samāsaḥ | nānāpramāṇayoścaturaśrayoḥ samāsaḥ  || 2.4  ||
hrasīyasaḥ karaṇyā varṣīyaso vṛddhramullikhet | vṛddhrasyākṣṇayārajjurubhe samasyati | taduktam  || 2.4  ||
caturaśrāccaturaśraṃ nirjihīrṣan yāvannirjihīrṣettasya karaṇyā varṣīyaso vṛddhramullikhet || 2.5  ||
vṛrdhasya pārśvamānīmakṣṇayetaratpārśvamupasaṃharet || 2.5  ||
sā yatra nipatettadapacchindyāt || 2.5  ||
chinnayā nirastam  || 2.5  ||
upasaṃhṛtākṣaṇayārajjuḥ sā catuṣkaraṇī chinnā cetarā ca yatpṛthagbhūte kurutastadubhayaṃ karoti  || 2.6  ||
tiryaṅmānī puruṣaṃ śeṣastrīn  || 2.6  ||
taduktam  || 2.6  ||
dīrghacaturaśraṃ samacaturaśraṃ cikīrṣan tiryaṅmānyāpacchidya śeṣaṃ vibhajyobhayata upadadhyāt || 2.7  ||
khaṇḍamāgantunā saṃpurayet || 2.7  ||
tasya nirhāra uktaḥ  || 2.7  ||


samacaturaśraṃ dīrghacaturaśraṃ cukīrṣanā yāvaccikīrṣettāvatīṃ pārśvamānīṃ kṛtvā yadadhikaṃ syādyathāyogamupadadhyāt || 3.1 ||
caturaśraṃ maṇḍalaṃ cikīrṣanmadhyātkoṭyāṃ nipātayet || 3.2  ||
pārśvataḥ parikṛṣyātiśayatṛtīyena saha maṇḍalaṃ parilikhet || 3.2  ||
sā nityā maṇḍalam  || 3.2  ||
yāvaddhīyate tāvadāgantu  || 3.2  ||
maṇḍalaṃ caturaśraṃ cikīrṣan viṣkambhaṃ pañcadaśa bhāgān kṛtvā dvāvuddharet | trayodaśāvaśiṣyante | sā nityā caturaśram  || 3.3 ||
pramāṇena pramāṇaṃ vidhīyate  || 3.4 ||
caturaśramādeśādanyat || 3.5 ||
dvābhyāṃ catvāri  || 3.6  ||
tribhirnava  || 3.6  ||
yāvatpramāṇā rajjustāvatastāvato vargān karoti  || 3.8  ||
ardhatṛtīyapuruṣā ṣaṭsapādān  || 3.8  ||
athātyanta pradeśaḥ  || 3.9  ||
yāvatā yāvatādhikena parilikhati tatpārśvayorupadadhāti | yacca tena caturaśraṃ kriyate tatkoṭyām  || 3.9  ||
ardhapramāṇena pādapramāṇaṃ vidhīyate  || 3.10  ||
ardhasya dvipramāṇāyāḥ pādapūraṇatvāt || 3.10  ||
tṛtīyena navamī kalā  || 3.10  ||


āgnyādheyike vihāre gārhapatyāhavanīyayorantarāle vijñāyate  || 4.1  ||
aṣṭāsu prakrameṣu brāhmaṇo'gnimādadhīta | ekādaśasu rājanyaḥ | dvādaśasu vaiśyaḥ  || 4.1  ||
caturviṃśatyāmaparimite yāvatā vā cakṣuṣā manyate tasmānnātidūramādheya iti sarveṣāmaviśeṣeṇa śrūyate  || 4.2 ||
dakṣiṇataḥ purastādvitṛtīyadeśe gārhapatyasya nedīyasi dakṣiṇāgnervijñāyate  || 4.3 ||
gārhapatyāhavanīyayorantarālaṃ pañcadhā ṣaḍdhā vā saṃvibhajya ṣaṣṭhaṃ saptamaṃ vā bhāgamāgantumupasamasya samaṃ traidhaṃ vibhajyāparasmiṃstṛtīye lakṣaṇaṃ kṛtvā gārhapatyāhavanīyayorantau niyamya lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇāpāyamya nimittaṃ karoti taddakṣiṇāgnerāyatanam | śrutisāmarthyāt || 4.4 ||
yajamānamātrī prācyaparimitā vā yathāsannāni havīṃṣi saṃbhavedevaṃ tiraścī prāñcau vedyaṃsāvunnayati | pratīcī śroṇī purastādaṃhīyasī paścātprathīyasī madhye saṃnatataraivamiva hi yoṣeti dārśikyā vedervijñāyate  || 4.5 ||
apareṇāhavanīyaṃ yajamānamātrī dīrghaṃ caturaśraṃ vihṛtya tāvatīṃ rajjumāyamya madhye lakṣaṇaṃ kṛtvā dakṣiṇayoḥ śroṇyaṃsayorantarā niyamya lakṣaṇena dakṣiṇāpāyamya nimittaṃ karoti | nimitte rajjuṃ niyamyāntau samasya | dakṣiṇāyāḥ śroṇerdakṣiṇamaṃsamālikhet | evamuttarataḥ | tiryaṅmānīṃ dviguṇāṃ tathā kṛtvā paścātpurastāccopalikhet | vimitāyāṃ purastātpārśvamānyāvupasaṃharet | śrutisāmarthyāt || 4.6 ||


triṃśatpadāni prakramā vā paścāttiraśvī bhavati | ṣaṭtriṃśatprācī caturviṃśatiḥ purastāttiraścīti saumikyā vedervijñāyate  || 5.1 ||
ṣaṭtriṃśikāyāmaṣṭādaśopasamasya aparasmādantāddvādaśasu lakṣaṇaṃ pañcadaśasu lakṣaṇaṃ pṛṣṭhyāntayorantau niyamya pañcadaśakena dakṣiṇāpāyamya śaṅkuṃ nihantyevamuttarataḥśroṇī | viparyasyāṃsau pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamya dvādaśike śaṅkuṃ nihanti | evamuttaratastāvaṃsau  || 5.2  ||
tadekarajjvā viharaṇam  || 5.2  ||
trikacatuṣkayoḥ pañcikākṣṇayārajjuḥ | tābhistrirabhyastābhiraṃsau | caturabhyastābhiḥśroṇī  || 5.3 ||
dvādaśikāpañcikayostrayodaśikākṣṇayārajjuḥ, tābhiraṃsau dvirabhyastābhiḥ śroṇī  || 5.4 ||
pañcadaśikāṣṭikayoḥ saptadaśikākṣṇayārajjuḥ | tābhiḥśroṇī  || 5.5  ||
dvādaśikāpañcatriṃśikayoḥ saptatriṃśikākṣṇayārajjuḥ | tābhiraṃsau  || 5.5  ||
etāvanti jñeyāni vediviharaṇāni bhavanti  || 5.6 ||
aṣṭaviṃśatyonaṃ padasahasraṃ mahāvediḥ  || 5.7  ||
dakṣiṇasmādaṃsāddvādaśasu dakṣiṇasyāṃ śroṇyāṃ nipātayet | chedaṃ viparyasyottarata upadadhyāt | sā dīrghā caturaśrā | tathā yuktāṃ saṃcakṣīta  || 5.7  ||
saumikyā veditṛtīyadeśe yajeteti sautrāmaṇyā vedervijñāyate  || 5.8  ||
prakramāsya dvikaraṇī prakramasthānīyā bhavati trikaraṇyā vā  || 5.8  ||
aṣṭikā daśiketi tiryaṅmānyau | dvādaśikā pṛṣṭhayā  || 5.8  ||
trīṇi caturviṃśāni padaśatāni sautrāmaṇikī vediḥ  || 5.9 ||
dvistāvā vedirbhavatītyaśvamedhe vijñāyate  || 5.10 ||


prakramasya dvikaraṇī prakamasthānīyā bhavati  || 6.1 ||
prakramo dvipadastripado vā | prakrame yāthākāmī śabdārthasya viśayitvāt || 6.2  ||
yajamānasyādhvaryorvā | eṣa hi ceṣṭānāṃ kartā bhavati  || 6.2  ||
rathamātrī nirūḍhapaśubandhasya vedirbhavatīti vijñāyate  || 6.3  ||
tasya khalvāhū rathākṣamātrī paścāttiryagīṣayā prācī | vipathayugena purastāt || 6.3  ||
yāvatā vā bāhye chidre  || 6.3  ||
tadekarajjvoktam | pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamyārdhākṣeṇārdhayugena śroṇyaṃsānnirharet || 6.4 ||
athāpyudāharanti  || 6.5  ||
aṣṭāśītiśatamīṣā tiryagakṣaścatuḥśatam | ṣaḍaśītiyugaṃ cāsya rathaścāraṇa ucyate iti rathaparimāṇam  || 6.5  ||
aratnibhirvā caturbhiḥ paścātṣaḍbhiḥ prācī tribhiḥ purastāt | tadekarajjvoktaṃ pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamya dvābhyāmadhyardheneti śroṇyaṃsānnirharet || 6.6 ||
yajamānamātrī catuḥsraktirbhavatīti paitṛkyā vedervijñāyate, tadekarajjvoktaṃ pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamyārdhena tataḥ śroṇyaṃsānnirharet || 6.7 ||
daśapadottarā vedirbhavatīti some vijñāyate | tadekarajjvoktaṃ pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamyārdhena tataḥ śroṇyaṃsānnirharet || 6.8 ||
tāṃ yugena yajamānasya vā padairvimāya śamyayā parimimīte  || 6.9 ||
pade yuge'ratnāviyati śamyāyāṃ ca mānārtheṣu yathākāmī śabdārthasya viśayitvāt || 6.10 ||
vimitāyāṃ purastātpārśvamānyā upasaṃharet | śrutisāmarthyāt || 6.11 ||


navāratni tiryaksaptaviṃśatirudagāyatamiti sadaso vijñāyate  || 7.1 ||
aṣṭhādaśetyekeṣāṃ  || 7.2 ||
tadekarajjvoktaṃ pañcadaśikenaivāpāyamyārdhapañcamaiḥ śroṇyaṃsānnirharet || 7.3 ||
prādeśamukhāḥ prādeśāntarālā bhavantītyuparavāṇāṃ vijñāyate | aratnimātraṃ caturaśraṃ vihṛtya sraktiṣu śaṅkūnnihatyārdhaprādeśena taṃ taṃ parilikhecchrutisāmarthyāt || 7.4 ||
vyāyāmamātrī bhavatīti gārhapatyacitervijñāyate  || 7.5 ||
caturaśretyekeṣām, parimaṇḍaletyekeṣām  || 7.6 ||
karaṇaṃ vyāyāmasya tṛtīyāyāmaṃ saptamavyāsaṃ kārayet || 7.7 ||
tā ekaviṃśatirbhavanti  || 7.8 ||
prāgāyāmāḥ prathame prastāre'parasminnudagāyāmāḥ  || 7.9 ||
maṇḍalāyāṃ mṛdo dehaṃ kṛtvā madhye śaṅkuṃ nihatyārdhavyāyāmena saha maṇḍalaṃ parilikhet || 7.10  ||
tasmiṃścaturaśramavadadhyādyāvatsambhavettannavadhā vyavalikhya traidhamekaikaṃ pradhikaṃ vibhajet || 7.10  ||
upadhāne caturaśrasyāvāntaradeśān prati sraktīḥ sampādayet || 7.11  ||
madhyānītarasminprastāre  || 7.11  ||
vyatyāsaṃ cinuyādyāvataḥ prastārāṃścikīrṣet || 7.11  ||
piśīlamātrā bhavantīti dhiṣṇyānāṃ vijñāyate  || 7.12 ||
caturaśrā ityekeṣāṃ parimaṇḍalā ityekeṣām  || 7.13 ||
mṛdo dehān kṛtvāgnīdhrīyaṃ navadhā vyavalikhya, ekasyāḥ sthāne'śmānamupapadhyāt || 7.14 ||
yathāsaṅkhyamitarā vyavalikhya yathāyogamupadadhyāt || 7.15 ||


bhavatīva khalu vā eṣa yo'gniṃ cinute iti vijñāyate | vayasāṃ vā eṣa pratimayā cīyata ityākṛticodanāt | pratyakṣavidhānādvā  || 8.1 ||
yāvadāmnānena veṇunā caturaśre ātmani puruṣānavamimote  || 8.2  ||
puruṣaṃ dakṣiṇe pakṣe puruṣaṃ pucche puruṣamuttare  || 8.2  ||
aratninā dakṣiṇato dakṣiṇaṃ pakṣaṃ vardhayati | evamuttarata uttaram  || 8.2  ||
prādeśena vitastyā vā paścātpuccham  || 8.2  ||
ekavidhaḥ prathamo'gnirdvividho dvitīyastrividhastṛtīyaḥ ta evamevodyantyaikaśatavidhāt || 8.3 ||
tadu ha vai saptavidhameva cinvīta | saptavidho vāva prākṛto'gniḥ | tata ūrdhvamekottarāniti vijñāyate  || 8.4 ||
ekavidhaprabhṛtīnāṃ na pakṣapucchāni bhavanti | saptavidhavākyaśeṣatvācchrutivipratiṣedhācca  || 8.5 ||
aṣṭavidhaprabhṛtīnāṃ yadanyatsaptabhyastatsaptadhā vibhajya pratipuruṣamāveśayet || 8.6  ||
ākṛtivikārasyāśrutatvāt || 8.6  ||
puruṣamātreṇa vimimīte' veṇunā vimimīte, iti vijñāyate  || 8.7 ||
yāvān yajamāna ūrdhvabāhustāvadantarāle veṇośchidre karoti madhye tṛtīyam  || 8.8 ||
apareṇa yūpāvaṭadeśamanupṛṣṭhyaṃ veṇuṃ nidhāya chidreṣu śaṅkūnnihatyonmucyāparābhyāṃ dakṣiṇāprākparilikhedāntāt || 8.9 ||
unmucya pūrvasmādaparasmin pratimucya dakṣiṇā pratyakparilikhedāntāt || 8.10 ||


unmucya veṇuṃ madhyame śaṅkāvantyaṃ veṇośchidraṃ pratimucyoparyupari lekhāsamaraṃ dakṣiṇā veṇuṃ nidhāyāntye chidre śaṅkuṃ nihatya tasminmadhyamaṃ veṇośchidraṃ pratimucya lekhāntayoritare pratiṣṭhāpya chidrayoḥ śaṅkū nihanti  || 9.1  ||
sa puruṣaścaturaśraḥ  || 9.1  ||
evaṃ puradakṣiṇaṃ catura ātmani puruṣānavamimīte  || 9.2  ||
puruṣaṃ dakṣiṇe pakṣe | puruṣaṃ pucche puruṣamuttare  || 9.2  ||
aratninā dakṣiṇato dakṣiṇamityuktam  || 9.2  ||
pṛṣṭhyāto vā puruṣamātrasyākṣṇayā veṇuṃ nidhāya pūrvasminnitaram | tābhyāṃ dakṣiṇaṃ aṃsaṃ nirharet | viparyasya śroṇī  || 9.3  ||
pūrvavaduttaramaṃsam  || 9.3  ||
rajjvā vā vimāyottaravedinyāyena veṇunā vimimīte  || 9.4 ||
sapakṣapuccheṣu vidhābhyāse'pacaye ca vidhāsaptamakaraṇīṃ puruṣasthānīyāṃ kṛtvā viharet || 9.5 ||
karaṇānāṣṭakānāṃ puruṣasya pañcamena kārayet || 9.6  ||
tāsāmevaikato'dhyardhāstaddvitīyam | puruṣasya pañcamo bhāga ekataḥ prādeśa ekataḥ tattṛtīyam  || 9.6  ||
sarvataḥ prādeśastaccaturtham | samacaturaśrāḥ pañcadaśabhāgīyāstatpañcamam  || 9.6  ||
ūrvdhvapramāṇamiṣṭakānāṃ jānoḥ jānoḥ pañcamena kārayet || 9.7  ||
ardhena nākasadāṃ pañcacūḍānāṃ ca  || 9.7  ||
yatpacyamānānāṃ pratihrasīta purīṣeṇa tatsampūrayedaniyataparimāṇatvātpurīṣasya  || 9.8 ||


upadhāne'dhyardhā daśa purastātpratīcīrātmanyupadadhāti | daśa paścātprācīḥ  || 10.1  ||
pañcapañca pakṣāgrayoḥ | pakṣāpyayayośca viśayāstāsāmardheṣṭakāmātrāṇi pakṣayorbhavanti  || 10.1  ||
pañcapañca pucchapārśvayordakṣiṇā udīcīśca  || 10.1  ||
pucche prādeśamupadhāya sarvamagniṃ pañcamabhāgīyābhiḥ pracchādayet || 10.2 ||
pañcadaśabhāgīyābhiḥ saṅkhyāṃ pūrayet || 10.3 ||
aparasmin prastāre'dhyardhā daśa dakṣiṇata udīcīrātmanyupadadhāti | daśottarato dakṣiṇāḥ  || 10.4  ||
yathā prathame prastāre pakṣau tathā puccham | yathā pucchaṃ tathā pakṣau viparītā apyaye  || 10.4  ||
sarvamagniṃ pañcamabhāgīyābhiḥ pracchādayet || 10.5 ||
pañcadaśabhāgīyābiḥ saṅkhyāṃ pūrayet || 10.6  ||
vyatyāsaṃ cinuyādyāvataḥ prastārāṃścikīrṣet || 10.6  ||
pañca citayo bhavanti | pañcabhiḥ purīṣairabhyūhatīti purīṣāntā citirarthāntaratvātpurīṣasya  || 10.7 ||
jānudaghnīṃ sāhasraṃ cinvīta prathamaṃ cinvānaḥ  || 10.8  ||
nābhidaghnīṃ dviṣāhasraṃ dvitīyamāsyadaghnīṃ triṣāhasraṃ tṛtīyamuttaramuttaraṃ jyāyāmsam  || 10.8  ||
mahāntaṃ bṛhantamaparimitaṃ svargakāmaścinvīteti vijñāyate  || 10.8  ||
dviṣāhasre dviprastārāścitayo bhavanti | triṣāhasra triprastārāścaturthaprabhṛtiṣvāhāreṣu nityamiṣṭakāparimāṇam  || 10.9 ||
vijñāyate ca ḥna jyāyāṃsaṃ citvā kanīyāṃsaṃ cinvīteti'  || 10.10 ||


caturaśrābhiragniṃ cinuta iti vijñāyata | samacaturaśrā anupapadatvācchabdasya  || 11.1 ||
pādamātrayo bhavanti aratnimātrayo bhavantyūrvasthimātrayo bhavantyaṇūkamātrayo bhavantīti vijñāyate  || 11.2 ||
caturbhāgīyamaṇūkam | pañcamabhāgīyāratniḥ | tathorvasthi  || 11.3  ||
