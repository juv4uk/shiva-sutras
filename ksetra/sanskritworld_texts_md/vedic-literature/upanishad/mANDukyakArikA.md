bahiḥprajño vibhuḥ viśvo hyantaḥprajñastu taijasaḥ |
ghanaprajñaḥ tathā prājña eka eva tridhā smṛtaḥ || K_1.1 ||

dakṣiṇākṣimukhe viśvo manasyantastu taijasaḥ |
ākāśe ca hṛdi prājñastridhā dehe vyavasthitaḥ || K_1.2 ||
viśvaḥ hi sthūlabhuṅnityaṃ taijasaḥ praviviktabhuk |
ānandabhuktathā prājñastridhā bhogaṃ nibodhata || K_1.3 ||

sthūlaṃ tarpayate viśvaṃ praviviktaṃ tu taijasam |
ānandaśca tathā prājñaṃ tridhā tṛptiṃ nibodhata || K_1.4 ||

triṣu dhāmasu yadbhojyaṃ bhoktā yaśca prakīrtitaḥ |
vadaitadubhayaṃ yastu sa bhuñjāno na lipyate || K_1.5 ||

prabhavaḥ sarvabhāvānāṃ satāmiti viniścayaḥ |
sarvaṃ janayati prāṇaśceto'ṃśūn puruṣaḥ pṛthak || K_1.6 ||

vibhūtiṃ prasavaṃ tvanye manyante sṛṣṭicintakāḥ |
svapnamāyāsarūpeti sṛṣṭiranyaiḥ vikalpitā || K_1.7 ||

icchāmātraṃ prabhoḥ sṛṣṭiriti sṛṣṭau viniścitāḥ |
kālātprasūtiṃ bhūtānāṃ manyante kālacintakāḥ || K_1.8 ||

bhogārthaṃ sṛṣṭiriti anye krīḍārthamiti cāpare |
devasyaiṣa svabhāvo'yamāptakāmasya kā spṛhā || K_1.9 ||

nivṛtteḥ sarvaduḥkhānāmīśānaḥ prabhuravyayaḥ |
advaitaḥ sarvabhāvānāṃ devāsturyo vibhuḥ smṛtaḥ || K_1.10 ||

kāryakāraṇabaddhau tāviṣyete viśvataijasau |
prājñaḥ kāraṇabaddhastu dvau tau turye na sidhyataḥ || K_1.11 ||

nātmānaṃ na parāścaiva na satyaṃ na api cānṛtam |
prājñaḥ kiñcana saṃvetti turyaṃ tatsarvadṛksadā || K_1.12 ||

dvaitasyāgrahaṇaṃ tulyamubhayoḥ prājñaturyayoḥ |
bījanidrāyutaḥ prājñaḥ sā ca turye na vidyate || K_1.13 ||

svapnanidrāyutāvādyau prājñastvasvapnanidrayā |
na nidrāṃ naiva ca svapnaṃ turye paśyanti niścitaḥ || K_1.14 ||

anyathā gṛhṇataḥ svapno nidrā tattvamajānataḥ |
viparyāse tayoḥ kṣīṇe turīyaṃ padamaśnute || K_1.15 ||
anādimāyayā supto yadā jīvaḥ prabudhyate |
ajamanidramasvapnamadvaitaṃ budhyate tadā || K_1.16 ||

prapañco yadi vidyeta nivarteta na saṃśayaḥ |
māyāmātramidaṃ dvaitamadvaitaṃ paramārthataḥ || K_1.17 ||

vikalpo vinivarteta kalpito yadi kenacit |
upadeśādayaṃ vādo jñāte dvaitaṃ na vidyate || K_1.18 ||

viśvasyātvavivakṣāyāmādisāmānyamutkṛṣṭam |
mātrāsaṃpratipattau syādāptisāmānyameva ca || K_1.19 ||

taijasasyotvavijñāna utkarṣo dṛśyate sphuṭam |
mātrāsaṃpratipattau syādubhayatvaṃ tathā vidham || K_1.20 ||

makārabhāve prājñasya mānasāmanyamutkaṭam |
mātrāsaṃpratipattau tu layasāmānyameva ca || K_1.21 ||

triṣu dhāmasu yattulyaṃ sāmānyaṃ vetti niścitaḥ |
sa pūjyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ vandyaścaiva mahāmuniḥ || K_1.22 ||

akāro nayate viśvamukāraścāpi taijasam |
makāraśca punaḥ prājñaṃ nāmātre vidyate gatiḥ || K_1.23 ||

oṃkāraṃ pādaśo vidyātpādā mātrā na saṃśayaḥ |
oṃkāraṃ pādaśo jñātvā na kiñcidapi cintayet || K_1.24 ||


yuñjīta praṇave cetaḥ praṇavo brahma nirbhayam |
praṇave nityayuktasya na bhayaṃ vidyate kvacit || K_1.25 ||

praṇavo hyaparaṃ brahma praṇavaśca paraḥ smṛtaḥ |
apūrvo'nantaro'bāhyo'naparaḥ praṇavo'vyayaḥ || K_1.26 ||

sarvasya praṇavo hyādirmadhyamantastathaiva ca |
evaṃ hi praṇavaṃ jñātvā vyaśnute tadanantaram || K_1.27 ||

praṇavaṃ hīśvaraṃ vidyātsarvasya hṛdi saṃsthitam |
sarvavyāpinamoṃkāraṃ matvā dhīro na śocati || K_1.28 ||

amātro'nantamātraśca dvaitasyopaśamaḥ śivaḥ |
oṃkāro vidito yena sa munirnetāro janaḥ || K_1.29 ||

vaitathyaṃ sarvabhāvānāṃ svapna āhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
antaḥsthānāttu bhāvānāṃ saṃvṛtatvena hetunā || K_2.1 ||

adīrghatvācca kālasya gatvā deśānna paśyati |
pratibuddhaśca vai sarvastasmin deśe na vidyate || K_2.2 ||

abhāvaśca rathādīnāṃ śrūyate nyāyapūrvakam |
vaitathyaṃ tena vai prāptaṃ svapna āhuḥ prakāśitam || K_2.3 ||

antasthānāttu bhedānāṃ tasmājjāgarite smṛtam |
yathā tatra tathā svapne saṃvṛtatvena bhidyate || K_2.4 ||

svapnajāgaritasthāne hyekamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
bhedānāṃ hi samatvena prasiddhenaiva hetunā || K_2.5 ||

ādāvante ca yannāsti vartamāne'pi tattathā |
vitathaiḥ sadṛśāḥ santo'vitathā iva lakṣitāḥ || K_2.6 ||

saprayojanatā teṣāṃ svapne vipratipadyate |
tasmādādyantavatvena mithyaiva khalu te smṛtāḥ || K_2.7 ||

apūrvaṃ sthānidharmo hi yathā svarganivāsinām |
tānayaṃ prekṣate gatvā yathaiveha suśikṣitaḥ || K_2.8 ||

svapnavṛttāvapi tvantaścetasā kalpitaṃ tvasat |
bahiścetogṛhītaṃ saddṛṣṭaṃ vaitathyametayoḥ || K_2.9 ||

jāgradvṛttāvapi tvantaścetasā kalpitaṃ tvasat |
bahiścetogṛhītaṃ sadyuktaṃ vaitathyametayoḥ || K_2.10 ||

ubhayorapi vaitathyaṃ bhedānāṃ sthānayoryadi |
ka etān budhyate bhedān ko vai teṣāṃ vikalpakaḥ || K_2.11 ||

kalpayatyātmanātmānamātmā devaḥ svamāyayā |
sa eva budhyate bhedāniti vedāntaniścayaḥ || K_2.12 ||

vikarotyaparān bhāvānantaścitte vyavasthitān |
niyatāṃśca bahiścitta evaṃ kalpayate prabhuḥ || K_2.13 ||

cittakālā hi ye'ntastu dvayakālāśca ye bahiḥ |
kalpitā eva te sarve viśeṣo nānyahetukaḥ || K_2.14 ||

avyaktā eva ye'ntastu sphuṭā eva ca ye bahiḥ |
kalpitā eva te sarve viśeṣastvindriyāntare || K_2.15 ||

jīvaṃ kalpayate pūrvaṃ vaco bhāvān pṛthagvidhān |
bāhyānādhyātmikāṃścaiva yathāvidyastathāsmṛtiḥ || K_2.16 ||

aniścitā yathā rajjurandhakāre vikalpitā |
sarpadhārādibhirbhāvaistadvadātmā vikalpitaḥ || K_2.17 ||

niścitāyāṃ yathā rajjvāṃ vikalpo vinivartate |
rajjureveti cādvaitaṃ tadvadātmaviniścayaḥ || K_2.18 ||

prāṇādibhiranantaiśca bhāvairetairvikalpitaḥ |
māyaiṣā tasya devasya yayā saṃmohitaḥ svayam || K_2.19 ||

prāṇa iti prāṇavido bhūtānīti ca tadvidaḥ |
guṇā iti guṇavidastattvānīti ca tadvidaḥ || K_2.20 ||

pādā iti pādavido viṣayā iti ca tadvidaḥ |
lokā iti lokavido devā iti ca tadvidaḥ || K_2.21 ||

vedā iti vedavido yajñā iti ca tadvidaḥ |
bhokteti ca bhoktṛvido bhojyamiti ca tadvidaḥ || K_2. 22 ||

sūkṣma iti sūkṣmavidaḥ sthūla iti ca tadvidaḥ |
mūrta iti mūrtavido'mūrta iti ca tadvidaḥ || K_2.23 ||

kāla iti kālavido diśa iti ca tadvidaḥ |
vādā iti vādavido bhuvanānīti tadvidaḥ || K_2.24 ||

mana iti manovido buddhiriti ca tadvidaḥ |
cittamiti cittavido dharmādharmau ca tadvidaḥ || K_2.25 ||

pañcaviṃśaka ityeke ṣaḍviśa iti cāpare |
ekatriṃśaka ityāhurananta iti cāpare || K_2.26 ||

lokāṃllokavidaḥ prāhurāśramā iti tadvidaḥ |
strīpuṃnapuṃsakaṃ laiṅgāḥ parāparamathāpare || K_2.27 ||

sṛṣṭiriti sṛṣṭivido laya iti ca tadvidaḥ |
sthitiriti sthitividaḥ sarve ceha tu sarvadā || K_2.28 ||

yaṃ bhāvaṃ darśayedyasya taṃ bhāvaṃ sa tu paśyati |
taṃ cāvati sa bhūtvāsau tadgrahaḥ samupaiti tam || K_2.29 ||

etaireṣo'pṛthagbhāvaiḥ pṛthageveti lakṣitaḥ |
evaṃ yo veda tattvena kalpayetso'viśaṅkitaḥ || K_2.30 ||

svapnamāye yathā dṛṣṭaṃ gandharvanagaraṃ yathā |
tathā viśvamidaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ vedānteṣu vicakṣaṇaiḥ || K_2.31 ||

na nirodho na cotpattirna baddho na ca sādhakaḥ |
na mumukṣurna vai mukta ityeṣā paramārthatā || K_2.32 ||

bhāvairasadbhirevāyamadvayena ca kalpitaḥ |
bhāvā apyadvayenaiva tasmādadvayatā śivā || K_2.33 ||

nātmabhāvena nānedaṃ na svenāpi kathañcana |
na pṛthaṅnāpṛthakkiñciditi tattvavido viduḥ || K_2.34 ||

vītarāgabhayakrodhairmunibhirvedapāragaiḥ |
nirvikalpo hyayaṃ dṛṣṭaḥ prapañcopaśamo'dvayaḥ || K_2.35 ||
tasmādevaṃ viditvainamadvaite yojayetsmṛtim |
advaitaṃ samanuprāpya jaḍavallokamācaret || K_2.36 ||

niḥstutirnirnamaskāro niḥsvadhākāra eva ca |
calācalaniketaśca yatiryādṛcchiko bhavet || K_2.37 ||

tattvamādhyātmikaṃ dṛṣṭvā tattvaṃ dṛṣṭvā tu bāhyataḥ |
tattvībhūtastadārāmastattvādapracyuto bhavet || K_2.38 ||

upāsanāśrito dharmo jāte brahmaṇi vartate |
prāgutpatterajaṃ sarvaṃ tenāsau kṛpaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ || K_3.1 ||

ato vakṣyāmyakārpaṇyamajāti samatāṃ gatam |
yathā na jāyate kiñcijjāyamānaṃ samantataḥ || K_3.2 ||

ātmā hyākāśavajjīvairghaṭākāśairivoditaḥ |
ghaṭādivacca saṃghātairjātāvetannidarśanam || K_3.3 ||

ghaṭādiṣu pralīneṣu ghaṭākāśādayo yathā |
ākāśe saṃpralīyante tadvajjīvā ihātmani || K_3.4 ||

yathaikasmin ghāṭākāśe rajodhūmādibhiryute |
na sarve saṃprayujyante tadvajjīvāḥ sukhādibhiḥ || K_3.5 ||

rūpakāryasamākhyāśca bhidyante tatra tatra vai |
ākāśasya na bhedo'sti tadvajjīveṣu nirṇayaḥ || K_3.6 ||

nākāśasya ghaṭākāśo vikārāvayavau yathā |
naivātmanaḥ sadā jīvo vikārāvayavau tathā || K_3.7 ||

yathā bhavati bālānāṃ gaganaṃ malinaṃ malaiḥ |
tathā bhavatyabuddhānāmātmāpi malino malaiḥ || K_3.8 ||

maraṇe saṃbhave caiva gatyāgamanayorapi |
sthitau sarvaśarīreṣu ākāśenāvilakṣaṇaḥ || K_3.9 ||

saṃghātāḥ svapnavatsarve ātmamāyāvisarjitāḥ |
ādhikye sarvasāmye vā nopapattirhi vidyate || K_3.10 ||

rasādayo hi ye kośā vyākhyātāstaittirīyake |
teṣāmātmā paro jīvaḥ khaṃ yathā saṃprakāśitaḥ || K_3.11 ||

dvayordvayormadhujñāne paraṃ brahma prakāśitam |
pṛthivyāmudare caiva yathākāśaḥ prakāśitaḥ || K_3.12 ||

jīvātmanorananyatvamabhedena praśasyate |
nānātvaṃ nindyate yacca tadevaṃ hi samañjasam || K_3.13 ||

jīvātmanoḥ pṛthaktvaṃ yatprāgutpatteḥ prakīrtitam |
bhaviṣyadvṛtyā gauṇaṃ tanmukhyatvaṃ hi na yujyate || K_3.14 ||

mṛllohavisphuliṅgādyaiḥ sṛṣṭiryā coditānyathā |
upāyaḥ so'vatārāya nāsti bhedaḥ kathañcana || K_3.15 ||

āśramāstrividhā hīnamadhyamotkṛṣṭadṛṣṭayaḥ |
upāsanopadiṣṭeyaṃ tadarthamanukampayā || K_3.16 ||

svasiddhāntavyavasthāsu dvaitino niścitā dṛḍham |
parasparaṃ virudhyante tairayaṃ na virudhyate || K_3.17 ||

advaitaṃ paramārtho hi dvaitaṃ tadbheda ucyate |
teṣāmubhayathā dvaitaṃ tenāyaṃ na viruddhyate || K_3.18 ||

māyayā bhidyate hyetannānyathājaṃ kathañcana |
tattvato bhidyamāne hi martyatāmamṛtaṃ vrajet || K_3.19 ||

ajātasyaiva bhāvasya jātimicchanti vādinaḥ |
ajāto hyamṛto bhāvo martyatāṃ kathameṣyati || K_3.20 ||

na bhavatyamṛtaṃ martyaṃ na martyamamṛtaṃ tathā |
prakṛteranyathābhāvo na kathañcidbhaviṣyati || K_3.21 ||
svabhāvenāmṛto yasya bhāvo gacchati martyatām |
kṛtakenāmṛtastasya kathaṃ sthāsyati niścalaḥ || K_3.22 ||

bhūtato'bhūtato vāpi sṛjyamāne samā śrutiḥ |
niścitaṃ yuktiyuktaṃ ca yattadbhavati netarat || K_3.23 ||

neha nāneti cāmnāyādindro māyābhirityapi |
ajāyamāno bahudhā māyayā jāyate tu saḥ || K_3.24 ||

saṃbhūterapavādācca saṃbhavaḥ pratiṣidhyate |
ko nvenaṃ janayediti kāraṇaṃ pratiṣidhyate || K_3.25 ||

sa eṣa neti netīti vyākhyātaṃ nihnute yataḥ |
sarvamagrāhyabhāvena hetunājaṃ prakāśate || K_3.26 ||

sato hi māyayā janma yujyate na tu tattvataḥ |
tattvato jāyate yasya jātaṃ tasya hi jāyate || K_3.27 ||

asato māyayā janma tattvato naiva yujyate |
vandhyāputro na tattvena māyayā vāpi jāyate || K_3.28 ||

yathā svapne dvayābhāsaṃ spandate māyayā manaḥ |
tathā jāgraddvayābhāsaṃ spandate māyayā manaḥ || K_3.29 ||

advayaṃ ca dvayābhāsaṃ manaḥ svapne na saṃśayaḥ |
advayaṃ ca dvayābhāsaṃ tathā jāgranna saṃśayaḥ || K_3.30 ||

manodṛśyamidaṃ dvaitaṃ yatkiñcitsacarācaram |
manaso hyamanībhāve dvaitaṃ naivopalabhyate || K_3.31 ||

ātmasatyānubodhena na saṃkalpayate yadā |
amanastāṃ tadā yāti grāhyābhāve tadagraham || K_3.32 ||

akalpamajaṃ jñānaṃ jñeyābhinnaṃ pracakṣate |
brahma jñeyamajaṃ nityamajenājaṃ vibudhyate || K_3.33 ||

nigṛhītasya manaso nirvikalpasya dhīmataḥ |
pracāraḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ suṣupte'nyo na tatsamaḥ || K_3.34 ||
līyate hi suṣupte tannigṛhītaṃ na līyate |
tadeva nirbhayaṃ brahma jñānalokaṃ samantataḥ || K_3.35 ||

ajamanindramasvapnamanāmakamarūpakam |
sakṛdvibhātaṃ sarvajñaṃ nopacāraḥ kathañcana || K_3.36 ||

sarvābhilāpavigataḥ sarvacintāsamutthitaḥ |
supraśāntaḥ sakṛjjyotiḥ samādhiracalo'bhayaḥ || K_3.37 ||

graho na tatra notsargaścintā yatra na vidyate |
ātmasaṃsthaṃ tadā jñānamajāti samatāṃ gatam || K_3.38 ||

asparśayogo vai nāma durdarśaḥ sarvayogibhiḥ |
yogino bibhyati hyasmādabhaye bhayadarśinaḥ || K_3.39 ||

manaso nigrahāyattamabhayaṃ sarvayoginām |
duḥkhakṣayaḥ prabodhaścāpyakṣayā śāntireva ca || K_3.40 ||

utseka udadheryadvatkuśāgreṇaikabindunā |
manaso nigrahastadvadbhavedaparikhedataḥ || K_3.41 ||

upāyena nigṛhṇīyādvikṣiptaṃ kāmabhogayoḥ |
suprasannaṃ laye caiva yathā kāmo layastathā || K_3.42 ||

duḥkhaṃ sarvamanusmṛtya kāmabhogānnivartayet |
ajaṃ sarvamanusmṛtya jātaṃ naiva tu paśyati || K_3.43 ||

laye saṃbodhayeccittaṃ vikṣiptaṃ śamayetpunaḥ |
sakaṣāyaṃ vijānīyātsamaprāptaṃ na cālayet || K_3.44 ||

nāsvādayetsukhaṃ tatra niḥsaṅgaḥ prajñayā bhavet |
niścalaṃ niścaraccittamekīkuryātprayatnataḥ || K_3.45 ||

yadā na līyate cittaṃ na ca vikṣipyate punaḥ |
aniṅganamanābhāsaṃ niṣpannaṃ brahma tattadā || K_3.46 ||

svasthaṃ śāntaṃ sanirvāṇamakathyaṃ sukhamuttamam |
ajamajena jñeyena sarvajñaṃ paricakṣate || K_3.47 ||
na kaścijjāyate jīvaḥ saṃbhavo'sya na vidyate |
etattaduttamaṃ satyaṃ yatra kiñcinna jāyate || K_3.48 ||

jñānenākāśakalpena dharmānyo gaganopamān |
jñeyābhinnena saṃbuddhastaṃ vande dvipadāṃ varam || K_4.1 ||

asparśayogo vai nāma sarvasattvasukho hitaḥ |
avivādo'viruddhaśca deśitastaṃ namāmyaham || K_4.2 ||

bhūtasya jātimicchanti vādinaḥ kecideva hi |
abhūtasyāpare dhīrā vivadantaḥ parasparam || K_4.3 ||

bhūtaṃ na jāyate kiñcidabhūtaṃ naiva jāyate |
vivadanto'dvayā hyevamajātiṃ khyāpayanti te || K_4.4 ||

khyāpyamānāmajātiṃ tairanumodāmahe vayam |
vivadāmo na taiḥ sārdhamavivādaṃ nibodhata || K_4.5 ||

ajātasyaiva dharmasya jātimicchanti vādinaḥ |
ajāto hyamṛto dharmo martyatāṃ kathameṣyati || K_4.6 ||

na bhavatyamṛtaṃ martyaṃ na martyamamṛtaṃ tathā |
prakṛteranyathābhāvo na kathañcidbhaviṣyati || K_4.7 ||

svabhāvenāmṛto yasya dharmo gacchati martyatām |
kṛtakenāmṛtastasya kathaṃ sthāsyati niścalaḥ || K_4.8 ||

sāṃsiddhikī svābhāvikī sahajā akṛtā ca yā |
prakṛtiḥ seti vijñeyā svabhāvaṃ na jahāti yā || K_4.9 ||

jarāmaraṇanirmuktāḥ sarve dharmāḥ svabhāvataḥ |
jarāmaraṇamicchantaścyavante tanmanīṣayā || K_4.10 ||

kāraṇaṃ yasya vai kāryaṃ kāraṇaṃ tasya jāyate |
jāyamānaṃ kathamajaṃ bhinnaṃ nityaṃ kathaṃ ca tat || K_4.11 ||

kāraṇādyadyananyatvamataḥ kāryamajaṃ yadi |
jāyamānāddhi vai kāryātkāraṇaṃ te kathaṃ dhruvam || K_4.12 ||

ajādvai jāyate yasya dṛṣṭāntastasya nāsti vai |
jātācca  jāyāmānasya na vyavasthā prasajyate || K_4.13 ||

hetorādiḥ phalaṃ yeṣāmādirhetuḥ phalasya ca |
hetoḥ phalasya cānādiḥ kathaṃ tairupavarṇyate || K_4.14 ||

hetorādiḥ phalaṃ yeṣāmādirhetuḥ kalasya ca |
tathā janma bhavetteṣāṃ putrājjanma pituryathā || K_4.15 ||

saṃbhave hetuphalayoreṣitavyaḥ kramastvayā |
yugapatsaṃbhave yasmādasaṃbandho viṣāṇavat || K_4.16 ||

phalādutpadyamānaḥ sanna te hetuḥ prasidhyati |
aprasiddhaḥ kathaṃ hetuḥ phalamutpādayiṣyati || K_4.17 ||

yadi hetoḥ phalātsiddhiḥ phalasiddhiśca hetutaḥ |
kataratpūrvaniṣpannaṃ yasya siddhirapekṣayā || K_4.18 ||

aśaktiraparijñānaṃ kramakopo'tha vā punaḥ |
evaṃ hi sarvathā buddhairajātiḥ paridīpitā || K_4.19 ||

bījāṅkurākhyo dṛṣṭāntaḥ sadā sādhyasamo hi sa |
na hi sādhyasamo hetuḥ siddhau sādhyasya yujyate || K_4.20 ||

pūrvāparāparijñānamajāteḥ paridīpakam |
jāyamānāddhi vai dharmātkathaṃ pūrvaṃ na gṛhyate || K_4.21 ||

svato vā parato vāpi na kiñcidvastu jāyate |
sadasatsadasadvāpi na kiñcidvastu jāyate || K_4.22 ||

heturna jāyate'nādeḥ phalaṃ cāpi svabhāvataḥ |
ādirna vidyate yasya tasya hyādirna vidyate || K_4.23 ||
prajñapteḥ sanimittatvamanyathā dvayanāśataḥ |
saṃkleśasyopalabdheśca paratantrāstitā matā || K_4.24 ||

prajñapteḥ sanimittatvamiṣyate yuktidarśanāt |
nimittasyānimittatvamiṣyate bhūtadarśanāt || K_4.25 ||

cittaṃ na saṃspṛśatyarthaṃ nārthābhāsaṃ tathaiva ca |
abhūto hi yataścārtho nārthābhāsastataḥ pṛthak || K_4.26 ||

nimittaṃ na sadā cittaṃ saṃspṛśatyadhvasu triṣu |
animitto viparyāsaḥ kathaṃ tasya bhaviṣyati || K_4.27 ||

tasmānna jāyate cittaṃ cittadṛśyaṃ na jāyate |
tasya paśyanti ye jātiṃ khe vai paśyanti te padam || K_4.28 ||

ajātaṃ jāyate yasmādajātiḥ prakṛtistataḥ |
prakṛteranyathābhāvo na kathañcidbhaviṣyati || K_4.29 ||

anāderantavattvaṃ ca saṃsārasya na setsyati |
anantatā cādimato mokṣasya na bhaviṣyati || K_4.30 ||

ādāvante ca yannāsti vartamāne'pi tattathā |
vitathaiḥ sadṛśāḥ santo'vitathā iva lakṣitāḥ || K_4.31 ||

saprayojanatā teṣāṃ svapne vipratipadyate |
tasmādādyantavattvena mithyaiva khalu te smṛtāḥ || K_4.32 ||

sarve dharmā mṛṣā svapne kāyasyāntanidarśanāt |
saṃvṛtte'smin pradeśe vai bhūtānāṃ darśanaṃ kutaḥ || K_4.33 ||

na yuktaṃ darśanaṃ gatvā kālasyāniyamādgatau |
pratibuddhaśca vai sarvastasmin deśe na vidyate || K_4.34 ||

mitrādyaiḥ saha saṃmantrya saṃbuddhau na prapadyate |
gṛhītaṃ cāpi yatkiñcitpratibuddho na paśyati || K_4.35 ||

svapne cāvastukaḥ kāyaḥ pṛthaganyasya darśanāt |
yathā kāyastathā sarvaṃ cittadṛśyamavastukam || K_4.36 ||
grahaṇājjāgaritavattaddhetuḥ svapna iṣyate |
taddhetutvāttu tasyaiva sajjāgaritamiṣyate || K_4.37 ||

utpādasyāprasiddhatvādajaṃ sarvamudāhṛtam |
na ca bhūtādabhūtasya saṃbhavo'sti kathañcana || K_4.38 ||

asajjāgarite dṛṣṭvā svapne paśyati tanmayaḥ |
asatsvapne'pi dṛṣṭvā ca pratibuddhau na paśyati || K_4.39 ||

nāstyasaddhetukamasatsadasaddhetukaṃ tathā |
sacca saddhetukaṃ nāsti saddhetukamasatkutaḥ || K_4.40 ||

viparyāsādyathā jāgradacintyān bhūtavatspṛśet |
tathā svapne viparyāsāddharmāṃstatraiva paśyati || K_4.41 ||

upalambhātsamācārādasti vastutvavādinām |
jātistu deśitā buddhairajātestrasatāṃ sadā || K_4.42 ||

ajātestrasatāṃ teṣāmupalambhādviyanti ye |
jātidoṣā na setsyanti doṣo'pyalpo bhaviṣyati || K_4.43 ||

upalambhātsamācārānmāyāhastī yathocyate |
upalambhātsamācārādasti vastu tathocyate || K_4.44 ||

jātyābhāsaṃ calābhāsaṃ vastvābhāsaṃ tathaiva ca |
ajācalamavastutvaṃ vijñānaṃ śāntamadvayam || K_4.45 ||

evaṃ na jāyate cittamevaṃ dharmā ajāḥ smṛtāḥ |
evameva vijānanto na patanti viparyaye || K_4.46 ||

ṛjuvakrādikābhāsamalātaspanditaṃ yathā |
grahaṇagrāhakābhāsaṃ vijñānaspanditaṃ tathā || K_4.47 ||

aspandamānamalātamanābhāsamajaṃ yathā |
aspandamānaṃ vijñānamanābhāsamajaṃ tathā || K_4.48 ||

alāte spandamāne vai nābhāsā anyato bhuvaḥ |
na tato'nyatra nispandānnālātaṃ praviśanti te || K_4.49 ||
na nirgatā alātātte dravyatvābhāvayogataḥ |
vijñāne'pi tathaiva syurābhāsasyāviśeṣataḥ || K_4.50 ||

vijñāne spandamāne vai nābhāsā anyatobhuvaḥ |
na tato'nyatra nispandānna vijñānaṃ viśanti te || K_4.51 ||

na nirgatāste vijñānāddravyatvābhāvayogataḥ |
kāryakāraṇatābhāvādyato'cintyāḥ sadaiva te || K_4.52 ||

dravyaṃ dravyasya hetuḥ syādanyadanyasya caiva hi |
dravyatvamanyabhāvo vā dharmāṇāṃ nopapadyate || K_4.53 ||

evaṃ na cittajā dharmāścittaṃ vāpi na dharmajam |
evaṃ hetuphalājātiṃ praviśanti manīṣiṇaḥ || K_4.54 ||

yāvaddhetuphalāveśastāvaddhetuphalodbhavaḥ |
kṣīṇe hetuphalāveśe nāsti hetuphalodbhavaḥ || K_4.55 ||

yāvaddhetuphalāveśaḥ saṃsārastāvadāyataḥ |
kṣīṇe hetuphalāveśe saṃsāraṃ na prapadyate || K_4.56 ||

saṃvṛtyā jāyate sarvaṃ śāśvataṃ nāsti tena vai |
sadbhāvena hyajaṃ sarvamucchedastena nāsti vai || K_4.57 ||

dharmā ya iti jāyante jāyante te na tattvataḥ |
janma māyopamaṃ teṣāṃ sā ca māyā na vidyate || K_4.58 ||

yathā māyāmayādbījājjāyate tanmayoṅkuraḥ |
nāsau nityo na cocchedī tadvaddharmeṣu yojanā || K_4.59 ||

nājeṣu sarvadharmeṣu śāśvatāśāśvatābhidhā |
yatra varṇā na vartante vivekastatra nocyate || K_4.60 ||

yathā svapne dvayābhāsaṃ cittaṃ calati māyayā |
tathā jāgraddvayābhāsaṃ cittaṃ calati māyayā || K_4.61 ||

advayaṃ ca dvayābhāsaṃ cittaṃ svapne na saṃśayaḥ |
advayaṃ ca dvayābhāsaṃ tathā jāgranna saṃśayaḥ || K_4.62 ||
svapnadṛkpracaran svapne dikṣu vai daśasu sthitān |
aṇḍajān svedajān vāpi jīvān paśyati yān sadā || K_4.63 ||

svapnadṛkcittadṛśyāste na vidyante tataḥ pṛthak |
tathā taddṛśyamevedaṃ svapnadṛkcittamiṣyate || K_4.64 ||

carañjāgarite jāgraddikṣu vai daśasu sthitān |
aṇḍajān svedajān vāpi jīvān paśyati yān sadā || K_4.65 ||

jāgraccittekṣaṇīyāste na vidyante tataḥ pṛthak |
tathā taddṛśyamevedaṃ jāgrataścittamiṣyate || K_4.66 ||

ubhe hyanyonyadṛśye te kiṃ tadastīti cocyate |
lakṣaṇāśūnyamubhayaṃ tanmate naiva gṛhyate || K_4.67 ||

yathā svapnamayo jīvo jāyate mriyate'pi ca |
tathā jīvā amī sarve bhavanti na bhavanti ca || K_4.68 ||

yathā māyāmayo jīvo jāyate mriyate'pi ca |
tathā jīvā amī sarve bhavanti na bhavanti ca || K_4.69 ||

yathā nirmitako jīvo jāyate mriyate'pi ca |
tathā jīvā amī sarve bhavanti na bhavanti ca || K_4.70 ||

na kaścijjāyate jīvaḥ saṃbhavo'sya na vidyate |
etattaduttamaṃ satyaṃ yatra kiñcinna jāyate || K_4.71 ||

cittaspandikamevedaṃ grāhyagrāhakavaddvayam |
cittaṃ nirviṣayaṃ nityamasaṅgaṃ tena kīrtitam || K_4.72 ||

yo'sti kalpitasaṃvṛtyā paramārthena nāstyasau |
paratantrābhisaṃvṛtyā syānnāsti paramārthataḥ || K_4.73 ||

ajaḥ kalpitasaṃvṛtyā paramārthena nāpyajaḥ |
paratantrādiniṣpattyā saṃvṛtyā jāyate tu saḥ || K_4.74 ||

abhūtābhiniveśo'sti dvayaṃ tatra na vidyate |
dvayābhāvaṃ sa buddhvaiva nirnimitto na jāyate || K_4.75 ||
yadā na labhate hetūnuttamādhamamadhyamān |
tadā na jāyate cittaṃ hetvabhāve phalaṃ kutaḥ || K_4.76 ||

animittasya cittasya yānutpattiḥ samādvayā |
ajātasyaiva sarvasya cittadṛśyaṃ hi tadyataḥ || K_4.77 ||

buddhvā nimittatāṃ satyāṃ hetuṃ pṛthaganāpnuvan |
vītaśokaṃ tathākāmamabhayaṃ padamaśnute || K_4.78 ||

abhūtābhiniveśāddhi sadṛśe tatpravartate |
vastvabhāvaṃ sa budhvaiva niḥsaṅgaṃ vinivartate || K_4.79 ||

nivṛttasyāpravṛttasya niścalā hi tathā sthitiḥ |
viṣayaḥ sa hi buddhānāṃ tatsāmyamajamadvayam || K_4.80 ||

ajamanidramasvapnaṃ prabhātaṃ bhavati svayam |
sakṛdvibhāto hyevaiṣa dharma dhātusvabhāvataḥ || K_4.81 ||

sukhamāvriyate nityaṃ duḥkhaṃ vivriyate sadā |
yasya kasya ca dharmasya graheṇa bhagavānasau || K_4.82 ||

asti nāstyasti nāstīti nāsti nāstīti vā punaḥ |
calasthirobhayābhāvairāvṛṇotye va bāliśaḥ || K_4.83 ||

koṭhyaścatasra etāstu grahairyāsāṃ sadā vṛtaḥ |
bhagavānābhiraspṛṣṭo yena dṛṣṭaḥ sa sarvadṛk || K_4.84 ||

prāpya sarvajñatāṃ kṛtsnāṃ brāhmaṇyaṃ padamadvayam |
anāpannādimadhyāntaṃ kimataḥ paramīhate || K_4.85 ||

viprāṇāṃ vinayo hyeṣa śamaḥ prākṛta ucyate |
damaḥ prakṛtidāntatvādevaṃ vidvāñśamaṃ vrajet || K_4.86 ||

savastu sopalambhaṃ ca dvayaṃ laukikamiṣyate |
avastu sopalambhaṃ ca śuddhaṃ laukikamiṣyate || K_4.87 ||

avastvanupalambhaṃ ca lokottaramiti smṛtam |
jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ ca vijñeyaṃ sadā buddhaiḥ prakīrtitam || K_4.88 ||
jñāne ca trividhe jñeye krameṇa vidite svayam |
sarvajñatā hi sarvatra bhavatīha mahādhiyaḥ || K_4.89 ||

heyajñeyāpyapākyāni vijñeyānyagrayāṇataḥ |
teṣāmanyatra vijñeyādupalambhastriṣu smṛtaḥ || K_4.90 ||

prakṛtyākāśavajjñeyāḥ sarve dharmā anādayaḥ |
vidyate na hi nānātvaṃ teṣāṃ kvacana kiñcanaḥ || K_4.91 ||

ādibuddhāḥ prakṛtyaiva sarve dharmāḥ suniścitāḥ |
yasyaivaṃ bhavati kṣāntiḥ so'mṛtatvāya kalpate || K_4.92 ||

ādiśāntā hyanutpannāḥ prakṛtyaiva sunirvṛtāḥ |
sarve dharmāḥ samābhinnā ajaṃ sāmyaṃ viśāradam || K_4.93 ||

vaiśāradyaṃ tu vai nāsti bhede vicaratāṃ sadā |
bhedanimnāḥ pṛthagvādāstasmātte kṛpaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ || K_4.94 ||

aje sāmye tu ye kecidbhaviṣyanti suniścitāḥ |
te hi loke mahājñānāstacca loko na gāhate || K_4.95 ||

ajeṣvajamasaṃkrāntaṃ dharmeṣu jñānamiṣyate |
yato na kramate jñānamasaṅgaṃ tena kīrtitam || K_4.96 ||

aṇumātre'pi vaidharmye jāyamāne'vipaścitaḥ |
asaṅgatā sadā nāsti kimutāvaraṇacyutiḥ || K_4.97 ||

alabdhāvaraṇāḥ sarve dharmāḥ prakṛtinirmalāḥ |
ādau buddhāstathā muktā budhyanta iti nāyakāḥ || K_4.98 ||

kramate na hi buddhasya jñānaṃ dharmeṣu tāyinaḥ |
sarve dharmāstathā jñānaṃ naitadbuddhena bhāṣitam || K_4.99 ||

durdarśamatigambhīramajaṃ sāmyaṃ viśāradam |
buddhvā padamanānātvaṃ namaskurmo yathābalam || K_4.100 ||
