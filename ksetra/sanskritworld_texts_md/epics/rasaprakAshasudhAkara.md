adhyāya 1

śaśikalākalitaṃ hi śubhānanaṃ śivanidhānamatho ṛṣipūjitam  |
jananapālanasaṃharaṇātmakaṃ hariharaṃ prathamaṃ praṇamāmyaham  || 1.1 ||
kumudakundasitāmbaradhāriṇīṃ vimalamauktikahārasuśobhitām  |
sakalasiddhagaṇairapi sevitāmaharahaḥ praṇamāmi ca śāradām  || 1.2 ||

rasanigamasudhābdhau mathyamāne mayaiva gaṇitarasaśatānāṃ saṃgrahaḥ procyate vai  || 8.1 ||
<jvarāṅkuśa (1)>
sukhaṇḍitaṃ hāriṇajaṃ viṣāṇaṃ rasena pācyaṃ jalajaṃbukasya  |
ruddhvātha bhāṇḍe vipacecca cullyāṃ yāmadvayaṃ śītagataṃ samuddharet || 8.2 ||
tato'ṣṭabhāgaṃ trikaṭuṃ niyojya niṣkapramāṇaṃ ca bhajetprabhāte  |
taṃ nāgavallīdalajena sārdhaṃ nihanti vātodbhavakaṃ jvaraṃ ca  || 8.3 ||
<jvarāṅkuśa (2)>
sūtagaṃdhaviṣakāravīkaṇā- dantibījamiti vardhitaiḥ kramāt |
marditaiśca daśaniṃbukadravai raktikārdhatulitā vaṭī kṛtā  |
bhakṣitā jvaragaṇānnihantyayaṃ recano jvaraharo'yamaṅkuśaḥ  || 8.4 ||
eka eva kathitastu so'malaḥ svedito'pi saha cūrṇajalena  |
yāmapūrvamapi raktikāmito bhakṣitaḥ sakalaśītajūrtihṛt || 8.5 ||
<jvarārirasa>
pāradaṃ rasakagaṃdhatālakaṃ tutthaṭaṃkaṇayutaṃ suśodhitam  |
mardayecca samamātramekataḥ kāravellajarasairdinaṃ tathā  || 8.6 ||
lepayecca ravibhājanodare cāṃgulārdhamapi mānameva hi  |
tāmrabhājanamukhaṃ nirudhya vai taṃ pacetsikatayaṃtramadhyataḥ  || 8.7 ||
dhānyakānyupari muñcitāni cetsaṃsphuṭanti yadi śuddhamucyate  |
jāyate'tiruciro jvarārikaḥ sevito jvaragaṇāpahārakaḥ  || 8.8 ||
māṣamātramuṣaṇaiḥ samaiḥ sadā parṇakhaṇḍasahitaiśca bhakṣitaḥ  |
nāśayeddhi viṣamodbhavān jvarānandhakāramiva bhāskarodayaḥ  || 8.9 ||
<taruṇajvarārirasaḥ>
tālatāmrarasagaṃdhatutthakān śāṇamātratulitān samastakān  |
niṣkamātrarucirāṃ manaḥśilāṃ mardayettriphalakāmbubhirdṛḍham  || 8.10 ||
golamasya ca vidhāya saṃpuṭe pācayecca puṭapākayogataḥ  |
arkavajripayasā subhāvayetsaptavāramatha dantikāśṛtaiḥ  || 8.11 ||
māṣamātrarasa eṣa bhakṣitaḥ śāṇamānamaricairyuto yadā  |
sārdhaniṣkakaguḍena yojitaḥ saurasairdvidaśapatrakairyutaḥ  || 8.12 ||
śītapūrvamatha dāhapūrvakaṃ dvyāhikaṃ ca sakalān jvarānapi  |
nāśayeddhi taruṇajvarārikaḥ sarvadoṣaśamanaḥ sukhāvahaḥ  || 8.13 ||
<jvaraghnī vaṭī>
bhāgaikaḥ syātpāradaḥ śodhitaśca elīyaḥ syātpippalī śreyasī ca  |
ākallo vai gaṃdhakaḥ sārṣapeṇa tailenātho śodhito buddhimadbhiḥ  || 8.14 ||
turyānbhāgānindravallīphalānāṃ kṛtvā cūrṇaṃ śoṣayetsūryatāpe  |
cūrṇaṃ caitadbhāvayettadrasena māṣaistulyāṃ kārayettadvaṭīṃ ca  || 8.15 ||
dattvā caikāṃ jātasadyojvarāya chinnāṅgāyāḥ kvāthapānaṃ vidheyam  |
doṣodbhūtaṃ sannipātodbhavāṃ ca jūrtiṃ samyaṅnāśayatyāśu tīvrām  || 8.16 ||
<jvarakṛntanarasaḥ>
śuddhaḥ sūto gaṃdhako vatsanābhaḥ pratyekaṃ vai śāṇamātrā vidheyā  |
dhūrtādbījaṃ kārayedvai triśaṇaṃ sarvebhyo vai dvaiguṇā hemadugdhā  || 8.17 ||
sūkṣmaṃ cūrṇaṃ kārayettatprayatnāddeyaṃ guṃjāyugmamānaṃ ca samyak |
khādedārdraṃ cānupāne jvarārtaḥ sadyo hanyātsarvadoṣotthajūrtim  || 8.18 ||
<jvarārirasaḥ>
sūtaṃ gaṃdhaṃ hiṅgulaṃ daṃtibījaṃ bhāgairvṛddhaṃ kārayecca krameṇa  |
cūrṇaṃ kṛtvā marditaṃ daṃtitoyairguṃjāmātro bhakṣitaścejjvarāriḥ  || 8.19 ||
<sūryarasaḥ>
ekaṃ bhāgaṃ vatsanābhaṃ ca kuryāddvau bhāgau ceṭṭaṅkaṇaṃ daṃtibījam  |
trīṇyevaite hiṃgulasyāpi turyaḥ sadyo jūrtiṃ nāśayatyeva sūryaḥ  || 8.20 ||
<pratijñāvācakarasaḥ>
śuddhaṃ sūtaṃ bhāgamekaṃ tu tālāddvau bhāgau cedvedasaṃkhyāḥ śilāyāḥ  |
tāmrasyaivaṃ bhāgayugmaṃ prakuryādbhallātaṃ vā vedabhāgaṃ tathaiva  || 8.21 ||
arkakṣīrairbhāvayecca trivāraṃ kṛtvā cūrṇaṃ kārayedgolakaṃ tat |
sthālīmadhye sthāpitaṃ tacca golaṃ dattvā mudrāṃ bhasmanā saiṃdhavena  || 8.22 ||
dhūmrasyaivaṃ rodhanaṃ ca prakuryācchāṇairdadyātsvedanaṃ mandavahnau  |
paścāttoyenaiva bhāvyaṃ ca cūrṇaṃ golaṃ kṛtvā maṃdavahnau vipācya  || 8.23 ||
paścādenaṃ bhakṣayedvai rasendraṃ vallaṃ caikaṃ śarkarācūrṇamiśram  || 8.24 ||
tadvatkṛṣṇāmākṣikeṇaiva jūrtiṃ hanyādetatsarvadoṣotthitāṃ vai  || 8.25 ||
<atisārasaṃgrahaṇīharā yogāḥ>
athātīsāraśamanān rasān saṃkathayāmi vai  |
bhakṣitāścaiva ye nityaṃ sadyaḥ pratyayakārakāḥ  || 8.26 ||
<śaṅkhodararasaḥ>
śuddhaṃ sūtaṃ gaṃdhakaṃ vai samāṃśaṃ citronmattairmardayedvāsaraikam  |
cūrṇairetaiḥ śaṃkhamāpūritaṃ vai bhāṇḍe sthāpyaṃ mudritavyaṃ prayatnāt || 8.27 ||
tasyādhastādaṣṭayāmaṃ prakuryādvahniṃ śīte karṣamātraṃ viṣaṃ hi  |
dattvā gharme trīṇi cātho puṭāni dadyāttadvatkanyakāyā rasena  || 8.28 ||
vallaṃ yojyaṃ jīrakeṇātha bhṛṃgyā kṣaudrairyuktaṃ bhakṣitaṃ saṃgrahaṇyām  |
śvāse śūle cānile śleṣmaje vā kāse'rśaḥsu viḍgrahe cātisāre  || 8.29 ||
<ānandabhairavī guṭī>
saubhāgyaṃ vai hiṃgulaṃ vatsanābhaṃ mārīcaṃ vai hemabījena yuktam  |
kṛtvā cūrṇaṃ sarvametatsamāṃśaṃ jambīraistanmarditaṃ yāmayugmam  |
guṃjāmātrā nirmitā bhakṣitā hi guṭyo hanyuḥ sannipātātisārān  || 8.30 ||
<atisārabhairavī guṭī>
jātīpattrī devapuṣpaṃ ca śuṇṭhī kaṅkollaṃ ceccaṃdanaṃ kuṃkumaṃ ca  |
kṛṣṇāyuktaṃ śuddhamākallakaṃ syātsarvāṇyevaṃ cūrṇayedvai samāni  || 8.31 ||
sūtasyaivaṃ bhasma miśraṃ prakuryādbhāgaṃ caikaṃ miśrayennāgaphenam  |
khalve sarvaṃ marditaṃ caikayāmaṃ kāryā golī vallamātrā jalena  || 8.32 ||
bhakṣedrātrau pāyayettaṃḍulodaṃ hanyātsarvān sarvadoṣātisārān  || 8.33 ||
<kanakasundararasaḥ>
mārīcaṃ ceṭṭaṃkaṇaṃ hiṃgulaṃ ca gaṃdhāśmā vai pippalī vatsanābham  |
dhūrtasyaivaṃ bījakānīha śuddhānyevaṃ kṛtvā tacca cūrṇaṃ vidheyam  || 8.34 ||
prātaḥ sāyaṃ bhakṣitaṃ vallamātraṃ jūrtiṃ raktaṃ nāśayeccātisāram  |
dadhyannaṃ vā bhojayettakrayuktaṃ hanyādevaṃ cāgnimāndyaṃ sutīvram  || 8.35 ||

adhyāya 9

<divyauṣadhī>
divyauṣadhīnāṃ nāmāni kathyante'tra mayādhunā  |
catuḥṣaṣṭimitāḥ samyag- rasabandhakarāḥ śubhāḥ  |
somavallī tathā sobhavṛkṣaṃ somakalā latā  || 9.1 ||
bhūpadminī gonasā ceduccaṭā ceśvarī latā  |
bhūtakeśī kṛṣṇalatā laśunī ca rudantikā  |
vārāhī saptapattrā ca nāginī sarpiṇī tathā  || 9.2 ||
chattriṇī caiva gośṛṃgī jyotirnāmnī ca raktikā  |
pattravallī kākinī ca cāṇḍālī tāmravallikā  || 9.3 ||
pītavallī ca vijayā mahauṣadhyamarī latā  |
navanītā rudravallī lambinī bhūmitumbikā  || 9.4 ||
gāndharvī vyāghrapādī ca gomārī ca triśūlinī  |
tridaṇḍī karasī bhṛṃga- vallī camarikā tathā  || 9.5 ||
karavallī latā caiva vajrāṅgī ciravallyapi  |
rohiṇī bilvinī bhūta- śocanī caiva kathyate  || 9.6 ||
mārkaṇḍī ca karīrī ca hyakṣarā kuṭajā tathā  |
mūlakandāmbuvallī ca munivallī ca kīrtitā  || 9.7 ||
ghṛtagandhā nimbuvallī tilakandātasītalā  |
bodhavallī sattvagandhā kūrmavallī ca mādhavī  || 9.8 ||
viśālā ca mahānāgī maṇḍūkī kṣīragandhikā  |
catuḥṣaṣṭiriti proktā divyauṣadhyo mahābalāḥ  || 9.9 ||
ekaikāyā rasenāpi sūto bandhatvamāpnuyāt |
anayā sādhitaḥ sūto jarādāridryanāśanaḥ  || 9.10 ||
tāstu lakṣaṇasaṃyuktāḥ somadevena bhāṣitāḥ  |
granthavistarabhītyātra nāmamātreṇa kīrtitāḥ  || 9.11 ||
<rasaudhyaḥ>
ataḥ paraṃ rasauṣadhyaḥ procyante śāstravartmanā  |
jalotpalā ciñcikā ca jalāpāmārgamāṃsike  || 9.12 ||
jalakumbhī meghanādā īśvarī cāparājitā  |
mālārjunī veṇukā ca śikhipādā ca tiktikā  || 9.13 ||
kāśmaryativiṣā proktā samaṅgā jālinī tathā  |
tuṣambukā ca durgandhā pāṣāṇī śukanāsikā  || 9.14 ||

paṭolī śaṭhikā mūrvā pāṭalā jalamūlakā  || 9.15 ||
rasājamārī kathitā śiṃśikā sitagandhikā  |
potakī ca viṣaghnī ca bṛhatī garuḍī tathā  || 9.16 ||
tulasī ca vidārī ca mañjiṣṭhā citrapālikā  |
jalapippalikā bhārṃgī maṇḍūkī cottamā tathā  || 9.17 ||
candrodakā sārivā ca hariṇī kukkuṭāpi ca  |
sarpākṣī haṃsapādī ca vanakuṣmāṇḍavallikā  || 9.18 ||

ardhacandrā hemapuṣpī mohinī vajrakandikā  || 9.19 ||

vṛścikālī guḍūcī ca vāsā śṛṅgī ca kathyate  || 9.20 ||
aṣṭaṣaṣṭiriti proktā rasauṣadhyo mahābalāḥ  |
sūtasya māraṇe proktā jāraṇe ca niyāmane  || 9.21 ||
<mahauṣadhyaḥ>
aṣṭaṣaṣṭirmahauṣadhyaḥ procyante rasaśāstrataḥ  |
ṛddhiḥ śoṣiṇyadhoguptā śrāvaṇī sārivā tathā  || 9.22 ||
jyotiṣmatī tejavatī rāsnā bākucī bimbikā  |
viṣāṇikā cāśvagandhā varṣābhūḥ śarapuṣpikā  || 9.23 ||

dravantī nīlinī caiva śatapuṣpā prasāraṇī  || 9.24 ||

vārṣikī śālmalī jātī grīṣmavarṣā tu yūthikā  || 9.28 ||
kekicūḍājagandhā ca lakṣmaṇā taruṇīti ca  |
aṣṭaṣaṣṭiriti proktā mahauṣadhyo mahābalāḥ  || 9.29 ||
<siddhauṣadhyaḥ>

devīlatā kālavarṇī vijayāsurī siṃhikā  || 9.30 ||
pālāśatilakā kṣetrī saṃvīrā tāmravallikā  |
nāhī kanyā tathā soma- rājikā ca ṭuṭumbhaṭī  || 9.31 ||
kuberākṣī gṛdhranakhī parpaṭī chidralambikā  |
kṣutkārī dugdhikā bhṛṃgī gaṅgeṭī śarapuṅkhikā  || 9.32 ||
aṣṭāvallī rājaśamī panasī ca jayantikā  |
viṣakharparikāvantī kākāṇḍolāmbumūlikā  || 9.33 ||
siddheśvarī haṃsapādī khoṭakā śṛṅgarīṭikā  |
adhaḥpuṣpī madhurākhyā śṛṅkhalā gṛñjanīti ca  || 9.34 ||
jārāvalī mahārāṣṭrī sahadeveśvarī tathā  |
kāṣṭhagodhāmatī deva- gāndhārī rajanīṅgudī  || 9.35 ||
palāśinī nākulī ca kāmbojikāśvinī tathā  |
cakravallī sarpadaṃṣṭrā śallakī rohitā tathā  || 9.36 ||
tauvarī vaṅgajā rāja- padmā jambīravallikā  |
gajapippalikā bhṛṃga- vallī caivārkavallikā  || 9.37 ||
jantukārī śigruvallī karavīrā śivāṭikā  |
nārācī kāñcanī cāja- gandhā sūtendrasiddhidāḥ  || 9.38 ||
aṣṭaṣaṣṭiriti proktāḥ siddhauṣadhyo rasādhikāḥ  |
sarvakāryakarā deha- lohasiddhipradāyakāḥ  || 9.39 ||

nimbūrasena saṃmardya piṣṭīṃ kṛtvā prayatnataḥ  || 11.91 ||
kāṃjikena tu tāṃ piṣṭīṃ stambhayedvāsaratrayam  |
sārayedbaṃgamadhye tu sūtakaṃ tadanaṃtaram  || 11.92 ||
sāritaṃ sūtakaṃ tena tālasattvena sādhayet |
tena vedhyaṃ drutaṃ tāmraṃ ṣoḍaśāṃśena yatnataḥ  || 11.93 ||
jāyate pravaraṃ tāraṃ caṃdranakṣatrasannibham  || 11.94 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (7)>
sūtakasya trayo bhāgā baṃgaṃ bhāgadvayaṃ tathā  |
mardayeddinamekaṃ tu kāṃjikena samanvitam  || 11.95 ||
sarvebhyastriguṇenātha sumbalena pramardayet |
snuhyarkadugdhena samaṃ bhāvayedvāsaratrayam  || 11.96 ||
yavapramāṇāṃ guṭikāṃ ravitāpena śoṣitām  |
kācakūpyāṃ nidhāyātha vahniṃ kuryātprayatnataḥ  || 11.97 ||
yāmaṣoḍaśaparyaṃtaṃ vālukāyaṃtrake pacet |
svāṃgaśītaṃ samuddhṛtya cordhvagaṃ satvamāharet || 11.98 ||
ṣoḍaśāṃśena śulbaṃ hi kuntavedhena vedhayet |
jāyate pravaraṃ tāraṃ haṭṭavikrayayogyakam  || 11.99 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (8)>
palāṣṭamātraṃ tālaṃ tu dvikarṣapramitaṃ rasam  |
nimbūrasena saṃmardyaṃ vāsaraikaṃ prayatnataḥ  || 11.100 ||
paścāttaṃ mardayeddhīmān tailenairaṇḍajena vai  |
vālukāyantramadhyasthaṃ pacedyāmāṃstu ṣoḍaśa  || 11.101 ||
paścātsattvaṃ samuddhṛtya mardayedekavāsaram  |
atasītilatailena kācakūpyāṃ nidhāpayet || 11.102 ||
pūrvavatpācayedvahnau svāṃgaśītaṃ samuddharet |
anenaiva prakāreṇa punarevaṃ tu kārayet || 11.103 ||
kūpītalasthitaṃ sattvaṃ grāhyaṃ cetpravaraṃ sadā  |
ṣoḍaśāṃśena śulbasya vedhaṃ kuryānna saṃśayaḥ  || 11.104 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (9)>
pāradaṃ ṭaṃkamānaṃ tu lavaṇaṃ dviguṇaṃ tathā  |
khalve vimardayettāvadyāvannaṣṭo raso bhavet || 11.105 ||
tāmraṃ gadyāṇakaṃ śuddhaṃ baṃgaṃ vallamitaṃ kuru  |
drute tāmre'tha lavaṇaṃ sūtakena samanvitam  || 11.106 ||
māṣamātraṃ pradātavyaṃ caturthāṃśena rūpyakam  |
tadrūpye marditaṃ sūtaṃ kṣeptavyaṃ jalayantrake  || 11.107 ||
tattrayodaśakaṃ rūpyaṃ jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 11.108 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (10)>
tālena nihataṃ baṃgaṃ tadbaṃgena tu rūpyakam  |
pacedyāmāṣṭakaṃ samyakkalka evaṃ prajāyate  |
vallaṃ gadyāṇake dadyādbaṃgaṃ stambhayate dhruvam  || 11.109 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (11)>
daradaṃ khaṇḍaśaḥ kṛtvā ṭaṃkatrayamitaṃ pṛthak |
vajrīkṣīreṇa tatsvedyaṃ dolāyaṃtreṇa vārtikaiḥ  || 11.110 ||
tāracūrṇaṃ samaṃ lepyaṃ luṅgatoyasamaṃ tathā  |
govaraiḥ pācayetsvalpameva dvādaśayāmakam  || 11.111 ||
piṣṭistambho bhavettena paścāttārarajaḥ pṛthak |
kuryāddaradakhaṇḍena samaṃ sīsaṃ ca dāpayet || 11.112 ||
gālayenmūṣikāmadhye śītaṃ kṛtvā tu khoṭakam  |
bhasma mūṣopari nyasya dhmāpayecca śanaiḥ śanaiḥ  || 11.113 ||
śuddhaśaṅkhanibhaṃ rūpyaṃ niṣkamātraṃ hi niḥsaret || 11.114 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (12)>
dvipalaṃ śuddhasūtaṃ ca dvipalaṃ rasakasya ca  |
tālakaṃ ca paladvaṃdvaṃ surmilaṃ yugmamātrakam  || 11.115 ||
kūpyāmāropayetsarvaṃ mukhaṃ tāmreṇa rundhayet |
vālukāyantrake samyakpaceddvādaśayāmakam  || 11.116 ||
kūpīmukhe tu yallagnaṃ sattvaṃ grāhyaṃ prayatnataḥ  |
śulbe ṣoḍaśavedhena kārayedrajataṃ varam  || 11.117 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (13)>
sūtakaṃ palamekaṃ tu śaṃkhābhaṃ surmilaṃ palam  |
eraṇḍataile ghṛṣṭaṃ taddhāritaṃ kharpare vare  || 11.118 ||
andhitaṃ tāmrapātreṇa mudritaṃ sudṛḍhaṃ kṛtam  |
paścāccullyāṃ samāropya vahniṃ kuryācchanaiḥ śanaiḥ  || 11.119 ||
sārdhaṃ yāmaṃ tataḥ pācyaṃ svāṃgaśītaṃ samuddharet |
tāmrapātre tu yallagnaṃ sarvaṃ sattvaṃ samāharet || 11.120 ||
ghṛtāktaṃ ṭaṃkaṇopetaṃ gālitaṃ mūṣikāmukhe  |
deyaṃ tadvallamātraṃ hi drute tāmre tu sattvakam  || 11.121 ||
śaṃkhābhaṃ jāyate tāraṃ nātra kāryā vicāraṇā  || 11.122 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (14)>
tālaṃ tāmraṃ rītighoṣaṃ samāṃśaṃ kuryādevaṃ gālitaṃ ḍhālitaṃ hi  |
amle varge saptavāraṃ praḍhālya paścādyojyaṃ tulyabhāge ca rūpye  |
śuddhaṃ rūpyaṃ ṣoḍaśākhyaṃ hi samyakjātaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ nānṛtaṃ satyametat || 11.123 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (15)>
śvetaṃ sauvīrakaṃ śuddhaṃ pācitaṃ viṣamuṣṭinā  |
svacche sūtavare vallaṃ nikṣiptaṃ rūpyakṛdbhavet || 11.124 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (16)>
śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaṃ surmilaṃ dṛśyate kvacit |
mṛtkharpare pācitaṃ hi nimbūkadravasaṃyutam  || 11.125 ||
ghaṭikādvayamānena śuddhakalkaḥ prajāyate  |
catuḥṣaṣṭyaṃśamānena vedhayecchulbakaṃ śubham  || 11.126 ||
jāyate pravaraṃ tāraṃ sarvadoṣavivarjitam  || 11.127 ||
<raupyakaraṇavidhiḥ (17)>
saptadhātumayī mūṣā kṣārabhasmaprapūritā  |
kadalyāḥ kṣārakeṇaiva tathāpāmārgasaṃbhavaiḥ  || 11.128 ||
madhye pāradakaṃ muktvā punarevaṃ prapūrayet |
anena vidhinā pūryā dvitīyā mūṣikā śubhā  || 11.129 ||
mudritavyā prayatnena govare puṭake nyaset |
sūtakaṃ bandhamāyāti vaṅgābhaṃ tu prajāyate  |
drutadrāvaṃ ghātasahaṃ dṛṣṭamevaṃ mayā khalu  || 11.130 ||
<kṛtrimamauktikakaraṇam>
netrāṇyāhṛtya matsyānāṃ paktvā dugdhena yāmakam  |
paścādākṛṣṇakaṇakānākṛṣya kila kaṇḍayet || 11.131 ||
tāni śālisametāni tāvacchubhrāṇi kārayet |
paścādiṣṭikacūrṇena haste kṛtvā pramardayet || 11.132 ||
mauktikāni hi jāyante kṛtānyevaṃ mayā khalu  || 11.133 ||
<sūkṣmamauktikebhyo bṛhanmauktikakaraṇam>
mūṣikāṃ kārayecchuddhāṃ sphāṭikīṃ dahanopalām  |
mauktikāni tu sūkṣmāṇi nimbūdrāve nidhāpayet || 11.134 ||
ahorātreṇa sarvāṇi navanītasamāni ca  |
tasya paṃkasya guṭikāṃ masṛṇāṃ tu prakārayet || 11.135 ||
paścāttāṃ mūṣikāmadhye citrāgharme dviyāmakam  |
catvāri kāṃsyabhāṇḍāni caturdikṣu gatāni ca  || 11.136 ||
arbhakāḥ pātayetsarvāḥ madhyabhājanakopari  |
badhyate mauktikaṃ śreṣṭha- taraṃ sarvaguṇairyutam  || 11.137 ||
<kṛtrimapravālakaraṇam>
śuddhaśaṅkhasya cūrṇaṃ hi sūkṣmaṃ kṛtvā prayatnataḥ  |
ardhabhāgaṃ ca daradaṃ cūrṇayenmatimāṃstataḥ  || 11.138 ||
sadyaḥ sūtāvikakṣīraṃ tena dugdhena mardayet |
vartiṃ vidhāya matimān kārpāsāsthiṣu svedayet || 11.139 ||
svāṃgaśītaṃ samuttārya pravālaṃ ruciraṃ bhavet || 11.140 ||

adhyāya 12

<vānarī vaṭī>
ātmaguptāphalaṃ śuṣkaṃ nistuṣaṃ cāṣṭapālikam  |
māṣasyāṣṭapalaṃ tadvajjalena paripeṣitam  || 12.1 ||
ārdraṃ kṛtvobhayaṃ samyakśilāpaṭṭena peṣayet |
kuṃkumaṃ kesaraṃ caiva jātīpatraṃ śatāvarī  || 12.2 ||
gokṣurekṣurabījāni lavaṃgaṃ maricaṃ kaṇā  |
śṛṅgāṭakaṃ karṣamitaṃ kuryādevaṃ pṛthakpṛthak || 12.3 ||
sūkṣmacūrṇaṃ vidhāyātha pūrvapiṣṭe nidhāpayet |
vaṭakān kārayetpaścātkarṣamātrān vipācayet || 12.4 ||
ghṛtaprasthatrayeṇaiva sutalathya nimajjayet |
mākṣike ghṛtamāne vai mukhaṃ rundhyāddinatrayam  || 12.5 ||
madhvājyamiśritaṃ bhuñjyādekaikaṃ vaṭakaṃ prage  |
saptakāni ca pañcaivamāhāraṃ madhuraṃ bhajet || 12.6 ||
dugdhaudanaṃ tathā rātrau kṣāramamlaṃ ca varjayet |
retaḥkṣayī tathā klībo gacchecca pramadāśatam  || 12.7 ||
aputraḥ putramāpnoti ṣaṇḍho'pi puruṣāyate  |
dṛṣṭapratyayayogo'yaṃ satyametadudīritam  || 12.8 ||
<śatāvaryādivājīkaro'valehaḥ (1)>
śatāvarīṃ kṣīravidārikāṃ ca prasthārdhamānāṃ pṛthageva kuryāt |
rasaṃ tathā śālmalimadhyamūlātprasthaṃ sitārdhāḍhakamatra deyam  || 12.9 ||
supācitaṃ vai mṛduvahninā tathā darvīpralepo'pi hi jāyate yathā  |
tvakpatrakailāḥ saha kesareṇa palapramāṇā hi tato vidadhyāt || 12.10 ||
lehe suśīte madhu bilvamātraṃ prātaḥ prabhakṣediha karṣamātram  || 12.11 ||
<śṛṅgātakādivājīkaro'valehaḥ (2)>
śṛṅgāṭakasyāpi palaṃ vidheyaṃ vārāhikandaśca palapramāṇaḥ  |
cūrṇīkṛtaṃ gālitameva vastrādbhṛṣṭaṃ tathājyena sitāsametam  || 12.12 ||
lavaṃgakṛṣṇāgarukeśarāṇāṃ palaṃ pradadyāddaśabhāgadugdham  |
lehaṃ sujātaṃ khalu bhakṣayettatkarṣapramāṇaṃ nitarāṃ prabhāte  |
kāmasya bodhaṃ kurute hi śīghraṃ nārīṃ ramedvai caṭakāyate'sau  || 12.13 ||
<kāmadīptikaravājīkaro'valehaḥ (3)>
śatāvarīgokṣuradarbhamūlaṃ śṛṅgāṭakaṃ nāgabalātmagupte  |
saṃcūrṇya sarvaṃ pṛthageva pālikaṃ kṣīreṇa pācyaṃ daśabhāgakena  || 12.14 ||
sitā pradeyā daśapālikātra pākaṃ vidadhyādapi cāgniyogāt |
madhuplutaṃ bhakṣitamardhayāmātkāmapradīptiṃ kurute sadaiva  || 12.15 ||
vīryasya vṛddhiṃ jaṭharāgnivṛddhiṃ kāmāgnivṛddhiṃ sahasā karoti  || 12.16 ||
<māṣādivājīkaro'valehaḥ (4)>
māṣāṇāmātmaguptāyā bījānāmāḍhakaṃ navam  |
jīvakarṣabhakau jīvāṃ medāṃ vṛddhiṃ śatāvarīm  || 12.17 ||
madhukaṃ cāśvagandhāṃ ca sādhayetprasṛtonmitām  |
rase tasminghṛtaprasthaṃ gavyaṃ daśaguṇaṃ payaḥ  || 12.18 ||
vidārīsvarasaprasthaṃ prasthamikṣurasasya ca  |
dattvā mṛdvagninā sādhyaṃ siddhasarpirnidhāpayet || 12.19 ||
śarkarāyāstugākṣīryāḥ kṣaudrasya ca pṛthakpṛthak |
bhāgāṃścatuṣpalāṃstatra pippalyāścāvapetpalam  || 12.20 ||
palaṃ pūrvamito līḍhvā tato'nnamupayojayet |
yadīcchedakṣayaṃ śukraṃ śephasaścottamaṃ balam  || 12.21 ||

adhyāya 13

<vīryastambhakarī vaṭī>
śrīvāsamastakīnāga- kesaraṃ ca lavaṃgakam  |
kaṃkolaṃ tulasībījaṃ khurāsānyahīphenakam  || 13.1 ||
jāvitrikābdhiśoṣaṃ ca karabhāgurukuṃkumam  |
kaṅkolakatugākṣīrī- jātīphalasamāṃśakān  || 13.2 ||
sarvāṇyevaṃ vicūrṇyātha nālikerodare kṣipet |
dugdhamadhye vipācyainaṃ dinānyevaṃ hi pañca ca  || 13.3 ||
nālikeraphalādgrāhyaṃ mardayenmadhunā saha  |
guṭikā kolamātrā hi bhakṣaṇīyā niśāmukhe  || 13.4 ||
vīryastaṃbhaṃ karotyugraṃ caturyāmāvadhiṃ tathā  || 13.5 ||
<śukrastambhakarī vaṭī>
lavaṃgaṃ śuddhakarpūraṃ jātīpatraṃ phalaṃ tathā  |
kuṅkumaṃ svarṇabījaṃ ca dhūrtaparṇaṃ prasūnakam  || 13.6 ||
mūlaṃ tvakcābdhiśoṣasya sarvāṇyekatra mardayet |
godugdhe śodhanīyaṃ ca bhāgamekaṃ prakalpayet || 13.7 ||
bhṛṅgīpattrabhavaṃ cūrṇaṃ bhāgaikaṃ svarṇagairikam  |
bhāvanāṃ postatoyena ekaviṃśatisaṃkhyayā  || 13.8 ||
kārayenmatimān vaidyaḥ śukrastaṃbhakarīṃ vaṭīm  || 13.9 ||
<retaḥstambhakarī vaṭī>
jātīphalārkakarahāṭalavaṅgaśuṇṭhīkaṅkolakeśarakaṇāharicandanāni etaiḥ samānamahiphenamanena cābhraṃ śvetaṃ nidhāya madhunā vaṭakān vidadhyāt |
māṣadvayonmitamamuṃ niśi bhakṣayitvā mṛṣṭaṃ payastadanu māhiṣamāśu pītvā kurvantu kāmukajanāḥ pratiruddhapātāścetāṃsi tāni cakitāni kalāvatīnām  || 13.10 ||
<vīryastambhakarī lepavaṭī>
skandhadeśācca saṃjātaṃ vīryaṃ dardurasaṃbhavam  |
ghanasāreṇa saṃyuktaṃ karahāṭasya cūrṇakam  || 13.11 ||
saṃmardya kārayeccūrṇaṃ vaṭīṃ mudgapramāṇakām  |
saṃgharṣya mukhatoyena liṃgalepaṃ prakārayet || 13.12 ||
yāmārdhaṃ dhārayedbinduṃ satyaṃ guruvaco yathā  || 13.13 ||
<śukrastambhakacūrṇam>
postakaṃ palamekaṃ vai śuṃṭhīkarṣaḥ sitā palaikā ca  |
karṣamitā tvakpayasā pītaṃ reto dhruvaṃ dhatte  || 13.14 ||
śrīgaṅgādharabhaktisaktamanaso vidyāvinodāmbudheḥ śrīgoḍānvayapadmanābhasudhiyastasyātmajenāpyayam  |
sadvaidyena yaśodhareṇa kavinā vidvajjanānandakṛdgrantho'yaṃ grathitaḥ karotu satataṃ saukhyaṃ satāṃ mānase  || 13.15 ||
deśānāṃ surarāṣṭramuttamatamaṃ tatrāpi jīrṇābhidhaḥ prākāro'sti sa vedaśāstraniratairvipraiśca saṃśobhitaḥ  |
tasmin śaṃbhupadāravindaratikṛcchrīpadmanābhaḥ svayaṃ tatputreṇa yaśodhareṇa kavinā graṃthaḥ svayaṃ nirmitaḥ  || 13.16 ||
saṃbodhāya satāṃ sukhāya sarujāṃ śiṣyārthasaṃsiddhaye vaidyānāmupajīvanāya viduṣāmudveganāśāya vai  |
śrīmaddurgapurātane'tinipuṇaḥ śrīpadmanābhātmajaḥ śrīmadbhaṭṭayaśodharaḥ kavivaro granthaḥ svayaṃ nirmame  || 13.17 ||
