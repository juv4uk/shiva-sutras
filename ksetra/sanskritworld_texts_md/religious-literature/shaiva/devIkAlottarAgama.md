sarveṣāmapi muktyarthaṃ muktimārgasya darśanam  |
deveśa jñānamācāraṃ kṛpayā kathayasva me  || 1 ||
īśvara uvāca
jñānācārau varārohe kathayāmi tavādhunā  |
praviśanti yato mokṣaṃ jñānino dhvastakalmaṣam  || 2 ||
yeṣāṃ bodhena saṃjātaṃ kālajñānaṃ varānane  |
na teṣāṃ jāyate bodhaḥ śāstrakoṭiśatairapi  || 3 ||
ato hi nirbhayo vidvānniḥśaṅko vigataspṛhaḥ  |
jñānotsāhaparo bhūyātśraddaddhāno nirākulaḥ  || 4 ||
nirmamaḥ karuṇopetaḥ sarvabhūtābhayapradaḥ  |
bhajetkālottaraṃ devi mumukṣuryogatatparaḥ  || 5 ||
sa brahmā sa śivo viṣṇuḥ sa indraḥ sa ṣaḍānanaḥ  |
sa guruḥ sarvadevāśca sa yogī sa tapodhanaḥ  || 6 ||
paṇḍitaḥ sa mahābhāgaḥ kṛtārthaḥ paramārthataḥ  |
caladvāyusamaṃ cittaṃ dhriyate yena niścalam  || 7 ||
sa upāyo mimokṣasya sadupāttaguṇastu saḥ  |
sā prajñā tadiha sthairyaṃ tatpuṇyaṃ vyavasāyinām  || 8 ||
tadeva tīrthaṃ dānaṃ ca sa tapaśca na saṃśayaḥ  |
yenopāyena badhyeta vāyubhiścalanaṃ manaḥ  || 9 ||
citte calati saṃsāro niścalo mokṣa eva tu  |
tasmāccittaṃ sthiraṃ kuryātprajñayā parayā budhaḥ  || 10 ||
ekāntikaṃ sukhaṃ yatra tathāivātyantikaṃ bhavet |
niṣkarmaṇi pare tattve ko na rajyeta paṇḍitaḥ  || 11 ||
nivṛtto viṣayajñānātniṣkalajñānatatparaḥ  |
anicchannapi medhāvī labhate mokṣamakṣayam  || 12 ||
asmitākalayā yuktaṃ caitanyaṃ sakalaṃ smṛtam  |
asmitārahitaṃ cetaścaitanyaṃ śaktirucyate  || 13 ||
tathā prakāśitaṃ viśvaṃ śaktidhyānamudāhṛtam  |
sarvālambavinirmuktaṃ niṣkalaṃ jñānamucyate  || 14 ||
ahamaṃśena yacchūnyaṃ cinmātrālokamadvayam  |
muktibījaṃ tadākhyātaṃ parayogapravartakam  || 15 ||
cakrāṇi nāḍayaḥ padma- devatābījamaṇḍalam  |
rūpamityādikaṃ kiñciddhyeyaṃ naiva kadācana  || 16 ||
kuhakaṃ mantrajālaṃ ca prāṇāyāmādi dhāraṇam  |
sarvametanna kartavyaṃ mokṣamakṣayamicchatā  || 17 ||
nātra pūjā namaskāro na japo dhyānameva ca  |
kevalaṃ jñānamityuktaṃ veditavyaṃ na kiñcana  || 18 ||
bahirāhitacittānāṃ jāyante bandhahetavaḥ  |
bahiścittaṃ nivāryaiva vindan loke na sīdati  || 19 ||
nātra kiñcidbahirnāntaṃ na madhyaṃ nāpyadhaḥ kvacit |
sarvākāraṃ nirākāraṃ svasaṃvedyaṃ virājate  || 20 ||
yadyadālokya yo jantuḥ kurute karmasañcayam  |
tadgatirjāyate yasmānnirālokaṃ tu cintayet || 21 ||
heturnāsti phalaṃ nāsti nāsti karma svabhāvataḥ  |
asadbhūtamidaṃ sarvaṃ nāsti loko na laukikaḥ  || 22 ||
nirālambamidaṃ sarvaṃ nirālambaprakāśitam  |
nirālambamidaṃ kṛtvā nirālambo bhaviṣyati  || 23 ||
vyomākāraṃ mahāśūnyaṃ vyāpakaṃ yo na bhāvayet |
saṃsārī sa bhavelloke bījakośakrimiryathā  || 24 ||
jñānotpattinimittaṃ tu kriyāścaryāḥ prakīrtitāḥ  |
yogaṃ sālambanaṃ tyaktvā niṣprapañcaṃ vicintayet || 25 ||
pātālātśaktiparyantaṃ sarvametadabhīpsitam  |
bhagnaṃ yaiḥ śūnyamantreṇa te smṛtāḥ śūnyavedinaḥ  || 26 ||
viṣaye lolupaṃ cittaṃ markaṭādapi cañcalam  |
sarvaśūnyapade sthitvā tato nirvāṇameṣyati  || 27 ||
sarvatattvādyasambhinnaṃ dehādbhinnaṃ tathaiva ca  |
ahamasmādyasambhinnaṃ caitanyaṃ sarvatomukham  || 28 ||
ākāśamiva sarvaṃ tu sabāhyābhyantaraṃ priye  |
parānandamarūpaṃ tu paśyannānandabhāgbhavet || 29 ||
nirindhano yathā vahniḥ svayameva praśāmyati  |
grāhyābhāvānmanastadvatsvayameva pralīyate  || 30 ||
mohikā mūrcchikā māyā svapnaśceti caturvidhaḥ  |
suṣuptirjāgṛtiścaiva sarvametatparityajet || 31 ||
dehātsūkṣmagatātprāṇāccittādbuddherahaṅkṛteḥ  |
sarvasmādbhinna evāhaṃ cintayan labhate citam  || 32 ||
sadābhibhūtaye cittaṃ nidrayā smaraṇādinā  |
bodhayitvā prayatnena kuryātsvasthaṃ punaḥ punaḥ  || 33 ||
yadā sthiraṃ bhaveccittaṃ cālayanna kathaṃcana  |
na kiñciccintayettatra sthirameva tu kārayet || 34 ||
āśrayālambanaṃ cittaṃ tadvatkuryānnirāśrayam  |
cañcalaṃ niścalaṃ kuryātniścalaṃ na tu cālayet || 35 ||
sarvabhūtalaye jāte yadyadvyoma sunirmalam  |
tattadrūpaṃ svakaṃ dhyāyedvyāptaṃ caiva tu nirmalam  || 36 ||
tadeva janmasāphalyaṃ pāṇḍityamidameva hi  |
caladvāyusamaṃ cittaṃ niścalaṃ dhriyate hi yat || 37 ||
naivordhvaṃ dhārayeccittaṃ na madhyaṃ nāpyadhaḥ kvacit |
antarbhāvavinirmuktaṃ sadā kuryānnirāśrayam  || 38 ||
nidrāyāṃ bodhayeccittaṃ vikṣiptaṃ śamayetpunaḥ  |
pakṣadvayaparityāge samprāpte naiva cālayet || 39 ||
nirāśrayaṃ sadā cittaṃ sarvālambanavarjitam  |
mano'vasthāvinirmuktaṃ vijñeyaṃ muktilakṣaṇam  || 40 ||
sarvālambanaśūnyaṃ ca dhārayitvā mano hṛdi  |
yajjñānaṃ jāyate spaṣṭaṃ tadabhyāsaparo bhavet || 41 ||
ye dhyāyanti paraṃ śūnyaṃ niṣkalaṃ niravasthitam  |
te yānti paramaṃ sthānaṃ janmamṛtyuvivarjitam  || 42 ||
devā devyastathā cānye dharmādharmau ca tatphalam  |
āśrayāśrayivijñānaṃ saṃsārasya ca bandhanam  || 43 ||
āśrayo dvandvamityuktaṃ dvandvatyāgātparodayaḥ  |
jīvanmuktastadā yogī dehatyāgādvimucyate  || 44 ||
vairāgyeṇa vapustyāgo na vai kāryo manīṣiṇā  |
ārambhataḥ kriyānāśe svayameva vipatsyate  || 45 ||
hṛtsaroje hyahaṃrūpā yā citirnirmalācalā  |
ahaṅkāraparityāgātsā citirmokṣadāyinī  || 46 ||
sarvopādhivinirmuktaṃ cidrūpaṃ yannirantaram  |
tacchivo'hamiti dhyātvā sarvāsaktiṃ visarjayet || 47 ||
deśajātyādisambaddhān varṇāśramasamanvitān  |
bhāvānetān parityajya svabhāvaṃ bhāvayedbudhaḥ  || 48 ||
ahameko na me kaścinnāhamanyasya kasyacit |
na taṃ paśyāmi yasyāhaṃ taṃ na paśyāmi yo mama  || 49 ||
ahameva paraṃ brahma jagannātho maheśvaraḥ  |
iti syānniścito mukto baddhaḥ syādanyathā pumān  || 50 ||
aśarīraṃ yadātmānaṃ paśyati jñānacakṣuṣā  |
tadā bhavati śāntātmā sarvato vigataspṛhaḥ  || 51 ||
yo'sau sarveṣu śāstreṣu paṭhyate hyaja īśvaraḥ  |
akāyo nirguṇo hyātmā so'hamasmi na saṃśayaḥ  || 52 ||
vijñāptimātro hi sadā viśuddhaḥ sarvatra yasmātsatataṃ vimuktaḥ  |
nādeyaheyo hyahamapratarkyastasmātsadā brahmamayo viśokaḥ  || 53 ||
āmastakaṃ pādatalāvasānaṃ sāntarbahiścarmapaṭāvanaddham  |
tatkṛtsnamevāmṛtarūpamanyaccidrūpamātmānamananyasiddhim  || 54 ||
īśo'hamevāsya carācarasya pitā ca mātā ca pitāmahaśca  |
dhyānaṃ samāsthāya padaṃ caturthaṃ dhyāyanti māmeva vimuktikāmāḥ  || 55 ||
brahmādibhirdevamanuṣyanāgairgandharvayakṣāpsarasāṃ gaṇaiśca  |
yajñairanekairahameva pūjyo māmeva sarve pratipūjayanti  || 56 ||
tapobhirugrairvividhaiśca dānairmāmeva sarve pratipūjayanti  |
bhūtāni cāhaṃ sthirajaṅgamāni yāvanti cānyānyahameva tāni  || 57 ||
na sthūlasūkṣmo na ca śūnyarūpo jñānaikarūpo jagadekabandhuḥ  |
nirantaro nirmala īśvaro'haṃ svapnādyavasthācyutiniṣprapañcaḥ  || 58 ||
anādivijñānamajaṃ purāṇaṃ guhāśayaṃ niṣkalamaprapañcam  |
nirañjanaṃ niṣpratimaṃ nirīśamadṛśyamagrāhyamacintyarūpam  || 59 ||
sanātanaṃ brahma nirantaraṃ yatpade pade so'hamiti prapaśyet |
yo bhāvatastiṣṭhati niṣprakampaḥ sa brahmabhūto'mṛtatāmupaiti  || 60 ||
jñānamevaṃ varārohe kathitaṃ mokṣasiddhaye  |
ācāraṃ kathyamānaṃ tu sāmprataṃ śṛṇu taṃ mayā  || 61 ||
na snānaṃ na japaḥ pūjā homo naiva ca sādhanam  |
agnikāryādikāryaṃ ca naitasyāsti maheśvari  || 62 ||
niyamo'pi na tasyāsti kṣetrapīṭhe ca sevanam  |
nārcanaṃ pitṛkāryādi tīrthayātrā vratāni ca  || 63 ||
dharmādharmaphalaṃ nāsti na tithirlaukikakriyā  |
santyajetsarvakarmāṇi lokācāraṃ ca sarvaśaḥ  || 64 ||
samayācāraniḥśeṣān kṛtyajātaṃ tu bandhanam  |
saṃkalpaṃ ca vikalpaṃ ca ye cānye kuladharmikāḥ  || 65 ||
siddhīśca vividhākārāḥ pātālādi rasāyanam  |
pratyakṣeṇāpi labhyerannaiva gṛhṇīta sādhakaḥ  || 66 ||
sarve te paśubandhāḥ syuradhomārgapradāyakāḥ  |
etairnāsti parā muktiścidrūpaṃ vyāpakaṃ vinā  || 67 ||
yena kena viśeṣeṇa sarvāvastho'pi yogadhṛk |
kṣetrapīṭhe ca sandehādvarjayedyadi kautukam  || 68 ||
kṛmikīṭapataṅgāśca tathā devi vanaspatīn  |
na nāśayedbudho jīvān paramārthamatiryataḥ  || 69 ||
na mūlotpāṭanaṃ kuryātpattracchedaṃ vivarjayet |
bhūtapīḍāṃ na kurvīta puṣpāṇāṃ ca nikṛntanam  || 70 ||
svayaṃpatitapuṣpaistu kartavyaṃ śivapūjanam  |
māraṇoccāṭanādīni vidveṣastambhane tathā  || 71 ||
jvarabhūtagrahāveśa- vaśyākarṣaṇamohanam  |
na kuryātkṣudrakarmāṇi kāṣṭhapāṣāṇapūjanam  || 72 ||
samo'mitre ca mitre ca samo loṣṭe ca kāñcane  |
abhilāṣo na kartavya indriyārthe kadācana  || 73 ||
ātmārāmo bhavedyogī nirbhayo vigataspṛhaḥ  |
samanindāpraśaṃsaśca sarvabhūtasamastathā  || 74 ||
samadṛṣṭistu kartavyā yathātmani tathā pare  |
vivādaṃ lokagoṣṭhīṃ ca kalahāśca vivarjayet || 75 ||
śāstragoṣṭhīṃ na kurvīta kubhāṣitasubhāṣitān  || 76 ||
īrṣyāṃ paiśunyadambhe ca rāgaṃ mātsaryameva ca  |
kāmakrodhau bhayaṃ śokaṃ tyajetsarvaṃ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ  || 77 ||
sarvadvandvavinirmuktaḥ santataṃ janavarjitaḥ  |
anenaiva śarīreṇa sarvajñaḥ san prakāśate  || 78 ||
jñānenaiva yathā mokṣastathā siddhirnirarthikā  |
tathāpi bhogamicchantaḥ siddhimicchanti sādhakāḥ  || 79 ||
aṇimādiguṇāvāptirjāyatāṃ vā na jāyatām  |
tathāpi mucyate dehī patiṃ vijñāya nirmalam  || 80 ||
pañcabhūtātmako dehaḥ śivastatraiva tiṣṭhati  |
śivādyavaniparyanto loko'yaṃ śaṅkarātmakaḥ  || 81 ||
īdṛśaṃ jñāninaṃ dṛṣṭvā pūjayanti ca ye narāḥ  |
gandhaṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ dhūpaṃ snānaṃ vastraṃ ca bhojanam  || 82 ||
nivedayanti ye kecidvāṅmanaḥkāyakarmabhiḥ  |
tathaiva te vimucyante muktimārgābhikāṅkṣiṇaḥ  || 83 ||
stutinindākarāstasya puṇyapāpe samāpnuyuḥ  |
yajjñānācaraṇaṃ pṛṣṭaṃ tatsarvaṃ kathitaṃ mayā  |
kālajñānaṃ varārohe kimanyatparipṛcchasi  || 84 ||

iti devīkālottarāgamaḥ parisamāptaḥ
