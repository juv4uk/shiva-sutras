akāro vāsudevaḥ syādākāraśca pitāmahaḥ |
pūjāyāmapi māṅgalye ākāraḥ parikīrttitaḥ || 1
ikāro ucyate kāmo lakṣmī rīkāra ucyate |
ukāraḥ śaṅkaraḥ prokta ūkāraścāpi rakṣaṇe || 2
ṛkāro devamātā syādṝkāro danujaprasūḥ |
ḻakāro devayoniḥ syālḹrmātā sadbhirucyate || 3
ekāraḥ kathito viṣṇuraikāraśca maheśvaraḥ |
okārastu bhavedbrahmā aukāro rudra ucyate || 4
aṃ syācca paramaṃ brahma aḥ syāddevo maheśvaraḥ |
kaḥ prajāpatiruddiṣṭo ko vāyuriti śabditaḥ || 5
kaścātmā ca samākhyātaḥ kaḥ prakāśa udāhṛtaḥ |
kaṃ śiro jalamākhyātaṃ kaṃ sukhaṃ ca prakīrtitam || 6
pṛthivyāṃ kuḥ samākhyātā kutsāyāṃ kuḥ prakīrtitaḥ |
khamindriyaṃ samākhyātaṃ khamākāśamudāhṛtam || 7
khaṃ svarge ca samākhyātaṃ khaṃ sarpe ca prakīrtitam |
tathā śvabhre ca khaṃ prāhuḥ khaṃ śūnye ca prakīrttitam || 8
go gaṇapatiruddiṣṭo go gandharvaḥ prakīrttitaḥ |
gaṃ gītaṃ gā ca gāthā syādgaurdhenurgauḥ sarasvatī || 9
gaurmātāpi samuddiṣṭā pṛthivyāṃ gauḥ prakīrttitā |
gho ghaṇṭāyāṃ samākhyātaḥ kiṅkiṇī ghā prakīrttitā || 10
upamā ghā samākhyātā kusvare ghuḥ prakīrttitaḥ |
hanane ghā samākhyātā gandhane ghaḥ prakīrttitaḥ || 11
ṅakāro bhairavaḥ khyāto ṅakāro viṣaye smṛtaḥ |
cakāraścandramāḥ khyātastaskaraśca prakīrttitaḥ || 12
nirmalaṃ chaṃ samākhyātaṃ taraṇī chaḥ prakīrttitaḥ |
chedane chaḥ samākhyāto vidvadbhiḥ śabdakovidaiḥ || 13
vegite jaḥ samākhyāto jaghane jaḥ prakīrttitaḥ |
jetā ca jaḥ samākhyātaḥ prasiddhaiḥ śabdakovidaiḥ || 14
jhaṃjhāvāte jhakāraḥ syānnaṣṭe jhaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ |
ñakāro gāyane prokto ñakāro ghargharadhvanau || 15
ṭaṅkāre ṭaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ṭā ṭo dhvanau ca prakīrtitaḥ |
ṭho maheśvara ākhyātaḥ śūnye ca ṭhaḥ prakīrtitaḥ || 16
bṛhaddhvanau ca ṭhaḥ proktastathā candrasya maṇḍale |
ḍakāraḥ śaṅkaraḥ proktastrāsadhvanyoḥ prakīrtitaḥ || 17
ḍhakāraḥ kīrtitā ḍhakkā nirguṇe ca dhvanāvapi |
ṇakāraḥ kīrtito jñāne nirṇaye'pi prakīrtitaḥ || 18
takāraḥ kathitaścauraḥ kroḍe pucche prakīrtitaḥ |
śiloccaye thakāraḥ syātthakāro bhayarakṣaṇe || 19
daṃ kalatre samākhyātaṃ do dānacchedadhātuṣu |
dhaṃ dhane ca dhaneśe dho dhā dhātari nidarśitaḥ || 20
dhiṣaṇā dhīḥ samākhyātā dhūśca syādbhāracintayoḥ |
nakāraḥ sugate bandhe nuḥ stutau ca prakīrtitaḥ || 21
netā nīśca samākhyātastaraṇau nauḥ prakīrtitā |
nakāraḥ saugate buddhau stuto sūrye ca kīrtitaḥ || 22
naśabdaḥ svāgate bandhau vṛkṣe sūrye ca kīrtitaḥ |
paḥ kuberaḥ samākhyātaḥ paścime ca prakīrtitaḥ || 23
pavane paḥ samākhyātaḥ pāḥ pāne pāśca pātari |
kaphe vāte phakāraḥ syāttathāhvāne prakīrtitaḥ || 24
jhaṃjhāvāte phakāraḥ syādakṣare ca prakīrtitaḥ |
kope phiśca samākhyātastathā niṣphalabhāṣaṇe || 25
vakṣaḥsthale ca baḥ prokto gadāyāṃ samudāhṛtaḥ |
nakṣatraṃ bhaṃ budhaiḥ proktaṃ bhramare bhaḥ prakīrtitaḥ || 26
bhā dīptirapi bhūrbhūmirbhīrbhayaṃ kathitā budhaiḥ |
maḥ śivaścandramā vedhā mā ca lakṣmīḥ prakīrtitā || 27
