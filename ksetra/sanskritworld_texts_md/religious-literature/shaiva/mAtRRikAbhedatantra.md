mātṛkābhedatantra

mātṛkābhedatantra, prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ
kailāsaśikhare ramye nānāratnopaśobhite |
papraccha parayā bhaktyā bhairavaṃ parameśvaram || 1.1
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
tripurāpūjanaṃ nātha svarṇaratnairviśeṣataḥ |
kalikāle svarṇarūpyaṃ guptabhāvaṃ tathā maṇim || 1.2
kenopāyena deveśa svarṇarūpyādi labhyate |
tadvadasva viśeṣeṇa yathā ratnādikaṃ bhavet || 1.3
yannoktaṃ sarvatantreṣu tadvadasva dayānidhe || 1.4
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yathā ratnādikaṃ bhavet |
mattejasā pāradena kiṃ ratnaṃ na hi labhyate || 1.5
tathā sāmudrakeṇaiva suśubhralavaṇena ca |
sambalasya prakāraṃ hi śṛṇu devi prayatnataḥ || 1.6
cīnatantrānusāreṇa pūjayetsiddhakālikām |
athavā pūjayeddevīṃ dakṣiṇāṃ kālikāṃ parām |
kālītantroktavidhinā saptāhaṃ japapūjanam || 1.7
satye caikaṃ tu tretāyāṃ dviguṇaṃ dvāpare trayam |
evaṃ sarvatra jānīyāccaturguṇajapaḥ kalau || 1.8
ānīya bahuyatnena sambalaṃ toladvayam |
vasurādyaṃ śivaṃ cādyaṃ māyābinduvibhūṣitam |
bījatrayaṃ cāṣṭaśataṃ prajapetsambalopari || 1.9
aśītitolakaṃ mānaṃ kṛṣṇadhenusamudbhavam |
dugdhamānīya yatnena cāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet || 1.10
vastrayuktena sūtreṇa dugdhamadhye vinikṣipet |
uttāpaṃ janayeddhīmānmandamandena vahninā || 1.11
bindu vedāntaparyantamardhaśoṣaṃ bhavedyadā |
tadaivottolya taddravyaṃ toyamadhye vinikṣipet || 1.12
tataḥ parīkṣā kartavyā pradadyātpāvakopari |
nirdhūmaṃ vāvake dravyaṃ dṛṣṭvā utthāpya yatnataḥ || 1.13
tatraiva prajapenmantraṃ sarvavandyanavātmakam |
ānīya bahuyatnena śuddhaṃ tāmraṃ manoharam || 1.14
sārdhena tolakaṃ tāmraṃ vahnimadhye vinikṣipet |
yathā vahnistathā tāmraṃ dṛṣṭvā utthāpya yatnataḥ || 1.15
guñjāpramāṇaṃ taddravyaṃ tatkṣaṇādyadi yojayet |
satyaṃ satyaṃ hi girije raupyaṃ bhavati niścitam || 1.16
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
kāraṇaṃ dugdharūpaṃ vā kena rūpeṇa śaṅkaraḥ |
tatprakāraṃ mahādeva kṛpayā vada śaṅkara || 1.17
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
ṭaṅkanamānayeddhīmān tolakaṃ tu catuṣṭayam |
vahniyogena girije lājarūpaṃ cakāra ha || 1.18
āmrapuṣpaṃ taddviguṇaṃ piṣṭvā milanamācaret |
tasyopari japenmantraṃ mahāmāyāṃ hi caṇḍike || 1.19
etattu guṭikāṃ kṛtvā melanaṃ kārayedyadi |
tadaiva dugdharūpaṃ syātsatyaṃ satyaṃ hi śailaje || 1.20
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
gandhahīnaṃ bhavenmadyaṃ kenopāyena śaṅkara |
tatsarvaṃ śrotumicchāmi yadi sneho'sti mā prati || 1.21
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
śivaṃ vahnisamārūḍhaṃ vāmanetravibhūṣitam |
bindunādasamāyuktaṃ gandhamādāya saṃlikhet || 1.22
uhyatāṃ padamuccāryaṃ cāṣṭottaraśataṃ yadi |
prajapetsādhakaśreṣṭho durgandhādivināśanam || 1.23


ṃātṛkābhedatantra, dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīdevyuvāca |
vada īśāna sarvajña sarvatattvavidāṃ vara |
yattvayā kathitaṃ nātha mama saṅge vihārataḥ || 2.1
kathaṃ vā jāyate putraḥ śukrasya katra saṃsthitiḥ |
vardhamānaṃ sadā liṅgaṃ praveśo vā kathaṃ bhavet || 2.2
bhītiyuktā hyahaṃ nātha trāhi māṃ duḥkhasaṅkaṭāt || 2.3
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
maṇipūraṃ mahāpadmaṃ suṣumṇāmadhyasaṃsthitam |
tasya nālena deveśi nābhipadmaṃ manoharam || 2.4
vakratrayasamāyuktaṃ sadā śukravibhūṣitam |
ūrdhvaṃ nālaṃ sahasrāre ataḥ śukravibhūṣitam || 2.5
tasmādeva stanadvandvaṃ vardhamānaṃ dine dine || 2.6
madhyanālaṃ suṣumṇāntaṃ vṛntākāraṃ suśītalam |
āyonyagramadhonālaṃ sadānandamayi śive || 2.7
śṛṇu cārvaṅgi subhage tanmadhye liṅgatāḍanāt |
yadrūpaṃ paramānandaṃ tannāsti bhuvanatraye || 2.8
nābhipadmaṃ tu yadrūpaṃ tacchṛṇuṣva samāhitaḥ |
bindusthānaṃ madhyadeśe sadā padmavirājitam || 2.9
bāhyadeśe cāṣṭapatraṃ caturasraṃ tu tadbahiḥ |
caturdvārasamāyuktaṃ suvarṇābhaṃ savṛttakam || 2.10
tatpatreṇa bhavetpuṣpaṃ vṛntayuktaṃ tripattrakam |
praphulle tu tripatrāre bāhye rudhiradarśanam || 2.11
etanmadhye maheśāni yadi syālliṅgatāḍanam |
padmamadhye gate śukre saṃtatistena jāyate || 2.12
puruṣasya tu yacchukraṃ śakteraktādhiko bhavet |
tadā kanyā bhaveddevi viparītaḥ pumān bhavet || 2.13
ubhayostulyaśukreṇa klībaṃ bhavati niścitam || 2.14
śṛṇu cārvaṅgi subhage puṣpamāhātmyamuttamam |
madhye tacchukrasaṃyoge vardhate taddine dine |
evaṃ diṅmāsasamprāpte tatpuṣpaṃ vṛntasaṃyutam || 2.15
galite parameśāni vyakto bhavati saṃtatiḥ || 2.16
śrīdevyuvāca |
kiṃcidrogādisambhūte kṛmikīṭādisambhave |
tasmajjīvaṃ praṇaśyanti sā nārī jīvyate katham || 2.17
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
asya puṣpasya māhātmyaṃ kiṃ vaktuṃ śakyate mayā |
bindusthānaṃ sahasraṃ tu puṣpamadhye priyaṃvade || 2.18
budbudā yatra tiṣṭhanti tatraiva saṃtatirbhavet |
evaṃ krameṇa deveśi sahasraṃ saṃtatiryadi |
vardhamānaṃ mahāpuṣpaṃ pīḍā kiṃcinna jāyate || 2.19
mayā sārdhaṃ maheśāni vihāraṃ kuru yatnataḥ |
vihāre yo bhavetputro gaṇeśaḥ sa ca kīrtitaḥ || 2.20
apare parameśāni tava putraprasādataḥ |
pṛthivyāṃ jāyate sṛṣṭirnirvighnena yathocitam || 2.21
etacchrutvā tato devi madanānalavihvalā |
śivenāliṅgitā devī śivākāreṇa vai tadā || 2.22


ṃātṛkābhedatantra, tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīdevyuvāca |
sarvatrāhaṃ śrutā nātha bhogaṃ cendriyapuṣṭidam |
bhogena mokṣamāpnoti kathaṃ vadasi yogabhṛt || 3.1
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
bhogena labhate yogaṃ bhogena kulasādhanam |
bhogena siddhimāpnoti bhogena mokṣamāpnuyāt || 3.2
tasmādbhogaṃ sadā kāryaṃ bāhyapūjā yathecchayā |
bhojanasya vidhānaṃ yattacchṛṇuṣva priyaṃvade || 3.3
ādhāre tu yā śaktirbhujagākārarūpiṇī |
ātmā parameśāni tanmadhye vartate sadā || 3.4
bhojanecchā bhavettasmānnirlipto jīvasaṃjñakaḥ |
saiva sākṣādguṇamayo nirguṇo jīva ucyate || 3.5
jīvasya bhojanaṃ devi bhrāntireva na saṃśayaḥ |
guṇayuktā kuṇḍalinī candrasūryāgnirūpiṇī || 3.6
mūlādhārācca tāṃ devīmājihvāntāṃ vibhāvayet |
śodhitānmatsyamāṃsādīn saṃmukhe sthāpayedbudhaḥ || 3.7
mūlamantraṃ samuccārya juhomi kuṇḍalīmukhe |
anena manunā devi pratigrāsaṃ samāharet || 3.8
pratigrāse pareśāni evaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ |
tadaiva brahmarūpo'sau satyaṃ satyaṃ sureśvari || 3.9
bhujyate kuṇḍalī devī iti cintāparo hi yaḥ |
mantrasiddhirbhavettasya jñānasiddhirna cānyathā || 3.10
evaṃ kṛte brahmarūpaḥ śivarūpaḥ svayaṃ hariḥ |
yogasiddhirbhavettasya cāṣṭasiddhirbhaviṣyati || 3.11
śatrubhirdīyate yattu kṛtrimaṃ dāruṇaṃ viṣam |
bhakṣaṇāttatkṣaṇe devi hyamṛtaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ || 3.12
mantreṇa śodhitaṃ dravyaṃ bhakṣaṇādamṛtaṃ bhavet |
yadaiva kālakūṭaṃ tu samudramathane priye || 3.13
tadā cānena manunā tatkṣaṇātkhāditaṃ mayā || 3.14
sarpākārā kuṇḍalinī yā devī paramā kalā |
bhujyate sarparūpeṇa tatraiva dāruṇaṃ viṣam || 3.15
iti te kathitaṃ kānte bhojanasya vidhānakam |
etatsarvaṃ maheśāni goptavyaṃ paśusaṃkaṭe || 3.16
śrīdevyuvāca |
śṛṇu nātha parānanda parāparakulātmaka |
vada me parameśāna homakuṇḍaṃ tu kīdṛśam || 3.17
śrīśiva uvāca |
maṇipūrasya bāhye tu nābhipadmaṃ manoharam |
aṣṭapatraṃ tathā vṛttaṃ tanmadhye kuṇḍadurlabham || 3.18
caturasrādikaṃ devi tatkuṇḍaṃ kāmarūpakam |
sarvakuṇḍasya deveśi vipraḥ kartā vidhīyate || 3.19
vartulaṃ bāhujātasya vaiśyasya cārdhacandrakam || 3.20
trikoṇaṃ pādajātasya homakuṇḍaṃ sureśvari |
evaṃ kuṇḍaṃ maheśāni nālatrayavibhūṣitam || 3.21
ūrdhvanālaṃ sahasrāre parāmṛtavibhūṣitam |
madhyanālaṃ nābhipadme mūlādhāre ca sundari || 3.22
āliṅgāgramadhonālaṃ sadānandamayaṃ śive |
homakuṇḍamidaṃ devi sarvatantre pariṣkṛtam || 3.23
yena homaprasādena sākṣādbrahmamayo bhavet |
viprasya cāhutihomaṃ vijñātavyaṃ catuṣṭayam || 3.24
kṣatriyasya trayaṃ devi vaiśyasya cāhutidvayam |
śūdrasyaikāhutirdevi muktiścāpi caturvidhā || 3.25
mahāmokṣaṃ brāhmaṇasya sāyujyaṃ kṣatriyasya ca |
sārūpyaṃ corujātasya sālokyaṃ śūdrajātiṣu || 3.26
bāhyakuṇḍaṃ bāhyahome eva hi suravandite |
jātibhede kuṇḍabhedaṃ kuryātsādhakasattamaḥ || 3.27
bāhyahome kāmyasiddhirbhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ |
jñānahome mokṣasiddhirlabhate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 3.28
iti te kathitaṃ kānte tantrāṇāṃ sāramuttamam |
na vaktavyaṃ paśoragre śapatho me tvayi priye || 3.29
śrīdevyuvāca |
madyapāne mahāpuṇyaṃ sarvatantre śrutaṃ mayā |
jātibhedaṃ na kathitamidānīṃ tatprakāśaya || 3.30
śrīśaṅkara uvāca |
sarvayajñādhipo vipraḥ saṃśayo nāsti pārvati |
sautrāmaṇyāṃ kulācāre catvāro brāhmaṇādayaḥ || 3.31
brāhmaṇasya mahāmokṣaṃ madyapāne priyaṃvade |
brāhmaṇaḥ parameśāni yadi pānādikaṃ caret || 3.32
tatkṣaṇācchivarūpo'sau satyaṃ satyaṃ hi śailaje || 3.33
toye toyaṃ yathā līnaṃ yathā tejasi tejasam |
ghaṭe bhagne yathākāśaṃ vāyau vāyuryathā priye || 3.34
tathaiva madyapānena brāhmaṇo brahmaṇi priye |
līyate nātra saṃdehaḥ paramātmani śailaje || 3.35
sāyujyādi mahāmokṣaṃ niyuktaṃ kṣatriyādiṣu |
sā nārī mānavī madyapāne devi na saṃśayaḥ || 3.36
sūkṣmasūtre yathā vahnirdehamadhye tathā śivā |
taporūpaṃ bṛhatsūtraṃ pūjārūpaṃ tathā hariḥ || 3.37
saṃyuktaṃ kurute yatra vardhamāno mahāṅkuśaḥ |
madyapānaṃ vinā devi tajjñānaṃ na hi labhyate |
ata eva hi vipreṇa madyapānaṃ sadā caret || 3.38
vedamātājapenaiva brāhmaṇo na hi śailaje |
brahmajñānaṃ yadā devi tadā brāhmaṇa ucyate || 3.39
devānāmamṛtaṃ brahma tadiyaṃ laukikī surā |
suratvaṃ bhogamātreṇa surā tena prakīrtitā || 3.40
mantratrayaṃ sadā pāṭhyaṃ brahmaśāpādi mocanam |
prakuryāttu dvijenaiva tadā brahmamayī surā || 3.41
havirāropamātreṇa vahnirdīpto yathā bhavet |
śāpamocanamātreṇa surā muktipradāyinī || 3.42
ata eva hi deveśi brāhmaṇaḥ pānamācaret |
sa brāhmaṇaḥ sa vedajñaḥ so'gnihotrī sa dīkṣitaḥ || 3.43
bahu kiṃ kathyate devi sa eva triguṇātmakaḥ || 3.44
muktimārgamidaṃ devi goptavyaṃ paśusaṃkaṭe |
prakāśātkāyahāniḥ syānnindanīyo na cānyathā || 3.45


ṃātṛkābhedatantra, caturthaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
kāraṇena mahāmokṣaṃ nirmālyena śivasya ca |
śrutaṃ vede purāṇe ca tava vaktre sureśvara || 4.1
agrāhyaṃ tava nirmālyamagrāhyaṃ kāraṇaṃ vibho |
mṛṣā vākyaṃ mahādeva kathaṃ vadasi yogabhṛt || 4.2
kāraṇena vinā devi mokṣajñānādikaṃ na hi |
mahāśaṅkhaṃ vinā devi na mantraḥ siddhidāyakaḥ || 4.3
sākṣādbrahmamayī mālā mahāśaṅkhākhyayā punaḥ |
śilāyantre ca vṛndāyāṃ gaṅgāyāṃ surapūjite |
naiva spṛśenmahāśaṅkhaṃ sparśanātkāṣṭhavadbhavet || 4.4
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
gaṅgā tu kāraṇaṃ vāri madyaṃ paramakāraṇam |
kāraṇasparśamātreṇa mālāḥ śuddhā bhavanti hi || 4.5
gaṅgāsparśena deveśa kāṣṭhavanmālikā katham |
vada me parameśāna iti me saṃśayo hṛdi || 4.6
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
kāraṇaṃ devadeveśi mokṣadaṃ sarvajātiṣu |
tathā svargādijanakaṃ gaṅgātoyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ || 4.7
kāraṇe nivaseddevi mahākālī parā kalā |
mahāvidyā vasennityaṃ surāyāṃ parameśvari || 4.8
mahāśaṅkhe vasennityaṃ pañcāśadvarṇarūpiṇī |
mahāvidyā vasennityaṃ mahāśaṅkhe ca sarvadā || 4.9
gaṅgāsparśanamātreṇa gaṅgāyāṃ līyate priye |
kāṣṭhasparśanamātreṇa kāṣṭhe vahnistṛṇe yathā || 4.10
gaṅgāsparśe tathā devi gaṅgāyāṃ līyate priye |
tatkṣaṇe ca mahāśaṅkhaḥ kāṣṭhavannātra saṃśayaḥ || 4.11
śilāyantre tulasyādau tathaiva parameśvari || 4.12
mahāśaṅkhākhyamālāyāṃ yo japetsādhakottamaḥ |
aṣṭasiddhiḥ kare tasya sa eva śambhuravyayaḥ |
maulau gaṅgā sthitā yasya gaṅgāsnānena tasya kim || 4.13
vārāṇasī kāmarūpaṃ haridvāraṃ prayāgakam |
gaṇḍakī badarikā devi gaṅgāsāgarasaṃgamam || 4.14
yasya bhaktirmahāśaṅkhe tasya darśanamātrataḥ |
tīrthasnānaphalaṃ sarvaṃ labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 4.15
iti te kathitaṃ kānte sarvaṃ paramadurlabham |
na vaktavyaṃ paśoragre prāṇānte parameśvari || 4.16


ṃātṛkābhedatantra, pañcamaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
pāradaṃ bhasmanirmāṇaṃ kenopāyena śaṃkara |
tadahaṃ śrotumicchāmi yadi te'sti kṛpā mayi || 5.1
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
pārade bhasmanirmāṇe nānāvighnāni pārvati |
ata eva hi tatrādau śāntiṃ kuryāddvijottamaḥ || 5.2
varayetkarmakartāraṃ vakṣyamāṇavidhānataḥ |
pūjayetṣoḍaśaliṅgaṃ pārthivaṃ parvatātmaje || 5.3
ṣoḍaśenopacāreṇa toḍaloktavidhānataḥ |
bhogayogyaṃ pradātavyaṃ madhuparkaṃ sureśvari || 5.4
pañcāmṛtena deveśaṃ snāpayecchuddhavāriṇā |
puruṣasya yathāyogyaṃ yugmavastraṃ nivedayet || 5.5
caturaṅgulivistāraṃ raupyanirmāṇapīṭhakam |
alaṃkāraṃ yathāyogyaṃ puruṣasya nivedayet || 5.6
alaktakayutaṃ vāpi dadyānmalayajaṃ śive |
ṣaḍaṅgadhūpaṃ deveśi pradadyācca punaḥ punaḥ || 5.7
ghṛtayuktaṃ tathā dīpaṃ dadyātkalyāṇahetave |
naivedyaṃ vividhaṃ ramyaṃ nānāphalasamanvitam || 5.8
śarkarāsaṃyutaṃ kṛtvā pāyasaṃ vinivedayet |
dadyāttoyaṃ maheśāni vijayāsaṃyutaṃ priye || 5.9
ṣaḍakṣaraṃ mahāmantraṃ gajāntakasahasrakam |
prajapetsādhakaśreṣṭhastataḥ siddho bhaveddhruvam || 5.10
athavā parameśāni dhanadāṃ dhanadāyinīm |
pūjayedbahuyatnena ṣoḍaśenopacārataḥ || 5.11
dvādaśāhvaṃ yajeddhīmān diksahasraṃ tato japet |
taddaśāṃśaṃ maheśāni homaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ || 5.12
homakarmādyaśaktaśceddviguṇaṃ japamācaret |
yadi prītā bhavetsā hi tadā kiṃ vā na sidhyati || 5.13
pratyahaṃ parameśāni kubero dīyate vasu |
bhasmanirmāṇakaṃ devi vicitraṃ tasya kiṃ śive || 5.14
gurave dakṣiṇāṃ dadyādyathāvibhavavistaraiḥ |
tataḥ siddho bhavenmantrī nātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 5.15
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
vidhānaṃ devadeveśa bhasmanirmāṇakarmaṇi |
sakṛtkṛte yena rūpe bhasmasājjāyate vibho || 5.16
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
ānīya pāradaṃ devi sthāpayetprastaropari |
tasyopari japenmantraṃ sarvavandyanavātmakam || 5.17
sāṣṭasahasraṃ deveśi prajapetsādhakāgraṇīḥ |
syayambhupuṣpasaṃyukte vastre cāruṇasaṃnibhe || 5.18
saṃsthāpya pāradaṃ devi mṛtpātrayugale śive |
puṣpayuktena sūtreṇa badhnīyādbahuyatnataḥ || 5.19
muktidhārājalenaiva dhānyasya parameśvari |
lepayedbahuyatnena raudre śuṣkaṃ ca kārayet || 5.20
punaśca lepayeddhīmān tato vahnau vinikṣipet |
aṣṭamīnavamīrātrau kṣipennaiva sureśvari || 5.21

nijeṣṭadevatārūpā dehasaṃsthā ca kuṇḍalī |
bhujyate saiva dehasthā kā cintā sādhakasya ca |
tanme brūhi mahādeva yadyahaṃ tava vallabhā || 14.2
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
bhogastu trividho devi divyavīrapaśukramāt |
nirlipto divyabhāvasthaḥ kuṇḍalī bhujyate yadi || 14.3
ajihvāntā kuṇḍalinī vīrasya vīravandite |
mahādevyāḥ prītaye ca prasādaṃ bhujyate paśuḥ || 14.4
dvijāterdivyabhāvaśca sadā nirvāṇadāyakaḥ |
vipro vīraśca nirvāṇī bhavatyeva na saṃśayaḥ || 14.5
sāyujyādi mahāmokṣaṃ niyuktaṃ kṣatriyādiṣu |
paśunā bhaktiyuktena prasādaṃ bhujyate yadi || 14.6
svargabhogī bhavatyeva maraṇe nādhikāritā |
janmāntaramavāpnoti mahādevyāḥ prasādataḥ || 14.7
divyavīramate dṛṣṭirjāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ |
divyavīraprasādena nirvāṇī nātra saṃśayaḥ || 14.8
prasādabhogī yo devi sa paśurnātra saṃśayaḥ |
maraṇe nādhikāro'sti paśubhāvasthitasya ca || 14.9
naiva muktirbhavettasya janma cāpnoti niścitam || 14.10
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
vada me parameśāna divyavīrasya lakṣaṇam |
yatkṛte divyavīrasya mahāmuktirbhaviṣyati || 14.11
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
sākṣādbrahmamayī devī cābhiśaptā ca vāruṇī |
śāpamocanamātreṇa brahmarūpā sudhā parā || 14.12
nivedanānmahādevyai tattaddevī bhavetkila |
mūlādhārātkuṇḍalinīmājihvāntāṃ vibhāvayet || 14.13
tanmukhe dānamātreṇa jñānavān sādhako bhavet |
yathaiva kuṇḍalī devī dehamadhye vyavasthitā || 14.14
tathaiva vāruṇīṃ dhyāyetkalāṅge sveṣṭadevatām |
kuṇḍalyā samabhāvena śaktivaktre pradāpayet || 14.15
ātmocchiṣṭaṃ mahāpūtaṃ tanmukhātparamāmṛtam |
avaśyameva gṛhṇīyāttādātmyena varānane || 14.16
utsṛṣṭādivicāro'pi kadācinnāsti brahmaṇi |
gaṅgātoyaṃ paraṃ brahma prasādaṃ kasya tadvada || 14.17
gaṅgāsāgaratoyaṃ vā prasādaṃ kasya vā bhavet |
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi tajjale snānamātrataḥ || 14.18
muktibhāgī bhavenmartyaḥ snānāvagāhanātkila |
pādādimastakāntaṃ vai snānakāle pramajjati || 14.19
pādasparśo na doṣāya parabrahmaṇi śailaje |
paramātmani līne ca tathaiva parameśvari || 14.20
iti te kathitaṃ devi divyavīrasya lakṣaṇam |
vīratantre ca kathitaṃ māhātmyaṃ prāṇavallabhe || 14.21
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi sādhikāyāśca lakṣaṇam |
divyaśaktirvīraśaktirguruśaktistathā parā || 14.22
kulaśaktiḥ kāminī ca navaśaktiḥ kumārikā |
śrīguruṃ pūjayedbhaktyā svadehadānapūrvakam || 14.23
anyathā tu svadehasya nigraho jāyate dhruvam |
saptajanmani sā devī pukkasī pativarjitā || 14.24
śivaṃ matvā svakāntaṃ ca pūjāsādhanamācaret |
kadācinna yajeccānyaṃ puruṣaṃ parameśvari || 14.25
anyasya yajanāccaṇḍi sarvanāśo bhaveddhruvam |
kāntasyāyurvihīnatvaṃ vipatiṃ ca pade pade || 14.26
dhananāśo bhavennityaṃ devyāḥ krodhaśca jāyate |
avaśyaṃ pūjayennityaṃ gurudevaṃ sanātanam || 14.27
bhadrābhadravicāraṃ ca yā karoti gurusthale |
tasyā mantraṃ krodhayuktaṃ vipattiśca pade pade || 14.28
varaṃ janamukhānnindā varaṃ prāṇān parityajet |
tathāpi pūjayeddevaṃ sākṣānnirvāṇadāyakam || 14.29
sadā bhayaṃ ca kāpaṭyaṃ varjayedgurupūjane || 14.30
śrīgurostejasaṃ bhaktyā yadi dhāraṇamācaret |
satyaṃ satyaṃ punaḥ satyaṃ kāśī sā nātra saṃśayaḥ || 14.31
abhaktyā parameśāni yadi dhāraṇamācaret |
japapūjādikaṃ tasyāḥ saṃdahettena tejasā || 14.32
śrīcaṇḍikovāca |
sapatnīkaṃ yajeddevaṃ guruṃ nirvāṇadāyakam |
tasya saṅgaṃ parityajya kathamātmaniyojanam || 14.33
śrīśaṃkara uvāca |
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi gurorājñānusārataḥ |
dhārayettejasaṃ bhaktyā svayaṃ lipsāvivarjitā || 14.34
gurupatnyāścātmajaśca śrīgurorātmajo yataḥ |
gurupatnī guruḥ sākṣādguruputro na saṃśayaḥ || 14.35
ekasya pūjanātkānta ubhayoḥ pūjanaṃ bhavet |
guruputro gaṇeśaśca guruputraḥ ṣaḍānanaḥ || 14.36
ekaṃ gurusutaṃ kānte pūjane yā sadā ratā |
anyaṃ gurusutaṃ kānte pūjayenna kadācana || 14.37
vīraṃ vā divyamūrtiṃ vā kadācinna hi pūjayet |
ekasya pūjanāddevi mahāsiddhīśvaro bhavet || 14.38
ubhayostrīṇi catvāri yā nārī pūjanaṃ caret |
tasyāḥ samastaṃ viphalaṃ dhyānādijapapūjanam || 14.39
yadi bhāgyavaśāddevi ekaṃ gurusutaṃ labhet |
manojñaṃ śāstravettāraṃ nigrahānugrahe ratam || 14.40
sundaraṃ yauvanonmattaṃ gurutulyaṃ jitendriyam |
prāṇānte'pi ca kartavyaṃ pūjanaṃ mokṣadāyakam || 14.41
no yajedyadi mohena saiva pāpamayī bhavet || 14.42
