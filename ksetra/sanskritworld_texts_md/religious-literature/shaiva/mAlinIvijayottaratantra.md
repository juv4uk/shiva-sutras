mālinīvijayottaratantram

sṛṣṭyadhikāraḥ prathamaḥ

1.1  jayanti jagadānandavipakṣakṣapaṇakṣamāḥ
1.1  parameśamukhodbhūtajñānacandramarīcayaḥ
1.2  jagadarṇavamagnānāṃ tārakaṃ tārakāntakam
1.2  sanatkumārasanakasanātanasanandanāḥ
1.3  nāradāgastyasaṃvartavasiṣṭhādyā maharṣayaḥ
1.3  jijñāsavaḥ paraṃ tattvaṃ śivaśaktyunmukhīkṛtāḥ
1.4  samabhyarcya vidhānena te tamūcuḥ praharṣitāḥ
1.4  bhagavadyogasaṃsiddhikāṅkṣiṇo vayamāgatāḥ
1.5  sā ca yogaṃ vinā yasmānna bhavettamato vada
1.5  ṛṣibhiryogamicchadbhiḥ sa tairevamudāhṛtaḥ
1.6  pratyuvāca prahṛṣṭātmā namaskṛtya maheśvaram
1.6  śṛṇudhvaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi sarvasiddhiphalapradam
1.7  mālinīvijayaṃ tantraṃ parameśamukhodgatam
1.7  bhuktimuktipradātāramumeśamamarārcitam
1.8  svasthānasthamumā devī praṇipatyedamabravīt
1.8  siddhayogeśvarītantraṃ navakoṭipravistaram
1.9  yattvayā kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ bhedatrayavisarpitam
1.9  mālinīvijaye tantre koṭitritayalakṣite
1.10  yogamārgastvayā proktaḥ suvistīrṇo maheśvara
1.10  bhūyastasyopasaṃhāraḥ prokto dvādaśabhistathā
1.11  sahasraiḥ so'pi vistīrṇo gṛhyate nālpabuddhibhiḥ
1.11  atastamupasaṃhṛtya samāsādalpadhīhitam
1.12  sarvasiddhikaraṃ brūhi prasādātparameśvara
1.12  evamuktastadā devyā prahasyovāca viśvarāṭ
1.13  śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi siddhayogeśvarīmatam
1.13  yanna kasya cidākhyātaṃ mālinīvijayottaram
1.14  mayāpyetatpurā prāptamaghorātparamātmanaḥ
1.14  upādeyaṃ ca heyaṃ ca vijñeyaṃ paramārthataḥ
1.15  śivaḥ śaktiḥ savidyeśā mantrā mantreśvarāṇavaḥ
1.15  upādeyamiti proktametatṣaṭkaṃ phalārthinām
1.16  malaḥ karma ca māyā ca māyīyamakhilaṃ jagat
1.16  sarvaṃ heyamiti proktaṃ vijñeyaṃ vastu niścitam
1.17  etajjñātvā parityajya sarvasiddhiphalaṃ labhet
1.17  tatreśaḥ sarvakṛcchāntaḥ sarvajñaḥ sarvakṛtprabhuḥ
1.18  sakalo niṣkalo'nantaḥ śaktirapyasya tadvidhā
1.18  sa sisṛkṣurjagatsṛṣṭerādāveva nijecchayā
1.19  vijñānakevalānaṣṭau bodhayāmāsa pudgalān
1.19  aghoraḥ paramo ghoro ghorarūpastadānanaḥ
1.20  bhīmaśca bhīṣaṇaścaiva vamanaḥ pivanastathā
1.20  etānaṣṭau sthitidhvaṃsarakṣānugrahakāriṇaḥ
1.21  mantramantreśvare śuddhe saṃniyojya tataḥ punaḥ
1.21  mantrāṇāmasṛjattadvatsaptakoṭīḥ samaṇḍalāḥ
1.22  sarve'pyete mahātmāno mantrāḥ sarvaphalapradāḥ
1.22  ātmā caturvidho jñeyastatra vijñānakevalaḥ
1.23  malaikayuktastatkarmayuktaḥ pralayakevalaḥ
1.23  malamajñānamicchanti saṃsārāṅkurakāraṇam
1.24  dharmādharmātmakaṃ karma sukhaduḥkhādilakṣaṇam
1.24  īśvarecchāvaśādasya bhogecchā saṃprajāyate
1.25  bhogasādhanasaṃsiddhyai bhogecchorasya mantrarāṭ
1.25  jagadutpādayāmāsa māyāmāviśya śaktibhiḥ
1.26  sā caikā vyāpinī sūkṣmā niṣkalā jagato nidhiḥ
1.26  anādyantāśiveśānī vyayahīnā ca kathyate
1.27  asūta sā kalātattvaṃ yadyogādabhavatpumān
1.27  jātakartṛtvasāmarthyo vidyārāgau tato'sṛjat
1.28  vidyā vivecayatyasya karma tatkāryakāraṇe
1.28  rāgo'nurañjayatyenaṃ svabhogeṣvaśuciṣvapi
1.29  niyatiryojayatyenaṃ svake karmaṇi pudgalam
1.29  kālo'pi kalayatyenaṃ tuṭyādibhiravasthitaḥ
1.30  tata eva kalātattvādavyaktamasṛjattataḥ
1.30  guṇānaṣṭaguṇāṃ tebhyo dhiyaṃ dhīto'pyahaṅkṛtim
1.31  tattridhā taijasāttasmānmano'kṣeśamajāyata
1.31  vaikārikāttato'kṣāṇi tanmātrāṇi tṛtīyakāt
1.32  śrotraṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā ghrāṇaṃ buddhīndriyāṇi tu
1.32  karmendriyāṇi vākpāṇipāyūpasthāṅghrayaḥ kramāt
1.33  kalādikṣitiparyantametatsaṃsāramaṇḍalam
1.33  samudrādi jagatkṛtsnaṃ parivartayatīcchayā
1.34  bhedaḥ paraḥ kalādīnāṃ bhuvanatvena yaḥ sthitaḥ
1.34  asṛjattamasāveva bhogināṃ bhogasiddhaye
1.35  ityanena kalādyena dharāntena samanvitāḥ
1.35  pumāṃsaḥ sakalā jñeyāstadavasthājighāṃsubhiḥ
1.36  avasthātritaye'pyasmiṃstirobhāvanaśīlayā
1.36  śivaśaktyā samākrāntāḥ prakurvanti viceṣṭitam
1.37  evaṃ jagati sarvatra rudrāṇāṃ yogyatāvaśāt
1.37  aṅguṣṭhamātrapūrvāṇāṃ śatamaṣṭādaśottaram
1.38  anugṛhya śivaḥ sākṣānmantreśatve niyuktavān
1.38  te svagocaramāsādya bhuktimuktiphalārthinām
1.39  brahmādīnāṃ prayacchanti svabalena samaṃ phalam
1.39  ṛṣibhyaste'pi te cānu manvantebhyo mahādhipāḥ
1.40  heyopādeyavijñānaṃ kathayanti śivoditam
1.40  brahmādistambaparyante jātamātre jagatyalam
1.41  mantrāṇāṃ koṭayastisraḥ sārdhāḥ śivaniyojitāḥ
1.41  anugṛhyāṇusaṃghātaṃ yātāḥ padamanāmayam
1.42  evamasyātmanaḥ kāle kasmiṃścidyogyatāvaśāt
1.42  śaivī saṃbadhyate śaktiḥ śāntā muktiphalapradā
1.43  tatsaṃbandhāttataḥ kaścittatkṣaṇādapavṛjyate
1.43  ajñānena sahaikatvaṃ kasya cidvinivartate
1.44  rudraśaktisamāviṣṭaḥ sa yiyāsuḥ śivecchayā
1.44  bhuktimuktiprasiddhyarthaṃ nīyate sadguruṃ prati
1.45  tamārādhya tatastuṣṭāddīkṣāmāsādya śāṅkarīm
1.45  tatkṣaṇādvopabhogādvā dehapāte śivaṃ vrajet
1.46  yogadīkṣāṃ samāsādya jñātvā yogaṃ samabhyaset
1.46  yogasiddhimavāpnoti tadante śāśvataṃ padam
1.47  anena kramayogena saṃprāptaḥ paramaṃ padam
1.47  na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti śuddhe svātmani tiṣṭhati
1.48  ātmā caturvidho hyeṣa punareṣa caturvidhaḥ
1.48  ācāryatvādibhedena śuddhātmā paripaṭhyate
1.49  nityāditritayaṃ kuryādguruḥ sādhaka eva ca
1.49  nityameva dvayaṃ cānyo yāvajjīvaṃ śivājñayā
1.50  upādeyaṃ ca heyaṃ ca tadetatparikīrtitam
1.50  jñātvaitajjñeyasarvasvaṃ sarvasiddhyaraho bhavet

iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre prathamo'dhikāraḥ samāptaḥ


vyāptyadhikāro dvitīyaḥ

2.1  athaiṣāmeva tattvānāṃ dharādīnāmanukramāt
2.1  prapañcaḥ kathyate leśādyogināṃ yogasiddhaye
2.2  śaktimacchaktibhedena dharātattvaṃ vibhidyate
2.2  svarūpasahitaṃ tacca vijñeyaṃ daśapañcadhā
2.3  śivādisakalātmāntāḥ śaktimantaḥ prakīrtitāḥ
2.3  tacchaktayaśca vijñeyāstadvadeva vicakṣaṇaiḥ
2.4  evaṃ jalādimūlāntaṃ tattvavrātamidaṃ mahat
2.4  pṛthagbhedairimairbhinnaṃ vijñeyaṃ tatphalepsubhiḥ
2.5  anenaiva vidhānena puṃstattvāttu kalāntikam
2.5  trayodaśavidhaṃ jñeyaṃ rudravatpralayākalaḥ
2.6  tadvanmāyāpi vijñeyā navadhā jñānakevalaḥ
2.6  mantrāḥ saptavidhāstadvatpañcadhā mantranāyakāḥ
2.7  tridhā mantreśvareśānāḥ śivaḥ sākṣānna bhidyate
2.7  bhedaḥ prakathito leśādananto vistarādayam
2.8  evaṃ bhuvanamālāpi bhinnā bhedairimaiḥ sphuṭam
2.8  vijñeyā yogasiddhyarthaṃ yogibhiryogapūjitā
2.9  eteṣāmeva tattvānāṃ bhuvanānāṃ ca śāṅkari
2.9  ya ekamapi jānāti so'pi yogaphalaṃ labhet
2.10  yaḥ punaḥ sarvatattvāni vettyetāni yathārthataḥ
2.10  sa gururmatsamaḥ prokto mantravīryaprakāśakaḥ
2.11  dṛṣṭāḥ saṃbhāṣitāstena spṛṣṭāśca prītacetasā
2.11  narāḥ pāpaiḥ pramucyante saptajanmakṛtairapi
2.12  ye punardīkṣitāstena prāṇinaḥ śivacoditāḥ
2.12  te yatheṣṭaṃ phalaṃ prāpya gacchanti paramaṃ padam
2.13  rudraśaktisamāveśastatra nityaṃ pratiṣṭhitaḥ
2.13  sati tasmiṃśca cihnāni tasyaitāni vilakṣayet
2.14  tatraitatprathamaṃ cihnaṃ rudre bhaktiḥ suniścalā
2.14  dvitīyaṃ mantrasiddhiḥ syātsadyaḥpratyayakārikā
2.15  sarvasattvavaśitvaṃ ca tṛtīyaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ smṛtam
2.15  prārabdhakāryaniṣpattiścihnamāhuścaturthakam
2.16  kavitvaṃ pañcamaṃ proktaṃ sālaṃkāraṃ manoharam
2.16  sarvaśāstrārthavettṛtvamakasmāccāsya jāyate
2.17  rudraśaktisamāveśaḥ pañcadhā paripaṭhyate
2.17  bhūtatattvātmamantreśaśaktibhedādvarānane
2.18  pañcadhā bhūtasaṃjñastu tathā triṃśatidhā paraḥ
2.18  ātmākhyastrividhaḥ prokto daśadhā mantrasaṃjñakaḥ
2.19  dvividhaḥ śaktisaṃjño'pi jñātavyaḥ paramārthataḥ
2.19  pañcāśadbhedabhinno'yaṃ samāveśaḥ prakīrtitaḥ
2.20  āṇavo'yaṃ samākhyātaḥ śākto'pyevaṃvidhaḥ smṛtaḥ
2.20  evaṃ śāmbhavamapyebhirbhedairbhinnaṃ vilakṣayet
2.21  uccārakaraṇadhyānavarṇasthānaprakalpanaiḥ
2.21  yo bhavetsa samāveśaḥ samyagāṇava ucyate
2.22  uccārarahitaṃ vastu cetasaiva vicintayan
2.22  yamāveśamavāpnoti śāktaḥ so'trābhidhīyate
2.23  akiṃciccintakasyaiva guruṇā pratibodhataḥ
2.23  jāyate yaḥ samāveśaḥ śāmbhavo'sāvudīritaḥ
2.24  sārdhametacchataṃ proktaṃ bhedānāmanupūrvaśaḥ
2.24  saṃkṣepādvistarādasya parisaṃkhyā na vidyate
2.25  saṃvittiphalabhedo'tra na prakalpyo manīṣibhiḥ
2.25  bhedo'paro'pi saṃkṣepātkathyamāno'vadhāryatām
2.26  jāgratsvapnādibhedena sarvāveśakramo budhaiḥ
2.26  pañcabhinnaḥ parijñeyaḥ svavyāpārātpṛthakpṛthak
2.27  tatra svarūpaṃ śaktiśca sakalaśceti tattrayam
2.27  iti jāgradavastheyaṃ bhede pañcadaśātmake
2.28  akalau dvau parijñeyau samyaksvapnasuṣuptayoḥ
2.28  mantrāditatpatīśānavargasturya iti smṛtaḥ
2.29  śaktiśaṃbhū parijñeyau turyātīte varānane
2.29  trayodaśātmake bhede svarūpamakalāvubhau
2.30  mantramantreśvareśānāḥ śaktiśaṃbhū ca kīrtitau
2.30  pralayākalabhede'pi svaṃ vijñānakalāvubhau
2.31  mantramantreśvareśānāḥ śaktīśāvapi pūrvavat
2.31  navadhā kīrtite bhede svaṃ mantrā mantranāyakāḥ
2.32  tadīśāḥ śaktiśaṃbhū ca pañcāvasthāḥ prakīrtitāḥ
2.32  pūrvavatsaptabhede'pi svaṃ mantreśeśaśaktayaḥ
2.33  śivaśceti parijñeyāḥ pañcaiva varavarṇini
2.33  svaṃ śaktiḥ sanijeśānā śaktiśaṃbhū ca pañcake
2.34  trike svaśaktiśaktīcchāśivapadaṃ vilakṣayet
2.34  svavyāpārādhipatvena taddhīnaprerakatvataḥ
2.35  icchānivṛtteḥ svasthatvādabhinnamapi pañcadhā
2.35  iti pañcātmake bhede vijñeyaṃ vastu kīrtitam
2.36  bhūyo'pyāsāmavasthānāṃ saṃjñābhedaḥ prakāśyate
2.36  piṇḍasthaḥ sarvatobhadro jāgrannāmadvayaṃ matam
2.37  dvisaṃjñaṃ svapnamicchanti padasthaṃ vyāptirityapi
2.37  rūpasthaṃ tu mahāvyāptiḥ suṣuptasyāpi taddvayam
2.38  pracayaṃ rūpātītaṃ ca samyakturyamudāhṛtam
2.38  mahāpracayamicchanti turyātītaṃ vicakṣaṇāḥ
2.39  pṛthaktattvaprabhedena bhedo'yaṃ samudāhṛtaḥ
2.39  sarvāṇi eva tattvāni pañcaitāni yathā śṛṇu
2.40  bhūtatattvābhidhānānāṃ yo'ṃśo'dhiṣṭheya iṣyate
2.40  piṇḍasthamiti taṃ prāhuḥ padasthamaparaṃ viduḥ
2.41  mantrāstatpatayaḥ seśā rūpasthamiti kīrtyate
2.41  rūpātītaṃ parā śaktiḥ savyāpārāpyanāmayā
2.42  niṣprapañco nirābhāsaḥ śuddhaḥ svātmanyavasthitaḥ
2.42  sarvātītaḥ śivo jñeyo yaṃ viditvā vimucyate
2.43  caturvidhaṃ tu piṇḍasthamabuddhaṃ buddhameva ca
2.43  prabuddhaṃ suprabuddhaṃ ca padasthaṃ ca caturvidham
2.44  gatāgataṃ suvikṣiptaṃ saṅgataṃ susamāhitam
2.44  caturdhā rūpasaṃsthaṃ tu jñātavyaṃ yogacintakaiḥ
2.45  uditaṃ vipulaṃ śāntaṃ suprasannamathāparam
2.45  manonmanamanantaṃ ca sarvārthaṃ satatoditam
2.46  pracaye tatra saṃjñeyamekaṃ tanmahati sthitam
2.46  ityevaṃ pañcadhādhvānaṃ tridhedānīṃ nigadyate
2.47  vijñānākalaparyantamātmatattvamudāhṛtam
2.47  īśvarāntaṃ ca vidyāhvaṃ śeṣaṃ śivapadaṃ viduḥ
2.48  evaṃ bhedairimairbhinnastatrādhvā parikīrtitaḥ
2.48  yugapatsarvamārgāṇāṃ prabhedaḥ procyate'dhunā
2.49  pārthivaṃ prākṛtaṃ caiva māyīyaṃ śāktameva ca
2.49  iti saṅkṣepataḥ proktametadaṇḍacatuṣṭayam
2.50  pṛthagdvayamasaṅkhyātamekamekaṃ pṛthakpṛthak
2.50  ādyaṃ dhārikayā vyāptaṃ tatraikaṃ tattvamiṣyate
2.51  ekamekaṃ pṛthakkṣārṇaṃ padārṇamanuṣu smaret
2.51  kālāgnibhuvanādyāvadvīrabhadrapurottamam
2.52  puraṣoḍaśakaṃ jñeyaṃ ṣaḍvidho'dhvā prakīrtitaḥ
2.52  āpyāyinyā dvitīyaṃ ca tatra tattvāni lakṣayet
2.53  trayoviṃśatyabādīni tadvaddhādyakṣarāṇi ca
2.53  padāni pañca mantrāśca ṣaṭpañcāśatpurāṇi ca
2.54  tattvāni sapta bodhinyā taccaturdhā purāṇi ca
2.54  tṛtīye sapta varṇāḥ syuḥ padamantradvayaṃ dvayam
2.55  utpūyinyā caturthaṃ tu tatra tattvatrayaṃ viduḥ
2.55  varṇatrayaṃ mantramekaṃ padamekaṃ ca lakṣayet
2.56  aṣṭādaśa vijānīyādbhuvanāni samāsataḥ
2.56  śivatattvaṃ paraṃ śāntaṃ kalā tatrāvakāśadā
2.57  svaraṣoḍaśakaṃ mantraṃ padaṃ caikaṃ vilakṣayet
2.57  ityevaṃ ṣaḍvidho'pyadhvā samāsātparikīrtitaḥ
2.58  śuddhāśuddhaṃ jagatsarvaṃ brahmāṇḍaprabhavaṃ yataḥ
2.58  tasmācchuddhamimaiḥ śuddhairbrahmāṇḍaiḥ sarvamiṣyate
2.59  brahmā viṣṇuśca rudraśca īśvaraśceti suvrate
2.59  pṛthageteṣu boddhavyaṃ śāntaṃ paticatuṣṭayam
2.60  yo hi yasmādguṇotkṛṣṭaḥ sa tasmādūrdhva ucyate
2.60  etatte kathitaṃ sarvaṃ kimanyatparipṛcchasi

iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre vyāptyadhikāro dvitīyaḥ samāptaḥ


mantroddhārādhikārastṛtīyaḥ
3.1  evamuktā mahādevī jagadānandakāriṇā
3.1  praṇipatya punarvākyamidamāha jagatpatim
3.2  evametanmahādeva nānyathā samudāhṛtam
3.2  yathākhyātaṃ tathā jñātamāditaḥ samanukramāt
3.3  śivādivasturūpāṇāṃ vācakān parameśvara
3.3  sāṃprataṃ śrotumicchāmi prasādādvaktumarhasi
3.4  ityuktaḥ sa maheśānyā jagadārtiharo haraḥ
3.4  vācakānavadanmantrān pāramparyakramāgatān
3.5  yā sā śaktirjagaddhātuḥ kathitā samavāyinī
3.5  icchātvaṃ tasya sā devi sisṛkṣoḥ pratipadyate
3.6  saikāpi satyanekatvaṃ yathā gacchati tacchṛṇu
3.6  evametaditi jñeyaṃ nānyatheti suniścitam
3.7  jñāpayantī jagatyatra jñānaśaktirnigadyate
3.7  evaṃbhūtamidaṃ vastu bhavatviti yadā punaḥ
3.8  jātā tadaiva tattadvatkurvatyatra kriyocyate
3.8  evameṣā dvirūpāpi punarbhedairanantatām
3.9  arthopādhivaśādyāti cintāmaṇiriveśvarī
3.9  tatra tāvatsamāpannā mātṛbhāvaṃ vibhidyate
3.10  dvidhā ca navadhā caiva pañcāśaddhā ca mālinī
3.10  bījayonyātmakādbhedāddvidhā bījaṃ svarā matāḥ
3.11  kādibhiśca smṛtā yonirnavadhā vargabhedataḥ
3.11  pṛthagvarṇavibhedena śatārdhakiraṇojjvalā
3.12  bījamatra śivaḥ śaktiryonirityabhidhīyate
3.12  vācakatvena sarvāpi śaṃbhoḥ śaktiśca śasyate
3.13  vargāṣṭakamiha jñeyamaghorādyamanukramāt
3.13  tadeva śaktibhedena māheśvaryādi cāṣṭakam
3.14  māheśī brāhmaṇī caiva kaumārī vaiṣṇavī tathā
