vīṇāśikhatantra

oṃ namo mahābhairavāya

kailāsaśikhare ramye nānāratnopaśobhite  |
nānādrumalatākīrṇe siddhacāraṇasevite  || 1 ||
tatra devaḥ suraśreṣṭhaḥ krīḍate umayā saha  |
stūyamāno mahāsiddhairmahākālādibhirgaṇaiḥ  ||  2 ||
ṛṣibhiśca mahābhāgairbhṛgvādyaiḥ surasattamaiḥ |
teṣāṃ madhye samutthāya devī vacanamabravīt || 3 ||
śrutaṃ sammohanaṃ tantraṃ tathā nayottaraṃ mahat |
śiraśchedaṃ ca deveśa tvatprasādātsudurlabham  || 4 ||
vratasādhyāni caitāni yāgasādhyāni vai punaḥ  |
anyasārā yato loke prāyo draviṇavarjitāḥ  || 5 ||
ebhyo'pi cottaraṃ yasmātkevalaṃ jñānasiddhidam  |
sarvakāmapradaṃ deva yathāvadbījapañcakaṃ  || 6 ||
uttaraṃ hṛdayaṃ hyeṣāṃ bhaktāya dātumarhasi  || 7 ||

śrī īśvara uvāca

yanna kasyacidākhyātaṃ śukrādīnāṃ ca yoginām  |
subhaktasya vinītasya gopitaṃ vai guhasya ca  || 8 ||
catuḥṣaṣṭiḥ samākhyātāḥ śiṣyāstantreṣu ye mayā  |
teṣāmapi na cākhyātaṃ brahmaviṣṇupuraḥsaraiḥ  || 9 ||
tadahaṃ sampravakṣyāmi cintāratnamivāparam  |
tantraṃ vīṇāśikhaṃ nāma nirvyājenāśusiddhidam  || 10 ||
devītumburusaṃyuktaṃ vīṇādhārasusaṃsthitam  |
śikhāyogena iṣyante tena vīṇāśikhā smṛtā  || 11 ||
yāgamādau pravakṣyāmi tantrasāraṃ sudurlabham  |
tenaiva varadā devyo nityaṃ devi bhavanti hi  || 12 ||
gṛhayāgamidaṃ devi yo jānātīha sādhakaḥ |
vratahomādṛte cāsmin susiddhiṃ labhate param  || 13 ||
śiṣyāṇāmāditaḥ kuryādyāgaṃ kṛtvā parigraham  |
nānyathā darśayettantraṃ na cāsau siddhimāpnuyāt || 14 ||
caturthyāmatha pañcamyāṃ navamyāmekādaśīṣu ca  |
grahaṇe vāpi kartavyā sarvadā cārkasomayoḥ  || 15 ||
caturthyāṃ yajanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ saubhāgyakaraṇaṃ mahat |
śrīkāmo yajanaṃ kuryātpañcamyāṃ susamāhitaḥ  || 16 ||
saṃgrāme vijayārthī vā pararāṣṭravimardanam  |
navamyāṃ pārthivaṃ yāgaṃ kurvīta bhaginīpriyam  || 17 ||
ekādaśyāṃ yajedyastu śivaloke mahīyate  |
sugupte nirjane deśe saridvāpītaṭe'pi vā  || 18 ||
kṛtvādau bhūmisaṃśuddhiṃ sāvitryā deśikottamaḥ  |
kṛtvā pūjāṃ prakurvīta śiṣyāṇāmadhivāsanam  || 19 ||
carukaṃ sādhane paścātsāvitryā dāpayedbudhaḥ  |
śiṣyāṇāṃ dantakāṣṭhaṃ ca sakṣīraṃdvādaśāṅgulam  || 20 ||
ācamya śiṣyamāhūya pañcatattvapariṣkṛtam  |
sāvitryā prokṣayedbhūyastattvajaptaṃ yathoditam  || 21 ||
pramārjayetkuśāgreṇa tasyāṅgāni samālabhet |
ālabhyaiva tu sāvitryā kṣālayetsakalaṃ kramāt || 22 ||
yāgabhūmau svaśiṣyāṃstu svapecca kuśasaṃstare  |
rakṣāṃ sadā śatairbījaiḥ kṛtvā dhyātvā ca tāḥ kramāt || 23 ||
tataḥ prabhāte vimale mukhaṃ prakṣālya sādhakaḥ  |
iṣṭāniṣṭāṃ gurau ceṣṭāṃ praṇipatya nivedayet || 24 ||
iṣṭāniṣṭān viditvā tu deśikaḥ sādhakasya tu  |
ādau deśe same bhūmau vivikte śalyavarjite  || 25 ||
maṇḍalaṃ saṃlikhetprājño yathāvadvidhipūrvakam  |
caturhastaṃ caturdvāramathavā caikahastakam  || 26 ||
maṇḍalaṃ saṃlikheddivyaṃ śālicūrṇena sūjjvalam  |
tatra madhye likhetpadmaṃ catuḥpattraṃ sakarṇikam  || 27 ||
śvetāsṛ kpītakṛ ṣṇāni kamalasya dalāni tu  |
prāgārabhya yathānyāyaṃ saumyeśāntāni lekhayet || 28 ||
karṇikāṃ ca tathā madhye śabalāṃ deśikottamaḥ  |
caturmūrtiṃ caturvarṇaṃ svena bijena tumburum  || 29 ||
caturvarṇamīśvaraṃ dhyāyenniviṣṭaṃ karṇikodare  |
tataḥ sitāṃ svabījena jayāṃ prāgdale vinyaset || 30 ||
bandhūkakusumaprakhyāṃ vijayāṃ dakṣiṇe dale  |
svacchacāmīkaraprakhyāmajitāṃ paścime dale  || 31 ||
bhinnāñjanacayaprakhyāmuttare cāparājitām  |
vinyasya pūjāṃ kurvīta svaiḥ svairbījairyathākramam  || 32 ||
puṣpadhūpaiśca balibhiryathākālāntaraiḥ śubhaiḥ |
sadvitānapatākāḍhyaṃ sragmālālaṃkṛtaṃ puram  || 33 ||
pradīptadīpakairdikṣu samantādavabhāsitam  |
nānābhakṣyānnapānaiśca svādubhirvyañjanaistathā  || 34 ||
phalairnānāvidhaiścaiva paritaḥ paryavasthitaiḥ |
kalaśairvāripūrṇaiśca daśadikṣu vyavasthitaiḥ  || 35 ||
cūtapallavasaṃvītaiḥ sragmālālaṃkṛtaiḥ śubhaiḥ  |
evaṃ yaṣṭvā yathānyāyaṃ śiṣyānāprokṣya vāriṇā  || 36 ||
sāvitryā mukhamāsādya vāsasā sakalīkṛtān  |
svaiḥ svairbījairnyasetpuṣpān śiṣyāṇāṃ karayordvayoḥ  || 37 ||
jayātpraveśayenmāyāṃ maṇḍalaṃ deśikottamaḥ  |
praveśya tatra śiṣyaṃ tu dvitricatuḥpañca eva vā  || 38 ||
tato'gnikāryaṃ kurvīta maṇḍalātpaścime bahiḥ  |
ullikhyoddhṛtya sāvitryā kuśān saṃstīrya sarvataḥ  || 39 ||
astrabījena cābhyukṣya gandhatoyena deśikaḥ  |
vahnimādāya tenaiva sāvitryā pūjayettataḥ  || 40 ||
tatastattvatrayaṃ nyasya vahnereva yathākramam  |
niruddhamāyātasmāttamaṅkuśena nirodhayet || 41 ||
māyayācchādayetpaścādastreṇaiva prabodhayet |
homadravyasya sarvasya kuryāttenaiva śodhanam  || 42 ||
tataśca sarpiṣo homaṃ vidhivatkārayedbudhaḥ  |
svabījaireva taddhutvā dīkṣāṃ śiṣyasya kārayet || 43 ||
saṃyojya vidhivadbījairmahābhūteṣu pañcasu  |
sakale tattvaṃ saṃyojya pariṣṭhāpya pare'dhvani  || 44 ||
niyoktavyaṃ tatastatra yatrāsavāṃsvaraparam?  |
eṣā dīkṣā ya thānyāyaṃ bhuktimuktiphalapradā  || 45 ||
taireva pañcabhistattvaṃ sakale sakalātmikā  |
niṣkale niṣkalā proktā sādhikārādhvanaḥ purā  || 46 ||
dīkṣayitvā tataḥ śiṣyān sādhikārapare sthitān  |
abhiṣicya svabījaistu bījān tebhyaḥ pradāpayet || 47 ||
nivedya samayān tasya hṛnmudrāṅgulidarśanāt |
anujñāṃ sādhakendrasya tasya dadyānmahātmanā  || 48 ||
tataḥ svavidyānaivedyaṃ bhakṣayetsādhakottamaḥ |
devyaḥ prītā bhavantyeva avaśiṣṭaṃ jale kṣipet || 49 ||
ācāryaṃ pūjayedbhaktyā sarvasvenāpare vidhā?  |
praṇāmaiḥ śaktidānaiśca yena vā tuṣyate guruḥ  || 50 ||
sragvī sitoṣṇīṣī caiva sarvālaṃkārabhūṣitaḥ  |
uccāsanasthaḥ prāgvaktraḥ kalpayetkoṣamaṇḍale  || 51 ||
gośakṛdbhasmaliptastu śucau janavivarjite  |
susame bhūmideśe tu prastāraṃ prastarecchuciḥ  || 52 ||
caturasramataḥ kṛtvā prastārarekhamujjvalam  |
kuryādekonapañcāśatkoṣṭhakān tu samān śubhān  || 53 ||
śatārdhārdhāsanāsīnaṃ pañcavargāntabindukam  |
śikhāsambhinnamūrdhāntaṃ kaṣākhyaṃ madhyakoṣṭhake  || 54 ||
vidigdikṣaṃsthakoṣṭheṣu tatpārśve bahiraṣṭasu  |
yaśavargānnyaseddevi aiśānyādiṣu tatkramāt || 55 ||
āgneyādiṣu koṣṭheṣu napuṃsakacatuṣṭayam  |
aiśānyādikramāddevi bījadvādaśakaṃ nyaset || 56 ||
tṛtīyapaṭktikoṣṭheṣu caturthe pañcaviṃśakam |
aiśānyādiṣu koṣṭheṣu bījāni kramaśo nyaset || 57 ||
prastāramevaṃ prastārya svaravarṇaṃ ca śobhane  |
bījaṣoḍaśakaṃ caiva proddharettu yathākramam  || 58 ||
kādipaṭktiṃ purākṛtya kramādvyastasamastakam  |
koṣṭhaikādaśabījena saṃyuktaṃ pañcaviṃśakam  || 59 ||
ātmatattvamiti khyātaṃ vidyākhyaṃ catustriṃśakam  |
śivatattvaṃ tu deveśi triṃśakoṣṭheṣu saṃyutam  || 60 ||
tattvatritayametaddhi nyāsaṃ ca samudāhṛtam  |
binduyuktānyaśesāṇi nyastavyāni yathākramam  || 61 ||
atrasiddhiḥ sthitā devi vijñeyā sādhakottamaiḥ  |
pañcaviṃśatikoṣṭhasthaṃ prathamaṃ bījamucyate  || 62 ||
dvisaptakoṣṭhakaṃ bījaṃ dvitīyaṃ samudāhṛtam  |
tṛtīyamaṣṭakoṣṭhasthaṃ binduyoniḥ caturthakam  || 63 ||
caturviṃśatikoṣṭhasthaṃ pañcamaṃ bījamucyate  |
bījāni devadevīnāṃ nirṇītāni yathākramam  || 64 ||
kālabaddhānilairbījaiḥ kalāyatheṣṭhayā yutam  |

yajanaṃ sampravakṣyāmi sarvasiddhipradāyakam  || 67 ||
karasaṃskāramādau tu kṛtvānena krameṇa tu  |
vakṣyamāṇena cānena digbandhaśodhyameva hi  || 68 ||
saṃhārāstreṇa digbandhaḥ prāṇāyāmapuraḥsaraḥ  |

nirdaheccātmadehaṃ tu vāriṇāplāvayettataḥ  |
dagdhvā tu prākṛtaṃdehaṃ bhasmakūṭamiva sthitam  || 72 ||
tataścāmṛtadhārābhirvidyādehaṃ vicintayet |
sravantaṃ mūrdhni paramaṃ praṇavaṃ ca adhomukham  || 73 ||
vāruṇāmṛtasaṃyuktaṃ śuddhasphaṭikanirmalam  |
kaṣākhyaṃ yatsmṛtaṃ bījaṃ rephadvayasamāyutam  || 74 ||
adha oṃkārasaṃyuktamūrdhvaṃ bindukabhūṣitam |

dhāraṇāyogamārgeṇa nirdahetsādhakottamaḥ  |
dehaṃ saṃśodhayenmantrī ghorapāpaṃ tu kalmaṣam  || 76 ||

hastau saṃśodhayetpaścādvidhireṣa prakīrtitaḥ  || 78 ||
