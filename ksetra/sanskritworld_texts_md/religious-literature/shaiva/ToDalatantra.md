ṭoḍalatantra, prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīdevyuvāca
brūhi me jagatāṃ nātha sarvavidyāmaya prabho  |
mahāvidyāsu sarvāsu pūjyāsu bhuvanatraye  || 1.1 ||
etāsāṃ dakṣiṇe bhāge nānārūpaṃ pinākadhṛk |
pṛthakpṛthakmahādeva kathayasva mayi prabho  || 1.2 ||
śrīśiva uvāca
śṛṇu cārvaṅgi subhage kālikāyāśca bhairavam  |
mahākālaṃ dakṣiṇāyā dakṣabhāge prapūjayet || 1.3 ||
mahākālena vai sārdhaṃ dakṣiṇā ramate sadā  |
tārāyā dakṣiṇe bhāge akṣobhyaṃ paripūjayet || 1.4 ||
samudramathane devi kālakūṭaṃ samutthitam  |
sarve devāḥ sadārāśca mahākṣobhamavāpnuyuḥ  || 1.5 ||
kṣobhādirahitaṃ yasmātpītaṃ hālāhalaṃ viṣam  |
ata eva maheśāni akṣobhyaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  || 1.6 ||
tena sārdhaṃ mahāmāyā tāriṇī ramate sadā  |
mahātripurasundaryā dakṣiṇe pūjayecchivam  || 1.7 ||
pañcavaktraṃ trinetraṃ ca prativaktre sureśvari  |
tena sārdhaṃ mahādevī sadā kāmakutūhalā  || 1.8 ||
ata eva maheśāni pañcamīti prakīrtitā  |
śrīmadbhuvanasundaryā dakṣiṇe tryambakaṃ yajet || 1.9 ||
svarge martye ca pātāle yā cādyā bhuvaneśvarī  |
etāsu ramate yena tryambakastena kathyate  || 1.10 ||
saśaktiśca samākhyātaḥ sarvatantraprapūjitaḥ  |
bhairavyā dakṣiṇe bhāge dakṣiṇāmūrtisaṃjñakam  || 1.11 ||
pūjayetparayatnena pañcavaktraṃ tameva hi  |
chinnamastādakṣiṇāṃśe kabandhaṃ pūjayecchivam  || 1.12 ||
kabandhapūjanāddevi sarvasiddhīśvaro bhavet |
dhūmāvatī mahāvidyā vidhavārūpadhāriṇī  || 1.13 ||
vagalāyā dakṣabhāge ekavaktraṃ prapūjayet |
mahārudreti vikhyātaṃ jagatsaṃhārakārakam  || 1.14 ||
mātaṃgīdakṣiṇāṃśe ca mataṃgaṃ pūjayecchivam  |
tameva dakṣiṇāmūrtiṃ jagadānandarūpakam  || 1.15 ||
kamalāyā dakṣiṇāṃśe viṣṇurūpaṃ sadāśivam  |
pūjayetparameśāni sa siddho nātra saṃśayaḥ  || 1.16 ||
pūjayedannapūrṇāyā dakṣiṇe brahmarūpakam  |
mahāmokṣapradaṃ devaṃ daśavaktraṃ maheśvaram  || 1.17 ||
durgāyā dakṣiṇe deśe nāradaṃ paripūjayet |
nākāraḥ sṛṣṭikartā ca dakāraḥ pālakaḥ sadā  || 1.18 ||
rephaḥ saṃhārarūpatvānnāradaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  |
anyāsu sarvavidyāsu ṛṣiryaḥ parikīrtitaḥ  || 1.19 ||
sa eva tasyā bhartā ca dakṣabhāge prapūjayet |
śrīdevī uvāca
yā cādyā paramā vidyā dvitīyā bhairavī parā  |
trailokyajananī nityā sā kathaṃ śavavāhanā  || 1.20 ||
śrīśiva uvāca
yā cādyā parameśāni svayaṃ kālasvarūpiṇī  |
śrīśivasya hṛdambhoje sthitā saṃhārarūpiṇī  || 1.21 ||
ata eva mahākālo jagatsaṃhārakārakaḥ  |
saṃhārarūpiṇī kālī yadā vyaktasvarūpiṇī  || 1.22 ||
tadaiva sahasā devi śavarūpaḥ sadāśivaḥ  |
tatkṣaṇāccañcalāpāṅgi sā devī śavavāhanā  || 1.23 ||
śrīdevī uvāca
śavarūpo mahādeva mṛtadehaḥ sadāśivaḥ  |
mṛtadehaṃ mahādeva salilaṃ vā kathaṃ nahi  || 1.24 ||
śrīśiva uvāca
yasmin vyaktā mahākālī śaktihīnaḥ sadāśivaḥ  |
śaktyā yukto yadā devi tadaiva śivarūpakaḥ  |
śaktihīnaḥ śavaḥ sākṣātpuruṣatvaṃ na muñcati  || 1.25 ||


ṭoḍalatantra, ḍvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīśiva uvāca
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yogasāraṃ samāsataḥ  |
ūrdhvamūlamadhaḥśākhaṃ vṛkṣākāraṃ kalevaram  || 2.1 ||
brahmāṇḍe yāni tīrthāni tāni santi kalevare  |
bṛhadbrahmāṇḍaṃ yadrūpaṃ tadrūpaṃ kṣudrarūpakam  || 2.2 ||
brahmāṇḍe vartate tīrthaṃ sārdhakoṭitrayātmakam  |
dvisaptatisahasrāṇi prakāśaṃ vīravandite  || 2.3 ||
caturdaśaṃ tu tanmadhye tanmadhye tritayaṃ śubham  |
tanmadhye parameśāni mahādhīrā ca muktidā  || 2.4 ||
vāsukī yā mahāmāyā bhujagākārarūpiṇī  |
sārdhatrivalayākārā pātālatalavāsinī  || 2.5 ||
saptasvargaṃ pareśāni krameṇa śṛṇu sādaram  |
bhūrlokaṃ ca bhuvarlokaṃ svarlokaṃ mahasaṃ tathā  || 2.6 ||
janalokaṃ tapaścaiva satyalokaṃ varānane  |
saptasvargamidaṃ bhadre pātālaṃ śṛṇu yatnataḥ  || 2.7 ||
atalaṃ vitalaṃ caiva sutalaṃ ca talātalam  |
mahātalaṃ ca pātālaṃ rasātalamataḥ param  || 2.8 ||
rasātalācca satyāntaṃ mahādhīrā pratiṣṭhitā  |
merumadhyasthitā nāḍī mahādhīrā ca muktidā  || 2.9 ||
satyaloke mahāviṣṇuṃ śivaṃ brahmāṇḍasaṃjñakam  |
tasya saṃdarśanārthāya vāsukī vyākulā sadā  || 2.10 ||
svargaṣaṭkaṃ bhedayitvā utthitā vāsukī yadā  |
sarvāḥ samudragāminya ūrdhvasrotā bhavanti hi  || 2.11 ||
evaṃ krameṇa deveśi śarīre nāḍayaḥ sthitāḥ  |
iḍā ca piṅgalā caiva suṣumṇā madhyavartinī  || 2.12 ||
nāḍīdvayena deveśi samīraṃ pūrayedyadi  |
tatastu prāṇamantreṇa kuṇḍalī ca kramaṃ caret || 2.13 ||
sahasrāre mahāpadme nitye cāvyayapaṅkaje  |
trāsayuktā kuṇḍalinī praviśennityamandiram  || 2.14 ||
sarvāḥ pātālagāminya ūrdhvasrotā bhavanti hi  |
etasmin samaye devi varṇamālāṃ vicintayet || 2.15 ||
aṣṭottaraśataṃ mūla- mantraṃ jñānena saṃjapet |
ānayettena mārgeṇa mūlādhāre punaḥ sudhīḥ  || 2.16 ||
ṣaṭcakradevatāstatra saṃtarpyāmṛtadhārayā  |
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi yonimudrāsanaṃ priye  || 2.17 ||
upaviśyāsane mantrī prāṅmukho vāpyudaṅmukhaḥ  |
jānudvayaṃ karābhyāṃ ca prakuryāddṛḍhabandhanam  || 2.18 ||
ṛjukāyena deveśi ājānu nāsikāṃ nayet |
prāṇamantreṇa deveśi samīraṃ bahuyatnataḥ  || 2.19 ||
pūrayetparameśāni kiṃcidvāyuṃ na recayet |
ṛjukāyaṃ pareśāni viparītaṃ prayatnataḥ  || 2.20 ||
pūrvoktenaiva vidhinā aṣṭottaraśataṃ japet |
ṣaṭcakradevatāstatra saṃtarpyāmṛtadhārayā  || 2.21 ||
ānayettena mārgeṇa mūlādhāre varānane  |
aśeṣarogaśamanaṃ yonimudrāsanaṃ priye  || 2.22 ||
bahu kiṃ kathyate devi mahāvyādhivināśanam  |
bahu kiṃ kathyate devi mantracaitanyakāraṇam  || 2.23 ||
ātmasākṣātkarī mudrā mahāmokṣapradāyinī  |
śatavaktraṃ yadi bhavettadā vaktuṃ na śakyate  || 2.24 ||
pañcavaktreṇa deveśi kiṃ mayā kathyate'dhunā  |
kuṣṭharogaviśiṣṭo'pi sa bhavetkāmarūpakaḥ  || 2.25 ||


ṭoḍalatantra, ṭṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīdevī uvāca
devadeva mahādeva saṃsārārṇavatāraka  |
baddhayoniṃ mahāmudrāṃ kathayasva dayānidhe  || 3.1 ||
śrīśiva uvāca
śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi baddhayoniṃ samāsataḥ  |
upaviśyāsane mantrī prāṅmukho vāpyudaṅmukhaḥ  || 3.2 ||
gudacchidre maheśāni svaliṅgāgraṃ niveśayet |
śrotre caiva tathā netre nāsāyāṃ ca tato mukhe  || 3.3 ||
aṅguṣṭhādi maheśāni krameṇa yojayetsudhīḥ  |
nāsikāyāṃ mukhe caiva samīraṃ bahuyatnataḥ  || 3.4 ||
pūrayetparameśāni kiṃcidapi na recayet |
śabdapratyakṣatāṃ kṛtvā varṇamālāṃ vicintayet || 3.5 ||
aṣṭottaraśataṃ mūla- mantraṃ tu prajapetsudhīḥ  |
so'haṃmantreṇa deveśi ānayettena vartmanā  || 3.6 ||
ṣaṭcakradevatāstatra saṃtarpyāmṛtadhārayā  |
kṛte phalaṃ maheśāni kathayiṣyāmi te'naghe  || 3.7 ||
śrīpārvatī uvāca
vada īśāna sarvajña sarvatattvavidāṃ vara  |
mantramārgeṇa deveśa kālikāyāḥ sudurlabham  || 3.8 ||
śrīśiva uvāca
śṛṇu devi sadānande kālikāmantramuttamam  |
yasyāḥ prasaṅgamātreṇa jīvanmukto bhavennaraḥ  || 3.9 ||
śivādyaṃ bindunādāḍhyaṃ vāmanetrāgnisaṃyutam  |
siddhavidyā tviyaṃ bhadre vidyārājñī sudurlabhā  || 3.10 ||
idaṃ bījatrayaṃ cādyaṃ gaganaṃ bindubhūṣitam  |
vāmaśravaṇasaṃyuktaṃ dvaṃdvaṃ cāparakaṃ śṛṇu  || 3.11 ||
īśānaṃ vahnisaṃyuktaṃ vāmanetrendusaṃyutam  |
dvitīyaṃ parameśāni sambodhanapadaṃ tataḥ  || 3.12 ||
punarbījatrayaṃ kūrcaṃ māyādvaṃdvaṃ ca ṭhadvayam  |
dvāviṃśatyakṣaraṃ mantraṃ vidyārājñī sudurlabhā  || 3.13 ||
vāgbhavādyā mahāvidyā śrīkālī devatā smṛtā  |
praṇavādyā mahāvidyā devatā siddhikālikā  || 3.14 ||
nijabījadvayaṃ kūrcaṃ bījaikaṃ parameśvari  |
tryakṣarī paramā vidyā cāmuṇḍā kālikā smṛtā  || 3.15 ||
etasyāḥ sadṛśī vidyā siddhidā nāsti sundari  |
yathā ṣaḍakṣarī vidyā tathā vidyā ca tryakṣarī  || 3.16 ||
nijabījatrayaṃ bhadre śmaśānakālikā tataḥ  |
punarbījatrayaṃ bhadre vahnikāntāṃ samuccaret || 3.17 ||
caturdaśākṣarī vidyā triṣu lokeṣu pūjitā  |
dakṣiṇākālikā siddha- kālikā guhyakālikā  || 3.18 ||
śrīkālikā bhadrakālī cāmuṇḍākālikā parā  |
śmaśānakālikā devi mahākālīti cāṣṭadhā  || 3.19 ||
nijabījaṃ maheśāni sambodhanapadaṃ tataḥ  |
punaśca kālikābījaṃ tato vahnivadhūṃ nyaset || 3.20 ||
iti cāṣṭavidhaṃ mantraṃ sarvatantreṣu gopitam  |
śrīdevī uvāca
śrutaṃ mahākālikāyā mantraṃ paramagopanam  || 3.21 ||
tārāyā mantrarājaṃ tu śrotumicchāmi sāmpratam  |
yasyāḥ prasaṅgamātreṇa bhavābdhau na nimajjati  || 3.22 ||
tanmantraṃ vada īśāna yadi sneho'sti māṃ prati  |
śrīśiva uvāca
candrabījaṃ samuccārya ādyaṃ vahnisamāgatam  || 3.23 ||
vāmanetrendusaṃyuktaṃ mantrarājamimaṃ priye  |
ekākṣarī mahāvidyā triṣu lokeṣu pūjitā  || 3.24 ||
īśānabindusaṃyuktaṃ vāmakarṇavibhūṣitam  |
ekākṣarī mahāvidyā mantrarājadvitīyakam  || 3.25 ||
śiraṃ bindusamārūḍhaṃ vāmanetrendusaṃyutam  |
ādyabījaṃ dvitīyaṃ ca astramantraṃ samuccaret || 3.26 ||
praṇavādyā yadā vidyā sogratārā prakīrtitā  |
vidyā caikajaṭā proktā mahāmokṣapradāyinī  || 3.27 ||
tārāstrarahitā tryarṇā mahānīlasarasvatī  |
vāgbhavādyā yadā vidyā vāgīśatvapradāyinī  || 3.28 ||
śrībījādyā mahāvidyā sadā lakṣmīpradāyinī  |
māyādyā paramā vidyā sadā siddhipradāyinī  || 3.29 ||
kūrcabījādikā caiṣā śabdarāśiprakāśinī  |
gaganādyā mahāvidyā nirvāṇamokṣadāyinī  || 3.30 ||
prāsādādyā mahāvidyā śivasāyujyadāyinī  |
prāṇabījādikā caiṣā vāñchāsiddhipradāyinī  || 3.31 ||
kālikādyā mahāvidyā muktidā siddhidā sadā  |
kālikāyāśca tārāyā ārādhanamihocyate  || 3.32 ||
prātarutthāya mantrajñaḥ sahasrāre guruṃ yajet |
ṣaṭcakraṃ bhedayitvā tu cāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet || 3.33 ||
tataḥ praṇamya vidhivatsnānakarma samārabhet |
adyetyādi samuccārya sauramāsaṃ samuccaret || 3.34 ||
devatāprītaye paścātsnāpayecchuddhavāriṇā  |
oṃkāraṃ ca samuccārya gaṅge ceti samuccaret || 3.35 ||
yamuneti tataḥ paścādgodāvari sarasvati  |
narmadeti samuccārya sindhukāverisaṃyutam  || 3.36 ||
asmin jale ca saṃnidhiṃ kuru śabdamatho vadet |
ānayedaṅkuśākhyena ravisaṃyuktamaṇḍalāt || 3.37 ||
mudrācatuṣṭayaṃ devi darśayedbahuyatnataḥ  |
rudrasaṃkhyaṃ japenmantramācchādya mīnamudrayā  || 3.38 ||
sūryāyābhimukhaṃ toyaṃ niḥkṣipya ravisaṃkhyayā  |
mūlamantraṃ samuccārya kṣālayeccaraṇadvayam  || 3.39 ||
caraṇānniḥsṛte toye trirnimajya japenmanum  |
mūlamantraṃ trirjaptvā tu mudrayā kumbhasaṃjñayā  || 3.40 ||
jalādutthāya deveśi tilakaṃ kulavartmataḥ  |
ātmavidyāśivaistattvairācāmetpayasā tataḥ  || 3.41 ||
vahnijāyānvitā mantrā vijñeyāḥ praṇavādikāḥ  |
gaṅge cetyādinā devi tīrthamāvāhanaṃ caret || 3.42 ||
mūlena darbhayā bhūmau trivāraṃ nikṣipetsudhīḥ  |
tajjalena saptavāramātmābhiṣekamācaret || 3.43 ||
aṅganyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā vāmahaste sureśvari  |
gaganaṃ vāyubījaṃ ca varuṇaṃ bhūmibījakam  || 3.44 ||
vahnibījaṃ binduyuktaṃ trivāraṃ japamācaret |
anena manunā devi tajjalaṃ cābhimantritam  || 3.45 ||
mūlamantraṃ samuccārya saptadhā tattvamudrayā  |
abhiṣecanamātreṇa mucyate sarvapātakaiḥ  || 3.46 ||
śeṣaṃ jalaṃ maheśāni dakṣahaste samānayet |
iḍayā pūrayettoyaṃ kṣālayeddehamadhyagam  || 3.47 ||
tataḥ piṅgalayā devi tattoyaṃ tu virecayet |
kṛṣṇavarṇaṃ tadudakaṃ pāparūpaṃ vicintayet || 3.48 ||
purato vajrapāṣāṇe phaṭkāreṇa vinikṣipet |
hastaṃ prakṣālya cācamya prāṇāyāmapuraḥsaram  || 3.49 ||
tarpayetkuladevaṃ ca sūryāyārghyaṃ nivedayet |
devatārghyaṃ tataḥ paścādgāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm  || 3.50 ||
praṇavaṃ ca samuddhṛtya kālikāyai tato vadet |
vidmahe iti saṃlikhya śmaśānaṃ ca samuddharet || 3.51 ||
vāsinīṃ ṅeyutāṃ devi dhīmahīti tato vadet |
tanno ghore maheśāni prajapettu pracodayāt || 3.52 ||
gāyatrīṃ prapaṭheddhīmān trivāraṃ jalamutkṣipet |
tato japenmahāmantraṃ gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm  || 3.53 ||
aṅganyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā ācamya parameśvari  |
iṣṭadevīṃ maheśāni dhyātvā tu parimaṇḍale  || 3.54 ||
oṃ tārāyai vidmahe iti mahogrāyai tato vadet |
dhīmahīti tataḥ paścāttato devi pracodayāt || 3.55 ||
prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā cāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet |
sūtrākāreṇa deveśi pūjāvidhirihocyate  || 3.56 ||
svastivācanasaṃkalpaṃ ghaṭaṃ saṃsthāpya yatnataḥ  |
mantreṇācamanaṃ kāryaṃ sāmānyārghyaṃ tato nyaset || 3.57 ||
tajjalairdvāramabhyukṣya dvārapūjāṃ samācaret |
trividhaṃ vighnamutsārya bhūtāpasaraṇaṃ tataḥ  || 3.58 ||
āsanaṃ ca samabhyarcya gurudevaṃ nametsudhīḥ  |
karaśuddhiṃ ca tālaṃ ca trayaṃ digbandhanaṃ tataḥ  || 3.59 ||
vahninā veṣṭanaṃ kāryaṃ bhūtaśuddhimathācaret |
mātṛkāyāḥ ṣaḍaṅgaṃ ca kuryādāntaramātṛkām  || 3.60 ||
mātṛkādhyānamuccārya bāhye tu mātṛkāṃ nyaset |
pīṭhanyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā prāṇāyāmaṃ samācaret || 3.61 ||
ṛṣyādikaṃ karāṅgaṃ ca varṇanyāsaṃ samācaret |
ṣoḍhānyāsaṃ tato devi vyāpakaṃ tadanantaram  || 3.62 ||
evaṃ samāhitamanāstattvanyāsaṃ samācaret |
bījanyāsaṃ tato devi vyāpakaṃ vinyasetsudhīḥ  || 3.63 ||
mūlena saptadhā dhyānaṃ mānasaiḥ pūjanaṃ caret |
viśeṣārghyaṃ pīṭhapūjāṃ punardhyānaṃ sanetrakam  || 3.64 ||
mudrādidarśanaṃ kāryamāvāhanaṣaḍaṅgakam  |
dhenvādikaṃ tataḥ prāṇa- pratiṣṭhāṃ mūlapūjanam  || 3.65 ||
ājñāprārthanamaṅgāni kālyādīn paripūjayet |
brāhmyādīnasitāṅgādīnmahākālaṃ prapūjayet || 3.66 ||
khaḍgādīn gurupaṅktiṃ ca punardevīṃ prapūjayet |
balidānaṃ tato homaṃ prāṇāyāmaṃ tato japam  || 3.67 ||
japaṃ samarpayeddhīmān prāṇāyāmaṃ tataścaret |
etasmin samaye devi kāraṇādīn samāharet || 3.68 ||
arghyaṃ dattvā maheśāni cātmānaṃ ca samarpayet |
stutiṃ ca kavacaṃ smṛtvā cāṣṭāṅgaṃ praṇametsudhīḥ  || 3.69 ||
śivo'hamiti saṃcintya saṃhāreṇa visarjayet |
aiśānyāṃ maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā cāṇḍālyucchiṣṭapūrvikām  || 3.70 ||
arghyaṃ saṃdhārya śirasi candanaṃ tu lalāṭake  |
naivedyaṃ kiṃcitsvīkṛtya viharecca nijecchayā  || 3.71 ||
saṃkṣepapūjāmathavā kuryānmantrī samāhitaḥ  |
ādau ṛṣyādikaṃ nyāsaṃ karaśuddhistataḥ param  || 3.72 ||
aṅgulīvyāpakanyāsau hṛdādinyāsa eva ca  |
