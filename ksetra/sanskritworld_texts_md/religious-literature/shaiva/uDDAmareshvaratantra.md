ūḍḍāmareśvaratantra, prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ
uḍḍīśena samākīrṇā yogivṛndasamākulā |
praṇamya śirasā devī gaurī pṛcchati śaṃkaram || 1.1
īśvara śrotumicchāmi lokanātha jagatprabho |
prasādaṃ kuru deveśa brūhi dharmārthasādhakam || 1.2
vaśīkaraṇamuccāṭaṃ mohanaṃ stambhanaṃ tathā |
śāntikaṃ pauṣṭikaṃ vātha karaṇāni bahūni ca || 1.3
cakṣurhāniṃ maheśāna śrutihāniṃ tathaiva ca |
jñānahāniṃ kriyāhāniṃ kīlakaṃ ca tathāparam || 1.4
kāryastambhaṃ sureśāna śoṣaṇaṃ pūraṇaṃ tathā |
mantradhyānaṃ viśeṣeṇa tatsarvaṃ vada me prabho || 1.5
anyacca vividhaṃ kāryaṃ prasādādbrūhi bhairava |
yasya vijñānamātreṇa manuṣyo bhuvi durlabhaḥ || 1.6
śrīrīśvara uvāca |
śṛṇu tvaṃ hi varārohe siddhyarthaṃ yadi pṛcchasi |
tadvadiṣyāmi te devi tatsarvaṃ samudāhṛtam || 1.7
auṣadhairmantrajāpaiśca ripuṃ hanyānna saṃśayaḥ |
uḍḍīśātsāramākṛṣya mayoktaṃ tava bhaktitaḥ || 1.8
uḍḍīśaṃ ca namaskṛtya rudraṃ caiva sudurlabham |
kapardinaṃ virūpākṣaṃ sarvabhūtabhayāpaham || 1.9
vakṣye rudrodbhavān yogān sarvaśatruvināśakān |
taistu prayojitaiḥ saḥ prāṇān hanti na saṃśayaḥ || 1.10
prathamaṃ bhūtakaraṇaṃ dvitīyonmādanaṃ tathā |
tṛtīyaṃ dveṣaṇaṃ cātha turyamuccāṭanaṃ tathā || 1.11
grāmoccāṭaṃ pañcamaṃ ca jalastambhaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhakam |
vahneḥ stambhakaraṃ cātha vaśīkaraṇamuttamam || 1.12
anyānapi prayogāṃśca bahūn śṛṇu varānane |
śivena kathitā yogā uḍḍīśe śāstraniścaye || 1.13
andhīkaraṇaṃ mūkīkaraṇaṃ badhirīkaraṇaṃ tathā |
bhūtajvarasya karaṇamastraśastrasya dūṣaṇam || 1.14
jaladoṣapraśamanaṃ dadhno madhuvināśanam |
vināśaṃ mattakaraṇaṃ gajavājiprakopanam || 1.15
ākarṣaṇaṃ bhujaṃgānāṃ mānavānāṃ tathā dhruvam |
vahnervināśanaṃ kuryātparṇānāṃ hi vināśanam || 1.16
gardabhasyātmakaraṇaṃ parakāyapraveśanam |
vetālapādukāsiddhimulvakājjvalanaṃ tathā || 1.17
anyān bahuprayogāṃśca raudrān romapraharṣaṇān |
vidyāmantraprayogādīnauṣadhāṃścābhicārikān || 1.18
guptāguptatarāḥ kāryā rakṣitavyāḥ prayatnataḥ |
uḍḍīśaṃ yo na jānāti sa ruṣṭaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati || 1.19
sumeruṃ cālayetsthānātsāgaraiḥ plāvayenmahīm |
sūryaṃ ca pātayedbhūmau nedaṃ mithyā bhaviṣyati || 1.20
yathaivendrasya vajraṃ ca pāśaṃ hi varuṇasya ca |
yamasya ca yathā daṇḍaṃ kuberasya gadā yathā || 1.21
vahneḥ śaktiryathā proktā khaḍgastu nirṛteryathā |
yathā vāyoścāṅkuśaṃ hi triśūlaṃ śūlapāṇinaḥ || 1.22
skandasya ca yathā śaktirviṣṇoścakraṃ sudarśanam |
tathaite ca mahāyogāḥ prayuktāḥ śatrukāraṇe || 1.23
anivṛtte nivartante amoghā nātra saṃśayaḥ |
saṃtuṣṭena prayuktena sidhyanti suvicārataḥ || 1.24
ratānāṃ karaṇaṃ vakṣye śatrusādhanamuttamam |
yenaiva kṛtamātreṇa bhūto gṛhṇāti mānavam || 1.25
nimbakāṣṭhaṃ samādāya caturaṅgulamānataḥ |
śatruviṣṭāsamāliptaṃ tathā nāma samālikhet || 1.26
citāṅgāreṇa tannāmnā dhūpaṃ dadyānmaheśvari |
citāntaḥ saṃsthito bhūtvā yasya gātramṛdāharet || 1.27
kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ caturdaśyāmaṣṭottaraśataṃ japet |
bhūto gṛhṇāti taṃ śīghraṃ mantreṇānena mantritaḥ || 1.28
uoṃ namo bhagavate sarvabhūtādhipataye virūpākṣāya nityaṃ krūrāya daṃṣṭriṇe vikarāline grahayakṣabhūtavetālena saha śaṃkara manuṣyaṃ daha daha paca paca gṛhṇa gṛhṇa gṛhṇāpaya gṛhṇāpaya huṃ phaṭsvāhā |
pratyānayaṃ yadīccheta tasyaiṣā kriyate kriyā |
dāpayeddevamīśānaṃ ghṛtena vacayā saha || 1.29
guggulaṃ pradadeddhūpaṃ ghṛtamiśraṃ samantataḥ |
mantreṇa mantrayitvā tu tataḥ svāsthyaṃ bhavetkila || 1.30
mantraḥ uoṃ namaḥ śivāya śāntāya prabhāya muktāya devādhidevāya śubhrabāhave vyādhiṃ śamaya śamaya amukaḥ svastho bhavatu namo'stu te |
iti svāsthyamantraḥ |
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi prayogaṃ bhuvi durlabham |
yenaiva kṛtamātreṇa jvaro gṛhṇāti mānavam || 1.31
nimbakāṣṭhākṛtiṃ kṛtvā caturaṅgulamānataḥ |
tasyā hṛdi viṣonmattarājikālavaṇaistathā || 1.32
nāma saṃlikhya prakṛtau pācyamānāyāṃ tataḥ param |
tatastu śatrunāmnā ca aṣṭottaraśataṃ japet || 1.33
tataḥ sa praharārdhena jvarabhūtena gṛhyate |
atha japamantraḥ uoṃ bakāmukhā cāmuṇḍā amukasya kṣīramāṃsaśoṇitabhojinī amukaṃ khaḥ khaḥ jvareṇa gṛhṇa gṛhṇa gṛhṇāpaya gṛhṇāpaya huṃ phaṭsvāhā |
pratyānayaṃ yadīccheta tasyaiṣā kriyate kriyā || 1.34
dugdhasnātanāmākṣarāṇi tadā svastho bhavediti |
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi prayogaṃ bhuvi durlabham || 1.35
yena vijñānamātreṇa śatroruccāṭanaṃ bhavet |
brahmadaṇḍī citābhasma gomayasya tathaiva ca || 1.36
kṣāraṃ cāpi samādāya kākajaṅgāsamanvitam |
kṣipecchirasi śatrūṇāṃ tūrṇamuccāṭayedripum || 1.37
tataḥ saṃsnāpayedenaṃ gokṣīreṇa samanvitam |
muṇḍanaṃ cātha keśānāṃ tataḥ svastho bhaveddhruvam || 1.38
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi śatroruccāṭanaṃ varam |
dhūpaṃ nirbhedakaṃ nāma svayaṃ rudreṇa bhāṣitam || 1.39
brahmadaṇḍī samūlā ca kākajaṅgā samanvitā |
nirmokaṃ sarparājasya dhūpamuccāṭayedripūn || 1.40
sarpakañcukamādāya kṛṣṇoragaśirastathā |
nikhanedyasya ca dvāre tamuccāṭayate haṭhāt || 1.41
brahmadaṇḍī surāmāṃsī kacchapasya śirastathā |
śmaśānabhasmasaṃyuktaṃ kapāle mānuṣe nyaset || 1.42
śatrordvāre nikhātena tūrṇamuccāṭayedripūn |
saptarātrau deveśi samūlaṃ naśyate gṛham || 1.43
atha vidveṣaṇaṃ vakṣye śatrūṇāṃ śṛṇu śaṃkari |
yenaiva kṛtamātreṇa vidveṣo jāyate nṛṇām || 1.44
brahmadaṇḍī samūlā ca kākamāṃsena saṃyutā |
jātīpuṣparasairbhāvyā saptarātraṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 1.45
tato mārjāramūtreṇa saptāhaṃ bhāvayettataḥ |
eṣa dhūpaḥ pradātavyaḥ śatrugotrasya madhyataḥ || 1.46
yathā gandhaṃ samāghrāti pitā putraiḥ samaṃ kaliḥ |
vidveṣaṇaṃ paraṃ teṣāṃ suhṛdbhirbāndhavaiḥ saha || 1.47
svasthīkaraṇakaṃ proktaṃ ghṛtagugguladhūpataḥ |
atha māraṇam |
surāṃ mānuṣamāṃsaṃ ca samādāya vicakṣaṇaḥ || 1.48
gṛdhrāsthi śatruviṣṭhāṃ ca dvāramadhye nikhātayet |
saptāhena bhavenmāro yathā rudreṇa bhāṣitam || 1.49
brahmadaṇḍī citābhasma surāmāṃsīsamāyutam |
ariṣṭasya ca pattrāṇi viṣaṃ rudhirameva ca || 1.50
mohāya ca prayoktavyaḥ śatrūṇāṃ nāmayogataḥ |
saptarātraprayogena sarvaśatrupraṇāśanam || 1.51
eteṣāṃ duṣṭayogānāṃ śatrave tadudāhṛtam |
śatamaṣṭottareṇaiva mantrayitvā vicakṣaṇaḥ || 1.52
uoṃ namo bhagavate śrīuḍḍāmareśvarāya amukamuccāṭaya uccāṭaya vidveṣaya vidveṣaya svāhā |
ekarātroṣito bhūtvā kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ samāhitaḥ |
devamabhyarcya ceśānaṃ śatamaṣṭādhikaṃ japet || 1.53
mantrametatprayoktavyaṃ siddhaye siddhikāmyayā |
śatrusainye prayoktavyaṃ rājñaśca vijayārthinaḥ || 1.54
lāṅgūlaṃ gṛhagodhāyāḥ kṛkalāsasya mastakam |
indragopakasaṃyuktaṃ jambūkasya śirastathā || 1.55
etāni samabhāgāni sūkṣmacūrṇāni kārayet |
amukte hi prayoktavyaṃ sarvaṃ janaśataṃ haret || 1.56
nṛkapālaṃ samūtraṃ ca pecakasya śirastathā |
vṛścikasya tu lāṅgūlaṃ hālāhalakameva ca || 1.57
śvetamaṇḍūkamāṃsaṃ ca mūtraṃ caiva gajoṣṭrayoḥ |
gṛhītvā samabhāgāni sūkṣmacūrṇāni kārayet || 1.58
etāni śoṣayitvā tu kalkaṃ kṛtvā punaḥ punaḥ |
uoṃ hīṃ yamāya śatrunāśanāya svāhā |
anne pāne pradātavyaṃ ripusainyavināśanam || 1.59
spṛṣṭamātreṇa tenaiva visphoṭāstu samantataḥ |
tasya dehe jvarastīvro bhavettīvrā ca vedanā || 1.60
mriyate saptarātrau pratyānayanavarjitaḥ |
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi yasya dhyānena sādhanam || 1.61
kathitaṃ caiva rudreṇa tridaśebhyaḥ prasādataḥ |
rudhiraṃ kṛṣṇasarpasya kukkuṭasya tu kasyacit || 1.62
kacchapasya mayūrāṇāṃ rocanaṃ jātyañjanaṃ tathā |
kuṅkumaṃ bhadramustaṃ ca tagaraṃ kṛṣṇameva ca || 1.63
etāni samabhāgāni puṣyārke ca samāharet |
aśleṣāyāṃ tu piṣṭāni kṛtvā pātre ca rājate || 1.64
daśāhaṃ sthāpayedbhūmyāṃ paścāduddhṛtya lepayet |
asidhārāṅgadā nāma svayaṃ rudreṇa bhāṣitā || 1.65
athālipettu gātrāṇi sa kṛtvāsthīnyathāpi vā |
phalapuṣpe tathā pattre dhūpasattaistathaiva ca || 1.66
agado'yaṃ mahāmantro bhūtānāṃ ca bhayāvahaḥ |
anne pāne mantrayitvā prayuñjīta vidhānataḥ || 1.67
uoṃ namo bhagavate rudrāya śāntāya divyayogāya divyarūpāya jaṭilabrahmacāriṇe agadokṣitridaśaiva mahābalaśataṃ manohaṃ ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ svāhā |
pīḍyamānaṃ japenmantraṃ śucirbhūtvā samāhitaḥ |
aṣṭottarasahasraṃ tu tato mantramimaṃ japet || 1.68
kanyayā peṣayaccaiva goṣṭhamadhye prayatnataḥ |
tadaśaktau citāyāṃ tu yathāśakti punaḥ punaḥ || 1.69
svayaṃ rudreṇa samproktaṃ sarvakāryaprasādhakam |
sarve caiva prayoktavyāḥ sarvaśatruvināśakāḥ || 1.70
tasmātsarvaprayatnena rakṣitavyāḥ prayogavān |
abhukte naiva prokta yaṃ krūre pāpajane tathā || 1.71
dātavyaṃ bhaktiyuktāya śāstrajñāyāmareśvari |
iti pārvatīśivasaṃvāde vīrabhadreśvaratantroddhṛte uḍḍāmareśvaramahātantre prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1.72


ūḍḍāmareśvaratantra, ḍvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi jalastambhanamuttamam |
kumbhīravelvadaṃṣṭrāni rudhiraṃ māṃsameva ca || 2.1
hṛdayaṃ kacchapasyaiva śiśumāraṃ punaḥ punaḥ |
vibhītakasya tailena sarvānekatra kārayet || 2.2
nyāsakarma tataḥ kṛtvā jale tiṣṭhedyathāsukham |
madrakasya rasaṃ grāhyaṃ lakṣaṇasya makarasya ca || 2.3
ḍuṇḍubhasya śiro grāhyaṃ sarvānekatra kārayet |
vibhītakasya taile tu pacyamāne ca dāpayet || 2.4
nyāsakarma prakurvīta saptāhaṃ tiṣṭhate jale |
tattailaṃ pācayetlauhe kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ samāhitaḥ || 2.5
caturmāsoṣito bhūtvā devamabhyarcya śaṃkaram |
abhimantrya ca mantreṇa mṛttikāsnānadakṣiṇam || 2.6
aṣṭādhikasahasraṃ tu japitvā homamācaret |
anena mantrayitvā tu tataḥ sidhyati nānyathā || 2.7
uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya jalaṃ stambhaya stambhaya huṃ phaṭsvāhā |
vakṣye'tha lūtākaraṇaṃ taṃ śṛṇuṣva samāsataḥ |
bhallātakarasaṃ guñjā viṣaṃ citrakameva ca || 2.8
kapikacchaparomāṇi cūrṇāṃ kṛtvā pradāpayet |
etatsarvasamāyukto lūtākaraṇamuttamam || 2.9
tasya rūpaṃ pravakṣyāmi jāyate yastu lakṣaṇaiḥ |
aṅgāni dhūmamāyānti mūrchayanti muhurmuhuḥ || 2.10
etadrūpaṃ bhavettasya lūtāvikṛtalakṣaṇam |
cikitsāṃ tasya vakṣyāmi yena sampadyate sukham || 2.11
uśīraṃ candanaṃ caiva priyaṅgutagaraṃ tathā |
raktacandanakuṣṭhaṃ ca lepo lūtāvināśanaḥ || 2.12
uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya kuhalīkurvanī svāhā |
mantrābhimantritaṃ kṛtvā tataḥ svastho bhaviṣyati |
kṛṣṇasarpaśiro grāhyaṃ mukhe niḥkṣipya sarṣapān || 2.13
bhallātakena saṃyuktaṃ kṛṣṇasūtreṇa veṣṭayet |
valmīkasya mṛdaḥ kośe antardhūmena pācayet || 2.14
kapikacchaparomāṇi saṃyuktaṃ ṣoḍaśāṃśakaiḥ |
viṣasya cūrṇaṃ kṛtvā tu śatrūṇāṃ mūrdhni niḥkṣipet || 2.15
śaradgrīṣmavasanteṣu lūtākaraṇamuttamam |
prasvinne ca tato gātre lagnāstasmiṃstu sarṣapāḥ || 2.16
lūtāṃ ca saviṣāṃ kuryānnātra kāryā vicāraṇā |
vedanājātamātreṇa mantrajāpaṃ tu pūrvavat || 2.17
pūrvo vidhānena svastho bhavati pūrvavat |
atha śasyavināśaṃ ca kathayāmi samāsataḥ || 2.18
yenaiva kṛtamātreṇa vajraṃ kṛtvā vicakṣaṇaḥ |
kṣetre sampātayedyasmiṃstasmiñśasyavināśanam || 2.19
māhendreṇa kṣipettatra prayogeṇa tu mantravit |
atha mantraṃ punarvakṣye prayogeṣu prayojakam || 2.20
aṣṭottaraśatenaiva mantreṇānena mantrayet |
uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya vajraṃ vināśaya vajraṃ surapatirājñāpaya huṃ phaṭsvāhā |
imaṃ yogaṃ prayuñjāno vidhipūrveṇa karmaṇā |
parṇānāṃ caiva yogena kṣipetparṇaṃ vinaśyati || 2.21
punaruccāṭanaṃ vakṣye śṛṇu putra yathātathā |
yenaiva kṛtamātreṇa grāmasyoccāṭanaṃ bhavet || 2.22
grāme caturṇāṃ ca pathāṃ mṛdamādāya buddhimān |
gomayenākṛtiṃ kṛtvā grāmasya ca caturdiśaḥ || 2.23
citākāṣṭhānalaṃ kṛtvā kokilākākapakṣakaiḥ |
hutvā cāhutisāhasraṃ tato bhasma samāharet || 2.24
abhedena samutsārya kṛtvā muṣṭiṃ sabhasmakam |
śatavārābhijaptena anenaiva tu mantritaḥ || 2.25
uoṃ namo bhagavate mahākālarudrāya tripuravināśanakāraṇāya daha daha dhama dhama paca paca matha matha mohaya mohaya unmādaya unmādaya ucchedaya ucchedaya śrīmahārudra ājñāpayati śabdakarī mohinī bhagavatī kheṃ kheṃ huṃ phaṭsvāhā |
grāme vā nagare vāpi bhasmaprakṣepaṇena ca |
ucchedanaṃ bhavatyeva ripūṇāṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ || 2.26
dūrīkṛtaṃ punarbhasma nagare vasate punaḥ |
kulvīkāraṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu yogaṃ samāsataḥ || 2.27
yena yojitamātreṇa punaḥ kulvo bhaviṣyati |
pādapāṃśusamāyuktairantarā ghṛtagomayaiḥ || 2.28
vṛṣabhasya punaḥ śatroḥ kṛtvā caivākṛtiṃ budhaḥ |
ekaviṃśativāraṃ hi mantreṇānena mantritām || 2.29
chedayettīvraśastreṇa tataḥ kulvo bhaviṣyati |
uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya kāmaprabhañjanāya amukaṃ cchaḥ cchaḥ svāhā |
prasthitānāṃ ca karaṇe mantreṇānena mantravit || 2.30
kṛtvā madhu ghṛtāktaṃ ca sthāne hyatra prayojayet |
snātvā ca gavyadugdhena tataḥ svastho bhaviṣyati || 2.31
atha unmattīkaraṇam |
mūlaṃ kanakabījasya ghṛtacūrṇaṃ samantataḥ |
gṛhacaṭakasya viṣṭā ca tathā karañjabījakam || 2.32
etadunmattakaraṃ cūrṇaṃ bhakṣaṇāttatkaraṃ vrajet |
ekaviṃśativāraṃ ca mantreṇānena mantritam || 2.33
khāne pāne pradātavyamunmattaḥ syānna saṃśayaḥ |
uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya amukamunmādaya unmādaya cchaḥ cchaḥ svāhā |
ajakṣīreṇa śoṇitena pibettu śatapuṣpikām || 2.34
ghṛtena saha vā pītvā tataḥ sampadyate sukham |
ityunmattīkaraṇam |
ekaviṃśativāraṃ hi mantritaṃ lavaṇaṃ kharam || 2.35
karavīrakakāṣṭhāgnau mantrairdhūmena pācayet |
lavaṇaṃ tantu saṃgṛhya cūrṇaṃ kṛtvā vicakṣaṇaḥ || 2.36
khāne pāne pradātavyaṃ mantreṇānena mantritam |
mantritaṃ śatavāraṃ ca śatrorvā yasya kasyacit || 2.37
bhakṣaṇācca bhavedandho nātra kāryā vicāraṇā |
uoṃ nama uḍḍāmareśvarāya śarīramandhaṃ kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ svāhā |
athānyatsampravakṣyāmi kuṣṭhīkaraṇamuttamam || 2.38
