atha saptaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
śrībhagavānuvāca ---


evaṃ kṛtvā yathāśāstraṃ divyamabjaja vai purā  |
pitryaṃ tadanu vai kuryātvidhidṛṣṭena karmaṇā  || 27.1 ||

pauṣkarauvāca---

jñātumicchāmi bhagavān tvacchāsanasthitaiḥ  |
samuddiśya pitṛśrāddhaṃ kāryamācāryapūrvakaiḥ  || 27.2 ||

keṣāṃ keṣāṃ ca tatkāryaṃ kasya kasya ca kīdṛśam  |
vidadhāti pañca pañcaiva (?kathaṃcaiva)samyaṅnirvartitaṃ hi yat || 27.3 ||

śrī bhagavānuvāca ---

samāśritya nimittaṃ vai deśakālādiktaṃ dvija  || 27.15 ||

ata eva hi viprendra pitṝṇāṃ tu kuśāsanam  || 27.41 ||

yatkiṃciddīyate bhaktayā brahmabhūtaṃ tu tadbhavet || 27.42 ||

purā vai hetunānena nṛṇāmaviditātmanām  |
karmavyatra kuśājālaṃ vihitaṃ kamalodbhava  || 27.43 ||

bhāvasthatatvatastābhisteṣāṃ vai na prayojanam  || 27.44 ||

bahistadāsane kāryā tvagre dairghyācchamādhikā  |
vaipulyācchamamānaṃ tu prāgvatpāvanatāṃ nayet || 27.46 ||
