kṛṣṇāmṛtamahārṇavaḥ

oṃ.

arcitaḥ saṃsmṛto dhyātaḥ kīrtitaḥ kathitaḥ śrutaḥ
yo dadātyamṛtatvaṃ hi sa māṃ rakṣatu keśavaḥ. || 1 ||

tāpatrayeṇa saṃtaptaṃ yadetadakhilaṃ jagat
vakṣyāmi śāntaye hyasya kṛṣṇāmṛtamahārṇavam. || 2 ||

te narāḥ paśavo loke kiṃ teṣāṃ jīvane phalam
yairna labdhā harerdīkṣā nārcito vā janārdanaḥ. || 3 ||

saṃsāre'sminmahāghore janmarogabhayākule
ayameko mahābhāgaḥ pūjyate yadadhokṣajaḥ. || 4 ||

sa nāma sukṛtī loke kulaṃ tena hyalaṃkṛtam
ādhāraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ yena viṣṇuḥ prasāditaḥ. || 5 ||

yajñānāṃ tapasāṃ caiva śubhānāṃ caiva karmaṇāṃ
tadviśiṣṭaphalaṃ nṛṇāṃ sadaivārādhanaṃ hareḥ. || 6 ||

kalau kalimaladhvaṃsisarvapāpaharaṃ harim
ye'rcayanti narā nityaṃ te'pi vandyā yathā hariḥ. || 7 ||

nāsti śreyaskaraṃ nṛṇāṃ viṣṇorārādhanānmune
yuge'smiṃstāmase loke satataṃ pūjyate nṛbhiḥ. || 8 ||

arcite sarvadeveśe śaṅkacakragadādhare
arcitāḥ sarvadevāḥ syuryataḥ sarvagato hariḥ. || 9 ||

svarcite sarvalokeśe surāsuranamaskṛte
keśave kaṃsakeśighne na yāti narakaṃ naraḥ. || 10 ||

sakṛdabhyarcya govindaṃ bilvapatreṇa mānavaḥ
muktibhāgī nirātaṅkī viṣṇuloke ciraṃ vaset. || 11 ||

sakṛdabhyarcito yena heḻayā'pi namaskṛtaḥ
sa yāti paramaṃ sthānaṃ yatsurairapi durlabham. || 12 ||

ṇāradaḥ:
samastalokanāthasya devadevasya śārṅgiṇaḥ
sākṣādbhagavato viṣṇoḥ pūjanaṃ janmanaḥ phalam. || 13 ||

ড়ulastyaḥ:
bhaktyā dūrvāṅkuraiḥ pumbhiḥ pūjitaḥ puruṣottamaḥ
harirdadāti hi phalaṃ sarvayajñaiśca durlabham. || 14 ||

vidhinā devadeveśaḥ śaṅkhacakradharo hariḥ
phalaṃ dadāti sulabhaṃ salilenāpi pūjitaḥ. || 15 ||

narake pacyamānastu yamena paribhāṣitaḥ
kiṃ tvayā nārcito devaḥ keśavaḥ kleśanāśanaḥ. || 16 ||

ḍharmaḥ:
narasiṃho hṛṣīkeśaḥ puṇḍarīkanibhekṣaṇaḥ
smaraṇānmuktido nṛṇāṃ sa tvayā kiṃ na pūjitaḥ. || 17 ||

dravyāṇāmapyabhāve tu salilenāpi pūjitaḥ
yo dadāti svakaṃ sthānaṃ sa tvayā kiṃ na pūjitaḥ. || 18 ||

garbhasthitāmṛtā vā'pi muṣitāste sudūṣitāḥ
na prāptā yairharerdīkṣā sarvaduḥkhavimocanī. || 19 ||

ṃārkaṇḍeyaḥ:
sakṛdabhyarcito yena devadevo janārdanaḥ
yatkṛtaṃ tatkṛtaṃ tena samprāptaṃ paramaṃ padam. || 20 ||

dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇāṃ nānyopāyastu vidyate
satyaṃ bravīmi deveśa hṛṣīkeśārcanāhate. || 21 ||

tasya yajñavarāhasya viṣṇoramitatejasaḥ
praṇāmaṃ ye'pi kurvanti teṣāmapi namo namaḥ. || 22 ||

ṃrīciḥ:
anārādhitagovindairnaraiḥ sthānaṃ nṛpātmaja
na hi samprāpyate śreṣṭhaṃ tasmādārādhayācyutam. || 23 ||

ātriḥ:
paraḥ parāṇāṃ puruṣastuṣṭo yasya janārdanaḥ
sa cā'pnotyakṣayaṃ sthānametatsatyaṃ mayoditam. || 24 ||

āṅgirāḥ:
yasyāntaḥ sarvamevedamacyutasyākhyayātmanaḥ
tamārādhaya govindaṃ sthānamagryaṃ yadīcchasi. || 25 ||

ড়ulahaḥ:
paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma yo'sau brahma sanātanam
tamārādhya hariṃ yāti muktimapyatidurlabhām. || 26 ||

aindramindraḥ paraṃ sthānaṃ yamārādhya jagatpatim
prāpa yajñapatiṃ viṣṇuṃ tamārādhaya suvrata. || 27 ||

prāpnotyārādhite viṣṇau manasā yadyadicchati
trailokyāntargataṃ sthānaṃ kimu lokottarottarān. || 28 ||

ye smaranti sadā viṣṇuṃ śaṅkhacakragadādharam
sarvapāpavinirmuktāḥ paraṃ brahma viśanti te. || 29 ||

tato'niruddhaṃ deveśaṃ pradyumnaṃ ca tataḥ param
tataḥ saṃkarṣaṇaṃ devaṃ vāsudevaṃ parātparam. || 30 ||

vāsudevātparaṃ nāsti iti vedāntaniścayaḥ
vāsudevaṃ praviṣṭānāṃ punarāvartanaṃ kutaḥ. || 31 ||

ātreyaḥ:
yo yānicchennaraḥ kāmānnārī vā varavarṇinī
tān samāpnoti vipulān samārādhya janārdhanam. || 32 ||

ড়ulaha sagte:

Brahmā:
bāhubhyāṃ sāgaraṃ tartuṃ ka iccheta pumān bhuvi
vāsudevamanārādhya ko mokṣaṃ gantumicchati. || 33 ||

śaṃkaraḥ:
kṛte pāpe'nutāpo vai yasya puṃsaḥ prajāyate
prāyaścittaṃ tu tasyoktaṃ harisaṃsmaraṇaṃ param. || 34 ||

na hyapuṇyavatāṃ loke mūḍhānāṃ kuṭilātmanāṃ
bhaktirbhavati govinde smaraṇaṃ kīrtanaṃ tathā. || 35 ||

tadaiva puruṣo mukto janmaduḥkhajarādibhiḥ
jitendriyo viśuddhātmā yadaiva smarate harim. || 36 ||

prāpte kaliyuge ghore dharmajñānavivarjite
na kaścitsmarate devaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ kalimalāpaham. || 37 ||

na kalau devadevasya janmaduḥkhāpahāriṇaḥ
karoti martyo mūḍhātmā smaraṇaṃ kīrtanaṃ hareḥ. || 38 ||

ye smaranti sadā viṣṇuṃ viśuddhenāntarātmanā
te prayānti bhavaṃ tyaktvā viṣṇulokamanāmayam. || 39 ||

garbhajanmajarārogaduḥkha saṃsārabandhanaiḥ
na bādhyate naro nityaṃ vāsudevamanusmaran. || 40 ||

yamamārgāṃ mahāghoraṃ narakāṇi yamaṃ tathā
svapne'pi ca na paśyeta yaḥ smaredgaruḍadhvajam. || 41 ||

hṛdi rūpaṃ mukhe nāma naivedyamudare hareḥ
pādodakaṃ ca nirmālyaṃ mastake yasya so'cyutaḥ. || 42 ||

govindasmaraṇaṃ puṃsāṃ pāparāśimahācalam
asaṃśayaṃ dahatyāśu tūlarāśimivānalaḥ. || 43 ||

āgastyaḥ:
smaraṇādeva kṛṣṇasya pāpasaṅghātapañjaram
śatadhā bhedamāyāti girirvajrahato tathā. || 44 ||

kṛṣṇe ratāḥ kṛṣṇamanusmarantaḥ tadbhāvitāstadgatamānasāśca.
te bhinnadehāḥ praviśanti viṣṇuṃ haviryathā mantrahutaṃ hutāśe. || 45 ||

sā hānistanmahatchidraṃ sā cāndhajaḍamūkatā
yanmuhūrtaṃ kṣaṇaṃ vā'pi vāsudevo na cintyate. || 46 ||

nārāyaṇo nāma naro narāṇāṃ prasaṃhya coraḥ kathitaḥ pṛthivyām
anekajanmārjitapāpasañcayaṃ dahatyaśeṣaṃ smṛtamātrayaiva. || 47 ||

yasya saṃsmaraṇādeva vāsudevasya śārṅgiṇaḥ
koṭijanmārjitaṃ pāpaṃ takṣaṇādeva naśyati. || 48 ||

kiṃ tasya bahubhistīrthaiḥ kiṃ tapobhiḥ kimadhvaraiḥ
yo nityaṃ dhyāyate devaṃ nārāyaṇamananyadhīḥ. || 49 ||

ye mānavā vigatarāgaparāvarajñā nārāyaṇaṃ suraguruṃ satataṃ smaranti
dhyānena tena hatakilbiṣacetanāste mātuḥ payodhararasaṃ na punaḥ pibanti. || 50 ||

he citta cintaya sveha vāsudevamaharniśam
nūnaṃ yaścintitaḥ puṃsāṃ hanti saṃsārabandhanam. || 51 ||

āloḍya sarvaśāstrāṇi vicārya ca punaḥ punaḥ
idamekaṃ suniṣpannaṃ dhyeyo nārāyaṇaḥ sadā. || 52 ||

smṛte sakalakalyāṇabhājanaṃ yatra jāyate
puruṣastamajaṃ nityaṃ vrajāmi śaraṇaṃ harim. || 53 ||

vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapasu caiva dāneṣu tīrtheṣu vrateṣu caiva
iṣṭeṣu pūrteṣu ca yatpradiṣṭaṃ puṇyaṃ smṛte tatkhalu vāsudeve. || 54 ||

āurvaḥ:
ārādhayaiva naro viṣṇuṃ manaso yadyadicchati
phalaṃ prāpnotyavikalaṃ bhūri svalpamathāpi vā. || 55 ||

yannāmakīrtanaṃ bhaktyā vilāyanamanuttamam
maitreyāśeṣapāpānāṃ dhātūnāmiva pāvakaḥ. || 56 ||

kalikalmaṣamatyugraṃ narakārtipradaṃ nṛṇām
prayāti vilayaṃ sadyaḥ sakṛtsaṃkīrtite'cyute. || 57 ||

anāyāsena cā'yānti muktiṃ keśavamāsritāḥ
tadvighātāya jāyante śakrādyāḥ paripanthinaḥ. || 58 ||

catussāgaramāsādya jambūdvīpottame kvacit
na pumān keśavādanyaḥ sarvapāpacikitsakaḥ. || 59 ||

yadabhyarcya hariṃ bhaktyā kṛte varṣaśatairapi
phalaṃ prāpnotyavikalaṃ kalau saṃkīrtya keśavam. || 60 ||

kṣīyate tu yadā dharmaḥ prāpte ghore kalau yuge
tadā na kīrtayetkaścinmuktidaṃ devamacyutam. || 61 ||

avaśenāpi yannāmni kīrtite sarvapātakaiḥ
pumān vimucyate sadyaḥ siṃhatrastamṛgairiva. || 62 ||

nārāyaṇeti mantro'sti vāgasti vaśavartinī
tathā'pi narake ghore majjantītyetadadbhutam. || 63 ||

ārtā viṣaṇṇāḥ śithilāśca bhītā ghoreṣu ca vyādhiṣu vartamānāḥ
saṃkīrtya nārāyaṅaśabdamātraṃ vimuktaduḥkhāḥ sukhino bhavanti. || 64 ||

Kauśikaḥ:
sakṛduccāritaṃ yaistu kṛṣṇeti na viśanti te
garbhāgāraṃ gṛhaṃ māturyamalokaṃ ca dussaham. || 65 ||

kva nākapṛṣṭhagamanaṃ punarāvṛttilakṣaṇam
kva japo vāsudeveti muktibījamanuttamam. || 66 ||

buddhyā buddhvā vadasvainaṃ harirityakṣaradvayam
smaraṇādeva kṛṣṇasya na punarjāyate kvacit. || 67 ||

he jihve mama nisnehe hariṃ kiṃ nuna bhāṣase
hariṃ vadasva kalyāṇi saṃsārodadhinaurhariḥ. || 68 ||

asāre khalu saṃsāre sārātsārataro hariḥ
puṇyahīnā na vindanti sāraṅgāśca yathā jalam. || 69 ||

kurukṣetreṇa kiṃ tasya kiṃ kāśyā puṣkareṇa vā
jihvāgre vartate tasya harirityakṣaradvayam. || 70 ||

Brahmovāca:
asāre khalu saṃsāre sāramekaṃ nirūpitam
samastalokanāthasya sāramārādhanaṃ hareḥ. || 71 ||

sā jihvā yā hariṃ stauti taccittaṃ yattadarpaṇam
tāveva kevalau ślāghyau yau tatpūjākarau karau. || 72 ||

yastu viṣṇuparo nityaṃ dṛḍhabhaktirjitendriyaḥ
svagṛhe'pi vasan yāti tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam. || 73 ||

śaṅkaraḥ:
sādhu sādhu mahābhāga sādhu dānavanāśan
yanmāṃ pṛcchasi dharmajña keśavārādhanaṃ prati. || 74 ||

nimiṣaṃ nimiṣārdhaṃ vā muhūrtamapi bhārgava
nādagdhāśeṣapāpānāṃ bhakti bhavati keśave. || 75 ||

kiṃ tena manasā kāryaṃ yanna tiṣṭhati keśave
mano muktiphalāvāptyau kāraṇaṃ saprayojanam. || 76 ||

rogo nāma na sā jihvā yayā na stūyate hariḥ
gartau nāma na tau karṇau yābhyāṃ tatkarma na śrutam. || 77 ||
nūnaṃ tatkaṇṭhaśālūkamathavā'pyupajihvikā
rogo nāma na sā jihvā yā na vakti harerguṇān. || 78 ||

bhārabhūtaiḥ kāryamebhiḥ kiṃ tasya nṛpaśordvija
caraṇau tau tu saphalau keśavālayagāminau
te ca netre mahābhāga yābhyāṃ sandṛśyate hariḥ. || 79 ||

kiṃ tasya caraṇaiḥ kāryaṃ vṛthāsañcaraṇairdvija
yairhi na vrajate jantuḥ keśavālayadarśane. || 80 ||

vedavedāṅgaviduṣāṃ munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām
ṛṣitvamapi dharmajña vijñeyaṃ tatprasādajam. || 81 ||

vicitraratnaparyaṅke mahābhogena bhoginaḥ
ramante nākanārībhiḥ keśavasmaraṇātphalam. || 82 ||

aśvamedhasahasrāṇāṃ yaḥ sahasraṃ samācaret
nāsau tatphalamāpnoti tadbhaktairyadavāpyate. || 83 ||

re re manuṣyāḥ puruṣottamasya karau na kasmānmukulīkurudhve
kriyājuṣāṃ ko bhavatāṃ prayāsaḥ phalaṃ hi yatpadamacyutasya. || 84 ||

viṣṇorvimānaṃ yaḥ kuryātsakṛdbhaktyā pradakṣiṇam
aśvamedhasahasrasya phalamāpnoti mānavaḥ. || 85 ||

pradakṣiṇaṃ tu yaḥ kuryādharibhaktyā samanvitaḥ
haṃsayuktivimānena viṣṇulokaṃ sa gacchati. || 86 ||

tīrthakoṭisahasrāṇi vratakoṭiśatāni ca
nārāyaṇapraṇāmasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm. || 87 ||

urasā śirasā dṛṣṭyā manasā vacasā tathā
padbhyāṃ karābhyāṃ jānubhyāṃ praṇāmo'ṣṭāṅga īritaḥ. || 88 ||

śāṭhyenāpi namaskāraṃ kurvataḥ śārṅgapāṇaye
śātajanmārjitaṃ pāpaṃ naśyatyeva na saṃśayaḥ. || 89 ||

saṃsārārṇavamagrānāṃ narāṇāṃ pāpakarmaṇām
nānyo dharttā jagannāthaṃ muktvā nārāyaṇaṃ param. || 90 ||

reṇukuṇṭhigātrasya kaṇā yāvanti bhārata
tāvadvarṣasahasrāṇi viṣṇuloke mahīyate. || 91 ||

pāvanaṃ viṣṇunaivedyaṃ subhojyamṛṣibhiḥ smṛtam
anyadevasya naivedyaṃ bhuktvā cāndrāyaṇaṃ caret. || 92 ||

koṭyaindavasahasraistu māsopoṣaṇakoṭibhiḥ
yatphalaṃ labhyate pumbhirviṣṇornaivedya bhakṣaṇāt. || 93 ||

trirātraphaladā nadyo yāḥ kāścidasamudragāḥ
samudragāstu pakṣasya māsasya saritāṃ patiḥ. || 94 ||

ṣaṇmāsaphaladā godā vatsarasya tu jāhnavī
viṣṇupādodakasyaite kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍasīm. || 95 ||

gaṇgāprayāgagayapuṣkaranaimiśāni saṃsevitāni bahuśaḥ kurujāṅgalāni
kālena tīrthasalilāni punanti pāpaṃ pādodakaṃ bhagavataḥ prapunāti sadyaḥ. || 96 ||

yāni kāni ca tīrthāni brahmāṇḍāntargatāni vai
viṣṇupādodakasyaite kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm. || 97 ||

snānaṃ pādodakaṃ viṣṇoḥ piban śirasi dhārayan
sarva pāpavinirmukto vaiṣṇavīṃ siddhimāpnuyāt. || 98 ||

yathā pādodakaṃ puṇyaṃ nirmālyaṃ cānulepanam
naivedyaṃ dhūpaśeṣaṃ ca ārārttiśca tathā hareḥ. || 99 ||

tulasyāstu rajojuṣṭaṃ naivedyasya ca bhakṣaṇam
nirmālyaṃ śirasā dhāryaṃ mahāpātakanāśanam. || 100 ||

bhaktyā vā yadi vā'bhaktyā cakrāṅkitaśilāṃ prati
darśanaṃ sparśanaṃ vā'pi sarvapāpapraṇāśanam. || 101 ||

sāḻagrāmodbhavo devo devo dvāravatībhavaḥ
ubhayoḥ snānatoyena brahmahatyāṃ vyapohati. || 102 ||

mlecchadeśe'śucau vā'pi cakrāṅko yatra tiṣṭhati
yojanāni tathā trīṇi mama kṣetraṃ vasundhare. || 103 ||

śālagrāmodbhavo devo  śailaṃ cakrāṅkamaṇḍitam
yatrāpi nīyate tatra vāraṇasyā śatādhikam. || 104 ||

sāḻagrāmodbhavo devo devo dvāravatībhavaḥ
ubhayoḥ saṃgamo yatra tatra muktirna saṃśayaḥ. || 105 ||

hariṇā muktidānīha muktisthānāni sarvaśaḥ
sa yasya sarvabhāveṣu tasya taiḥ kiṃ prayojanam. || 106 ||

hariryāti hariryāti dasyuvyājena yo vadet
so'pi sadgatimāpnoti gatiṃ sukṛtino yathā. || 107 ||

vāsudevaṃ parityajya yo'nyaṃ devamupāsate
tyaktvā'mṛtaṃ sa mūḍhātmā bhuṅkte hālāhalaṃ viṣam. || 108 ||

tyaktvāmṛtaṃ yathā kaścidanyapānaṃ pibennaraḥ
tathā hariṃ parityajya cānyaṃ devamupāsate. || 109 ||

svadharmaṃ tu parityajya paradharmaṃ yathā caret
tathā hariṃ parityajya cānyaṃ devamupāsate. || 110 ||

gāṃ ca tyaktvā vimūḍhātmā gardabhīṃ vandate yathā
tathā hariṃ parityajya cānyaṃ devamupāsate. || 111 ||

vāsudevaṃ parityajya yo'nyaṃ devamupāsate
tṛṣito jāhnavītīre kūpaṃ khanati durmatiḥ. || 112 ||

yathā gaṅgodakaṃ tyaktvā pibetkūpodakaṃ naraḥ
tathā hariṃ parityajya yo'nyaṃ devamupāsate. || 113 ||

svamātaraṃ parityajya śvapākīṃ vandate tathā
tathā hariṃ parityajya yo'nyaṃ devamupāsate. || 114 ||

yāvatsvasthamidaṃ piṇḍaṃ nirujaṃ karaṇānvitam
tāvatkuruṣvā'tmahitaṃ paścāttāpena tapyase. || 115 ||

yāvatsvāsthyaṃ śarīreṣu karaṇeṣu ca pāṭavam
tāvadarcaya govindamāyuśyaṃ sārthakaṃ kuru. || 116 ||

smaryatāṃ tu hṛṣīkeśo hṛṣīkeṣu dṛḍheṣu ca
adṛḍheṣu hṛṣīkeṣu hṛṣīkeśaṃ smaranti ke. || 117 ||

yāvaccintayate janturviṣayān viṣasannibhān
tāvaccetsmarate viṣṇuṃ ko na mucyeta bandhanāt. || 118 ||

yāvatpralapate janturviṣayān viṣasannibhān
tāvaccetsmarate viṣṇuṃ ko na mucyeta bandhanāt. || 119 ||

ṣūta uvāca:
jñātvā viprāstithiṃ samyagdaivajñaiḥ samudīritām
kartavya upavāsaśca anyathā narakaṃ vrajet. || 120 ||

kṣaye vā'pyathavā vṛddhau samprāpte vā dinakṣaye
upoṣyā dvādaśī puṇyā pūrvaviddhāṃ parityajet. || 121 ||

pūrvaviddhāṃ prakurvāṇo naro dharmānnikṛntati
saṃtatestu vināśāya sampado haraṇāya ca. || 122 ||

kalāvedhe tu viprendrā daśamyaikādaśīṃ tyajet
surāyā bindunā spṛṣṭaṃ gaṅgāmbha iva santyajet. || 123 ||

śvadṛtau pañcagavyaṃ ca daśamyā dūṣitāṃ tyajet
ekādaśīṃ dvijaśreṣṭhāḥ pakṣayorubhayorapi. || 124 ||

tasmādviprā na viddhā hi kartavyaikādasī kvacit
viddhā hanti purāpuṇyaṃ śrāddhaṃ ca vṛṣalīpatiḥ. || 125 ||

japtaṃ dattaṃ hutaṃ snātaṃ tathā pūjā kṛtā hareḥ
tatsarvaṃ vilayaṃ yāti tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā. || 126 ||

ekādaśyāṃ yadā brahman dinakṣayatithirbhavet
upoṣyā dvādaśī tatra trayodaśyāṃ tu pāraṇam. || 127 ||

pratipatprabhṛtayaḥ sarvā udayādudayādraveḥ
sampūrṇā iti vijñeyā harivāsaravarjitāḥ. || 128 ||

aruṇodayakāle tu daśamī yadi dṛśyate
pāpamūlaṃ tadā jñeyamekādaśyupavāsanam. || 129 ||

aruṇodayakāle tu daśamī yadi dṛśyate
na tatraikādaśī kāryā dharmakāmārthanāśinī. || 130 ||

catastro ghaṭikāḥ prātararuṇodaya ucyate
yatīnāṃ snānakālo'yaṃ gaṅgāmbhaḥsadṛśaḥ smṛtaḥ. || 131 ||

udayātprāgyadā viprā muhūrtadvayasaṃyutā
sampūrṇaikādaśī nāma tatraivopavasedvratī. || 132 ||

udayātprāktrighaṭikā vyāpinyekādaśī yadā
sandigdhaikādaśī nāma varjyā dharmārthanāśinī. || 133 ||

putrapautravivṛddhyarthaṃ dvādaśyāmupavāsayet
tatra kratuśataṃ puṇyaṃ trayodaśyāṃ tu pāraṇaṃ. || 134 ||

udayātprāgdvighaṭikāvyāpinyekādaśī yadā
saṅkīrṇaikādaśī nāma varjyā dharmārthakāṅkṣibhiḥ. || 135 ||

putrarājyavivṛddhyarthaṃ dvādaśyāmupavāsanam
tatra kratuśataṃ puṇyaṃ trayodaśyāṃ tu pāraṇam. || 136 ||

daśamīśeṣasaṃyuktā gāndhāryā samupoṣitā
tasyāḥ putraśataṃ naṣṭaṃ tasmāttāṃ parivarjayet. || 137 ||

bahvāgamavirodheṣu brāhmaṇeṣu vivādiṣu
upoṣyā dvādaśī puṇyā trayodaśyāṃ tu pāraṅam. || 138 ||

ekādaśyāṃ tu viddhāyāṃ samprāpte śravaṇe tathā
upoṣyā dvādaśī puṇyā pakṣayorubhayorapi. || 139 ||

uparāgasahasrāṇi vyatīpātāyutāni ca
amālakṣaṃ tu dvādaśyāḥ kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm. || 140 ||

śuddhāpi dvādaśī grāhyā parato dvādaśī yadi
viṣaṃ tu daśamī jñeyā'mṛtaṃ caikādaśī tithiḥ.
viṣapradhānā varjyā sā'mṛtā grāhyā pradhānataḥ. || 141 ||

dvādaśyāṃ bhojanaṃ caiva viddhāyāṃ haryupoṣaṇam
yaḥ kuryānmandabuddhitvānnirayaṃ so'dhigacchati. || 142 ||

yāni kāni ca vākyāni viddhopāsyāparāṇi tu
dhanadārcāparāṇi syurvaiṣṇavī na daśāyutā. || 143 ||

athavā mohanārthāya mohinyā bhagavān hariḥ
arthitaḥ kārayāmāsa vyāsarūpī janārdanaḥ. || 144 ||

dhanadārcāvivṛddhyarthaṃ mahāvittalayasya ca
asurāṇāṃ mohanārthaṃ pāṣaṇḍānāṃ vivṛddhaye
ātmasvarūpāvijñaptyai svarūpāprāptaye tathā. || 145 ||

evaṃ viddhāṃ parityajya dvādaśyāmupavāsane
koṭijanmārjitaṃ pāpamekayaiva vinaśyati
tataḥ koṭiguṇaṃ vā'pi niṣiddhasyetarairjanaiḥ. || 146 ||

yadanādikṛtaṃ pāpaṃ tadūrdhvaṃ yatkariṣyati
tatsarvaṃ vilayaṃ yāti pareṣāmupavāsanāt
na ca tasmātpriyatamaḥ keśavasya mamāpi vā. || 147 ||

ekādaśyā hyavedhe tu dvādaśīṃ na parityajet
pāraṇe maraṇe caiva tithistātkālikī smṛtā. || 148 ||

brahmacārī gṛhastho vā vānaprastho yatistathā
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdro bhartṛmatī tathā. || 149 ||

abhartṛkā tathā'nye vā sūtavaidehikādayaḥ
ekādaśyāṃ na bhuñjīta pakṣayorubhayorapi. || 150 ||

ekādaśyāṃ tu yo bhuṅkte mohenā'vṛtacetanaḥ
śuklāyāmatha kṛṣṇāyāṃ nirayaṃ yāti sa dhruvam. || 151 ||

vivecayati yo mohācchuklā kṛṣṇeti pāpakṛt
ekādaśīṃ sa vai yāti nirayaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ. || 152 ||

yathā gaurnaiva hantavyā śuklā kṛṣṇeti bhāmini
ekadāśyāṃ na bhuñjīta pakṣayorubhayorapi. || 153 ||

yāni kāni ca vākyāni kṛṣṇaikādaśivarjane
bharaṇyādiniṣedhe'pi tāni kāmyaphalārthinām. || 154 ||

kāmino'pi hi nityārthaṃ kuryurevopavāsanam
prīṇanārthaṃ harernityaṃ na tu kāmavyapekṣayā. || 155 ||

tasmācchuklāmatho kṛṣṇāṃ bharaṇyādiyutāmapi
pratyavāyaniṣedhārthamupavāsīta nityaśaḥ
prīṇanārthaṃ hareśvāpi viṣṇulokasya cā'ptaye. || 156 ||

kalā vā ghaṭikā vā'pi apare dvādaśī yadi
dvādaśadvādaśīrhanti pūrvedyuḥ pāraṇe kṛte. || 157 ||

atiriktā dvādaśī cetsa tāṃ nopoṣayedyadi
dvādaśadvādaśīrhanti dvādaśī cātilaṅghitā. || 158 ||

dvādaśyāmatiriktāyāṃ yo bhuṅkte pūrvavāsare
dvādaśadvādaśīrhanti dvādaśīṃ na parityajet. || 159 ||

dvādaśīṃ śravaṇopetāṃ yo nopoṣyātsumandadhīḥ
pañcasaṃvatsarakṛtaṃ puṇyaṃ tasya vinaśyati. || 160 ||

ekādaśīmupoṣyātha dvādaśīmapyupoṣayet
na tatra vidhilopaḥ syādubhayordevatā hariḥ. || 161 ||

pārayitvodakenāpi bhuñjāno naiva duṣyati
aśitānaśitā yasmādāpo vidvadbhirīritāḥ
ambhasā kevalenātha kariṣye vratapāraṇam. || 162 ||

na kāśī na gayā gaṅgā na reva na ca gautamī
na cāpi kauravaṃ kṣetraṃ samā bhūpa harerdināt. || 163 ||

aśvamedhasahasrāṇi vājapeyāyutāni ca
ekādaśyupavāsasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm. || 164 ||

ekādaśīsamutthena vahninā pātakendhanam
bhasmībhavati rājendra api janmaśatodbhavam. || 165 ||

nedṛśaṃ pāvanaṃ kiñcinnarāṇāṃ bhuvi vidyate
yādṛśaṃ padmanābhasya dinaṃ pātakahānidam. || 166 ||

tāvatpāpāni dehe'smiṃstiṣṭhanti manujādhipa
yāvannopoṣayejjantuḥ padmanābhadinaṃ śivam. || 167 ||

ekādaśendriyaiḥ pāpaṃ yatkṛtaṃ bhavati prabho
ekādaśyupavāsena tatsarvaṃ vilayaṃ nayet. || 168 ||

ekādaśīsamaṃ kiñcitpāpatrāṇaṃ na vidyate
vyājenāpi kṛtā rājanna darśayati bhāskarim. || 169 ||

śrī Vedavyāsa uvāca:
sa brahmahā sa goghnaśca stenaḥ sa gurutalpagaḥ
ekādaśyāṃ tu bhuñjānaḥ pakṣayorubhayorapi. || 170 ||

varaṃ svamātṛgamanaṃ varaṃ gomāṃsabhakṣaṇam
varaṃ hatyā surāpānamekādaśyāṃ tu bhojanāt. || 171 ||

ekādaśīdine puṇye bhuñjate ye narādhamāḥ
avalokya mukhaṃ teṣāmādityamavalokayet. || 172 ||

pṛthivyāṃ yāni pāpāni brahmahatyādikāni ca
annamāśritya tiṣṭhanti samprāpte harivāsare. || 173 ||

ṛugmāṅgadaḥ:
aṣṭavarṣādhiko yastu aśītirna hi pūryate
yo bhuṅkte mānavaḥ pāpo viṣṇorahani cāgate. || 174 ||

pitā vā yadi vā putro bhāryā vā'pi suhṛjjanaḥ
padmanābhadine bhuṅkte nigrāhyo dasyuvadbhavet. || 175 ||

ḍharmavibhūṣaṇaḥ:
prātarharidine lokāstiṣṭhadhvaṃ caikabhojanāḥ
akṣāralavaṇāḥ sarve haviṣyānniṣeviṇaḥ. || 176 ||

avanītapaśayanāḥ priyāsaṃgavivarjitāḥ
smaradhvaṃ devadeveśaṃ purāṇapuruṣottamam. || 177 ||

sakṛdbhojanasaṃyuktā hyupavāse bhaviṣyatha
akṛtaśrāddhanicayā jalapiṇḍodakakriyāḥ. || 178 ||

Brahmā:
upoṣya dvādaśīṃ puṇyāṃ sarvapāpakṣayapradām
na paśyati yamaṃ vā'pi narakānna ca yātanām. || 179 ||

raṭantīha purāṇāni bhūyo bhūyo varānane
na bhoktavyaṃ na bhoktavyaṃ samprāpte harivāsare. || 180 ||

dvādaśī na pramoktavyā yāvadāyuḥ pravartate
arcanīyo hṛṣīkeśo viśuddhenāntarātmanā. || 181 ||

bhaktyā grāhyo hṛṣīkeśo na dhanairdharaṇīsurāḥ
bhaktyā sampūjito viṣṇuḥ phalaṃ datte samīhitam. || 182 ||

jalenāpi jagannāthaḥ pūjitaḥ kleśanāśanaḥ
paritoṣaṃ prayātyāśu tṛṣārtāstu yathā jalaiḥ. || 183 ||

āsīnasya śayānasya tiṣṭhato vrajato'pi vā
ramasva puṇḍarīkākṣa hṛdaye mama sarvadā. || 184 ||

sarvagaścaiva sarvātmā sarvāvasthāsu cācyuta
ramasva puṇḍarīkākṣa nṛsiṃha hṛdaye mama. || 185 ||

karāvalambanaṃ dehi śrī kṛṣṇa kamalekṣaṇa
bhavapaṅkārṇave ghore majjato mama sarvadā. || 186 ||

trāhi trāhi jagannātha vāsudevācyutāvyaya
māṃ samuddhara govinda duḥkhasaṃsārasāgarāt. || 187 ||

etatpuṇyaṃ paraṃ guhyaṃ pavitraṃ pāpanāśanam
āyuṣyaṃ ca yaśasyaṃ ca kaliduḥsvapnanāśanam. || 188 ||

kalau pāpaṃ kiyanmātraṃ hatyāsteyādisambhavam
smṛte manasi govinde dahyate tūlarāśivat. || 189 ||

kalau keśavabhaktānāṃ na bhayaṃ vidyate kvacit
smṛte saṃkīrtite dhyāte saṃkṣayaṃ yāti pātakam. || 190 ||

adhyetavyamidaṃ śāstraṃ śrotavyamanasūyayā
bhaktebhyaśca pradātavyaṃ dhārmikebhyaḥ punaḥ punaḥ. || 191 ||

adhīyāna idaṃ śāstraṃ viṣṇormāhātmyamuttamam
sarvapāpavinirmuktaḥ prāpnoti paramaṃ padam. || 192 ||

śrutvā dharmaṃ vijānāti śrutvā tyajati durmatim
śrutvā jñānamavāpnoti śrutvā mokṣaṃ ca gacchati. || 193 ||

tasmādidaṃ samāśrāvyaṃ śrotavyaṃ ca sadaiva hi
kutarkadāvadagdhebhyo na dātavyaṃ kathañcana. || 194 ||

saṃsāraviṣapānena ye mṛtāḥ prāṇino bhuvi
amṛtāya smṛtasteṣāṃ kṛṣṇāmṛtamaharṇavaḥ. || 195 ||

klinnaṃ pādodakenaiva yasya nityaṃ kaḻebaram
tīrthakoṭisahasraistu snāto bhavati pratyaham. || 196 ||

tīrthakoṭisahasraistu sevitaiḥ kiṃ prayojanam
toyaṃ yadi pibennityaṃ sāḻagrāmaśilācyutam. || 197 ||

sāḻagrāmaśilāsparśaṃ ye kurvanti dine dine
vāñchanti karasaṃsparśaṃ teṣāṃ devāḥ savāsavāḥ. || 198 ||

duḥsaho nārako vahnirduḥsahā yamakiṅkarāḥ
viṣamaśvāntakapathaḥ pretatvaṃ cātidāruṇam. || 199 ||

vicintya manasā'pyevaṃ pātakādvinivarttayet
smaraṇaṃ kīrtanaṃ viṣṇoḥ sadaiva na parityajet. || 200 ||

śrī Vedavyāsaḥ:
acyutānantagovindanāmoccāraṇabhīṣitāḥ
naśyanti sakalā rogāḥ satyaṃ satyaṃ vadāmyaham. || 201 ||

sakṛduccāritaṃ yena harirityakṣaradvayam
baddhaḥ parikarastena mokṣāya gamanaṃ prati. || 202 ||

evaṃ brahmādayo devā ṛṣayaśca tapodhanāḥ
kīrtayanti suraśreṣṭhaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum. || 203 ||

kiṃ tasya dānaiḥ kiṃ tīrthaiḥ kiṃ tapobhiḥ kimadhvaraiḥ
yo nityaṃ dhyāyate devaṃ nārāyaṇamananyadhīḥ. || 204 ||

nityotsavo nityatā ca nityaśrīrnityaśo jayaḥ
yeṣāṃ hṛdistho bhagavānmaṅgalāyatano hariḥ. || 205 ||

jīvaṃścaturdaśādūrdhvaṃ puruṣo niyamena tu
strī vā'pyanūnadaśakaṃ dehaṃ mānuṣamārjate. || 206 ||

caturdaśordhvajīvīni saṃsāraścādivarjitaḥ
ato'vittvā paraṃ devaṃ mokṣāśā kā mahāmune. || 207 ||

ācaturdaśamādvarṣātkarmāṇi niyamena tu
daśāvarāṇāṃ dehānāṃ kāraṇāni karotyayam
ataḥ karmakṣayānmuktiḥ kuta eva bhaviṣyati. || 208 ||

samānāṃ viṣamā pūjā viṣamāṇāṃ samā tathā
kriyate yena devo'pi svapadādbhraśyate hi saḥ. || 209 ||

vittaṃ bandhurvayaḥ karma vidyā caiva tu pañcamī
etāni mānyasthānāni garīyo yadyaduttaram. || 210 ||

guṇānusāriṇīṃ pūjāṃ samāṃ dṛṣṭiṃ ca yo naraḥ
sarvabhūteṣu kurute tasya viṣṇuḥ prasīdati. || 211 ||

yathā suhṛtsu kartavyaṃ pitṛśatrusuteṣu ca
tathā karoti pūjādi samabuddhiḥ sa ucyate. || 212 ||

tiryakpuṇḍraṃ na kurvīta samprāpte maraṇe'pi ca
na cānyannāma vibrūyātparaṃ nārāyaṇādṛte. || 213 ||

naivedyaśeṣaṃ devasya yo bhunakti dine dine
sikthe sikthe bhavetpuṇyaṃ cāndrāyaṇaśatādhikam. || 214 ||

ūrdhvapuṇḍramṛjuṃ saumyaṃ lalāṭe dṛśyate
sa caṇḍālo'pi śuddhātmā pūjya eva na saṃśayaḥ. || 215 ||

aśucirvā'pyanācāro manasā pāpamācaran
śucireva bhavennityamūrdhvapuṇḍrānkito naraḥ. || 216 ||

ūrdhvapuṇḍravihīnasya śmaśānasadṛśaṃ mukham
avalokya mukhaṃ teṣāmādityamavalokayet. || 217 ||

yajño dānaṃ tapaścaiva svādhyāyaḥ pitṛtarpaṇam
vyarthaṃ bhavati tatsarvamūrdhvapuṇḍraṃ vinā kṛtam. || 218 ||

gopīcandanaliptāṅgo yaṃ yaṃ paśyati cakṣuṣā
taṃ taṃ śuddhaṃ vijānīyānnātra kāryā vicāraṇā. || 219 ||

āsphoṭayanti pitaraḥ pranṛtyanti pitāmahāḥ
vaiṣṇavo'smatkule jātaḥ sa naḥ santārayiṣyati. || 220 ||

jīvitaṃ viṣṇubhaktasya varaṃ pañcadinānyapi
na tu kalpasahasraistu bhaktihīnasya keśave. || 221 ||

kiṃ tena jātamātreṇa bhūbhāreṇānnaśatruṇā
yo jāto nārcayedviṣṇuṃ na smarennāpi kīrtayet. || 222 ||

yo dadāti dvijātibhyaścandanaṃ gopimarditam
api sarṣapamātreṇa punātyāsaptamaṃ kulam. || 223 ||

jñānī ca karmāṇi sado'ditāni
kuryādakāmaḥ satataṃ bhavet. || 224 ||

atītānāgatajñānī trailokyodvaraṇakṣamaḥ
etādṛśo'pi nā'cāraṃ śrautaṃ smārtaṃ parityajet. || 225 ||

yadeva vidyayā karoti śraddhayopaniṣadā
sadeva vīryavattaraṃ bhavati. || 226 ||

kurvaneveha karmāṇi jijīviṣecchataṃ samāḥ
evaṃ tvayi nānyatheto'sti na karma lipyate nare. || 227 ||

ācāraścaiva sādhūnāmātmanastuṣṭireva ca
vedapraṇihito dharmo hyadharmastadviparyayaḥ. || 228 ||

niṣkāmaṃ jñānapūrvaṃ tu nivṛttamiha cocyate
nivṛttaṃ sevamānastu brahmābhyeti sanātanam. || 229 ||

iti kṛṣṇāmṛtamahārṇavaḥ samāptaḥ
