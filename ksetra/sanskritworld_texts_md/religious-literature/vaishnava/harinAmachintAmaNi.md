śrīharināmacintāmaṇi
śrīgodrumacandrāya namaḥ

(1)
prathamapariccheda
śrīnāmamāhātmyasūcanā

gadāi gaurāṅga jaya jāhnavā jīvana  |
sītādvaita jaya śrīvāsādi bhakta gaṇa  || 1.1 ||

lavaṇa jaladhi tīre        nīlācale śrī mandire
dāru brahma puruṣa pradhāna  |
jīve nistārite hari         arcā rūpe avatari
bhoga mokṣa karena pradāna  || 1.2 ||
sei dhāme śrī caitanya          mānave karite dhanya
sannyāsī rūpete bhagavān  |
kalite ye yuga dharma         bujhāite tāra marma
kāśī miśra ghare adhiṣṭhāna  || 1.3 ||
nija bhakta vṛnda laye          nije kalpa taru haye
kṛṣṇa prema dena sarva jane  |
nānā mate bhakta mukhe          bhakta katha śuni sukhe
jīva śikṣā dena suyatane  || 1.4 ||
eka dina bhagavān         samudre kariyā snāna
śrī siddha bakule hari dāse  |
mili ānandita mane         jijñāsilā sayatane
kise jīva tare anāyāse  || 1.5 ||
prabhura caraṇa dhari'      aneka vinaya kari'
galadaśru pulaka śarīra  |
haridāsa mahāśaya      kāṅdite kāṅdite kaya
prabhu tava līlā sugabhīra  || 1.6 ||
āmi ati akiñcana       nāhi mora vidyā dhana
tava pada āmāra sambala  |
e hena ayogya jane       praśna kari' akāraṇe
bala prabhu habe kibā phala  || 1.7 ||
tumi kṛṣṇa svayaṃ prabho        jīva uddhārite vibho
navadvīpa dhāme avatāra  |
kṛpā kari' rāṅgā pāya       rākha more gaura rāya
tabe citta praphulla āmāra  || 1.8 ||
tomāra ananta nāma       tavānanta guṇa grāma
tava rūpa sukhera sāgara  |
ananta tomāra līlā       kṛpā kari' prakāśilā
tāi āsvādaye e pāmara  || 1.9 ||
cinmaya bhāskara tumi       kiraṇera kaṇa āmi
tumi prabhu, āmi nitya dāsa  |
caraṇa pīyūṣa tava       mama sukha suvaibhava
tava nāmāmṛte mora āśa  || 1.10 ||
e mata adhama āmi       ki balite jāni svāmī
tabu ājñā kariba pālana  |
yā balābe mora mukhe       tomāre baliba sukhe
doṣa guṇa nā kari gaṇana  || 1.11 ||
kṛṣṇatattva

ekamātra icchāmaya kṛṣṇa sarveśvara  |
nitya śaktiyoge kṛṣṇa vibhu parātpara  || 1.12 ||

kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇaśakti

kṛṣṇaśakti kṛṣṇa haite nā haya svatantra  |
yei śakti sei kṛṣṇa kahe vedamantra  || 1.13 ||
kṛṣṇa vibhu, śakti tāṅra vaibhava svarūpa  |
ananta vaibhave kṛṣṇa haya eka rūpa  || 1.14 ||

trividha vaibhava

śaktira prakāśa yei sei ta vaibhava  |
vibhura vaibhava mātra haya anubhava  || 1.15 ||
vaibhava trividha tava gaurāṅga sundara
cidacitjīva tina śāstrera gocara  || 1.16 ||

cidvaibhava

ananta vaikuṇṭha ādi yata kṛṣṇa dhāma  |
govinda śrīkṛṣṇa hari ādi yata nāma  || 1.17 ||
dvibhuja muralīdhara ādi yata rūpa  |
bhaktānandaprada ādi guṇa aparūpa  || 1.18 ||
vraja rasalīlā navadvīpe saṅkīrtana  |
ei rūpa kṛṣṇa līlā vicitra gaṇana  || 1.19 ||
e samasta cidvaibhava aprākṛta haya  |
āsiyau e prapañce prāpañcika naya   || 1.20 ||
cidvyāpāra samudaya viṣṇutattvasāra  |
viṣṇupada bali vede gāya bāra bāra  || 1.21 ||

kṛṣṇera cidvibhute viṣṇutattva śuddhatattva

nāhi tāhe jaḍadharma māyāra vikāra  |
jaḍātīta viṣṇutattva śuddhasattvasāra  || 1.22 ||
śuddhasattva rajastamogandhavirahita  |
rajastamomiśra miśrasattva suvidita  || 1.23 ||
govinda vaikuṇṭhanātha kāraṇodaśāyī  |
garbhodaśāyī āra kṣīrasindhuśāyī  || 1.24 ||
āra yata svāṃśa paricita avatāra  |
sei saba śuddhasattva viṣṇutattvasāra  || 1.25 ||
goloke vaikuṇṭhe āra kāraṇasāgare  |
athavā e jaḍe thāke, viṣṇunāma dhare  || 1.26 ||
praveśi e jaḍa viśva māyāra adhīśa  |
viṣṇunāma prāpta vibhu sarvadeva īśa  || 1.27 ||
māyāra īśvara māyī śuddhasattvamaya  |

miśrasattva

miśrasattva brahmā śiva ādi saba haya  || 1.28 ||

cidvaibhavera vistṛti

e samasta viṣṇutattva āra viṣṇudhāma  |
tava cidvaibhava nātha tava līlāgrāma  || 1.29 ||

acidvaibhava māyatattva

virajāra ei pāre yata vastu haya  |
acitvaibhava tava cauddalokamaya  || 1.30 ||
māyāra vaibhava bali bale devīdhāma  |
pañcabhūta mano buddhi ahaṅkāra nāma  || 1.31 ||
e bhūrloka, bhuvarloka āra svargaloka  |
maharloka, janatapasatyabrahmaloka  || 1.32 ||
atalasutalādi nimna loka sāta  |
māyika vaibhava tava śuna jagannātha  || 1.33 ||
cidvaibhava pūrṇatattva māyā chāyā tāra  |

jīvavaibhava

cidaṇusvarūpa jīva vaibhava prakāra  || 1.34 ||
ciddharmavaśataḥ jīva svatantra gaṭhana  |
saṅkhyāya ananta sukha tāra prayojana  || 1.35 ||

mukta jīva

sei sukha hetu yārā kṛṣṇere barila  |
kṛṣṇapāriṣada muktarūpete rahila  || 1.36 ||

baddha vā bahirmukha jīva

yārā punaḥ nijasukha kariyā bhāvanā   |
pārśvasthitā māyā prati karila kāmanā  || 1.37 ||
sei saba nityakṛṣṇabahirmukha haila  |
devīdhāme māyākṛta śarīra pāila  || 1.38 ||
puṇya pāpa karma cakre paḍiyā ekhana  |
sthūla liṅga dehe sadā karena bhramaṇa  || 1.39 ||
kabhu svarge uṭhe, kabhu niraye paḍiyā  |
caurāśi lakṣa yoni bhoge bhramiyā bhramiyā  || 1.40 ||

tathāpi kṛṣṇadayā

tumi vibhu, tomāra vaibhava jīva haya  |
dāsera maṅgala cintā tomāra niścaya  || 1.41 ||
dāsa yāhā sukha māni kare anveṣaṇa  |
tumi tāhā kṛpā kari kara vitaraṇa  || 1.42 ||

prākṛta śubhakarma karmakāṇḍa

māyāra vaibhave ye anitya sukha cāya
tomāra kṛpāya se anāyāse pāya  || 1.43 ||
sei sukha prāptyupāya śubhakarma yata  |
niramile dharmayajñayogahomavrata  || 1.44 ||
sei saba śubhakarma sadā jaḍamaya  |
cinmayī pravṛtti tāhe kabhu nā milaya  || 1.45 ||
tāhāra sādhane sādhya jaḍamaya phala  |
uccaloka bhoga sukha tāhāte prabala  || 1.46 ||
sei saba karmabhoga nāhi ātmaśānti  |
tāhāte prayāsa karā atiśaya bhrānti  || 1.47 ||
sei saba śubha karma upāya haiyā   |
anitya upeya sādhe loka sukha diyā  || 1.48 ||

sei avasthā haite uddhārera upāya

kabhu yadi sādhu saṅge jānite se pāre  |
āmi jīva kṛṣṇadāsa, yāya māyā pāre  || 1.49 ||
se virala phala mātra sukṛtijanita  |
tuccha karmakāṇḍa nāhi karile vihita  || 1.50 ||

jñānakāṇḍa, brahmalaya sukha

āra yini māyāra yantraṇā mātra jāni  |
mukti lābhe yatnavān tini hana jñānī  || 1.51 ||
se saba lokera janya tumi dayāmaya  |
jñānakāṇḍa brahmavidyā diyācha niścaya  || 1.52 ||
sei vidyā māyāvāda kariyā āśraya  |
jaḍa mukta haye brahme jīva haya laya  || 1.53 ||

brahmavastu ki?

sei brahma tava aṅgakānti jyotirmaya  |
virajāra pāre sthita tāte haya laya  || 1.54 ||
ye saba asure viṣṇu karena saṃhāra  |
tāhārau sei brahme yāya māyāpāra  || 1.55 ||
kṛṣṇabahirmukha

karmī jñānī ubhaye i kṛṣṇabahirmukha  |
kabhu nāhi āsvādaya kṛṣṇadāsyasukha  || 1.56 ||
bhaktyunmukhī sukṛti

bhaktira unmukhī sei sukṛti pradhāna  |
tāra phale jīva bhaktasādhusaṅga pāna  || 1.57 ||
śraddhāvān haye kṛṣṇabhaktasaṅga kare  |
nāme ruci, jīve dayā, bhaktipatha dhare  || 1.58 ||

karmī o jñānīra prati kṛpāya gauṇapatha vidhāna

dayāra sāgara tumi jīvera īśvara  |
karmī jñānī bahirmukha uddhāre tatpara  || 1.59 ||
karmapathe jñānapathe pathika ye jana  |
tāhāra uddhāra lāgi tomāra yatana  || 1.60 ||
sei sei pathikera maṅgala cintiyā  |
gauṇabhakti patha eka rākhila kariyā  || 1.61 ||

karmīra pakṣe karmera gauṇabhakti patha

karmī varṇāśrame thāki sādhusaṅga kari  |
karma mājhe bhakti kare gauṇapatha dhari  || 1.62 ||
tāra kṛta karma saba hṛdaya śodhiyā  |
tirohita haya śraddhā bīje sthāna diyā  || 1.63 ||

jñānīra gauṇapatha

jñānī sukṛtira bale bhaktera kṛpāya  |
ananya bhaktite śraddhā bīje sthāna diyā  || 1.64 ||
tumi bala mora dāsa māyāra vipāke  |
cāhe anya tuccha phala chāḍiyā āmāke  || 1.65 ||
āmi jāni tāra yāte haya sumaṅgala  |
bhukti mukti chāḍāiyā dī bhakti phala  || 1.66 ||

gauṇapathera prakriyā

tāra kāma anusāre cālāñā tāhāre
gauṇapatha bhaktimārge śraddhā dī tāre  || 1.67 ||
e tomāra kṛpā prabhu tumi kṛpāmaya  |
kṛpā nā karile kise jīva śuddha haya  || 1.68 ||
kalite gauṇapathera durdaśā

satyayuge dhyānayoge kata ṛṣigaṇe  |
śuddha kari' dile prabhu nijabhaktidhane  || 1.69 ||
tretāyuge yajñakarme aneka śodhile  |
dvāpare arcanamārge bhakti bilāile  || 1.70 ||
kali āgamane nātha jīvera durdaśā  |
dekhi jñāna karma yoga chāḍila bharasā  || 1.71 ||
alpa āyu, bahu pīḍā, balabuddhihrāsa  |
ei saba upadrava jīve kaila grāsa  || 1.72 ||
varṇāśrama dharma āra sāṅkhya yoga jñāna  |
kalijīve uddhārite nahe balavān  || 1.73 ||
jñānakarmagata ye bhaktira gauṇapatha  |
kaṇṭake saṅkīrṇa hañā haila vipatha  || 1.74 ||
pṛthakupāya dhari upeya sādhane  |
vighna bahutara haila jīvera jīvane  || 1.75 ||

nāmālocanāra mukhyapatha

prabhu tumi jīvera maṅgala cintā kari  |
kali yuge nāma saṅge svayamavatari  || 1.76 ||
yuga dharma pracārile nāma saṅkīrtana  |
mukhya pathe jīva pāya kṛṣṇa prema dhana  || 1.77 ||
nāmera smaraṇe āra nāmasaṅkīrtana  |
ei mātra dharma jīva karibe pālana  || 1.78 ||

sādhyasādhana o upāya upeyera abhedatākrame nāmera mukhyatā

yei ta sādhana sei sādhya yabe haila  |
upāya upeya madhye bheda nā rahila  || 1.79 ||
sādhyera sādhane āra nāhi antarāya  |
anāyāse tare jīva tomāra kṛpāya  || 1.80 ||
āmi ta adhama ati majiyā viṣaye  |
nā bhajinu nāma tava ati mūḍha haye  || 1.81 ||
dara dara dhārā cakṣe brahmaharidāsa  |
paḍila prabhura pade chāḍiyā niḥśvāsa  || 1.82 ||
hari bhakta bhakti mātre vinoda yāhāra  |
harināma cintāmaṇi jīvana tāhāra  || 1.83 ||

iti śrīharināmacintāmaṇau nāmamāhātmasūcanaṃ nāma prathamaḥ paricchedaḥ  |

(2)
dvitīya pariccheda
nāmagrahaṇavicāra

gadāi gaurāṅga jaya jāhnavā jīvana  |
sītādvaita jaya śrīvāsādi bhakta gaṇa  || 2.1 ||
mahāpreme haridāsa karena rodana  |
preme tāre gauracandra dilā āliṅgana  || 2.2 ||
balena tomāra sama bhakta kothā āra  |
sarvatattvajñātā tumi sadā māyāpāra  || 2.3 ||
ananya bhajanera śreṣṭhatā

nicakule avatari dekhāle sakale  |
dhane māne kule śīle kṛṣṇa nāhi mile  || 2.4 ||
ananya bhajane yāṅra śraddhā atiśaya  |
devatā apekṣā śreṣṭha sei mahāśaya  || 2.5 ||

śrīharidāsera nāmācāryyatā

nāmatattva sarvasāra tomāra vidita  |
ācāre ācāryya tumi pracāre paṇḍita  || 2.6 ||
bala haridāsa nāmamahimā apāra  |
śuniyā tomāra mukhe ānanda āmāra  || 2.7 ||
vaiṣṇavalakṣaṇa

eka nāma yāra mukhe vaiṣṇava se haya  |
tāre gṛhī yatna kari mānibe niścaya  || 2.8 ||
vaiṣṇavataralakṣaṇa

nirantara yāra mukhe śuni kṛṣṇanāma  |
sei se vaiṣṇavatara sarvaguṇadhāma  || 2.9 ||
vaiṣṇavatamalakṣaṇa

vaiṣṇava uttama sei yāhāre dekhile  |
kṛṣṇanāma āse mukhe kṛṣṇabhakti mile  || 2.10 ||
hena kṛṣṇanāma jīva ki rūpe karibe  |
tāhāra vidhāna tumi āmāre balibe  || 2.11 ||
kara yuḍi haridāsa balena vacana  |
prema gadagada svara sajala nayana  || 2.12 ||
nāmera svarūpa

kṛṣṇanāma cintāmaṇi anādi cinmaya  |
yei kṛṣṇa sei nāma ekatattva haya  || 2.13 ||
caitanya vigraha nāma nitya mukta tattva  |
nāma nāmī bhinna naya nitya śuddha sattva  || 2.14 ||
e jaḍa jagate tāṅra akṣara ākāra  |
rasarūpe rasikete sattva avatāra  || 2.15 ||
kṛṣṇa vastu haya cāri dharme paricita  |
nāma rūpa guṇa karma anādi vihita  || 2.16 ||
nāma nityasiddha

nitya vastu rasarūpa kṛṣṇa se advaya  |
sei cāri paricaye vastu siddha haya  || 2.17 ||
sandhinī śaktite tāṅra cāri paricaya  |
nityasiddha rūpe khyāta sarvadā cinmaya  || 2.18 ||
kṛṣṇa ākarṣaye sarva viśvagata jana  |
sei nitya dharma gata kṛṣṇa nāma dhana  || 2.19 ||
kṛṣṇarūpa nitya

kṛṣṇarūpa kṛṣṇa haite sarvadā abheda  |
nāma rūpa eka vastu nāhika prabheda  || 2.20 ||
śrīnāma smarile rūpa āise saṅge saṅge  |
rūpa nāma bhinna naya nāce nānā raṅge  || 2.21 ||


kṛṣṇaguṇa nitya

kṛṣṇaguṇa catuḥṣaṣṭhi ananta apāra  |
yāṅra nija aṃśarūpe saba avatāra  || 2.22 ||
yāṅra guṇa aṃśe brahmā śivādi īśvara  |
yāṅra guṇe nārāyaṇa ṣaṣṭhi guṇeśvara  || 2.23 ||
sei saba nityaguṇe nitya nāma tāṅra  |
ananta saṅkhyāya vyāpta vaikuṇṭha vyāpāra  || 2.24 ||
kṛṣṇalīlāra nityatva

sei guṇa taraṅgete līlāra vistāra  |
goloke vaikuṇṭha vraja saba cidākāre  || 2.25 ||
cidvastute nāma, rūpa, guṇa, līlā vastu haite pṛthaknaya

nāma rūpa guṇa līlā abhinna udaya  |
acitsamparke baddha jīve bhinna haya  || 2.26 ||
śuddha jīve nāma rūpa guṇa kriyā eka  |
jaḍāśrita dehe bheda ei se viveka  || 2.27 ||
kṛṣṇe nāhi jaḍagandha ataeva tāṅya  |
nāma rūpa guṇa līlā ekatattva bhāya  || 2.28 ||
nāmera sarvamūlatva
ei cāri paricaya madhye nāma tāṅra  |
sakalera ādi se pratīti sabākāra  || 2.29 ||
ataeva nāma mātra vaiṣṇavera dharma  |
nāme prasphuṭita haya rūpa guṇa karma  || 2.30 ||
kṛṣṇera samagra līlā nāme vidyamāna  |
nāme se parama tattva tomāra vidhāna  || 2.31 ||
vaiṣṇava o vaiṣṇavaprāye bheda āche

sei nāma baddha jīva śraddhā sahakāre  |
śuddha rūpe laile vaiṣṇava bali tāre  || 2.32 ||
nāmābhāsa yāra haya se vaiṣṇavaprāya  |
kṛṣṇakṛpā bale krame śuddha bhāva pāya  || 2.33 ||
ei māyika jagate kṛṣṇanāma o jīva ei duiṭi mātra cidvyāpāra

nāma sama vastu nāi e bhava saṃsāre  |
nāme se parama dhana kṛṣṇera bhāṇḍāre  || 2.34 ||
jīva nije cidvyāpāre kṛṣṇanāma āra  |
āra saba prāpañcika jagatsaṃsāra  || 2.35 ||

mukhya gauṇa bhede nāma dui prakāra

mukhya o gauṇa bhede kṛṣṇanāma dviprakāra  |
mukhyanāmāśraye jīva pāya sarva sāra  || 2.36 ||
cillīlā āśraya kari yata kṛṣṇa nāma  |
sei sei mukhya nāma sarva guṇa dhāma  || 2.37 ||

mukhya nāma

govinda gopāla rāma śrīnandanandana  |
rādhānātha hari yaśomatī prāṇadhana  || 2.38 ||
madanamohana śyāmasundara mādhava  |
gopīnātha vrajagopa rākhāla yādava  || 2.39 ||
ei rūpa nitya līlā prakāśaka nāma  |
e saba kīrtane jīva pāya kṛṣṇa dhāma  || 2.40 ||

gauṇa nāma o tāhāra lakṣaṇa

jaḍā prakṛtira paricaye nāma yata   |
prakṛtira guṇe gauṇa vedera sammata  || 2.41 ||
sṛṣṭikartā paramātmā brahma sthitikara  |
jagatsaṃhartā pātā  yajñeśvara hara  || 2.42 ||

mukhya o gauṇa nāmera phalabheda

eirūpa nāma karmajñānakāṇḍagata  |
puṇya mokṣa dāna kare śāstrera sammata  || 2.43 ||
nāmera ye mukhya phala kṛṣṇa prema dhana  |
tāra mukhya nāme mātra labhe sādhu gaṇa   || 2.44 ||

nāma o nāmābhāsamātra phalabheda

eka kṛṣṇanāma yadi mukhe bāhirāya  |
athavā śravaṇapathe antarete yāya  || 2.45 ||
śuddha varṇa haya vā aśuddha varṇa haya  |
tāte jīva tare ei śāstrera nirṇaya  || 2.46 ||
kintu eka kathā ithe āche suniścita  |
nāmābhāsa haile vilambe haya hita  || 2.47 ||
