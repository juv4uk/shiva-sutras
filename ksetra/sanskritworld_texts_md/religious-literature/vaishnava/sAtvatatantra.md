sātvatatantra, prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ


śrīsūta uvāca |
ya eko bahudhā kṛṣṇaḥ sṛṣṭyādau bahudheyate |
tamahaṃ śaraṇaṃ yāmi paramānandavigraham || 1.1
kailāsaśikharāsīnaṃ śivaṃ śivakaraṃ param |
nāradaḥ paripapraccha sarvabhūtahite rataḥ || 1.2
bhagavan śrotumicchāmi hareradbhutakarmaṇaḥ |
śrīkṛṣṇasyāprameyasya nānā līlātanūrvibhoḥ || 1.3
yadarthaṃ yatsvarūpaṃ ca yadyatkāle yathā rataḥ |
gṛhṇāti bhagavān svasthastanmamākhyātumarhasi || 1.4
avatāranimittaṃ yaccirādvigrahasambhavam |
prathamaṃ tattvato brūhi tvaṃ parāparavittamaḥ || 1.5
śrīśiva uvāca |
pṛṣṭaṃ mahābhāga tvayā bhāgavatottama |
yadahaṃ noditaḥ samyagbhagavadvīryavarṇane || 1.6
śrīviṣṇoravatārāṇāṃ virājaśca mahāmate |
kathane naiva paśyāmi pāraṃ varṣaśatairapi |
tathāpi sāramuddhṛtya tantrarūpeṇa nārada || 1.7
varṇayāmi yathaivoktamīśvareṇa dayālunā |
jayapūrvaṃ namaskṛtya goparūpiṇamīśvaram || 1.8
vakṣye sātvatatantrākhyaṃ bhagavadbhaktivardhanam |
yadāsīdekamavyaktaṃ nityaṃ cidrūpamavyayam || 1.9
brahmeti yadvidurvijñā bhagavāniti sātvatāḥ |
tadāsantamivātmānaṃ matvā dṛśya vinā vibhuḥ || 1.10
dviprakāramabhūtsatyaṃ sattābhūtvā svayaṃ svayam |
taccicchaktisvarūpeṇa prakṛtiḥ puruṣo mahān || 1.11
sarvalokaikanilayo bhagavāniti śabdyate |
tasyaiva śaktiḥ prakṛtiḥ kāryakāraṇarūpiṇī || 1.12
guṇatrayasvarūpeṇa yā svayaṃ bhidyate punaḥ |
yaḥ kālastaṃ vadantyeke hareśceṣṭāṃ duranvayām || 1.13
yasmādguṇatrayakṣobhātpṛthagbhūto'bhavatpurā |
jīvasya yasmādbhavati śubhāśubhaphalagrahaḥ || 1.14
tatkarma mahato janmaheturavyaktamūrtimat |
bhāvānāṃ pariṇāmo hi yato bhavati sarvadā || 1.15
tamevāhurvedavidaḥ sūkṣmarūpaṃ svabhāvakam |
ukto'yaṃ puruṣaḥ sākṣādīśvaro bhagavattanuḥ || 1.16
kālakarmasvabhāvasthaḥ prakṛtiṃ prati noditaḥ |
puruṣādhiṣṭhitā devī prakṛtirguṇasaṃgrahā || 1.17
mahattattvamabhūttattatparijñānakriyātmakam |
tasmājjāto hyahaṃkārastrividho daivanoditāt || 1.18
vaikārikastaijasaśca tāmasaśceti yaṃ viduḥ |
vaikārikānmanodevā jātā jñānakriyādhipāḥ || 1.19
marutkeśau diśaḥ sūryo nāsatyo jñānanodakāḥ |
vahnīndramitrakopetā ete karmāpanodakāḥ || 1.20
rājasādviṣayagrāhajñānakarmasvarūpiṇaḥ |
tvagrasajñā śrutiścakṣurghrāṇaṃ buddhiśca tanmayāḥ || 1.21
vākpāṇipāyūpasthāśca gatiśceti kriyātmakāḥ |
tāmasātpañcabhūtāni tanmātrāṇi ca bhāgaśaḥ || 1.22
prathamaṃ tāmasājjajñe śabdastasmādabhūnnabhaḥ |
śabdarūpāttu nabhasaḥ sparśastasmādabhūnmarut || 1.23
maruto'bhūttatastejastejaso rūpamuttamam |
rūpamātrādraso jajñe tasmādāpo'bhavan śucīḥ || 1.24
adbhyo jāto gandhaguṇo gandhādbhūmirajāyata |
mahattattvamahaṃkāraḥ saśabdasparśatejasaḥ || 1.25
rasagandhāvime sarve smṛtā prakṛtivikriyāḥ |
śabdasya prakṛtereva saṃdṛśyante yato budhaiḥ || 1.26
ato'bhavan prakṛtayo vikārān vikṛtīrviduḥ |
ākāśe śabdamātraṃ syādvāyau sparśaḥ saśabdakaḥ || 1.27
rūpaṃ tejasi śabdaśca sparśaścaiva jale tathā |
rasaśabdasparśarūpaṃ pṛthivyāṃ sarvameva hi || 1.28
kāraṇānāṃ yataḥ kārye samanvayavidhistataḥ |
dṛśyate tvadhikastatra guṇo yāvati kaśca ha || 1.29
mahadādīni tattvāni puruṣasya mahātmanaḥ |
kāryāvatārarūpāṇi jānīhi dvijasattama || 1.30
sarvāṇyetāni saṃgṛhya puruṣasyecchayā yā |
aṃśairutpādayāmāsurvirājaṃ bhuvanātmakam || 1.31
tasya cāntargataṃ chidraṃ pañcāśatkoṭivistṛtam |
daśottarādhikairetaiḥ saptabhirbahirāvṛtam || 1.32
tamāhuḥ puruṣasyaiva gehaṃ yatrāviśatsvayam |
yato'cetanamevāsītkevalaṃ sarvavistaram || 1.33
narādutpannatattvānāṃ saṃgrahe nārasaṃjñake |
ayanaṃ tasya yadabhūttasmānnārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ || 1.34
virāḍdehe yadavasadbhagavān purasaṃjñake |
ataḥ puruṣanāmānamavāpa puruṣaḥ paraḥ || 1.35
yadā sa bhagavān devo mūlaprakṛtivistaraḥ |
nārāyaṇena rūpeṇa virajamaviśatsvayam || 1.36
tadā cetanamāpādya cirādvigraha utthitaḥ |
samaṣṭiśabdatāvācyo dvisaptabhuvanāśrayaḥ || 1.37
yasmin carācaraṃ bhūtaṃ sraṣṭā brahmāharestanūḥ |
tatra lokamayaṃ yāvattaṃ virājaṃ vido viduḥ || 1.38
tasyābhimāninaṃ jīvaṃ vairājaṃ puruṣābhidham |
tadantaryāmiṇaṃ devaṃ nārāyaṇamanāmayam || 1.39
sarvajīvaikanilayaṃ bhagavantaṃ pracakṣate |
avatārasahasrāṇāṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam || 1.40
yasyāṃśena rajoyuktaḥ sṛṣṭau brahmā vyajāyata |
viṣṇuḥ sattvaguṇādhīśaḥ sthitau sthāpayituṃ jagat || 1.41
tamasā rudrarūpo'bhūtpratisaṃcaraṇo vibhuḥ |
ete viṣṇorguṇamayā avatārāḥ kriyākṛtāḥ || 1.42
eṣāmaṃśāvatārānme nibodha gadato mama |
brahmaṇo'ṃśena samabhūnmarīciratriraṅgirāḥ || 1.43
pulastyaḥ pulahaścaiva kraturdakṣo dvijottama |
bhṛgurvasiṣṭho'tharvā ca kardamādyāḥ prajeśvarāḥ || 1.44
eṣāṃ putrāśca pautrāśca prapautrāśca mahaujasaḥ |
sṛṣṭyarthamudgatāḥ sarve bhagavadvīryasaṃyutāḥ || 1.45
viṣṇoraṃśena samabhūddharmo yajño bṛhattrivṛt |
svāyambhuvādyā manavo dvisaptā lokaviśrutāḥ || 1.46
eṣāṃ putrāśca pautrāśca tathendrādyāśca devatāḥ |
viṣṇvaṃśayuktā lokānāṃ pālakāḥ kathitā mayā || 1.47
rudrasyāṃśena śataśo jātā rudragaṇāḥ pṛthak |
sarpāśca śataśo jātā ye ca hiṃsrāḥ svabhāvataḥ || 1.48
ete saṃkṣepataḥ proktā rudrasyāṃśāstamojuṣaḥ |
saṃhāriṇo'sya jagato ghorarūpā vilakṣaṇāḥ || 1.49
atha te sampravakṣyāmi līlādehān hareḥ pṛthak |
śuddhasattvamayān śāntān lokapremāspadān śṛṇu || 1.50
ya īśa eko bhagavānananto brahmasvarūpī puruṣo'dhiyajñaḥ |
pātuṃ punarviśvamasau svakāryaṃ bheje tanūstaṃ praṇamāmi kṛṣṇam || 1.51
iti śrīsātvatatantre śivanāradasaṃvāde prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1.52


ṣātvatatantra, dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ
śrīsiva uvāca |
sākṣādbabhūva bhagavān paripātumīśo vedān yugādisamaye hayaśīrṣanāmā |
hatvā suretaravarau madhukaiṭabhākhyau nastaścakāra viśrutīḥ śrutibhirvimṛgyaḥ || 2.1
lokānnivṛttiparatāṃ pracikīrṣurādau bhūtvā catuḥsanatayā bhagavān vimuktyai |
provāca yogamamalaṃ viśadāśayebhyo bhogān viraktiparatāṃ svayamācacāra || 2.2
deveṣu nāradatanurbhagavān viśuddhaṃ naiṣkarmyayogamavahatkhalu pāñcarātram |
dharmaṃ tathā bhagavatā kathitaṃ viśeṣaśiṣyeṣvasau paramanirvṛtimādadhānam || 2.3
ādau dadhāra dharaṇīdharaṇāya dhātuḥ svāyambhuvoktiparipālakakāla eva |
nārāyaṇo'khilagururgurukoladehaṃ tenāhanadditisutaṃ daśanāgraghātaiḥ || 2.4
bhūmeradho dharaṇimaṇḍalamaprameyaḥ śeṣākhya āsa jagataḥ sthitaye nitāntam |
yasmin kalārpitamidaṃ likhivaccakāsti nāgādhipairmunigaṇaiḥ parisevitāṅghriḥ || 2.5
tasmādadhaḥ kamaṭha āsa viśālarūpī brahmāṇḍabhāṇḍaparivistṛtādivyakāyaḥ |
śeṣo'pi yatra paribhāti sutantutulyo yaṃ cāryamā pitṛpatiḥ samupāsate vai || 2.6
dṛṣṭvā dṛśārdhavayasāpi vihāya māturdehaṃ dhruvaṃ madhuvane tapasābhitaptam |
bhūtvā kṛpāmayavapurbhagavān svalokaṃ prādātstuvanti yatayo munayo'pi yaṃ vai || 2.7
ālokya kardamatapo bhagavān vibhūtyai saṃśuddhadivyavapuṣāvirabhūtsaśuklaḥ |
tasmā adādvaramajātmajaputrarūpamānandabindupayasā ca cakāra tīrtham || 2.8
yajñe sa eva rucinā manuputriputra āhūtisūtirasurāraṇivahnikalpaḥ |
trailokyagopanavidhau suranātha īśa nāmnā suyajña iti viśrutakīrtirāśiḥ || 2.9
siddheśvaraśca samabhūtkapilākhya īśaḥ śrīdevahūtitanayo vitatāna tasyai |
yogaṃ svaśaktisahitaṃ cidacidvibhāgaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ tathā svabhimukheṣu jagāda śuddham || 2.10
yogeśvaro'tritanayo bhagavānananto dattākhya āsa samatāvavadatsvacaryām |
prahlādahaihayayaduṣvaparāyaṇeṣu śiṣyeṣu śikṣitakathāṃ kathayan gurubhyaḥ || 2.11
nārāyaṇo nara ṛṣipravarāvabhūtāṃ dharmasya dakṣaduhitaryadhimūrtipatnyām |
dhīropakārakaruṇāśayakāyaśuddhaṃ tīvraṃ tapaḥ pracaratāṃ surarājatāpam || 2.12
nābherabhūdṛṣabhasaṃjñasadāptakāmo yogeśvaraḥ sutaśatairavadatprajābhyaḥ |
dharmaṃ tataḥ paramayogijanāvacaryāṃ naiṣkarmyalakṣaṇaparāṃ svayamācacāra || 2.13
dhātrantike susanakādibhirīryamāṇe cetoguṇān vigalituṃ bhagavān sa haṃsaḥ |
provāca tattvamamalaṃ sadayārdracittā yasmādguṇāguṇavibhāgamabhūnmunīnām || 2.14
vene mṛte dvijajanairanu bāhuyugmaṃ saṃmathyamānasamaye pṛthurūpa āsīt |
lokakṣudhāṃ praśamayan pṛthivīṃ dudoha sarvāṇi bhūtikaraṇāni ca sarvabhūtyai || 2.15
dakṣasya yajñavihite śivaśaktihetoḥ prāptājyabhāmamadhikaṃ bhṛguṇābhidattam |
tatrāṣṭabāhurabhavadbhagavān bhavāya prāptā nutiḥ suranarādikṛtāpi tena || 2.16
jātaḥ priyavratakule gaya ityudārakīrtiṃ tatāna bhagavān tanuvāṅmanobhiḥ |
tenāpi yajñatanurīśvara indrarūpī spardhāṃ cakāra mahatāṃ madamādadhānaḥ || 2.17
saṃvatsarasya tanayaḥ sa ha yāminīnāmālokanādivividhaṃ mudamācikīrṣuḥ |
śrīkāmadevavapuṣā hyavatīrya devo devyomayā madanakelibhirārarāma || 2.18
prācīnabarhitanayāṃstapasā sutaptān dṛṣṭvā svaśāntavapuṣāvirabhūdanantaḥ |
dattvā svapādabhajanaṃ vasatāṃ gṛheṣu kanyāṃ ca vṛkṣajanitāmadiśaddayāluḥ || 2.19
svārociṣe tuṣitayā dvijavedaśīrṣājjāto vibhuḥ sakaladharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭhaḥ |
yadbrahmacaryāniyamānṛṣayo'pyaśikṣan sākṣājjagadgurutayāvacacāra śuddhān || 2.20
dharmādabhūtsutatayā bhagavāṃstṛtīye manvantare trijagataḥ sthitaye kṛpāluḥ |
śrīsatyasena iti durjanayakṣarakṣān yastānapāharadasau suranāthamitraḥ || 2.21
turye'ntare sarasi vāraṇarājarājaṃ grāheṇa tīvrabalinā parikarṣayantam |
nārāyaṇetyabhihite hariruddadhāra tasmādbhavārṇavajalādapi devarājaḥ || 2.22
vaikuṇṭha āsa bhagavān dvijavarya mudrā devyā mayā tadanurūpajayābhidhānaḥ |
vaikuṇṭhadarśanamakārayadaprameyastasyāu pañcamamanoḥ samaye prasiddham || 2.23
ṣaṣṭhe'ntare tu bhagavān dvijaśāpakhinnadehān surānavanatānavalokya sannaḥ |
vairājavipratanayo'jitasaṃjña īśo devāsurairamathayatsahasā payodhim || 2.24
devāsure jalanidhermathanādviṣaṣṇe hastāccyute girivare sahasārdracittaḥ |
bhūtvā tu kūrmavapuradbhutamuddadhāra mene ca parvatavivartanagātrakaṇḍūm || 2.25
dugdhāmbudhāvururujāṃ pracikīrṣurīśa ādāya pūrṇakalaśaṃ sudhayā nitāntam |
āyurvidhānanigamaṃ khalu yajñabhoktā dhanvantariḥ samabhavadbhagavānnarāṇām || 2.26
dhanvantareramṛtapūrṇaghaṭe svavārā cūrṇīyamāna amare śaraṇaṃ praviṣṭe |
mohinyabhūtsa bhagavānasurāsurāṇāṃ mohāya tāpaviramāya sadāptakāmaḥ || 2.27
satyavratāya janatarpaṇataḥ svarūpaṃ mātsyaṃ mahākaruṇayā pravitatya sadyaḥ |
kalpārṇave'pyavadadacyuta ātmatattvaṃ bhūrūpanāvivasate viharan dvijebhyaḥ || 2.28
trailokyaduḥkhadalanāya nṛsiṃharūpaṃ kṛtvā svabhaktamavituṃ kila lāṅgalāgraiḥ |
dhṛtvāsurendramasurendraviśālatīvravakṣaḥsthalaṃ sthalamivāgranakhairdadāra || 2.29
yasmin prapaśyati baliḥ sagaṇaṃ trilokaṃ tenāpi vāmanatanuṃ bhagavān gṛhītvā |
saṃyācya sammitapadatritayaṃ baleḥ svaṃ kṛtvā triviṣṭapamadādaditeḥ sutebhyaḥ || 2.30
bhaktānahaṃ samanuvarta iti svavācaṃ sākṣātprakartumiva bhūvivare praviṣṭaḥ |
vairocanergṛhamarakṣayadaprameyo nāmnā gadādhara iti kṣapayan dayāvān || 2.31
bhūtvā tu bhārgavakule nijatātanāśādrāmo mahāparaśukaṃ parigṛhya tīkṣṇam |
kṣatraṃ nivārya kṣititalaṃ parihṛtya bhūyo dattvā dvijāya hyavasatsa mahendrapṛṣṭhe || 2.32
vṛndārakaiḥ pariniṣevitapādapadmaḥ śrīrāmacandra iti sūryakulābdhijātaḥ |
devārināśanavidhau kuśikānvayena nīto maheśadhanurājagavaṃ babhañja || 2.33
jñātvā tato bhṛgukulodbhavadhīravīraṃ rāmaṃ sugaurarucirāṃ pariṇīya sītām |
gatvā gṛhān gṛhapateḥ piturāptajāyāvācaṃ niśamya vanavāsamagātsabhāryaḥ || 2.34
tīrtvā gāṅgapayo'nujānugamanācchrīcitrakūṭaṃ giriṃ tyaktvā duṣṭavirādharādhadamano dhāvan dhanurdhārayan |
hatvā krūrasurendravairihariṇaṃ mārīcasaṃjñaṃ tato laṅkeśāhṛtasītayā khalu punaḥ prāpto dṛśāmīdṛśām || 2.35
candraṃ caṇḍakaraṃ pracaṇḍapavanaṃ mene sumandānilaṃ mālāṃ mālatimallikāṃ śucikalāṃ gītaṃ sphuliṅgāyitam |
ityevaṃ vanitāparāyaṇanaraṃ hāsyannivālokayanṛkṣaṃ manmathasāyakāhṛtamano reme priyāśaṅkayā || 2.36
gatvā vānararājavālinamahāmitreṇa setuṃ tato baddhvā vāridhimātarattaratamaṃ sākaṃ plavaṃgairmudā |
chittvā rākṣasayakṣalakṣamamalā sītā saputrānujaṃ laṅkeśaṃ jvaladagninā bhagavatā cāptā punaḥ sā purī || 2.37
sūryādiśaktimavihṛtya śaśāsa bhūmiṃ govipraprājñaparisevanasarvadharmaḥ |
udriktabhaktinamitānanayatsvanāthān sarvān vanādhivasataḥ svapadaṃ suśāntam || 2.38
tasyānujo bharatasaṃjña udārabuddhī rāmājñayā nijagṛhe nivasannapi śrīm |
tyaktvā vanasthavratavānabhavattato vai gandharvakoṭimathanaṃ viharaṃścakāra || 2.39
śrīlakṣmaṇastadavaro vanametya rāmaṃ sītāṃ niṣecya bahukaṣṭa āsīt |
bāhye'pi cāsya vacasā vanametya dehaṃ saṃtyajya tatpadamagādarisainyavahniḥ || 2.40
śatrughnasaṃjña uruvikramaśuddhabuddhiḥ śauryeṇa darpadalano dviṣatāṃ dayāluḥ |
dīneṣu daityalavaṇāntaka āryasevī svānyeṣu sāmyamatirājanatābhirāmaḥ || 2.41
mārkaṇḍanāmamunaye bhuvanāni deṣṭuṃ māyālaye tanutare jaṭhare mukundaḥ |
nyagrodhapattrapuṭakośaśayāna āsīdaṅguṣṭhapānaparamaḥ śiśuraprameyaḥ || 2.42
vṛtrasya ghoravapuṣā paritāpitānāṃ saṃrakṣaṇāya bhagavān yudhi nirjarāṇām |
ādyo hyabhūdgaruḍakiṃnaragītakīrtisteṣāṃ suduḥkhabhayaśokavināśaśīlaḥ || 2.43
aṅguṣṭhaparvasumitān śramaṇān dvijāgryān dṛṣṭvā tu goṣpadapayogatasarvadehān |
brahmaṇya indrahasitān samidaṃśuhastān gurvarcanāya kṛpayāvadamūn prapannān || 2.44
duṣyantabījamadhigamya śakuntalāyāṃ jāto hyajo'pi bhagavānadhiyajñakart
|
vismāpayan bahunṛpān bahuvājimedhān sākṣādiyāja bahu dānamadādameyaḥ || 2.45
sarvān janān kaliyuge balabuddhihīnān dṛṣṭvā kṛpāparavaśo vasuvīryajāyām |
jātaḥ parāśarasakāśata ādidevo vedān samāhitatayā vibhajiṣyati sma || 2.46
vṛṣṇeḥ kule tu bhagavān baladevanāmā yasmādbalānatibalānadalatsurārīn |
yallāṅgalāgrakalanātkururājadhānī dhāneva dhāmasahitā calitātibhītā || 2.47
bhūmerjanasya nijapādaparāyaṇasya vṛṣṇerajo'pi bhagavān sukhamādadhānaḥ |

ekameva yajetkṛṣṇaṃ sarvadevamayaṃ dhiyā || 8.4
nityaṃ naimitakaṃ kāryaṃ tathāvaśyakameva ca |
gṛhāśramī viṣṇubhaktaḥ kuryātkṛṣṇaṃ dhiyā smaran || 8.5
eteṣu cānyadevānāṃ yā pūjā vidhinā smṛtā |
sāpi kṛṣṇārcanātpaścātkriyeta hṛdi taṃ smaran || 8.6
anyadā tvanyadevānāṃ pṛthakpūjāṃ na ca smaret |
kāmyaṃ niṣiddhaṃ ca tathā naiva kuryātkadācana || 8.7
kalatraputramitrādīn hitvā kṛṣṇaṃ samāśritāḥ |
harikīrtiratā ye ca teṣāṃ kṛtyaṃ na vidyate || 8.8
kṛtvānyadevatāpūjāṃ sakāmāṃ balinā dvija |
bhaktibhraṣṭo bhavedāśu saṃsārānna nivartate || 8.9
kāmātmā niranukrośaḥ paśughātaṃ samācaran |
paśulomasamaṃ varṣaṃ narake paripacyate || 8.10
yajñe paśorālabhane naiva doṣo'sti yadvacaḥ |
api pravṛttī rāgiṇāṃ nivṛttistu garīyasī || 8.11
kṛtvānyadevatāpūjāṃ paśuṃ hitvā narādhamāḥ |
yadi te svargatiṃ yānti narakaṃ yānti ke tadā || 8.12
sa māṃ punarbhakṣayitā yasya māṃsamadāmyaham |
iti māṃsaniruktaṃ vai varṇayanti manīṣiṇaḥ || 8.13
viṣṇubhaktiṃ samāśritya paśughātaṃ samācaran |
kṛtvānyadevatāpūjāṃ bhraṣṭo bhavati niścitam || 8.14
mānuṣyaṃ prāpya ye jīvā na bhajanti hareḥ padam |
te śocyā sthāvarādīnāmapyekaśaraṇā yadi || 8.15
ahaṃ brahmā surendrāśca ye bhajāmo divāniśam |
tato'dhiko'sti ko devaḥ śrīkṛṣṇātpuruṣottamāt || 8.16
yatprasādaṃ pratīkṣante sarve lokāḥ sapālakāḥ |
sāpi lakṣmīryaccaraṇaṃ sevate tadanādṛtā || 8.17
tato'dhiko'sti ko devo lakṣmīkāntājjanārdanāt |
yannāmni ke na saṃyānti puruṣāḥ paramaṃ padam || 8.18
dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇāṃ mūlaṃ yaccaraṇārcanam |
tato'dhiko'sti ko devaḥ kṛpāsindhormahātmanaḥ || 8.19
bhajanasyālpamātreṇa bahu manyeta yaḥ sadā |
tato'dhiko'sti ko devaḥ sukhārādhyājjagadguroḥ || 8.20
yena kenāpi bhāvena yo'pi ko'pi bhajan janaḥ |
labhate'bhīpsitāṃ siddhiṃ mokṣaṃ cāpyakutobhayam || 8.21
tato'dhiko'sti ko devaḥ devakīdevinandanāt |
yo jaganmuktaye kīrtimavatīrya tatāna ha || 8.22
ato'nyadevatāpūjāṃ tyaktvā balividhānataḥ |
sadgurorupadeśena bhajetkṛṣṇapadadvayam || 8.23
śṛṇuyātpratyahaṃ viṣṇoryaśaḥ paramamaṅgalam |
uccārayenmukhenaiva nāma cittena saṃsmaret || 8.24
prītiṃ kuryādvaiṣṇaveṣu abhakteṣu vivarjayet |
daivopalabdhaṃ bhuñjāno nātiyatnaṃ caretsukhe || 8.25
gṛheṣvatithivattiṣṭhedyadyetānnaiva bādhate |
eṣāṃ bādhe pṛthaktiṣṭhedvaiṣṇaveṣu ca saṅgavān || 8.26
brahmacārī gṛhī vāpi vānaprastho yatiśca vā |
vinā vaiṣṇavasaṅgena naiva siddhiṃ labhejjanaḥ || 8.27
bhaktasaṅgaṃ vinā bhaktirnaiva jāyeta kasyacit |
bhaktiṃ vinā na vairāgyaṃ na jñānaṃ mokṣamaśnute || 8.28
ata āśramaliṅgāṃśca hitvā bhakteḥ samaṃ vaset |
yatsaṅgācchrutikīrtibhyāṃ harau bhaktiḥ prajāyate || 8.29
viṣṇubhaktaprasaṅgasya nimeṣeṇāpi nārada |
svargāpavargau na sāmyaṃ kimutānyasukhādibhiḥ || 8.30
ato yatnena puruṣaḥ kuryātsaṅgaṃ harerjanaiḥ |
tiryañco'pi yato muktiṃ labhante kimu mānuṣāḥ || 8.31
tatsaṅgenaiva puruṣo viṣṇuṃ prāpnoti niścitam |
vinā vairāgyajñānābhyāṃ yato viṣṇustadantike || 8.32
yeṣāṃ saṅgāddhareḥ saṅgaṃ sakṛdākarṇya mānavaḥ |
parityaktuṃ na śaknoti yadi bhadrasaridbhavet || 8.33
arasajño'pi tatsaṅgaṃ yadi yāti kathaṃcana |
bhūtvā rasajño'pi mahān karmabandhādvimucyate || 8.34
tatastaddharmanirato bhagavatyamalātmani |
prāpnoti paramāṃ bhaktiṃ sarvakāmapradāyinīm || 8.35
bhaktiṃ viditvā puruṣo muktiṃ necchati kaścana |
sālokyādipadaṃ cāpi kimu cānyasukhaṃ dvija || 8.36
phalaṃ vinā viṣṇubhaktā muktiṃ yānti dvijottama |
tata eva vidurnānya ānandamayamuttamam || 8.37
yato harirlikhitavathṛdaye vartate sadā |
teṣāṃ premalatābaddhaḥ paramānandavigrahaḥ || 8.38

śrīśiva uvāca |
yadādisatye viprendra narā viṣṇuparāyaṇāḥ |
na yajanti vinā viṣṇumanyadevaṃ kathaṃcana || 9.2
tadātmapūjāprāptyarthaṃ sarvadevamayaṃ ca vai |
pūjayāmo hṛṣīkeśaṃ kāyavāṅmanasā dvija || 9.3
tadā tuṣṭo vibhuḥ prāha devadevo rameśvaraḥ |
avatīrya yajiṣyāmi yuṣmāṃllokāṃśca yājayan || 9.4
tataḥ sarve janā yuṣmān yajiṣyanti samāhitāḥ |
anena pūjā yuṣmākaṃ bhaviṣyati sukhāvahā || 9.5
ahaṃ coktaḥ pṛthaktena śrīnivāsena brāhmaṇa |
kalpitairāgamairnityaṃ māṃ gopāya maheśvara || 9.6
madavajñāpāpaharaṃ nāmnāṃ sahasramuttamam |
paṭhiṣyasi sadā bhadraṃ prārthitena mayā punaḥ || 9.7
tato me hyāgamaiḥ kṛṣṇamācchādya na tu devatāḥ |
yantrairmantraiśca tantraiśca darśitāḥ phaladā dvija || 9.8
tairāgamairmandadhiyā hitvā kṛṣṇaṃ jagadgurum |
bhajanti devatā anyā balidānena nityaśaḥ || 9.9

bhogāvasāne te yānti narakaṃ svatamomayam || 9.10
dṛṣṭvā tathāvidhāṃ lokān pāpāśaṅkitamānasaḥ |
gato'haṃ vāsudevasya caraṇe śaraṇaṃ dvija || 9.11
stutiṃ ca cakre praṇataḥ praśrayānatakaṃdharaḥ |
samāhitamanā vipra prāñjaliḥ puruṣottamam || 9.12
oṃ namo'stu kṛṣṇāya vikuṇṭhavedhase tvatpādalīlāśrayajīvabandhave |
sadāptakāmāya mahārthahetave vijñānavidyānidhaye svayambhuve || 9.13
eko'si sṛṣṭeḥ purato laye tathā yugādikāle ca vidāṃ samakṣataḥ |
anyatra nānātanubhirvirājate tasmā anantācaritāya te namaḥ || 9.14
pradhānakālāśayakarmasākṣiṇe tatsaṃgrahāpāravihārakāriṇe |
kṛṣṇāya nānātanumīyuṣe same kṛtānurāgāya namo namaste || 9.15
śṛṇvanti gāyanti gṛṇanti ye yaśo jagatpavitraṃ jagadīśitustava |
te'nyaṃ na paśyanti sukhāya hyātmano vinā bhavatpādaniṣevaṇādbahiḥ || 9.16
niṣkiṃcanā ye tava pādasaṃśrayāḥ puṣṇanti te tatsukhamātmasambhavam |
jānanti tattvena vidustataḥ parāḥ kāmaiḥ samākṛṣṭadhiyo vicakṣaṇāḥ || 9.17
ahaṃ tu sākṣāttava pādapaṅkajaṃ nityaṃ bhajāno'pi pṛthaṅmatirvibho |
purātmamānaṃ pracikīrṣurātmanaḥ sakāśato'pyadya malaṃ nikṛntayan || 9.18
athāpi te deva padāmbujadvayaṃ nikāmalābhāya sadāstu me hareḥ |
yaccintanātsarvamano'nukūlātsiddhirbhavatyeva kimu prakīrtanāt || 9.19
iti me saṃstutiṃ jñātvā bhagavān praṇatārtihā |
mamākṣigocaraṃ rūpamakarotsa dayāparaḥ || 9.20
sāndrānandamahendranīlamaṇivaddehodgataprollasatsvarṇairmauliṣu hārakuṇḍalayugaiḥ keyūrakāñcyaṅgadaiḥ |
mañjīrairvilasatpiśaṅgavalayaṃ lakṣmyāṅkitaṃ śāśvataṃ sarveśaṃ karuṇākaraṃ suravarairbhaktaiḥ samāsevitam || 9.21
dṛṣṭvā tatpadapaṅkajaṃ hṛdi dadhe govindadāmodaraśrīkṛṣṇeti mukhairvadan trijagato bharturmudāhaṃ tadā |
netrairnirjharavāripūramiva me gātre ca harṣastato vāṇyāṃ gadgadatāṃ vilokya bhagavānmāmāha bhaktapriyaḥ || 9.22
jñatāṃ me suravarya vāñchitatarāṃ madbhaktasaṅgāvaliṃ tatte'haṃ pravadāmi te karuṇayā bhaktāya sākaṃ varaiḥ |
mallīlāṃ gadato bhaviṣyati bhavatsarve janā vaiṣṇavāḥ |
bhāryā cāpi tayānukūlasukhadā bhaktāgraṇīrme bhavān || 9.23

ahaṃ ca tān varāṃllabdhvā kṛtārtho'smi dvijarṣabha || 9.24
atastaddinamārabhya pārvatī bhuvaneśvarī |
matsaṅgādvaiṣṇavī bhūtvā māmāpṛcchatsureśvarī || 9.25
bhavānmahābhāgavataḥ kumārādyā maheśvarāḥ |
kuberādyā devatāśca nandīśādyāśca me gaṇāḥ || 9.26
