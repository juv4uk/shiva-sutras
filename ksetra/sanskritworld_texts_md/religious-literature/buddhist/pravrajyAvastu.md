tāḥ kathayanti taṃ duṣṭhulasamudācāraṃ muktvā ko'nya evaṃvidhaṃ pravrājayiṣyatyetatprakaraṇaṃ bhikṣavo bhagavata ārocayanti | bhagavān saṃlakṣayati | yaḥ kaścidādīnavo bhikṣavaḥ hastacchinnakaṃ pravrājayanti | bhagavānāha | na bhikṣuṇā hastacchinnakaḥ pravrājayitavyaḥ upasaṃpādayitavyo vā bhikṣurhastacchinnakaṃ pravrājayatyupasaṃpādayati sātisāro bhavati ||
