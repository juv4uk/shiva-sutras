kṣemendra: narmamālā


prathamaḥ parihāsaḥ

yenedaṃ svecchayā sarvaṃ māyayā mohitaṃ jagat |
sa jayatyajitaḥ śrīmān kāyasthaḥ parameśvaraḥ  || 1.1 ||
asti svastimatāmagryaṃ maṇḍitaṃ budhamaṇḍalaiḥ  |
khaṇḍitākhaṇḍalāvāsadarpaṃ kaśmīramaṇḍalam  || 1.2 ||
yasmin prājyabhujastambhastambhitāhitavikramaḥ  |
trivikrama iva śrīmānananto balijinnṛpaḥ  || 1.3 ||
tena prajopasargeṣu vāriteṣu vivekinā  |
durniyogiṣu .. .. .. nīteṣu smṛtiśeṣatām  || 1.4 ||
vidagdhacūḍāmaṇinā kenacitkeliśālinā  |
vidvadgoṣṭhīgariṣṭhena kaścitsahṛdayo janaḥ  || 1.5 ||
hāsāyātītakāyasthacaritaṃ kartumīritaḥ  |
karoti tatprasaṅgena durācāraviḍambanām  || 1.6 ||
kṛtaviśvaprapañcāya namo māyāvidhāyine  |
utpattisthitisaṃhārakāriṇe purahāriṇe  || 1.7 ||
vyāpine janmahīnāya nirguṇāya kalābhṛte  |
sarvādhikāriṇe sarvakālakūṭāśanāya te  || 1.8 ||
purā hateṣu daityeṣu viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā  |
duḥkhito dīrghavairāgyastadgehagaṇanāpatiḥ  || 1.9 ||
gatvā vaitaraṇītīraṃ tapo vārṣasahasrakam  |
svamūtraculakāhāraḥ suravairāccakāra saḥ  || 1.10 ||
tuṣṭastametya varadaḥ kaliḥ sākṣādabhāṣata  |
sarvadevavināśāya gaccha vatsa mahītalam  || 1.11 ||

anena kalamāstreṇa maddattena prahāriṇā  |
vicchinnadīpakusumāndhūpahīnānnirambarān  || 1.12 ||
bhraṣṭālayāndhūliliptānhāhābhūtānśvabhirvṛtān  |
kariṣyasi surānsarvānbhaktapānīyakāṅkṣiṇaḥ  || 1.13 ||
jagati brāhmaṇānāṃ ca vṛtticchede tvayā kṛte  |
yajñacchedādvinaṅkṣyanti divi devā na saṃśayaḥ  || 1.14 ||
daityakṣaye kṛte yasmādbhavatā divi roditam  |
tasmāttvaṃ diviro nāma bhuvi khyāto bhaviṣyasi  || 1.15 ||
maṣī sakalamā yasya kālī kavalitākhilā  |
sadā sakalamāyasya tasya sarvārthasiddhidā  || 1.16 ||
tvadvaṃśe'tra bhaviṣyanti daityā divirarūpiṇaḥ  |
yairiyaṃ labdhavibhavaiḥ pṛthivī na bhaviṣyati  || 1.17 ||
ityuktvāntarhite tasminkalau kalmaṣamānasaḥ  |
yāti kāle suvipule mahīmavatatāra saḥ  || 1.18 ||
saunikena prajāto'tha bhūtale marmaghātinā  |
sa *kuddālikabhāryāyāṃ jagadunmūlanavrataḥ  || 1.19 ||
tīkṣṇaistadanvaye jātaiḥ sarvavṛttivilopibhiḥ  |
rūkṣairna kasyacinmitraiḥ pāpaiḥ sarvāpahāribhiḥ  || 1.20 ||
kalpāntairiva sarvatra grastasthāvarajaṅgamaiḥ  |
maṣīviliptasarvāṅgaiḥ kālenāliṅgitairiva  || 1.21 ||
adhogatairmṛdutaraiḥ stabdhairabhyudgataiḥ kṣaṇāt |
purīṣairiva kāyasthaiḥ kāyasthairdoṣakāribhiḥ  || 1.22 ||

sevākāle bahumukhairlubdhakairbahubāhubhiḥ  |
vañcane bahumāyaiśca bahurūpaiḥ surāribhiḥ  || 1.23 ||
vyāptāsu nagaragrāmapurapattanabhūmiṣu  |
tasminkāle maṣīliptakalamena khamullikhan  || 1.24 ||
nanarta kartarīhasto bhūrjaprāvaraṇaḥ kaliḥ  |
bhastrākakṣyābhidhāno'yaṃ sarvabhakṣo mahāsuraḥ  |
jāto jagatkṣayāyeti piśācanicayā jaguḥ  || 1.25 ||
devāpahāriṇā tena goghāsalavaṇacchidā  |
bhujyate pīyate bhūri divireṇa divāniśam  || 1.26 ||
bhaktyā bhagavato viṣṇostrailokyākramaṇe purā  |
dharmaḥ prayāto dravatāṃ maṣīrūpeṇa tiṣṭhati  || 1.27 ||
devanāgamanuṣyāṇāṃ nityanaimittikacchidaḥ  |
tasya kāyasthanāthasya trailokyākramaṇe punaḥ  || 1.28 ||
kaliḥ prayāto dravatāṃ maṣīrūpeṇa tiṣṭhati  |
yathā svargapradā gaṅgā tathaiṣā narakapradā  || 1.29 ||
vyathitaḥ prathitairgrāmairnigaḍairlaguḍaistathā  |
bhayādvairāgyamāpannaḥ sa babhūva mahāvratī  || 1.30 ||
bhagnavyatho'tha santyajya vrataṃ prāyāddigantaram  |
kālena vismṛto'bhyetya bhūrjajño'līkaniḥspṛhaḥ  || 1.31 ||
kramādgrāmaniyogena nagare gaṇanāpateḥ  |
dambhasambhāvitaḥ prāpa gṛhakṛtyaṃ vidhervaśāt || 1.32 ||

tasyānujīvibhiḥ krūrairanuvartanajīvibhiḥ  |
vasuhīnā vasumatī kṛtā prakaṭataskaraiḥ  || 1.33 ||
dambhadhvajo niṣprapañco lubdhakaḥ kalamākaraḥ  |
sūcīmukho bhūrjagupto mahīmaṇḍaśca duḥsahāḥ  || 1.34 ||
daityāvatārāḥ saptaite tanmāhātmyānniyoginaḥ  |
luṇṭhyā vadhyāśca pūjyā ye sarva ityavadanmadāt || 1.35 ||
upatāpo vajrapātaḥ parigho dvārabhañjakaḥ  |
dhūmaketuḥ kapimukhaḥ kukṣibhedo gṛholmukaḥ  || 1.36 ||
aṣṭau piśācāstasyaite bhaṭamukhyāḥ puraḥsarāḥ  |
martyalokavināśāya babhramuryaṣṭipāṇayaḥ  || 1.37 ||
sa vṛtaḥ sevakaśataiḥ sadā dambhaharārcane  |
stotraṃ paṭhati hāheti kurvansāsruvilocanaḥ  || 1.38 ||
sugirā cittahāriṇyā paśyantyā dṛśyamānayā  |
hyaḥ kiyanto mayā dattāḥ prāyasthā vijayeśvare  || 1.39 ||
jayatyullāsitānantamahimā parameśvaraḥ  |
ādau sthitānāmupari prayāntvete trisaptatiḥ  || 1.40 ||
yaḥ sphītaḥ śrīdayābodhaparamānandasampadā  |
prāyasthāne mṛtā bhaṭṭāḥ kṛṣyantāṃ gulphadāmabhiḥ  || 1.41 ||
vidyoddyotitamāhātmyaḥ sa jayatyaparājitaḥ  |
{nirdhāmadhūmakartāro} grāmānyāntu niyoginaḥ  || 1.42 ||
sarvānandasvarūpāya sarvamaṅgalyahetave  |
sarvasvaharaṇaṃ kṛtvā vadhyā daṇḍaniṣedhinaḥ  || 1.43 ||
sarvakleśāpahartre ca cidrūpabrahmaṇe namaḥ  |
pīḍitāḥ prasravantyeva prajā guggulubījavat || 1.44 ||
ityādistotramukharo ghaṇṭābadhiritākhilaḥ  |
samādiśyāviśaccāśu niyoginibiḍāṃ sabhām  || 1.45 ||
marīcaḥ prathitasthānamāsthito janaduṣkṛtaiḥ  |
dadarśa dūrādāyāntaṃ kāryadūtaṃ niyoginam  || 1.46 ||
padāla[gnā]śivaṃ devagṛhoccāṭanacākrikam  |
susūkṣmadalavinyāsavibhāgonnataṭuppikam  || 1.47 ||
atasīkusumacchāyaṃ mṛdusparśāṅgarakṣikam  |
jātyakastūrikāmodasthūlatūlapaṭīvṛtam  || 1.48 ||
hastāṅgulīnyastahaimatriguṇāvartavālikam  |
dūrādhvaklamasocchvāsātkurvāṇaṃ vikṛtīrmukhe  || 1.49 ||
dṛṣṭvā piśunamāyāntaṃ taṃ bhāgavatamantike  |
utthāya harṣādālambya pāṇau pārśve nyaveśayat || 1.50 ||
iti gṛhakṛtyādhipatiḥ  |
piśunebhyo namastebhyo yatprasādānniyoginaḥ  |
dūrasthā api jāyante sahasraśrotracakṣuṣaḥ  || 1.51 ||
so'bravīttvāmahaṃ śrutvā sthitaṃ śaktimatāṃ dhuri  |
prāpto devagṛhādeṣa rāśimārgapradarśakaḥ  || 1.52 ||
vastrālaṅkāraratnādi yatkiṃciddevaveśmasu  |
vidyamānamaśeṣaṃ tatkariṣye prakaṭaṃ tava  || 1.53 ||
vijayeśvaravārāhamārtaṇḍādiṣu vidyate  |
tvadbhāgyopacayādrāśirapoṣyaparipūrakaḥ  || 1.54 ||

abhīrurapavādeṣu niḥśaṅkaḥ pātakeṣu ca  |
tatra tīkṣṇo bhṛśaṃ śaśvatkriyatāṃ paripālakaḥ  || 1.55 ||
sa cāsti bhuvi vikhyātaḥ kāyastho bhavatā samaḥ  |
vinā dhanaṃ vināyāsaṃ sarvasvaharaṇaṃ vinā  || 1.56 ||
brahmahatyā na gaṇyante govadheṣu kathaiva kā  |
prabhubhaktikṛtā yena mūlādunmūlyate janaḥ  || 1.57 ||
anye'pi santi sarvatra tadvidhastu na labhyate  |
nītaḥ svajanako yena nidhanaṃ bandhane dhanī  || 1.58 ||
yadi nāma bhavatpuṇyaiḥ sa sameṣyati madgirā  |
tatsvagehaṃ nidhānānāṃ viddhi svacchandamandiram  || 1.59 ||
iti bruvāṇamasakṛtkarṇe vihitasaṃvidam  |
mahattamastamavadattūrṇamānīyatāmiti  || 1.60 ||
tataḥ sa satvaraṃ dvitrairādarāya gatāgataiḥ  |
tamānināya niścitya pāpinaṃ paripālakam  || 1.61 ||
iti cākrikaḥ puṃścalako vā  |
kāṣṭhastabdhonnatagrīvaḥ saniḥspandordhvalocanaḥ  |
kāmalāharitacchāyaśiraḥśāṭakakañcukaḥ  || 1.62 ||
lambamānena mahatāmedhyakroḍānukāriṇā  |
*udareṇa dareṇeva vyāptaḥ piśitaveśmanā  || 1.63 ||
tīvradarpo mahākopaḥ prāṇahṛnniṣpratikriyaḥ  |
sopadravaḥ sopatāpaḥ sannipāta iva jvaraḥ  || 1.64 ||
sa mahāntaṃ samāsādya duḥsahaṃ daṃśanaṃ viṭam  |
līlayaiva vaśīkṛtya lebhe devagṛhānbahūn  || 1.65 ||
tato mūrtairivāyāsaiḥ sahasaiva puraḥsaraiḥ  |
argha velāṃ yayau kartumasaṅkhyaiḥ parivāritaḥ  || 1.66 ||
kācaro'yaṃ hiraṇyākṣaḥ pūrvavairamanusmaran  |
devānavāptaḥ saṃhartumiti taṃ bubudhe janaḥ  || 1.67 ||
bhayātpalāyya yāteṣu dhanikeṣu surālayāt |
tamayuḥ punarakṣīṇā devāgāranivāsinaḥ  || 1.68 ||
bhaṭairargalitadvārakavāṭasphoṭanākulaiḥ  |
prārabdhe gṛhabhāṇḍādiviluṇṭhanamahotsave  || 1.69 ||
sahasā hṛtavastrāṇāṃ gṛhiṇīnāṃ samāyayau  |
santrastabālakānāṃ ca karuṇo rodanadhvaniḥ  || 1.70 ||
iti paripālakaḥ  |
athāyayau cirāvāptabahuharṣaskhaladgatiḥ  |
kṛśaḥ śanaiścarākāro dhūsaraḥ kṣutkṣatodaraḥ  || 1.71 ||
bahucchidraśiraḥśāṭalaḍatparyantatūstakaḥ  |
śatacakralikāsyūtamalaliptāṅgarakṣakaḥ  || 1.72 ||
śīrṇajīrṇapaṭīguptakakṣyāniyamitāñcalaḥ  |
yācitānītasaṃśuṣkapāda[tra]vyathitaḥ khalaḥ  || 1.73 ||
lekhādhikārī niḥsvo'pi lekhasaṃskāragarvitaḥ  |
paripālakanirdiṣṭo vāyubhakṣa ivoragaḥ  || 1.74 ||
tadgehinī śīrṇavastrakha[ṇḍā]vṛtakaṭītaṭā  |
kajjalāliptanāsāgrā laḍanmṛtkarṇabhūṣaṇā  || 1.75 ||

kṣudhitāpatyakupitā śūrpārdhāvṛtamastakā  |
samutthāya saśūtkāraṃ kaṣantī bahuśaḥ sphijau  || 1.76 ||
patyau cirātprāptapade hṛṣṭādāya yatastataḥ  |
..kṣepāpūpadhūpādyairgaṇādhipamapūjayat || 1.77 ||
so'pyanekārthasandeśānākarṇyāvahitaḥ prabhoḥ  |
dāpyaprasāritakaro lekhānaskhalito'likhat || 1.78 ||
dhāvatkalamacītkāratāraḥ kapirivāhataḥ  |
dīnārāngaṇayannāśu dadau lekhaśatadvayam  || 1.79 ||
paripālakapādānāṃ yatkiṃcidupayujyate  |
darvī bṛsī paṭalikā kuṇḍabhāṇḍakaraṇḍikā  || 1.80 ||
ityādilekhadānena prasiddhiṃ paramāṃ gataḥ  |
so'cireṇābhavatpuṣṭaḥ pūrṇapāṇirmadoddhataḥ  || 1.81 ||
lekhapattrāṇi vigalallocanaḥ parivācayan  |
cakāra vikṛtīstāstā nānābhrūnetrakuñcanaiḥ  || 1.82 ||
iti lekhakopādhyāyaḥ  |
tato gṛhītamadhyasthacchattrabhaṅgavyavasthayā (?)  |
āyayau gañjadiviro bhaṭṭabhāgavatārthitaḥ  || 1.83 ||
sa prāyasthakhalīkārānmānī santyaktakarpaṭaḥ  |
śiraḥśāṭakavinyāsaśvitritārdhalalāṭabhūḥ  || 1.84 ||
sarvadevagṛhagrāmarāśisaṃhāratatparaḥ  |
rajjuśeṣīkṛtāśeṣanirjara..ripālakaḥ  || 1.85 ||
sa prāpya pradadau dīrghāṃ śaratṣaṇmāsakalpanām  |
yasyā madhye'sti likhitaṃ sārdhaṃ lakṣacatuṣṭayam  || 1.86 ||
svārthopāyaṃ tataḥ pṛṣṭastena svīkārasaṃvidā  |
uvāca bhāgasantoṣātkiṃcitsadbhāvamāsthitaḥ  || 1.87 ||
asmindevagṛhe te te prasiddhāḥ paripālakāḥ  |
vikrītanijasarvasvāḥ prayātā madvirodhinaḥ  || 1.88 ||
bhavato'dya tu kartavyā snehādupakṛtirmayā  |
kulācāryaḥ sa bhagavāneko hi gururāvayoḥ  || 1.89 ||
svīkṛtairiha dānena pañcaṣaiścākrikāśivaiḥ  |
bhujyate nikhilaṃ devadravyaṃ bhuktiśca pārṣadī  || 1.90 ||
vikrītaśeṣaṃ yatkiñcidvidyate suramaṇḍale  |
tanmatenaiva tatsarvaṃ bhujyate nijavattvayā  || 1.91 ||
tathā hi tāmrajaḥ pūrvaṃ mahānnīto ghaṭo mayā  |
bhaktitastacchatāṃśena kṛtā ghaṇṭā surālaye  || 1.92 ||
kālena ghaṇṭāṃ vikrīya tadaṃśena kṛtā ghaṭī  |
krameṇa bhakṣitā sāpi kṛtā śeṣeṇa ghaṇṭikā  || 1.93 ||
ciraṃ sañcūrṇitā sāpi kṛtā sūkṣmajhilīmalī  |
iti kṛtvā tataḥ stokaghaṇṭāṃśaḥ paripālitaḥ  || 1.94 ||
evaṃ caturbhujā luṇṭhiḥ kriyate śivapūjayā  |
santi dhānyasahasrāṇi kriyatāṃ bhavatātra tu  |
krayavikrayikā nāma tato vijñapyase mayā  || 1.95 ||
ityupāyaśataistaistaistaduktaiḥ paripālakaḥ  |
*jaraṭhākhurivākṣoṭaṃ śūnyaṃ cakre surālayam  || 1.96 ||
iti gañjadiviraḥ  |

athānyarāśipravaṇaḥ pravīṇaḥ sādhuluṇṭhane  |
āpatpraśamanaṃ prāpa grāmaṃ tasmānniyogavit || 1.97 ||
tasyāvaskarasaṃchannamahārauravasodare  |
khaṇḍasphuṭitanāsāgravāridhānīmahādhane  || 1.98 ||
dāmaprotajaraddvāraskhalatkhaḍakhaḍārave  |
yasya sthitirabhūdgehe kukuṭṭīkoṭarodare  || 1.99 ||
dagdhakambalikākhaṇḍakṛtamuṇḍāvaguṇṭhanaḥ  |
śvajagdhajīrṇaśīrṇāgropānatkhañjaḥ śanairvrajan  || 1.100 ||
karpaṭītilamṛddarbhapavitrārghasamudgakaiḥ  |
dārupāttrīṃ vahanpūrṇāṃ bhagnāmākhuvikhaṇḍitām  || 1.101 ||
snāyī japoccalatkūrcaḥ sadācārapade sthitaḥ  |
dinārdhamapaṭhatstotraṃ gatvā yaḥ suramandiram  || 1.102 ||

tasyaiva daivādāyātakāryasyāśu niyoginaḥ  |

śiśire yasya nāṅgāraṃ pradaduḥ prātiveśminaḥ  |

tataḥ sudhādhavalitaṃ tasya sammārjitāṅganam  |
bahudāsamabhūdgehaṃ sindūrodaramandiram  || 1.106 ||
miṣṭabhojanasañjātanavalāvaṇyasacchaviḥ  |
tasyābhūttaruṇī bhāryā di[nai]rapsarasaḥ samā  || 1.107 ||
tato'pi vrajato grāmaṃ karaṇḍaśayanādikam  |
niryayau purataśchattraṃ kalaśaṃ tāmrakuṇḍakam  || 1.108 ||
śaktiḥ patadgraho ghaṇṭā tāmrapātramupānahau  |
karikā bhagavatpādā bhūrjabhastrātha sruksruvau  || 1.109 ||
akṣasūtraṃ maṣībhāṇḍaṃ darpaṇaḥ snānaśāṭikā  |
sampuṭīṭuppikākhaḍgāḥ pāduke mantrapustikā  || 1.110 ||
nakṣatrapattrikā khaḍgapatraṃ lohitakambalaḥ  |
pavitrasūtrakaṃ tantrī sūcī kalamakartarī  || 1.111 ||
vacā jatumayī rakṣā kṣurikā yogapaṭṭakaḥ  |
stotramantrāṇi gaṅgāmṛdbilvamucchiṣṭaphālakam  || 1.112 ||
ityekīkṛtya tasyāgre bhāṇḍopaskaraṇaṃ yayau  |
itastataḥ samānītamapunardānacetasā  || 1.113 ||
uccaiḥkṛtalatāpāṇirbhāṭakānītaghoṭakaḥ  |
jhāṅkārapārṣṇiprahatirvadanenāñcatā muhuḥ  || 1.114 ||
santarjayanniva janaṃ jyo..rtamiva kurvatā  |
śithilasthūlavasanaḥ sa yayau puramutphalan  || 1.115 ||
praviveśa tato grāmaṃ sudhautasitakarpaṭaḥ  |
kṣayāya grāmyamatsyānāṃ vṛddho baka ivāgataḥ  || 1.116 ||
atha bhojanacaryādivyapadeśaiḥ sahasraśaḥ  |
tasya yakṣeśvarasyeva nidhānānyāyayuḥ puraḥ  || 1.117 ||
yogī haraṇacintāsu prayogī bhūrjayojane  |
