kṛtirmaitreyanāthasya ||
oṃ namo maitreyanāthāya ||


yā sarvajñatayā nayatyupaśamaṃ śāntaiṣiṇaḥ śravakān
yā mārgajñatayā jagaddhitakritāṃ lokārthasaṃpādikā  |
savārkāramidaṃ vadanti munayo viśvaṃ yayā saṃgatā
stasyai śrāvakabodhisattvagaṇino buddhasya manne namaḥ ||

sarvākārajñatāmārgaḥ śāsitrā yo'tra deśita |
dhīmantī vīkṣīṣīraṃstamanālīḍhaṃ parairiti  || 1.1 ||
smṛtau cādhāya sūtrārthaṃ dharmacaryā daśātmikā |
sūkhena pratipatsīrannityārambhaprayojanam  || 1.2 ||
prajñāpāramitāṣṭābhīḥ padārthaiḥ samudīritā  |
sarvākārajñatā mārgajñatā sarvajñatā tataḥ  || 1.3 ||
sarvākārābhisaṃbodho murdhaprārpto'nupūrvikaḥ  |
ekakṣaṇabhisaṃbodho dharmakāyaśca te'ṣṭadhā  || 1.4 ||
cittotpādo'vavādaśca nirvedhāṅgaṃ caturvidhaṃ  |
ādhāraḥ pratipatteśca dharmadhātusvabhāvakaḥ  || 1.5 ||
ālambanaṃ samuddeśaḥ saṃnāhaprasthitikriye  |
saṃbhārāśca saniryāṇāḥ sarvākārajñatā muneḥ  || 1.6 ||
dhyāmīkaraṇatādīni śiṣyakhaṅgapathau ca yau  |
mahānuśaṃso dṛṅmārga aihikāmutrikairguṇaiḥ  || 1.7 ||
kāritramadhimuktiśca stutastobhitaśaṃsitāḥ  |
pariṇāme'numode ca manaskārāvanuttamau  || 1.8 ||
nirhāraḥ śuddhiratyantāmityayaṃ bhāvanāpathaḥ  |
vijñānāṃ bodhisattvānāmiti mārgajñatoditā  || 1.9 ||
prajñayā na bhave sthānaṃ kṛpayā na śame sthitiḥ  |
anupāyena dūratvamupāyenāvidūratā  || 1.10 ||
vipaksapratipaksau ca prayogaḥ samatāsya ca  |
dṛṅmārgaḥ śrāvakādīnāmiti sarvajñateṣyate  || 1.11 ||
ākārāḥ saprayogāśca guṇā doṣāḥ salaksaṇāḥ  |
mokṣanirvedhabhāgīye śaikṣo'vaivartiko gaṇaḥ  || 1.12 ||
samatābhavaśāntyośca kṣetraśuddhiranuttarā  |
sarvākārābhisaṃbodha eṣa sopāyakauśalaḥ  || 1.13 ||
liṅgaṃ tasya vivṛddhiśca nirūḍhiścittasaṃsthitiḥ  |
caturdhā ca vikalpasya pratipakṣaścaturvidhaḥ  || 1.14 ||
pratyekaṃ darsanākhye ca bhāvanākhye ca vartmani  |
ānantaryasamādhiśca saha vipratipattibhiḥ  || 1.15 ||
mūrdhābhisamayaḥ tredhā daśadhā cānupūrvikaḥ  |
ekakṣaṇābhisaṃbodho lakṣaṇena caturvidhiḥ  || 1.16 ||
svābhāvikaḥ sasāṃbhogo nairmāṇiko'parastathā  |
dharmakāyaḥ sakāritraścaturdhā samudīritaḥ  || 1.17 ||
cittotpādaḥ parārthāya sammyaksaṃbodhikāmatā  |
samāsavyāsataḥ sā ca yathāsutraṃ sa cocyate  || 1.18 ||
bhūhemacandrajvalanairnidhiratnākarārṇavaiḥ  |
vajrācalauṣadhīmitraiścintāmaṇyarkagītibhiḥ  || 1.19 ||
nṛpagañjamahāmārgayānaprasravaṇodakaiḥ  |
ānandoktinadīmeghairdvāviṃśatividhaḥ sa ca  || 1.20 ||
pratipattau ca satyeṣu buddharatnādiṣu triṣu  |
asaktāvapariśrāntau pratipatsaṃparigrahe  || 1.21 ||
cakṣuḥṣu pañcasu jñeyaḥ ṣaṭsvabhijñāguṇeṣu ca  |
dṛṅmārge bhāvanākhye cetyavavādo daśātmakaḥ  || 1.22 ||
mṛdutikṣṇendriyau śraddhādṛṣṭiprāptau kulaṃkulau  |
ekavīcyantarotpadya kārākārākaniṣṭhagāḥ  || 1.23 ||
plutāstrayo bhavasyāgraparamo rūparāgaha  |
dṛṣṭadharmaśamaḥ kāyasākṣī khaṅgaśca viṃśatiḥ  || 1.24 ||
ālambanata ākārāddhetutvātsaṃparigrahāt |
caturvikalpasaṃyogaṃ yathāsvaṃ bhajatāṃ satām  || 1.25 ||
śrāvakebhyaḥ sakhaḍgebhyo bodhisattvasya tāyinaḥ  |
mṛdumadhyādhimātrāṇāmūṣmādīnāṃ viśiṣṭatā  || 1.26 ||
ālambanamanityādi satyādhāraṃ tadākṛtiḥ  |
niṣedho'miniveśāderheturyānatrayāptaye  || 1.27 ||
rūpādyāyavyayau viṣṭhāsthitī prajñaptyavācyate  |
rūpādāvasthitisteṣāṃ tadbhāvenāsvabhāvatā  || 1.28 ||
tayormithaḥsvabhāvatvaṃ tadanityādyasaṃsthitiḥ  |
tāsāṃ tadbhāvaśūnyatvaṃ mithaḥ svabhāvyametayoḥ  || 1.29 ||
anudgraho yo dharmāṇāṃ tannimittāsamīksaṇam  |
parīkṣaṇaṃ ca prajñāyāḥ sarvasyānupalambhataḥ  || 1.30 ||
rūpāderasvabhavāvatvaṃ tadabhāvasvabhāvatā  |
tadajātiraniryāṇaṃ śuddhistadanimittatā  || 1.31 ||
tannimittānadhiṣṭhānānadhimuktirasaṃjñatā  |
samādhistasya kāritraṃ vyākṛtirmananāksayaḥ  || 1.32 ||
mithastrikasya svābhāvyaṃ samādheravikalpanā  |
iti nirvedhabhāgīyaṃ mṛdumadhyādhimātrataḥ  || 1.33 ||
dvaividhyaṃ grāhyakalpasya vastutatpratipaksataḥ  |
moharāśyādibhedena pratyekaṃ navadhā tu saḥ  || 1.34 ||
dravyaprajñaptyadhiṣṭhāno dvividho grāhako mataḥ  |
svatantrātmādirūpeṇa skandhādyāśrayatastathā  || 1.35 ||
cittānavalīnatvādi naīḥsvābhāvyādideśakaḥ  |
tadvipaksaparityāgaḥ sarvathā saṃparigrahaḥ  || 1.36 ||
ṣoḍhādhigamadharmasya pratipaksaprahāṇayoḥ  |
tayoḥ paryupayogasya prajñāyāḥ kṛpayā saha  || 1.37 ||
śiṣyāsādhāraṇatvasya parārthānukramasya ca  |
jñānasyāyatnavṛtteśca pratiṣṭhā gotramucyate  || 1.38 ||
dharmadhātorasaṃbhedādgotrabhedo na yujyate  |
ādheyadharmabhedāttu tadbhedaḥ parigīyate  || 1.39 ||
ālambanaṃ sarvadharmāste punaḥ kuśalādayaḥ  |
laukikādhigamākhyāścaṃ ye ca lokettarā matāḥ  || 1.40 ||
sāsravānāsravā dharmāḥ saṃskṛtāsaṃskṛtāśca ye  |
śiṣyasādhāraṇā dharmā ye cāsādhāranā muneḥ  || 1.41 ||
sarvasattvāgratā cittaprahāṇādhigamatraye  |
tribhirmahattvairūddeśo vijñeyo'ya svayaṃbhuvām  || 1.42 ||
dānādau ṣaḍvidha teṣāṃ pratyekaṃ saṃgraheṇa yā  |
saṃnāhapratipattiḥ sā ṣaḍbhiḥ ṣaṭkairyathodithā  || 1.43 ||
dyānārūpyeṣu dānādau mārge maitryādikeṣu ca  |
gatopalambhayoge ca trimaṇḍalaviśuddhiṣu  || 1.44 ||
uddeśe ṣaṭsvabhijñāsu sarvākārajñatānaye  |
prasthānapratipajjñeyā mahāyānādhirohiṇī  || 1.45 ||
dayā dānādikaṃ ṣaṭkaṃ śamathaḥ savidarśanaḥ  |
yuganaddhaśca yo mārga upāye yacca kauśalam  || 1.46 ||
jñānaṃ puṇyaṃ ca mārgaśca dhāraṇī bhūmayo daśa  |
pratipakṣaśca vijñeyaḥ saṃbhārapratipatkramaḥ  || 1.47 ||
labhyate prathamā bhumirdaśadhā parikarmaṇā  |
āśayo hitavastutvaṃ sattveṣu samacittatā  || 1.48 ||
tyāgaḥ sevā ca mitrāṇāṃ saddharmālambanaiṣaṇā  |
sadā naiṣkramyacittatvaṃ buddhakāyagatā spṛhā  || 1.49 ||
dharmasya deśanā satyaṃ daśamaṃ vākyamiṣyate  |
jñeyaṃ ca pārikarmaiṣāṃ svabhāvānupalambhataḥ  || 1.50 ||
śīlaṃ kṛtajñatā kṣāntiḥ pramodyaṃ mahatī kṛpā  |
gauravaṃ guruśuśrūṣā vīryaṃ dānādike'ṣṭamam  || 1.51 ||
atṛptatā śrute dānaṃ dharmasya ca nirāmiṣam  |
buddhakṣetrasya saṃśuddhiḥ saṃsārāparikheditā  || 1.52 ||
hrīrapatrāpyamityetatpañcadhā mananātmakam  |
vanāśālpecchatā tuṣṭirdhūtasaṃlekhasevanam  || 1.53 ||
śikṣāyā aparityāgaḥ kāmānāṃ vijugupsanam  |
nirvitsarvāstisaṃtyāgo'navalinānapekṣate  || 1.54 ||
saṃstavaṃ kulamātsaryaṃ sthānaṃ saṃgaṇikāvaham  |
ātmotkarṣaparāvajñe karmamārgān daśāśubhān  || 1.55 ||
mānaṃ stambhaṃ viparyāsaṃ vimatiṃ kleśamarṣaṇam  |
vivarjayan samāpneti daśaitān pañcamīṃ bhuvam  || 1.56 ||
dānaśīlakṣamāvīryadhyānaprajñāprapūraṇāt |
śiṣyakhaḍgaspṛhātrāsacetasāṃ parivarjakaḥ  || 1.57 ||
yācito'navalinaśca sarvatyāge'pyadurmanāḥ  |
kṛśo'pi nārthināṃ kṣeptā ṣaṣṭhīṃ bhūmiṃ samaśrute  || 1.58 ||
ātmasattvagraho jīvapudgalocchedaśāśvataḥ  |
nimittahetvoḥ skandheṣu dhātuṣvāyataneṣu ca  || 1.59 ||
traidhātuke pratiṣṭhānaṃ saktirālīnacittatā  |
ratnatritayaśīleṣu taddṛṣṭyabhiniveśitā  || 1.60 ||
śūnyatāyāṃ vivadaśca tadvirodhaśca viṃśatiḥ  |
kalaṅkā yasya vicchinnāḥ saptamīmetyasau bhuvam  || 1.61 ||
trivimokṣamukhajñānaṃ trimaṇḍalaviśuddhatā  |
karūṇāmananā dharmasamataikanayajñatā  || 1.62 ||
anutpādakṣamājñānaṃ dharmāṇāmekadheraṇā  |
kalpanāyāḥ samuddhātaḥ saṃjñādṛkkleśavarjanam  || 1.63 ||
śamathasya ca nidhyaptiḥ kauśalaṃ ca vidarśane  |
cittasya dāntatā jñānaṃ sarvatrāpratighāti ca  || 1.64 ||
sakterabhūmiryatrecchaṃ kṣetrāntaragatiḥ samam  |
sarvatra svātmabhāvasya darśanaṃ ceti viṃśatiḥ  || 1.65 ||
sarvasattvamanojñānamabhijñākrīḍanaṃ śubhā  |
buddhakṣetrasya niṣpattirbuddhasevāparīkṣaṇe  || 1.66 ||
akṣajñānaṃ jinakṣetraśuddhirmāyopamā sthitiḥ  |
saṃcintya ca bhavādānamidaṃ karmāṣṭadhoditam  || 1.67 ||
praṇidhānānyanantāni devādīnāṃ rūtajñatā  |
nadīva pratibhānānāṃ garbhāvakrāntirūttamā  || 1.68 ||
kulajātyośca gotrasya parivārasya janmanaḥ  |
naiṣkramyabodhivṛkṣāṇāṃ guṇapūreśca saṃpadaḥ  || 1.69 ||
nava bhūmīratikramya buddhabhūmau pratiṣṭhate  |
yena jñānena sā jñeyā daśamī bodhisattvabhūḥ  || 1.70 ||
pratipakṣo'ṣṭadhā jñeyo darṣanābhyāsamārgayoḥ  |
grāhyagrāhakavikalpānāmaṣṭānāmupaśāntaye  || 1.71 ||
uddeśe samatāyāṃ ca sattvārthe yatnavarjane  |
atyantāya ca niryāṇaṃ niryāṇaṃ prāptilakṣaṇam  || 1.72 ||
sarvākārajñatayāṃ ca niryāṇaṃ mārgagocaram  |
niryāṇapratipajjñeyā seyamaṣṭavidhātmikā  || 1.73 ||

|| abhisamayālaṅkāre prajñāpāramitopadeśaśāstre
sarvākārajñatādhikāraḥ prathamaḥ  ||



dhyāmīkaraṇatā bhābhirdevānāṃ yogyatāṃ prati  |
viṣayo niyato vyāptiḥ svabhāvastasya karma ca  || 2.1 ||
caturṇamāryasatyānāmākārānupalambhataḥ  |
śrāvakāṇāmayaṃ mārgo jñeyo mārgajñatānaye  || 2.2 ||
rūpādiskandhaśūnyatvācchūnyatānāmabhedataḥ  |
uṣmāṇaḥ anupalambhena teṣāṃ murdhagataṃ matam  || 2.3 ||
kṣāntayasteṣu nityādiyogasthānaniṣedhataḥ  |
daśa bhūmīḥ samārabhya vistarāsthānadeśanat || 2.4 ||
agradharmagataṃ proktamāryaśrāvakavartmani  |
tatkasya hetorbuddhena buddhvā dharmāsamīkṣaṇāt || 2.5 ||
paropadeśavaiyarthyaṃ svayaṃbodhātsvayaṃbhuvām  |
gambhīratā ca jñānasya khaḍgānāmabhidhīyate  || 2.6 ||
śuśrūṣā yasya yasyārthe yatra yatra yathā yathā  |
sa so'rthaḥ khyātyaśabdo'pi tasya tasya tathā tathā  || 2.7 ||
grahyārthakalpanāhānādgrāhakasyāprahāṇataḥ  |
ādhārataśca vijñeyaḥ khaḍgamārgasya saṃgrahaḥ  || 2.8 ||
prajñapteravirodhena dharmatāsūcanākṛtiḥ  |
ussmagaṃ mūrdhagaṃ rūpādyahānādiprabhāvitam  || 2.9 ||
adyātmaśūnyatādyābhī rūpāderaparigrahāt |
kṣāntiḥ rūpādyanutpādādyākārairagradharmatā  || 2.10 ||
kṣāntijñānakṣaṇaiḥ satyasatyaṃ prati caturvidhaiḥ  |
mārgajñatāyāṃ dṛṅmārgaḥ sānuśaṃso'yamucyate  || 2.11 ||
ādhārādheyatābhāvāttathatābuddhayormithaḥ  |
paryāyeṇānanujñānaṃ mahattā sāpramāṇatā  || 2.12 ||
parimāṇāntatābhāvo rūpāderavadhāraṇam  |
tasyāṃ sthitasya buddhatve'nugrahātyāgatādayaḥ  || 2.13 ||
maītryādi śūnyatā prāptirbuddhatvasya parigrahaḥ  |
sarvasya vyavadānasya sarvādhivyādhiśātanam  || 2.14 ||
nirvāṇagrāhaśāntatvaṃ buddhebho rakṣaṇādikam  |
aprāṇivadhamārabhya sarvākārajñatānaye  || 2.15 ||
svayaṃ sthitasya sattvānāṃ sthāpanaṃ pariṇāmanam  |
danādīnāṃ ca saṃbodhāviti mārgajñatākṣaṇāḥ  || 2.16 ||
sarvato damanaṃ nāma sarvataḥ kleśanirjayaḥ  |
upamakrāviṣahyatvaṃ bodhirādhārapūjyatā  || 2.17 ||
adhimuktistridhā jñeyā svārthā ca svaparārthakā  |
parārthikaivetyeṣa ca pratyekaṃ trividheṣyate  || 2.18 ||
mṛdvī madhyādhimātrā ca mṛdumṛdvādibhedataḥ  |
sā punastrividhetyevaṃ saptaviṃśatidhā matā  || 2.19 ||
stutiḥ stobhaḥ praśaṃsā ca prajñāparamitāṃ prati  |
adhimokṣasya mātrāṇāṃ navakaistribhiriṣyate  || 2.20 ||
viśeṣaḥ pariṇāmastu tasya kāritramuttamam  |
nopalambhākṛtiścāsāvaviparyāsalakṣaṇaḥ  || 2.21 ||
vivikto buddhapuṇyaughasvabhāvasmṛtigocaraḥ  |
sopāyaścānimittaśca buddhairabhyanumoditaḥ  || 2.22 ||
traidhātukāprapannaśca pariṇāmo'parastridhā  |
mṛdurmadhyo'dhimātraśca mahāpuṇyodayātmakaḥ  || 2.23 ||
upāyānupalambābhyāṃ śubhamūlānumodanā  |
anumode manaskārabhāvaneha vidhīyate  || 2.24 ||
svabhāvaḥ śreṣṭhatā tasya sarvasyānabhisaṃskṛtiḥ  |
nopalambhena dharmāṇāmarpaṇā ca mahārthatā  || 2.25 ||
buddhasevā ca dānādirūpāye yacca kauśalam  |
hetavo'trādhimokṣasya dharmavyasanahetavaḥ  || 2.26 ||
mārādhiṣṭhānagambhīradharmatānadhimuktate  |
skandhādyabhiniveśaśca pāpamitraparigrahaḥ  || 2.27 ||
phalaśuddhiśca rūpādiśuddhireva tayordvayoḥ  |
abhinnācchinnatā yasmāditi śuddhirudīritā  || 2.28 ||
kleśajñeyatrimārgasya śiṣyakhaḍgajinaurasām  |
hānādviśuddhirātyantikī tu buddhasya sarvathā  || 2.29 ||
mṛdumṛdvādiko mārgaḥ śuddhirnavasu bhūmiṣu  |
adhimātrādhimātrādermalasya pratipakṣataḥ  || 2.30 ||
tridhātupratipakṣatvaṃ samatā manameyayoḥ  |
mārgasya ceṣyate tasya parihārataḥ  || 2.31 ||

|| abhisamayālaṅkāre prajñāpāramitopadeśaśāstre
mārgajñatādhikāro dvitīyaḥ  ||



nāpare na pare tīre nāntarāle tathoḥ sthitā  |
adhvanāṃ samatājñānātprajñāpāramitāmatā  || 3.1 ||
anupāyena dūraṃ sā sanimittopalambhataḥ  |
upāyakauśalenāsyāḥ samyagāsannatoditā  || 3.2 ||
rūpādiskandhaśūnyatve dharmeṣu tryadhvageṣu ca  |
dānādau bodhipakṣeṣu caryāsaṃjñā vipakṣatā  || 3.3 ||
dānādiṣvanahaṃkāraḥ pareṣāṃ tanniyojanam  |
saṅgakoṭīniṣedho'yaṃ sukṣmaḥ saṅgo jinādiṣu  || 3.4 ||
tadgāmbīryaṃ prakṛtyaiva vivekāddharmapaddhateḥ *iti*  |
ekaprakṛtikaṃ jñānaṃ dharmāṇāṃ saṅgavarjanam  || 3.5 ||
dṛṣṭādipratiṣedhena tasyā durbodhatoditā *iti*  |
rūpādibhiravijñānāttadacintyatvamiṣyate  || 3.6 ||
evaṃ kṛtvā yathokto vai jñeyaḥ sarvajñatānaye  |
ayaṃ vibhāgo niḥśoṣo vipakṣapratipakṣayoḥ  || 3.7 ||
rūpādau tadanityādau tadapuriprapūrayoḥ  |
tadasaṅgatve caryāyā: prayogaḥ pratiṣedhataḥ  || 3.8 ||
avikāro na kartā ca prayogo duṣkarastridhā  |
yathābhavyaṃ phalaprāpteravandhyo'bhimataśca saḥ  || 3.9 ||
aparapratyayo yaśca saptadhā khyātivedakaḥ  |
caturdhā mananā tasya rūpādau samatā matā  || 3.10 ||
dharmajñānānvayajñānakṣāntijñānakṣaṇātmakaḥ  |
duḥkhādisatye dṛṅmārga eṣa sarvajñatānaye  || 3.11 ||
rūpaṃ na nityaṃ nānityamatitāntaṃ viśuddhakam  |
anutpannāniruddhādi vyomābhaṃ lepavarjitam  || 3.12 ||
parigraheṇa nirmuktamavyāhāraṃ svabhāvataḥ  |
pravyāhāreṇa nāsyārthaḥ pareṣu prāpyate yataḥ  || 3.13 ||
nopalambhakṛdatyantaviśuddhirvyādhyasaṃbhavaḥ  |
apāyocchittyakalpatve phalasākṣātkriyāṃ prati  || 3.14 ||
