|| oṃ namaḥ sarvajñāya ||

adhigataparamārthaḥ śrīghanavyūha eva prati tuṣitajanārthaṃ yo'bhavacchvetaketuḥ  |
tata iha jagadarthaṃ cābhavacchākyaketuḥ sa jayatu jitamṛtyurdarśitānantamāyaḥ  || 1.1 ||
ajarāmaratāṃ prāptamajarāmarakārakam  |
ajarāmaratāprāptyai natvā saṃbuddhamādarāt || 1.2 ||
durbodhaṃ mandabuddhīnāṃ duṣkaraṃ calacetasām  |
sāriṣṭaṃ vañcanaṃ mṛtyorucyate śāstrasaṃgatam  || 1.3 ||
sarvathā vañcanaṃ mṛtyormuktānāmeva yujyate  |
amuktānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ kiyatkālavilambanam  || 1.4 ||
āyurindriyacittānāṃ nirodho mṛtyulakṣaṇam  |
upāyairjīvitaṃ dīrghaṃ mṛtyuvañcanamucyate  || 1.5 ||
yāvajjīvati saṃsāre dharmācārī jitendriyaḥ  |
tāvatpuṇyādisaṃbhāraṃ prasūte bodhisādhakam  || 1.6 ||
na vinā jīvitātsāraṃ saṃsāre kiṃ cidīkṣyate  |
nānopāyairatastūrṇaṃ mṛtyuvañcanamārabhet || 1.7 ||
na duṣkaraṃ yataḥ kiṃ cidupāyairyuktiyojitaiḥ  |
maṇimantrauṣadhīnāṃ ca prabhāvaḥ sarvasaṃmataḥ  || 1.8 ||
lakṣaṇāddūrato mṛtyuṃ jñātvā yuktā pratikriyā  |
maste patati vajrāgnau suprajño'pi karoti kim  || 1.9 ||
bāhyādhyātmikabhedena dvidhā maraṇalakṣaṇam  |
kathyate jñāyate yena maraṇaṃ bhāvi tatparaiḥ  || 1.10 ||
mṛtyorlakṣaṇamājñātuṃ vāñchā cetkasya cidbhavet |
tena svasthaśarīreṇa jñātavyaṃ saṃśayo'nyathā  || 1.11 ||
dhātuvaiṣamyato rogāttimirādiprabhāvataḥ  |
bhayaśokādisāmarthyātpratibhāti yato'nyathā  || 1.12 ||
daivabhūtobhayakṣobhānmṛtyuṃ trividhalakṣaṇam  |
prāpyāriṣṭasamudbhūtirato bhedena lakṣayet || 1.13 ||
bāhyābhyantarabhedena daivakṣobho dvidheṣyate  |
bhūtakṣobho'pi dvividhastathaiveti nirūpayet || 1.14 ||
daivasaṃkṣobhato mṛtyurādhidaivika ucyate  |
daivakṣobho'naśanādivajrapātādilakṣaṇaḥ  || 1.15 ||
bhūtasaṃkṣobhato mṛtyurādhibhautika ucyate  |
bhūtakṣobhastu pittādivahnitoyādyupadravaḥ  || 1.16 ||
ubhayakṣobhato mṛtyurubhayājjo nigadyate  |
ubhayakṣobho vajrādipittaśleṣmādyupadravaḥ  || 1.17 ||
ariṣṭānāmanantatvānnotpattiryugapanmatā  |
kramaśo'pi na sarveṣāmapi tvekādisaṃkhyayā  || 1.18 ||
adṛṣṭvā lakṣaṇaṃ yatra mṛtyurdṛṣṭaḥ kva cidbhavet |
niśceturdūṣaṇaṃ tatra na tu lakṣaṇanāstitā  || 1.19 ||
dṛṣṭvāpi lakṣaṇaṃ yatra na mṛtyurdṛśyate kva cit |
abhrāntau tatra dharmādibādhakāntarakalpanam  || 1.20 ||
niścite dūrato mṛtyau prasiddhirmahatī bhavet |
yathāśakti parityāge pratīkāre ca vartanam  || 1.21 ||
sadā samāhito bhūtvā tadgataikāgramānasaḥ  |
bāhyāni prathamaṃ tāvallakṣaṇānyupalakṣayet || 1.22 ||
lakṣaṇānāṃ paricchedakālo dvividha iṣyate  |
niyato'niyataśceti vijñeyaḥ sa tu yatnataḥ  || 1.23 ||
saṃkrāntipūrṇamāsyādau prabhātādikṣaṇātmakaḥ  |
kālo niyata ityukto'niyatastu viparyayāt || 1.24 ||
sphuratsaṃdhi kapolaṃ syādatyarthaṃ mṛtyudarśanāt |
vātaśītādisaṅgācca lakṣaṇīyamataḥ pṛthak || 1.25 ||
karkaṭo rāśirāṣāḍhaḥ puṣyastu makaro mataḥ  |
karkaṭe makare cāpi jāyate mṛtyulakṣaṇam  || 1.26 ||
āṣāḍhasaṃkrāntidine madhyāhne padamātrikā  |
uttarābhimukhasthasya cchāyā svābhāvikī bhavet || 1.27 ||
puṣyasaṃkrāntidivase saiva saptapadībhavet |
vyabhre nabhasi madhyāhne cchāyāyāśca nirīkṣaṇam  || 1.28 ||
pratimāsaṃ padavṛddhiḥ pratimāsaṃ padakṣayaḥ  |
amuṣmādanyathābhāve mṛtyuśaṅkā prajāyate  || 1.29 ||
ekaikapadavṛddhiḥ syādāṣāḍhānmakarāvadheḥ  |
ekaikapādyahrāsaḥ syānmakarātkarkaṭāvadheḥ  || 1.30 ||
āṣāḍhātkramaśo vṛddhiḥ puṣyāddhrāsaḥ kramāttathā  |
chāyāvikāramālocya māsānmaraṇamādiśet || 1.31 ||
kṣayakāle bhavedvṛddhirvṛddhikāle kṣayo yadi  |
tameva kālamārabhya mṛtyurmāsādviniścitaḥ  || 1.32 ||
vṛddhikāle'pyativṛddhau nānārogādupadravaḥ  |
kṣayakāle'pyatikṣaye mṛtyureva na saṃśayaḥ  || 1.33 ||
āṣāḍhapuṣyayorante pūrvaṃ karkaṭamatsyayoḥ  |
yāni ṣaṭṣaḍdinānyatra kālākālanirūpaṇam  || 1.34 ||
āṣāḍhāntadinairebhiḥ śrāvaṇādau śubhāśubham  |
puṣyaśeṣadinaiścāpi māghādiṣu śubhāśubham  || 1.35 ||
pādayostālukāṃ viddhvā nābhau vedho yadā bhavet |
ahorātratrayādūrdhvaṃ pañcatvaṃ jāyate tadā  || 1.36 ||
pādayostālukāṃ viddhvā yadi cakṣuśca vidhyati  |
tadā māsatrayādūrdhvaṃ gamanaṃ yamasadmani  || 1.37 ||
pādayostālukāṃ viddhvā nāsikāṃ yadi vidhyati  |
tribhirdinaistadā nūnamekākī yāti pañcatām  || 1.38 ||
kuṭiprasrāvayoḥ kāle tulyaṃ syādyadi hañchikā  |
tasyāmeva hi velāyāṃ mṛtyurvarṣeṇa tasya hi  || 1.39 ||
nāsikāgaddrikāvedhe maraṇaṃ pañcavatsaraiḥ  |
jihvāgrādarśane mṛtyurvāsaraiḥ syāttribhirnṛṇām  || 1.40 ||
māsaiścaturbhirmṛtyuḥ syātkarṇāgre tīkṣṇavedhanāt |
sadyo mṛtyurbhavennūnamūrṇāsthāne pravedhanāt || 1.41 ||
tulyakālaṃ yadā vedhaścandrasūryagato bhavet |
tadā māsādbhavenmṛtyuḥ puṇyamevārjayettataḥ  || 1.42 ||
bhagaliṅgasamāyoge madhye śeṣe ca hañchikā  |
syāccettulyaṃ tadā māsaiḥ pañcatvaṃ yāti pañcabhiḥ  || 1.43 ||
hastayoḥ pādayoścaiva kaniṣṭhānāṃ ca saṃdhayaḥ  |
catvāro yasya vidhyanti tulyaṃ māsātsa mṛtyubhāk || 1.44 ||
hṛtkaṇṭhamadhyayorvedhastulyakālaṃ yadā bhavet |
pakṣatrayeṇa mṛtyuḥ syādyadi dharmaṃ na sevate  || 1.45 ||
tulyakālaṃ yadā vidhyettālukānāṃ trayaṃ muhuḥ  |
tribhirdinaistadā mṛtyuryadi śakrasamo bhavet || 1.46 ||
vāmākṣiputtalīcchāyāṃ yo na paśyati darpaṇe  |
saptāhānmriyate nūnaṃ yadi na syātpratikriyā  || 1.47 ||
maithune sati ghaṇṭāyā nādaścetkarṇayorbhavet |
tribhirmāsaistadā mṛtyuryadi brahmasamo bhavet || 1.48 ||
kṛṣṇaṃ yadi bhavecchukraṃ śuklāyāṃ pratipattithau  |
ṣaḍbhirmāsaistadā mṛtyurlohitaṃ vyādhisūcakam  || 1.49 ||
karṇamūle bhruvormadhye mastakāgre pṛthakpṛthak |
yadi vedho'pratīkāro mṛtyuḥ sadyastadā bhavet || 1.50 ||
pādāṅguṣṭhaṃ samārabhya nābhiparyantago bhavet |
vedhaḥ sadāho yasyāsau ṣaḍmāsānmriyate dhruvam  || 1.51 ||
pādāṅguṣṭhāddhṛdayāntaṃ yāvatkaṇṭhaṃ śiro'thavā  |
māsatrayātpakṣatrayānmṛtyurekadinātkramāt || 1.52 ||
nāsāgramāṃsaśaithilyānmṛtyuḥ syātsaptarātrataḥ  |
kapolamāṃsavicchedānmṛtyuḥ syātpañcamāsataḥ  || 1.53 ||
cakṣurnāsikayormadhye spandanaṃ cenna dṛśyate  |
jñātvā pañcadinānmṛtyumādadyāddharmasaṃvalam  || 1.54 ||
netrayostārake mliṣṭe sahasā bhavato yadi  |
ṣaḍmāsābhyantare mṛtyuḥ pratikārakriyā na cet || 1.55 ||
akasmānnāsikā vakrā karṇau bhraṣṭau svadeśataḥ  |
netre ca vartulībhūte saptāhānmṛtyudarśanāt || 1.56 ||
akasmāddhṛdayaṃ nimnaṃ syāccetpakṣāttadā mṛtiḥ  |
grīvāpārśvasthayornāḍyorvicchedādapi pakṣataḥ  || 1.57 ||
akasmātkṛṣṇarekhā syājjihvāyā yadi madhyataḥ  |
dṛḍho vā dantasaṃdaṃśastadā mṛtyurdvirātrataḥ  || 1.58 ||
karṇaśaṣkulikāpṛṣṭhanāḍīvicchedadarśanāt |
tasminneva dine mṛtyurathavā pañcavāsaraiḥ  || 1.59 ||
nakheṣu raktavarṇeṣu sahasā yadyaraktatā  |
ṣaḍbhirmāsaistadā mṛtyuryadi mantrādi nācaret || 1.60 ||
akasmājjāyate sthūlaḥ kṛśaḥ kruddho bhayākulaḥ  |
yastasya mṛtyurvarṣeṇa yadi dharmaṃ na sevate  || 1.61 ||
indriyāṇi na gṛhṇanti yathāsvaṃ yasya gocarān  |
muhuḥ skhalati vāṇī ca muhuśca mativibhramaḥ  || 1.62 ||
cakṣuṣī sravato nityaṃ dṛṣṭarūpe'pi vibhramaḥ  |
darpaṇe salile vāpi svacchāyāṃ yo na paśyati  || 1.63 ||
rātrāvindradhanuḥ paśyeddivā nakṣatramaṇḍalam  |
ameghe vidyutaṃ paśyetsphurantīṃ dakṣiṇāśritām  || 1.64 ||
divā chāyāpathaṃ paśyedulkāyāḥ patanaṃ tathā  |
haṃsakākamayūrāṇāṃ paśyedekatra melakam  || 1.65 ||
candradvayaṃ dvisūryaṃ vā svaśirojvalanaṃ tathā  |
gandharvanagaraṃ paśyedvṛkṣāgre śikhare gireḥ  || 1.66 ||
paśyetpretapiśācānadṛśyānanyāṃśca bhīṣaṇān  |
prakampate bhṛśaṃ caiva mūrchito vā bhavenmuhuḥ  || 1.67 ||
chardiṃ mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca suvarṇarajataprabham  |
paśyedekaikaśastasya mṛtyurmāsāvadherbhavet || 1.68 ||
kaṇṭḥoṣṭhatālujihvānāṃ chedaṃ chidraṃ tanau tathā  |
yaḥ paśyettasya mṛtyuḥ syātṣaḍmāsābhyantare dhruvam  || 1.69 ||
kaṇṭhoṣṭhatālurasanādantā yasya pṛthakpṛthak |
śuṣyantyabhīkṣṇaṃ ṣaḍmāsānmaraṇaṃ tasya nirdiśet || 1.70 ||
nakṣatracakrasaptarṣidiśāṃ rātrāvadarśanam  |
saptāhamṛtyorjāyeta jvālā vākasmikī tanau  || 1.71 ||
arundhatīṃ rohiṇīṃ ca dhruvaṃ chāyāpathaṃ tathā  |
rātrāvapaśyato'kasmātṣaḍmāsānmṛtyumādiśet || 1.72 ||
kalaṅkarahitaṃ candraṃ sūryaṃ raśmivivarjitam  |
saraśmikaṃ citrabhānuṃ candraṃ cāpi saraśmikam  || 1.73 ||
rātrau sūryaṃ divā candraṃ svanetre jvalanaṃ tathā  |
tārāṃ merupramāṇāṃ ca samudraṃ ca nadīmiva  || 1.74 ||
nābhau hikkāṃ gude hañchāṃ varṇaṃ padmopamaṃ mukhe  |
galake piṭakān raktān gātre varṇavicitratām  || 1.75 ||
hṛdaye krandanaṃ sāśru prakampamatha tāluni  |
candrāṃśuṃ vahnisaṃsparśaṃ sūryāṃśuṃ himaśītalam  || 1.76 ||
candracchidraṃ ravicchidraṃ chidraṃ bhūmau tathāmbare  |
ātmanaiva hi yaḥ paśyetpaśyenmṛtyuṃ sa pakṣataḥ  || 1.77 ||
mūtraśukrapurīṣāṇi tulyakālaṃ patanti cet |
varṣānmṛtyurbhavettatra bhaiṣajyādi pratikriyā  || 1.78 ||
ardhaṃ śītaṃ tathā coṣṇamardhaṃ yasya kalevaram  |
saptarātrādbhavenmṛtyuḥ paralokaṃ vicintayet || 1.79 ||
hakāraḥ śītasaṃsparśaḥ phukāro vahnisaṃnibhaḥ  |
yasyānubhūyate tasya daśāhādyamadarśanam  || 1.80 ||
anāmikānāṃ mūleṣu dṛṣṭayā kṛṣṇarekhayā  |
aṣṭādaśadinādūrdhvaṃ mṛtyuḥ prākkarmadoṣataḥ  || 1.81 ||
yasya mastakamāruhya trivarṇo yāti vegataḥ  |
kṛkalāso mṛtistasya pañcamāsādviniścitā  || 1.82 ||
śabdo na śrūyate dehāddhastābhyāmapamārjitāt |
sarvāṅgaśītalatvena daśāhānmaraṇaṃ dhruvam  || 1.83 ||
yugapannirgamo bāhujaṅghayoryasya gacchataḥ  |
vināśo brahmasūtrasya mṛtyustasyaikavarṣataḥ  || 1.84 ||
kardame pāṃśudeśe vā purataḥ pṛṣṭhato'pi vā  |
khaṇḍaṃ pādodaye nyūnaṃ mṛtyurmāsacatuṣṭayāt || 1.85 ||
akasmātpuruṣaṃ paśyetkṛṣṇapiṅgalavarṇakam  |
varṣatrayātyayānmṛtyuryadi bhrāntyā na kalpitaḥ  || 1.86 ||
dvimāsamṛtyurhṛtpādau snātamātrasya śuṣyataḥ  |
trirātramṛtyurdurgandho jāyate vikṛtākṛtiḥ  || 1.87 ||
kharārkadivase toyairāpūrya mukhakoṭaram  |
pratyahaṃ phūtkṛtaṃ kurvan kramāttanmadhyasaṃsthitam  || 1.88 ||
paśyedindradhanuścitraṃ dīrghāyuḥ puruṣaḥ sadā  |
ṣaḍmāsāyurna tatpaśyediti karmavicitratā  || 1.89 ||
kharārkadivase paśyetpuro gaganamadhyataḥ  |
muktāhārānivālūnānmuktāphalavibhūṣaṇān  || 1.90 ||
indranīlamaṇicchāyān keśoṇḍukasamākṛtīn  |
dṛśyadeśaṃ parityajya deśāntaravisarpiṇaḥ  || 1.91 ||
nāgānanekaśaḥ paśyetsaṃyatāniva saṃtatam  |
teṣāmadṛṣṭau ṣaḍmāsānmaraṇaṃ parikīrtitam  || 1.92 ||
prabhāte vātha sāyāhne jyotsnāyāṃ vā ciraṃ puraḥ  |
vitatya bāhū svacchāyāṃ dṛṣṭvā paśyecchanairnabhaḥ  || 1.93 ||
tatrāpi dṛśyate chāyā dhavalā nararūpiṇī  |
śiraso'darśanāttasya mṛtyuḥ syādvarṣamadhyataḥ  || 1.94 ||
putrabhāryāvināśaḥ syādvāmapāṇeradarśanāt |
dakṣiṇādarśanātpitṛbhrātrādīnāṃ mahīyasām  || 1.95 ||
jānūpari sthitau bāhū kṛtvā mūrdhni tathāñjalim  |
rambhāphalanibhāṃ chāyāṃ lakṣayenmadhyatastayoḥ  || 1.96 ||
yadā tvekaṃ dalaṃ tatra vikāsi parilakṣyate  |
tasyāmeva tithau nūnaṃ ṣaḍmāsānmaraṇaṃ bhavet || 1.97 ||
sūryamārgagate vāyau lakṣaṇīyamidaṃ trayam  |
śrotre śirasi netre ca śabdo dhūmo dyutistathā  || 1.98 ||
karṇau pidhāya sudṛḍhaṃ gambhīro gulgulo dhvaniḥ  |
na śrūyate pañcadinaṃ daśa pañcadaśāthavā  || 1.99 ||
viṃśatiṃ divasān yāvatpañcaviṃśatimeva vā  |
bāṇābdhiguṇanetrenduvarṣairmṛtyuryathākramam  || 1.100 ||
eṣāmapyantarāleṣu śodhyāḥ ṣoḍaśa vāsarāḥ  |
varṣasyaiva caturthāṃśaiḥ kramaśaścābhivardhitaiḥ  || 1.101 ||
mastakopari niryāntīṃ saptāhaṃ dhūmamālikām  |
yo na paśyati tasya syānmṛtyurvarṣatrayāvadheḥ  || 1.102 ||
svanetrayoścaturdikṣu dṛśyante'ṅgulipīḍanāt |
mārjāranetratulyāni jyotīṃṣi jhaṭitikramāt || 1.103 ||
apīḍane'pi taddṛṣṭau mṛtyuḥ syācchatavāsaraiḥ  |
etatsāmānyato jñeyaṃ viśeṣastvayamucyate  || 1.104 ||
candrasyādhastathordhvaṃ ca karṇanāsopakaṇṭhayoḥ  |
naṣṭe jyotiṣi mṛtyuḥ syātṣaṭtrinetrendumāsataḥ  || 1.105 ||
sūrye'pi yadi candrasya kramaḥ syātpūrvavattadā  |
digbāṇaguṇanetreṣu dineṣu maraṇaṃ bhavet || 1.106 ||
svarūpasya viparyāsātṣaḍmāsānmaraṇaṃ bhavet |
pañcamāsādbhavenmṛtyurnāsāgrasya vināśataḥ  || 1.107 ||
bādhirye śrotrayormṛtyuścaturmāsātyayādbhavet |
svasthendriyasya patane mṛtyurmāsatrayādbhavet || 1.108 ||
bhrūmadhye jyotiṣo'dṛṣṭau dvimāsādyamadarśanam  |
ekamāsātyayānmṛtyurvṛṣaṇe golakakṣayāt || 1.109 ||
parākṣṇoḥ pratibimbasyādṛṣṭau pakṣādvināśanam  |
jihvāyā aparāvṛttau daśāhānmṛtyusaṃgamaḥ  || 1.110 ||
nābherviparyayānmṛtyuḥ pañcāhādupaśliṣyati  |
pārśvadvayasyākṛṣṭau tu mṛtyuḥ syādvāsaratrayāt || 1.111 ||
samastagātrastabdhatve mṛtyurekāhiko bhavet |
lalāṭasthatrirekhāṇāṃ nāśānmṛtyurahastrayāt || 1.112 ||
svasthasya lālājihvā cenniḥsparśā dravavarjitā  |
yāvatpañcadinaṃ mṛtyuḥ pañcāśaddivasaistathā  || 1.113 ||
amlāditvaṃ ca rasato nīlāditvaṃ ca varṇataḥ  |
vikāraḥ śukramūtrāṇāṃ ṣaḍmāsānmṛtyudarśanāt || 1.114 ||
yugapatpañcadhāraṃ vā vāmāvartaṃ vigandhi ca  |
mūtraṃ yasya bhavenmṛtyustasya ṣaḍmāsamadhyataḥ  || 1.115 ||
kṛṣṇe vā yadi vā śukle pakṣe reto'tikṛṣṇakam  |
mṛtyuḥ ṣaḍmāsato no cettenaiva syātpratikriyā  || 1.116 ||
kṛṣṇabhāgaṃ parityajya gṛhītvā śuklabhāgakam  |
candre prabhañjane vāti praveśātsyātpratikriyā  || 1.117 ||
sthiratve'pi svadehasya tacchāyā cañcalā yadi  |
