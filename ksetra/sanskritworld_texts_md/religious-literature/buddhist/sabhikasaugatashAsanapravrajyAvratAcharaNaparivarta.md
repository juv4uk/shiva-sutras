sabhikasaugataśāsanapravrajyāvratacaraṇaparivarta


(sam 4 b4: additionalverses)
athāśoko mahārājaḥ śrotuṃ subhāṣitaṃ punaḥ |
upaguptaṃ yatiṃ natvā sāñjalirevamabravīt || *1* ||
bhadanta śrotumicchāmi punastasya jagadguroḥ |
munīndrasyānubhāvatvaṃ tatsamādeṣṭumarhati || *2* ||
iti saṃprārthitaṃ rājñā śrutvā so'rhan yatiḥ sudhīḥ |
taṃ narendraṃ samāmantrya saṃpaśyannevamādiśat || *3* ||

(sam 40 6; bka c99 4; bka t71 8)
atha śṛṇu samādhāya yathā me guruṇoditam |
tathāhaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi tava puṇyapravṛddhaye || 1 ||
tadyathābhūnmahāpuryāṃ mathurāyāṃ mahājanaḥ |
sādhubhogābhidhaḥ śreṣṭhī sarvadravyasamṛddhimān || 2 ||
tasya bhāryā subhadrāṃgī sundarī ratisaṃnibhā |
sādhvī śrīsadṛśākārā priyaṃvadābhidhā varā || 3 ||
tasyāstriyaṃtrikā jātā dārikā vyaṃgikāśrayā |
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā dārikāṃ mātā babhūva vyāhatāśayā || 4 ||
janako'pi samālokya dārikāṃ tāṃ triyantrikām |
lajjāvidaritasvāntastasthau niśvāsatatparaḥ || 5 ||
tataḥ sa janakaḥ śreṣṭhī nimittajñān vicakṣaṇān |
tīrthikān sahasāmantrya dārikāṃ tāmadarśayat || 6 ||
triyantritāṃ śiśuṃ dṛṣṭvā nimittajñaḥ sa tīrthikaḥ |
dārikeyamabhadreti śreṣṭhino'gre nyavedayat || 7 ||
tacchrutvā janakaḥ śreṣṭhī duḥkhaśaṃkākulāśayaḥ |
snehaśokāgnitaptātmā tasthau saṃmohitendriyaḥ || 8 ||
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā sā satī bhāryā śrutvā tattīrthikoditam |
snehaśokāgnisaṃtaptā babhūva vyāhatāśayā || 9 ||
tasmin kaṇe supuṇyātmā sarvavidyāvicakṣaṇā |
sā parivrājikā tatra śreṣṭhigṛha upācarat || 10 ||
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā samupāyātāṃ śreṣṭhī sa sahasotthitaḥ |
sādaraṃ samupāmantrya śuddhāsane nyaveśayat || 11 ||
sādhubhogastataḥ śreṣṭhī tāṃ parivrājikāṃ mudā |
abhyarcya bhojayitvā ca saṃprasādyaivamabravīt || 12 ||
bhadrike dārikā jātā mamātmajā triyantrikā |
imāṃ te dātumicchāmi samanvāhartumarhasi || 13 ||
iti nivedya sa śreṣṭhī samādāya nijāṃ sutām |
tāṃ tasyāḥ purataḥ sthāpya bahu dravyaṃ ca prabravīt || 14 ||
iyaṃ me dārikā bhadre bhavatyai saṃkalpitā mayā |
tadbhavatyā samādāya saṃpālyā svātmajā yathā || 15 ||
tvaṃ putraputrīhīnāsi nirbhartṛkā nirākulā |
tasmāttvayā pālanīyā svātmajeva śiśuriyam || 16 ||
iti nivedya sa śreṣṭhī sadhātrīṃ tāṃ sutāṃ mudā |
bahudravyānvitāṃ tasyai datvaivaṃ punarabravīt || 17 ||
yadeyaṃ saṃpravṛddhā syāttadā te dāsikā bhavet |
tasmādyāvadiyaṃ bālā tāvatpālyā tvayā sadā || 18 ||
tataḥ pravrājya sve dharme saṃcārayasva sanmate |
adhyāpaya ca sarvāṇi śāstrāṇyapi yathākramam || 19 ||
ityuktvā sa pitā putrīṃ vastrālaṃkāramaṇḍitām |
tāṃ tasyai brahmacāriṇyai saṃkalpya pradadau muda || 20 ||
tatpradattāṃ samādāya sadhātrīṃ sopacārikām |
datvā bhadrāśiṣaṃ tasmai śirī nāmā tato'carat* || 21 ||
tatra sā svāśrame gatvā sadhātrīṃ tāṃ pramoditā |
svātmajāmiva saṃpālya sahasā samapoṣayat || 22 ||
evaṃ saṃpālyamānā sā dine dine'bhivardhitā |
paripuṣṭendriyā kāntā sarvamanoharābhavat || 23 ||
tatastayānuśāsinyā pravrājitā yathāvidhim |
sā yatisaṃvaraṃ dhṛtvā kāṣāyacīvarācarat || 24 ||
tataḥ sā sumatī kanyā sarvā lipīraśikṣata |
tataḥ krameṇa sarvāṇi śāstrāṇi samaśikṣata || 25 ||
evamadhītya sā vijñā sarvavidyāntapāragā |
mahādhīriti nāmābhūtsubuddhitvāttriyantrikā || 26 ||
krameṇa kuṭilāṃgī sā śālaproccābhavatsudhīḥ |
nijadharmānubhāvena pūrvapāpakṣayaṃ gate || 27 ||
catuḥṣaṣṭi kalādhītā vedādhyayanacāturī |
sarvavādimanotrāsaṃ saṃharantī vyarājata || 28 ||
sarve'pi vādino vijñāstāṃ jetuṃ naiva śekire |
saiva pravādinaḥ sarvān vinirjityābhyarājata || 29 ||
tatkīrtisurabhistasyāḥ samantataḥ prasāritā |
tatsumatiṃ samakarṇya sarve'tivismayaṃ yayuḥ || 30 ||
tadā tatra dvijo vidvān sarvavedāntapāragaḥ |
vidyākarābhidho mānī dakṣiṇātaḥ samāgataḥ || 31 ||
sa pārśvaṃ tāmrapaṭṭena veṣṭayitvā samunmanāḥ |
ulkāmujvālitāṃ dhṛtvā pure tatra mudāviśat || 32 ||
yo mayā saha saṃvādaṃ kartumatra samicchati |
tasyāhamupagaccheyamiti mānī mudācarat || 33 ||
tamāyātaṃ samālokya māthurikā mahājanāḥ |
taṃ dvijaṃ samupāmantrya sarve pyevaṃ babhāṣire || 34 ||
sādho mātra samāgaccha dhṛtvolkāṃ jvalitāmimām |
yā parivrājikā sādhvī sā prājñā vidyate satī || 35 ||
iti tairgaditaṃ śrutvā vidyākaro'timānikaḥ |
māthurikānupāsṛtya sāhaṃkāraḥ samabravīt || 36 ||
kā parivrājikā prājñā vidyate'tra kuhāśritā |
tāmahaṃ draṣṭumicchāmi taddarśayitumarhathaḥ || 37 ||
iti taduktamākarṇya sarve'pi te mahājanāḥ |
brāhmaṇaṃ taṃ samālokya saṃpraṣṭumevamabravīt || 38 ||
sādho yadi tayā sārdhaṃ saṃvādaṃ kartumicchasi |
tāṃ kāntāṃ sumatiṃ vijñāṃ darśayiṣyāmahe vayam || 39 ||
iti sarvairapi proktaṃ śrutvā sa brāhmaṇo mudā |
saṃpaśyanstān janān sarvān puraḥ sthitvaivamabravīt || 40 ||
bho bhavantastayā sārdhaṃ saṃvādaṃ kartumutsahe |
tadenāṃ samupānīya darśayantu puro mama || 41 ||
iti tena samākhyātaṃ śrutvā tatheti te janāḥ |
sahasā tatrāśrame gatvā tasyā yaterupāśrayan || 42 ||
tān vīkṣya samupāsīnān sā parivrājikā mudā |
kimarthamāgatā yūyamiti saṃparyapṛcchata || 43 ||
tacchrutvā te janāḥ sarve natvā sāṃjalayo mudā |
tāṃ mahādhiyamāpaśyanmāthurā evamabruvan || 44 ||
eko'tra brāhmaṇo vidvān sarvaśāstravicakṣaṇaḥ |
vedāntapārago vijño dakṣiṇātaḥ samāgataḥ || 45 ||
tattena saha saṃvādaṃ kartuṃ yadi tvamicchasi |
sarvalokasabhāmadhye svaguṇaṃ saṃprakāśaya || 46 ||
śaknosi yadi vijñena saṃvādaṃ kuru mānini |
parājitā cettena tvaṃ vayamapyupahāsyatām || 47 ||
iti tairuktamākarṇya sā parivrājikā sudhīḥ |
vāḍhamicchāmi saṃvādaṃ kartuṃ tena dvijena hi || 48 ||
sarvalokasabhāmadhye darśayiṣyāmi madguṇam |
iti tayā samuditaṃ śrutvā sarve savismayāḥ || 49 ||
sarveme tatpravṛttāntaṃ rājñaḥ puro nyavedayan |
tatsamākarṇya rājā sa yathāmūbhyāṃ prabhāṣitam || 50 ||
kārayata tathā yūyamiti tānavadajjanān |
tadanujñāṃ samāsādya sarve te muditā janāḥ || 51 ||
āmantrya tajjanānetadvṛttaṃ proktvaivamūcire |
parivrājikayā sārdhamamuyāpyativijñayā || 52 ||
amuko brāhmaṇo vidvān saṃvādaṃ kartumicchati |
tacchrotuṃ ye'bhivāñcchanti jñātuṃ caitadviśeṣatām || 53 ||
sarve tatra samāyāntu pure'taḥ saptame dine |
iti rājñā samādiṣṭaṃ prabhāṣantaḥ samantataḥ || 54 ||
ghaṇṭāghoṣaṃ samuccārya codayata sato janān |
iti tatkathitaṃ śrutvā sarve'pi sajjanā mudā || 55 ||
tatra te muditāḥ sarve yathādiṣṭaṃ mahībhṛtā |
tathā ghaṇṭāṃ samunnādya sarvāṃllokān vyanodayan || *55* ||
tathāhīti pratijñāya ghaṇṭāghoṣamacārayan |
tatsamākarṇya sarve'pi sajjanā mathurāśritāḥ || 56 ||
tadvivādaṃ samicchantaḥ śrotuṃ draṣṭuṃ pracerire |
tadā vidyākaro mānī manasaivaṃ vyacintayat || 57 ||
sā parivrājikā vijñā kīdṛgvidyāvicakṣaṇā |
iti vicintya satrasthastāṃ parivrājikāṃ satīm || 58 ||
draṣṭuṃ tatrāśrame gatvā lokānevamapṛcchata |
yā parivrājikā prājñā sā kā kuhasamāśritā || 59 ||
tāṃ bhavantaḥ samāmantrya saṃdarśayantu me drutam |
iti tatkathitaṃ śrutvā tatrāntikasamāśritāḥ || 60 ||
tāṃ parivrājikāṃ sādhvīṃ samāmantryaivamabruvan |
bho bhadre brāhmaṇo vidvān bhavatīṃ draṣṭumicchati || 61 ||
tadbhavatī tamāmantrya saṃdarśayatu sādaram |
iti taduktamākarṇya sā parivrājikā mudā || 62 ||
taṃ vidvāṃsaṃ samāmantrya purataḥ samupāśrayat |
tāṃ parivrājikāṃ kāntāṃ manoramātisundarīm || 63 ||
samīkṣya suciraṃ tasthau rāgāgnijvalitāśayaḥ |
sā parivrājikā cāpi dṛṣṭvā taṃ sundaraṃ dvijam || 64 ||
saṃrāgavyākulasvāntā paśyantyeva samāśrayat |
tayordvayordaivayogādrāgavahniḥ samutthitaḥ || 65 ||
tenābhidravitasvāntau sneharaktau babhūvatuḥ |
tataḥ sa brāhmaṇaḥ kānto yuvā rāgākulendriyaḥ || 66 ||
tāṃ kāntāṃ yauvanīṃ rāmāṃ saṃpaśyan raha abravīt |
ayi bhadre subhadrāṃgi tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā mama mānasam || 67 ||
rāgāgnirdīpyate dagdhuṃ tacchamīkartumarhati |
yadi te'sti dayā bhadre brāhmaṇe'smin vicakṣaṇe || 68 ||
tatsudṛṣṭyā samālokya māmāśvāsyābhinandaya |
iti taduktamākarṇya sā parivrājikā api || 69 ||
kāmarāgāvilasvāntā tasthau saṃmohitā yathā |
evaṃ saṃmohitasvāntāṃ tāṃ vīkṣya brāhmaṇo'pi saḥ || 70 ||
āliṃgya sudṛśāpaśyan samāśvāsyaivamabravīt |
ayi bhadre yadi te'sti snehamaitramano mayi || 71 ||
śrutvā me'bhihitaṃ vākyaṃ cittaṃ samabhinandaya |
sumate yanmayā sārdhaṃ vivādaṃ kartumicchasi || 72 ||
ahamapi tvayā sārdhaṃ vivādaṃ kartumutsahe |
tadyadi te mayi snehastanme hitaṃ vacaḥ śṛṇu || 73 ||
tanmanasā samādhāya samabhikartumarhati |
iti taduktamākarṇya kāmāturā mahāsudhīḥ || 74 ||
vidyākaraṃ samālokya vihasaṃtyevamabravīt |
bho dvijendra mahāprājña samādiṣṭaṃ tvayā yathā || 75 ||
tathāhaṃ kartumicchāmi satyamiti pravakṣyatām |
iti tayā samākhyātaṃ śrutvāsau cāturo mudā || 76 ||
tāṃ smitānanaḥ prāha manojarasavihvalaḥ |
yadyevaṃ kriyate rāme tathāvayoḥ samāgamam || 77 ||
yāvajjīvaṃ sukhaṃ bhuktvā ramamāṇau caremahi |
tadabhisamayaṃ kṛtvā guptamāvāṃ vidhāya tat || 78 ||
evaṃ tatra sabhāmadhye saṃlapiṣyāvahe kila |
yo'tra vivādite jetā tasya sa hatamānikaḥ || 79 ||
śaraṇe prāpya śiṣyatvaṃ saṃcareta sadānugaḥ |
ityabhisamayaṃ kṛtvā sākṣīkṛtvā mahājanān || 80 ||
tatra sarvasabhāmadhye saṃvadiṣyāmahe mithaḥ |
mayā cennirjitā tvaṃ strī tadā loke na ninditā || 81 ||
kimāścaryaṃ jitā bālā puṃsā sateti vakṣyate |
yadi tatra sabhāmadhye nirjito'haṃ tvayā striyā || 82 ||
tadā loke bhaviṣyāmi nindyamānaḥ samantataḥ |
pumānapi dvijaḥ prājño bālayāpi striyā jitaḥ || 83 ||
iti hāsyapathaṃ yāsye vidyākaro'tiviśrutaḥ |
iti tvayā tadā tatra saṃvāde nau pravartite || 84 ||
ante pratyuttaraṃ naiva dātavyamapi kiṃcana |
tathā jitvā mayā tvaṃ strī śiṣyāpi me bhavestadā || 85 ||
evaṃ samāgamaṃ nau syādvāvajjīvaṃ sukhaṃ mahat |
iti taduktamākarṇya sā kāmarāgadāhitā || 86 ||
tatheti patibhāṣitvā brāhmaṇaṃ taṃ vyanodayat |
evaṃ sa brāhmaṇastatra kṛtvābhisamayaṃ tayā |
lokāsamīkṣyamāṇaśca svālaye prācaranmudā || 87 ||
tato'nyasmin dine prātaḥ vidvān kāmasamutsukaḥ |
mahājanasabhāmadhye gatvāmantryaivamabravīt || 88 ||
bhavanto'dya tayā sārdhaṃ parivrājikayā saha |
saṃvādaṃ kartumicchāmi tadānayantu tāṃ drutam || 89 ||
iti tenoditaṃ śrutvā sarve'pi te mahājanāḥ |
tatheti samupāmantrya tāṃ yatimevamabruvan || 90 ||
bhavati yadayaṃ vipraḥ sarvaśāstravicakṣaṇaḥ |
bhavatyā saha saṃvādaṃ kartumadya samicchati || 91 ||
tadbhavantī sahānena vivadituṃ yadīcchati |
tatra raṃge sabhāmadhye samāgatyābhitiṣṭhatu || 92 ||
iti taduktamākarṇya sā parivrājikā mudā |
taṃ bhartāramabhīcchantī raṃgabhūmyāṃ samāśrayat || 93 ||
vidyākaro'pi āgatya tatra raṃge sabhāśraye |
mahādhīmukhainālokya pramanāḥ samupāśrayat || 94 ||
tatra raṃge sabhāmadhye tāvubhau samupāśritau |
dṛṣṭvā śrutvāpi te sarve mahājanā upāśrayan || 95 ||
taddraṣṭuṃ kautukākrāntaṃ viprāśca brahmacāriṇaḥ |
nṛpatipramukhā lokāḥ sarve tatra samāgatāḥ || 96 ||
tathānye'pi janāḥ santaḥ śilpivaṇikprajādayaḥ |
sarve'tra samupāyātāstaddraṣṭuṃ samupāśrayan || *96* ||
tān draṣṭuṃ samupāsīnān sarvāṃllokān samīkṣya saḥ |
brāhmaṇa utthitaḥ paśyan sāñjalirevamabravīt || 97 ||
bhavanto'tra yadasmābhiḥ svamatimadamānikam |
kṛtaṃ vaṭukacāpalyaṃ kartumarhantu tatkṣamām || 98 ||
yadi mayā vivāde'tra nārīyaṃ nirjitā tadā |
kimāścaryaṃ vadellokaḥ puṃsā nārī jiteti ca || 99 ||
yadyetayā striyāpyatra vivāde'haṃ vinirjitaḥ |
pumānapi jito nāryā hīti syāṃ sarvaninditaḥ || 100 ||
tadatra samayaṃ kṛtvā vivādaṃ kartumutsahe |
tadbhavanto'pi madhyasthā draṣṭumarhantu sādhavaḥ || 101 ||
yadyeṣātra vivāde māṃ parājetuṃ hi śaknuyāt |
asyāḥ śiṣyatvamāsādya bhaveyāhaṃ sadānugaḥ || 102 ||
yadyatrāhamimāṃ jitvā jayaśriyaṃ samāpnuyām |
mama śiṣyatvamāsādya bhavedeṣā sadānugā || 103 ||
iti saṃprārthya vipro'sau tāṃ parivrājikāṃ satīm |
paśyan vyākaraṇapṛśnaṃ pṛṣṭvā samabhyacodayat || 104 ||
tatpratyuttaramuddiśya sā parivrājikā sudhīḥ |
brāhmaṇaṃ taṃ vinirjitya vyasmāyayan sabhājanān || 105 ||
tacchrutvā te janāḥ sarve vismayāpannamānasāḥ |
prājñeyaṃ yatirityuktvā svasvālayamupācaram || 106 ||
tatparedyustathā tatra sā parivrājikāgatā |
te'pi santo janāḥ sarve samāgatya samāśrayan || 107 ||
tataḥ sa brāhmaṇastatra sabhāmadhye samāśritaḥ |
tāṃ parivrājikāṃ paśyañcchāstrārthaṃ paryapṛcchata || 108 ||
tacchāstrārthaṃ samākhyāya yuvatirapi sā sudhīḥ |
vidvāṃsamapi taṃ jitvā sarvān sato'nvamodayat || 109 ||
evaṃ ca tṛtīye'pyahni sā yoṣidapi sanmatiḥ |
sarvakalābhisaṃvāde vispaṣṭārthamudāharat || 110 ||
evaṃ turyadine cāpi pañcame divase tathā |
ṣaṣṭhe'pi vāsare saivaṃ yathārthaṃ samupādiśat || 111 ||
evaṃ sa brāhmaṇastatra saptame'hni samāśrite |
kriyābandhaṃ taya kṛtvā raṃgabhūmimupāsarat || 112 ||
trapākulacitta iva janānāha sucāturaḥ |
adyāpyahaṃ yadi jitastasyāḥ syāmanugo nanu || 113 ||
jeṣyati pratyahaṃ saivamekasminnapyahaṃ kimu |
vāṇīcchalājjitā sā tu yato yoṣitsarasvatī || 114 ||
iti proktvā dvijaḥ kāmī kāmāturīṃ mahādhiyam |
pragalbhavacasā nāriṃ vedāntaṃ samapṛcchata || 115 ||
tatra vijñāpi sā sādhvī smṛtvābhisamayaṃ sudhīḥ |
api pratyuttaraṃ kiñcidasamarthaiva no dadau || 116 ||
tataḥ sa brāhmaṇaḥ paśyanmahājanān sabhāśritān |
sānandasuprasannāsyaḥ sāñjalirevamabravīt || 117 ||
bhavanto dṛśyatāmatra labhate ko jayaśriyam |
iti samīkṣya prākhyātumarhanti mānyathā khalu || 118 ||
iti tatprārthitaṃ śrutvā sarve'pi te sabhājanāḥ |
vidyānidhiṃ mahāprājñaṃ saṃpraśaṃsyaivamabruvan || 119 ||
kovidātra bhavāñjetā yatireṣā parājitā |
tadiyaṃ bhavataḥ śiṣyā bhaveta sarvadānugā || 120 ||
ekasminneva divase mahādhīstena nirjitā |
kathamatra saṃdeha iti sarve savismayāḥ || 121 ||
iti sarvaiḥ samākhyātaṃ śrutvā sa brāhmaṇo mudā |
tāṃ parivrājikāṃ paśyan vihasannevamabravīt || 122 ||
ayi bhadre samāyāhi yathābhisamayaṃ kṛtam |
tathānugatvamāsādya cara dharmaṃ mamānugā || 123 ||
iti taduktamākarṇya sā parivrājikā smitā |
lajjābhihatacitteva tasthāvabhinatānanā || 124 ||
tataḥ sa mudito vipro daṇḍaṃ cchatramupānaham |
datvā tāṃ sahasāmantrya samāśvāsyaivamabravīt || 125 ||
ayi bhadre tathā siddhaṃ yathā mayā samīhitam |
tattvaṃ mudā mayā sārdhaṃ sukhaṃ cara samāvraja || 126 ||
iti tatkathitaṃ śrutvā sā parivrājikā tataḥ |
daṇḍamupānahau cchatraṃ gṛhītvā prasthitā śanaiḥ || 127 ||
tataḥ sa brāhmaṇo dhṛtvā tāṃ parivrājikāṃ mudā |
vijayaśrīmahotsāhairvāsālayamupācarat || 128 ||
tatra vāse samāśritya vidvān so'tyabhinanditaḥ |
