atha khalvāyuṣmānānanda utthāyāsanādekāṃsamuttarāsaṃgaṃ kṛtvā dakṣiṇajānumaṇḍalaṃ pṛthivyāṃ pratiṣṭhāpya yena bhagavāṃstenāñjaliṃ praṇamya kṛtakarapuṭo bhūtvā bhagavataḥ mukhamavalokya yadīdānīṃ tasyāṃ velāyāmimāṃ gāthā abhāṣat |
