ahorātravratakathā


oṃ namaḥ śrīdharmadhātave |

ākāśanirmalo bhūto niṣprapañcaguṇāśrayaḥ |
pañcaskandhātmakaḥ śāntastasmai stūpātmane namaḥ || 1 ||

evaṃ mayā śrutamekasmin samaye buddho bhagavāñchrāvastyāṃ viharati sma jetavane'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme mahatā bhikṣusaṃghena sārdhamanekairdevanāgayakṣagandharvāsuragaruḍakinnaramahoragaiḥ sthavirabhikṣuvītarāgasaṃghairbodhisattvaśatasahasraiḥ saṃbahulaiśca śrāvakairdaśabhirlokapālaiścaturmahārājakāyikairanekaiścaturvarṇakairmanuṣyairanekaśatasahasraiḥ parivāraiḥ parivṛtaiḥ | tasmin sabhāmaṇḍale sarve dharmaśravaṇikā lokā bhagavantamabhivīkṣya caityavratakathāmanudhyāyantaḥ samāsthitāḥ | atha khalu subhūtiḥ sthaviraḥ svāsanātsamutthāyaikāṃsamuttarāsaṅgaṃ kṛtvā triḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya savyajānubimbaṃ bhuvi sthāpya bhagavantaṃ praṇamyaitadavocat |

ahorātravrataṃ nātha kasmin kāle pravartate |
tadvidhiṃ ca kathaṃ brūhi tatsarvaṃ jagatāṃ prabho || 2 ||

bhagavānāha |

sādhu sādhu subhūte tvaṃ śṛṇu sattvānukampaka |
ahorātravrataṃ nityaṃ tadvidhiṃ kathitaṃ mayā || 3 ||
āsītpūrvavideheṣu nāmnā gandhavatī purī |
tasyāṃ rājā mahātejā indrapṛṣṭo mahāprabhuḥ || 4 ||
so'ntaḥpure sabhāṃ kṛtvā vasubandhuṃ jagadgurum |
praṇamya sahasotthāya papraccha vidhivanmudā || 5 ||
śrotumicchāmi tatsarvaṃ yadvratānāṃ samuttamam |
yasya puṇyaprabhāvena sattvā yānti sunirvṛtim || 6 ||
tatsarvaṃ gadatāṃ dhīra sarvasattvānukampaka |
bhavatā cānupālyo'yaṃ lokaśca duṣkṛtākulaḥ || 7 ||

vasubandhuruvāca |

śṛṇu rājanmayā khyātaṃ vratānāṃ vratamuttamam |
ahorātravrataṃ pūrvaṃ vipaśyinā prabhāṣitam || 8 ||
aśvinīpūrṇamāsyāṃ tu vrataṃ kuryādmahodayam |
caityaṃ saṃpūjayecchuddhaḥ ṣoḍaśairvidhibhistathā || 9 ||
bhojanānte trayodaśyāṃ kuryāddantādiśodhanam |
caturdaśyāṃ viśeṣeṇa kṣurakarmāṇi kārayet || 10 ||
snānaṃ kṛtvā ca tīrtheṣu śucivastrāvṛtaḥ sudhīḥ |
madhyāhne pūjayeccaityaṃ pradīpaṃ ca pradāpayet || 11 ||
sūrye ṣaṣṭhagate bhāge prāśayecca kṣīrodanam |
punarśaucādikaṃ kṛtvā dharmaṃ ca śruṇuyānmudā || 12 ||
pratyūṣe pūrṇamāsyāṃ tu snātvā vrataṃ samārabhet |
ādau bhūmitalaṃ śodhya dharmaśālāṃ prakārayet || 13 ||
gandhodakaiḥ suśītaiśca caityapuṃgavaṃ snāpayet |
gokṣīraiḥ pañcaratnaiśca śilābhasmābhilepayet || 14 ||
catvāraśca dhvajāḥ sthāpyā vitānaṃ saha cāmaraiḥ |
digmālā vividhaiḥ puṣpairlambayecca jinālaye || 15 ||
tadanu vratamārabhya guruṃ vajradharaṃ namet |
triratnaśaraṇaṃ kṛtvā dhyāpayeddharmadhātukam || 16 ||
dharmadhātumayaṃ caityaṃ jinānāṃ gṛhamuttamam |
anādibuddha-m-ākhyātaṃ sarvatathāgatālayam |
sarvadevālayaṃ caityamārādhya vratamācaret || 17 ||
sahasradalapadmasthaṃ candrabimbaprabhāsvaram |
raktaṃ bhoḥkārasaṃbhūtaṃ caturmukhaṃ virājitam || 18 ||
raktagauramukhaṃ mūlaṃ dakṣiṇe kuṅkumāruṇam |
paścime padmaraktaṃ ca [!] uttare pītaraktakam || 19 ||
aṣṭabhujaṃ virājantaṃ vāgīśvaraṃ jagadgurum |
dhanurbāṇadharaṃ dvābhyāṃ pāśāṅkuśadharaṃ tathā |
prajñāpustaṃ tathā khaḍgaṃ vajraghaṇṭāṃ tathāparaiḥ || 20 ||
sarvarasasamāyuktaṃ lalitāsanasaṃsthitam |
divyābharaṇabhūṣāṅgaṃ pañcabuddhamukūṭinam || 21 ||
vāgīśvaraṃ mahāśuklaṃ sarvadharmapratiṣṭhitam |
evaṃ svahṛdi saṃbhāvya puraḥ sthāpya prapūjayet || 22 ||
pādyācamanamarghaṃ ca dattvā bhaktisamanvitaḥ |
jalajaiḥ sthalajaiḥ puṣpaiḥ sugandhaiḥ pañcagandhakaiḥ || 23 ||
sāṃyojikaiḥ sugandhaiśca sudhūpairdhūpanaistathā |
ghṛtaprayuktadīpaiśca naivedyairdivyabhojanaiḥ || 24 ||
chattradhvajapatākābhirnṛtyagītasuvādanaiḥ |
phalamūlādibhiḥ pattraiḥ pūgatāmbūlakaistathā || 25 ||
nānauṣadhisamāyuktairdivyairmānuṣyakaistathā |
kundapuṣpākṣatairdūrvairlājayā pañcaratnakaiḥ || 26 ||
kṣīraiḥ śaṃkhasupuṣpārghaṃ pradadyāccaityapuṃgave |
dhāraṇīṃ caiva stotraṃ ca paṭhedgītaṃ ca gāyayet || 27 ||
pradakṣiṇāsahasraṃ ca yāvatkuryātpramoditaḥ |
rātriṃ divā susaṃpūrṇaṃ pracarecca pradakṣiṇām || 28 ||
yatra devālaye tīrthe sarvān devān prapūjayet |
hīnajātiprasaṅgaṃ ca sparśanaṃ naiva kārayet || 29 |
sparśite saṃcaretsnātvā pañcagavyena śodhayet |
asparśe'pi caretsnānaṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ saṃcaredvratī || 30 ||

ahorātraṃ susaṃpūrṇaṃ na nidrāṃ kārayedvratī |
caityadhyānasamāyuktaḥ japan stotraṃ paṭhaṃścaret || 31 ||
nānāgītamahotsāhairnṛtyairvādyaiḥ sughoṣakaiḥ |
hāhākāraiḥ samullāsaiḥ kārayetsaṃpradakṣiṇām || 32 ||
viśrāmaṃ vā na kuryācca khedaṃ vā vratamānasaḥ |
ahorātre susaṃpūrṇe prātaḥ snātvā vrataṃ caret || 33 ||
caityapūjāṃ yathā kṛtvā gurupūjāṃ samācaret |
annaṃ vastraṃ suvarṇaṃ ca vrīhiratnāni bhūṣitān || 34 ||
ācāryāya pradātavyā yathāśakti subhaktitaḥ |
yajñaṃ kuryāttato vajrī vrataṃ pūrṇaṃ prapūrayet || 35 ||
abhiṣekaṃ tato dattvā pañcasūtraṃ ghaṭaṃ tathā |
pratyekaṃ vratināṃ dattvā āśīrvādyaṃ pradāpayet || 36 ||
kaumārīpūjanaṃ kṛtvā śeṣānnaṃ ca prabhuñjayet |
dadhiśarkarasaṃyuktaṃ pañcāmṛtaṃ subhojanam || 37 ||
gaṇacakraṃ tataḥ kuryādvidhijñastadvidhiṃ yathā |
ityevaṃ vratarājaṃ tadyaḥ kuryācchuddhamānasaḥ || 38 ||
vipulaṃ bhogamābhujya sarvasukhasamanvitaḥ |
paratra sukhamāpnoti dharmaloke mahīpate || 39 ||

indrapṛṣṭa uvāca |

bhagavan sarvavinnātha punarvaktuṃ tvamarhasi |
saṃsāre bahudhā jātisteṣāṃ pracāraṇaṃ katham || 40 ||
vratapūjāphalaṃ sarvaṃ kālaṃ ca samayaṃ dinam |
kutra sthāne vrataṃ kāryaṃ tatsarvaṃ vada sāṃpratam || 41 ||

vasubandhuruvāca |

śṛṇu rājanmahābhāga saṃkṣepādgaditaṃ mayā |

avandhyākṣarayuktena adbhirvratīn susiñcayet |
havargāntākṣarairmantrairvratimūrdhni pratiṣṭhitam || 48 ||

aṅganyāsaṃ karanyāsaṃ kṛtvā guruṃ ca vandayet || 49 ||

śaṅkhasthaṃ pratigṛhṇātu arghaṃ dattaṃ mayā mudā || 53 ||

dhūpo'yaṃ ghrāṇasaṃtarpo divyagandhasuyojitaḥ |
pradattastava toṣārthaṃ gṛhyatāṃ bhaktavatsala || 57 ||

ghṛtatailasamujjvālo mayā dattaḥ pragṛhyatām || 58 ||
annaṃ caturvidhaṃ nātha rasānāṃ ṣaḍvidhaṃ tathā |

lājākṣataphalārcaiva dūrvākundāni bhaktitaḥ |
prakṣiptāni mayā tubhyaṃ gṛhāṇa hitamānasaḥ || 62 ||
sauvarṇādikṛtaṃ chattraṃ dhvajāni vividhāni ca |
patākādīni sarvāṇi pragṛhya jina modatām || 63 ||
dhīmān pujāṃ tathā kṛtva stotramevaṃ ca kārayet |
jinendratoṣaṇārthāya kārayecca pradakṣiṇām || 64 ||
dharmadhātumayaṃ caityaṃ sarvalokasvarūpiṇam |
ālayaṃ sarvabuddhānāṃ jinadhātuṃ namāmyaham || 65 ||
namaste jinadhātūnāṃ pratyakṣaṃ samalīlayā |
triyānasamayānandaṃ satataṃ tvāṃ samāśraye || 66 ||
jinajaṃ sakalasthaṃ ca puṇyakṣetraṃ namāmyaham |
pavitrajanasukhārthaṃ sevitārthapradāyakam || 67 ||
praṇamāmi muniśreṣṭhaṃ maṅgalārthapradāyakam |
janānāṃ bhayahantāraṃ lokanāthaṃ maheśvaram || 68 ||
devatāḥ sarvalokasthāḥ saṃbhūtā jinamaṇḍale |

dhvajapātākavastreṣu śudrāṇāṃ ca pracakṣyate || 110 ||
svarṇakārā lohakārāḥ kāṃsyakṛtkṛṣikādayaḥ |
eteṣāṃ ceha hastena caityapuṃgavapūjanam || 111 ||
taṇḍulī vyañjanī cāpi krayavikrayakāriṇaḥ |
etaiḥ svavṛttivastūni ḍhaukanīyāni bhaktitaḥ || 112 ||
śaulinī dhvananavartī māṃsavartī tathā surī |
etairdīpaḥ pradātavyaḥ kuladharmapramāṇataḥ || 113 ||
carmakāraśca mātaṅgo niyogī rajako dhvajī |
etaiśca dūrataḥ sthitvā vandanīyo jinālayaḥ || 114 ||
ṣaṭtriṃśajjātibhirevaṃ svakulavṛtticāraṇaiḥ |
anyaiśca vividhairnityaṃ pūjyo mānyo jinālayaḥ || 115 ||
yeṣāṃ vratavikāro na pūjane jinapuṃgave |
etaiḥ pradakṣiṇāṃ kṛtvā vandanīyaḥ svabhaktitaḥ || 116 ||
evaṃ bhūyaścareddharmī vratarājamaharniśam |
tasya puṇyaṃ yathā proktaṃ kuṣṭhavyādhikṣayaṃ vrajet || 117 ||
pañcānantaryapāpāni yāsyantyapi na saṃśayaḥ |
kiṃ punaścānyapāpāni janmāntarakṛtānyapi || 118 ||
na hī sa vidyate rājan sarvatra bhuvaneṣvapi |
ahorātravrataṃ puṇyaṃ yo'nyathātvaṃ kariṣyati || 119 ||
śravādapi haretpāpaṃ darśanātpuṇyamāpnuyāt |
anumodāttathā caivaṃ vratināṃ tu kimucyate || 120 ||
vratināṃ saptabhāgāni kṛtvā puṇyaṃ viśāradaḥ |
ekabhāgaṃ labhedrājā caikaṃ tu sārathījanaḥ |
sarvasattvasya caikaṃ hi taccheṣaṃ labhate vratī || 121 ||
iti śrutvā gurorvākyamindrapṛṣṭo mahāprabhuḥ ||
tataḥ sattvahitārthāya vrataṃ kartuṃ samudyataḥ || 122 ||
vārāṇasīpuraṃ gatvā vasubandhuvaco yathā |
vihāraṃ ca pratiṣṭhāpya caityarājaṃ jinālayam |
saṃghabhojyaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā yayau svargaṃ janaiḥ sudhīḥ || 123 ||

tato bhikṣavo bhagavantametadūcuḥ |

bhagavanmuniśārdūla punarvaktuṃ tvamarhasi |
kena kena purā nātha vratarājamidaṃ kṛtam || 124 ||

bhagavānāha |

purā svargeṣu śakreṇa ahorātravrataṃ kṛtam |
sa ṣaṭpāramitāṃ dhārya caityarājamapūjayat |
pradakṣiṇāṃ sadā kṛtvā yathāvidhi mahotsavaiḥ || 125 ||
pātāle ca purā vīro daityendro balavānabhūt |
nirghoṣadamano nāma devāsurapramardakaḥ || 126 ||
indraṃ svarge nirākṛtya pṛthivīmavadhāritam |
tenāsurasahāyena trailokyaṃ svavaśe kṛtam || 127 ||
pṛthivīmaṇḍalaṃ gatvā gato'sau maghavatpurim |
indrapṛṣṭamahāsthāne devatāpratimāṃ sthitām |
pañcapradakṣiṇāṃ kṛtvā prajñāpārasthito'bhavat || 128 ||
pātāleṣu punargatvā so'carattadvratottamam |
nāginyo nāgarājāśca pātāleṣvacaraṃstathā || 129 ||
nāgaiḥ phaṇavibhūṣābhirdevanāgendrakalpitam |
pūjitaṃ satataṃ bhaktyā caityarājaṃ vidhānataḥ || 130 ||
ṛṣayaśca purā sapta jalāśrayapratiṣṭhitam |
pupūjuḥ jinadhātusthaṃ vidhibhiśca viśāradāḥ || 131 ||
nāginyo viṣadarpāndhāḥ pūjyamānāñjinālayam |
tāpitān kārayāmāsurviṣāṅgāraiḥ pradāhanaiḥ || 132 ||
tatastaiḥ śāpitā evaṃ nāginyaḥ śambukāḥ khalu |
bhavadhvaṃ saptajanmāni punaścaityasya sevakāḥ || 133 ||
tataścānte punaḥ prāpya mānuṣyaṃ puṇyabhāvataḥ |
eṣā sā nāyakā yā tu dharmadakṣaṃ patiṃ labhet || 134 ||
tatasta vañcitāḥ sarvāḥ śambukā dharmamānasāḥ |
dhānyāṃ pradakṣiṇāṃ cakrurbhāvayantyo jinālayam || 135 ||
tataḥ puṇyānubhāvena devyaḥ saptakumārikāḥ |
bhūtā rājasutā dhanyāḥ prālabhan paramaṃ padam || 136 ||
tāsāmekā vratī nāmnā dharmadakṣapativratā |
tasyāḥ puṇyaprabhāvena dharmadakṣo'labhadvratam || 137 ||
dharmadakṣo mahārājā yathāvidhi vrataṃ caran |
bhāryayā saha dharmātmā sukhāvatyāṃ mahīpatiḥ || 138 ||
ityevaṃ bhikṣavo jñātvā vratānāṃ vratamuttamam |
caityasevā sadā kāryā vratapuṇyapracāraṇaiḥ || 139 ||
caityavratasamaṃ puṇyaṃ nāsti satyaṃ tribhūvane |
tasmāccaityaḥ sadā vandyaḥ pūjanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ || 140 ||
