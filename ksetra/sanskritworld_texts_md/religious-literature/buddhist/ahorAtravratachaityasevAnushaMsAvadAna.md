ahorātravratacaityasevānuśaṃsāvadāna



oṃ namo ratnatrayāya |

śrīmadratnatrayaṃ natvā tridhātubhuvanādhipam |
ahorātravratakathāṃ vakṣye gurūpadeśataḥ || 1 ||
kukkuṭārāmamāsīnamupaguptaṃ yatīśvaram |
kṛtāñjalipuṭo'śokaḥ prārthayadevamādarāt || 2 ||

bhadanta śrotumicchāmi caityavratavidhiṃ punaḥ |
ahorātravratasyāpi vidhiṃ puṇyāni sadguro || 3 ||
tadbhavān sadguruḥ śāstā lokapuṇyapravṛddhaye |
ahorātravratasyātra vidhimādeṣṭumarhati || 4 ||
iti tena narendreṇa prārthitaṃ sa sudhīryatiḥ |
śrutvā taṃ nṛpamālokya punarevamupādiśat || 5 ||
śṛṇu sādhu mahārāja yathā me guruṇoditam |
tathāhaṃ te pravakṣyāmi śrutvā caivaṃ samācara || 6 ||
purā sa bhagavāñchāstā śākyasiṃho jagadguruḥ |
śrāvakairbhikṣubhiḥ sārdhaṃ cailakaiścāpyupāsakaiḥ || 7 ||
bhikṣuṇībhiḥ suśīlābhirbodhisattvagaṇairapi |
vihāre jetakāraṇye vyaharatsaugatāśrame || 8 ||
tadā sa bhagavān buddho lokānāṃ puṇyavṛddhaye |
sabhāmadhye samāsīno dharmamādeṣṭumārabhat || 9 ||
tadā sarve'marendrādilokapālā maharddhikāḥ |
tatsaddharmāmṛtaṃ pātuṃ śraddhayā samupāgatāḥ || 10 ||
brahmādibrāhmaṇāścāpi maharṣayastapasvinaḥ |
catvāraśca mahārājāḥ sasainyaparicārakāḥ || 11 ||
daityendrā nāgarājāśca yakṣagandharvakinnarāḥ |
siddhā vidyādharāḥ sādhyā grahāśca vasavastathā || 12 ||
rākṣasāśca mahāvirā garuḍāśca mahoragāḥ |
yogino yatayaścāpi rājānaḥ kṣātriyā api || 13 ||
amātyā mantriṇaścāpi vaiśyā rājakumārakāḥ |
śreṣṭhino dhaninaḥ śūdrāḥ sārthavāhā māhājanāḥ || 14 ||
vaṇijaḥ śilpinaścāpi paurā janapadāstathā |
grāmyāḥ kārvaṭikāścāpi tathānyadeśavāsinaḥ || 15 ||
sarve te samupāgatya buddhaṃ taṃ śṛīghanaṃ mudā |
satkṛtya śraddhayābhyarcya kṛtvā pradakṣiṇānyapi || 16 ||
kṛtāñjalipuṭā natvā niṛīkṣya saṃpramoditāḥ |
tatsaddharmāmṛtaṃ pātumupatasthuḥ samāhitāḥ || 17 ||
tadāsanātsamutthāya subhūtiḥ sthaviro yatiḥ |
udvahannuttarāsaṅgaṃ jānubhyāṃ bhuvi saṃsthitaḥ || 18 ||
kṛtāñjalipuṭo natvā bhagavantaṃ jagadgurum |
śrīghanaṃ taṃ samālokya prārthayadevamādarāt || 19 ||
bhagavannātha sarvajña vijānīyādbhavān guruḥ |
sarvalokā ime śāsturdharmaṃ śrotuṃ samāsthitāḥ || 20 ||
tadatra bhuvane khyātamahorātravrataṃ kadā |
pravartate tadākhyāhi tadvidhiṃ ca kathaṃ prabho || 21 ||
iti tenārhatā pṛṣṭe bhagavān sa munīśvaraḥ |
tāṃ sabhāṃ taṃ subhūtiṃ ca samālokyaivamabravīt || 22 ||
sādhu śṛṇu subhūte tvaṃ sarvasattvahitārthabhṛt |
ahorātravrataṃ puṇyaṃ vidhiṃ ca kathyate mayā || 23 ||
purā pūrvavidehe'bhūtkhyātā gandhavatī purī |
tasyāṃ rājā mahāvijña indrapṣṭābhidhaḥ prabhuḥ || 24 ||
so'ntaḥpure samānīya vasubandhuṃ jinātmajam |
sabhāyāṃ svāsane sthāpya satkṛtya śraddhayārcayat || 25 ||
tataḥ sa bhūpatī rājā utthāya samupāśritaḥ |
kṛtāñjaliḥ praṇatvainaṃ prārthayadevamādarāt || 26 ||
bhadanta yadime lokā asaddharmābhimāninaḥ |
tadeṣāṃ mānaghātāya saddharmaṃ samupādiśa || 27 ||
yacchrutvaiva tvime sarve saddharmapratibodhitāḥ |
sarvapāpavinirmuktā bhaveyurbodhilābhinaḥ || 28 ||
iti rājñārthitaṃ śrutvā vasubandhuḥ sa ātmavit |
indrapṛṣṭaṃ mahīndraṃ taṃ samālokyaivamabravīt || 29 ||
śṛṇu sādhu mahārājā tava puṇyapravṛddhaye |
sarvasattvahitārthaṃ tadahorātramahāvratam || 30 ||
yathā mayā śrutaṃ pūrvaṃ vipaśyinā prabhāṣitam |
mahatpuṇyānuśaṃsā ca tadvidhiśca pracakṣyate || 31 ||
saṃsāre'tra mahatpuṇyaṃ prāptumicchanti ye narāḥ |
caritavyamahorātravrataṃ taiḥ śraddhayā mudā || 32 ||
bhaktyā caranti ye martyā ahorātravrataṃ mudā |
te sarve vimalātmānaḥ pariśuddhatrimaṇḍalāḥ || 33 ||
bodhicittaṃ samāsādya caritvā bodhicārikām |
sarvasattvahitaṃ kṛtvā pūrya pāramitāḥ kramāt || 34 ||
sarvakleśagaṇāñjitvā sarvamāragaṇānapi |
samyagbodhiṃ samāsādya bhaveyuḥ sugatā dhruvam || 35 ||
atītaiśca jinaiḥ sarvaiḥ kṛtvaitadvratamuttamam |
sarvamāragaṇāñjitvā saṃbodhiḥ sādhitā khalu || 36 ||
vartamānaiśca saṃbuddhaiḥ sarvairetadvrataṃ kṛtam |
etatpuṇyānubhāvairhi saṃbodhiḥ sādhitā drutam || 37 ||
tathā cānāgataiḥ sarvaiḥ kṛtvā caitadvrataṃ varam |
etatpuṇyavipākaistu prāpsyate bodhiruttamā || 38 ||
tathānye bodhisattvāśca saṃbodhivratacārakāḥ |
etadvratavaraṃ dhṛtvā caranti bodhisaṃvaram || 39 ||
tathā sarve'marendrāśca kṛtvā caitadvrataṃ mudā |
devalokādhināthāste bhavanti bodhibhāginaḥ || 40 ||
evaṃ sarve mahāsattvā ṛṣayo munayo'pi ca |
etadvratavaraṃ kṛtvā bhavanti brahmacāriṇaḥ || 41 ||
evaṃ ye ye narāścāpi carantyetadvrataṃ mudā |
te te sarve'pi tatpuṇyairbhavanti bodhibhāginaḥ || 42 ||
ye cāpyetadvrataṃ dṛṣṭvā śrutvāpi cānumoditāḥ |
prasīdanti tathā te'pi tatpuṇyaṃ prāpnuvanti hi || 43 ||
etatpuṇyānubhāvena naiva gacchanti durgatim |
sarvadā saśubhaṃ saukhyaṃ bhuktvā yānti jinālayam || 44 ||
tasmātsarvaprayatnena saddharmasukhavāñchibhiḥ |
ahorātravrataṃ bhaktyā caritavyaṃ naraiḥ sadā || 45 |
ahorātravratotpannapuṇyasaṃkhyā na vidyate |
aprameyamasaṃkhyeyamiti buddhaiḥ prakathyate || 46 ||
sarvairapi munīndraistatsaṃkhyāṃ kartuṃ na śakyate |
mayātra śakyamekena tatpuṇyaṃ gaṇituṃ katham || 47 ||
yasya puṇyānubhāvena mahānti pātakānyapi |
nirdahya pariśuddhātmā tatkṣaṇādyāti sadgatim || 48 ||
yathābhilaṣitaṃ kāryaṃ sādhayitvā hyavighnataḥ |
sarvadā satsukhaṃ bhuktvā saṃyāntyante jinālayam || 49 ||
iti matvā mahatpuṇyamahorātravrataṃ varam |
dharmārthakāmamokṣāptyai caritavyaṃ sadā naraiḥ || 50 ||
tathā cette mahatpuṇyamihāmutra śubhaṃ sadā |
kramādbodhivrataṃ prāpya bodhisattvo bhaveddhruvam || 51 ||
kramātkleśān vinirjitya saṃsārasukhanandinaḥ |
jitvā māragaṇān sarvān saṃbuddhapadamāpnuyāḥ || 52 ||
iti tena samākhyātaṃ dhīmatā vasubandhunā |
śrutvaivaṃ sumahatpuṇyamindrapṛṣṭo mumoda saḥ || 53 ||
tataḥ sa indrapṛṣṭākhyo mahārājo'numoditaḥ |
ahorātravrataṃ kartuṃ cārayituṃ samaicchata || 54 ||
tataḥ sa nṛpa utthāya sāñjaliḥ samupācaran |
vasubandhuṃ tamānatvā prārthayadevamādarāt || 55 ||
bhadanta bhavatādiṣṭamahorātravratodbhavam |
mahatpuṇyaṃ niśamyāhaṃ kartumicchāmi sāṃpratam || 56 ||
tasmāttadvidhimādeṣṭumarhasi me jagaddhite |
sarvathāhaṃ cariṣyāmi cariṣyanti prajā api || 57 ||
tadatra sarvasattvānāṃ hitāya puṇyasiddhaye |
ahorātravratasyaivaṃ vidhimādeṣṭumarhasi || 58 ||
iti tena narendreṇa prārthitaṃ sa mahāmatiḥ |
vasubandhurniśamyaivamindrapṛṣṭaṃ tamabravīt || 59 ||
śṛṇu rājan yathākhyātaṃ munīndreṇa vipaśyinā |
tathāhaṃ te pravakṣyāmi vratarājavidhiṃ kramāt || 60 ||
svayaṃbhūcaityabhūkṣetre vihāre sugatāśrame |
puṇyakṣetre nadītīre saṃgame saritāṃ tathā || 61 ||
tīrthe samudratīre ca hradatīre sarastaṭe |
udyāne parvate'raṇye vivikte vanāśrame || 62 ||
caityālaye śubhe sthāne prāsāde mandire gṛhe |
evamanyatra bhūdeśe śuddhasthāne manorame || 63 ||
eteṣu pariśuddheṣu yatrepsite viśodhite |
dharmaśālāṃ vidhāyādau mṛdgomayairvilepayet || 64 ||
caturdhvajān pratiṣṭhāpya vitanuyādvitānakam |
puṣpamālādibhiścitrairduṣyaiḥ paṭṭaiśca maṇḍayet || 65 ||
