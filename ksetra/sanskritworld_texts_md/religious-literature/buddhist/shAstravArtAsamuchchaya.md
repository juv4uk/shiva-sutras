granthaprastāvanā: mokṣa-sādhanarūpa se dharma kī upādeyatā


praṇamya paramātmānaṃ vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā  |
sattvānāmalpabuddhīnāṃ śāstravārttāsamuccayam  || [1.1]1 ||
yaṃ śrutvā sarvaśāstreṣu prāyastattvaviniścayaḥ  |
jāyate dveṣaśamanaḥ svargasiddhisukhāvahaḥ  || [1.1]2 ||
duḥkhaṃ pāpātsukhaṃ dharmātsarvaśāstreṣu saṃsthitiḥ  |
na kartavyamataḥ pāpaṃ kartavyo dharmasaṃcayaḥ  || [1.1]3 ||
hiṃsānṛtādayaḥ pañca tattvāśraddhānameva ca  |
krodhādayaśca catvāra iti pāpasya hetavaḥ  || [1.1]4 ||
viparītāstu dharmasya eta evoditā buddhaiḥ  |
eteṣu satataṃ yatnaḥ samyakkāryaḥ sukhaiṣiṇā  || [1.1]5 ||
sādhusevā sadā bhaktyā maitrī sattveṣu bhāvataḥ  |
ātmīyagrahamokṣaśca dharmahetuprasādhanam  || [1.1]6 ||
upadeśaḥ śubho nityaṃ darśanaṃ dharmacāriṇām  |
sthāne vinaya ityetatsādhusevāphalaṃ mahat || [1.1]7 ||
maitrīṃ bhāvayato nityaṃ śubho bhāvaḥ prajāyate  |
tato bhāvodayājjantordveṣāgnirupaśāmyati  || [1.1]8 ||
aśeṣadoṣajananī niḥśeṣaguṇaghātinī  |
ātmīyagrahamokṣeṇa tṛṣṇāpi vinivarttate  || [1.1]9 ||
evaṃ guṇagaṇopeto viśuddhātmā sthirāśayaḥ  |
tattvaviddhiḥ samākhyātaḥ samyagdharmasya sādhakaḥ  || [1.1]10 ||
upādeyaśca saṃsāre dharma eva buddhaiḥ sadā  |
viśuddho muktaye sarvaṃ yato'nyadduḥkhakāraṇam  || [1.1]11 ||
anityaḥ priyasaṃyoga iherṣyāśokavatsalaḥ  |
anityaṃ yauvanaṃ cāpi kutsitācaraṇāspadam  || [1.1]12 ||
anityāḥ sampadastīvra-kleśavargasamudbhavāḥ  |
anityaṃ jīvitaṃ ceha sarvabhāvanibandhanam  || [1.1]13 ||
punarjanma punarmṛtyurhīnādisthānasaṃśrayaḥ  |
punaḥ punaśca yadataḥ sukhamatra na vidyate  || [1.1]14 ||
prakṛtyasundaraṃ hyevaṃ saṃsāre sarvameva yat |
ato'tra vada kiṃ yuktā kvacidāsthā vivekinām  || [1.1]15 ||
muktvā dharmaṃ jagadvandyamakalaṅkaṃ sanātanam  |
parārthasādhakaṃ dhīraiḥ sevitaṃ śīlaśālibhiḥ  || [1.1]16 ||
āha tatrāpi no yuktā yadi samyagnirūpyate  |
dharmasyāpi śubho yasmādbandha eva phalaṃ matam  || [1.1]17 ||
na cāyasasya bandhasya tadā hemamayasya ca  |
phale kaścidviśeṣo'sti pāratantryāviśeṣataḥ  || [1.1]18 ||
tasmādadharmavattvājyo dharmo'pyevaṃ mumukṣubhiḥ  |
dharmādharmakṣayānmuktirmunibhirvarṇitā yataḥ  || [1.1]19 ||
ucyate evamevaitatkintu dharmo dvidhā mataḥ  |
saṃjñānayoga evaikastathānyaḥ puṇyalakṣaṇaḥ  || [1.1]20 ||
jñānayogastapaḥ śuddhamāśaṃsādoṣavarjitam  |
abhyāsātiśayāduktaṃ tadvimukteḥ prasādhanam  || [1.1]21 ||
dharmastadapi cetsatyaṃ kiṃ na bandhaphalaḥ sa yat |
āśaṃsā varjito'nyo'pi kiṃ naivaṃ cedna yattathā  || [1.1]22 ||
bhogamuktiphalo dharmaḥ sa pravṛttītarātmakaḥ  |
samyagmithyādirūpaśca gatistantrāntareṣvapi  || [1.1]23 ||
tamantareṇa tu tayoḥ kṣayaḥ kena prasādhyate  |
sadā syānna kadācidvā yadyahetuka eva saḥ  || [1.1]24 ||
tasmādavaśyameṣṭavyaḥ kaścidhetustayoḥ kṣayeṃ  |
sa eva dharmo vijñeyaḥ śuddho muktiphalapradaḥ  || [1.1]25 ||
dharmādharmakṣayānmuktiryaccoktaṃ puṇyalakṣaṇam  |
heyaṃ dharmaṃ tadāśritya na tu saṃjñānayogakam  || [1.1]26 ||
atastatraiva yuktāsthā yadi samyagnirūpyate  |
saṃsāre sarvamevānyatdarśitaṃ duḥkhakāraṇam  || [1.1]27 ||
tasmācca jāyate muktiryathā mṛtyādivarjitā  |
tathopariṣṭādvakṣyāmaḥ samyakśāstrānusārataḥ  || [1.1]28 ||
idānīṃ tu samāsena śāstrasamyaktvamucyate  |
kuvādiyuktyapavyākhyā-nirāsenāvirodhataḥ  || [1.1]29 ||


(2) bhūtacaitanyavāda-khaṇḍana

pṛthivyādimahābhūta-kāryamātramidaṃ jagat |
na cātmadṛṣṭasadbhāvaṃ manyante bhūtavādinaḥ  || [1.2]30 ||
acetanāni bhūtāni na taddharmo na tatphalam  |
cetanāsti ca yasyeyaṃ sa evātmeti cāpare  || [1.2]31 ||
yadīyaṃ bhūtadharmaḥ syātpratyekaṃ teṣu sarvadā  |
upalambhyeta sattvādi-kāṭhinatvādayo yathā  || [1.2]32 ||
śaktirūpeṇā sā teṣu sadāto nopalabhyate  |
na ca tenāpi rūpeṇa satyasatyeva cenna tat || [1.2]33 ||
śaktivetanayoraivyaṃ nānātvaṃ vātha sarvathā  |
aikye sā cetaneveti nānātve'nyasya sā yataḥ  || [1.2]34 ||
anabhivyaktirapyasyā nyāyato nopapadyate  |
āvṛtirna yadanyena tattvasaṃkhyāvirodhataḥ  || [1.2]35 ||
na cāsau tatsvarūpeṇa teṣāmanyatareṇa vā  |
vyañjakatvapratijñānātnāvṛtirvyañjakaṃ yataḥ  || [1.2]36 ||
viśiṣṭapariṇāmabhā- -ve'pi hyatrāvṛtirna vai  |
bhāvatāptestathā nāma-vyañjakatvaprasaṅgataḥ  || [1.2]37 ||
na cāsau bhūtabhinno yattato vyaktiḥ sadā bhavet |
bhede tvadhikabhāvena tattvasaṃkhyā na yujyate  || [1.2]38 ||
svakāle'bhinna ityevaṃ kālābhāve na saṅgatam  |
lokasiddhāśraye tvātmā hanta ! nāśrīyate katham  || [1.2]39 ||
nātmāpi loke no siddho jātismaraṇasaṃśrayāt |
sarveṣāṃ tadabhāvaśca citrakarmavipākataḥ  || [1.2]40 ||
loke'pi naikataḥ sthānādāgatānāṃ tathekṣyate  |
aviśeṣeṇa sarveṣāmanubhūtārthasaṃsmṛtiḥ  || [1.2]41 ||
divyadarśanataścaiva tacchiṣṭāvyabhicārataḥ  |
pitṛkarmādisiddheśca hanta ! nātmāpyalaukikaḥ  || [1.2]42 ||
kāṭhinyābodharūpāṇi bhūtānyadhyakṣasiddhitaḥ  |
cetanā tu na tadrūpā sā kathaṃ tatphalaṃ bhavet?  || [1.2]43 ||
pratyekamasatī teṣu na ca syādreṇutailavat |
satī cedupalabhyeta bhinnarūpeṣu sarvadā  || [1.2]44 ||
asatsthūlatvamaṇvādau ghaṭādau dṛśyate yathā  |
tathāsatyeva bhūteṣu cetanāpīti cenmatiḥ  || [1.2]45 ||
nāsatsthūlatvamaṇvādau tebhya eva tadudbhavāt |
asatastatsamutpādo na yukto'tiprasaṅgataḥ  || [1.2]46 ||
pañcamasyāpi bhūtasya tebhyo'sattvāviśeṣataḥ  |
bhavedutpattirevaṃ ca tattvasaṃkhyā na yujyate  || [1.2]47 ||
na tajjananasvabhāvāścette'tra mānaṃ na vidyate  |
sthūlatvotpāda iṣṭaścettatsadbhāve'pyasau samaḥ  || [1.2]48 ||
na ca mūrttāṇusaṅghāta-bhinnaṃ sthūlatvamityadaḥ  |
teṣāmeva tathābhāvo nyāyyaṃ mānāvirodhataḥ  || [1.2]49 ||
bhede tadadalaṃ yasmātkathaṃ sadbhāvamaśnute  |
tadabhāve'pi tadbhāve sadā sarvatra vā bhavet || [1.2]50 ||
na caivaṃ bhūtasaṅghāta-mātraṃ caitanyamiṣyate  |
aviśeṣeṇa sarvatra tadvattadbhāvasaṅgateḥ  || [1.2]51 ||
evaṃ sati ghaṭādīnāṃ vyaktacaitanyabhāvataḥ  |
puruṣānna viśeṣaḥ syātsa ca pratyakṣabādhitaḥ  || [1.2]52 ||
atha bhinnasvabhāvāni bhūtānyeva yatastataḥ  |
tatsaṃghāteṣu caitanyaṃ na sarveṣvetadapyasat || [1.2]53 ||
svabhāvo bhūtamātratve sati nyāyānna bhidyate  |
viśeṣaṇaṃ vinā yasmānna tulyānāṃ viśiṣṭatā  || [1.2]54 ||
svarūpamātrabhede ca bhedo bhūtetarātmakaḥ  |
anyabhedakabhāve tu sa evātmā prasajyate  || [1.2]55 ||
havirguḍakaṇikkādi-dravyasaṅghātajānyapi  |
yathā bhinnasvabhāvāni khādyakāni tatheti cet || [1.2]56 ||
vyaktimātrata evaiṣāṃ nanu bhinnasvabhāvatā  |
rasavīryavipākādi-kāryabhedo na vidyate  || [1.2]57 ||
tadātmakatvamātratve saṃsthānādivilakṣaṇā  |
yatheyamasti bhūtānāṃ tathā sāpi kathaṃ na cet || [1.2]58 ||
kartrabhāvāttathā deśa-kālabhedādyayogataḥ  |
na cāsiddhamado bhūta-mātratve tadasaṃbhavāt || [1.2]59 ||
tathā ca bhūtamātratve na tatsaṅghātabhedayoḥ  |
bhedakābhāvato bhedo yuktaḥ samyagvicintyatām  || [1.2]60 ||
ekastathāparo neti tanmātratve tathāvidhaḥ  |
yatastadapi no bhinnaṃ tatastulyaṃ ca tattayoḥ  || [1.2]61 ||
syādetadbhūtajatve'pi grāvādīnāṃ vicitratā  |
lokasiddheti siddhaiva na sā tanmātrajā nanu  || [1.2]62 ||
adṛṣṭākāśakālādi-sāmagrītaḥ samudbhavāt |
tathaiva lokasaṃvitteranyathā tadabhāvataḥ  || [1.2]63 ||
na ceha laukiko mārgaḥ sthito'smābhirvicāryate  |
kiṃ tvayaṃ yujyate kveti tvannītau coktavanna saḥ  || [1.2]64 ||
mṛtadehe ca caitanyamupalabhyeta sarvathā  |
dehadharmādibhāvena tattaddharmādi nānyathā  || [1.2]65 ||
na ca lāvaṇyakārkaśya-śyāmatvairvyabhicāritā  |
mṛtadehe'pi sadbhāvādadhyakṣeṇaiva saṃgateḥ  || [1.2]66 ||
na cellāvaṇyasadbhāvo na sa tanmātrahetukaḥ  |
ata evānyasadbhāvādastyātmeti vyavasthitam  || [1.2]67 ||
na prāṇādirasau mānaṃ kiṃ tadbhāve'pi tulyatā  |
tadabhāvādabhāvaścedātmābhāve na kā pramā  || [1.2]68 ||
tena tadbhāvabhāvitvaṃ na bhūyo nalikādinā  |
saṃpādite'pyetatsiddheḥ so'nya eveti cedna tat || [1.2]69 ||
vāyusāmānyasaṃsiddhestatsvabhāvaḥ sa neti cet |
atrāpi na pramāṇaṃ vaścaitanyotpattireva cet || [1.2]70 ||
na tasyāmeva saṃdehāttavāyaṃ kena neti cet |
tattatsvarūpabhāvena tadabhāvaḥ kathaṃ nu cet || [1.2]71 ||
tadvailakṣaṇyasaṃvitteḥ mātṛcaitanyaje hyayam  |
sute tasminna doṣaḥ syānna na bhāve'sya mātari  || [1.2]72 ||
na ca saṃsvedajādyeṣu mātrabhāvena tadbhavet |
pradīpajñātamapyatra nimittatvānna bādhakam  || [1.2]73 ||
itthaṃ na tadupādānaṃ yujyate tatkathaṃcana  |
anyopādānabhāve ca tadevātmā prasajyate  || [1.2]74 ||
na tathābhāvinaṃ hetumantareṇopajāyate  |
kiñcinnaśyati naikāntādyathāha vyāsamaharṣiḥ  || [1.2]75 ||
nāsato vidyate bhāvo nābhāvo vidyate sataḥ  |
abhayorapi dṛṣṭo'ntastvanayostattvadarśibhiḥ  || [1.2]76 ||
nābhāvo bhāvamāpnoti śaśaśṛṅge tathāgateḥ  |
bhāvo nābhāvametīha dīpaścenna sa sarvathā  || [1.2]77 ||
evaṃ caitanyavānātmā siddhaḥ satatabhāvataḥ  |
paralokyapi vijñeyo yuktimārgānusāribhiḥ  || [1.2]78 ||

sato'sya kiṃ ghaṭasyeva pratyakṣeṇa na darśanam  |
astyeva darśanaṃ spaṣṭamahaṃpratyayavedanāt || [1.3]79 ||
bhrānto'haṃ gururityeṣaḥ satyamanyastvasau mataḥ  |
vyabhicāritvato nāsya gamakatvamathocyate  || [1.3]80 ||
pratyakṣasyāpi tattyājyaṃ tatsadbhāvāviśeṣataḥ  |
pratyakṣābhāsamanyaccedvyabhicāri na sādhu tat || [1.3]81 ||
ahaṃpratyayapakṣe'pi nanu sarvamidaṃ samam  |
atastadvadasau mukhyaḥ samyakpratyakṣamiṣyatām  || [1.3]82 ||
gurvī me tanurityādau bhedapratyayadarśanāt |
bhrāntatābhimatasyaiva sā yuktā netarasya tu  || [1.3]83 ||
ātmanātmagraho'pyasya tathānubhavasiddhitaḥ  |
tasyaiva tatsvabhāvatvātna tu yuktyā na yujyate  || [1.3]84 ||
na ca buddhiviśeṣo'yamahaṃkāraḥ prakalpyate  |
dānādibuddhikāle'pi tathāhaṃkāravedanāt || [1.3]85 ||
ātmanātmagrahe tasya tatsvabhāvatvayogataḥ  |
sadaivāgrahaṇaṃ hyevaṃ vijñeyaṃ karmadoṣataḥ  || [1.3]86 ||
ataḥ pratyakṣasaṃsiddhaḥ sarvaprāṇabhṛtāmayam  |
svayaṃjyotiḥ sadaivātmā tathā vede'pi paṭhyate  || [1.3]87 ||


(4) ātmā tathā karma ke sambandha meṃ matamatāntara

atrāpi varṇayantyeke saugatāḥ kṛtabuddhayaḥ  |
kliṣṭaṃ mano'sti yannityaṃ tadyathoktātmalakṣaṇam  || [1.4]88 ||
yadi nityaṃ tadātmaiva saṃjñābhedo'tra kevalam  |
athānityaṃ tataścedaṃ na yathoktātmalakṣaṇam  || [1.4]89 ||
yaḥ kartā karmabhedānāṃ bhoktā karmaphalasya ca  |
saṃsarttā parinirvātā sa hyātmā nānyalakṣaṇaḥ  || [1.4]90 ||
ātmatvenāviśiṣṭasya vaicitryaṃ tasya yadvaśāt |
narādirūpaṃ taccitramadṛṣṭaṃ karmasaṃjñitam  || [1.4]91 ||
tathā tulye'pi cārambhe sadupāye'pi yo nṛṇām  |
phalabhedaḥ sa no yukto yuktyā hetvantaraṃ vinā  || [1.4]92 ||
tasmādavaśyameṣṭavyaṃ tatra hetvantaraṃ paraiḥ  |
tadevādṛṣṭamityāhuranye śāstrakṛtaśramāḥ  || [1.4]93 ||
bhūtānāṃ tatsvabhāvatvādayamityapyanuttaram  |
na bhūtātmaka evātmetyetadatra nidarśitam  || [1.4]94 ||
karmaṇo bhautikatvena yadvaitadapi sāmpratam  |
ātmano vyatiriktaṃ tatcitrabhāvaṃ yato matam  || [1.4]95 ||
śaktirūpaṃ tadanye tu sūrayaḥ saṃpracakṣate  |
anye tu vāsanārūpaṃ vicitraphaladaṃ matam  || [1.4]96 ||
anye tvabhidadhatyatra svarūpaniyatasya vai  |
karturvinānyasaṃbandhaṃ śaktirākasmikī kutaḥ  || [1.4]97 ||
tatkriyāyogataḥ sā cettadapuṣṭau na yujyate  |
tadanyayogābhāve ca puṣṭirasya kathaṃ bhavet || [1.4]98 ||
astyeva sā sadā kantu kriyayā vyajyate param  |
ātmamātrasthitāyā na tasyā vyaktiḥ kadācana  || [1.4]99 ||
tadanyāvaraṇābhāvādbhāve vāsyaiva karmatā  |

śāstrādatīndriyagaterarthāpattyāpi gamyate  |
anyathā tatra nāśvāsaśchadmasthasyopajāyate  || [10.1]596 ||
pramāṇapañcakāvṛttirevaṃ tatra na yujyate  |
tathāpyabhāvaprāmāṇyamiti dhyāndhyavijṛmbhitam  || [10.1]597 ||
vedāddharmādisaṃsthāpi hantātīndriyadarśinam  |
vihāya gamyate samyakkuta etadvicintyatām  || [10.1]598 ||
na vṛddhasampradāyena chinnamūlatvayogataḥ  |

prāmāṇyaṃ rūpaviṣaye saṃpradāye na yuktimat |

nityatvāpauruṣeyatvādyasti kiñcidalaukikam  |

svayaṃ rāgādimānnārthaṃ vetti vedasya nānyataḥ  |
na vedayati vedo'pi vedārthasya gatiḥ kutaḥ  || [10.1]604 ||
tenāgnihotraṃ juhuyātsvargakāma iti śrutau  |
khādetśvamāṃsamityeṣa nārtha ityatra kā pramā  || [10.1]605 ||
pradīpādivadiṣṭaścettacchabdo'rthaprakāśakaḥ  |
svata eva pramāṇaṃ na kiñcidatrāpi vidyate  || [10.1]606 ||
viparītaprakāśaśca dhruvamāpadyate kvacit |
tathā hīndīvare dīpaḥ prakāśayati raktatām  || [10.1]607 ||
tasmānna cāviśeṣeṇa pratītirupajāyate  |
saṅketasavyapekṣatve svata evetyayuktimat || [10.1]608 ||
sādhurna veti saṅketo na cāśaṅkā nivartate  |
tadvaicitryopalabdheśca svāśayābhiniveśataḥ  || [10.1]609 ||
vyākhyāpyapauruṣeyyasya mānābhāvānna saṅgatā  |
mitho viruddhabhāvācca tatsādhutvādyaniściteḥ  || [10.1]610 ||
nānyapramāṇasaṃvādāttatsādhutvaviniścayaḥ  |
so'tīndriye na yannyāyyastattadbhāvavirodhataḥ  || [10.1]611 ||
tasmādvyākhyānamasyedaṃ svābhiprāyanivedanam  |
jaiminyāderna tulyaṃ kiṃ vacanenāpareṇa vaḥ  || [10.1]612 ||
eṣa sthāṇurayaṃ mārga iti vaktīha kaścana  |
anyaḥ svayaṃ bravīmīti tayorbhedaḥ parīkṣyatām  || [10.1]613 ||
na cāpyapauruṣeyo'sau ghaṭate sūpapattitaḥ  |
vaktṛvyāpāravaikalye tacchabdānupalabdhitaḥ  || [10.1]614 ||
vaktṛvyāpārabhāveti tadbhāve laukikaṃ na kim  |
apauruṣeyamiṣṭaṃ vo vaco dravyavyapekṣayā  || [10.1]615 ||
dṛśyamāne'pi cāśaṅkādṛśyakartṛsamudbhavā  |
nātīndriyārthadraṣāramantareṇa nivartate  || [10.1]616 ||
pāpādatredṛśī buddhirna puṇyāditi na pramā  |
na loko hi vigānatvāttadbahutvādyaniściteḥ  || [10.1]617 ||
bahūnāmapi saṃmoha-bhāvānmithyāpravartanāt |

atīndriyārthadraṣṭā tu pumān kaścidyadīṣyate  |

apauruṣeyatāpyasya nānyato hyavagamyate  |
karturasmaraṇādīnāṃ vyabhicārādidoṣataḥ  || [10.1]620 ||
nābhyāsa evamādīnāmapi kartāvigānataḥ  |
smaryate ca vigānena hantehāpyaṣṭakādayaḥ  || [10.1]621 ||
abhyāsaḥ karmaṇāṃ satyamutpādayati kauśalam  |
svakṛtādhyayanasyāpi tadbhāvo na virudhyate  |
gauravāpādanārthaṃ ca tathā syādanivedanam  || [10.1]622 ||
mantrādīnāṃ ca sāmarthyaṃ śābarāṇāmapi sphuṭam  |
pratītaṃ sarvaloke'pi na cāpyavyabhicāri tat || [10.1]623 ||
vede'pi paṭhyate hyeṣa mahātmā tatra tatra yat |

dharmādharmavyavastheyaṃ yujyate nānyataḥ kvacit || [10.1]626 ||


2. bauddha ke sarvajñatākhaṃḍana kā khaṃḍana

atrāpi prājña ityanya itthamāha subhāṣitam  |
iṣṭo'yamarthaḥ śakyeta jñātuṃ so'tiśayo yadi  || [10.2]627 ||
ayamevaṃ na vetyanya-doṣo nirdoṣatāpi vā  |
durlabhatvātpramāṇānāṃ durbodhetyapare viduḥ  || [10.2]628 ||
atrāpi bruvate vṛddhāḥ siddhamavyabhicāryapi  |
loke guṇādivijñānaṃ sāmānyena mahātmanām  || [10.2]629 ||
tannītipratipattyāderanyathā tanna yuktimat |
viśeṣajñānamapyevaṃ tadvadabhyāsato na kim  || [10.2]630 ||
doṣāṇāṃ hrāsadṛṣṭyeha tatsarvakṣayasaṃbhavāt |
tatsiddhau jñāyate prājñaistasyātiśaya ityapi  || [10.2]631 ||
hṛdgatāśeṣasaṃśīti-nirṇayādiprabhāvataḥ  |
tadātve vartamāne tu tadvyaktārthāvirodhataḥ  || [10.2]632 ||
na cāsyādarśane'pyadya sāmrājyasyeva nāstitā  |
saṃbhavo nyāyayuktastu pūrvameva nidarśitaḥ  || [10.2]633 ||
pratibhālocanaṃ tāvadidānīmapyatīndriye  |
suvaidyasaṃyatādīnāmavisaṃvādi dṛśyate  || [10.2]634 ||
evaṃ tatrāpi tadbhāve na virodho'sti kaścana  |
tadvyaktārthāvirodhādau jñānabhāvācca sāmpratam  || [10.2]635 ||
sarvatra dṛṣṭe saṃvādādadṛṣṭe nopajāyate  |
jñāturvisaṃvādāśaṅkā tadvaiśiṣṭyopalabdhitaḥ  || [10.2]636 ||
vastusthityāpi tattādṛgna visaṃvādakaṃ bhavet |
yathottaraṃ tathā dṛṣṭeriti caitanna sāṃpratam  || [10.2]637 ||
siddhyetpramāṇaṃ yadyevamapramāṇamatheha kim  |
na hyekaṃ nāsti satyārthaṃ puruṣe bahubhāṣiṇi  || [10.2]638 ||
yata ekaṃ na satyārthaṃ kintu sarvaṃ yathāśrutam  |
yatrāgame pramāṇaṃ sa iṣyate paṇḍitairjanaiḥ  || [10.2]639 ||
ātmā nāmī pṛthakkarma tatsaṃyogādbhavo'nyathā  |
muktirhiṃsādayo mukhyāstannivṛttiḥ sasādhanā  || [10.2]640 ||
atīndriyārthasaṃvādo viśuddho bhāvanāvidhiḥ  |
yatredaṃ yujyate sarvaṃ yogivyaktaṃ sa āgamaḥ  || [10.2]641 ||
adhikāryapi cāsyeha svayamajño'pi yaḥ pumān  |
kathitajñaḥ punardhīmāṃstadvaiyarthyamato'nyathā  || [10.2]642 ||
paricittādidharmāṇāṃ gatyupāyābhidhānataḥ  |
sarvārthaviṣayo'pyeṣa iti tadbhāvasaṃsthitiḥ  || [10.2]643 ||

gyārahavāṃ stabaka
1. śabdārthasaṃbaṃdhakhaṃḍana kā khaṃḍana

anye tvabhidadhatyatra yuktimārgakṛtaśramāḥ  |
śabdārthayorna saṃbandho vastusthityeha vidyate  || [11.1]644 ||
na tādātmyaṃ dvayābhāva-prasaṃgādbuddhibhedataḥ  |
śastrādyuktau mukhacchedādisaṃgātsamayasthiteḥ  || [11.1]645 ||

vandhyetarādiko bhedo rāmādīnāṃ yathaiva hi  |

paramārthaikatānatve'pyanyadoṣopavarṇanam  |

(2) jñāna tathā kiryā ke bīca prādhānyāprādhānya kā praśna

jñānādeva niyogena siddhimicchanti kecana  |
anye kriyāta eveti dvābhyāmanye vicakṣaṇāḥ  || [11.2]673 ||
jñānaṃ hi phaladaṃ puṃsāṃ na kiryā phaladā matā  |

asādhyārambhiṇastena samyagjñānaṃ na jātucit |
