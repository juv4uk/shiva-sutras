## Key Finding

The Baums transcription is ANALYTIC — it breaks sūtras into morphemes with hyphens, uses `=` for pratyāhāra boundaries, and capitalizes technical markers (T, C, N, Ñ, Ṅ, P, Ṭ, Ṛ, Ṣ). The Kāśikā (Sharma) gives sūtras in continuous sandhi form. Therefore most "conflicts" are notation-convention differences, not textual variants.

## Category 3: ORIGINAL GENUINE (9 sūtras) — ADJUDICATED

### 1.1.18 — GENUINE → RESOLVED (confirmed variant)
- Kāśikā: ūṃ
- Baums: oṃ
- Evidence: sanskritdictionary.com reads "ūm̐" (with anunāsika); Sanskrit Library SLP1 gives "U~" (= ūṃ); GRETIL Kāśikā (paninipu.htm) reads "ūṃ"; TextGrid independent mirror reads "oṃ". Kielhorn noted 1.1.17-18 originally formed one rule.
- Analysis: Well-known variant. Kāśikā tradition (Sharma) reads ūṃ (long ū + anusvāra). Baums/Böhtlingk tradition reads oṃ. Both are attested in different manuscript traditions. Neither is "wrong".
- Status: `GENUINE` — `RESOLVED` (variant, both attested)
- Notes: Affects pragṛhya rules — whether ū+anunāsika or o+anunāsika is the substituted form. Functionally similar but phonologically distinct. Both should be retained with provenance labels.

### 1.4.37 — GENUINE → RESOLVED (parser artifact)
- Kāśikā: krudha-druha-īrṣya-asūya-arthānāṃ yaṃ prati kopaḥ
- Baums: krudhḥ-druhḥ-īrṣyḥ=asūyān ām
- Analysis: The Kāśikā parser captured commentary words ("arthānāṃ", "prati", "kopaḥ") as part of the sūtra, while Baums gives the bare sūtra. The core sūtra is the same (krudha-druha-īrṣya-asūyā + aṃ pratyāhāra).
- Status: `RESOLVED` as parser artifact (Kāśikā commentary intrusion)
- Preferred witness: Baums (cleaner sūtra text)

### 4.2.94 — GENUINE → RESOLVED (numbering/alignment error)

### 5.3.5 — GENUINE → PROVISIONALLY RESOLVED
- Kāśikā: etado 'ś
- Baums: etad-aḥ=an
- Analysis: etado 'ś = etadaḥ aś (sandhi form). Baums gives "etad-aḥ=an" where "an" is lowercase (not "aÑ" with capital). This could be sandhi resolution of the Kāśikā "ś" form, or a genuine difference in the pratyāhāra (aŚ vs aÑ). Since Baums writes "an" in lowercase (not aÑ), this is most likely a notation difference.
- Verdict: Likely NOTATION but the pratyāhāra identity (aŚ vs aÑ) needs confirmation from Böhtlingk edition.
- Status: `PROVISIONALLY RESOLVED` as notation (pending Böhtlingk confirmation)

### 8.4.28 — GENUINE → RESOLVED (confirmed variant)

- Evidence: Sanskrit Library explicitly notes "A.8.4.28: upasargAd anotparaH [kASikA: upasargAd bahulam]". sanskritdocuments.org shows both readings side by side: "उपसर्गादनोत्परः (उपसर्गाद् बहुलम्)". The Mahābhāṣya tradition discusses this variant: "anotparaḥ" was replaced by "bahulam" in the Kāśikā tradition.
- Verdict: GENUINE textual variant, well-documented.
  - Sūtra-pāṭha (Baums/Böhtlingk): "upasargād anoṭparaḥ" — the original sūtra text
  - Kāśikā (Sharma): "upasargād bahulam" — an interpretive gloss that became standard
- Status: `GENUINE` — `RESOLVED` (confirmed variant)
- Preferred witness: Baums (original sūtra-pāṭha); Kāśikā reading preserved as tradition-level interpretation.
- Notes: Most important genuine variant in the set. Affects the scope of ṇ→ṇ substitution after upasargas.

Consider normalizing hyphens and morpheme boundaries before comparison.
