# H-SIVA-ORDER: Canonical Ordering Hypothesis

## Statement
The canonical ordering of the Śiva-sūtras (42 phonemes) possesses an exceptionally strong Consecutive Ones Property (C1P) relative to the phonological classes required by the Aṣṭādhyāyī. The ordering is highly rigid and optimized for the interval-layout problem.

## Nature of the Hypothesis
This is a combinatorial hypothesis regarding the **sequence** of elements, completely ignoring the `it`-markers. It treats the 42 target sets as constraints on a linear permutation.

## Verification Requirements
- Must prove that the 42 target sets form a nearly unique linear ordering modulo reversal and trivial symmetries.
- Must identify the Minimal Determining Set (MDS) of constraints that forces this ordering.
- Must reconstruct the target sets from grammatical operations rather than pratyāhāra expansion to avoid circularity.
