# H-SIVA-MARKERS: Marker Addressing Hypothesis

## Statement
Given the canonical sound ordering of the Śiva-sūtras, the specific placement of `it`-markers (anubandhas) provides an unusually compact, optimized, and unambiguous addressing system for the required target phonological classes.

## Nature of the Hypothesis
This is an information-theory and addressing hypothesis. It operates on **Level 2**. It assumes the sounds are already laid out in a C1P-compatible order, and evaluates how well the markers are placed to generate short, unambiguous names (pratyāhāras) for the valid intervals.

## Verification Requirements
- Must be evaluated strictly *after* H-SIVA-ORDER.
- Must prove that the historical placement of markers is optimal (or near-optimal) for addressing the required subsets compared to alternative marker placements on the same sound sequence.
- Must analyze the role of duplicate markers (like `ṇ`) and symmetry breaking.
